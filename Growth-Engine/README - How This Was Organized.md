# Growth-Engine — Organized Folder Structure

This was originally a Notion export containing **1,632 files**: 774 already sat inside
five pre-built folders (Marketing, Sales Funnel, Marketing Funnel, legacy-docs, Automation),
and **858 files were loose** directly in the root with no folder at all.

This pass sorts every loose file into a topic folder based on its title, and leaves the
five pre-existing structured folders untouched (just moved under a `00 ...` prefix so they
sort to the top, unchanged inside).

## Folders

- **00 * (pre-existing structure)** — the 5 folders that were already organized in the export (Marketing, Sales Funnel, Marketing Funnel, legacy-docs, Automation). Left as-is. Note: "Marketing" and "Marketing Funnel" overlap heavily (same M11–M16 sub-pages) — worth a manual check to see if one is an older duplicate of the other.
- **01 VA Operations & Training** — VA/Supervisor onboarding, training, SOPs, quick-reference cards, portals, checklists, offboarding.
- **02 Sales Scripts & Conversion** — objection handling, call scripts, proposals, closing, upsell/cross-sell, sales funnel stages.
- **03 Outreach & Lead Generation** — LinkedIn/WhatsApp/cold email outreach, lead magnets, lead scoring, CRM, prospecting.
- **04 Marketing & Content Systems** — SEO, branding, social media, content calendars, growth hacking, email newsletters.
- **05 Automation & AI Systems** — n8n workflows, chatbot setup, AI systems, Zapier/Make automations.
- **06 Packages, Pricing & Service Catalog** — all NIVY LEVEL 1–4 package docs, pricing tiers, service catalogs, ISO/compliance offerings.
- **07 Strategy, Planning & Roadmaps** — master plans, phase/stage roadmaps, audits, workspace restructuring plans.
- **08 KPI, Performance & Dashboards** — KPI trackers, dashboards, scorecards.
- **09 Policies, Legal & Contracts** — NDAs, data protection, refund policy, payment terms.
- **10 Client & Case Studies** — client onboarding kits, case studies, executive summaries.
- **11 Research & Market Intelligence** — market research, target lists, directory-listing platforms (JustDial, IndiaMART, Clutch, G2, etc.).
- **12 System Hubs, Index & Navigation** — hub/index/home pages and short generic pages (About, Overview, Links, etc.).
- **13 Archive & Deprecated** — anything flagged old/deprecated/being consolidated/orphaned.
- **14 Assets & Data Exports** — images, CSVs, xlsx, zip files.
- **15 Miscellaneous** — ~79 files (~5% of the total) that didn't clearly match a category — mostly one-off Notion pages, default Notion template boilerplate ("Click me to..." tips), and single-instance topics. Worth a quick manual skim.

## Notes
- Original Notion filenames (with their long hash IDs) were kept as-is so nothing breaks if you re-import to Notion; only the folder location changed.
- Sorting was done by matching keywords in each page's title — it's a strong first pass but not perfect, especially for the Miscellaneous folder and any page whose title doesn't reflect its content.
