## 🗺️ Master Customer Journey Flow

```
STRANGER
   ↓
[1] ATTENTION → Content, Ads, Outbound, SEO
   ↓
[2] INTEREST → Case Studies, Webinars, Lead Magnets
   ↓
[3] LEAD CAPTURE → Forms, WhatsApp, Booking, Chatbots
   ↓
[4] LEAD MANAGEMENT → CRM, Scoring, Segmentation
   ↓
[5] NURTURING → Email Sequences, WhatsApp, Retargeting
   ↓
[6] CONVERSION → Sales Calls, Proposals, Close
   ↓
[7] ONBOARDING → Welcome, Setup, Kickoff
   ↓
[8] DELIVERY → Reports, KPIs, Campaign Execution
   ↓
[9] RETENTION → Community, VIP Support, Renewals
   ↓
[10] EXPANSION → Upsells, Cross-sells, Upgrades
   ↓
[11] REFERRAL → Ambassador Program, Share & Earn
   ↓
[12] ECOSYSTEM → Partners, Freelancers, Viral Loops
   ↓
LONG-TERM BRAND ASSET
```
