## Day 1 — WhatsApp Business (1 hour)

**Why first:** Every lead in India communicates on WhatsApp. This is your primary sales channel.

**What to do:**

1. Go to the Google Play Store or App Store
2. Search for "WhatsApp Business" (it is a separate app from regular WhatsApp)
3. Download and install it
4. Open it and set up with your business phone number
5. Fill in these fields completely:
    - Business name: Nivy Digital (or your exact brand name)
    - Business category: Marketing Agency
    - Business description: Write 2-3 sentences about what you do and who you help. Example: "Nivy Digital helps small businesses and startups grow their revenue through done-for-you digital marketing. We handle your Meta ads, SEO, content, and WhatsApp marketing so you can focus on running your business."
    - Business hours: Set the hours you will actually respond
    - Website: Leave blank for now if you don't have one
    - Address: Your city is enough
6. Set an Auto-Reply message for when you're away. Go to Settings > Business Tools > Away Message. Write: "Hi! Thank you for reaching out to Nivy Digital. We have received your message and will get back to you within 2 hours during business hours (9am-7pm). If this is urgent, please reply with URGENT and we'll prioritize your message."
7. Set a Greeting message for new contacts. Go to Settings > Business Tools > Greeting Message. Write: "Welcome to Nivy Digital! We help businesses grow through smart digital marketing. How can we help you today?"

**Done when:** WhatsApp Business is installed, profile is complete, auto-reply is set.

## Day 1 — HubSpot CRM (1 hour)

**Why:** You need one place to track every person you talk to. Without this, leads fall through the cracks.

**What to do:**

1. Go to [hubspot.com](http://hubspot.com) on your laptop or desktop (easier to set up on desktop)
2. Click "Get started free" — the free plan is all you need
3. Sign up with your business email (create a Gmail like [hello@nivy.com](mailto:hello@nivy.com) if you don't have one yet — Google Workspace is free for 14 days then $6/month, or just use a free Gmail for now)
4. Complete the setup wizard — select "Marketing Agency" as your business type
5. Skip any paid upgrade prompts
6. Once inside, go to Contacts > Deals > and create your first pipeline:
    - Click "Sales" in the top menu
    - Click "Deals"
    - Click "Pipelines" > "Create pipeline"
    - Name it: Nivy Sales Pipeline
    - Add these stages in order:
        1. New Lead
        2. Contacted
        3. Discovery Call Booked
        4. Proposal Sent
        5. Negotiation
        6. Closed Won
        7. Closed Lost
7. Bookmark HubSpot. You will open it every single day.

**Done when:** HubSpot account created, pipeline set up with 7 stages.

## Day 2 — Calendly Booking Link (30 minutes)

**Why:** When someone is interested, you need to send them ONE link. No back-and-forth asking "when are you free?"

**What to do:**

1. Go to [calendly.com](http://calendly.com)
2. Click "Sign up free"
3. Sign up with Google (easiest)
4. Connect your Google Calendar when prompted — this is important so you don't get double bookings
5. Set up your first event type:
    - Click "+ New Event Type"
    - Choose "One-on-One"
    - Name it: "Free Strategy Call with Nivy Digital"
    - Duration: 30 minutes
    - Set your available hours (e.g. Mon-Fri, 10am-6pm)
    - Location: Google Meet (Calendly will auto-create the link)
    - Description: "In this free 30-minute call, we'll look at your current marketing, identify the 2-3 biggest growth opportunities, and tell you exactly what we'd do if you worked with us. No pressure, no sales pitch — just clarity."
6. Save it
7. Copy your booking link (it looks like [calendly.com/yourname/strategy-call](http://calendly.com/yourname/strategy-call))
8. Save this link somewhere — you will paste it everywhere

**Done when:** Booking link is live and you've tested it yourself by making a test booking.

## Day 2 — Tally Lead Capture Form (45 minutes)

**Why:** When someone visits your Instagram bio or WhatsApp, you need a form to capture their info so you can follow up properly.

**What to do:**

1. Go to [tally.so](http://tally.so)
2. Click "Sign up for free"
3. Sign up with Google
4. Click "New form"
5. Name it: "Nivy Digital — Free Marketing Audit"
6. Add these questions in order:
    - Your full name (Short answer, required)
    - Your WhatsApp number (Short answer, required)
    - Your business name (Short answer, required)
    - Your business type / industry (Dropdown: E-commerce, Restaurant, Coaching/Consulting, Real Estate, Healthcare, Retail, Other)
    - Your monthly marketing budget (Dropdown: Under 10,000/month, 10,000-25,000/month, 25,000-50,000/month, 50,000+/month, Not sure yet)
    - What is your biggest marketing challenge right now? (Long answer, required)
    - How did you hear about us? (Dropdown: Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Referral, Google, Other)
7. Add a Thank You page message: "Thank you! We've received your details and our team will WhatsApp you within 4 hours to schedule your free audit call. Keep an eye on your WhatsApp."
8. Publish the form
9. Copy the form link
10. Go to HubSpot > Forms > and note that you'll manually log form responses for now (you'll automate this in Phase 3)

**Done when:** Form is live, you can see the link, test submission works.

## Day 3-4 — Instagram or LinkedIn Profile (3 hours total)

**Choose ONE platform to start.** If your clients are business owners (B2B), use LinkedIn. If your clients are restaurants, coaches, e-commerce stores (B2C businesses), use Instagram. You can do both later.

**For Instagram:**

1. Switch to a Professional Account: Profile > Settings > Account > Switch to Professional Account > Business
2. Profile photo: A clear, professional photo of your face. Not a logo.
3. Name field: Your name
4. Username: @nivydigital or similar
5. Bio (you have 150 characters):
    - Line 1: What you do (e.g. "We grow businesses with digital marketing")
    - Line 2: Who you help (e.g. "Helping Indian SMEs get more leads & clients")
    - Line 3: Social proof if any (e.g. "15+ brands grown" or "Varanasi's #1 growth agency")
    - Line 4: CTA with link (e.g. "Book free audit call")
6. Link in bio: Paste your Calendly booking link OR your Tally form link
7. Create 3 content posts before you start outreach (see Day 5)

**For LinkedIn:**

1. Profile photo: Professional headshot
2. Banner image: Create a simple one on Canva (free) — search "LinkedIn banner" in Canva templates
3. Headline: Do not write "Founder at Nivy Digital". Write: "I help [target client type] get more leads and clients through digital marketing | Founder, Nivy Digital"
4. About section: Write 150-200 words. Start with who you help, what problem you solve, what makes you different, and end with a CTA to book a call.
5. Add your services (LinkedIn has a Services section — use it)
6. Set your featured section to show your Calendly link

**Done when:** Profile is complete with bio, photo, link, and at least 3 posts drafted.

## Day 5-6 — Your First 9 Content Posts (4 hours)

**Why:** Before you do outreach, your profile needs to look active and credible. If someone gets a DM from you and visits your profile and it's empty, they won't respond.

**Create these 9 posts using Canva ([canva.com](http://canva.com) — free):**

Post 1 — Who we are: Introduce Nivy Digital. What you do, who you help, why you started.

Post 2 — Problem post: "The #1 reason most [your target client] businesses are not getting leads online" — then explain the problem you solve.

Post 3 — Result post: Share a result you've achieved (even from personal projects, freelance work, or hypothetical case study based on your knowledge). Be honest about what it was.

Post 4 — How-to post: "5 things every [business type] needs on their Instagram to get more clients" — give real value.

Post 5 — Myth post: "3 myths about digital marketing that are costing you money"

Post 6 — Service post: What exactly does Nivy Digital offer? List your services with prices or price range.

Post 7 — Behind the scenes: Show your workspace, your process, your day. People buy from people.

Post 8 — FAQ post: Answer the top 3 questions clients ask before hiring a marketing agency.

Post 9 — CTA post: "If you want us to audit your marketing for free, click the link in bio"

**Posting schedule:** Post 1 today, then 1 post per day. Do not post all 9 at once.

**Canva setup (5 minutes):**

1. Go to [canva.com](http://canva.com)
2. Sign up free with Google
3. Search "Instagram post" or "LinkedIn post" in templates
4. Pick one template and use it for all 9 posts (consistency = brand)
5. Change colors to match your brand (pick 2 colors and stick to them)

## Day 7 — Simple Proposal Template (2 hours)

**Why:** When someone says yes to a call, you need to send them a proposal within 24 hours. Create it now so it's ready.

**Create this in Google Docs ([docs.google.com](http://docs.google.com) — free):**

Page 1 — Cover: Nivy Digital logo (or name), "Digital Marketing Proposal", client name, date

Page 2 — Understanding Your Business: Write 2-3 sentences about their business and the specific problem they mentioned on the call. This shows you listened.

Page 3 — Our Recommended Solution: List the specific services you are recommending for THIS client. Not a generic list.

Page 4 — What We Will Deliver: Month 1, Month 2, Month 3 deliverables. Be specific.

Page 5 — Investment: Your packages. Start with 3 tiers:

- Starter: 15,000-20,000/month (1-2 services)
- Growth: 30,000-40,000/month (3-4 services)
- Premium: 60,000+/month (full service)

Page 6 — Why Nivy: 3-4 reasons. Your approach, your guarantee, your responsiveness.

Page 7 — Next Steps: "To get started, reply to this email with your preferred start date and we will send you the agreement and invoice within 24 hours."

**Save it as a template.** For each client, duplicate it, personalize pages 2-3, and send.

## Day 8-14 — First Outreach (1-2 hours per day)

**This is the most important part of Phase 1. Do this every single day.**

**Who to message:**

Start with the people closest to you who might be potential clients or know potential clients:

- Business owners in your city (Varanasi and nearby)
- Restaurants, coaching businesses, real estate agents, clinics, e-commerce stores
- Old classmates who now run businesses
- People in your family's network
- Business owners who follow you or whose content you see

**How to find them on Instagram:**

1. Search hashtags like #VaranasiRestaurant #VaranasiBusiness #UPBusiness
2. Look at who is running ads (they are already spending money on marketing)
3. Look at local business pages that have fewer than 5,000 followers (they need help)

**WhatsApp outreach message (personalize for each person):**

"Hi [Name], I came across [their business name] on Instagram. I work with [their business type] businesses to help them get more clients through digital marketing. I noticed [one specific thing about their business — e.g. 'your food looks amazing but your Instagram engagement is quite low']. I'd love to offer you a free 30-minute marketing audit — completely no obligation. Would that be useful to you?"

**Instagram DM message:**

"Hi [Name]! Love what you're building with [business name]. Quick question — are you currently running any paid ads or trying to grow your leads online? I'm asking because I specialize in helping [business type] get more clients through digital marketing and I offer free 30-min audits for businesses I think I can genuinely help. Would you be open to a quick call?"

**Target: 10 outreach messages per day minimum.** In 7 days that is 70 conversations started. Even if only 5% respond positively, that is 3-4 discovery calls booked.

**Log every outreach in HubSpot:**

- Every person you message = create a Contact in HubSpot
- Add them to your pipeline at "New Lead"
- When they reply, move them to "Contacted"
- When they book a call, move them to "Discovery Call Booked"
