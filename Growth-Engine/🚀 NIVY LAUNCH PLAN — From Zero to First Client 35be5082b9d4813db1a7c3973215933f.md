## The Simple Truth Before You Start

You do not need to build everything at once. The 12-stage OS is the full vision. But to get your first lead and first client, you only need 7 things working:

1. A WhatsApp Business account
2. A free HubSpot CRM account
3. A Tally form (free)
4. One piece of content posted daily on Instagram or LinkedIn
5. A Calendly booking link
6. A simple proposal template
7. You showing up and doing outreach every day

That is it. The automations, n8n, and AI come AFTER you have clients, not before.

---

## The 4 Phases

| Phase | Timeline | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 - Foundation | Week 1-2 | Set up free tools, create your online presence, send first outreach messages |
| Phase 2 - Pipeline | Week 3-4 | Build your lead capture system, start getting enquiries, close first client |
| Phase 3 - Systems | Month 2 | Automate follow-ups, set up n8n, deliver for first clients professionally |
| Phase 4 - Scale | Month 3+ | AI, referrals, expansion, ecosystem |

---

## Important Rules

- Do Phase 1 completely before touching Phase 2. Every tool in Phase 1 is free.
- Do not install n8n until Phase 3. You don't need it yet.
- Do not buy any paid tools until you have at least 1 paying client.
- Revenue first. Perfect systems later.
