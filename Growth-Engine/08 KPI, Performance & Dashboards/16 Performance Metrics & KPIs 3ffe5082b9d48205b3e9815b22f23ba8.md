# 16. Performance Metrics & KPIs

## 

### Purpose of Metrics System

- Track performance across the entire funnel
- Identify bottlenecks quickly
- Improve conversion and efficiency
- Make data-driven decisions

---

### Daily Metrics (Execution Level)

Track per VA:

- Leads added
- Messages sent
- Follow-ups sent
- Replies received
- Calls booked

**Purpose:**

- Ensure daily activity consistency
- Monitor individual performance

---

### Weekly Metrics (Conversion Level)

Track:

- Total replies
- Interested leads
- Calls booked
- Calls attended
- Conversion rate (reply → call)

**Purpose:**

- Identify drop-offs in funnel
- Improve messaging and conversion

---

### Monthly Metrics (Business Level)

Track:

- Total clients acquired
- Revenue generated
- Average deal size
- Close rate (%)

**Purpose:**

- Measure overall business growth
- Optimize pricing and sales strategy

---

### Benchmarks per VA

**Daily Benchmarks:**

- 200+ leads added
- 50–80 messages sent
- 5–10 replies
- 1–2 calls booked

**Monthly Benchmarks:**

- 20–40 calls generated
- 3–8 clients (depending on close rate)

---

### Key Rules (CRITICAL)

- Track metrics daily without fail
- Focus on improving weak areas
- Compare performance across VAs
- Use data to guide decisions, not assumptions

---

### Output of This System

- Clear visibility of performance
- Improved efficiency over time
- Predictable scaling
- Data-driven growth engine
