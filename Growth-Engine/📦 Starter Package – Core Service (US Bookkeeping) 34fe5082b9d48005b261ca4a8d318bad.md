## 🔹 13. FAQs (Objections)

**Q: Why $399?**

→ Because this covers end-to-end monthly bookkeeping, reconciliation, and reporting—not just data entry. You get clean, tax-ready books and consistent reporting.

**Q: I already have a bookkeeper**

→ No problem. We can do a quick review and either collaborate or take over to improve accuracy, reporting, and timeliness.

**Q: Can you do taxes?**

→ This plan focuses on bookkeeping. We keep your books fully tax-ready and can coordinate with your CPA or upgrade you to our Tax package.

**Q: How do you ensure data security?**

→ We use secure access methods, role-based permissions, and standard cloud tools with encryption. No local data storage.

**Q: What if my transactions exceed 300?**

→ We’ll either charge a small overage or recommend upgrading to the Growth package for better value.

**Q: Do you support eCommerce integrations?**

→ Yes, basic integrations are supported. Advanced multi-channel setups are covered in higher plans.

**Q: How do we communicate?**

→ Primary via email with optional WhatsApp for quick updates. You’ll also receive a monthly summary with reports.

**Q: When will I receive my reports?**

→ Within 5–7 days after month-end, consistently every month.

**Q: What happens during onboarding?**

→ We collect access, review your current books, fix any issues (if needed), and set up a smooth monthly workflow.

**Q: Do you offer refunds?**

→ We ensure alignment during onboarding. If there’s a service mismatch in the first cycle, we work to resolve or adjust the scope.

**Q: Can you handle multiple entities?**

→ Not in this plan. Multi-entity setups are handled in higher packages.

**Q: Will you categorize all my expenses?**

→ Yes, based on standard accounting practices and your business context. We can also follow custom rules if provided.

**Q: Do you provide audit support?**

→ We maintain audit-ready books. Direct audit handling can be added as a separate service.

**Q: Can you migrate me from another software?**

→ Yes, migration to supported systems can be done as a one-time setup/cleanup project.

**Q: What if my past books are messy?**

→ We’ll first do a one-time cleanup to fix everything, then maintain accurate books going forward.

**Q: Is there a contract or lock-in?**

→ We typically work on a monthly model with clear scope. Long-term plans can be discussed for better pricing.
