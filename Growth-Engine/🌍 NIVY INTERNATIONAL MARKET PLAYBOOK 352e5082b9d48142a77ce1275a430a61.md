### 2. USA Outreach Protocol

**Platform Priority for USA:**

1. LinkedIn (B2B, decision-makers) — primary platform
2. Email ([Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) or [Hunter.io](http://Hunter.io) sourced) — secondary
3. Instagram (B2C, personal brands) — tertiary
4. WhatsApp — ONLY after they respond. Never cold.

**Tone for USA Outreach:**

- Direct. No pleasantries before the point.
- Lead with the outcome, not the service.
- Use data and social proof where possible.
- Never say "kindly" or "revert back" — these signal non-native communication.

**USA LinkedIn Message — Template:**

> *"Hi [Name], I noticed you're building [specific thing from profile]. We work with [type of business] to [specific outcome — e.g. generate 10–20 qualified calls/month through LinkedIn outreach]. Would it be worth a 15-minute conversation?"*
> 

**USA Cold Email — Template:**

> *Subject: [First name] — quick question about [specific thing]*
> 

> *"Hi [Name], I came across [their business/content]. We help [ICP type] with [specific outcome]. We recently did [specific result] for a similar business. Worth a quick 15 min to see if there’s a fit? [Your name], Nivy"*

## ⚖️ LEGAL / COMPLIANCE REFERENCE — INTERNATIONAL OUTREACH

### CAN-SPAM (USA Email Outreach)

- Every cold email must include: your business name, physical address, unsubscribe option
- Honour unsubscribe requests within 10 business days
- Subject line must not be deceptive
- No purchased or scraped email lists without verified opt-in source

### GDPR (EU / UK Outreach)

- Cold B2B email is permitted under GDPR if there is a "legitimate interest" — the offer must be relevant to their business
- Must include opt-out option in every email
- Do NOT store EU personal data without a lawful basis
- If a contact requests data deletion — comply within 30 days

### General Safe Practices

- Never claim results you cannot prove
- Never make income or revenue guarantees ("you'll get X leads") — use "our clients typically see" language instead
- Never misrepresent the team location if directly asked
- Keep all lead data secure — do not share prospect lists externally
