**MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is entered into as of [DATE] between **Nivy Empires** ("Nivy Next"), with a place of business at [ADDRESS — pending, see Task 46/47 follow-up], and **[COUNTERPARTY NAME]** ("Counterparty"), collectively the "Parties."

**1. Purpose.** The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship [describe: e.g., "a services engagement in digital marketing/web development/AI automation"] (the "Purpose") and in connection with the Purpose may disclose certain confidential information to each other.

**2. Definition of Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means any non-public business, technical, financial, or operational information disclosed by either Party, whether oral, written, or electronic, that is designated confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature.

**3. Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was already known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without duty of confidentiality.

**4. Obligations.** The receiving Party will: (a) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (b) protect it with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care; (c) not disclose it to third parties except employees/contractors with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement.

**5. Term.** This Agreement remains in effect for [X years — typically 2–3] from the date above. Confidentiality obligations for information disclosed during the term survive for [X years — typically 3–5, or "indefinitely" for trade secrets] after termination.

**6. Return/Destruction.** Upon request or termination, the receiving Party will return or destroy all Confidential Information and confirm in writing, except as required to retain a copy for legal/compliance record-keeping.

**7. No License.** Nothing in this Agreement grants any license or ownership right in the disclosing Party's Confidential Information beyond the limited use permitted under Section 4.

**8. Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of [JURISDICTION — pending founder decision; likely candidates given target markets: India (home jurisdiction) or the jurisdiction of the specific counterparty's country], without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

**9. No Obligation.** Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to proceed with the Purpose or any further business relationship.
