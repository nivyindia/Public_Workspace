**MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT**

Entered into as of [DATE] between **Nivy Empires** ("Nivy Next," "Provider") and **[CLIENT NAME]** ("Client").

**1. Structure.** This Agreement governs the overall relationship between the Parties. Specific services, deliverables, timelines, and fees for each engagement are set out in a Statement of Work ("SOW") referencing this Agreement. In case of conflict, the SOW controls for that engagement's specifics; this Agreement controls for general terms.

**2. Services.** Provider will perform the services described in each executed SOW using commercially reasonable skill and care consistent with industry standards for the relevant service line(s) (digital marketing, web development, IT services, AI & automation, virtual assistant, graphic design, or video editing).

**3. Fees & Payment.** Fees are as specified in each SOW. Unless otherwise stated: invoices are due within [15/30] days of issue; late payments accrue [interest rate — jurisdiction-dependent, confirm with legal] per month; Provider may pause work on undisputed overdue invoices after [X days'] written notice.

**4. Client Responsibilities.** Client will provide timely access, feedback, approvals, and materials reasonably necessary for Provider to perform the services. Delays caused by Client-side dependencies extend applicable timelines proportionally and do not constitute Provider breach.

**5. Intellectual Property.** Upon full payment for the applicable SOW, Client owns the final work product delivered under that SOW, excluding: (a) Provider's pre-existing tools, frameworks, templates, and methodologies (licensed to Client for use with the deliverable, not owned by Client); (b) third-party/open-source components, which remain governed by their own licenses.

**6. Confidentiality.** Governed by the Parties' NDA (see NDA_Template.md) if separately executed, or by confidentiality terms incorporated here by reference — **flag for legal: confirm whether a standalone NDA or MSA-embedded confidentiality clause is the intended structure**, since this research program built both as separate templates.

**7. Data Handling.** Provider will handle Client data consistent with its Information Security Policy and Data Handling Policy (available on request), including the cross-border transfer disclosure in Data Handling Policy §6 (India-based delivery team) — **this section is the primary place the UAE data-localization question (Data Handling Policy §5) needs to be legally resolved before UAE clients sign.**

**8. Warranties & Disclaimer.** Provider warrants services will be performed in a professional manner. EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED, SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND — **standard limitation language; needs jurisdiction-specific legal wording, this is a placeholder structure only.**

**9. Limitation of Liability.** Provider's total liability under this Agreement and any SOW is limited to the fees paid for the specific SOW giving rise to the claim in the preceding [3/6] months — **placeholder cap; must be set with legal and, ideally, only after the insurance gap flagged in the Vendor Security Questionnaire Answer Bank is resolved (an uninsured liability cap is a real business risk, not just a legal drafting question).**

**10. Term & Termination.** This Agreement remains in effect until terminated by either Party with [30/60] days' written notice, or immediately for material breach not cured within [15] days of notice. Active SOWs survive termination of this Agreement through their own completion unless otherwise agreed.

**11. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution.** [PENDING — see NDA_Template.md §8 flag]. Consider whether cross-border clients (UK/US/Canada/Australia/UAE) warrant an arbitration clause over litigation, given the practical difficulty of litigating from India against a foreign counterparty and vice versa — **flag as a founder + legal strategic decision, not just a drafting detail.**
