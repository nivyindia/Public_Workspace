| Freelancer marketplaces | Upwork, Fiverr | Low-cost entry point for price-sensitive buyers; commoditizes single-service work (logo, one-off video edit) |

**Name discrepancy found — "Z Rankings":** The original entry's description (16+ years, 16,000+ clients, 28+ countries, identical service list: SEO/branding/web dev/ORM/digital audits/AI automation) is a near-verbatim match to **EZ Rankings'** own published marketing copy. Direct checks confirm: (1) zrankings.com is an unrelated ski-resort ranking site, not a digital marketing agency — so the original Semrush-directory source likely mis-recorded or truncated the name; (2) ezrankings.com is a real India-based agency (Noida) with published pricing (SEO $225–$1,099/mo national tier, $650–$2,600/mo ecommerce tier, $300–$900/mo local tier — see ezrankings.com/seo-pricing.html). Recommend treating "Z Rankings" as EZ Rankings going forward, but flag this for a human to sanity-check against the original source material before finalizing — this is an inference from matching text, not a confirmed identity.

**Eminenture and Fusion Business Solutions (FBSPL):** both companies' own websites were checked directly and confirmed to publish no pricing anywhere (consistent with custom-quote B2B positioning). However, both have Clutch profiles showing a min. project size $1,000+ / avg. hourly rate <$25/hr band — identical to VerveLogic's. This is client-reported aggregate data via Clutch, not company-published pricing, so treat it as directional market-rate evidence, not a quotable number.
