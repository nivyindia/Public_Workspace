**Module:** 02 — Market Intelligence (feeds Deliverable 4 / Task 10b, Deliverable 4.4 pricing) | **Status:** v1 — built from real, publicly published pricing (named competitors + market-rate guides); updated 2026-08-08 after checking competitor websites directly | **Purpose:** A starting reference to sanity-check `Package_Pricing_AllServiceLines_v1.md` against real market numbers. Not a full substitute for direct quotes from the 5 India-based direct competitors (VerveLogic, ColorWhistle, EZ Rankings [formerly recorded as "Z Rankings" — see Competitor_Data_Collection_Master.md for the identity note], Eminenture, Fusion/FBSPL) — none publish complete package pricing, but all 5 now have at least a partial pricing signal from direct site checks or Clutch aggregate data.

## How to Read This
Two data layers, kept separate on purpose:
1. **India-based/offshore direct competitors** — the actual competitive set Nivy Next sells against. Thin data (most quote-only), but what exists is included.
2. **US-market named providers per service line** — not who Nivy Next competes head-to-head with, but the price ceiling US/UK/Canada/Australia buyers already know and compare offshore quotes against. This is the number a prospect has in their head before they talk to Nivy Next.

## 1. Digital Marketing
**India-based direct competitors:** ColorWhistle — no fixed packages ("no one-size-fits-all"), but their own published methodology confirms tiered/custom builds starting low-budget for startups. VerveLogic — publishes an actual SEO pricing page (fixed plans) and shows Clutch min. project size $1,000+, avg. hourly rate <$25/hr — meaningfully below Nivy Next's current hypothesis floor. **EZ Rankings** *(likely = the entry previously recorded as "Z Rankings" — see identity note in Competitor_Data_Collection_Master.md)* — publishes full tiered SEO pricing directly on their site: National SEO $225–$1,099/mo, Ecommerce SEO $650–$2,600/mo, Local SEO $300–$900/mo — the lowest published floor of any direct competitor checked so far.
**US-market anchor (per Clutch 2026 data, NEWMEDIA, Emin Media, others):** SEO $500–$30,000/mo (most agencies $1,500–$5,000); PPC management 10–20% of ad spend or $1,500–$5,000/mo flat; content marketing $4,000–$15,000/mo; boutique-agency blended retainer typically $2,500–$7,500/mo.
**Read:** Nivy Next's current Starter ($1,500–$2,500) sits right at the market floor for a US-facing buyer, but well above what India-based competitors anchor at — EZ Rankings' $225/mo entry point in particular is dramatically below Nivy Next's floor, not just modestly under it. This sharpens the core positioning tension from a soft gap into a hard one: price as a US-comparable agency, or compete against a $225/mo published floor. Recommend explicitly deciding which lane before finalizing, rather than splitting the difference by accident — and treating EZ Rankings' pricing as the real low-end anchor a price-sensitive prospect may already have seen.

## 2. IT Services
**India-based direct competitors:** Fusion Business Solutions (FBSPL) and Eminenture — both confirmed via direct site check (2026-08-08) to publish no pricing anywhere on their own websites, consistent with custom-quote positioning. Both have Clutch aggregate profiles showing min. project size $1,000+, avg. hourly rate <$25/hr — the same band as VerveLogic — but this is client-reported aggregate data, not company-published pricing, so treat as directional only.
**US-market anchor (Dataprise, VC3, Corsica, multiple 2026 MSP pricing guides — highly consistent across 8+ sources):** Managed IT is priced **per-user/month**, not flat retainer: $100–$175/user/mo (small business, 10–50 users), $150–$250/user/mo (mid-size, 50–250 users), $250–$400/user/mo for compliance-heavy (HIPAA/CMMC-adjacent) environments.
**Read:** This is the one service line where Nivy Next's current flat-fee tiers ($800–$1,500 / $2,000–$4,500 / $5,000–$10,000+) use a **different pricing model** than the entire market, which prices per-user. A 15-user client at market rate ($100–$175/user) would land at $1,500–$2,625/mo — inside Nivy's current Growth tier, so the totals are roughly compatible, but presenting it as flat tiers instead of per-user pricing will look unfamiliar to buyers who've already gotten 2-3 per-user quotes. Recommend switching this service line's pricing structure to per-user before quoting real prospects.

## 3. AI & Automation
**India-based direct competitors:** No India-based direct competitor publishes automation-specific pricing; this is a newer service category industry-wide, so most pricing data below is US-market agency-agnostic guidance rather than named-competitor quotes.
**US-market anchor (converged across 6 sources — Digital Agency Network, Monetizebot, Layer3Labs, AGIX, CueBytes, Taskip):** Single-workflow pilot build $1,000–$7,500 one-time; multi-workflow build $8,000–$25,000; ongoing monitoring/maintenance retainer $500–$5,000/mo (median $2,800–$7,000/mo for small-mid market); enterprise/multi-agent systems $50,000–$250,000+.
**Read:** Nivy Next's current tiers ($1,000–$2,000 Starter / $2,500–$5,000 Growth / $6,000–$15,000+ Scale) sit comfortably inside the converged market range — this is the best-validated service line of the seven. No change recommended.

## 4. Web Development
**India-based direct competitors:** ColorWhistle Clutch data — min. project size $5,000+, avg. hourly rate $25–$49/hr, real client-reported spend of $3,000 (redesign) to $12,500+ (ongoing). VerveLogic — Clutch min. project size $1,000+, avg. hourly rate <$25/hr, positioned as lower-cost than ColorWhistle.
**US-market anchor (Clutch 2026 survey + 6 pricing guides):** Small business brochure site $3,000–$15,000 (61% of Clutch-surveyed buyers spent under $10,000); boutique agency floor commonly $6,000+; e-commerce/SaaS builds $45,000–$500,000+.
**Read:** Nivy Next's current tiers ($2,500–$5,000 Starter / $6,000–$15,000 Growth / $15,000–$50,000+ Scale) track the US-market data closely and sit meaningfully above both India-based competitors on the low end — consistent with a "US-quality, India-delivered" positioning rather than a race-to-the-bottom one. No change recommended, but worth stress-testing Starter tier against ColorWhistle's $5,000 project floor specifically, since that's the closest apples-to-apples comparison available.

## 5. Virtual Assistant Services
**India-based direct competitors:** Eminenture — no pricing published on eminenture.com (checked directly 2026-08-08, including VA-specific service pages); positioned as hourly/seat-based, custom quote. Clutch aggregate shows a general min. project $1,000+/<$25/hr band (not VA-specific), for directional context only.
**US/global-market anchor (9 named providers, converged data):** Offshore/Philippines-based: Wing Assistant $699–$1,299/mo (general), specialist roles to $1,299/mo. US-based/hybrid: Time Etc $390–$2,160/mo (10–60 hrs); Wishup $1,299–$2,999/mo; MyOutDesk ~$1,988/mo (staff-leasing model); Prialto $1,350–$1,988/mo per "unit" of 55 hrs (team model with backup built in); Belay (US-only, premium) doesn't publish pricing but reviews suggest $1,200–$5,500+/mo.
**Read:** Nivy Next's current tiers ($600–$1,000 Starter / $1,200–$2,000 Growth / $3,000–$7,000 Scale) undercut nearly every named provider above, including the offshore-positioned ones (Wing Assistant's floor is $699, close to Nivy's floor). Given the ICP card's #1 decision criterion is *reliability over cost* (not lowest price), this suggests real room to price up toward $800–$1,200 Starter without losing competitiveness — worth testing rather than assuming the current low anchor is required to win.

## 6. Graphic Design
**Named competitors (not India-specific — this category is dominated by Design-Pickle-model providers globally):** Design Pickle $486.75–$2,049+/mo (3 tiers + custom); Kimp Graphics $599–$699/mo; Flocksy ~$1,200–$1,900+/mo; ManyPixels, Penji, DigitalPolo in the $400–$1,300/mo band depending on model (pooled vs. dedicated designer).
**Read:** Nivy Next's current tiers ($800–$1,500 Starter / $2,000–$4,000 Growth / $4,500–$9,000 Scale) sit above Design Pickle/Kimp's entry tiers but below their premium/dedicated tiers — reasonably positioned as a mid-market option. No change recommended, though the Starter tier could arguably drop toward Kimp's $599–$699 anchor if Nivy Next wants to compete on the crowded low end rather than the mid-market.

## 7. Video Editing
**Named competitors:** Vidchops $495–$995/mo; VideoScale $495–$3,000/mo (clear 3-tier structure: $495–$750 entry / $995–$1,500 mid / $1,899–$3,000 premium); Kimp Video $799–$1,397/mo; Visugu $699–$999/mo.
**Read:** Nivy Next's current tiers ($1,000–$2,000 Starter / $2,500–$5,000 Growth / $5,500–$10,000+ Scale) price meaningfully **above** this entire named-competitor set — VideoScale's premium tier ($1,899–$3,000) is still below Nivy's Growth tier floor. This is the widest gap found in this benchmark. Either Nivy Next's video offering includes materially more (e.g., bundled with graphic design, higher production value, faster turnaround) and that needs to be explicit in the pitch, or this tier is priced meaningfully above market and worth revisiting.

## Cross-Cutting Observations
1. **The 5 India-based direct competitors genuinely don't publish full pricing** (confirmed again on this pass) — Task 10b (anonymized pricing inquiries) is still the only way to get their real numbers. This benchmark uses market-rate data and the two competitors with partial public data as the best available substitute for a starting point.
2. **Video Editing is the one line worth revisiting first** — it's the largest and clearest gap between Nivy Next's hypothesis and observed market pricing.
3. **IT Services should switch pricing models** (flat tier → per-user) before it's quoted to a real prospect — the dollar totals are fine, the structure isn't what buyers expect.
4. **Virtual Assistant may be underpriced** relative to the ICP's own stated decision criteria (reliability over cost) — a case for testing a higher floor, not just accepting the current one.
