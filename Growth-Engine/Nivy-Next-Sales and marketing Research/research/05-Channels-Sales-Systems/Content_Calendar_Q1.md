## Monthly Theme Rotation (per Master Blueprint's Thought-Leadership Calendar Framework)

| Week | Theme | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Industry trend / market observation | Establishes founder POV, timely relevance |
| Week 2 | AI/automation angle | Ties to Pillar 2/4 (speed, proof) and the AI & Automation service line specifically |
| Week 3 | Country-specific regulatory or market shift | Reinforces Pillar 3 (trust) and country-tone positioning |
| Week 4 | Client success story / case-study angle | Reinforces Pillar 4 (proof) — swap for a "hypothetical outcome" framing until real case studies exist |

## Weekly Cadence (LinkedIn — founder's voice, per LinkedIn_Outreach_Sequence_v1.md content-led path)

- 2–3 posts/week: 1 proof-point/case-study angle, 1 POV/industry-trend post, 1 behind-the-scenes/team post
- Daily: 10–15 minutes of genuine comment engagement on target-ICP posts (warms the audience before any connection requests)

## Format Mix (per 2026 content practice — thought leadership resurgence + AI-search structuring)

| Format | Cadence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Long-form pillar article | 1/month per service line rotation | Structure with a clear direct-answer summary near the top for AI answer-engine extraction |
| LinkedIn post (founder voice) | 2–3/week | Mix proof, POV, behind-the-scenes per weekly cadence above |
| Short case-study / outcome post | As real projects close | Replace hypothetical framing with real client language the moment VoC/case studies exist |
| Comparison / "vs." page | 1–2/month | Highest AEO value — buyers and AI systems both use these heavily during evaluation |
| Original data/research piece | 1/quarter | Per 2026 trend data, original research is the highest-differentiation content type — even a small internal survey or aggregated project-outcome data counts |
