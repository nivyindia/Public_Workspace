# Cold Email Sequence v1

**Module:** 05 — Channels & Sales Systems | **Status:** Draft v1 (ready to test — treat every line as a hypothesis per the Email Testing Matrix, Deliverable 7) | **Last updated:** 2026-08-06

Built from the Messaging House pillars, the 7 ICP cards' objections/decision criteria, and the country tone guidelines. Test one variable at a time against a 100+ contact sample before declaring a winner (per Deliverable 7 methodology) — do not scale a sequence past a pilot batch without data.

---

## Sequence Structure (5 touches over 14 days)

| Touch | Day | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 1 | Pattern-interrupt + one specific, credible claim. Ask for a reaction, not a meeting. |
| 2 | Day 4 | Add proof (case study angle) + soft CTA |
| 3 | Day 8 | Reframe around their likely top objection |
| 4 | Day 11 | Short, low-friction "still worth a look?" bump |
| 5 | Day 14 | Breakup email — permission-based close |

**Rule:** every email is under 120 words. SaaS/tech buyers and US/AU buyers in particular penalize long cold emails (per country snapshots).

---

## Master Template — Digital Marketing × SaaS/Tech ICP (USA/UK primary)

**Touch 1 — Subject: "quick one on [Company]'s pipeline"**
> Hi {FirstName} — noticed {Company} is scaling {signal: new hires / funding / product launch}. Most SaaS teams at your stage are running marketing through a solo hire or a generalist agency that's never worked SaaS buying cycles specifically.
>
> We work exclusively with B2B SaaS on pipeline-focused growth (not vanity traffic) — happy to send over what that's looked like for a company your size, no pitch attached. Worth a look?
>
> {Sender}

**Touch 2 — Subject: "the SaaS-specific part"**
> Following up — the short version of what we do differently: we report on trial-to-paid conversion and CAC, not just traffic, because that's what actually gets a SaaS marketing budget renewed.
>
> If useful, I can share a 2-minute breakdown of how we'd approach {Company}'s current funnel. No obligation either way.
>
> {Sender}

**Touch 3 — Subject: "re: offshore/agency concerns"**
> {FirstName} — if part of the hesitation is "we tried an outside team before and it felt slow/disconnected," that's fair, and it's the #1 thing we hear from SaaS founders before working with us.
>
> What we do differently: named point of contact, weekly reporting for the first 90 days, sprint-based delivery — not monthly check-ins. Happy to walk through exactly how that works.
>
> {Sender}

**Touch 4 — Subject: "still worth 15 min?"**
> Circling back — still worth a quick call, or is timing just off right now? Either answer's fine, just don't want to keep cluttering your inbox if it's a no.
>
> {Sender}

**Touch 5 — Subject: "closing the loop"**
> I'll leave it here for now — if pipeline growth becomes a priority again, feel free to reach out any time. Wishing {Company} a strong quarter.
>
> {Sender}

## Variant Angles by ICP (swap into Touch 1 hook + Touch 3 objection reframe)

| ICP | Touch 1 Hook | Touch 3 Objection Reframe |
|---|---|---|
| IT Services × Professional Services | "Client-sensitive data + an unmonitored IT setup is a bigger risk than most firms realize until an audit forces the issue." | Lead with documented security/data-handling policy and compliance statements (Pillar 3) |
| AI & Automation × E-commerce | "Most AI automation projects fail because teams try to automate everything at once. We start with one workflow, prove ROI, then scale." | Reframe "AI project risk" objection — no big-bang commitment, pilot-first |
| Web Development × SaaS/Prof Services | "A site that ships on the date promised, and one you can actually update yourself after launch." | Reframe "agencies disappear after launch" — care-plan retainer, ongoing access |
| Virtual Assistant × Founders | "Most VA hires fail from the freelancer-gamble problem — no backup coverage, no accountability." | Reframe with backup coverage + trial period entry offer |
| Graphic Design × E-commerce/DTC | "Your content calendar probably outpaces your design bandwidth right now." | Reframe consistency/turnaround SLA concerns |
| Video Editing × DTC/SaaS | "Most edited video underperforms because it's polished, not built to hook in the first 3 seconds." | Reframe "quality vs. speed" tradeoff — performance-first editors |

## Country Tone Adjustments (apply on top of the ICP variant)

| Country | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| USA | Keep as-is — direct, numbers-led |
| Canada | Soften CTAs ("worth a look?" → "would it be useful to compare notes?"), add one line referencing CASL-compliant, opt-out-respecting outreach |
| UK | Cut any superlative language; lead Touch 1 with the problem observation, not a claim about Nivy Next; let Touch 2's proof point carry more weight |
| Australia | Shorten further (under 90 words); name the time-zone/async solution explicitly in Touch 2, since it's a known objection there |
| UAE | Add a Touch 1.5 (Day 2) WhatsApp-style follow-up note if a number is available; invite a video call earlier (by Touch 2, not Touch 3) — relationship-first market |

## Compliance Guardrails (hard requirements, not optional)

- **Canada (CASL):** implied consent only applies to existing business relationships; cold B2B outreach needs a clear, easy opt-out in every email and must stop immediately on request.
- **UK/EU (PECR/GDPR):** B2B cold email is generally permitted but must include sender identity, a valid opt-out, and no misleading subject lines.
- **USA (CAN-SPAM):** valid physical address, clear opt-out, honor opt-outs within 10 business days.
- **Australia (Spam Act):** requires consent for commercial electronic messages — B2B cold email should stick to genuinely relevant, individually-addressed outreach and include unsubscribe.
- All of the above are logged per contact in the CRM's Consent/Compliance field (see CRM_Pipeline_Setup_Notes.md).

## What to Track Per Send (feeds Email Testing Matrix, Deliverable 7)

Open rate, reply rate, positive-reply rate, meeting-booked rate — segmented by ICP, country, and which variant (subject/opener/CTA) was used. Do not change more than one variable per 100-contact test batch.
