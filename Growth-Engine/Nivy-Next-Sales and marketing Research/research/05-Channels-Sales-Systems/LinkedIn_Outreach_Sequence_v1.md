# LinkedIn Outreach Sequence v1

**Module:** 05 — Channels & Sales Systems | **Status:** Draft v1 (ready to test against the LinkedIn Testing Matrix, Deliverable 7) | **Last updated:** 2026-08-06

LinkedIn was flagged as the top or near-top preferred channel across every ICP card and every country snapshot — this is the highest-leverage channel to get right first.

---

## Sequence Structure (4 touches over ~10 days)

| Touch | Timing | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Connection request | Get accepted — no pitch in the note |
| 2 | Day 1 after accept | Value-first message, no ask |
| 3 | Day 4 after Touch 2 | Soft CTA tied to a specific proof point |
| 4 | Day 8 after Touch 3 | Direct meeting ask with easy out |

**Rule:** never pitch in the connection note — acceptance rate drops sharply when the note includes a sales ask. Keep the note under 300 characters, reference something specific and real (a post, a role change, a company milestone).

---

## Master Template — Digital Marketing × SaaS/Tech ICP

**Connection Note:**
> Hi {FirstName} — came across {Company}'s recent {signal: funding/launch/post} and wanted to connect. Following your work in the SaaS growth space.

**Touch 2 (Day 1 after accept) — no ask:**
> Thanks for connecting, {FirstName}. Saw {specific detail from their profile/post} — curious how you're thinking about {relevant pain point, e.g. "trial-to-paid conversion"} at {Company}'s stage. Always interesting to compare notes with people building in this space.

**Touch 3 (Day 4) — soft CTA:**
> One thing that's come up a lot in conversations like this: most SaaS teams end up measuring marketing on traffic instead of pipeline impact, and it quietly costs them budget at renewal time. We put together a short breakdown of how we think about it for teams your size — want me to send it over?

**Touch 4 (Day 8) — direct ask, easy out:**
> No pressure either way, but if it'd be useful to trade notes on {Company}'s growth plan for 15 minutes, happy to grab time — otherwise all good, enjoy following your progress here.

## Variant Angles by ICP (swap Touch 2/3 topic)

| ICP | Touch 2 Discussion Angle | Touch 3 Soft CTA Hook |
|---|---|---|
| IT Services × Professional Services | Data security posture as firms scale client volume | Offer to share the vendor security questionnaire answer bank |
| AI & Automation × E-commerce | Where AI automation pilots tend to fail (scope creep) | Offer a single-workflow automation pilot breakdown |
| Web Development × SaaS/Prof Services | Site launches that slip past their promised date | Offer to share a launch-timeline case example |
| Virtual Assistant × Founders | The real cost of a bad freelancer VA hire | Offer the trial-period structure detail |
| Graphic Design × E-commerce/DTC | Content calendar vs. design bandwidth mismatch | Offer to share turnaround-time benchmarks |
| Video Editing × DTC/SaaS | What makes the first 3 seconds of an edit convert | Offer a hook-rate teardown of one of their existing videos (high-personalization, high-response) |

## Country Tone Adjustments

| Country | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| USA | As-is |
| Canada | Slightly warmer, more patient pacing — don't rush to Touch 4's meeting ask; consider adding a 5th "check-in" touch before asking directly |
| UK | Understated — avoid "curious," "excited," or enthusiasm-heavy language; let the substance of Touch 3 carry the message |
| Australia | Faster pacing acceptable — can compress to 3 touches over 6 days; be direct and efficient |
| UAE | Add explicit invite to a video call by Touch 3 rather than Touch 4 — relationship-first market rewards moving to face-to-face faster |

## Content-Led Alternative Path (for founder/brand-authority building)

Separate from 1:1 outreach — supports inbound over time (Marketing Research 2.20, LinkedIn Marketing module):
1. Post 2–3x/week in the founder's voice: one proof-point/case-study post, one point-of-view/industry-trend post, one behind-the-scenes/team post.
2. Engage (comment, don't just like) on 10–15 target-ICP posts per day before sending any connection requests — warms the audience.
3. Track accept rate and reply rate for connections sent to people who've seen ≥1 post vs. cold connections — expect a meaningfully higher accept rate on the warmed group.

## What to Track (feeds LinkedIn Testing Matrix, Deliverable 7)

Connection accept rate, Touch 2→3 reply rate, meeting-booked rate — segmented by ICP, country, and connection-note variant. Test one variable (note wording, Touch 2 angle, or CTA) per 100-contact batch, same discipline as the email sequence.

---

## Compliance Note

LinkedIn outreach isn't covered by CAN-SPAM/CASL/PECR the way email is, but the same courtesy rules apply: honor "not interested," don't message immediately after every profile view, and keep frequency reasonable to avoid platform restrictions.
