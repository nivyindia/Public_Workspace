**Purpose:** Task 44 (produce 3 case studies) is genuinely blocked on real client interviews — no amount of desk research substitutes for that, and this doc doesn't try to. What it does: pre-build the structure, question set, and placement plan so that the moment a real client agrees to a case study, the turnaround from interview to published asset is fast instead of starting from a blank page.

## 1. Which 3 Case Studies to Prioritize First

Pull from the Odoo CRM (`Odoo_CRM_Setup_Guide_v1.md`) "Closed Won" automation, which already auto-queues a case-study interview request at 60 days post-close. Prioritize the first 3 closed-won deals that hit that 60-day mark, weighted toward:
1. A service line with a live pillar article already published (Digital Marketing, IT Services, Web Development have the strongest existing SEO content to pair a case study with)
2. A client willing to be named (named + logo performs far better than anonymized — but have an anonymized fallback ready, see §3)
3. A result with a clean, quantifiable before/after (hours saved, revenue lift, cost reduction) — avoid picking the "best relationship" over the "best provable number"

## 2. Interview Question Bank (for the case-study-specific conversation — separate from the general VoC bank in `VoC_Interview_Plan_and_Question_Bank_v1.md`)

**Before/Situation**
- What was the specific problem or bottleneck before working with Nivy Next? (Get them to describe it concretely, not just "we needed help with X")
- What had you tried before, if anything? Why didn't it work?
- What was the moment or trigger that made you decide to look for a partner?

**Decision**
- What made you choose Nivy Next over the alternatives you considered? (Ties directly to the Top 3 Decision Criteria in the relevant ICP card — ask this to confirm or correct the hypothesis)
- Was there a specific objection or hesitation you had before signing? What resolved it?

**Process**
- Walk me through what the engagement actually looked like — onboarding, first deliverable, ongoing cadence.
- Was there anything that surprised you (positively or negatively) about working with us?

**Result**
- What changed, specifically? Ask for a number if at all possible: hours saved/week, % increase in X, revenue/cost figure, time-to-launch.
- If you had to describe the impact to another founder in one sentence, what would you say? (This is your pull-quote.)

**Permission**
- Can we use your name/company/logo, or would you prefer to stay anonymized?
- Can we quote you directly, and can we send you the draft before it's published?

## 3. Case Study Structure Template

```
# [Client/Industry] — [One-line result headline, e.g., "How [Client] Cut Support Response Time 60% in 6 Weeks"]

**Service Line:** [one of the 7]
**Client:** [Named, or "A [industry] company with [size descriptor]" if anonymized]

## The Challenge
[2-3 sentences, pulled from "Before/Situation" answers — concrete, specific, not generic]

## Why [Client] Chose Nivy Next
[2-3 sentences, pulled from "Decision" answers — this is the section that should tie back to
the relevant ICP card's Top 3 Decision Criteria, confirming or updating that hypothesis]

## What We Did
[3-5 sentences or a short bulleted list, pulled from "Process" answers — specific enough to be
credible, not a generic service description]

## The Result
[The number(s) from "Result" answers, presented prominently — this is the section prospects
skim to first]

> "[Pull quote from the Result section's one-sentence answer]"
> — [Name, Title, Company] *(or "— [Anonymized title], [Industry]" if not named)*

## [CTA tying back to the relevant pillar article and package tier]
```

## 5. Fallback if a Named Case Study Isn't Available in Time

Use an **anonymized "Client Story"** format (same structure above, industry-descriptor instead of name) rather than continuing to run the labeled-illustrative-example placeholder indefinitely — anonymized-but-real outperforms illustrative-and-labeled-as-such for buyer trust, and doesn't require full logo/name permission to ship.
