# Pricing Corridors by Country

**Module:** 04 — Positioning & Offers | **Status:** Draft v1 (working hypothesis) | **Last updated:** 2026-08-06

Base prices in `Package_Pricing_AllServiceLines_v1.md` are USD-anchored to US market norms. Apply the adjustment factors and currency guidance below per country, drawn directly from the country snapshots (Phase 2).

## Adjustment Factors
| Country | Currency | Adjustment vs. USD Base | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| USA | USD | 1.0x (baseline) | Base market — largest, most price-tolerant given high digital-ad-agency benchmarks ($56.9B market) |
| Canada | CAD | 0.9–1.0x | Slightly more price-sensitive per country snapshot; price in CAD, not USD-converted |
| UK | GBP | ~0.85x of USD figure, converted to GBP | UK SME retainers commonly £1,500–£5,000/month — roughly aligns with Growth-tier USD pricing converted at typical FX, confirming no major premium/discount needed beyond currency conversion |
| Australia | AUD | 1.1–1.3x (in AUD terms) | AUD SMB retainers run AUD $2,000–$14,500/month — nominally higher due to currency, but real local-cost comparison (vs. AUD $ local agency rates) still positions Nivy Next as materially cheaper given the documented 370k-worker talent shortage |
| UAE | AED/USD | 1.0x, plus 5% VAT itemized | Dual AED/USD pricing; VAT must be itemized separately per UAE snapshot — do not bury it in the headline price |

## Practical Pricing Guidance
- **Always quote in local currency** (or offer a clear dual-currency option for UAE) — a USD quote to a UK or Australian buyer reads as not-locally-adapted and undermines trust signals built elsewhere.
- **UK and Canada:** avoid appearing to undercut too aggressively — both markets associate rock-bottom pricing with the low-cost freelancer competition explicitly flagged as a weakness in the industry ("65% of agencies face intense competition from freelancers/small firms"). Price at the researched local corridor, not below it.
- **Australia:** lead with the talent-shortage rationale (documented, factual) rather than a pure "we're cheaper" pitch — frames Nivy Next as solving a real capacity problem, not just undercutting.
- **UAE:** always itemize VAT (5%) separately in every quote; never present a VAT-inclusive number as the headline price, since buyers expect to see it broken out.
