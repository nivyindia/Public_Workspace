---

## Bundling Logic (Cross-Sell)
Package pricing is deliberately structured so entry offers are low-friction and priced to convert into a Growth-tier retainer within 60–90 days. Multi-service bundles (e.g., Digital Marketing + Graphic Design + Video Editing as a combined "Growth Marketing" bundle) should carry a **10–15% bundle discount** versus buying tiers separately — this operationalizes the "one partner, not five vendors" messaging pillar and directly counters the competitor weakness (fragmented single-discipline delivery) identified in the SWOT.
