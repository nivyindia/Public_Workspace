# ICP Card — AI & Automation (Primary Segment: E-commerce / DTC Brands)

**Module:** 03 — ICP & Buyer Research | **Status:** Draft v1 | **Last updated:** 2026-08-06

## Firmographics
| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| Company size | 10–100 employees |
| Revenue band | $3M–$50M revenue |
| Industry | Shopify/e-commerce brands, DTC, marketplace sellers (Amazon, Noon in UAE) |
| Growth stage | Post-launch, scaling operations; feeling operational strain from manual processes |
| Geography | Primary: USA, Australia; Secondary: UAE, UK |
| Tech stack signal | Shopify/WooCommerce, Klaviyo, Gorgias/Zendesk, spreadsheet-based ops still common |
| Current solution | Manual customer support, manual inventory/order workflows, or no automation at all |

## Buying Committee
| Role | Concern |
|---|---|
| Founder/CEO (economic buyer) | Operational cost reduction, ability to scale without proportionally scaling headcount |
| Ops/COO (champion, if exists) | Reducing manual workload, error rates, fulfillment/support bottlenecks |
| Customer Support Lead (influencer) | Whether automation will hurt customer experience or genuinely help |

## Top 3 Decision Criteria
1. **Clear, quantified ROI** — this ICP wants to see "this automation saves X hours/week or reduces support tickets by Y%," not abstract AI hype.
2. **Low implementation risk** — fear of a costly, complex AI project that doesn't ship. Buyers strongly prefer a scoped pilot over a big-bang implementation.
3. **Integration with existing tools** — must work with Shopify/Klaviyo/Gorgias stack, not require a rip-and-replace.

## Top 3 Objections (anticipated — validate via VoC interviews)
1. "AI/automation sounds expensive and complicated for a business our size." → Counter with a small, fixed-scope pilot (e.g., automate one workflow: order status inquiries or inventory sync) with a clear price and timeline.
2. "We tried a chatbot/automation tool before and it made customer experience worse." → Counter with a human-in-the-loop design and a defined escalation path for edge cases.
3. "How is this different from just buying a SaaS automation tool ourselves?" → Counter by positioning Nivy Next as the implementation and ongoing optimization partner, not a tool reseller — the value is in correct setup, integration, and iteration, not the software itself.

## Preferred Channel
LinkedIn, referral (DTC/e-commerce founder communities and Slack groups), and marketplaces (Upwork/Clutch) for smaller, lower-commitment first engagements.

## Recommended Entry Offer
A single named-workflow automation pilot (e.g., "automate your top 3 repetitive support ticket types") — small, fast, provable ROI — as the wedge into a broader AI/automation retainer covering more workflows plus reporting/analytics.
