# ICP Card — Accounting-Adjacent / Ops Services (Segment: SMB Finance-Ops & Back-Office Buyers)

**Module:** 03 — ICP & Buyer Research | **Status:** Draft v1 (hypothesis-stage) | **Last updated:** 2026-08-06

## Scope Note
This is not an "accounting firm" ICP — Nivy Next does not offer bookkeeping/tax/audit services. This card covers buyers whose pain sits *adjacent* to accounting: manual invoicing, reconciliation-adjacent admin, reporting roll-ups, and vendor/expense tracking that eats founder or office-manager time. The service fit is a cross-sell of **Virtual Assistant** (execution) + **AI & Automation** (workflow automation of repetitive finance-adjacent admin), not a new 8th service line.

| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| Company size | 5–50 employees |
| Revenue band | $500K–$10M |

## Buying Committee
| Role | Concern |
|---|---|
| Office Manager / Ops Lead (primary day-to-day pain-holder, often the initiator) | Time drain, error risk in manual reconciliation-adjacent work |
| Founder/Owner (budget approval) | Cost vs. hiring a part-time employee; trust with financial-adjacent data |
| Fractional Controller/Bookkeeper (where present, consulted not decision-maker) | Wants clean, consistent inputs handed to them — an ally, not a blocker, if positioned correctly |

## Top 3 Decision Criteria
1. **Data handling trust** — this ICP is more sensitive about data access than other segments because the admin work touches financial records; a clear data-handling policy and access-control approach (Deliverable 8) is a decision factor, not just a compliance checkbox.
2. **Integration fit over raw cost** — buyers want the automation/VA support to plug into their existing QuickBooks/Xero setup, not replace it or require a system migration.
3. **Reduced error rate, not just reduced hours** — the pitch that resonates is fewer reconciliation-adjacent mistakes reaching the (real) accountant, not just "we'll save you time."

## Top 3 Objections (anticipated — validate via VoC interviews)
1. "This sounds like it needs to touch our financial data — how is that secured?" → Counter with the Data Handling Policy and access-control tiering (least-privilege access, no general ledger write access) once Deliverable 8 is finalized.
2. "We already have a bookkeeper — why do we need this too?" → Counter by positioning as *upstream* of the bookkeeper (clean inputs) rather than competing with them; explicitly not an accounting service.
3. "What if the automation breaks something before our books close?" → Counter with a defined human-review checkpoint before anything touches the accounting system directly (VA-in-the-loop, not fully autonomous for this segment specifically).

## Preferred Channel
Referral from existing professional-services and real-estate clients (highest-trust path for anything finance-adjacent), plus direct outreach to office managers/ops leads specifically (not just founders) — a different buying-committee entry point than the other 7 ICP cards.

## Recommended Entry Offer
A scoped "ops audit" (map current manual finance-adjacent workflows, flag the 2–3 highest-error/highest-time-cost ones) as a low-commitment paid diagnostic, converting into an ongoing VA + automation retainer — mirrors the trust-building logic used in the Virtual Assistant ICP's trial-period offer, but calibrated for a more risk-averse buyer.
