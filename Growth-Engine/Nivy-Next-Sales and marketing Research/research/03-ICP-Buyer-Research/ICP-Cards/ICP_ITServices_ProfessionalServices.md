# ICP Card — IT Services (Primary Segment: Professional Services Firms)

**Module:** 03 — ICP & Buyer Research | **Status:** Draft v1 | **Last updated:** 2026-08-06

## Firmographics
| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| Company size | 15–200 employees |
| Revenue band | $2M–$30M |
| Industry | Law firms, accounting/advisory firms, consultancies, agencies, architecture/engineering firms |
| Growth stage | Established, profitable, growth via efficiency rather than fundraising |
| Geography | Primary: UK, USA; Secondary: Canada, Australia |
| Tech stack signal | Legacy on-prem systems, Microsoft 365, practice-management software (Clio, PracticePanther, etc.) |
| Current solution | An informal "IT guy" or a local break-fix IT provider, not a strategic technology partner |

## Buying Committee
| Role | Concern |
|---|---|
| Managing Partner/Owner (economic buyer) | Risk avoidance, uptime, security/compliance, doesn't want surprises |
| Operations Manager (champion) | Day-to-day reliability, staff productivity, ease of getting support |
| IT-literate partner or senior associate (influencer) | Technical credibility, modern tooling vs. legacy systems |
| External accountant/advisor (informal influencer) | Cost predictability, sometimes a referral source |

## Top 3 Decision Criteria
1. **Trust and security posture** — this ICP is the most risk-averse of the three priority segments; a documented security policy and clear compliance answers (see Trust & Compliance checklist) matter more here than almost anywhere else.
2. **Responsiveness and reliability (SLA-backed)** — "my system went down and I couldn't reach anyone" is the single most damaging story a prospect can have heard about a previous vendor.
3. **Local regulatory fluency** — UK GDPR/PECR awareness (UK), PIPEDA/CASL (Canada), APP (Australia) — this ICP explicitly checks for this before signing.

## Top 3 Objections (anticipated — validate via VoC interviews)
1. "You're not local — what happens if something breaks at 2am our time?" → Counter with documented SLA, escalation path, and time-zone coverage plan.
2. "Our data is sensitive (client files, financials) — how do we know it's safe with an offshore provider?" → Counter with the Trust & Compliance toolkit (Deliverable 8): security policy, data handling policy, references.
3. "We've never worked with an outsourced IT partner before — this feels risky." → Counter with a low-risk pilot scope (e.g., one system migration or a defined support-desk trial period) before a full relationship commitment.

## Preferred Channel
Referral (this ICP trusts peer recommendation heavily — law firms refer to law firms, accountants to accountants), LinkedIn, and industry associations/professional bodies.

## Recommended Entry Offer
A fixed-scope IT security/infrastructure audit as the entry point (low-risk, clearly bounded, directly addresses their #1 decision criterion), converting into an ongoing managed-IT or support retainer.
