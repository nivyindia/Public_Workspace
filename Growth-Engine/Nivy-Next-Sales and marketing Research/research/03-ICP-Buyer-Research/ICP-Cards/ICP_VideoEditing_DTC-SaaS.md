# ICP Card — Video Editing (Primary Segment: E-commerce/DTC & SaaS Crossover)

**Module:** 03 — ICP & Buyer Research | **Status:** Draft v1 | **Last updated:** 2026-08-06

## Firmographics
| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| Company size | 5–100 employees |
| Revenue band | $500K–$20M |
| Industry | E-commerce/DTC (UGC ad content, product videos), SaaS (demo/explainer videos, testimonials) |
| Growth stage | Actively running paid social/video ad campaigns, or building out a content/YouTube presence |
| Geography | All 5 target countries; USA highest volume given video-ad-heavy DTC market |
| Tech stack signal | Running Meta/TikTok video ads, has raw footage/UGC piling up unedited, active YouTube or LinkedIn video presence |
| Current solution | Founder editing themselves, an inconsistent freelancer, or simply not producing video despite knowing they should |

## Buying Committee
| Role | Concern |
|---|---|
| Founder/Marketing lead (economic buyer) | Video quality and turnaround speed, especially for time-sensitive ad campaigns |
| Growth/performance marketer (champion, DTC) | Volume of ad variants for testing, hook/edit style that performs |
| Product marketing lead (champion, SaaS) | Clarity and polish for demo/explainer content used in sales |

## Top 3 Decision Criteria
1. **Turnaround speed for ad testing cadence (DTC)** — performance marketers need multiple edited variants quickly to keep testing cycles moving; slow editors directly cap their ability to scale ad spend.
2. **Understanding of platform-native style** — TikTok/Reels-style editing is different from polished SaaS demo editing; buyers want a partner who understands the specific format, not generic video editing.
3. **Raw footage/UGC management** — buyers often have a backlog of unedited raw footage; ability to handle bulk intake and prioritization is a practical, underrated decision factor.

## Top 3 Objections (anticipated — validate via VoC interviews)
1. "Editors we've used before don't understand what makes an ad hook work — the edits look nice but don't convert." → Counter with a portfolio specifically showing performance-oriented ad edits, not just polished brand videos.
2. "We generate a lot of raw footage and it just piles up unedited." → Counter with a defined intake/batch workflow and turnaround SLA for backlog clearing.
3. "SaaS demo videos need to be accurate about the product — will the editor understand what they're showing?" → Counter with a structured briefing/review process involving the product team before final delivery.

## Preferred Channel
Referral within DTC/performance-marketing communities, LinkedIn (for SaaS-focused editing), and marketplaces (Upwork) for initial discovery.

## Recommended Entry Offer
A batch trial (e.g., "5 ad variants from your existing raw footage" for DTC, or "1 demo video" for SaaS) as the low-commitment entry point, converting into an ongoing monthly video-editing retainer sized to content volume.
