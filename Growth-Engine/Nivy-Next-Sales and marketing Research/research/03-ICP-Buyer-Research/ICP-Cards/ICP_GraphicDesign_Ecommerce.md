# ICP Card — Graphic Design (Primary Segment: E-commerce / DTC Brands)

**Module:** 03 — ICP & Buyer Research | **Status:** Draft v1 | **Last updated:** 2026-08-06

## Firmographics
| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| Company size | 3–50 employees |
| Revenue band | $500K–$15M |
| Industry | E-commerce/DTC brands, also professional services (pitch decks, brand refresh) |
| Growth stage | Scaling brand presence, launching new product lines, or rebranding |
| Geography | All 5 target countries; USA and Australia highest volume for e-commerce |
| Tech stack signal | Active on Instagram/TikTok, running paid social ads, uses Canva internally (signal of unmet need for professional design) |
| Current solution | Founder or junior team member doing design in Canva, or an inconsistent rotation of freelancers |

## Buying Committee
| Role | Concern |
|---|---|
| Founder/Brand lead (economic buyer) | Brand consistency, design that actually converts (not just looks nice), speed of turnaround |
| Marketing/social media manager (champion) | Volume and reliability of asset delivery to keep up with content calendar |

## Top 3 Decision Criteria
1. **Speed and volume capacity** — DTC brands need a steady stream of ad creative, social assets, and packaging/product design; a designer who can't keep pace with the content calendar is a dealbreaker.
2. **Brand consistency across a growing asset library** — buyers fear a fragmented look as different freelancers touch different assets over time.
3. **Design that performs, not just looks good** — for ad creative specifically, buyers increasingly ask for data on what design styles convert, not just aesthetic opinion.

## Top 3 Objections (anticipated — validate via VoC interviews)
1. "We've used freelancers before and gotten inconsistent quality/style." → Counter with a dedicated design pod (not a rotating freelancer pool) and a documented brand style guide as a required first deliverable.
2. "Can you really turn around ad creative fast enough for our testing cadence?" → Counter with a defined turnaround SLA (e.g., 48-hour standard turnaround) and a batch-request workflow.
3. "How do you make sure the designs actually convert, not just look nice?" → Counter with a design process that references performance data/benchmarks, and offer A/B creative variants as standard practice.

## Preferred Channel
Instagram/social proof (portfolio visibility matters enormously here), referral within DTC founder communities, and marketplaces (Upwork/Dribbble-adjacent discovery).

## Recommended Entry Offer
A small fixed-scope project (e.g., a 10-asset ad creative batch or a brand style guide) as the entry point, converting into an ongoing monthly design-pod retainer sized to the client's content volume needs.
