# ICP Card — Virtual Assistant Services (Primary Segment: Solo Founders & Professional Services)

**Module:** 03 — ICP & Buyer Research | **Status:** Draft v1 | **Last updated:** 2026-08-06

## Firmographics
| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| Company size | 1–30 employees (often a solo founder or very small team) |
| Revenue band | $200K–$5M |
| Industry | Professional services (real estate agents, consultants, coaches), e-commerce, small agencies |
| Growth stage | Founder is personally the bottleneck — doing admin work that should be delegated |
| Geography | All 5 target countries; UAE and Australia show strong relative demand (talent/cost pressure) |
| Tech stack signal | Gmail/Outlook, basic CRM or none, manual scheduling |
| Current solution | Doing it themselves, or a previous VA relationship that underdelivered (common pain point in this category) |

## Buying Committee
| Role | Concern |
|---|---|
| Founder/Owner (sole decision-maker in most cases) | Reclaiming time, trust that tasks will actually get done correctly without heavy oversight |
| Office manager (in slightly larger firms) | Coordination with the VA, quality control |

## Top 3 Decision Criteria
1. **Reliability over cost** — this ICP has often been burned by a cheap VA who was unreliable; consistency and communication matter more than shaving a few dollars off the rate.
2. **Low management overhead** — buyers want a VA who needs minimal hand-holding, not another thing to manage.
3. **Clear scope of what a VA can/can't do** — ambiguity about capabilities is a common source of disappointment; buyers want a concrete task list, not a vague "assistant for anything" pitch.

## Top 3 Objections (anticipated — validate via VoC interviews)
1. "I tried a VA before from [platform] and it didn't work out — how are you different?" → Counter with a structured onboarding process, defined SOPs, and a trial period with clear success criteria.
2. "I don't have time to train someone." → Counter with Nivy Next handling onboarding/process documentation as part of the service, not leaving it entirely to the client.
3. "What if my VA is unavailable/sick — do I lose support entirely?" → Counter with backup coverage as a standard part of the service (a structural advantage over hiring an individual freelancer directly).

## Preferred Channel
Referral (strongest channel for this ICP — founders trust other founders' VA recommendations), LinkedIn, and marketplaces (Upwork) for initial discovery, converted to a direct relationship.

## Recommended Entry Offer
A low-commitment trial period (e.g., 2 weeks or 20 hours) with a clearly scoped task list, converting into an ongoing part-time or full-time VA retainer — directly addresses the "reliability" and "scope clarity" decision criteria above.
