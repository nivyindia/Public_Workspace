# ICP Card — Digital Marketing (Primary Segment: SaaS / Tech)

**Module:** 03 — ICP & Buyer Research | **Status:** Draft v1 | **Last updated:** 2026-08-06

## Firmographics
| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| Company size | 10–150 employees |
| Revenue band | $1M–$20M ARR |
| Industry | B2B SaaS, vertical software, tech-enabled services |
| Growth stage | Post-seed to Series B; scaling GTM, not pre-product-market-fit |
| Geography | Primary: USA, UK; Secondary: Canada, Australia |
| Tech stack signal | HubSpot/Salesforce, Intercom/Zendesk, Segment/analytics stack in place |
| Current solution | Either no dedicated marketing hire yet, or a solo in-house marketer stretched across too many channels |

## Buying Committee
| Role | Concern |
|---|---|
| CEO/Founder (economic buyer, <50 employees) | Pipeline growth, CAC efficiency, doesn't want to manage an agency relationship closely |
| Head of Marketing/Growth (champion, 50+ employees) | Execution capacity, proof of channel expertise, wants to look good to leadership |
| Head of Sales (influencer) | Lead quality and volume, sales-marketing alignment |
| Finance/Ops (approver, 50+ employees) | ROI justification, contract terms, budget predictability |

## Top 3 Decision Criteria (from country/VoC framework)
1. **Proof of relevant SaaS experience** — case studies with SaaS-specific metrics (CAC, trial-to-paid conversion, pipeline influenced), not generic "we grew traffic" claims.
2. **Speed and responsiveness** — SaaS buyers move fast and expect the same from vendors; slow onboarding or communication is a top objection.
3. **Channel expertise depth over generalist breadth** — even though Nivy Next's overall pitch is breadth, this specific buyer wants to see genuine SEO/content/paid expertise, not a jack-of-all-trades team.

## Top 3 Objections (anticipated — validate via VoC interviews)
1. "How do we know you understand SaaS buying cycles, not just e-commerce/local business marketing?" → Counter with SaaS-specific case studies and language.
2. "We tried an offshore agency before and it didn't work." → Counter with async-friendly process, named point of contact, response-time SLA.
3. "Can you actually move fast, or will this feel like working with a big slow agency?" → Counter with sprint-based engagement model and weekly (not monthly) reporting cadence for the first 90 days.

## Preferred Channel
LinkedIn (founder/marketer audience is highly active there), SEO/content (SaaS buyers research heavily before contacting vendors), and warm referral from other SaaS founders/portfolio networks.

## Recommended Entry Offer
A scoped, fixed-price "Growth Audit" (SEO + paid + content diagnostic) as a low-commitment entry point, converting into a monthly retainer once trust is established — mirrors the pricing-corridor logic already used across country snapshots (SMB/mid-market retainer bands of $2,000–$15,000+/month in the US).
