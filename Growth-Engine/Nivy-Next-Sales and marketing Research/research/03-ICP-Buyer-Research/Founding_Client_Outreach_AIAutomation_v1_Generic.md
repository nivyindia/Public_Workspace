# Founding-Client Outreach — AI & Automation Pilot Cohort (v1, Generic)

**Module:** 03/05 — bridges Founding_Client_and_VoC_Bridge_Plan_v1.md and Cold_Email_Sequence_v1.md | **Status:** Draft v1, generic — slot in names before sending | **Last updated:** 2026-08-06

## Why AI & Automation Leads
Top-scored industry (Industry_Prioritization_Scoring_v1.md), clearest entry offer (single named-workflow pilot, $800–$1,500 one-time per Package_Pricing_AllServiceLines_v1.md), and the ICP's #1 objection — "AI projects are risky/expensive" — is solved directly by the pilot structure itself. Easiest founding-client story to tell.

## The Offer (cohort framing, per Bridge Plan)
"We're running a pilot cohort of 5 e-commerce/DTC founders to automate one repetitive workflow (support tickets, order status, inventory sync) — free or heavily discounted, in exchange for a documented before/after result, a quote, and permission to use it as a case study. X of 5 spots left."

Discount level, exact wording of the discount, and whether it's free vs. paid-discounted — flag for your call before first send (affects margin and how "real" the case study reads to future prospects).

## Sequence — 3 Touches (shorter than the standard 5-touch, cohort urgency compresses it)

**Touch 1 — Day 1 — Subject: "pilot cohort — automating one workflow, free case study slot"**
> Hi {FirstName} — I'm building Nivy Next's AI & Automation practice and running a small pilot cohort: 5 e-commerce/DTC founders, one automated workflow each (support tickets, order status, inventory sync — you pick), done free/heavily discounted in exchange for an honest before/after writeup.
>
> No big commitment — one workflow, not a platform overhaul. {X} of 5 spots left. Worth 15 min to see if there's a fit?
>
> {Sender}

**Touch 2 — Day 4 — Subject: "the pilot in one line"**
> Following up — the short version: we pick your most repetitive, lowest-judgment workflow, automate just that one thing, and measure it (hours saved or tickets deflected). If it doesn't move the number, you've lost nothing but a conversation.
>
> Still {X} of 5 spots open. Want me to send the 2-minute breakdown?
>
> {Sender}

**Touch 3 — Day 8 — Subject: "closing the cohort soon"**
> {FirstName} — filling the last couple of pilot spots this week. If the timing's right, happy to jump on a quick call; if not, no worries at all — I'll leave the door open for later.
>
> {Sender}

## Notes
- Keeps the existing sequence's core rules: under 120 words/email, one clear ask per touch, permission-based close.
- Swaps the standard 5-touch structure for 3 because cohort/scarcity framing loses credibility if it drags past ~10 days.
- Once you have your network list, Touch 1's opener changes for warm contacts ("Hi {FirstName} — since you run {Company}...") vs. cold ("noticed {Company} is scaling...", pulling the signal-based hook from Cold_Email_Sequence_v1.md's AI & Automation variant).
