## Interview Format
20–30 minutes, video call, recorded with explicit consent stated at the start ("Do you mind if I record this so I can focus on the conversation instead of note-taking?"). Offer a small incentive if useful in your market (e.g., a $25 gift card or a free hour of the relevant service) — increases response rate, especially for lost prospects and churned clients.

## Question Bank by Segment

### Current Clients
1. Walk me through what led you to start looking for a [service] partner in the first place.
2. What made you choose us over other options you considered?
3. What almost stopped you from signing with us?
4. What result matters most to you from this relationship?
5. Has anything surprised you — positively or negatively — since we started working together?
6. If a friend or colleague asked you why they should work with us, what would you say?
7. What would make you a client for another 3 years?

### Lost Prospects (Win/Loss)
1. What vendor or approach did you end up choosing instead of us?
2. What was the deciding factor in that choice?
3. Was there a concern about working with us that we never fully resolved?
4. Looking back, is there anything we could have said or shown you that would have changed your decision?
5. How would you describe the ideal vendor for this need, in your own words?

### Churned Clients
1. What was the original trigger for deciding to end the relationship?
2. Was there a specific moment or event, or was it more of a gradual drift?
3. What was missing — in delivery, communication, or results?
4. Is there anything that would make you consider working together again in the future?

### Non-Customer ICP-Fit Prospects (Cold Research Interviews)
1. How are you currently handling [service area] today?
2. What's the most frustrating part of that current approach?
3. Have you ever looked into outsourcing this? What stopped you, if anything?
4. What would "great" look like if you were to hand this off to a partner?
5. What would make you trust an outsourced partner enough to hand this over?

## Data Coding Framework
Tag every interview transcript with:
- **Decision driver:** Price / Trust / Speed / Expertise / Compliance
- **Objection type:** Price / Trust / Timing / Authority / Competitor / Other
- **Channel of discovery:** Referral / LinkedIn / SEO / Marketplace / Other
- **Country:** USA / Canada / UK / Australia / UAE
- **Service line:** (one of the 7)
- **Sentiment:** Positive / Neutral / Negative

Store each coded transcript as `YYYYMMDD_VoC_[ClientName-or-Anon]_[Segment].md` in this folder.

## Synthesis Process (run after every 5+ new interviews)
1. Aggregate coded tags into a ranked list of top 5 decision drivers and top 5 objections per ICP.
2. Compare against the existing hypothesis-stage ICP cards — confirm, correct, or flag conflicting evidence.
3. Pull 3–5 strong verbatim phrases (in the client's own words) per ICP for use in messaging (Deliverable 2.20) — store in a running `Messaging_Language_Bank.md`.
4. Produce a dated synthesis file: `YYYYMMDD_VoC_Synthesis_v1.md`, and log any resulting ICP card updates in the Decision Log.
