## 🚪 PART 1 — Entry Point Packages (What to Lead With)

Do not open every conversation with your full package list. Lead with one solution to one problem. Match the market.

### 🇺🇸 USA Market — Lead With These

| Lead Type | Best Entry Package | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local service business (roofing, HVAC, plumbing) | 📦 Basic Ads Setup + Local SEO Starter | They want local visibility and inbound calls fast |
| E-commerce brand | 📦 Shopify Setup or Basic Website Starter | Gets them live — upsell comes once store is running |
| Agency / consultant | 📦 SEO Starter or Social Media Starter | They already understand marketing — sell the outcome |
| SaaS / tech startup | 📦 Business Email Setup + Basic Website | Foundational needs — easy yes, fast upsell runway |
| Brick-and-mortar retail | 📦 Social Media Starter + Graphic Design Starter | Visual-first business — social proof and presence |

> ⚠️ USA businesses expect agency-quality language. Lead with outcomes, not features. Say "more booked appointments" not "we run ads".
> 

### 🌍 International Market (UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, India) — Lead With These

| Lead Type | Best Entry Package | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small business / startup | 📦 Basic Website Starter or SEO Starter | Cost-effective entry — show ROI, then grow the account |
| Service business (coaching, consulting) | 📦 Social Media Starter or Video Editing Starter | Content builds credibility — fast visible result |
| E-commerce | 📦 Shopify Setup + Basic Ads Setup | Full launch setup — easy bundle pitch |
| Professional services (accountant, lawyer) | 📦 Business Email Setup + Basic Website Starter | Legitimacy and presence — trusted entry point |

> ⚠️ International leads are often more price-sensitive. Lead with ROI and results. Use cost comparisons ("this costs less than one missed client").

## 📈 PART 2 — The Upsell Ladder (Level 1 → 2 → 3 → 4)

Every Nivy package is part of a natural upgrade path. Your job is to get the lead onto the ladder — then help them climb it.

```
Level 1 (Entry) → Level 2 (Growth) → Level 3 (System) → Level 4 (AI/Enterprise)
```

### How to think about each level:

| Level | What the Client Is Buying | When to Upsell to Next Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 — Entry | "Get me started" — foundational setup | After 4–8 weeks when they're live and want more |
| Level 2 — Growth | "Help me scale" — ongoing management & growth | After 2–3 months when results are showing |
| Level 3 — System | "Build my machine" — full integrated systems | When they ask about multiple channels at once |
| Level 4 — AI/Enterprise | "Automate everything" — AI-powered growth | When they're revenue-stable and want to scale fast |

### Example upsell paths:

**SEO Path:**

📦 SEO Starter (Level 1) → 📦 SEO Growth Package (Level 2) → Growth Engine System (Level 3)

**Social Media Path:**

📦 Social Media Starter (Level 1) → 📦 Social Media Growth System (Level 2) → Authority System (Level 3)

**Ads Path:**

📦 Basic Ads Setup (Level 1) → 📦 Google Ads PPC Growth System (Level 2) → Multi-Channel Ads System (Level 3)

**Website Path:**

📦 Basic Website Starter (Level 1) → Website Maintenance Growth (Level 2) → Conversion System (Level 3)

> 💡 **Upsell trigger phrase:** *"A lot of our clients who started with [Level 1 package] moved to [Level 2] around month 2 once they started seeing traction. It's worth knowing about so you're ready."*

## 🔗 PART 3 — Cross-Sell Combos (Packages That Go Together)

Cross-selling is not random. Certain packages solve complementary problems. When a lead buys one, the other becomes an easy yes.

### 🔥 Top Cross-Sell Combos

| Package A | Natural Add-On (Package B) | Why They Go Together |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 📦 Basic Website Starter | 📦 SEO Starter | A website with no SEO gets no traffic |
| 📦 Social Media Starter | 📦 Graphic Design Starter | Social needs visuals — they always run together |
| 📦 Social Media Starter | 📦 Video Editing Starter | Reels and video content drive social growth |
| 📦 Basic Ads Setup | 📦 Basic Website Starter | You can't run ads to nothing — landing page is essential |
| 📦 SEO Starter | 📦 Graphic Design Starter | Blog + branded visuals = full content marketing |
| 📦 Shopify Setup | 📦 Basic Ads Setup | Store needs traffic — ads are the natural next step |
| 📦 Business Email Setup | 📦 Basic Website Starter | Professionalism combo — domain, email, website as one pitch |

### 🧠 How to pitch a combo:

> *"Most of our clients doing [Package A] also get [Package B] at the same time — they go hand in hand. Want me to show you what a combined package would look like?"*
> 

> *"[Package A] gets you [outcome]. But here's the thing — without [Package B], you'd be leaving [specific gap] open. We can bundle both and keep the cost efficient."*
>

## 🎯 PART 4 — Conversation Triggers (Read the Signal, Know the Pitch)

Leads give you signals in every message. Learn to read them — each signal maps to a package.

| What They Say | What They Actually Need | Package to Mention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "We don't rank on Google" | Organic search visibility | 📦 SEO Starter → SEO Growth Package |
| "Our website looks outdated" | Modern web presence | 📦 Basic Website Starter |
| "We're not getting enough leads" | Lead generation system | 📦 Basic Ads Setup → Lead Generation System (L3) |
| "We're posting on social but getting no engagement" | Better content strategy | 📦 Social Media Starter → Social Media Growth System |
| "We tried ads but it didn't work" | Proper ads management | 📦 Google Ads PPC Growth System (Level 2) |
| "We need to look more professional" | Brand presence | 📦 Business Email Setup + Basic Website + Graphic Design |
| "We sell online but our store isn't converting" | CRO + store optimisation | 📦 Shopify Setup or Conversion System (L3) |
| "I want to scale my business" | Full growth system | Level 2 or Level 3 packages — ask about current setup first |
| "We want to automate our marketing" | AI/automation solutions | Level 4 — AI Marketing / Sales System |
| "Our competitors are doing [X]" | Competitive positioning | Whatever [X] is — match and exceed it |

> 💡 **Rule:** Never recommend a package before you understand the lead's current situation. Ask 1–2 diagnostic questions first. The trigger above is your cue to ask — not to pitch immediately.

## 🌍 PART 5 — Market-Specific Guidance

### 🇺🇸 USA — High-Ticket Framing

- Lead with ROI, speed, and outcomes
- USA businesses are used to paying for quality — don't undersell
- Avoid saying "cheap" or "affordable" — say "efficient" or "results-based"
- Common objection: *"We already have an agency"* → "We work alongside agencies or replace what isn't working — 15 minutes tells you which."
- Reference the USA Package Blueprint for exact positioning

### 🌍 International — Value & ROI Framing

- Leads are more price-aware — anchor on what they lose by not acting
- Use specific numbers: *"One new client from this pays for 3 months of the package"*
- Build trust before pitching — 2–3 messages of value before the offer
- Common objection: *"We don't have budget right now"* → "That's exactly why clients come to us — we start where you are and grow from there."

## 🔑 The 3 Rules of Selling Nivy Packages

1. **Understand before pitching** — ask one good question before you mention a package name
2. **Always move toward a call** — packages are explained on calls, not in DMs
3. **Ladder thinking** — every client you win at Level 1 is a Level 2 upsell opportunity in 60 days
