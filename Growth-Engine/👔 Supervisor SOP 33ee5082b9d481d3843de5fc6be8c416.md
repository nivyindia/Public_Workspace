# 👔 Supervisor SOP

<aside>
📋

For: Supervisors managing a team of 5–10 VAs. Read this fully before your first day as a supervisor.

</aside>

## Your role in one sentence

You are not here to just track numbers — you are here to push performance, solve blockers fast, and ensure every VA hits their targets every day.

---

## Daily schedule

| Time | Task | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 AM | Morning brief | Send targets to all VAs in team group. Format: "Today's targets: [Leads] leads / [Messages] messages / [Follow-ups] follow-ups. Reports due 7 PM. Any blockers — reply here." |
| 10:20 AM | Confirm everyone is online | Chase anyone who hasn't responded within 20 minutes |
| 1:00 PM | Midday checkpoint | Ask each VA for their Report 1 numbers. Flag anyone below 40% of target. |
| 1:15 PM | Spot-check messages | Review 5 messages from each VA. Flag script violations immediately. |
| 5:00 PM | Push underperformers | Personally message any VA below 60% and find out the blocker |
| 7:00 PM | Submit team report | See format below |

## Coaching framework — when a VA is underperforming

1. Identify which number is low — leads, messages, replies, or calls?
2. Diagnose the cause — skill issue (doesn't know how) or effort issue (not doing the work)?
3. Skill issue → Review their last 5 messages together. Give specific feedback line by line.
4. Effort issue → 1-on-1 message: "Your numbers today are [X]. Target is [Y]. What's blocking you?"
5. Follow up next day — check if the specific number improved.

> Rule: Never coach in public. Always address performance issues 1-on-1 in a private message.
>

## Quality control — daily message spot-check

Check 5 messages per VA every day. Flag any of these:

| Issue | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Script not followed (custom message not approved yet) | Send correct script, note in mistake log |
| Wrong niche targeted | Remove lead from CRM, correct targeting |
| Pitching in the connection request | Warn VA — this gets accounts flagged |
| Sending pricing over DM | Immediate correction — redirect to call |
| Fake or duplicate lead in CRM | Note in mistake log, formal warning if repeated |

---

## Escalation protocol

Escalate to your VA Manager only when:

- A VA is unresponsive for 2+ days with no explanation
- Reports appear to have fake or inflated numbers
- A technical or access issue you cannot resolve within 24 hours
- A conduct issue (inappropriate messaging, complaints from leads)

**Escalation message format:**

```
VA Name:
Issue:
What I already tried:
What I need from you:
```

## KPI thresholds — what to do at each level

| VA's daily score | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 80%+ | Acknowledge publicly in group. Note for weekly leaderboard. |
| 65–79% | No action needed. Monitor. |
| 40–64% | Private check-in. Identify blocker. Review tomorrow. |
| Below 40% | Same-day escalation conversation. If 3 days in a row → formal warning to manager. |
| No report submitted | Mark absent. Chase before 9 PM. If no response — log as absent. |

## Promotion and removal decisions

**Promote a VA to senior or track for leadership when:**

- Consistent 80%+ score for 3+ weeks
- Strong reply rate and booking rate
- Helps other VAs without being asked

**Initiate removal process when:**

- Below 40% for 5+ consecutive days with no valid reason
- Three formal warnings logged
- Fake data confirmed in CRM or reports
