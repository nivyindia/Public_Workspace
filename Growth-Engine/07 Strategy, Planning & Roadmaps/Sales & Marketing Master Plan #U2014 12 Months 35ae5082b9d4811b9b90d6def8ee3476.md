# 📡 Channel Strategy {#channels}

## Outbound (Months 1–6: primary driver)

- LinkedIn outreach: 100 connections/week per VA
- Cold email (Apollo): 200 emails/week per VA
- WhatsApp (India/UAE): 50 messages/week
- Target: 5 meetings/week from Month 1

## Inbound (Months 3–12: growing contribution)

- Blog: 8 posts/month → SEO traffic builds over 3–6 months
- LinkedIn content: 5 posts/week → inbound DMs
- Email newsletter: weekly → nurture existing leads
- Lead magnets: 2 per quarter → build email list
- Target: 5 inbound leads/month by Month 6, 20+/month by Month 12

## Referral (Months 4–12)

- 90-day client referral ask: systematic
- Partner network: 5 active referral partners by Month 6
- Target: 3+ referral leads/month by Month 8

# 🗓️ Monthly Execution Plan {#monthly}

## Months 1–2: Build & Launch

- Hire and train 3 VAs
- Launch LinkedIn + cold email outreach
- Website live with booking page
- First 2 clients signed
- HubSpot CRM configured

## Months 3–4: Scale Outbound

- VA team to 5
- Weekly meeting targets: 5+/week
- Blog content publishing begins
- LinkedIn personal brand posting daily
- First case study published

## Months 5–6: Inbound Begins Working

- 8+ blog posts/month live
- Email list at 300+
- 3+ inbound leads/month
- Referral program launched
- 20+ active clients

## Months 7–12: Compound Growth

- Inbound ≥ outbound in lead volume
- VA team at 10+
- Partner network generating referrals
- MRR compounding monthly
- Full sales automation live
