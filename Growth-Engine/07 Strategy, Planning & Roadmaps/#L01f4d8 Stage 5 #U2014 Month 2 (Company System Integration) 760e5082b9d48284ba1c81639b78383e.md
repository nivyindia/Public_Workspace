# 💰 STEP 2 — SALARY + INCENTIVE SYSTEM

> 💡 **Quick note for VAs reaching Stage 5:** Your base salary is $300/month and commission starts from your first closed deal. This was introduced briefly in your onboarding — Stage 5 is where you see the full structure because this is when performance-based pay becomes most relevant. If you weren’t told about salary before starting, speak to your supervisor.
> 

## 💵 BASE SALARY

> Every VA on the team receives a fixed base salary, paid monthly.
> 

Base Salary: **$300 USD per month**

- Paid at end of each calendar month
- Requires minimum attendance: daily report submitted 22+ days out of 30
- Missing 5+ reports in a month = base salary forfeited for that month
- No report = absent. No exceptions.

---

## 🎯 PERFORMANCE INCENTIVES — PER DEAL CLOSED

> Every time a call you booked converts into a paying client, you earn a commission on top of base salary.
> 

| Deals Closed (Monthly) | Commission Per Deal | Monthly Bonus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 deals | $15 per deal | Up to $30 |
| 3–5 deals | $20 per deal | Up to $100 |
| 6–10 deals | $25 per deal | Up to $250 |
| 11+ deals | $30 per deal | $330+ |

> A "deal closed" = a call you booked that the closer converts into a signed, paying client. Confirmed by supervisor. CRM status must show: Booked → Call → Closed.
> 

---

## 🌟 WEEKLY REWARDS — Small Wins, Big Motivation

> Every week, the top-performing VAs get recognised and rewarded. These reset every Monday.
> 

### 🥇 Weekly Top Performer (1 VA per week)

- Criteria: Highest calls booked + reply rate combined
- Reward: **Food voucher or meal treat** (equivalent to ~$8–10 USD)
- Announced every Saturday in the team group

### 🥈 Weekly Most Improved (1 VA per week)

- Criteria: Biggest % improvement vs their own last week
- Reward: **Movie ticket or entertainment voucher** (~$5–8 USD)
- For new VAs especially — rewards effort, not just results

### 🟢 Perfect Attendance Bonus (Any VA)

- Criteria: Report submitted every single day Mon–Sat
- Reward: **$10 USD added to that month's salary**
- Tracked monthly, paid with base salary

---

## 💼 TEAM LEADER POSITION — How to Earn It

> Team Leader is the highest position a VA can reach. It comes with increased pay, authority, and responsibility.
> 

### Entry Requirements (ALL must be met):

- Minimum 2 months at Stage 5 or above
- Top 20% of team by calls booked for 2+ consecutive months
- 60+ total calls booked (cumulative)
- Zero disciplinary issues (no data violations, no fake leads)
- Supervisor nomination required — you cannot self-apply

### Team Leader Benefits

**Salary:** $450 USD/month base (vs $300 VA base) | **Commission:** Same per-deal structure as VAs | **Team Bonus:** +$5 USD per deal closed by any VA you supervise | **Title:** Team Leader — Nivy Acquisition Team | **Certificate:** Team Leader Certificate issued on appointment | **Authority:** Supervise up to 8–10 VAs, run team calls, flag issues to manager

### Team Leader Responsibilities:

- Daily check-in with each VA on your team
- Flag non-performing VAs to the manager within 48 hours
- Review and validate team reports each week
- Lead a 30-minute team call every Monday

> ⚠️ Team Leader is earned by performance and trust — not by time served or seniority. The fastest route is consistent results + zero disciplinary issues.
>
