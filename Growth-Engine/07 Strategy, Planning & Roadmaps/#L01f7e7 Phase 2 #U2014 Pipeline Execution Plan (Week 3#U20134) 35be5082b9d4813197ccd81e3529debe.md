## 🗓️ WEEK 3, DAY 1–2 — Discovery Call Mastery

### Pre-Call Prep (15 minutes before every call)

- [ ]  Look up client’s business: Instagram, Google Maps, website
- [ ]  Write down **3 specific things you noticed that could be improved**
- [ ]  Open Google Doc: note their name, business, your observations, what service fits them
- [ ]  Have your Calendly confirmation email ready
- [ ]  Be on the call **5 minutes early**

### The 30-Minute Discovery Call Script (follow exactly)

**Minutes 0–5 — Warm Up:**

> *"Great to meet you! Tell me quickly — what does [business name] do and who are your best customers?"*
> 

Listen. Let them talk. Take notes.

**Minutes 5–15 — Diagnose Their Problem:**

> - *"What’s your biggest challenge with getting clients right now?"*
> 

> - *"What have you tried before for marketing? What worked, what didn’t?"*
> 

> - *"How are you currently getting most of your leads?"*
> 

> - *"If I could solve one marketing problem for you, what would it be?"*
> 

Listen more than you talk. Take notes on every answer.

**Minutes 15–22 — Present Your Thinking:**

> *"Based on what you’ve told me, I can see 2–3 specific things we could do to help you get more [leads/customers/revenue]. Here’s what I’d recommend..."*
> 

Tell them SPECIFICALLY what you’d do for THEIR business. Not generic services.

**Minutes 22–27 — Handle the Obvious Questions:**

> **"How much does it cost?"** → *"It depends on the exact scope, but our packages start from ₹15,000/month. I’ll send you a proposal today with 2–3 options tailored to your situation."*
> 

> **"How long before I see results?"** → *"Honestly, most clients see early indicators in 30–45 days, meaningful results by Month 2–3. I’ll be completely transparent throughout."*
> 

> **"I’ve tried agencies before and it didn’t work."** → *"I hear that a lot. Can I ask — what specifically didn’t work? Because usually it’s one of three things: wrong targeting, wrong messaging, or no follow-up system. I want to make sure we don’t repeat it."*
> 

> **"I need to think about it."** → *"Of course — this is a real decision. Can I ask what specifically you need to think about? If it’s budget, I can adjust the scope. If it’s results certainty, I can share similar businesses we’ve helped. I just want to make sure you have everything you need."*
> 

> **"You’re too expensive."** → *"I completely understand. What budget are you working with? I’d rather find a scope that fits than lose you over this."*
> 

**Minutes 27–30 — Close the Call:**

> *"I’ll send your proposal today with 3 package options — you choose what makes sense for your budget. Is there anything specific you want me to include?"*
> 

### Immediately After Every Call (within 2 hours)

## 🗓️ WEEK 3, DAY 5–7 — The Follow-Up System

> **Most agencies lose clients because they follow up once and stop. This sequence is what separates closers from people who "tried".**
> 

### 5-Touch Follow-Up Sequence (for EVERY proposal sent)

| Touch | Day | Channel | Message |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Day 0 (proposal sent) | Email + WhatsApp | Proposal + warm message |
| 2 | Day 2 | WhatsApp | *"Hi [Name], just checking you received the proposal okay? Happy to answer anything!"* |
| 3 | Day 5 | Email | *"Hi [Name], following up on the proposal. Have you had a chance to look? Happy to discuss."* |
| 4 | Day 8 | WhatsApp | *"Hi [Name], one last check-in. Even if you’re not ready now, happy to clarify anything."* |
| 5 | Day 14 | Email | *"Hi [Name], leaving the proposal open. Whenever you’re ready to grow your marketing, we’re here."* |

**Log every follow-up in HubSpot as a Note** with date and channel.

## 🗓️ WEEK 4 — Close Your First Client

### When Someone Says Yes:

**Step 1 — Reply immediately:**

> *"That’s great news! Let’s get started. I’ll send the agreement and invoice today."*
> 

**Step 2 — Create the Service Agreement in Google Docs:**

- [ ]  Scope of work (exactly what you deliver each month)
- [ ]  Timeline (start date, key dates)
- [ ]  Payment terms (monthly in advance, due on 1st)
- [ ]  Notice period (30 days written notice to cancel)
- [ ]  Confidentiality clause
- Keep it **1–2 pages max** — simple and clear
- Or send via **PandaDoc** with built-in e-signature

**Step 3 — Send the invoice via Razorpay (or GPay):**

- Include: your business name, client name, service, amount, due date

**Step 4 — When payment clears, send WhatsApp:**

> *"Hi [Name]! Payment received — welcome to Nivy Digital! We’re excited to start working on your growth. I’ll send a quick onboarding questionnaire today. Your kickoff call will be [date/time]. We’re going to do great things together!"*
> 

**Step 5 — Set up PandaDoc notification:**

- [ ]  Turn on document tracking — you get notified when they open the proposal
- [ ]  When they open but don’t sign within 24hrs → WhatsApp: *"Hi [Name], just saw you reviewed the proposal! Any questions I can answer?"*

**Step 6 — Set up client onboarding (see Phase 3 detail):**

- [ ]  Send onboarding Tally form (new form to create — see below)
- [ ]  Create Notion client portal (Phase 3 builds full automation for this)
- [ ]  Create Google Drive folder: Client Name → Campaigns → Assets → Reports
- [ ]  Add client to HubSpot **Active Clients** pipeline

### Client Onboarding Tally Form (create this now):

- [ ]  Go to Tally → New Form → Name: **"Nivy Digital — Client Onboarding"**
- [ ]  Questions:
    1. Company name + website
    2. Describe your target customer in detail
    3. Your 3 biggest competitors
    4. Best-selling product or service
    5. Monthly revenue target
    6. Current marketing setup (what’s running now)
    7. Google Analytics access (email to share with)
    8. Facebook Business Manager access (email to share with)
    9. Instagram page admin access (email to share with)
    10. Who is our main point of contact (name + WhatsApp)
- [ ]  Publish → send to client as first step of onboarding
- [ ]  ✅ Onboarding form ready

### Referral Ask at Closing (do this every time):

In your closing/welcome email add:

> *"P.S. If you know any other business owner who could use better marketing, I’d love an introduction. We offer [1 month free / ₹5,000] for every client who signs with us on your recommendation."*
>
