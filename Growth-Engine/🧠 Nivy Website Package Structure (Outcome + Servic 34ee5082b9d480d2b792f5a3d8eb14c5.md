# 🧠 Nivy Website Package Structure (Outcome + Services Model)

# 🎯 Core Concept

The website is structured in a **2-layer system**:

### 🟢 Layer 1 — Public View (Outcome-Based)

Used for homepage and main navigation.

Clients see simple goals, not technical services.

### 🔵 Layer 2 — Detailed View (Service-Based)\Used inside each package page for users who want specifics.

---

# 🚀 LAYER 1 — WEBSITE FRONT (PUBLIC VIEW)

## 🟢 Main Packages Shown on Homepage

### 1. Get Online & Look Professional

👉 For businesses starting digital presence

**Result:** Professional online identity

---

### 2. Get Leads Every Month

👉 For businesses wanting customers

**Result:** Consistent monthly inquiries

---

### 3. Get Sales System

👉 For businesses struggling with conversions

**Result:** Leads turn into paying customers

---

### 4. Automate Your Business

👉 For scaling businesses

**Result:** Business runs with automation systems
