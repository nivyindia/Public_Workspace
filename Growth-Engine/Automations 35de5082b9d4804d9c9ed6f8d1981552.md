# Automations

International markets use many layers of sales automation — not just chatbots. Large companies build entire automated revenue systems where lead generation, qualification, nurturing, follow-ups, closing, onboarding, upselling, and retention are partially automated.

Here are the major categories of sales automation used globally:

---
