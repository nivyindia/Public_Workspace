## 🔵 TRACK 1 — LINKEDIN SPECIALIST

> **Best for:** VAs who are strong at research, professional communication, and building relationships with business owners.
> 

### 🎯 Role Definition

You own LinkedIn end-to-end. Your job is to build a consistent, high-quality pipeline of engaged prospects using LinkedIn as the primary channel.

### ⎡ Daily Work

**LinkedIn Daily Work:**

- Deep lead research on LinkedIn (filtered by niche, role, company size, activity)
- Send 15–20 personalised connection requests per day
- Follow up with all pending connections (Day 2–3 after connect)
- Handle all LinkedIn conversations — move every reply toward a call
- Identify VIP leads (decision-makers, active posters, warm signals)
- Update CRM after every single action

### 📊 Daily Targets

| Task | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Connection requests sent | 15–20 |
| First messages sent | 20–25 |
| Follow-ups sent | 15–20 |
| Conversations handled | All open threads same day |
| Calls booked (weekly) | 5–8 |

### 📝 LinkedIn Specialist Scripts

**Optimised Connection Request (Niche-specific)**

```
Hi [NAME], I work with [niche] businesses helping them get more qualified leads — saw your profile and wanted to connect.
```

**VIP Lead First Message (After Connect)**

```
Thanks for connecting [NAME].

I noticed you’re running [type of business] — are you currently focused on growing your client base or is pipeline pretty steady right now?
```

**Re-engagement Message (Cold connection, no reply in 7+ days)**

```
Hey [NAME], circling back — I didn’t want to just sit in your connections without saying hi.

We help [niche] businesses get more booked calls through outreach. Worth a quick 10-minute chat?
```

### 📈 Success KPIs

- Reply Rate: 15%+
- Connection acceptance rate: 40%+
- Calls booked from LinkedIn per week: 5+

### ✅ LinkedIn Specialist Checklist (Daily)

- [ ]  15–20 connection requests sent
- [ ]  All accepted connections messaged within 24 hours
- [ ]  All open conversations checked and replied to
- [ ]  CRM updated after every action
- [ ]  No lead left waiting more than 24 hours

## 📧 TRACK 2 — EMAIL + COLD OUTREACH SPECIALIST

> **Best for:** VAs who are structured, disciplined, and good at writing clear, direct, professional messages. Works well for B2B niches.
> 

### 🎯 Role Definition

You own cold email and multi-step outreach sequences. Your job is to build, run, and optimize email sequences that generate replies and booked calls at scale.

### ⎡ Daily Work

**Email Daily Work:**

- Source and verify 30–50 email leads per day (Apollo, LinkedIn, manual search)
- Verify all emails before sending (use [Hunter.io](http://Hunter.io) or [Snov.io](http://Snov.io))
- Send 30–50 cold emails per day using approved sequences
- Follow up on Day 3 and Day 7 for all non-replies
- Handle all email replies — qualify and move toward call
- Track open rates and reply rates per sequence
- Update CRM after every email sent or replied

### 📊 Daily Targets

| Task | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Emails verified and sent | 30–50 |
| Follow-up emails sent | 20–30 |
| Replies handled (same day) | 100% |
| Calls booked (weekly) | 4–6 |
| Open rate target | 40%+ |
| Reply rate target | 8–15% |

### 📝 Cold Email Specialist Scripts

**Email 1 — Cold Opener**

```
Subject: Quick question for [NAME]

Hi [NAME],

I help [niche] businesses get more qualified leads and booked calls without spending more on ads.

Would it make sense to connect for 15 minutes this week to see if we can do the same for [Company]?

[Your Name]
Business Development | Nivy
```

**Email 2 — Follow-Up (Day 3, no reply)**

```
Subject: Re: Quick question for [NAME]

Hi [NAME], just following up on my note from a few days ago.

I know your inbox is busy — I’ll keep it short: we help [niche] businesses get 3–5 extra qualified calls per week through targeted outreach.

Worth a quick call this week?

[Your Name]
```

**Email 3 — Final Follow-Up (Day 7)**

```
Subject: Last one from me

Hi [NAME], last follow-up — I don’t want to clutter your inbox.

If the timing isn’t right, no worries at all. Feel free to reach out whenever it makes sense.

[Your Name]
```

**Reply Handler — “Tell Me More”**

```
Great to hear back!

The easiest way to walk you through it is a quick 15-minute call — I can show you exactly what we’d do for [Company].

Are you free [Day Option 1] or [Day Option 2]?
```

### 📈 Success KPIs

- Open rate: 40%+
- Reply rate: 8–15%
- Calls booked per week: 4–6
- Bounce rate: under 5% (email hygiene)

### ✅ Email Specialist Checklist (Daily)

- [ ]  All emails verified before sending
- [ ]  Sending volume within daily safe limit (max 50 cold emails)
- [ ]  All replies handled same day
- [ ]  Follow-up sequence running on correct days
- [ ]  CRM updated after every send and reply

## 📱 TRACK 3 — WHATSAPP + COMMUNITY SPECIALIST

> **Best for:** VAs who are natural conversationalists, quick responders, and comfortable with informal but professional messaging. Best for warm markets and referral-heavy niches.
> 

### 🎯 Role Definition

You own WhatsApp outreach and community-based lead generation. Your job is to build warm conversations at scale through WhatsApp, Facebook Groups, LinkedIn groups, and online communities.

### ⎡ Daily Work

**WhatsApp & Community Daily Work:**

- Source 25–40 WhatsApp leads per day (from LinkedIn, business directories, referrals)
- Send 20–30 new WhatsApp messages per day using approved scripts
- Monitor and engage in 3–5 Facebook / LinkedIn / niche communities daily
- Identify warm leads from community activity (people asking for help, posting about problems)
- Start conversations with community warm leads — move to WhatsApp or call
- Follow up on all WhatsApp conversations Day 2 and Day 5
- Update CRM after every action

### 📊 Daily Targets

| Task | Target |
| --- | --- |
| WhatsApp messages sent | 20–30 |
| Community engagements (comments/DMs) | 10–15 |
| Follow-ups sent | 15–20 |
| Warm community leads identified | 5–10 |
| Calls booked (weekly) | 4–6 |

### 📝 WhatsApp + Community Specialist Scripts

**WhatsApp First Message (Cold)**

```
Hi [NAME], hope you don’t mind the message — I came across your business online and wanted to reach out.

We help [niche] businesses get more qualified leads through outreach. Quick question — are you currently focused on growing your client base?
```

**WhatsApp Follow-Up (Day 2, no reply)**

```
Hi [NAME], just following up on my message from yesterday.

Is growing your client pipeline something that’s on your radar right now?
```

**Community Warm Lead Opener (Facebook/LinkedIn Group)**

```
Hey [NAME], saw your post about [topic they mentioned] — we actually help businesses with exactly that.

Would you be open to a quick chat? I can share what’s been working for similar businesses.
```

**Converting to a Call**

```
Great to hear! The best way to walk you through it is a 15-minute call — I can show you exactly what we’d do for your business.

Are you free [Day] at [Time]?
```

### 📈 Success KPIs

- Reply rate on WhatsApp: 20%+
- Community warm leads per week: 20+
- Calls booked per week: 4–6
- CRM updated same day: 100%

### ✅ WhatsApp Specialist Checklist (Daily)

- [ ]  All messages personalised (no blasting)
- [ ]  All replies handled within 2 hours
- [ ]  3–5 communities checked and engaged
- [ ]  All warm leads from communities added to CRM
- [ ]  No single contact messaged more than once per day
