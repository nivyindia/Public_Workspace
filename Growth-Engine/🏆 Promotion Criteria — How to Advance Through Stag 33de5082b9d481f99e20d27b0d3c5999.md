# 🏆 Promotion Criteria — How to Advance Through Stages

> This page explains exactly what is required to advance to each level. Use it to track your own progress from Day 1.
> 

---

## Why This Exists

Promotion at Nivy is 100% performance-based. There are no shortcuts based on time served, seniority, or relationships. This page states the exact criteria so every VA knows what they are working toward and can self-assess at any point.

---

## Stage Advancement — Stages 1 to 6

Stages 1 through 6 advance automatically by timeline. You do not need to "pass" an exam or be nominated. You simply move forward when the time is right **and** you have met minimum output.

| Stage | Advances To | Time | Minimum Requirement to Advance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stage 1 (Day 1) | Stage 2 | End of Day 1 | Daily report submitted + 5+ messages sent |
| Stage 2 (Days 2–7) | Stage 3 | End of Day 7 | Daily reports submitted 5 of 7 days + at least 1 call booked |
| Stage 3 (Week 2) | Stage 4 | End of Day 14 | 3+ calls booked in the week + CRM fully updated |
| Stage 4 (Weeks 3–4) | Stage 5 | End of Day 28 | Consistent daily reports + at least 2 quality calls booked with handovers |
| Stage 5 (Month 2) | Stage 6 | End of Day 60 | Minimum attendance (22+ reports in 30 days) + KPIs tracked weekly |
| Stage 6 (Month 2–3) | Eligible for Stage 7 | Day 90 review | Performance review with supervisor — see Stage 7 criteria below |

> If you do not meet the minimum requirement to advance, your supervisor will notify you and give you 5 additional days. Consistent failure to meet minimums results in a performance review.
> 

---

## Stage 7 — Specialization Track

**Stage 7 is merit-based and requires supervisor approval.** You cannot self-advance.

### All of the following must be true:

- [ ]  Minimum **30 qualified calls booked** (cumulative, confirmed in CRM)
- [ ]  Consistent daily performance for **45+ consecutive days** (no week with fewer than 3 reports)
- [ ]  Reply Rate averaging **12%+** over last 30 days
- [ ]  Booking Rate averaging **20%+** over last 30 days
- [ ]  Zero disciplinary issues (no fake leads, no data violations, no unreported absences)
- [ ]  Supervisor nominates you — you do not apply yourself

### How nomination works:

Your supervisor reviews performance at the end of Month 3 (Day 90 review). VAs who meet all criteria above are notified. If you believe you qualify but have not been notified, you may ask your supervisor to review your metrics — but the final decision is theirs.

---

## Stage 8 — Team Leader

**Stage 8 is the highest level. It is by supervisor nomination only.**

### All of the following must be true:

- [ ]  Minimum **60 qualified calls booked** (cumulative, confirmed in CRM)
- [ ]  Top **20% of the team** by calls booked for **2+ consecutive months**
- [ ]  Zero disciplinary issues (ever — any data violation or fake lead report = permanent disqualification)
- [ ]  Has been at Stage 5 or above for at least **2 full months**
- [ ]  Has demonstrated ability to help other VAs (answered questions, shared scripts, supported team)
- [ ]  Supervisor nomination — cannot self-apply

### What happens after nomination:

1. Supervisor nominates VA to senior management
2. Senior management reviews performance records
3. If approved, VA is given a 2-week trial as Team Leader (managing 3–5 VAs)
4. After 2 weeks, full Team Leader status confirmed or revoked based on trial performance
