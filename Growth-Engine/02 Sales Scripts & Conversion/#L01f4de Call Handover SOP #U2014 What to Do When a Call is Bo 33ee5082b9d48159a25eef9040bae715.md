# 📞 Call Handover SOP — What to Do When a Call is Booked

<aside>
📌

This page is for VA reference. When a prospect books a call, your job doesn’t end there — it starts. A poorly handed-over call is a wasted call. The closer needs specific information to run a great discovery session. Your handover directly affects whether the call converts.

</aside>

---

## Why Handover Quality Matters

A closer going into a call without context is flying blind. They don’t know:

- Why the prospect agreed to the call
- What pain they mentioned in conversation
- What service they’re most interested in
- What objections they already raised
- What the prospect’s business does

A closer with a full handover can open the call with: *"I understand you mentioned your books have been behind for a few months and you’re worried about tax season — let’s start there."* That’s a completely different conversation.

**Every missed handover = higher chance of no-show or lost deal.**

---

## When to Submit a Handover

- Submit the handover form within 30 minutes of a call being confirmed
- If the call is booked for the same day, submit immediately
- Submit to your supervisor via the reporting channel AND update CRM status to "Booked"

---

## The Call Handover Form

Copy this template and fill it in completely. Do not leave any field blank. If you don’t know something, write "Unknown."

```
CALL HANDOVER FORM

=== BASIC DETAILS ===
Prospect Name:
Business Name:
Business Type / Industry:
Country / Location:
Call Date:
Call Time (with timezone):
Call Platform (Zoom / Google Meet / Phone):
Meeting Link:

=== CONTACT DETAILS ===
Email Address:
Phone / WhatsApp:
LinkedIn Profile URL:

=== DISCOVERY CONTEXT ===
How we connected (LinkedIn / WhatsApp / Email / Instagram / Facebook):
First contact date:
Number of messages before they agreed to call:
Which service are they most interested in?:

=== PROSPECT SITUATION ===
What is their main pain or challenge? (as they described it):
Current setup for this function (e.g. who handles their accounting now):
Business size (staff count if known):
Estimated budget (if mentioned):
Timeline (are they looking to start soon or just exploring?):

=== CONVERSATION HISTORY ===
Key things they said during our conversation:
Any objections raised and how I responded:
Anything that built rapport or trust:
Any red flags or concerns:

=== YOUR ASSESSMENT ===
Heat level (Hot / Warm / Cool):
Likelihood to convert (High / Medium / Low):
Why you think they’re a good fit:
Anything the closer should watch out for:
```

## After Submitting the Handover

**Step 1 — Update CRM:**

Change prospect status from "Interested" to "Booked." Add the call date and time in the Notes column.

**Step 2 — Send confirmation message to prospect:**

Send this message within 1 hour of booking:

> *"Hi [Name], confirming our call on [Day, Date] at [Time, Timezone]. You’ll receive a meeting link shortly from our team. Looking forward to speaking!"*
> 

**Step 3 — Send reminder 24 hours before:**

> *"Hi [Name], just a reminder about your call tomorrow at [Time]. The meeting link is: [link]. See you then!"*
> 

**Step 4 — After the call (follow up with closer):**

Check with your supervisor or closer within 24 hours of the call to find out the outcome. Update CRM to:

- "Call Complete — Proposal Sent"
- "Call Complete — Not a Fit"
- "Call Complete — Follow-up Needed"
- "No Show"

**If No Show:**

Send this message within 1 hour:

> *"Hi [Name], we had a call scheduled today at [time] — hope everything’s okay. Would you like to reschedule? Happy to find another time that works."*
>

## Common Handover Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- |
| Submitting handover after the call | Closer has no context — call starts cold |
| Leaving pain/challenge blank | Closer can’t personalise the opening |
| No confirmation message sent | Higher no-show rate |
| No reminder sent | Higher no-show rate |
| CRM not updated | Supervisor can’t track pipeline |
| Heat level left blank | Closer doesn’t know how much time to invest |

---

## Handover Quality Scoring

Your supervisor scores each handover:

| Score | What It Means |
| --- | --- |
| 5/5 | All fields complete, context is rich, confirmation + reminder sent |
| 4/5 | Minor fields missing but core context present |
| 3/5 | Significant gaps — closer had to find info themselves |
| 2/5 | Most context missing — call went in blind |
| 1/5 | Handover submitted after the call or barely filled in |

Handover quality is part of your overall QC score. Consistently poor handovers — even if you’re booking calls — will affect your performance rating.
