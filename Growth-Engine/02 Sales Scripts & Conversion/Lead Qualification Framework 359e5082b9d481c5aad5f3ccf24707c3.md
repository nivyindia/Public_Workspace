# ✅ Qualification Criteria {#criteria}

A lead is **qualified** when it meets the majority of these criteria:

| Criteria | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Matches ICP role (Founder, CEO, Coach, SME Owner, Agency) | 🔴 Must-have |
| Company size matches ICP (1–50 employees) | 🔴 Must-have |
| Geography matches target market | 🔴 Must-have |
| Has a valid, verified email address | 🔴 Must-have |
| Has a LinkedIn profile (active preferred) | 🟠 Strong |
| Company has a website | 🟠 Strong |
| Pain signals visible (posts, job ads, content gaps) | 🟡 Bonus |
| Recently funded or growing | 🟡 Bonus |
| No VA or marketing team evident | 🟡 Bonus |

# 📊 Lead Scoring Model {#scoring}

Score each lead out of 10:

| Signal | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Role exactly matches ICP | +2 |
| Company size matches | +2 |
| Geography matches | +2 |
| Valid verified email | +1 |
| Active LinkedIn (posts in last 30 days) | +1 |
| Pain signal visible | +1 |
| Company has no obvious marketing/VA team | +1 |
| **Total** | **/10** |

**Scoring tiers:**

- 8–10: 🔥 Hot lead — prioritise immediately
- 5–7: 🟡 Warm lead — include in standard sequence
- Below 5: ❌ Disqualify or cold pool

# ☑️ Qualification Checklist (VA Use) {#checklist}

Before adding any lead to CRM, VA must confirm:

- [ ]  Name and title are verified (not guessed)
- [ ]  Email is verified (Apollo green tick OR Hunter validation)
- [ ]  Company name is real and searchable
- [ ]  LinkedIn URL is correct and active
- [ ]  Lead is not already in HubSpot CRM (check for duplicate)
- [ ]  Lead is not on the Do Not Contact list
- [ ]  Lead matches at least 3 of the 5 must-have criteria
- [ ]  Lead score has been calculated and logged
- [ ]  ICP type tagged (ICP #1 / #2 / #3 / #4)
- [ ]  Source tagged (Apollo / LinkedIn / Inbound / Referral)

# 🏅 Lead Tiers {#tiers}

| Tier | Score | Action | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🔥 Hot | 8–10 | Personalised outreach within 24 hrs | Senior VA / Founder |
| 🟡 Warm | 5–7 | Standard email sequence in Apollo | VA |
| 🟦 Cold | 3–4 | Add to nurture pool — contact in 30 days | VA |
| ❌ Dead | 0–2 | Archive — do not contact | — |

---

# ❌ Disqualification Rules {#disqualify}

Auto-disqualify any lead that:

- Is a direct competitor
- Has generic email only (info@, hello@, admin@)
- Company has 50+ employees (not our ICP)
- Is in a restricted industry (government, military, legal services without context)
- Is already a client or past client (route to retention team)
- Is on the Do Not Contact list
- Has unsubscribed from previous emails
