# 5. Follow-Up System

## 

### Purpose of Follow-Up System

- Recover missed opportunities
- Increase reply rate significantly (2x–3x)
- Move cold prospects into active conversations

Reality:

- Most replies come from follow-ups, not first messages

---

### Follow-Up Schedule (Standard)

- **Day 0** → Initial outreach
- **Day 2** → Follow-up 1
- **Day 4** → Follow-up 2
- **Day 7** → Follow-up 3

Optional Advanced:

- Day 14 → Re-engagement

Rule:

- Minimum **3 follow-ups per lead** before dropping

---

### Follow-Up Scripts

**Follow-up 1 (Day 2)**

> Just checking if you'd like the **Roofing Growth Blueprint** I mentioned.
> 

**Follow-up 2 (Day 4)**

> I can also show how many roofing leads are available in your area if you're interested.
> 

**Follow-up 3 (Day 7)**

> Happy to share the blueprint if you're still open to seeing how other roofing companies are growing.
> 

---

### Tracking Follow-Ups in CRM

Each lead must have:

- Follow-up stage (0, 1, 2, 3)
- Last contacted date
- Next follow-up date

Example:

- Day 0 → Stage 0
- Day 2 → Stage 1
- Day 4 → Stage 2
- Day 7 → Stage 3

Rule:

- No lead should be left without a follow-up status

---

### Re-Engagement Strategy (Advanced)

For leads who didn’t respond after 7 days:

**Re-engagement Message (Day 14)**

> Not sure if this is relevant right now, but I can show how many leads roofing companies are missing in your area — happy to share if you'd like.
> 

Purpose:

- Restart conversation
- Catch leads who were busy earlier

---

### Follow-Up Rules (CRITICAL)

- Keep messages short and polite
- Do NOT sound pushy or desperate
- Always provide value or curiosity
- Stop after 3–4 attempts if no response

---

### Output of This System

Per VA:

- 50–100 follow-ups/day
- 30–50% of total replies come from follow-ups
