# 7. Conversion System (Reply → Call)

## 

### Purpose of Conversion System

- Turn replies into booked calls
- Move prospects from interest → action
- Keep process simple and fast

This is where **interest becomes real opportunity**.

---

### Reply Handling Scripts

**If Reply = YES / Interested**

> Great — sharing it here.
> 
> 
> This shows how roofing companies generate consistent leads using a simple system.
> 
> If you want, I can also show how many leads you're currently missing in your area.
> 

---

**If Reply = “Tell me more”**

> It’s a simple system using multiple channels like Google, referrals, and content to generate consistent jobs.
> 
> 
> I can show exactly how it applies to your area — want to do a quick 10–15 min call?
> 

---

**If Reply = Question (price/service)**

> It depends on your current setup — that’s why we do a quick audit first.
> 
> 
> I can show you what’s working and what’s missing for your business.
> 

---

### Lead Magnet Delivery Flow

Step 1 — Send Assets:

- Roofing Growth Blueprint (PDF)
- Growth Engine Diagram
- Lead Calculator (optional)

Step 2 — Add Context:

> This explains how roofing companies build consistent lead flow using a structured system.
> 

Step 3 — Transition to Opportunity:

> Based on your area, there’s likely a significant number of leads being missed.
> 

Step 4 — Move to Call:

> I can break this down specifically for your business in 10–15 minutes.
> 

---

### Audit Pitch Script

Goal: Move them to a call without sounding salesy

**Core Line:**

> I can show you how many leads you're currently missing and what can be improved.
> 

---

**Alternative Lines:**

- “I can quickly audit your current setup and show gaps”
- “We can map out your local opportunities in 10–15 minutes”

---

### Call Booking Process (Calendly)

Step 1 — Share Link:

> You can pick a time here: [Calendly Link]
> 

Step 2 — Keep It Simple:

- No long explanations
- No pressure

Step 3 — Confirm Booking:

> Got it — see you at the scheduled time.
> 

---

### Conversion Rules (CRITICAL)

- Respond quickly (within few hours)
- Keep replies short and clear
- Always move toward call
- Do NOT over-explain in chat

---

### Output of This System

Per VA / System:

- 5–10 replies/day
- 1–3 calls booked/day
