# 💰 Price Objections {#price}

**“It’s too expensive.”**

> "I understand — and I'd love to make sure you're comparing apples to apples. Most clients find that when they factor in the cost of their own time plus the cost of inconsistent freelancers, we're actually cheaper. Can I ask — what's your current cost of handling this in-house?"
> 

**“I can get a VA on Upwork for $5/hour.”**

> "Absolutely, you can — and many of our clients tried that first. The difference is that with us, you get a trained team, documented SOPs, and accountability — not a solo freelancer you have to manage. The question isn't the hourly rate; it's whether the work actually gets done consistently."
> 

**“I don't have the budget right now.”**

> "That's completely fair. Can I ask — is it a cash flow issue, or is it a matter of not being sure the ROI is there? Because if it's the second, I'd love to show you what our clients typically get back in the first 60 days."
> 

**“Can you do it for less?”**

> "I want to work with you — and I also want to make sure we can deliver properly. Rather than cut the price, can we look at starting with a smaller scope and scaling up? That way you see results first, and we earn the larger engagement."
>

# 🤝 Trust & Credibility Objections {#trust}

**“I’ve never heard of Nivy Digital.”**

> "That's fair — we're building our name. What I can offer you is full transparency: here's exactly what we'll do, by when, and how we measure it. And if after 30 days you're not happy, we'll refund the difference. Does that address the risk?"
> 

**“Do you have case studies or references?”**

> "We're building our case study library right now — what I can share is the specific results from [client type / early engagement]. I'd also be happy to get on a call with one of our current clients if that helps."
> 

**“How do I know you'll actually deliver?”**

> "Great question. Every engagement starts with a documented SOP so you can see exactly how we work. We give you a client portal with weekly reporting, and we set clear KPIs in week one. You're never in the dark."
>

# ⏰ Timing Objections {#timing}

**“Not right now — maybe in a few months.”**

> "Completely understand. Can I ask what needs to change in the next few months to make this the right time? That way I can follow up with something actually relevant, rather than just checking in."
> 

**“We're too busy to onboard a new vendor right now.”**

> "I actually hear that a lot — and it's usually a sign that you need us more, not less. Our onboarding takes 48 hours and we do most of the heavy lifting. The whole point is to reduce your workload from day one."
> 

**“We're in a transition period / restructuring.”**

> "Makes total sense — let's park this until the dust settles. Can I check in with you in 6 weeks? And in the meantime, is there one small thing I can help with that would make the transition easier?"
>

# 🤔 Need / Fit Objections {#need}

**“I don’t think we need a VA.”**

> "That's worth exploring. When we spoke, you mentioned [specific pain point they shared]. How are you handling that right now? I ask because that's exactly what our clients said before they started — and it turns out the need was there, they just hadn't quantified it yet."
> 

**“We already have someone who does this.”**

> "Great — and are they keeping up with the volume? A lot of our clients have an internal person, but use us for overflow or for the specialist tasks that person isn't trained in. Is capacity ever an issue?"
> 

**“I’d rather do it myself.”**

> "I respect that — and honestly, for some founders that makes sense. The question I'd ask is: what's the highest-value use of your time? If it's not outreach or admin — is it worth protecting your hours for the things only you can do?"
>

# 🎯 Competitor Objections {#competitor}

**“We’re already working with [competitor].”**

> "That's great — are you happy with what they're delivering? I ask because we often work alongside other providers in different areas. What are they handling for you, and is there anything they're not covering?"
> 

**“We looked at [Wishup / Boldly / other] and they were cheaper.”**

> "Worth comparing properly — can I ask what's included in their package? The key difference with us is [key differentiator]. I'd rather you make the right choice than just the cheapest one."
>

# 🔄 General Stalls {#stalls}

**“Let me think about it.”**

> "Of course. Can I ask — is there a specific concern I haven't addressed, or is it more about timing? I want to make sure you have everything you need to make a confident decision."
> 

**“I need to discuss with my partner / team.”**

> "Absolutely — that makes sense. Would it be helpful if I joined that conversation, or sent over a one-page summary you could share with them? Happy to do either."
> 

**[No reply after proposal]**

> "Hi [Name], just checking in on the proposal I sent over. Happy to answer any questions or adjust the scope if needed — just let me know where you're at. No pressure at all."
