# FAQ — 43 Account Management

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: How is this different from Stage 42 (Implementation and Delivery Setup) and Stage 44 (Customer Success Planning)?**
Stage 42 is the one-time setup of delivery operations for a new client. This stage is the ongoing, week-to-week relationship management — communication rhythm, reporting, portfolio health — that continues for the life of the account. Stage 44 builds on this with a more structured, proactive success-planning framework (goal-setting, adoption tracking, expansion signals) rather than the baseline communication cadence covered here.

**Q: How many accounts should one account manager handle?**
Roughly 8 active accounts as a quality threshold, per the source material — beyond that, per-client attention degrades and CSAT/retention typically suffer.

**Q: What's the standard communication cadence?**
Weekly personal update (WhatsApp or equivalent), bi-weekly or monthly formal performance report, and a monthly strategy review call — see [templates.md](templates.md) for the exact rhythm.

**Q: What happens if a client's CSAT score drops?**
It should trigger immediate account-manager (and, if severe, founder) intervention — not simply be logged and monitored passively. Silent dissatisfaction building unnoticed is one of the most preventable causes of churn.

**Q: Who owns the renewal conversation?**
The account manager, starting at 60 days before contract expiry, with a defined touchpoint sequence through to the renewal date. High-value accounts may involve founder/leadership at later stages of the sequence.
