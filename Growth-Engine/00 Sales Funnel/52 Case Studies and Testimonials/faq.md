**Q: When's the best time to ask for a testimonial?**
Right when a client expresses spontaneous positive feedback ("this is amazing!") or right after a milestone with a strong NPS score — asking in the moment, not batching it for later, captures both enthusiasm and specific memory before they fade.

**Q: Why do case studies need quantified results?**
Vague claims ("saved time," "great experience") are far less persuasive and less usable in a proposal than specific numbers (hours saved, deals closed, cost saved). The template requires numbers for this reason.

**Q: Does a client need to approve a testimonial before it's used?**
Yes — always, whether AI-formatted or hand-written. Never publish or use in a proposal without explicit client sign-off on the final wording.

**Q: Which review platforms matter most?**
Google Business Profile and Clutch are marked critical priority in the source material — set these up and prioritize collection there first.

**Q: How does this connect to Stage 31 Solution Mapping?**
Stage 31 pulls relevant, tagged case studies from this library to match against a new prospect's specific problems — this stage is what keeps that library populated and current.
