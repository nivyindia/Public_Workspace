| Source | Category | Countries | Cost | API/Export | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Statista | Market reports | Global | Paid subscription (verify) | Limited | Good for quick sourced figures |
| IBISWorld | Industry deep-dives | US/UK/AU-strong | Enterprise (verify) | No | Best for Porter's Five Forces style analysis |
| Ministry of MSME (India) | Government stats | India | Free | Some open data | Primary source for India SME counts |
| US Census Bureau (SUSB) | Government stats | US | Free | Yes (open data) | Statistics of US Businesses dataset |
| ONS Business Population Estimates | Government stats | UK | Free | Yes (open data) | Annual UK SME census |
| ABS Business Counts | Government stats | Australia | Free | Yes (open data) | Australian Bureau of Statistics |
| UAE Ministry of Economy | Government stats | UAE | Free | Limited | Corporate Tax and business-registration updates |
| G2 / Capterra / Trustpilot | Review mining | Global | Free to browse | No public scrape API — respect ToS | Pain-point language source |
| Reddit (r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur, country subs) | Community signal | Global | Free | Reddit API (rate-limited) | High-signal informal pain points |
| Google Trends | Demand trend proxy | Global | Free | Unofficial API | Directional only, not absolute volume |
| Local chamber of commerce / trade association sites | Industry reports | Per-country | Free/member | Varies | Often has free downloadable reports |
