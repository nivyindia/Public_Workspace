## Lead Databases

**Apollo.io** — apollo.io — Category: B2B contact database — Countries: Global, strongest US/UK/AU/UAE coverage — Industries: all — Best use: primary email+phone sourcing at SME scale — Data: name, title, email, phone, company firmographics — Data quality: high, self-reported verification ~95%+ — Filters: title, size, geo, industry, tech-stack — API: yes — Export: yes (CSV) — Scraping: not needed (native export) — Automation: sequences + Zapier/Make — Free plan: yes (limited credits) — Paid: ~$49–99/mo — Pros: best value, built-in verification and sequencing — Cons: enterprise data less deep than ZoomInfo — Alternatives: ZoomInfo, RocketReach, Snov.io — Related stages: [07 Contact Discovery](../07 Contact Discovery/README.md), [10 Lead Verification](../10 Lead Verification/README.md)

**ZoomInfo** — zoominfo.com — Category: enterprise B2B database — Countries: strongest US/global enterprise — Industries: all, esp. large enterprise — Best use: large-company/corporate targeting — Data quality: very high — API: yes — Free plan: no — Paid: high, custom quote — Pros: most accurate enterprise data — Cons: expensive — Alternatives: Apollo, RocketReach (budget)

**Lusha** — lusha.com — Category: contact enrichment — Best use: phone-based sales, LinkedIn/company-page enrichment via Chrome extension — Free plan: yes — Paid: yes — Alternatives: Apollo, SalesQL

**Snov.io** — snov.io — Category: email finder + verifier + drip automation — Best use: budget cold-email campaigns — Free plan: yes — Paid: yes — Alternatives: Hunter.io, Apollo

**RocketReach** — rocketreach.co — Category: personal + work email lookup — Best use: founder/influencer contact resolution — Free plan: yes (limited) — Paid: yes — Alternatives: Apollo, Snov.io

**Clearbit** — clearbit.com — Category: company + person enrichment API — Best use: CRM auto-enrichment on form-fill — Free plan: no — Paid: yes — Alternatives: People Data Labs, FullContact

## LinkedIn / Professional Network Sources

**LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — linkedin.com/sales — Category: advanced people/company search — Best use: precision decision-maker targeting with saved lead lists — Paid: ~$80/mo — Export: via HubSpot native integration or manual — Alternatives: free LinkedIn search (fewer filters)

**Phantombuster** — phantombuster.com — Category: browser automation / scraping — Best use: LinkedIn, Google Maps, Instagram export + scheduling — Free trial + paid — API: yes — Alternatives: TexAu

**TexAu** — texau.com — Category: multi-step automation + enrichment — Free credits + paid — Alternatives: Phantombuster

**Evaboot** — evaboot.com — Category: Sales Navigator export cleaner — Best use: clean CSV from a Sales Nav search URL — Paid — Alternatives: Airscale, Linked Helper

**Airscale** — Category: budget Sales Navigator scraper (Chrome extension) — Free/very low cost — Best use: unlimited-lead export replacing native Sales Nav export limits

## Startup / Funding / Tech Databases

## Government & Public Registries

**Companies House (UK)** — gov.uk/government/organisations/companies-house — Free, searchable by SIC code/company size — Fully legal, authoritative

**ABR / ASIC Connect (Australia)** — abr.business.gov.au / asic.gov.au — Free, all registered AU businesses

**Dubai Chamber of Commerce** — dubaichamber.com — Free, Dubai-registered businesses

**Abu Dhabi Chamber** — abudhabichamber.ae — Free, Abu Dhabi businesses

**DED (Dubai Economic Department)** — Free-zone company lists, partially public

**IRS EIN Database (US)** — irs.gov — Free, confirms registered US business existence

**SEC EDGAR (US)** — sec.gov/edgar — Free, public company filings
