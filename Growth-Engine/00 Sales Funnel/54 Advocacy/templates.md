## Advocate Candidate Criteria (starting point)
- NPS 9-10 across 2+ consecutive survey cycles (Stage 51), not a single high score
- Has given at least one testimonial or case study (Stage 52), OR
- Has made at least one successful referral (Stage 53)

## Reference Call Ask
```
Hi [Name], I have a quick favor to ask. We're speaking with a prospect
in [similar industry/situation] who'd really benefit from hearing about
your experience with us — would you be open to a brief 10-15 minute
call with them sometime in the next couple weeks?

No pressure at all if the timing isn't right — just thought of you
given how well things have gone.
```

## Content/Co-Marketing Ask
```
Hi [Name], we're putting together [content type — case study interview /
webinar / guest post] on [topic], and given your experience with
[specific result], I think your perspective would add a lot. Would you
be interested in a short conversation about it?
```

## Advocate Recognition Message (periodic, not just when asking for something)
```
Hi [Name], just wanted to take a moment to say thank you — your support
[specific thing they did, e.g., the reference call last month] has
genuinely meant a lot to our team. We really value the relationship.
```
