# FAQ — 19 WhatsApp Outreach

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: Should we use WhatsApp for every market?**
No — confirm fit first in Stage 15's channel plan. It's a strong primary channel in markets like India/UAE and a poor fit for cold B2B outreach in markets like the US/UK.

**Q: How many WhatsApp messages can one number send per day?**
Follow the conservative daily limit in [methods.md](methods.md)/source SOP (roughly 25-30 total) — exceeding it risks the number being flagged or banned.

**Q: Can I send links or a brochure in the first message?**
No — keep the first message text-only, under 4 lines, ending with a soft question.

**Q: What if someone asks "who is this" or "how did you get my number"?**
Respond professionally, identify yourself and where you found their business, and offer to leave them alone. Mark the contact Dead afterward.

**Q: Is unofficial browser automation an option for sending at volume?**
Not recommended — it risks number bans. Use the sanctioned WhatsApp Business API/BSP route once volume exceeds what the standard app can handle manually.
