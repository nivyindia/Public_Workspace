# FAQ — 48 Cross Sell Strategy

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: How is this different from Stage 47 Upsell Identification?**
Stage 47 covers expanding within the same service line based on results-based triggers (e.g., an SMM client hitting a follower-growth threshold moving to SMM L2). Stage 48 covers adding a *different* service to a client already on one Level 2 retainer, using the golden combination table, plus recognizing when a client is ready for a Level 3 integrated system.

**Q: What's the core principle behind the whole sell → upsell → cross-sell flow?**
Start clients small, deliver results, then expand the relationship — a $300/month client has the potential to become a $3,000+/month account over time, per the source playbook.

**Q: When should a Level 3 system upgrade be recommended instead of a simple cross-sell?**
Once a client already holds 2+ Level 2 services, has a growing budget, or explicitly says something like "I want everything working together" — that's the signal to book a strategy-team call rather than pitch a third standalone service.

**Q: Do USA and international clients get the same pitch?**
No — the source playbook is explicit that USA clients respond better to ROI/dollar-amount framing and can absorb higher-ticket offers, while international clients are more price-conscious initially and respond better to value-for-money and local price comparisons.

**Q: Who closes a cross-sell or Level 3 upgrade once a client is interested?**
Same handoff discipline as Stage 47 — the person who spots the signal opens the conversation and books the call; the closer/strategy team handles the actual close.
