## Scoring Rule Table — Fit / Demographic Signals

| Attribute | Points | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Title: Founder / CEO / Owner / Director | +15 | Decision maker |
| Title: Manager / Head of Dept | +8 | Influencer, may need sign-off |
| Company size matches ICP band | +10 | Primary fit |
| Geography: Tier-1 target market (e.g., US, UK, UAE, Australia) | +10-15 | Highest budget markets |
| Geography: Tier-2 target market (e.g., India Tier-1 cities) | +5 | Growing market |
| Industry matches best-fit niche list | +10 | See Stage 02 ICP |
| Budget signal mentioned (explicit range stated) | +20 | Qualified buyer |

## Scoring Rule Table — Behavioral / Intent Signals

| Behavior | Points | How to Track |
|---|---|---|
| Submitted contact/quote form | +30 | CRM form fill |
| Booked a discovery call | +50 | Booking tool → CRM |
| Downloaded a lead magnet | +20 | Form/landing page tool |
| Opened 3+ emails | +10 | CRM email tracking |
| Clicked a link in email | +15 | CRM email tracking |
| Visited pricing page | +20 | CRM tracking pixel |
| Visited services page | +10 | CRM tracking pixel |
| Replied to cold email | +25 | Outreach tool → CRM |
| Replied to LinkedIn DM | +25 | Manual/CRM entry |
| Engaged on WhatsApp | +15 | Manual entry |

## Scoring Rule Table — Negative Signals

| Behavior | Points | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Unsubscribed from email | −30 | No longer interested |
| Marked email as spam | −50 | Disqualify |
| Explicitly said "not interested" | −50 | Remove from active pipeline |
| Free email domain, no company context | −5 | May be low intent |
| Email bounced (hard bounce) | Disqualify | Bad contact anchor — route back to Stage 10 |

## Score Thresholds & Actions

| Score | Tier | Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| 0-19 | Cold | Nurture sequence only, no active outreach |
| 20-49 | Warm | VA follow-up within 3 business days |
| 50-79 | Hot | Senior VA/founder contact within 24 hours |
| 80+ | Priority | Founder contacts same day, direct call attempt |

## Slack Alert Message Format

```
🔥 HOT LEAD: [Name] from [Company] — Score: [X]
   Source: [Lead Source]
   Interest: [Service Interest]
   Reason: [one-line score-reason summary]
   Action: Follow up within 24 hrs
   🔗 View in CRM: [contact link]
```

## Daily VA Scoring Checklist (Manual Method)

1. Filter CRM to contacts updated in last 24 hours
2. For each, check activity feed
3. Apply fit table (once per lead, refresh if ICP fields changed)
4. Apply behavior table (cumulative, add new points only)
5. Apply any negative signals
6. Enter Total Score, set Tier
7. If Hot/Priority, notify #sales immediately

## Simplified /10 Fit-Only Model (For Teams Not Yet Tracking Behavior)

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Role exactly matches ICP | +2 |
| Company size matches | +2 |
| Geography matches | +2 |
| Valid verified email | +1 |
| Active LinkedIn (posted in last 30 days) | +1 |
| Pain signal visible (posts, job ads, content gaps) | +1 |
| No obvious marketing/VA team in place | +1 |
| **Total** | **/10** |

Tiers: 8-10 = Hot · 5-7 = Warm · <5 = Cold.
