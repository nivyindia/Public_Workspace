## Manual Audit Methods

- **Direct site visit** — company overview, services, pricing pages, portfolio, testimonials read directly from the competitor's own site
- **Review-site reading** — Google Reviews, Trustpilot, Clutch, G2 read manually for what's praised and what's complained about
- **Sales team debrief** — capture what competitors prospects actually mention by name during live sales conversations; this real-world signal often surfaces competitors that generic research misses

## Tool-Assisted Methods

- **Social media competitor tracking** — Social Blade, Meta Business Suite Insights, Not Just Analytics for growth/engagement comparison
- **SEO & traffic tools** — SimilarWeb, Ubersuggest, BuiltWith for traffic estimates, keyword targeting, and tech stack
- **Ad transparency tools** — Meta Ads Library, Google Ads Transparency Center, TikTok Creative Center to see live competitor ad creative and messaging
- **Content monitoring** — Feedly, BuzzSumo, Google Trends for competitor blog/content cadence and topic focus

## AI-Assisted Methods

- LLM synthesis of competitor site + review content into a structured 12-section audit (see [README.md §7](README.md#7-ai-section))
- AI-drafted positioning-response scripts from documented weaknesses

## Manual vs. Automated

| Method | Manual | Semi-Automated | Fully Automated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pricing page check | Analyst visits page | Scheduled page-change monitor (Visualping) | Automated diff + alert pipeline |
| Review sentiment scan | Analyst reads reviews | Manual export + LLM summary | Scheduled scrape (where ToS allows) + LLM classification dashboard |
| Ad creative tracking | Analyst checks Ads Library | Scheduled screenshot capture | N/A — ad libraries generally require manual/browser interaction |

## Community & Referral Methods

- Prospect-mentioned competitors captured directly in CRM notes during sales calls, reviewed periodically to catch competitors not found through desk research
