## Proposal Document Structure

**Section 1: Executive Summary** — who we are (2 sentences), what we understood their challenge to be, what we're proposing and why.

**Section 2: Recommended Solution** — service package selected, specific deliverables (bullet list), what's included/not included.

**Section 3: Pricing** — monthly retainer or one-time fee (clearly stated), payment terms (e.g., invoiced monthly, due within 14 days), optional add-ons with pricing.

**Section 4: Timeline & Onboarding** — start date, onboarding steps, expected time to see results.

**Section 5: Why Us** — 2-3 relevant case studies/results, credentials/certifications, 1-2 testimonials.

**Section 6: Next Steps** — clear CTA ("sign below / reply to confirm / book a call if you have questions"), proposal validity ("valid for 14 days").

## Follow-Up Cadence
- **Day 3:** "Just checking you received the proposal — any questions?"
- **Day 7:** "Happy to jump on a 10-minute call to walk through any questions before you decide."
- **Day 12:** Final follow-up (see Stage 24 Follow Up Systems cadence)

## Negotiation Response Table

| Ask | Response |
|---|---|
| "Can you do it cheaper?" | "Our pricing reflects the quality and expertise we bring. If budget is a concern, we could start with a smaller scope and expand from there." |
| "Can we start with just one service?" | "Absolutely — let's start with [service], and we can add more as you see results." |
| "We need to think about it" | "Of course! What information would help you decide? Happy to answer any specific questions." |
| "We're comparing with others" | "That makes sense. When you're comparing, look beyond price — consider quality assurance, qualifications, and responsiveness." |

**Never discount more than the standard threshold (10% per source SOP — verify current policy) without named approver sign-off.**

## Contract & Sign-Off Checklist
1. Simple service agreement (1-2 pages): scope, pricing, payment terms, notice period, confidentiality
2. Sign via e-signature tool (PandaDoc/DocuSign/Signable)
3. Send signed copy to client + file in company records
4. Log in CRM: deal stage → Closed Won
5. Trigger onboarding workflow
