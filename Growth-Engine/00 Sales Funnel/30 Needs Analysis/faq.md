# FAQ — 30 Needs Analysis

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: Isn't this already covered by the discovery call notes (Stage 29)?**
Stage 29's notes are a real-time capture of the conversation. This stage structures those notes into a ranked, documented needs analysis that Stage 31/33 can build directly from — the difference between raw notes and a usable brief.

**Q: What if the call didn't fully surface a problem's implication or desired outcome?**
Flag it as a gap rather than guessing. A short follow-up clarification message (see [templates.md](templates.md)) is often faster and more accurate than assuming.

**Q: Who finalizes the document?**
The rep who ran the call, even if an AI draft did the initial structuring — they have context the transcript alone doesn't fully capture.

**Q: How does ranking work if the prospect mentioned several problems?**
Rank by how strongly they expressed the cost of inaction and how specific their desired outcome was — not by which problem sounds easiest for the company to solve.

**Q: What happens after this document is finalized?**
It becomes the direct input to Stage 31 Solution Mapping, which designs the specific solution against each ranked problem.
