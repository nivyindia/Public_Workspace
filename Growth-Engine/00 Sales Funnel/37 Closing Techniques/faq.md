# FAQ — 37 Closing Techniques

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: Is there a dedicated internal "closing techniques" SOP this stage was built from?**
No — no internal doc treats closing as a discrete, separate step. This stage is synthesized from the "Next Steps" and sign-off structure already present in the Proposal & Deal Closing SOP (Stage 33/36), formalized here as its own stage since the funnel's 54-stage structure calls for it explicitly.

**Q: Why does this stage avoid artificial urgency tactics?**
The source material's tone throughout (negotiation scripts, follow-up cadence) is consultative and honest rather than pressure-based — fabricating urgency (e.g., false deadlines) isn't consistent with that approach and risks damaging trust with the prospect.

**Q: How is this different from Stage 33's "Send Timing & Format" and "Follow-Up Cadence"?**
Stage 33 covers getting the proposal out and following up on schedule. Stage 37 covers recognizing and acting on buying signals that arise during that process, and executing the actual close — a judgment layer on top of the mechanical cadence.

**Q: What happens if a prospect never gives a clear buying signal?**
The standard follow-up cadence (Stage 24/33) continues on schedule regardless; if it completes without a signal or response, the deal is routed to 90-day nurture rather than force-closed.
