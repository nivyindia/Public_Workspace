# FAQ — 38 Deal Desk and Approval Workflows

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: Isn't this just extra bureaucracy that slows deals down?**
Done well, it should be faster than ad hoc email approvals — a clear routing rule (discount ≤10% → AM, >10% → founder) means most requests get an answer within hours, not days, because the rep already knows exactly who to ask and what information to include.

**Q: What counts as something that needs deal-desk approval, versus something a rep can just decide?**
Anything outside the standard, pre-approved terms already published in Stage 34 (Pricing and Packaging) — non-standard discount size, non-standard payment schedule, custom scope, or rush delivery. Standard-tier pricing and standard payment terms don't need this stage at all.

**Q: Who is the deal-desk approver in a small team?**
Often the founder directly, per the discount policy referenced in Stage 34's Pricing Strategy doc ("hourly rate — never negotiate below $20/hr effective" as a hard floor, everything else routed to the founder). Larger teams introduce a tiered structure as volume grows.

**Q: What happens if a rep closes a deal with a non-standard term without going through this process?**
The deal should not be considered fully closed/bookable until the approval record exists — this is why the checklist in [checklists.md](checklists.md) treats a missing approval record as an audit failure, not a minor administrative gap.

**Q: Does this stage apply to every single deal?**
No — most deals close entirely within the standard pricing and terms from Stage 34 and never touch this stage. This stage exists specifically for the exception path.
