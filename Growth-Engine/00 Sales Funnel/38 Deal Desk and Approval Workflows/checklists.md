# Checklists — 38 Deal Desk and Approval Workflows

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

## Request Submission QC
- [ ] Deal-desk request includes the specific ask (discount %, non-standard payment terms, custom scope, rush delivery) — not a vague "can we do something special"
- [ ] Current deal value and proposed final value both stated
- [ ] Reason for the request documented (competitive pressure, cash-flow constraint, strategic account, etc.)

## Routing QC
- [ ] Request routed to the correct approval tier based on the discount/term-deviation thresholds in [templates.md](templates.md)
- [ ] Requests above the highest defined threshold escalate to the founder/leadership, never auto-approved by a rule

## Approval QC
- [ ] Every approval or rejection has a named approver on record — never approved "by default" through silence
- [ ] Approval decision is time-stamped and logged against the specific deal, not just communicated verbally
- [ ] Approved terms are reflected exactly in the final proposal/contract (Stage 33/36) — no drift between what was approved and what was signed

## Audit / Completeness Checks
- [ ] No deal closes with a non-standard term that lacks a corresponding approval record
- [ ] Monthly audit sample: pull 10% of closed deals and confirm every discount/term deviation has a matching approval log entry
- [ ] Approval turnaround time tracked — slow approvals are a common source of lost deals
