# FAQ — 20 SMS Outreach

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: Can we use SMS as a cold-outreach channel like email or LinkedIn?**
Not by default in this funnel — SMS is scoped to confirmation/reminder/re-engagement use cases because unsolicited commercial SMS carries stricter regulation in most markets than email or LinkedIn DMs. Any exception requires a documented lawful basis and sign-off.

**Q: Why does a confirmation SMS need opt-out language?**
Many jurisdictions require it even for messages tied to an existing relationship/booking. Include it as standard practice rather than deciding case by case.

**Q: What happens when someone replies STOP?**
They're immediately and permanently suppressed from all future SMS. This must be actioned right away, not batched.

**Q: Does a reminder SMS reduce no-shows meaningfully?**
Track it — this is the primary justification for using SMS in this funnel at all, so the no-show-rate comparison (with vs. without reminder) should be monitored per Stage 28 handoff.

**Q: What's TCPA and does it apply outside the US?**
TCPA is a US law governing consent for commercial calls/texts. It doesn't directly apply elsewhere, but most markets have an equivalent — verify the correct regulation for each target market rather than assuming TCPA-style rules are universal.
