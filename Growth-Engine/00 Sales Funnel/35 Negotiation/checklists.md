# Checklists — 35 Negotiation

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

## Pre-Response QC
- [ ] Objection matched to a standard category before drafting a response (not improvised from scratch)
- [ ] No discount offered verbally before checking it against the standard threshold

## Discount QC
- [ ] Any discount beyond the standard threshold has a named approver on record before being communicated
- [ ] Hourly-rate floor (Stage 34) never breached regardless of pressure
- [ ] Scope-creep requests are billed as add-ons, never folded into the base price for free

## De-escalation QC
- [ ] "We need to think about it" and "comparing with others" responses reframe on value, not just repeat the price
- [ ] If four rounds of the escalation ladder (Stage 34) are exhausted without agreement, the deal is politely declined and routed to 90-day nurture rather than under-priced to force a close

## Documentation QC
- [ ] Final negotiated price, discount %, and approver logged in the CRM
- [ ] Negotiated terms match exactly what gets written into the contract (Stage 36)

## Duplicate / Accuracy / Completeness Checks
- [ ] No deal has two different "final" negotiated figures recorded across CRM and contract
