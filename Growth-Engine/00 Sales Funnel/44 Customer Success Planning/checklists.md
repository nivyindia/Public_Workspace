# Checklists — 44 Customer Success Planning

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

## Lifecycle Touchpoint QC
- [ ] Every touchpoint in the lifecycle map (Day 1, Day 7, Day 30, monthly, quarterly, annual) actually happens on schedule — not skipped when things seem fine
- [ ] Monthly report sent by the agreed date, including completed work, quantified results, and next month's plan
- [ ] NPS survey sent monthly to every active client

## Churn-Risk Monitoring QC
- [ ] Warning signs (7+ day silence, negative report feedback, reduced task volume, cancellation question, NPS <6, invoice dispute) reviewed weekly, not just noticed incidentally
- [ ] Each warning sign gets the response matched to its defined severity — medium signs get a proactive check-in, critical signs get a same-day founder call
- [ ] No critical warning sign goes unaddressed for more than 24 hours

## NPS & Feedback QC
- [ ] NPS score logged and trended per client, not just captured as a one-off number
- [ ] Promoters (9-10) receive a review/referral ask
- [ ] Passives (7-8) receive a "what would make this a 10?" follow-up
- [ ] Detractors (0-6) receive a founder call within 24 hours

## Save-a-Client QC
- [ ] Any cancellation conversation follows the structured save script — understand the real reason before offering a fix
- [ ] Retention offers made (reassignment, scope adjustment, pause instead of cancel, bonus value) are logged, not just verbally offered
