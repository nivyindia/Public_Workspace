## Client Lifecycle Touchpoint Map

| Touchpoint | When | Owner | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome email + team intro | Day 1 | Automated | Email |
| Onboarding/kickoff call | Day 1-3 | Account Manager | Video call |
| Week 1 check-in | Day 7 | Account Manager | WhatsApp/Slack |
| Month 1 review | Day 30 | Account Manager / Founder | Email + optional call |
| Monthly progress report | Every month | Delivery team | Report |
| Quarterly strategy review | Every 3 months | Account Manager | Video call |
| Renewal reminder | 14+ days before renewal | Automated | Email |
| Referral invitation | Month 1 complete | Automated | Email |
| Annual review | Month 12 | Founder | Video call |

## Retention Rate Targets by Tenure

| Tenure | Churn Rate Target |
|---|---|
| Month 1-3 | < 20% |
| Month 4-6 | < 15% |
| Month 7-12 | < 10% |
| Month 12+ | < 5% |

## Churn Warning Signs & Response

| Warning Sign | Severity | Response |
|---|---|---|
| No client reply in 7+ days | Medium | Proactive check-in |
| Negative feedback on a report | High | Founder calls within 24 hrs |
| Client reduces scope/volume | Medium | Check on satisfaction |
| Client asks about cancellation | Critical | Founder calls same day |
| NPS score < 6 | Critical | Founder personal email + call |
| Client disputes invoice | High | Founder handles immediately |

## Save-a-Client Script (when cancellation is raised)
```
"[Name], I really appreciate you telling me directly. Can I ask — is this
about the results, the process, or the budget? I want to understand
completely."

[Listen carefully]

"What would need to change for this to work for you?"

[Offer: reassign team member / adjust scope / pause instead of cancel /
add bonus value]

"I don't want to lose you as a client. Here's what I can do: [specific
offer]. Would that work?"
```

## NPS Survey (monthly)
```
Q1: On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend us to a friend
or colleague? [0-10 slider]
Q2: What's the main reason for your score? [open text]
Q3: What could we do better? [open text]
```

## NPS Score Actions

| Score | Label | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 9-10 | Promoter | Ask for public review + referral invitation |
| 7-8 | Passive | "What would make this a 10?" + address the gap |
| 0-6 | Detractor | Founder calls within 24 hrs to understand and resolve |
