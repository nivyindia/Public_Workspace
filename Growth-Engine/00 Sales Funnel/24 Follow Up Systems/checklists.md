# Checklists — 24 Follow Up Systems

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

## Follow-Up Cadence QC
- [ ] Follow-up spacing matches the documented cadence (e.g., Day 1 → 4 → 10 → 21) per channel
- [ ] No channel exceeds its maximum follow-up count (typically 3) before being marked cold
- [ ] Every follow-up message references a value-add item, not a bare "checking in" bump

## Cold-Marking QC
- [ ] Contact marked `cold` only after exhausting the defined follow-up count on that channel
- [ ] Cold contact automatically scheduled for 90-day re-engagement, not left with no next action
- [ ] Cold status is per-channel, not accidentally applied funnel-wide while other channels are still active (Stage 21)

## Re-Engagement QC
- [ ] Re-engagement sequence uses distinct, lighter-touch copy — not a repeat of the original cadence verbatim
- [ ] Re-engagement contacts tracked separately so their conversion rate can be measured against fresh contacts

## Duplicate / Accuracy / Completeness Checks
- [ ] No contact receives two follow-up messages on the same channel on the same day
- [ ] No contact with an active reply/booking still receives an automated follow-up
