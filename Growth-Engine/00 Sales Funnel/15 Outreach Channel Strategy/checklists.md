# 15 Outreach Channel Strategy — Checklists

---

## Before a Channel Plan Is Approved

- [ ] Persona (Stage 03) reviewed for channel fit
- [ ] Market compliance constraints checked (cold-calling registries, messaging-app business policies, email opt-in rules)
- [ ] Historical performance log checked, if data exists for this segment/market combination
- [ ] Primary and secondary channel both justified, not defaulted
- [ ] Sequencing logic defined if multi-channel (parallel vs. sequential, and order)
- [ ] Team capacity/skill confirmed available for the chosen channel(s)

## New-Market Entry Checklist

- [ ] Compliance summary drafted and reviewed (AI-assisted draft is a starting point only — verify against current local regulation, involve legal counsel if operating at meaningful scale)
- [ ] Channel norms researched (e.g., is WhatsApp an accepted business channel here, are cold calls restricted)
- [ ] First campaign in the new market treated as a test — smaller volume, results logged before scaling

## Post-Campaign Review

- [ ] Channel-level outcome data logged (or correctly marked Unattributed if not cleanly attributable)
- [ ] Channel plan decision revisited: did the chosen mix perform as expected? Log a note either way for future reference
