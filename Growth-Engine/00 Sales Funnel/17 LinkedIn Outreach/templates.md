## Connection Request Note (160 chars max)

**Pain-focused:**
```
Hi [First Name], I help [persona type] like you with [pain area].
Happy to connect and share how!
```

**Curiosity-based (no pitch):**
```
Hi [First Name], saw your work in [industry] — impressive.
I work with [persona type] on [relevant area]. Would love to connect.
```

## DM Sequence

**Message 1 — sent immediately after connection accepted**
```
Hi [First Name]! Thanks for connecting.

I'm [Your Name] from [Company]. We help [industry] businesses with [1-line value statement].

Curious — what's the one thing eating most of your time/budget on [relevant area] right now?
```

**Message 2 — sent 2 days later if no reply**
```
Hi [First Name], following up in case my last message got buried.

Quick example: we recently helped a [similar industry] business [specific result].

Would a 15-20 min call make sense to explore if something similar applies to you?
[booking link]
```

**Message 3 — sent 5 days later (breakup)**
```
Hi [First Name], last message from me — promise!

If [pain area] ever becomes a priority, feel free to reach out or book here anytime:
[booking link]

Either way, good luck with everything you're building.
```

## Formatting Rules
- Connection notes: under 160 characters
- DM 1: no pitch beyond one line, end with a low-friction question
- No links in DM 1 (reduces spam-pattern signals)
- All messages professional but conversational — LinkedIn tone is less formal than email
