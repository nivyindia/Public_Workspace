# 🎯 Pricing Philosophy {#philosophy}

1. **Value-based, not hourly** — We price on outcomes and scope, not hours worked
2. **Retainer-first** — Monthly retainers are the core model; one-off projects are secondary
3. **Market-segmented** — India pricing vs. international pricing reflects real purchasing power differences
4. **Transparent** — Clients know exactly what's included; no hidden fees
5. **Results-anchored** — Every proposal frames ROI, not just cost

# 📦 Service Tiers {#tiers}

## 🌱 Starter — "Get Started"

For: Solo founders and early-stage businesses who need basic VA support.

- 20 hours/month of VA time
- 1 dedicated VA
- Services: Admin, research, scheduling, basic outreach
- Reporting: Monthly summary
- Support: Email only

## 🚀 Growth — "Build Your Pipeline"

For: Founders and coaches ready to scale lead gen and outreach.

- 40 hours/month of VA time
- 1–2 VAs (specialist allocation)
- Services: Lead scraping, LinkedIn outreach, cold email, content scheduling, CRM management
- Reporting: Weekly KPI dashboard
- Support: Email + WhatsApp

## 🌟 Pro — "Full Marketing Operations"

For: SMEs and agencies needing a full marketing ops team.

- 80 hours/month of VA time
- 2–3 VAs (dedicated team)
- Services: Full lead gen + outreach + content + CRM + automation monitoring + reporting
- Reporting: Weekly dashboard + monthly strategy call
- Support: Priority WhatsApp + monthly Zoom call

## 🏆 Enterprise — "Dedicated Team"

For: Agencies wanting white-label capacity or SMEs needing full-time equivalent support.

- 160+ hours/month
- 3–5 VAs
- Custom scope
- Dedicated account manager
- SLA-backed delivery
- Bespoke reporting

# 🤝 Discount & Negotiation Policy {#negotiation}

## Discounts We Offer

- **3-month prepay:** 10% discount
- **6-month prepay:** 15% discount
- **Referral:** 1 month free for every referred client who signs
- **Early adopter (first 10 clients):** 20% off first 3 months

## What We Don't Discount

- Hourly rate — never negotiate below $20/hr effective
- Scope creep — any extra work beyond agreed scope is billed as add-on
- Rush delivery — premium pricing applies for same-day requests

## If Prospect Pushes Back on Price

1. First: Reframe on ROI and outcomes
2. Second: Offer smaller starting scope (Starter tier) with upgrade path
3. Third: Offer prepay discount if cash flow is their concern
4. Fourth: If still not viable — politely decline and add to 90-day nurture

# 💬 Pricing Communication Guide {#communication}

**Never lead with price.** Always:

1. Understand their problem first (discovery call)
2. Propose a solution (scope of work)
3. Then present price in context of value/ROI

**Proposal structure:**

1. Summary of what we heard on the call (their pain)
2. Proposed solution (which tier + customisation)
3. Outcomes and what success looks like
4. Investment (price)
5. Next steps (sign + start date)

**Anchor the ROI:**

> "Our Growth package is $1,000/month. If we book you 5 extra discovery calls per month and you close 1 — at an average deal size of $2,000 — you've already 2x'd your investment in month one."
