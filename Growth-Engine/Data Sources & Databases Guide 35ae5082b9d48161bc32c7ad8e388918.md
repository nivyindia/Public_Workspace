# 🚀 [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) Guide {#apollo}

## Setup

1. Create account at [apollo.io](http://apollo.io)
2. Install Apollo Chrome extension
3. Connect your sending email (use a warmed subdomain, e.g. [outreach@mail.nivydigital.com](mailto:outreach@mail.nivydigital.com))
4. Set up email warmup (Apollo has built-in warmup — enable it for 2 weeks before sending)

## Building a Lead List

1. Go to **Prospecting** → People
2. Apply filters:
    - Job title: CEO, CFO, Founder, Director, MD
    - Company size: 5–200 employees
    - Location: United Kingdom / United States / UAE / Australia
    - Industry: Professional Services, Real Estate, E-Commerce, Technology
3. Exclude: Students, interns, job seekers (use "not" filters)
4. Export: Save to a sequence or export to CSV
5. Verify emails before sending (Apollo does this automatically at 95%+ accuracy)

## Sequence Setup

1. Go to **Sequences** → Create New
2. Add steps: Email Day 1, Auto follow-up Day 4, Auto follow-up Day 10
3. Set sending hours: Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm recipient local time
4. Max emails per day per inbox: 50 (to avoid spam flags)
5. Enable open/click tracking

# 🔍 LinkedIn Sales Navigator Guide {#linkedin}

## Filters to Use

- **Title:** CEO, Founder, Director, CFO, Managing Director, Owner
- **Company headcount:** 2–200
- **Geography:** United Kingdom / United States / UAE / Australia
- **Industry:** Accounting, Consulting, Real Estate, Retail, Technology, Healthcare
- **Posted in last 30 days:** Filter to find active users (better response rates)

## Saving Leads

- Save leads to a "Lead List" per market/campaign
- Export to CRM using LinkedIn → HubSpot native integration (Sales Navigator required)
- Or export manually and upload to Apollo

## Connection Note Best Practice

- Always send a personalized note with every connection request
- 300 characters max — reference something specific to their profile

# 🌐 Free / Alternative Sources {#free}

**India:**

- [JustDial.com](http://JustDial.com) — search by category and city
- IndiaMART — find SME buyers and sellers
- Google Maps — search business type + city, grab contact info
- Local business WhatsApp groups (ask to be added by existing contacts)

**UAE:**

- Dubai Chamber of Commerce directory (free)
- Abu Dhabi business directory
- LinkedIn groups for UAE entrepreneurs

**UK:**

- Companies House ([companieshouse.gov.uk](http://companieshouse.gov.uk)) — search by SIC code and company size
- LinkedIn (most UK professionals are active)

**US:**

- Crunchbase (funded startups)
- Product Hunt (tech/SaaS companies)
- Apollo (most comprehensive for US SMEs)

# ✅ Data Quality Rules {#quality}

- **Never use unverified emails** — causes bounce rate spikes and damages sender reputation
- **Always verify before sending** — use Apollo's built-in verification or [Hunter.io](http://Hunter.io)
- **Acceptable bounce rate:** Under 3% — if higher, pause and clean the list
- **No personal mobile numbers** — only use business emails and LinkedIn
- **GDPR compliance (UK/EU):** Only contact people at their business email with a legitimate business interest basis. Always include unsubscribe option.
- **Update lists regularly** — remove bounced emails, unsubscribes, and closed companies monthly
