# 🎯 Program Overview {#overview}

**Nivy Digital Referral Program:**

- Any client, partner, or contact who refers a business that becomes a paying client earns a commission
- **Commission: 10% of the first 3 months’ invoices** for the referred client
- No cap on referrals — refer as many as you like
- Commission paid within 30 days of the referred client’s first invoice being paid
- Referral is tracked via a unique referral code or email intro

**Why 10% of 3 months?**

It’s meaningful enough to motivate action (e.g., a £500/mo client = £150 commission), but doesn’t erode margin beyond the acquisition phase.

# 🤝 The Referral Ask System {#ask}

## When to Ask

- **Day 90** after a client signs (first ask — they’ve experienced enough value)
- **After a client compliment or positive feedback** — strike while sentiment is warm
- **At every quarterly check-in call**

## How to Ask (Script)

> "[Name], we’ve really enjoyed working with you and I’m glad things are going well. We grow mainly through referrals, and I wanted to let you know — if you ever know any other business owners who could benefit from what we do, we’d love an introduction. We actually pay a 10% referral commission on the first 3 months. No pressure at all, just wanted to put it out there!"
> 

## Follow-Up if They Say "I’ll Think of Someone"

- Add to HubSpot task: follow up in 2 weeks
- Send a reminder message with the referral details
- Make it easy: give them a 1-paragraph "what to say" script they can forward

## Easy Referral Message Template (for clients to send):

> "Hey [friend], I use Nivy Digital for [accounting/IT/marketing] and it’s been brilliant. They’re a full-service team at a fraction of the usual cost. I thought of you — it might be worth a quick call. Here’s their booking link: [[cal.com](http://cal.com) link]"
>

# 🤝 Partner Referral Program {#partners}

## Who Makes an Ideal Partner?

- Chartered accountants / CPA firms (can refer IT + marketing clients)
- Law firms (refer compliance + digital clients)
- Business consultants (refer full-suite clients)
- Recruitment agencies (refer VA program buyers)
- Software vendors (Xero, HubSpot, n8n implementers)

## Partner Onboarding

1. Sign a simple referral partner agreement (1-page)
2. Receive a unique referral code (trackable)
3. Get a Nivy Digital partner intro pack (PDF: what we do, pricing overview, case studies)
4. Monthly check-in call to discuss pipeline

## Partner Agreement Terms (Summary)

- 10% commission on first 3 months for any referred client
- Referral is valid for 90 days from first introduction
- Commission paid within 30 days of first invoice paid
- Either party can terminate the agreement with 30 days notice

# 📊 Tracking & Payouts {#tracking}

- Every referral logged in HubSpot: "Referral Source" field = referrer name
- Referral code (e.g., REF-JOHNSMITH) used in booking forms and emails
- Monthly referral report: who referred, which clients signed, commission owed
- Commission paid via bank transfer with a simple commission statement

**Target:** £1,000+/month in commission payouts by Month 9 (signals £10,000+/mo in referral-sourced revenue)
