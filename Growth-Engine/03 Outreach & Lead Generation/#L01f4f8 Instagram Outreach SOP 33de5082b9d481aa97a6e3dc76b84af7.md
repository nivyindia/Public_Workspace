# 📸 Instagram Outreach SOP

> Instagram is a supplementary outreach channel. It works best for visual service businesses, coaches, agency owners, and entrepreneurs who are active on the platform. It should be used alongside LinkedIn and WhatsApp — not instead of them.
> 

---

## When to Use Instagram for Outreach

Use Instagram outreach when:

- Your assigned niche has active Instagram accounts (agencies, coaches, consultants, local businesses)
- The lead doesn’t respond on LinkedIn after 2–3 follow-ups
- The lead’s Instagram profile shows active business content (posts within the last 30 days)

Do NOT use Instagram outreach for:

- Leads with no profile photo or under 100 followers
- Inactive accounts (last post 3+ months ago)
- Personal accounts with no business connection

---

## Daily Targets

| Activity | Daily Target | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profile follows | 10–20 | Follow before messaging to warm up |
| DMs sent | 15–20 | New accounts: start with 10/day for Week 1 |
| Story reactions / comments | 5–10 | Warm up the lead before DM |
| Follow-up DMs | 10–15 | Day 3 follow-up on no-reply |

---

## Profile Setup Before Outreach

Before sending a single DM, your Instagram profile must look professional:

- [ ]  Profile photo: same clean headshot used on LinkedIn
- [ ]  Username: your name or name + nivy (e.g. @rahul.nivy)
- [ ]  Bio (copy this template):
    
    > Business Development | Helping [niche] businesses get more clients | Nivy
    > 
- [ ]  Website link in bio: [www.thenivy.com](http://www.thenivy.com)
- [ ]  At least 3–5 posts published before starting outreach (use approved content topics)
- [ ]  Account set to public

## Instagram Outreach Scripts

### 1A. First DM — Cold Opener

> Hi {Name}! Came across your profile — love what you’re doing with {business/page}.
> 

> Quick question: are you currently focused on growing your client base, or mostly through word of mouth?
> 

---

### 1B. First DM — After Story Reaction (Warm Opener)

> Hey {Name}, glad you liked that! I work with {niche} businesses on getting more consistent leads.
> 

> Quick question — is growing your pipeline something you’re actively working on right now?
> 

---

### 1C. First DM — After They Follow You Back

> Thanks for the follow {Name}!
> 

> I noticed you’re in {niche} — is getting more clients something that’s on your radar right now?
> 

---

### 2A. Follow-Up — Day 3 (No Reply)

> Hey {Name}, just following up on my message from a few days ago.
> 

> I help {niche} businesses get more qualified leads through outreach — happy to share something useful if you’re open to it.
> 

---

### 2B. Final Follow-Up — Day 7

> Hi {Name}, last message from me — don’t want to clutter your DMs.
> 

> If you ever want to explore how to get more consistent clients, just drop me a message and I’ll be right back.
> 

> Good luck with everything! 👍
> 

---

### 3A. Positive Reply Handler — They Show Interest

> Great to hear from you!
> 

> The easiest way to show you what we do is a quick 15-minute call — I can walk you through exactly what we’d do for your business.
> 

> Are you free {day} at {time}?
> 

---

### 3B. They Ask for More Info

> Happy to share details! To make sure I send the right thing — what’s your biggest challenge right now with {lead gen / clients / growth}?
>

## CRM Updates for Instagram Leads

Use the same CRM status flow as all other platforms:

New → Messaged → Replied → Interested → Booked → No Response

In the Platform column, enter: **Instagram**

---

## Account Safety on Instagram

- New accounts: maximum 10 DMs/day for the first week
- Never send the same DM to more than 3 people in a row without changing the opener
- Do not DM from a new account that has 0 posts and no followers — it will be flagged as spam immediately
- If Instagram restricts your DM ability: stop for 48 hours and report to supervisor
- Space your DMs throughout the day — do not send 20 in 30 minutes
