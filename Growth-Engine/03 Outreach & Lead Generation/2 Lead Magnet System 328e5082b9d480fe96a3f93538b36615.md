# 2. Lead Magnet System

## 

### Purpose of Lead Magnet System

- Build trust before selling
- Educate prospects about growth opportunities
- Position yourself as an authority
- Create curiosity about full system implementation

This is the **entry point of your entire funnel**.

---

### Roofing Growth Blueprint (PDF)

What it includes:

- Overview of how roofing companies generate leads
- Introduction to multi-channel growth
- Basic explanation of the 6D Growth Engine
- Simple actionable ideas

Structure:

1. Problem (inconsistent leads)
2. Opportunity (market demand)
3. System (6D framework intro)
4. Simple actions they can understand

Important:

- Keep it simple and visual
- Do NOT reveal full advanced strategies

---

### Growth Engine Diagram

Purpose:

- Visual representation of the system
- Helps client quickly understand the process

Includes:

- 6D framework flow
- Lead sources → conversion → jobs

Why it works:

- People understand visuals faster than text
- Makes your system look structured and professional

---

### Lead Calculator

Purpose:

- Show potential growth using numbers
- Help client visualize revenue opportunity

Example Inputs:

- Current leads/month
- Conversion rate
- Average job value

Output:

- Potential leads/month
- Potential revenue increase

Psychology:

- Client sees real numbers
- Creates urgency and belief

---

### Opportunity Report

This is a **high-impact differentiator**.

Includes:

- Search demand in their city
- Competitor visibility
- Missed lead opportunities

Example:

- “Roof repair near me” searches/month
- Number of competitors ranking

Purpose:

- Show real market demand
- Create urgency
- Make the problem tangible

---

### How to Use Lead Magnet in Sales

Step 1 — Outreach

- Offer blueprint with curiosity-based message

Step 2 — When They Reply

- Send blueprint + diagram + calculator

Step 3 — Add Opportunity Insight

- Mention missed leads in their area

Step 4 — Transition to Call

Message:

- “I can also show how many leads you're missing locally”

Step 5 — Book Audit

- Push for 10–15 min call

---

### Key Strategy (VERY IMPORTANT)

- Give **60–70% knowledge**
- Keep **30–40% hidden**

Why:

- Enough value to build trust
- Not enough for full implementation

Result:

- Client understands opportunity
- Feels need for expert help
