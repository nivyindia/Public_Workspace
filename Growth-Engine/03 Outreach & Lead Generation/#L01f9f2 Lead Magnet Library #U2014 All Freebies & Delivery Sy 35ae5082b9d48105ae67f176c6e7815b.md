# 📄 LEAD MAGNET #1 — VA HIRING CHECKLIST {#lm1}

**Format:** 1-page PDF (branded Canva template)

**Target:** US/UK founders considering hiring a VA

**Gate:** Email opt-in via [Tally.so](http://Tally.so) form

**Delivery:** Brevo automation — instant email with PDF link

## Checklist Content

```
✅ THE VA HIRING CHECKLIST — Nivy Digital

Before You Hire:
□ List every task you do in a week (time audit)
□ Identify tasks that don't need YOU specifically
□ Prioritize: which tasks cost you the most time?
□ Define your budget (10–20 hrs/week = $300–$600/month)
□ Decide: generalist VA or specialist VA?

During the Hiring Process:
□ Write a clear VA brief (role, tasks, hours, tools)
□ Ask for a relevant work sample or test task
□ Test English communication (async message exchange)
□ Check timezone overlap (min. 4 hrs overlap recommended)
□ Verify: can they use your tools? (HubSpot, Asana, Slack, etc.)

Onboarding Your New VA:
□ Create a welcome doc: company overview, values, tone
□ Set up communication channel (Slack/WhatsApp)
□ Assign first 3 tasks with clear briefs and deadlines
□ Schedule a daily check-in (15 min async update)
□ Review output at end of Week 1 — give clear feedback
□ Set 30-day trial milestones

✅ You're ready to scale!
Book your free VA strategy call: [Cal.com link]
nivydigital.com
```

# 🛠️ HOW TO CREATE & DELIVER LEAD MAGNETS {#delivery}

## Creation Workflow

1. **Design in Canva** → Use Nivy branded template (logo, colors, fonts)
2. **Export as PDF** → Save to Google Drive (public view link)
3. **Create [Tally.so](http://Tally.so) form** → Fields: First Name, Email, (optional: Company)
4. **Set up Brevo automation** → Form submission → instant email with PDF link
5. **Add to website** → Dedicated landing page OR inline blog CTA
6. **Connect to HubSpot** → via n8n: Tally submission → HubSpot new contact
7. **Tag in HubSpot** → Lead Source: "Lead Magnet" + which magnet

## [Tally.so](http://Tally.so) Form Setup

1. [tally.so](http://tally.so) → New form
2. Add fields: Name, Email
3. Thank you page message: "Check your inbox — your [magnet name] is on its way! 🎉"
4. Settings → Integrations → Webhook → paste n8n webhook URL
5. Share: embed on blog/landing page or share direct link

## Brevo Delivery Automation

1. Brevo → Automations → Create workflow
2. Trigger: Contact added to list "[Lead Magnet Name]"
3. Action: Send email → use template below
4. Delay: Immediate
