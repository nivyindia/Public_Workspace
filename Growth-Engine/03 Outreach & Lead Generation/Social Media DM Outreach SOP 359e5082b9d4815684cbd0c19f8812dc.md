# 💬 DM Sequence {#sequence}

## Step 1 — Warm Engagement (before DMing)

Before sending a DM:

- Like 1–2 of their recent posts
- Leave a **genuine comment** (not generic — actually reference the post content)
- This makes the DM feel less cold and increases response rates significantly

## Step 2 — First DM

> "Hey [Name]! I noticed your post on [topic] — really resonated with me. We work with [their type of business] helping them with [relevant service]. Would love to connect if it ever makes sense!"
> 

## Step 3 — Follow-Up (Day 5, if no response)

> "Hey [Name], just following up on my message — happy to share more if useful. No pressure!"
> 

**Stop after 2 DMs. Never triple-message a cold prospect.**

# 📊 Engagement-First Strategy {#engagement}

For building pipeline via social media without direct selling:

1. **Post valuable content** relevant to your ICP (e.g., "3 GST mistakes Indian SMEs make")
2. **Comment on ICP posts** with value-adding insights
3. **Respond to their stories/polls** on Instagram to open a natural conversation
4. **Engage in Facebook groups** — answer questions, establish credibility, then DM after value is given

**This approach converts 2–3x better than cold DM alone.**

# ✅ Response Handling {#responses}

| Response | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Positive / curious | Move to booking: send link or ask preferred channel |
| "How did you get my DMs?" | Be transparent: "I saw your profile/post and thought we could help" |
| Not interested | Thank them, wish them well. Remove from outreach list. |
| No response after 2 DMs | Archive the thread. Log in CRM. Revisit in 60 days. |

---

# 📝 Rules & Best Practices {#rules}

- Never mass-DM — platforms detect and restrict this behavior
- Always personalise — reference something specific to their business or post
- Never lead with a pitch — lead with relevance or a compliment
- Keep messages mobile-friendly (short, no long blocks of text)
- Do not use automated DM tools without platform compliance review
- Always track outreach in CRM — note the platform, date, and response
