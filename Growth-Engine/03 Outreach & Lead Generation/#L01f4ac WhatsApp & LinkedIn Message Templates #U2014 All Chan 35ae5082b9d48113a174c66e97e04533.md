# 💌 LINKEDIN DM SEQUENCES

## Sequence A — After Connection Accepted (24 hours later)

**Message 1 — Value First, No Pitch:**

> Hi [Name]! Thanks for connecting.
> 

> 
> 

> Noticed you're running [Company] in [Industry] — impressive work. Quick question out of genuine curiosity: what's the #1 operational challenge in your business right now?
> 

> 
> 

> (No sales pitch — I genuinely ask this to everyone I connect with. The answers are always interesting.)
> 

> 
> 

> — Abhishek
> 

**Message 2 — If They Replied (follow their thread):**

> That's a really common one actually — a lot of [Industry] founders we talk to mention the same thing.
> 

> 
> 

> We've helped a few solve exactly that using [relevant service]. Happy to share what worked if useful — no obligation.
> 

> 
> 

> Worth a 20-minute call this week?
> 

**Message 3 — If No Reply after 5 days:**

> Hi [Name] — just checking in. I shared something earlier about [their challenge] — happy to share how others in your space have solved it if you're interested.
> 

> 
> 

> Either way, wishing you a great week ahead!
>
