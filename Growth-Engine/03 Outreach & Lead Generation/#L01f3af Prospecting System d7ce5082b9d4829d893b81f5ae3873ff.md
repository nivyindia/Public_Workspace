# 🎯 Prospecting System

## 

### Purpose of Prospecting System

- Create a **consistent flow of new potential clients daily**
- Ensure outreach team always has fresh leads
- Build a scalable lead pipeline

This is the **foundation of the entire growth engine**.

---

### Lead Sources (Where to Find Roofing Companies)

Primary Sources:

- Google Maps (highest quality local leads)
- Facebook Business Pages
- LinkedIn (owners, managers)
- Yelp / Yellow Pages / Local directories
- Contractor listing websites

Secondary Sources:

- Instagram business profiles
- Local business groups
- Referral lists / associations

Priority Order:

1. Google Maps (top priority)
2. Facebook
3. LinkedIn

---

### Ideal Prospect Criteria

Target only businesses that:

- Offer roofing services (repair, replacement, installation)
- Have an active phone number or contact method
- Are already operating (not brand new)

High-Quality Prospects:

- Have website but poor SEO
- Low Google reviews or poor rating
- Not ranking in top 3 on Google Maps
- In competitive cities (high demand)

Avoid:

- Already highly optimized businesses (top-ranked + strong reviews)
- Very small or inactive businesses

---

### Data Collection Structure

Each lead must include:

- Company Name
- Owner / Contact Name (if available)
- Platform Source (Google, FB, LinkedIn, etc.)
- City / Location
- Phone Number
- Email (if available)
- Website link

Optional (Advanced):

- Google rating
- Number of reviews
- Notes (e.g., “not ranking”, “no website”)

Rule:

- Clean, accurate data = better conversion

---

### Daily Prospecting Targets

Per VA:

- 200–300 leads/day

Time Allocation:

- 2–3 hours/day for prospecting

Weekly Output:

- 1000–1500 leads per VA

Scaling:

- 1 VA → 6000 leads/month
- 3 VAs → 18,000 leads/month

---

### Lead Qualification Basics

Simple Qualification System:

Level 1 (Basic):

- Roofing business ✔
- Contact available ✔

Level 2 (Better Leads):

- Not ranking in top Google results
- Low reviews (<50 ideally)
- Weak online presence

Level 3 (High Priority):

- Has demand in area
- Has website but poor optimization
- Likely missing leads

---

### Prospecting Rules (IMPORTANT)

- Focus on **volume + consistency**
- Do not overthink each lead
- Speed matters more than perfection
- Always maintain data accuracy

---

### Output of This System

At the end of each day, each VA should have:

- 200+ new leads added to CRM
- Ready for outreach next step
