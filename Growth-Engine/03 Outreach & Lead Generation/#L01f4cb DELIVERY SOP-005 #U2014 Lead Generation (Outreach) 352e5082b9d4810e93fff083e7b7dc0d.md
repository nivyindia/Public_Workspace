## 📅 4-WEEK MONTHLY DELIVERY CYCLE

### WEEK 1 — SETUP & TARGETING

**Day 1–2: ICP Confirmation & Target List Build**

- Confirm Ideal Client Profile (ICP) with client:
    - Industry / Niche
    - Company size (if B2B)
    - Job title / decision-maker role
    - Geography (India / USA / UK / other)
    - Pain points the client’s service solves
- Build prospect list for Week 1 (minimum 100 prospects):
    - L1: LinkedIn search + manual scraping (Sales Navigator if available)
    - L2: LinkedIn Sales Navigator + email finder ([Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) / [Hunter.io](http://Hunter.io))
- Format prospect list: First Name | Company | Title | LinkedIn URL | Email (L2) | Source
- Submit to supervisor for ICP match check before outreach begins

**Day 3: Message Sequence Confirmation**

- Confirm approved message sequence with client (3-touch minimum):
    - Touch 1: Connection request with personalised note (LinkedIn) OR cold DM
    - Touch 2: Follow-up value message (3–5 days after Touch 1)
    - Touch 3: Soft close / booking ask (3–5 days after Touch 2)
- All messages must be approved by client IN WRITING before use
- Save approved messages in client delivery folder

**Day 4–5: Platform Setup & Warm-Up Check**

- Verify LinkedIn account health: connection limit not exceeded, no restriction warnings
- Set daily outreach limits (see limits table below)
- Configure CRM entries or delivery tracker for this month’s prospect batch

---

**Daily Outreach Limits (Non-Negotiable)**

| Platform | Daily Limit | Weekly Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn Connections | 15–20/day | 80–100/week | Never exceed — account ban risk |
| LinkedIn DMs (1st connections) | 30–40/day | 150–200/week | After connection accepted |
| Instagram DMs | 20–30/day | 100–150/week | New accounts: start at 10/day |
| WhatsApp Cold | 20–25/day | 100/week | Requires number list; must be opt-in sourced |
| Cold Email (L2) | 50–100/day | 250–500/week | Must use sending domain (not personal email) |

---

### WEEK 2 — OUTREACH EXECUTION & REPLY MANAGEMENT

**Day 8–12: Daily Outreach Execution**

- Send Touch 1 to 15–20 new prospects daily (LinkedIn)
- Send Touch 2 to all prospects who accepted connections in Week 1 but have not replied
- Log every action in delivery tracker: Prospect Name | Platform | Touch Sent | Date | Response (Yes/No/Later/No Reply)

**Reply Handling Protocol:**

- Positive reply (interest expressed): Immediately notify client + supervisor. Pass to client or Closer. Do NOT attempt to close without authorisation.
- Objection reply: Use approved objection handling responses (provided by client or from Nivy objection bank)
- Not interested reply: Thank them. Mark as Dead. Do not follow up again.
- No reply: Move to Touch 2 on Day 4–5 after Touch 1

**Day 12: Week 2 Client Update**

- Send weekly update: Prospects contacted | Replies received | Positive replies | Calls booked
- Include 2–3 sample reply screenshots (positive ones preferred)

---

### WEEK 3 — FOLLOW-UP & PIPELINE MANAGEMENT

**Day 15–19: Touch 3 Sequence + New Prospect Batch**

- Send Touch 3 to all Touch 2 non-responders
- Begin new prospect batch (Week 3 list): add 100 new prospects
- For prospects who replied positively but have not booked: send booking link or calendar invite
- For prospects who said "follow up later": add to 30-day re-engagement list

**Pipeline Health Check:**

- Total prospects contacted to date
- Reply rate (should be > 5% for L1, > 8% for L2)
- Positive reply rate (should be > 2% for L1, > 3% for L2)
- Calls booked (running total)
- Blocked / Dead prospects (do not contact again)

**Day 19: Week 3 Update**

- Send pipeline update to client: running totals, key positive replies, next steps

---

### WEEK 4 — FINAL PUSH & MONTHLY REPORTING

**Day 22–25: Final Outreach Push**

- Complete Touch 3 to all remaining prospects
- Close out month: mark every prospect as: Booked / Interested / Following Up / No Reply / Not Interested / Dead
- Compile final prospect list with status column — send to client for CRM entry

**Day 26–28: Monthly Report**

- Build monthly lead gen report:
    - Total Prospects Contacted
    - Total Replies
    - Reply Rate %
    - Positive Replies
    - Positive Reply Rate %
    - Calls Booked
    - Call Booking Rate %
    - Top Performing Message (which touch got most replies)
    - Observations: what worked, what didn’t, audience insights
    - Next Month Recommendation: ICP tweak, message revision, platform focus
- Submit to supervisor for QC

**Day 28–30: Client Delivery**

- Send monthly report + full prospect status list
- Log delivery in tracker

## 📊 KPI STANDARDS TABLE

| KPI | L1 Starter Target | L2 Growth Target | Escalation Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prospects Contacted / Month | 300–400 | 500–700 | < 200 (execution issue) |
| Reply Rate | > 5% | > 8% | < 2% for full month |
| Positive Reply Rate | > 2% | > 3% | < 0.5% for full month |
| Calls Booked / Month | 5–10 | 10–20 | 0 calls booked in Month 1 |
| Report Delivery | By Day 28 | By Day 27 | Day 30+ = SLA breach |

---

## 🚨 ESCALATION RULES

| Situation | Action | Escalate To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn account restricted or banned | Stop all outreach. DO NOT appeal without supervisor. Screenshot restriction notice. | Supervisor immediately |
| Prospect threatens legal action or spam complaint | Do not respond. Screenshot everything. | Supervisor immediately |
| Positive reply converts to lead (booking intent) | Notify client + supervisor within 24 hours. Do NOT attempt to close. | Client + Supervisor |
| Reply rate < 1% after 2 full weeks | Message review required. Do not continue with same sequence. Present 3 message alternatives. | Supervisor |
| Client requests outreach to a restricted category (e.g. financial advice, medical, political) | Do not proceed. Consult supervisor for compliance check. | Supervisor |
| VA account flagged for spam on any platform | Pause outreach on that platform. Switch to alternative. | Supervisor same day |
| Prospect list quality is poor (> 50% wrong ICP) | Stop building from same source. Re-confirm ICP with client. | Supervisor |
