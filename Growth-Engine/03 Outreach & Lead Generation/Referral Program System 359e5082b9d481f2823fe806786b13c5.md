# ⭐ Why Referrals? {#why}

- Referral leads close at **3–5x higher rate** than cold outreach
- Zero customer acquisition cost
- Pre-qualified — the referrer has already sold Nivy for us
- Builds loyalty — clients who refer become more committed themselves
- **Target:** 30% of new clients from referrals by month 6

---

# 🎡 Referral Program Structure {#structure}

## Reward Options

| Referral Outcome | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Referred lead books a discovery call | — (thank you message) |
| Referred lead signs as a client | 1 month free service credit |
| Referred lead signs for 3+ months | 1 month free + ₹5,000 / $100 cash bonus |
| Agency refers 3+ clients in 90 days | Dedicated white-label partnership deal |

## Program Name

> **"The Nivy Growth Circle"** — Exclusive to active clients and trusted partners
> 

---

# 🔗 How It Works (Client Facing) {#how}

1. Client receives a **unique referral link** after onboarding
2. They share it with any founder, SME, or coach who might need VA support
3. Referred contact books a discovery call via the link
4. If they sign: referrer gets credited on next invoice
5. Referral tracked via CRM tag in HubSpot: `Referral — [Referrer Name]`

## Client Communication

- Referral program introduced at **end of month 1 check-in call**
- Reminder sent via WhatsApp at month 3
- Acknowledged publicly on LinkedIn when a referral closes (with permission)

# 📊 Referral Tracking Process {#tracking}

| Step | Action | Tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Referral link created | Generate unique URL or UTM code per client | Bitly / UTM builder |
| Referred lead enters CRM | Tag: Source = Referral, Referrer = [Client Name] | HubSpot |
| Discovery call booked | Notify referrer via WhatsApp thank you | Manual |
| Deal closed | Apply credit to referrer’s next invoice | Invoice system |
| Monthly report | Review referral pipeline | HubSpot report |
