# 📧 Domain & Inbox Setup {#domain}

> Never send cold email from your main domain. Use a secondary sending domain to protect brand reputation.
> 

## Setup Steps

1. **Buy secondary domain** — e.g., `getnivy.com` or `nivyteam.com` (similar to main brand)
2. **Set up 2–3 inboxes** on that domain (e.g., `alex@`, `sarah@`, `team@`)
3. **Add DNS records:** SPF, DKIM, DMARC (guides in Apollo)
4. **Warm up inboxes** for 3–4 weeks before sending cold email
    - Use **Mailreach** or **Warmup Inbox** tool
    - Start at 5 emails/day → ramp to 50 over 4 weeks
5. **Monitor reputation** weekly via Google Postmaster Tools

## Warm-Up Schedule

| Week | Daily Volume |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1 | 5–10 warm-up emails/day |
| Week 2 | 15–20 warm-up emails/day |
| Week 3 | 30–40 warm-up emails/day |
| Week 4 | 50+ — ready to send campaigns |

# 🚀 Apollo Sequence Setup {#apollo}

## Creating a Sequence in Apollo

1. Go to **Sequences** → **New Sequence**
2. Name: `[ICP]-[Market]-[Date]` (e.g., `Founder-US-May2026`)
3. Set sending window: Mon–Fri, 9am–11am local time of recipient
4. Set daily cap per mailbox: 50–100
5. Add emails (Step 1 → Step 4 with delays below)
6. Turn on **A/B testing** for subject lines
7. Add leads from cleaned, validated list
8. Launch and monitor

## Sequence Timing

```
Email 1 (Day 0):    Initial outreach
Email 2 (Day 3):    Follow-up #1 — add value
Email 3 (Day 7):    Follow-up #2 — social proof
Email 4 (Day 14):   Break-up email — last attempt
```

# ⚠️ Sending Rules & Limits {#rules}

- **Max 50–100 emails/day per warmed inbox**
- **Never send on weekends** (lower open rates, higher spam flags)
- **Unsubscribe link mandatory** in every email footer
- **Personalise at minimum:** First name, company name, one specific detail
- **Do not use spam trigger words:** free, guarantee, make money, 100%, urgent, click here
- **Monitor bounce rate:** If above 3%, pause and re-validate list
- **Monitor spam complaints:** If above 0.1%, pause sequence immediately

# 📊 Tracking & Optimisation {#tracking}

## Weekly Review Checklist

- [ ]  Open rate vs. target (40–60%)

- [ ]  Bounce rate (must stay under 3%)
- [ ]  Best-performing subject line this week
- [ ]  Sequences paused or flagged
- [ ]  New leads added to sequences
