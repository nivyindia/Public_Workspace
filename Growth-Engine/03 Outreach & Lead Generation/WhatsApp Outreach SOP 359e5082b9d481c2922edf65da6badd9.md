# 📱 When to Use WhatsApp {#when}

WhatsApp outreach is most effective:

- **In India and UAE** — WhatsApp is the primary business communication channel
- **As a warm follow-up** — after a LinkedIn connection or cold email has been sent
- **For referral-based leads** — when you have a mutual contact or warm intro
- **Never as a cold first-touch in UK/US** — too invasive in those markets

---

# 🔍 Finding Prospects on WhatsApp {#finding}

1. Source number from LinkedIn (often listed in contact info)
2. Source from business website (contact/footer)
3. Source from Google My Business listing
4. Receive via referral or warm intro
5. Never purchase or scrape WhatsApp numbers — violates ToS and burns reputation

# 💬 Message Sequence {#sequence}

## Day 1 — First Contact

> "Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] from Nivy Digital. We help [their industry] businesses with [relevant service — accounting/IT/marketing]. I noticed [something specific about their business]. Would you be open to a quick 15-min call to see if we can add value? No pressure at all."
> 

**Rules:**

- Keep it under 3 sentences
- Always personalise the [something specific] detail
- End with a low-commitment ask

## Day 4 — Follow-Up 1 (if no reply)

> "Hi [Name], just following up on my message from earlier this week. Happy to share more about what we do if useful. Let me know!"
> 

## Day 10 — Follow-Up 2 (final)

> "Hi [Name], I'll leave it here — just wanted to say the offer still stands if you ever need support with [service]. Have a great week!"
> 

**After 2 follow-ups — stop. Mark as cold in CRM.**

# 📝 Tone & Rules {#rules}

- Always be **professional but conversational** — not robotic
- Never use **all caps, excessive emojis, or sales-heavy language**
- Always use **first name** — never "Dear Sir/Madam"
- Messages must be **short** — WhatsApp is not for long paragraphs
- Use **voice notes** only if you have an existing relationship
- Send between **10am–12pm or 2pm–4pm local time** — never early morning or evening

---

# ✅ Response Handling {#responses}

| Response Type | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Interested / "tell me more" | Move to booking: send [Cal.com](http://Cal.com) link |
| "Not now but later" | Set CRM reminder for 30 days, follow up |
| "Not interested" | Reply politely: "No problem! Thank you for your time." Mark as lost. |
| No response after Sequence | Mark as cold, do not contact again for 90 days |
| Rude/hostile response | Do not engage further. Block if necessary. Log in CRM. |

---

# ⛔ Do Not Do List {#dont}

- ❌ Never send unsolicited media files (PDFs, images) as a cold opener
- ❌ Never send more than 3 messages in the sequence
- ❌ Never message on weekends in professional contexts
- ❌ Never send the same message to 100+ contacts simultaneously (spam risk)
- ❌ Never add prospects to WhatsApp groups without explicit consent
- ❌ Never use WhatsApp Business API for cold outreach without proper compliance setup
