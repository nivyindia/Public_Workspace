# 🔄 Lead Flow Diagram {#flow}

```
SOURCE (Apollo / LinkedIn / Inbound)
        ↓
SCRAPE & COLLECT (Apollo export / manual LinkedIn list)
        ↓
VALIDATE (email verification — Hunter.io / NeverBounce)
        ↓
QUALIFY (match against ICP criteria)
        ↓
ENRICH (add LinkedIn URL, company size, pain signal)
        ↓
CRM ENTRY (HubSpot — tag: New Lead)
        ↓
ASSIGN TO OUTREACH (SD-04 — Outbound Outreach)
```
