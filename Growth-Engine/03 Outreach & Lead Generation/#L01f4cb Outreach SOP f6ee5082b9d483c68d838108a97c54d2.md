## Step 15 — Client Onboarding

[Phase 15 — Client Onboarding](Phase%2015%20%E2%80%94%20Client%20Onboarding%20b70e5082b9d482a2a24381d92149f516.md)

Goal: Gather all information needed to start the project.

Steps:

Send onboarding questionnaire

Collect business information

Collect account access (tools, ads accounts, CRM etc.)

Define success metrics

Confirm communication channels

Typical onboarding items:

Target audience

Business goals

Brand assets

Past marketing campaigns

Logins / access

Communication channel (Slack / Email)

---

## Step 16 — Project Handover to Delivery Team

Once onboarding is complete, transfer the client to the fulfillment team.

Steps:

Create internal project task

Add client details to project management tool

Share onboarding notes

Assign delivery team members

Schedule kickoff meeting

Project management tools often used:

Asana

ClickUp

Trello

Notion

Internal handover should include:

Client background

Goals

Scope of work

Deadlines

Key contacts

---

## Step 17 — Kickoff Meeting

Goal: Align expectations before work begins.

Steps:

Introduce team members

Review project goals

Confirm deliverables

Confirm communication process

Agree on reporting schedule

Example agenda:

Project overview

Timeline

Roles & responsibilities

Communication process

Next steps

---

## Step 18 — Project Execution & Reporting

Delivery team performs the service and reports progress.

Steps:

Execute service tasks

Track performance metrics

Send regular updates to client

Provide monthly reports

Optimize results

Common reporting frequency:

Weekly update

Monthly performance report

Quarterly strategy review

---

## Step 19 — Client Retention & Upselling

Goal: Keep clients long-term and increase contract value.

Steps:

Review results regularly

Suggest improvements

Offer additional services

Renew contracts

Request testimonials or referrals

Examples of upsells:

Additional marketing channels

Higher ad spend management

Extra content production

Advanced analytics reporting

## Outreach Team Role Structure

| Role | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Lead Research VA | Find prospects |
| Outreach VA | Send connection requests |
| Follow-up VA | Send message sequences |
| Appointment Setter | Book meetings |
| Sales Closer | Close client |

This structure allows scaling outreach with multiple VAs.
