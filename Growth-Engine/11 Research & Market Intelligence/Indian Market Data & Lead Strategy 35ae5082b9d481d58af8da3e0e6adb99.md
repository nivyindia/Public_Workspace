# 💬 Outreach Approach for India {#outreach}

## Primary Channel: WhatsApp

WhatsApp is the dominant business communication tool in India. Most SME owners respond faster on WhatsApp than email.

**Sequence:**

1. Day 1: WhatsApp message (short, personalised, low-pressure)
2. Day 4: Follow-up on WhatsApp if no reply
3. Day 8: LinkedIn connection request + message
4. Day 15: Final WhatsApp message, close loop

## Secondary Channel: LinkedIn

- Target: Founders, Directors, CFOs of Indian SMEs
- Personalise with reference to their business/industry
- Follow up in DMs after connection

## Language:

- Use **English for all outreach** — it is the professional business language across Indian SMEs
- Hindi can be used in very casual WhatsApp contexts if you are confident in it

## Hook Lines That Work in India:

- "Are you facing issues with GST reconciliation or ITC claims?"
- "We help [sector] businesses stay 100% compliant without hiring a full-time CA."
- "We handle your accounting, IT, and marketing — so you can focus on growth."

# ⚖️ Indian Market Compliance Note {#compliance}

- Outreach to Indian businesses via WhatsApp and email does not fall under GDPR
- India’s Personal Data Protection (PDPB) law is evolving — stay updated
- Always offer easy opt-out in messages
- Never send bulk unsolicited WhatsApp messages using automated tools — account risk
- Use manual or semi-manual outreach for India channel
