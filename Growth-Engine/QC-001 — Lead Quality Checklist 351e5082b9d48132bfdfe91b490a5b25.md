## QC-001 — Lead Quality Checklist

Complete for every lead batch before marking the day's leads as submitted.

### For Each Lead in the Batch:

- [ ]  Lead is a decision maker (Founder / CEO / Owner / Director / Head of)
- [ ]  Lead is from an approved target market (US / UK / Canada / AU / UAE)
- [ ]  Lead's company is 1–50 employees (SME range)
- [ ]  Lead has a pain signal visible in their profile, website, or recent posts
- [ ]  Lead has at least one valid contact method (LinkedIn URL / email / phone)
- [ ]  Lead is NOT a duplicate (CRM was searched before adding)
- [ ]  Lead does NOT fail any hard disqualifier from KB-001
- [ ]  Qualification score (1–5) is entered in CRM

### For the Full Batch:

- [ ]  All required CRM fields are filled (no blank required cells)
- [ ]  Source column is filled for every entry
- [ ]  Date Added is today's date
- [ ]  Notes field has at least 1 line of context per lead
- [ ]  Daily count matches the number of rows added to CRM

---

## How to Submit

Once all boxes are checked:

1. Confirm total lead count
2. Include this confirmation in your daily report: "Lead QC complete. [X] leads added. All passed QC-001."
3. Manager will not review a lead batch that has not passed this checklist

---

**If you find a lead that fails a check — remove it. Do not submit below-standard leads.** Quality over quantity. A bad lead wastes outreach budget and poisons the pipeline.
