# 📧 Newsletter Strategy {#strategy}

**Frequency:** Weekly (every Tuesday 9am local time)

**Audience:** Leads, prospects, clients, referral partners

**Goal:** Stay top of mind, build authority, warm leads for conversion

**Tone:** Professional but human — practical advice, not corporate fluff

**Length:** 300–500 words max — scannable, short sections

---

# 📚 Email Content Pillars {#pillars}

| Pillar | Description | Example Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 💡 Tip of the Week | 1 actionable business tip | "3 ways to reduce your tax bill this year" |
| 🌍 Industry News | Relevant update for ICP | "UAE Corporate Tax update: what changed" |
| 💼 Case Study Snippet | Short win from a client | "How we saved a UK startup £4,000 in tax" |
| 🛠️ Tool Spotlight | Useful tool for SMEs | "Why we use [Cal.com](http://Cal.com) for booking" |
| 📣 Nivy Update | New service, team news | "We now offer Australian BAS filing" |
| 🎁 Free Resource | Lead magnet or checklist | "Download: 50-Point Website Audit Checklist" |

Rotate pillars across weeks. Don't always lead with Nivy news — lead with value.

# 📋 Newsletter Template {#template}

```
Subject: [Catchy, specific, benefit-led]
Preview text: [Complements the subject line]

Hi [First Name],

[1-sentence hook — what's the email about?]

[Short main section — tip, story, or update. 150–200 words max]

[📌 Quick link or resource]

[CTA — 1 clear action: book a call, download something, reply to us]

Have a great week,
[Your name]
Nivy Digital

P.S. [Optional bonus tip or reinforcement of CTA]
```

# 📝 List Building {#list}

**Sources:**

- Website lead magnets (primary)
- Cold email opt-ins ("reply to get our resource")
- LinkedIn followers who opt in
- Client onboarding (auto-subscribe with consent)
- Referral partner networks

**Rules:**

- Always double opt-in
- Never add people without consent
- Offer easy unsubscribe on every email
- GDPR/CAN-SPAM compliant at all times

**Target:** 500 subscribers by Month 6, 2,000 by Month 12

---

# ⚙️ Platform Setup {#platform}

**Recommended platform:** HubSpot (free tier works for <1,000 contacts)

**Alternative:** Mailchimp or ConvertKit

Setup checklist:

- [ ]  Custom domain email (e.g., [hello@nivydigital.com](mailto:hello@nivydigital.com))
- [ ]  Email signature branded and consistent
- [ ]  Welcome sequence (3 emails) live
- [ ]  Unsubscribe and preference center active
- [ ]  DKIM/SPF/DMARC configured for deliverability
