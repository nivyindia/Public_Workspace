# 🎨 Brand & Positioning Assets

## 

### Purpose of Brand & Positioning Assets

- Make your agency look premium and different
- Build trust before the sales call
- Increase conversion rates across all stages

---

### 6D Growth Engine Branding

Core Idea:

- Package your service as a **named proprietary system**

Name:
👉 **6D Roofing Growth Engine™**

Usage:

- In outreach (“We use a 6D system…”)
- In calls (“This is how the system works…”)
- In proposals (visual diagram)

Why it works:

- Makes your offer feel unique
- Increases perceived value
- Positions you as a system builder, not a freelancer

Assets to Create:

- Simple diagram (6 steps)
- 1-page explanation
- Visual flow (lead → conversion → jobs)

---

### Messaging Framework

Core Messaging Pillars:

1. **Problem**
- Inconsistent leads
- Dependence on referrals
1. **Opportunity**
- High local demand
- Missed leads in their area
1. **Solution**
- 6D Growth Engine system
1. **Outcome**
- 20–40 leads/month
- Consistent jobs

---

### Outreach Positioning

Do NOT position as:

- Marketing agency
- Service provider

Position as:
👉 Growth system expert

Messaging Style:

- Curiosity-driven
- Value-first
- No selling in first message

Example Angle:

> “I created a simple system that shows how roofing companies generate consistent jobs.”
> 

---

### Proposal Positioning

Your proposal should feel like a **growth roadmap**, not a service list

Structure:

1. Current situation
2. Opportunity in their area
3. 6D system overview
4. Phases (Foundation → Growth → Scaling)
5. Expected outcomes

Avoid:

- Listing tasks (SEO, posts, etc.)

Focus on:

- Results (leads, jobs, growth)

---

### Authority Building

Goal:

- Make prospects trust you before speaking

Methods:

- Share insights (LinkedIn posts, short tips)
- Before/after examples (when available)
- Simple educational content
- Case-style breakdowns

Quick Wins:

- Post 2–3 times/week
- Share small insights about roofing growth
- Show proof (even early-stage results)

---

### Key Rules (CRITICAL)

- Always talk in terms of **systems, not services**
- Keep messaging simple and outcome-focused
- Be consistent across all platforms
- Avoid overcomplicating branding

---

### Output of This System

- Higher trust before calls
- Better conversion rates
- Premium positioning
- Easier sales conversations
