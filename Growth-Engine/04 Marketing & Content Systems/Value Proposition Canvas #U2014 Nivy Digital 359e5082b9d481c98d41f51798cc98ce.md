# 👤 Customer Profile {#customer}

## Customer Jobs (What they're trying to do)

- Grow revenue without hiring a large internal team
- Stay compliant across multiple jurisdictions (US, UK, UAE, AU, IN)
- Build a professional online presence that generates inbound leads
- Automate repetitive tasks to free up founder/director time
- Find trustworthy, qualified service partners they can delegate to

## Customer Pains

- High cost of local CPAs, agencies, and IT firms
- Poor quality offshore services with no accountability
- Compliance confusion across international markets
- Inconsistent or non-existent digital marketing results
- No single vendor that handles everything end-to-end

## Customer Gains

- Cost savings of 40–70% vs. local equivalents
- Peace of mind from qualified, supervised delivery
- A professional, growing online presence
- Time back from delegating operational work
- One trusted point of contact across all functions

# 🗺️ Value Map {#value-map}

## Pain Relievers (How Nivy Digital removes pains)

- **Fixed, transparent pricing** — no surprise invoices
- **Qualified teams** — CPAs, certified IT professionals, trained VAs
- **International expertise** — US GAAP, UK VAT, UAE CT, AUS BAS, Indian GST
- **Full supervision** — we don't just outsource, we manage and QC
- **Single contract, single relationship** — no vendor juggling

## Gain Creators (How Nivy Digital creates gains)

- Packaged services that scale with the client
- Monthly reporting and performance visibility
- Automation systems that compound over time
- Proactive outreach and marketing that builds pipeline
- Long-term advisory relationship vs. transactional service

## Products & Services Offered

- Bookkeeping, accounting, payroll, tax filing, compliance
- Website build, app development, IT infrastructure
- SEO, social media, content marketing, ads
- Workflow automation (n8n, Zapier, ManyChat)
- Supervised VA outreach teams
