# 🎯 Content Strategy Overview {#strategy}

**Core principle:** Every piece of content we produce must:

1. Educate or help our ICP (not just promote ourselves)
2. Be optimised for SEO (long-term compounding value)
3. Be repurposable across 5+ channels
4. Include one clear CTA

**Content goal by Month 6:** 200+ monthly organic website visitors from content

**Content goal by Month 12:** 1,000+ monthly organic visitors + 20+ inbound leads from content

# ⚙️ Content Production Workflow {#workflow}

**Step 1 — Topic Research (Monday of prior week)**

- Use Ahrefs/Google to find keyword with search volume + low competition
- Add to content calendar with keyword + target URL slug

**Step 2 — AI-Assisted Drafting (Tuesday)**

- Use Claude/ChatGPT to draft the long-form blog post
- Prompt: "Write a 2,000-word blog post on [topic] targeting [keyword]. Include H2s, bullet points, a FAQ section, and a CTA at the end."
- Human review and edit for accuracy + brand voice

**Step 3 — Design Assets (Wednesday)**

- Create blog featured image (Canva)
- Create 5 LinkedIn post graphics from the blog
- Create Instagram carousel (Canva template)

**Step 4 — Schedule (Thursday)**

- Schedule blog post on WordPress/Webflow
- Schedule LinkedIn posts in Buffer/Hootsuite
- Schedule Instagram posts in Buffer
- Draft newsletter in HubSpot

**Step 5 — Publish + Engage (Friday–Monday)**

- Publish blog post
- Go live with social content
- Respond to all comments within 2 hours during business hours

# 🔄 Repurposing System {#repurpose}

**1 blog post → 10+ pieces of content:**

- 1 long-form LinkedIn article
- 5 standalone LinkedIn posts (one insight per post)
- 3 Instagram carousel slides
- 1 email newsletter section
- 1 YouTube script / talking head video
- 2 short-form tweets/X posts
- 1 WhatsApp Channel message
- Quotes pulled for Instagram quote graphics

**This system means 8 blog posts/month = 80+ pieces of content at minimal extra effort.**
