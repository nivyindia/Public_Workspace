# 🎯 Ideal Partner Profile {#profile}

A good Nivy partner:

- Serves the **same ICP** (founders, coaches, SMEs, agencies)
- Does **not compete** with Nivy Digital directly
- Has an **existing audience or client base** of 50+ relevant contacts
- Values **quality and reliability** (not just cheap)
- Is **active and engaged** (not a dormant business)

**Best partner categories:**

- Business coaches and consultants
- Web design / development agencies
- Accountants and financial advisors serving SMEs
- HR and recruitment firms
- Marketing agencies (content, PPC, SEO) needing VA capacity
- SaaS tools for founders (Notion, HubSpot, Calendly resellers)

# 📤 Outreach & Onboarding Process {#process}

## Step 1: Identify Partner

- Search LinkedIn for ideal partner profiles
- Check existing network for warm intros
- Review who our clients are already working with

## Step 2: Warm Up

- Connect on LinkedIn
- Engage with their content for 1–2 weeks
- Find a natural conversation starter

## Step 3: Approach

> "Hi [Name], I’ve been following your work with [audience type] — we serve the same people from a different angle. I think there could be a great referral/co-marketing fit. Would you be open to a 20-min chat?"
> 

## Step 4: Partnership Call

- Explain what Nivy does (10 min)
- Understand their client base and needs (10 min)
- Propose the simplest starting point (referral link)
- Agree next step and timeline

## Step 5: Onboard the Partner

- Send Partner Welcome Kit (overview, ICP description, referral link, commission structure)
- Add to Partner CRM in HubSpot
- Schedule first check-in (30 days)
