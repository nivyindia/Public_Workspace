# 🏷️ BRAND IDENTITY {#identity}

## Who We Are

**Nivy Digital** is a multi-vertical organization from Lucknow, India, operating across Education, Healthcare, Digital Solutions, Job Placement, Tax & Accounting, and social impact (Nivy Care Foundation).

**Nivy Digital (the agency arm)** helps businesses grow with:

- Virtual Assistant services
- Digital Marketing
- AI & Automation
- Lead Generation

## Brand Positioning

- **For:** SMBs and founders in India, US, UK, UAE, and Australia
- **Who need:** A reliable, affordable team to handle growth operations
- **We are:** India's most trusted VA & digital growth agency
- **Unlike:** Freelancer platforms (we're a managed, accountable agency)
- **Our difference:** AI-first processes + human accountability + global experience

## Brand Personality

| Trait | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| **Professional** | Polished, accurate, no typos, branded deliverables |
| **Approachable** | Warm tone, friendly language, never corporate jargon |
| **Expert** | Data-backed, confident, shows knowledge without arrogance |
| **Action-oriented** | Clear next steps, no fluff, gets to the point |
| **Global mindset** | Speaks to Indian and international clients equally |

# 🖼️ LOGO USAGE {#logo}

## Logo Versions Needed

- [ ]  **Primary logo** — full name + icon, on white background
- [ ]  **Dark version** — for dark backgrounds
- [ ]  **Icon only** — for social media profile pictures, favicon
- [ ]  **Horizontal version** — for email signatures, letterheads

## Logo Rules

- ✅ Use logo on white, light grey, or brand dark background
- ✅ Maintain clear space equal to the logo height around all sides
- ❌ Never stretch or distort the logo
- ❌ Never place on clashing backgrounds
- ❌ Never use drop shadows on the logo
- ❌ Never recreate the logo in different fonts

## File Formats

| Use Case | Format |
| --- | --- |
| Website, digital use | PNG (transparent background) |
| Print, high-res | SVG or PDF |
| Social media profile | JPG 400x400px |
| Email signature | PNG, max 200px wide |

## Recommended Palette

| Color | Name | Hex Code | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🔵 | **Primary Blue** | `#1A73E8` or `#0A66C2` | CTAs, headings, key UI elements |
| ⚫ | **Deep Navy** | `#0D1B2A` | Hero backgrounds, dark sections |
| ⚪ | **Clean White** | `#FFFFFF` | Page backgrounds, card backgrounds |
| 🩶 | **Light Grey** | `#F5F7FA` | Section backgrounds, subtle dividers |
| 🟠 | **Accent Orange** | `#FF6B2B` | Highlights, badges, small accents |
| 📝 | **Body Text** | `#2D3748` | All paragraph text |

## Color Usage Rules

- Primary Blue = all buttons, links, and primary CTAs
- Deep Navy = hero sections, dark backgrounds
- Orange = used sparingly — badges, "new" labels, small highlights only
- Never use more than 3 colors in a single design piece

# ✏️ TYPOGRAPHY {#typography}

> ⚠️ **Action needed:** Confirm fonts (Google Fonts recommended for free use)
> 

## Recommended Font Stack

| Role | Font | Weight | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary Heading** | Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans | Bold (700) | H1: 48–64px, H2: 36px, H3: 24px |
| **Body Text** | Inter | Regular (400) | 16–18px |
| **Accent / Callout** | Plus Jakarta Sans | SemiBold (600) | 14–16px |
| **Code / Technical** | JetBrains Mono | Regular | 14px |

## Typography Rules

- Headlines: sentence case (not ALL CAPS except for labels/badges)
- Line height: 1.5x font size minimum for body text
- Max line length: 70 characters (improves readability)
- No more than 2 font families on any page

# 🗣️ TONE OF VOICE {#voice}

## Our Voice Is:

- **Confident, not arrogant** — "We've helped 50+ businesses" not "We're the best"
- **Clear, not corporate** — "We manage your social media" not "We leverage omnichannel digital engagement strategies"
- **Warm, not casual** — Professional but approachable; no slang
- **Action-focused** — Every piece of copy leads somewhere

## Writing Examples

| ❌ Don't Write | ✅ Write Instead |
| --- | --- |
| "We leverage synergistic solutions" | "We build systems that grow your business" |
| "Our team is comprised of highly skilled professionals" | "Our team has handled 200+ projects across 3 continents" |
| "Please don't hesitate to reach out" | "Drop us a message — we reply within 4 hours" |
| "We are committed to delivering value" | "We deliver results, or we make it right" |

## CTAs That Convert

- "Book Your Free 30-Min Call" (not "Contact Us")
- "Get a Free Strategy Session" (not "Learn More")
- "See How It Works" (not "Click Here")
- "Start Growing Today" (not "Sign Up")

# 📸 PHOTOGRAPHY & IMAGERY STYLE {#imagery}

## Style Guidelines

- **Real people over stock photos** wherever possible
- **Bright, clean, modern** — avoid dark, cluttered, or overly corporate images
- **Diverse representation** — show India + international clients and contexts
- **Tech-forward** — laptops, dashboards, data visuals reinforce agency credibility

## Free Image Sources

- [Unsplash.com](http://Unsplash.com) — free high-quality photos
- [Pexels.com](http://Pexels.com) — free stock photos
- [Storyset.com](http://Storyset.com) — free illustrations (great for service pages)
- [unDraw.co](http://unDraw.co) — free customizable illustrations (brand color matching)

# 🚫 BRAND DON'TS {#donts}

- ❌ Never use Comic Sans, Papyrus, or decorative fonts
- ❌ Never use gradients on the logo
- ❌ Never use more than 3 colors in one design
- ❌ Never use unlicensed images (always Unsplash/Pexels/owned)
- ❌ Never publish content with typos or grammatical errors (use Grammarly)
- ❌ Never use ALL CAPS for body text
- ❌ Never post blurry or low-resolution images
- ❌ Never share client data or confidential info publicly

# 🖌️ CANVA BRAND KIT SETUP {#canva}

## Steps to Set Up in Canva

1. Canva account → Brand Kit → Create Brand Kit
2. Upload logo (all versions — primary, dark, icon)
3. Add brand colors (hex codes from palette above)
4. Set brand fonts (Inter/Plus Jakarta Sans)
5. Create templates for: LinkedIn post | Instagram carousel | Email header | Proposal cover

- [ ]  Proposal cover page (A4 or Letter)
