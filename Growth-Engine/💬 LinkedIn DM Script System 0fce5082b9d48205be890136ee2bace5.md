# 💬 LinkedIn DM Script System

## Phase 6 — LinkedIn DM Script System

This phase turns **LinkedIn connections into booked meetings**. The goal is **conversation → trust → call**, not pitching immediately on LinkedIn.

---

# Step 1 — Thank Them for Connecting

Send this **after they accept your request**.

**Message 1 (Day 0)**

> Hey {{First Name}}, thanks for connecting!
> 
> 
> Quick question — how are you currently generating leads for {{Company Name}}?
> 

Why this works:

- It’s **short**
- It’s **curiosity-based**
- It starts a **conversation**

---

# Step 2 — Qualify the Prospect

If they respond, ask a **simple follow-up question**.

**Message 2**

> Got it. Out of curiosity, are you looking to scale that this year or just maintain the current flow?
> 

This helps you understand:

- Budget
- Growth intention
- Need for marketing

---

# Step 3 — Soft Positioning

Now briefly mention what you do.

**Message 3**

> Makes sense.
> 
> 
> We’ve been helping {{industry}} companies generate more qualified leads through {{your service}}.
> 

Example:

> We’ve been helping roofing companies generate more local leads using paid ads.
> 

Do **NOT hard sell yet**.

---

# Step 4 — Offer Value

Give something useful.

**Message 4**

> I noticed a couple of opportunities that could help increase your leads.
> 
> 
> Happy to share if you're open to it.
> 

This creates **curiosity**.

---

# Step 5 — Book the Call

Now invite them to a call.

**Message 5**

> If you're open to it, we could jump on a quick 15-minute call and I can show you what’s working for similar businesses.
> 

Optional scheduling tool:

- Calendly
- Google Calendar

Example:

> Here's my calendar if you'd like to pick a time: (Calendly link)
> 

---

# Follow-Up System (Very Important)

Many prospects **don’t reply immediately**.

Follow-up schedule:

Day 2

Day 4

Day 7

Example follow-up:

> Just wanted to follow up in case my message got buried.
> 

Second follow-up:

> No worries if now isn’t the right time — just let me know 👍
> 

---

# Weekly Performance Expectations

From **100 new LinkedIn connections**:

- 40–60 accept
- 15–25 conversations
- 5–10 discovery calls
- 1–3 new clients

---

# Daily LinkedIn Lead Generation Routine

30 minutes daily:

1. Find leads using LinkedIn Sales Navigator
2. Send 20–30 connection requests
3. Respond to messages
4. Send follow-ups
5. Book meetings

---

# Pro Tip: Optimize Your Profile

Before messaging prospects, improve your profile on LinkedIn:

Headline example:

> Helping SaaS companies generate 30–50 qualified leads/month through paid ads
> 

Add:

- Case studies
- Client results
- Clear offer

This increases reply rates **2–3x**.
