## About Nivy

Nivy is a global professional services ecosystem with two specialist divisions:

| Division | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Nivy Advisory** | Accounting, Bookkeeping, CPA, Tax & Compliance |
| **Nivy Next** | IT Services, Digital Marketing, Web & App Development, Automation |

All LinkedIn activity — profile setup, outreach, content, and lead magnets — is designed to drive leads into the Nivy ecosystem.

---

## Profile Goal

A well-built profile does five things without you actively selling:

1. Builds credibility with prospects who check your profile after receiving a connection request
2. Represents the Nivy brand professionally across international markets
3. Starts conversations through content and inbound discovery
4. Generates warm leads via lead magnets and comment visibility
5. Books meetings through clear calls to action

---

## Outreach Readiness Checklist

Complete every item before starting outreach. This checklist is your go/no-go gate.

- [ ]  Profile photo uploaded (clean headshot, neutral background)
- [ ]  Banner uploaded (choose from approved options — see Visual Identity sub-page)
- [ ]  Headline set (choose from approved templates — see Visual Identity sub-page)
- [ ]  Custom URL set — format: `linkedin.com/in/yourname-nivy`
- [ ]  Location set to your assigned target market
- [ ]  About section completed (copy-paste template — see Profile Sections sub-page)
- [ ]  Experience section added (Business Development Associate | Nivy)
- [ ]  Services section filled in (see Profile Sections sub-page)
- [ ]  Skills added (see Profile Sections sub-page)
- [ ]  Featured section populated (website + Calendly link minimum)
- [ ]  Creator Mode enabled + 5 hashtags added
- [ ]  Minimum 5 posts published before outreach begins
- [ ]  Minimum 10 connections made
- [ ]  Education section added (any degree, diploma, or certification)

> Do not start outreach until every item above is ticked. An incomplete profile reduces your acceptance rate and can undermine the Nivy brand.

## Profile Funnel

Every section of your profile has a job. A prospect visiting your profile moves through this funnel:

Banner → Headline → About → Featured → Experience → Skills → Posts → Lead Magnets → Contact / Book a Call

Each section builds on the previous. By the time a prospect reaches your contact information or Calendly link, they should already understand what Nivy does, what problems it solves, and why they should talk to you.

---

## Keywords to Use Across Your Profile

Distribute these keywords naturally across your headline, about section, experience description, posts, and comments. Consistent keyword usage improves your ranking in LinkedIn search results.

**Finance:** Accounting Outsourcing • Bookkeeping Services • CPA Services • Financial Reporting • Tax Compliance • Payroll Management

**Growth:** Lead Generation • Business Automation • CRM Optimization • Marketing Automation • Cold Outreach • B2B Sales

**Industries:** Agencies • Roofing Companies • HVAC Businesses • Consultants • SaaS Companies • E-commerce Brands

---

## Rules & Standards

- Never represent yourself as anything other than a Business Development Associate at Nivy
- Do not promise services, pricing, or timelines — refer all detailed questions to your supervisor
- Do not deviate from the approved About section template without supervisor approval
- Do not use personal social media photos or casual photos as your profile picture
- Report any negative prospect interactions to your supervisor immediately
- Update your CRM every day — your profile setup work only counts if the leads you generate are tracked
