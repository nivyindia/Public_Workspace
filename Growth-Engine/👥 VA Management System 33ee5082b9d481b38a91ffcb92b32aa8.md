## Purpose

This system exists to build a reliable, self-running VA team that generates consistent lead flow without founder involvement. The goal is not just to hire VAs — it is to build a system that finds, trains, tracks, and replaces them with minimal friction.

---

## Hiring Sources

| Source | Quality | Speed | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| College / institute partnerships | High — motivated students | Slow — relationship-based | Low |
| LinkedIn job posts | Medium — mix of quality | Medium | Low |
| Upwork / Freelancing platforms | Variable | Fast | Medium |
| WhatsApp / Telegram groups | Low–Medium | Fast | Free |
| Internal referrals from existing VAs | High | Fast | Free |

**Ideal VA profile:**

- Basic to good English communication
- Comfortable using LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and email
- Willing to follow SOPs without improvising
- Consistent — shows up daily and submits reports
- No prior experience required — attitude beats background

**Red flags during screening:**

- Asks to work without reporting structure
- Cannot commit to daily hours
- Wants to negotiate targets before starting
- Has no access to a working laptop or phone

## Onboarding Process

New VAs follow the 8-stage training program. As a manager, your job is to make sure:

1. VA has access to all required pages before Day 1 — use the Supervisor Onboarding Checklist
2. A supervisor is assigned before the VA’s first day (1 supervisor per 10 VAs)
3. VA is added to the correct WhatsApp reporting group
4. CRM sheet access is granted and VA’s name is added
5. VA’s performance is reviewed at end of Week 1 — decide continue / retrain / release

**Decision gate at Day 7:**

| Final Score | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| 75%+ | Deploy to live outreach |
| 60–74% | 2 extra days coaching, then re-assess |
| Below 60% | Release — not a fit |

## Daily Task Structure per VA

| Task | Time | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prospecting — finding new leads | 2–3 hrs | 40–150 leads (stage-dependent) |
| Outreach — sending first messages | 2–3 hrs | 20–80 messages (stage-dependent) |
| Follow-ups — chasing cold leads | 1–2 hrs | All Day 3, 7, 14 contacts |
| Reply handling — managing conversations | 1 hr | All replies within 2 hours |
| CRM update | 30 min | 100% same-day accuracy |
| Daily report submission | 15 min | Before 7:00 PM |

---

## Performance Benchmarks

**Per VA (daily, fully deployed Month 2+):**

- 80–150 leads added
- 40–80 messages sent
- 5–10 replies
- 1–2 calls booked

**Per VA (monthly, fully deployed):**

- 20–40 calls generated
- 3–8 clients converted (depending on closer rate)

**Team benchmarks (10 VAs):**

- 1,000+ leads/day
- 300–400 messages/day
- 10–20 calls booked/day

---

## Management Rules

- Track performance daily — not weekly. Problems compound fast if unchecked.
- Give feedback within 24 hours of a mistake — delayed feedback is ignored feedback
- Replace consistently low performers within 2 weeks — do not carry dead weight
- Reward top performers visibly — weekly leaderboard, bonuses, recognition in group
- Never lower targets to protect feelings — the benchmark exists for a reason
- One supervisor per 10 VAs maximum — beyond that, quality degrades

## Communication Groups Structure

| Group | Members | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Announcements | All VAs | One-way: policy updates, reminders, leaderboard |
| Open Discussion | All VAs + supervisors | Questions, troubleshooting, general chat |
| Team groups | 10 VAs + 1 supervisor | Daily reports, targets, team-specific coaching |
| Management | Supervisors + managers only | Escalations, batch performance, hiring decisions |

## Scaling the Team

**Phase 1 — Launch (10–20 VAs)**

- 1–2 supervisors
- Manual Google Sheets CRM
- WhatsApp reporting
- Weekly leaderboard shared manually

**Phase 2 — Growth (50+ VAs)**

- Dedicated QC team (1 QC officer per 20 VAs)
- Partial automation: lead assignment via n8n or Make
- Dedicated recruiter (5–10 new VAs/week pipeline)
- Automated leaderboard

**Phase 3 — Scale (100+ VAs)**

- Full CRM system replacing Google Sheets
- Automated lead scraping and assignment
- Engagement tracking automation
- VA self-service portal for reporting
