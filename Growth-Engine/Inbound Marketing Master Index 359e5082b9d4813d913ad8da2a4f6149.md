# 🎓 Inbound Marketing Philosophy

Outbound gets you meetings. **Inbound builds the brand that makes outbound work.** Every piece of content we create:

1. Educates our ICP (financial pain, tech gaps, marketing failure)
2. Demonstrates our expertise (we know your world)
3. Creates a reason to reach out or engage
4. Builds a permanent SEO asset on our website
