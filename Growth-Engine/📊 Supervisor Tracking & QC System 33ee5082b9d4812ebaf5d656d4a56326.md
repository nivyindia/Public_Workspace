## 2. QC Scoring Sheet

Audit 10–20 leads per VA per day randomly:

| Date | VA Name | Lead Quality /10 | Message Quality /10 | Personalisation /10 | Accuracy /10 | Total /40 | Pass/Fail | Remarks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |

**Scoring guide:**

- Lead Quality: Does the lead fit the ICP exactly? Correct industry, role, location, company size?
- Message Quality: Is the script followed? Is the tone professional? No grammar errors?
- Personalisation: Is the message specific to the prospect or clearly copy-pasted?
- Accuracy: Is the CRM entry correct? Status, date, platform, notes all accurate?

## 10. KPI Scoring Formula

| Component | Weight | How to Calculate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KPI Completion | 30% | (Total Done ÷ Total Target) × 30 |
| QC Score | 30% | (QC Total ÷ 40) × 30 |
| Consistency | 20% | (Days Targets Met ÷ Total Days) × 20 |
| Discipline | 20% | (Compliant Days ÷ Total Days) × 20 |

**Final Score = Sum of all components (out of 100)**
