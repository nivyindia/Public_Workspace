# M02 — Tools

Pulls from the shared free/OSS stack in `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §2, plus stage-specific additions below. All pricing/limits marked "verify current" — free-tier terms change without notice.

## From the Shared Stack

| Function | Tool | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Reference doc hosting | Notion free tier | Source material (SD-05 Inbound Marketing Hub, Customer Acquisition Channels list) already lives here |
| Tracking selected channels | Airtable free tier or Notion database | Same tier already recommended elsewhere in the shared stack for tracker-style needs (e.g., M18's experiment tracker) |

## Stage-Specific Additions

| Function | Free / OSS tool | Notes | Paid upgrade path |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEO/organic channel research | Google Keyword Planner, Google Trends, Ubersuggest (free tier, verify current cap) | Already the shared SEO research stack used elsewhere in Track M | Ahrefs/SEMrush once ranking budget exists |
| Social platform reach check | Each platform's own free analytics/insights (LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube Studio, etc.) | No paid tool needed to check whether the ICP is active on a platform — public engagement patterns and free platform analytics are usually enough at this decision stage | Social listening tools (Brandwatch, etc.) only at much larger scale |
| Channel/ICP fit documentation | Notion (existing) | The channel-to-ICP scoring itself is a documentation exercise, not a tool-dependent one | — |
| Marketplace/directory research | Direct manual review of the relevant marketplace (Clutch, GoodFirms, Upwork, etc.) | Free to browse and assess fit; most professional-service marketplaces don't require payment just to evaluate whether they're a fit | Paid listing/premium placement only once a marketplace channel is actually selected |

## Notes

- As with M01, no tool in this list makes the channel *decision* — tools here support research (is the ICP actually here?) and tracking (which channels did we commit to and why), not the judgment call itself.
- Verify current free-tier caps (Ubersuggest daily query limits, any platform analytics gating) before relying on them for a high-volume research pass.
