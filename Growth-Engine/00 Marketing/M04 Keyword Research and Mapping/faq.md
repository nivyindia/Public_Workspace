# M04 — FAQ

**Q: Do we need a paid SEO tool (Ahrefs/SEMrush) to do keyword research properly?**
No. Google Keyword Planner, Search Console, Trends, Ubersuggest, and AnswerThePublic together cover volume estimation, existing-performance data, and long-tail discovery at no cost. A paid tool becomes worth it once the tracked keyword list is large enough (several hundred+) that manual cross-checking across free tools becomes the bottleneck, not before.

**Q: Keyword Planner shows a volume *range*, not an exact number — is that a problem?**
It's normal for accounts without active ad spend, and it's still enough precision to sort keywords into the 1–5 volume tiers this stage's scoring formula uses. Exact volume isn't necessary for prioritization; relative ranking is.

**Q: What if two keywords seem to target the same page?**
That's the cannibalization check in `checklists.md` — before assigning a new keyword to a new URL, confirm no existing page already targets it or a close variant. If two pages end up competing for the same term, consolidate them rather than letting both stay live.

**Q: How often should the Master Keyword Tracker be refreshed?**
Monthly for the competitor gap-analysis re-crawl; quarterly (or whenever M03 pillars change) for a full seed-discovery refresh. A stable niche with slow-moving competitors can stretch the cadence; a fast-moving one shouldn't.

**Q: Can AI just generate the whole keyword list?**
An LLM is useful for the brainstorming pass in M04.1 (long-tail phrasing, question variants) but cannot verify actual current search volume or current SERP intent — those require the tools listed in `tools.md`. Treat AI output here as an unverified first draft, not a finished tracker entry.
