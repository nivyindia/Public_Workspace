# M01 — Brand and Positioning Foundation

**Phase:** A — Foundation
**Status:** ✅ Built to pilot depth (2026-07-23)
**Feeds into:** Every downstream Track M stage — M02 (Channel Selection), M03 (Content Pillars), M08/M09 (Content Engine), M11–M17 (Social Channels) all reuse the positioning, voice, and visual standards defined here rather than each stage re-deciding tone or identity on its own.
**Cross-track input from:** Sales Funnel Stage 02 (ICP Definition) and Stage 03 (Buyer Persona) — this stage does not re-derive who the buyer is; it takes the already-finalized ICP/persona and turns it into brand voice, visual identity, and a positioning statement.

---

## 1. What This Stage Covers

M01 is the one-time (then periodically-revisited) strategic foundation that answers: who is Nivy Digital, what do we stand for, how do we look, and how do we sound. It is deliberately scoped to **brand and positioning only** — deciding *which channels* to run that voice on is M02's job, and turning it into a repeatable content system is M03's job. Keeping the line there means M01 stays a foundation document, not a growing catch-all.

## 2. Sub-Stages

1. **Company positioning statement** — the one-paragraph "for who / who need / we are / unlike / our difference" statement every other piece of copy traces back to.
2. **Mission, vision, and values** — the internal compass that keeps messaging consistent as new people (VAs, freelancers, AI agents) join and write on the brand's behalf.
3. **ICP/persona alignment check** — confirm the positioning statement actually speaks to the ICP and persona already defined in Sales Funnel Stages 02–03, rather than drifting into generic agency language.
4. **Key differentiators** — the 3–5 reasons a prospect chooses Nivy Digital over a freelancer platform or a generic VA/marketing shop.
5. **Visual identity** — logo rules, color palette, typography.
6. **Tone of voice** — the do/don't writing rules every piece of content (M09 drafts, social posts, outreach copy) gets checked against.
7. **Brand don'ts** — the guardrails that keep every VA, freelancer, or AI-assisted draft from producing off-brand output.

## 3. Why This Was Built the Way It Was

Per `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §4 (Batch 1), M01 was scoped as a **mining-and-structuring** stage rather than a from-scratch write: two internal Notion docs already existed — "Executive Summary & Company Positioning" (company overview, mission/vision/values, ICP, positioning statement, differentiators, market opportunity, core messaging) and "Brand Guidelines — Logo, Colors, Fonts & Voice" (identity, logo rules, color palette, typography, tone of voice, imagery style, brand don'ts). Both were marked `🟡 In Progress` / `⬜ Todo` in their own metadata — meaning some fields (final brand colors, final fonts) were still open decisions at time of writing, not confirmed facts. This stage's files are honest about that distinction: confirmed positioning language is stated as fact; the color palette and font stack are carried over as the *recommended* options those source docs proposed, flagged as pending final sign-off, not invented as if already locked.

## 5. What's Not Built Yet

The color palette and font stack in `templates.md`/`resources.md` are the source doc's own **recommendations**, explicitly marked "action needed: confirm" in the original — final sign-off (exact hex codes, licensed font purchase if needed, logo file production) is a real-world design task outside what this documentation stage can complete on its own. This stage documents the decision framework and the current best-candidate answers; it does not fabricate a "final" brand kit that hasn't actually been approved.
