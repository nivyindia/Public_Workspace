# M01 — Methods

Unlike most Track M stages, M01 has no "manual vs. automated" spectrum in the usual sense — positioning and brand identity are strategic, human-judgment decisions, not production tasks with a faster/slower axis. What varies here is **how the decision gets made and validated**, not how fast it gets executed.

## Method 1 — Founder-Led Direct Definition

- The founder (or founding team) writes the positioning statement, mission/vision/values, and differentiators directly, from first-hand knowledge of the business.
- This is how Nivy Digital's existing "Executive Summary & Company Positioning" doc was produced — it is marked `Owner: Nivy Digital Founder` in its own metadata.
- Best for: early-stage agencies (fewer than ~10 team members) where one or two people hold the full picture of what makes the business different.

## Method 2 — ICP/Persona-Backward Validation

- Instead of writing positioning in the abstract, start from the finalized Sales Funnel Stage 02 (ICP) and Stage 03 (Persona) documents and ask, for each positioning claim: "would this specific persona actually find this differentiator compelling?"
- This is the method this stage's content was built with — every claim carried over from the source docs was checked against whether it plausibly speaks to the ICP (founders, coaches/consultants, SMEs, agencies) rather than accepted at face value.
- Best for: any stage where positioning has already been drafted once and needs a sense-check before it's treated as final.

## Method 3 — Competitive Differentiation Mapping

- List direct competitors (freelancer platforms, generic VA services, traditional marketing agencies) and, for each, name the one thing Nivy Digital does that they structurally can't or don't.
- The existing source material already does a version of this implicitly (e.g., "Unlike freelancer platforms, we're a managed, accountable agency") — this method makes that comparison explicit and repeatable rather than a one-off line.
- Best for: refreshing differentiators periodically as competitors and the market shift, without redoing the whole positioning exercise from scratch.

## Method 4 — Style-Guide Codification

- Once voice and identity decisions are made (Methods 1–3), the actual *methods work* left is codifying them into a reference document (this stage's `templates.md` and `resources.md`) so every VA, freelancer, or AI-drafting tool has one source of truth instead of tacit "house style" knowledge only the founder holds.
- This is the only part of M01 that benefits from a repeatable process — everything upstream of it is a judgment call.

## Choosing a Method

| Situation | Recommended method |
|---|---|
| First time defining brand/positioning from scratch | Method 1 |
| Positioning already exists but hasn't been checked against the ICP | Method 2 |
| Positioning feels generic / competitors say similar things | Method 3 |
| Voice/identity decisions are made but not written down anywhere central | Method 4 |

No method here is "automatable" in the way M09's drafting methods are — see `automation.md` for the honest note on why, and the one place automation genuinely helps (distribution, not decision-making).
