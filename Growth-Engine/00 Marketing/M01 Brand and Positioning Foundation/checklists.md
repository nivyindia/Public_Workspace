# M01 — Checklists

## Foundation Sign-Off Checklist

- [ ] Positioning statement written in full "for who / who need / we are / unlike / our difference" form (not just a tagline)
- [ ] Positioning statement checked against Sales Funnel Stage 02 (ICP) — does it actually speak to the ICP segments defined there (founders/solo operators, coaches/consultants, SMEs, agencies)?
- [ ] Positioning statement checked against Sales Funnel Stage 03 (Persona) — does the tone match how that persona prefers to be communicated with?
- [ ] Mission, vision, and values written and agreed by whoever owns final say (founder, per the source doc's own ownership field)
- [ ] 3–5 key differentiators listed, each phrased as a real structural difference (not a vague superlative like "we're the best")
- [ ] Core messaging: at least 2–3 headline options and one 30-second elevator pitch drafted

## Visual Identity Checklist

- [ ] Logo: primary, dark-background, icon-only, and horizontal versions exist as actual files (not just "planned" — the source doc lists these as still-needed as of last update)
- [ ] Color palette confirmed as final, not just "recommended" (source doc flags this as action-needed)
- [ ] Font stack confirmed as final and licensed if needed (Google Fonts options carry no licensing cost)
- [ ] Canva Brand Kit set up with logo, colors, and fonts loaded

## Voice & Consistency Checklist (applies to every piece of content downstream)

- [ ] Tone-of-voice guide reviewed by anyone writing brand-facing copy before their first piece
- [ ] "Brand Don'ts" list reviewed — no jargon phrases from the do/don't table, no unlicensed imagery, no typos (Grammarly-checked)
- [ ] CTAs pulled from or modeled on the approved CTA list, not invented ad hoc per piece
- [ ] Imagery sourced only from the approved list (Unsplash, Pexels, Storyset, unDraw) or real/owned photos

## Periodic Review (not one-time)

- [ ] Positioning and differentiators re-checked at least once per quarter, or immediately if the ICP/persona (Sales Funnel Stages 02–03) changes
- [ ] Brand guidelines doc status field updated in Notion when a section moves from "action needed" to "confirmed" — keeps the source-of-truth accurate for anyone (human or AI agent) referencing it later
