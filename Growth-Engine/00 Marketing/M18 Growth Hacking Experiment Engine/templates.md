## Template 3 — Experiment Tracker (Airtable/Notion base columns)

```
| Tactic | Category | Impact | Effort | Priority Score | Hypothesis | Success Metric | Owner | Start Date | Decision Date | Result | Decision (Scale/Iterate/Kill) | Notes |
```

- **Result** — the actual measured outcome against the success metric.
- **Decision** — one of Scale / Iterate / Kill, per `checklists.md`.
- **Notes** — reason for the decision, confounding factors, or the adjustment made if iterating.
