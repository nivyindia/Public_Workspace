# M18 — FAQ

**Q: Isn't this just the same as the channel stages (M11–M17)?**
No — M18 doesn't execute a channel, it decides what to test next across all of them and tracks whether it worked. Once a tactic proves out, execution moves into the owning channel stage's own `methods.md`; M18 keeps the experiment record, not the ongoing execution.

**Q: How many tactics should run at once?**
3–5 per month by default, per the scoring method's own cadence. Method 3 in `methods.md` allows running several low-effort tactics in parallel, but high-effort tactics (score 4–5 on Effort) run one at a time since they usually compete for the same person's time.

**Q: What if a tactic doesn't fit neatly into 30 days — like a referral program with a Day-60 trigger?**
The 30-day window is a default, not a rule. Use the tactic's own natural cycle where one exists (see the checklist note in `checklists.md`) and note the adjusted window in the tracker.

**Q: Who decides scale vs. kill?**
A human, always — see `automation.md`. The workflow can flag when a decision is due and pull the data together, but scoring and the final call are judgment, not something to auto-approve.

**Q: Where did the tactic bank come from — is any of it invented?**
All of it is mined from your existing internal Growth Hacking Master List doc (Section SD-06), carried over as-is by category. No new tactics were fabricated to pad the list — see `resources.md` for the full attribution.

**Q: What happens to a killed tactic — does it get deleted?**
No. It stays logged with a reason (Template 5 in `templates.md`), so it isn't blindly re-tested in six months without knowing it was already tried.

**Q: Does every tactic need a paid tool?**
No. The Paid & Distribution category in the tactic bank is the only one that inherently costs money to run; everything else in the LinkedIn, Email, Content, Community, Automation, and Partnership categories can run on the free/OSS stack in `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §2.
