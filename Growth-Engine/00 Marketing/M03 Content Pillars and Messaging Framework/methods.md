# M03 — Methods

Same nature as M01/M02: this is a strategic definition stage, not a production task, so "method" here means how the pillar/messaging decision gets made and reconciled, not how fast content gets produced against it.

## Method 1 — Service-Backward Pillar Derivation

- Start from the actual service lines the business offers (per the existing "What We Do" breakdown: Virtual Assistance, Lead Generation & Outreach, Content & Social Media, Sales Support, Marketing Operations) and derive a content pillar per service line or logical grouping of service lines.
- This ensures every pillar has something real and sellable behind it, rather than a pillar that sounds good but doesn't map to anything the business actually delivers.

## Method 2 — Existing-Pillar Reconciliation

- Where more than one internal doc has already defined pillars independently (as happened here — SD-05's Content Pillars table and the Email Newsletter System's Email Content Pillars table used overlapping but not identical language), reconcile them into one framework rather than running two slightly different pillar sets in parallel.
- This is the primary method used to build this stage's content — see `README.md` §3 for which docs were reconciled and how.

## Method 3 — Keyword-Backward Validation

- For each candidate pillar, check that a real, searchable keyword or search intent exists behind it (using the same free SEO research tools named in `tools.md`) — a pillar with no searchable demand behind it may still be worth covering for authority/trust reasons, but should be flagged as such rather than assumed to drive SEO traffic.

## Method 4 — Content-Type Mapping

- For each pillar, decide which formats it's naturally suited to (a "Client Success" pillar suits case studies and testimonials far better than a how-to guide; an "Automation" pillar suits how-to videos and blog tutorials) — this mapping is what lets M09 and M11–M17 pick a format quickly instead of guessing per piece.

## Choosing a Method

| Situation | Recommended method |
|---|---|
| Defining pillars for the first time | Method 1 |
| Two or more docs already have overlapping pillar language | Method 2 |
| Unsure if a pillar has real search demand behind it | Method 3 |
| Content is being produced but the pillar-to-format choice feels ad hoc | Method 4 |

As with M01/M02, no method here is itself automatable — see `automation.md` for the one place automation genuinely helps once pillars are defined.
