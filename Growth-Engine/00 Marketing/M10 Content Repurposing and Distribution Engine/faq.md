# M10 — FAQ

**Q: Does M10 post directly to social platforms?**
No. M10 drafts and schedules into Postiz; each channel stage (M11-M16) owns actual posting cadence, engagement, and platform-specific growth tactics. M10's job ends at "approved content is queued."

**Q: Does every piece of M09 content need to be repurposed into all 7+ formats?**
No — the repurposing matrix (`templates.md`) is a checklist to make a deliberate Y/N call per format, not a mandate that everything becomes everything. A short tactical blog post might only warrant a LinkedIn post and an email segment; a flagship pillar page might warrant all formats.

**Q: Can the AI draft repurposed content directly from the blog's published URL instead of the M09 handoff packet?**
It could technically fetch and summarize the live URL, but the handoff packet (pull-quotes, key stat, suggested formats — see M09's `templates.md`) is deliberately curated by a human at publish time, which keeps repurposing grounded in what the writer actually intended to be quotable rather than whatever an AI summary pulls out.

**Q: What happens if a format's AI draft strays from what the source piece actually says?**
It gets caught at the human-review gate in `checklists.md` — "no claim beyond what the source supports" is an explicit check specifically because repurposing is a common place for drift/embellishment to creep in.

**Q: Is Postiz the only option for scheduling?**
It's the recommended default because it's self-hosted with no channel-count cap. Buffer's free tier (capped at 3 channels — verify current) or Publer are listed as fallback options in the shared tool stack if self-hosting isn't feasible right now.

**Q: How does M10 interact with M18 (Growth Hacking Experiments)?**
M18 can use M10's repurposing matrix as a source of experiment variants (e.g., testing two different hook lines for the same LinkedIn post) — M18 owns the Impact × Effort tracking of those experiments, M10 owns producing the actual draft variants to test.
