# M19 — Checklists

## Pre-Launch Checklist (Before Inviting Anyone)

- [ ] At least one validated offer/service exists — per the standing caution in the existing internal material, don't launch a community before there's something real to build it around
- [ ] Platform selected, matched to where the ICP already spends time (`methods.md`)
- [ ] Ground rules written (what's welcome, what's not, how disputes get handled)
- [ ] Clear entry point defined (one link, one place to join — not scattered across platforms)
- [ ] Founder has committed the time to be heavily present for the first 30 days

## Phase 1 — Seeding Checklist (First 14–30 Days)

- [ ] 10–30 initial members invited personally by the founder, not via broad promotion
- [ ] Founder replies to every message during this window
- [ ] Rules and tone modeled by the founder's own posts, not just stated in a pinned message
- [ ] No premature pitching — this phase is about proving the space is worth being in, per Method 1's standing caution

## Phase 2 — Growth Checklist (After Signal)

- [ ] Signal confirmed: members replying to each other, not just to the founder, before opening up promotion
- [ ] Community CTA added to relevant M09–M17 content (bio links, newsletter footer, post CTAs)
- [ ] Member-invites-member mechanic active (Method 3)
- [ ] Recurring event cadence established (Method 4 — AMA, office hours, or themed thread)

## Ongoing Moderation Checklist

- [ ] Rules enforced consistently, not selectively
- [ ] Spam/self-promotion from non-members or bad-faith members removed promptly
- [ ] No AI-generated engagement (fake replies, bot activity) used to simulate activity — the space's value is real human presence, not the appearance of it
- [ ] Monthly top-inviter/most-engaged-member recognition kept genuine, not gamed

## Lead Handoff Checklist (To M22)

- [ ] Buying-intent message flagged by a moderator (not auto-detected — see `automation.md`)
- [ ] Lead logged with source = "Community" so attribution is traceable
- [ ] Handoff follows the same inbound record structure every other Track M stage uses into Sales Funnel Stage 06, once M22 is built
