# M19 — Community Building

**Phase:** G — Community & Partners
**Status:** ✅ Built to pilot depth (2026-07-23)
**Feeds from:** M09–M17 (community members are recruited from content/channel audiences), M18 (community-adjacent tactics like group seeding and ambassador loops originate in the experiment engine)
**Feeds into:** M20 (Partnerships, Co-Marketing & PR) — an active community is itself a partnership/co-marketing asset; M22 (Inbound-to-CRM Bridge) — community-sourced leads are inbound records like any other

---

## 1. What This Stage Covers

M19 is about building and running an **owned community of your ICP** — a WhatsApp group, Discord/Slack server, or similar space where founders, coaches, and other target buyers gather around a shared topic, not a paid execution workforce. This distinction matters because your existing internal material covers two different things that both get called "community": (1) an owned-audience community of prospects/clients, which is what this stage builds, and (2) a structured internal growth-execution team with departments, leaders, and commission tiers, which is a resourcing/operations model, not a marketing funnel stage — it's referenced here only where it's directly relevant (the ambassador/referral-leaderboard mechanics), not adopted wholesale.

The goal of an owned community is trust and proximity: a space where the ICP self-selects in, sees you show up consistently, and moves toward being a client without a hard sell every time.

## 2. Sub-Stages

1. **Platform selection** — WhatsApp, Discord, Slack, or Circle-style community platform, matched to where the ICP already spends time.
2. **Seeding** — first 10–30 members, founder heavily involved (see `checklists.md` for the phased approach).
3. **Content & engagement cadence** — what keeps the space alive between posts (see `methods.md`).
4. **Moderation & culture-setting** — rules, tone, and who enforces them.
5. **Growth loops** — how the community grows itself (referral/leaderboard mechanics, member-invites-member).
6. **Monetization/conversion path** — how community membership eventually connects to a paid relationship, without the community itself feeling like a sales funnel.

## 3. Relationship to Adjacent Stages

- **M11–M16 (social channels)** feed the community with content and drive people toward it; the community itself is not a broadcast channel — it's a two-way space.
- **M18 (Growth Hacking)** is where community-growth tactics (e.g., "join 5 Facebook groups," "start a Discord community") are sourced and scored before being executed here.
- **M20 (Partnerships)** and this stage overlap at the edges: an engaged community is itself something partners want access to, and partner audiences are a source of new community members.
- **M22 (Inbound-to-CRM Bridge)** picks up any lead that surfaces from community activity (a member asking a buying question, requesting a call) the same way it picks up leads from any other Track M stage.
