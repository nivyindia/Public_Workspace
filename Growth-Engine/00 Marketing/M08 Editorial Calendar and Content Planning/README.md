# M08 — Editorial Calendar & Content Planning

**Phase:** C — Content Engine
**Feeds:** M09 (Long-Form Content Production — the calendar is the production queue), M10 (Content Repurposing & Distribution Engine), M11–M16 (Social Channels — pull publish dates from the calendar), M17 (Email Newsletter — pulls content to feature)
**Fed by:** M03 (Content Pillars & Messaging Framework), M04 (Keyword Research & Mapping — funnel-stage tags sequence the calendar)
**Build status:** 🟡 Built to pilot depth this session (Batch 3). See `references.md` for a data-gap note.

## Purpose

M08 is where keyword targets (M04) and content pillars (M03) become an actual publishing schedule with owners and dates. It's the coordination layer between "what should we write about" (upstream) and "who writes it, when, and where does it go next" (downstream into M09 production and M10 repurposing). Without this stage, content gets planned pillar-by-pillar in isolation and publishing becomes reactive rather than sequenced.

## Inputs

- Keyword-to-funnel-stage map (M04) — determines what mix of awareness/consideration/decision content to schedule
- Content pillars (M03)
- Team capacity (how many pieces per week/month the business can realistically produce)

## Outputs

- Live editorial calendar (dates, titles, target keyword, funnel stage, owner, status)
- Content backlog (approved but not-yet-scheduled ideas)
- Weekly/monthly publishing cadence commitment

## Sub-Stages

| # | Sub-stage | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| M08.1 | Calendar structure & cadence planning | Sets the realistic publishing rhythm (e.g., 2 blog posts/week) based on actual team capacity, not aspiration |
| M08.2 | Funnel-stage content mix | Balances the calendar across awareness/consideration/decision content so the funnel doesn't skew top-heavy or bottom-heavy |
| M08.3 | Editorial workflow & role assignment | Defines the brief → draft → review → publish pipeline and who owns each step |
| M08.4 | Cross-channel sequencing | Times blog publishing against social (M11–M16) and email (M17) distribution so a piece doesn't sit unpromoted |
| M08.5 | Calendar maintenance & backlog grooming | Recurring review to keep the calendar realistic, not aspirational-and-ignored |

## How This Stage Is Used

The calendar is a living document, reviewed weekly. New keyword targets from M04 and new pillar ideas from M03 enter the backlog (M08.5) before being scheduled; nothing jumps straight from idea to publish date without a backlog review, since that's how calendars become unrealistic.
