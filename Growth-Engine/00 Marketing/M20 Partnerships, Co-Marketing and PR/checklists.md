# M20 — Checklists

## Partner Identification Checklist

- [ ] Candidate matches the Ideal Partner Profile (`methods.md`): same ICP, non-competing, 50+ relevant contacts, active and engaged
- [ ] Candidate sourced from existing network, LinkedIn search, or client's existing relationships (per the outreach process)
- [ ] Not already a competing referral source for another active partner (avoid channel conflict)

## Partner Outreach & Onboarding Checklist (5-Step Process)

- [ ] **Step 1 — Identify:** partner researched and matched against the Ideal Partner Profile
- [ ] **Step 2 — Warm up:** connected on LinkedIn, engaged with their content for 1–2 weeks before pitching
- [ ] **Step 3 — Approach:** outreach sent framed around the shared audience, not a cold ask
- [ ] **Step 4 — Partnership call:** what Nivy does explained, partner's client base/needs understood, simplest starting point proposed (usually a referral link)
- [ ] **Step 5 — Onboard:** Partner Welcome Kit sent (overview, ICP description, referral link, commission structure); added to Partner CRM; first 30-day check-in scheduled

## Tier Assignment Checklist

- [ ] Partner assigned a tier (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum) matching actual commitment level, not aspirational
- [ ] Commission/benefit structure confirmed against the tier table in `methods.md`/`templates.md`
- [ ] Formal agreement drafted for Gold/Platinum tiers; informal link sufficient for Bronze

## PR & Media Outreach Checklist

- [ ] Strategy/angle session completed before list-building starts (what's the actual pitch-worthy story)
- [ ] Media list built: 20–50 relevant journalists/blogs/podcasts, researched not scraped generically
- [ ] Press release drafted and internally approved before sending
- [ ] Every claim in the press release fact-checked — no invented metrics or unverifiable claims, per the standing Track M no-fabrication rule
- [ ] Outreach sent with genuine personalization per journalist/publication, not a mass blast
- [ ] Coverage tracked as it comes in and logged for M09/M10 repurposing

## Affiliate/Referral Program Formalization Checklist (Method 5)

- [ ] Informal referral volume (Method 1) has actually outgrown manual tracking before formalizing — don't over-build early
- [ ] Program structure (commission rates, tiers, payout rules) designed and documented
- [ ] Platform selected and configured (see `tools.md` for the free-first-then-paid path)
- [ ] Onboarding materials and launch sequence prepared before inviting existing partners/clients to the formal program

## Lead Handoff Checklist (To M22)

- [ ] Partner-referred or PR-driven lead tagged with source (partner name or "PR — [outlet]") before entering the CRM
- [ ] Follow-up prioritized per the existing internal note that referral leads close faster than average
- [ ] Handoff follows the same inbound record structure every other Track M stage uses into Sales Funnel Stage 06, once M22 is built
