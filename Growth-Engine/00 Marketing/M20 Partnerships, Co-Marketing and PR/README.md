# M20 — Partnerships, Co-Marketing & PR

**Phase:** G — Community & Partners
**Status:** ✅ Built to pilot depth (2026-07-23)
**Feeds from:** M18 (partnership tactics sourced/scored there before execution here), M19 (community as a partnership asset and member source)
**Feeds into:** M22 (Inbound-to-CRM Bridge) — partner-referred and PR-driven leads are inbound records like any other; also feeds M09/M10 (co-branded content, guest content, press mentions become repurposable content assets)

---

## 1. What This Stage Covers

M20 covers three related but distinct activities that your existing internal material treats as one connected motion: **partnerships** (referral, white-label, co-marketing, integration, and community partners), **PR & media outreach** (press releases, journalist/podcast outreach, coverage), and **affiliate/referral program mechanics** where they apply to external partners rather than internal community members (that distinction from M19 is covered in `faq.md`).

The common thread across all three is that Nivy earns reach and credibility through someone else's audience or platform, rather than buying it directly — which is why they're grouped in one stage instead of three.

## 2. Sub-Stages

1. **Partner identification** — sourcing candidates against the Ideal Partner Profile (`methods.md`).
2. **Partner outreach & onboarding** — the 5-step process mined from existing internal material (`checklists.md`).
3. **Partnership tiering** — Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum structure, matched to commitment level.
4. **Co-marketing execution** — joint webinars, co-branded content, cross-promotion.
5. **PR & media outreach** — press releases, media list building, journalist/podcast pitching.
6. **Affiliate/referral program setup** — formalized commission structure for partners who actively refer.
7. **Tracking & reporting** — attribution of partner-driven and PR-driven leads.
8. **Handoff to M22** — any lead sourced through this stage becomes an inbound record the same way any other Track M stage's leads do.

## 3. Relationship to Adjacent Stages

- **M18 (Growth Hacking)** is where partnership tactics from the tactic bank (e.g., "partner with web design agencies," "partner with business coaches") are sourced and scored before a real partnership process starts here.
- **M19 (Community Building)** and this stage overlap where a partner wants access to your community's audience, or where a partner's community becomes a source of new members/leads for you — each stage owns its own side of that exchange.
- **M09/M10 (Content)** benefit from this stage's output: co-branded content, guest posts secured through partnerships, and press coverage are all repurposable content assets once secured.
- **M22 (Inbound-to-CRM Bridge)** is the eventual destination for any lead this stage generates, matching the Track M-wide rule that nothing in Track M closes a deal directly.
