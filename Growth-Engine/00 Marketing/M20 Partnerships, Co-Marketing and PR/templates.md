# M20 — Templates

## Template 1 — Partnership Tiers Reference

| Tier | Type | Benefit to Partner | Commitment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bronze — Referral | Sends leads | 10% MRR commission per referred client (first 3 months) | Informal, just a link |
| Silver — Active Referral | Actively introduces clients | 15% MRR + co-marketing access | Monthly check-in |
| Gold — Strategic | Regular pipeline + joint go-to-market | 20% MRR + white-label option + co-branded content | Quarterly planning call |
| Platinum — White-Label | Resells services | Custom margin structure | Formal agreement + SLA |

(Mined from the existing internal Partnership & JV Strategy doc — carried over as-is, not re-invented. Rates should be re-confirmed against current business economics before being offered to a new partner.)

## Template 3 — Partner Agreement (Simple, Bronze/Silver Tier)

```
NIVY [BRAND] REFERRAL PARTNER AGREEMENT

Partner Name: _______________
Date: _______________
Tier: Bronze / Silver / Gold

Terms:
- Partner refers qualified leads to [Brand]
- Commission: [X]% of MRR for first [X] months per referred client
- Payment: Monthly invoice credit or bank transfer
- Attribution: Via unique referral link or named introduction
- Either party may exit with 30 days notice

Signed: _______________ ([Brand])
Signed: _______________ (Partner)
```
