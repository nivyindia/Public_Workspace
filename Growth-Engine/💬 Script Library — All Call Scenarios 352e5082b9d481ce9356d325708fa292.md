## 🔹 SCRIPT 1 — CALL OPENING

### Branch A: Standard warm lead (VA handover)

> *"Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] from Nivy. [VA Name] mentioned you'd been looking at ways to [pain point from notes]. I just wanted to spend 20 minutes understanding your situation and see if we're genuinely the right fit. Sound good?"*
> 

### Branch B: Lead is already familiar with Nivy

> *"Hi [Name], good to connect. I know [VA Name] already walked you through some of what we do — I'm not here to repeat that. I just wanted to get into the specifics of your situation and see exactly where we can move the needle. Can I ask you a couple of questions first?"*
> 

### Branch C: Lead seems hesitant or distracted

> *"I know you've got a busy schedule — I'll keep this tight. If it's not the right fit I'll tell you straight. Fair?"*
>

## 🔹 SCRIPT 2 — DISCOVERY BRIDGES

*Use these to move naturally between discovery questions.*

**After Q1 (business overview):**

> *"Got it. So you're primarily serving [X type of clients]. What's the main challenge slowing growth right now?"*
> 

**After Q2 (main challenge):**

> *"That makes sense — we hear that a lot from [similar businesses]. Have you tried to fix that before? What happened?"*
> 

**After Q3 (what they've tried):**

> *"So the problem isn't that you haven't tried — it's that [reframe their experience]. Let me ask you this — if we actually solved [pain point] in 90 days, what would that do for your business?"*
> 

**After Q4 (articulate the value):**

> *"That's a real number — [restate their answer]. Last thing before I share what I think makes sense: what's your rough timeline and budget for this?"*
>

## 🔹 SCRIPT 3 — OBJECTION HANDLING (DECISION TREES)

---

### 🚧 OBJECTION: "It's too expensive"

```
IF they say price before you pitch:
→ "Totally understand. Before we talk numbers, can I ask —
   what budget were you working with for this?"
→ (They give a number)
   IF number is workable → match a lower tier
   IF number is too low → "Got it. Here's the honest truth —
   at [their number] we can't do what I'm describing.
   What we can do is [starter option]. Want me to walk you through that?"

IF they say price AFTER you pitch:
→ "What part feels off — the total, the monthly, or
   the return you'd expect from it?"
→ IF it's the total:
   "Let me break down what you're actually getting...
   [restate the deliverables and outcome]
   Does the value make sense, even if the timing is the challenge?"
→ IF it's uncertainty about return:
   "Fair. Let me give you a realistic picture of what our
   [similar] clients see in the first 90 days..."
```

---

### 🚧 OBJECTION: "I need to think about it"

```
IF they say "I need to think about it":
→ "Of course — what specifically do you want to think through?
   Is it the budget, the timing, or whether this is the right approach?"

→ IF budget:
   → Go to budget objection tree above

→ IF timing:
   → "What's the thing that needs to happen first before
      this makes sense for you?"
   → (Listen. Address the specific blocker.)
   → "If that wasn't in the way, would you move forward?"
   → IF yes: "Then let's figure out the timing together."
   → IF no: "Okay — sounds like there's something else. What is it?"

→ IF "just want to think":
   → "Totally get it. In my experience, when someone says that,
      it's usually one specific concern they haven't said out loud yet.
      What is it for you?"
```

---

### 🚧 OBJECTION: "I'll start next month"

```
IF they say "next month" or "after [event]":
→ "What's happening next month that changes things?"
→ (Listen)
→ IF the reason is real (budget cycle, launch, etc.):
   → "That makes complete sense. Can we lock in the start date now
      so we're ready to go on [their date] without losing another
      30 days?"
   → (Get verbal commitment + set a calendar follow-up)
→ IF the reason is vague:
   → "What would it cost your business to wait another 30 days
      on [pain point they described]?"
   → (Let them answer. Let the silence work.)
```

---

### 🚧 OBJECTION: "I've tried this before and it didn't work"

```
IF they reference a bad past experience:
→ "I hear you — what happened exactly?"
→ (Let them explain fully. Do NOT defend the industry.)
→ "That sounds like [X was the problem — tool issue / execution issue /
   wrong package for the goal]. Can I ask — was the problem the
   strategy itself, or who was running it?"
→ IF execution:
   → "That's actually the most common version of this story.
      The approach works — the execution is what broke it.
      Here's what's different about how we run this..."
→ IF strategy:
   → "Fair. Then let me ask you differently —
      what outcome were you actually trying to reach?
      Because if it's [X], we'd actually go about it differently."
```

---

### 🚧 OBJECTION: "Send me more information"

```
IF they ask for a deck / brochure / email:
→ "Of course. Before I send anything — what specifically
   would you want to see that would help you make a decision?"
→ IF they give a specific answer:
   → "Actually I can cover that right now — it'll be faster.
      Can I take 2 minutes?"
→ IF they just want to disengage:
   → "Totally fine. I'll send you something. Can I ask —
      is there a specific concern I haven't addressed today?"
   → (Address the real concern. Then send follow-up.)
```

---

### 🚧 OBJECTION: "I need to speak to my partner / co-founder first"

```
IF they need someone else's input:
→ "Of course. Can we get them on a call together?
   It usually takes 20 minutes and saves a week of back and forth."
→ IF they say yes: Schedule a 3-way call immediately.
→ IF they say no:
   → "Okay — what's the one thing your partner would need
      to hear to feel comfortable moving forward?"
   → (Get the answer. Address it. Give them a clear talking point to use.)
```

## 🔹 SCRIPT 4 — CLOSE LINES

### Trial Close

> *"Based on what we've covered — does this feel like the right fit for where you want to take [business name]?"*
> 

### Commitment Close

> *"Great. Here's how we move forward: I'll send the agreement today. Once signed, we schedule your onboarding call within 48 hours and you're live within [X days]. Does that work?"*
> 

### Urgency Close (genuine, not pushy)

> *"One thing worth knowing — we take on a limited number of new clients each month to protect delivery quality. I can confirm your slot today if you're ready. Do you want me to hold it?"*
> 

### Last-chance close (before hanging up)

> *"Before we wrap up — is there anything that would need to be different for this to be a yes? I'd rather address it now than have you sitting with an unanswered question."*
>

## 🔹 SCRIPT 5 — FOLLOW-UP MESSAGES

### 24-Hour Follow-Up (interested but no close)

> *"Hey [Name], good speaking with you earlier. Just wanted to send over a quick summary of what we discussed: [1-2 lines about their situation + the package discussed]. Let me know if any questions come up — happy to get on a quick 10-minute call to sort them. [Your name]"*
> 

### 3-Day Follow-Up

> *"Hey [Name], following up from our call. Have you had a chance to think through [the specific thing they wanted to consider]? Happy to answer anything. [Your name]"*
> 

### 7-Day Final Follow-Up

> *"Hey [Name], last follow-up from my side — I don't want to crowd your inbox. If the timing isn't right or priorities have shifted, totally understood. If you do want to revisit [the solution] at some point, I'm here. Either way, wishing you the best with [their business]."*
>

## 🔹 SCRIPT 6 — INDIA vs USA CALL TONE GUIDE

| Element | India Market | USA / International Market |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening energy | Warm, conversational, relationship-first | Concise, direct, value-first |
| Discovery depth | Can go deeper, more personal | Stay business-focused |
| Pricing conversation | Negotiate-friendly culture — hold firm politely | Frame as investment, ROI-focused |
| Decision timeline | Often fast if trust is built | Slower, may need 2–3 touches |
| Close style | Relationship close (*"Let's work together"*) | Logic close (*"This is the next step"*) |
| Follow-up frequency | Daily is acceptable | Every 3–5 days maximum |
