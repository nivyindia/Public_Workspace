# SOP-VA-008 — Instagram Outreach

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who Uses This:** VA Executive (assigned leads only)
> 

---

## Purpose

To send targeted Instagram DM outreach to qualified leads in creative, personal-brand, or agency industries — using natural, non-spammy openers.

---

## Pre-requisites

- Lead has an active Instagram business account
- Manager has confirmed Instagram is the approved channel for this lead
- KB-002 read and understood
- Nivy Instagram account credentials provided by Manager

---

## Daily Volume Limits

| Action | Daily Max |
| --- | --- |
| New DMs | 10–15 |
| Follow-up DMs | 5–10 |

> Instagram is stricter than LinkedIn. Exceeding limits risks account restriction. When in doubt, stay under.
> 

---

## Best Use Cases for Instagram Outreach

- Creative agencies, photographers, videographers, designers
- Personal brand consultants, coaches, speakers
- E-commerce brand owners
- Social media managers running client accounts
- Any lead who is clearly more active on Instagram than LinkedIn

---

## DM Format Rules

- Max 3 lines for the first message
- Casual but professional tone — Instagram is more informal than email
- Reference something specific from their feed or bio
- Do NOT include a link in the first DM — often blocked or marked as spam
- No emoji overuse — 1–2 maximum

---

## Message Templates

**Template A — After Engaging With Their Content**

> "Hey [Name] — love what you’re building with [brand/project]. We work with [type of business] to help with [pain area]. Would it be worth a quick chat? 👋"
> 

**Template B — Story Reply Method**

If the lead posts a Story:

- React to the story with a brief response
- Then follow up in DMs:

> "Hey — loved your recent story about [topic]. We actually work with a lot of [their niche] businesses on exactly that. Happy to share what we've seen work?"
> 

**Template C — Cold DM**

> "Hey [Name] — came across your page and noticed you're doing great work in [niche]. We help [business type] with [pain area]. Worth a quick conversation?"
> 

---

## Follow-Up Cadence

| Step | Timing |
| --- | --- |
| Message 1 | Day 0 |
| Follow-Up 1 | Day 5 (if no reply) |
| Follow-Up 2 (Final) | Day 12 (if no reply) |

After 2 follow-ups with no reply → Mark as **Dead** in CRM.

---

## Reply Handling

| Reply | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Positive / Curious | CRM → Interested. Escalate to Manager within 30 mins. |
| Not interested | "No worries at all! Feel free to reach out anytime." Mark Dead. |
| No reply after 2 follow-ups | Mark Dead. |

---

## Output Standard

- 10–15 new DMs daily within limits
- CRM updated same day
- Positive replies escalated within 30 minutes

---

## Common Mistakes

- Sending links in the first DM
- Using the same template word-for-word for all leads
- Messaging leads who clearly have nothing in common with Nivy's ICP
- Not using the story reply method when a lead posts relevant content — this has a much higher open rate

---

## QC Checklist

- [ ]  Daily volume within limits
- [ ]  Opener references something specific
- [ ]  No links in first message
- [ ]  CRM updated same day
- [ ]  Positive replies escalated within 30 minutes
