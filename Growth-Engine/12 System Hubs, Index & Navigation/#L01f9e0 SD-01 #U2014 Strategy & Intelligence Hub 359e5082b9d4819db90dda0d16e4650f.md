# 📊 SALES & MARKETING MASTER PLAN {#master-plan}

## The 3-Engine Growth Model

```
Engine 1: OUTBOUND (Active hunting)
└─ LinkedIn outreach + cold email + WhatsApp
└─ Target: 500-2,000 touches/month
└─ Expected: 1-3% response rate = 5-60 leads/month

Engine 2: INBOUND (Passive attraction)
└─ Website SEO + directory listings + content
└─ Target: grow from 0 to 5,000 visitors/month by Month 12
└─ Expected: 1% conversion = 50 leads/month at full velocity

Engine 3: REFERRAL (Amplification)
└─ Client referral program + partnerships
└─ Target: 20% of new clients from referrals by Month 6
└─ Lowest CAC channel
```

| Week | Priority Actions |
