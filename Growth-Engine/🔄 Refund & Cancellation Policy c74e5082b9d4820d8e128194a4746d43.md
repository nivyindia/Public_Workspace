# 🔄 Refund & Cancellation Policy

### Refund Eligibility

- **Strategy & Planning Phase:** Full refund available within 3 business days of payment if no work has commenced.
- **Work In Progress:** Once work has begun, refunds are provided on a pro-rata basis for uncompleted deliverables only.
- **Completed Deliverables:** No refunds are issued for completed work, approved deliverables, or services already rendered.
- **Performance-Based Services:** No refunds for SEO, PPC, or other performance marketing services after the initial 14-day period, as these require sustained effort to show results.
- **Dissatisfaction Claims:** If deliverables don't meet agreed specifications, clients must report issues within 7 days of delivery for review and potential remediation before refund consideration.

### Cancellation Terms

- **Notice Period Requirements:**
    - Monthly retainers: 30 days written notice required
    - Quarterly packages: 45 days written notice required
    - Annual contracts: 60 days written notice required
- **Early Termination Fees:**
    - For contracts under 6 months: 50% of remaining contract value
    - For contracts over 6 months: 30% of remaining contract value or the equivalent of 2 months' service fee, whichever is greater
- **Project-Based Work:** Cancellation after project commencement will result in billing for all completed work plus 25% of remaining project value as compensation for resource allocation.
- **Rush Projects:** Projects designated as "rush" or "priority" incur 75% cancellation fee regardless of completion status.

### Non-Refundable Expenses

- **Ad Spend:** All media buying and advertising spend processed through platforms is non-refundable once deployed.
- **Third-Party Tools & Subscriptions:** Costs for software licenses, stock media, plugins, or SaaS tools purchased specifically for client projects are non-refundable.
- **Domain & Hosting Fees:** All domain registration, renewal, and hosting fees are non-refundable.
- **Setup & Onboarding Fees:** Initial setup, onboarding, and account configuration fees are non-refundable.
- **Subcontractor Services:** Specialized services from third-party vendors (photographers, videographers, translators) are non-refundable once contracted.
- **Paid Analytics Tools:** Subscription costs for analytics, reporting, or research tools dedicated to client campaigns.
