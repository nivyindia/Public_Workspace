# 90-Day VA Training & Execution Schedule

Here's the full 90-day schedule built from your targets doc, daily schedule format, Notion material, and Daily Reporting System — with outreach starting from Day 1.

---

### Phase 1 — Warmup (Days 1–15)

*Focus: Setup + first live outreach. Targets: 2→15 connections/day, 5–15 messages/day*

**Every day follows this structure:**

| Time | Block | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00–10:20 | Morning Brief | Supervisor assigns daily targets + KPI review |
| 10:20–10:50 | Micro Learning | Topic of the day (taught with examples) |
| 10:50–11:10 | Setup | Login, open CRM, open tracking sheet |
| 11:10–1:00 | Work Block 1 | Execution (see daily topic below) |
| 1:00–1:15 | Report 1 | Submit checkpoint — leads/messages so far |
| 1:15–1:30 | Break | — |
| 1:30–2:45 | Work Block 2 | Execution continued |
| 2:45–3:45 | Lunch | — |
| 3:45–4:45 | Work Block 3 | Good vs bad client identification (25 leads) |
| 4:45–5:00 | Report 2 | Submit work, supervisor reviews |
| 5:00–5:15 | Break | — |
| 5:15–6:15 | Work Block 4 | Fix errors, improve lead quality |
| 6:15–6:45 | Final QC | Supervisor deep quality check |
| 6:45–7:00 | Final Report | Submit daily report (leads, messages, replies, meetings, sales, revenue) |

---

**Day 1** — Micro Learning: Company overview, VA role, partnership agreement, reporting system explained. Work: Profile setup — LinkedIn photo, headline, bio, keywords. Target: Profile 100% complete.

**Day 2** — Micro Learning: Performance-first mindset, 90-day ramp model. Work: Featured section, lead magnets, hashtags, comment strategy. Target: Profile fully optimised.

**Day 3** — Micro Learning: ICP — who is our ideal client, niche targeting (roofing model), red flags. Work: Find 30 leads manually using Google + LinkedIn company-to-owner method. Messages: 5.

**Day 4** — Micro Learning: Warm lead master table, hiring signal targeting. Work: Find 30 leads via LinkedIn Jobs + demand job posts. Messages: 8–10. Connections: 5.

**Day 5** — Micro Learning: LinkedIn connection request strategy, safe daily limits. Work: Send 10–15 connection requests with personalised notes. 15 messages. Report lead quality score.

**Day 6** — Micro Learning: LinkedIn DM system — message 1 and 2 of the 6-message sequence. Work: Send 15 messages across LinkedIn. Begin tracking all replies in sheet.

**Day 7** — Micro Learning: Content engagement strategy — warm up prospects before DMing. Work: Like and comment on 20 prospect posts. Send 15 DMs. Connections: 10. Weekly review by supervisor.

**Day 8** — Micro Learning: Facebook Group strategy. Work: Join 5 relevant groups. Post in 2. Send 10 Facebook DMs. LinkedIn: 10 messages. Total: 20–25 touchpoints.

**Day 9** — Micro Learning: WhatsApp Business DM scripts. Work: WhatsApp outreach to 10 contacts. LinkedIn: 15 messages. Facebook: 10 DMs. Total: 35 touchpoints.

**Day 10** — Micro Learning: Instagram DM scripts. Work: Instagram outreach — 10 DMs. Combine with LinkedIn (15) and Facebook (10). Total touchpoints: 35+.

**Day 11** — Micro Learning: Telegram group outreach + Google Maps local targeting. Work: Join 3 Telegram groups, post once. Extract 20 leads from Google Maps. Send 10 messages. Total: 30 touchpoints.

**Day 12** — Micro Learning: Upwork and Fiverr bidding, proposal writing basics. Work: Submit 2–3 Upwork proposals. Find 5 Fiverr job posts to respond to. LinkedIn: 15 messages.

**Day 13** — Micro Learning: Cold email setup — Hunter.io, Snov.io, email finder tools. Work: Find 20 email addresses. Write 5 cold emails using the approved template. Send all 5.

**Day 14** — Micro Learning: General outreach tracker — how to log DM / Email / Proposal in one place. Work: Full outreach day — LinkedIn (15), Email (5), Facebook (10), WhatsApp (5). Total: 35. Submit tracker.

**Day 15** — Phase 1 review. Supervisor checks: profile quality, lead database quality, tracker accuracy, all daily reports. Pass minimum = 200 total messages sent, tracker fully updated, no fake leads. Underperformers retraining begins.

### Phase 2 — Controlled Outreach (Days 16–45)

*Focus: Scale outreach + start qualifying leads. Targets: 15–20 connections/day, 40–60 messages/day, 0–1 meetings/week*

**Days 16–20 — Lead Qualification**

Day 16: BANT, MEDDIC, CHAMP qualification frameworks. Work: Apply to 20 existing leads in sheet. Score each one.

Day 17: Quality control scenario table — lead scoring and penalties. Work: QC your own leads, remove bad ones, add 30 new qualified leads.

Day 18: 3 questions to ask every warm lead. Identify tyre-kicker vs real buyer. Work: Reply to all warm leads with qualifying questions.

Day 19: What information to collect before passing to closer. Work: Build handover brief template. Practice filling it for 3 warm leads.

Day 20: Appointment setting flow (Phase 9) — moving from interest to meeting in one message. Work: Attempt to book meetings with 3 warm leads. Report outcomes.

**Days 21–30 — Appointment Setting & Follow-Up**

Day 21: Calendar link setup, meeting confirmation message. Work: Send calendar link to 2 warm leads. Confirm meeting.

Day 22: Follow-up system — Phase 12, 3-touch rule (Day 0, Day 3, Day 7). Work: Run follow-up sequence on all non-responders from Week 1–2.

Day 23: Handling "let me think about it" and ghost scenarios. Work: Re-engage 5 ghosted leads with follow-up message 2.

Day 24: Meeting handover SOP (Phase 11) — the 5-field briefing. Work: Write full handover briefs for any meetings booked this week.

Day 25: Role-play drill — VA briefs supervisor as the closer. Graded. Feedback session.

Days 26–30: Full outreach execution. 40–60 messages/day across all platforms. 15–25 follow-ups/day. 10–15 emails/day. Weekly target: 2–4 meetings booked. Daily report every evening.

**Days 31–45 — Scale & Optimise**

Each day: 50–80 total touchpoints. Morning brief assigns platform focus (rotate: LinkedIn, Email, Facebook, Instagram daily). Micro learning covers: Apollo.io cold email system, email sequence automation, engagement techniques master table, mistakes and optimisation (10 common errors), how to improve reply rate, script testing method.

Weekly supervisor review every Friday: reply rate must hit 10%+, meeting rate 30%+, QC score 30/40+, KPI completion 70%+.

Month 1 target per VA: 3–5 deals closed, $600–$1,500 revenue.

---

### Phase 3 — Scaling + Closing (Days 46–90)

*Focus: High volume outreach + full sales closing. Targets: 20–30 connections/day, 80–120 messages/day, 40–80 follow-ups/day, 1–2 meetings/day*

**Days 46–55 — Sales Closing Track**

Day 46: Sales call system — discovery call structure, solution presentation.

Day 47: Objection handling — 5 most common objections with exact responses.

Day 48: Three closing techniques — trial close, assumptive close, urgency close.

Day 49: Offer and pricing presentation — how to present without losing the prospect.

Day 50: Proposal presentation and deal closing — Phase 13 full sequence.

Day 51: Contract and payment setup — Phase 14, tools, payment links.

Day 52: Client onboarding — Phase 15, onboarding form, marking Closed Won in CRM.

Day 53: Mock sales call — full role play, discovery to close. Graded by supervisor.

Day 54: AI tools overview — cold email automation, personalisation, Apollo.io advanced.

Day 55: Growth hacking master list — 5 tactics to implement immediately.

**Days 56–90 — Full Execution**

Daily structure identical — morning brief, 4 work blocks, 3 reports. Outreach targets: 120–180 total touchpoints/day. Funnel target by Day 90 per VA: 2,000–3,000 messages sent, 200–400 replies, 60–120 interested, 25–40 meetings, 10–15 sales.

Weekly: supervisor QC, performance tier review. Score below 50% = removal. 50–65% = retrain. 65–80% = continue. 80%+ = incentives and promotion discussion.

Month 3 target per VA: 10–15 deals, $3,000–$7,500 revenue. Team of 10: $30,000–$75,000/month.

---

**One rule that never changes across all 90 days:** Final report submitted every day by 7:00 PM — leads, messages, follow-ups, emails, replies, meetings, sales, revenue. No report = warning. Three warnings = removed from program.
