# ⚙️ The Engine Overview {#overview}

```
NEW LEAD ENTERS
      ↓
[Outbound] + [Inbound Content] + [Referral]
      ↓
Lead warms up via email nurture + LinkedIn content
      ↓
Books a call → converts to client
      ↓
Delivers exceptional results
      ↓
Client refers 1–2 people → repeats the cycle
      ↓
Content about that win creates 10 more leads
```

**The flywheel self-accelerates when:** quality is consistently high, referral incentives are in place, and content is published regularly.

# 🤝 Component 1: Referral System {#referral}

## How It Works

- Every client is asked for a referral at the 90-day mark
- Formal referral program: **10% commission on first 3 months of any referred client**
- Referral partners (accountants, lawyers, consultants) get a dedicated partner agreement

## Referral Ask Script

> "[Client name], we've really enjoyed working with you. If you know any other business owners who might benefit from what we do — accounting, IT, marketing — we'd love an introduction. We offer a 10% referral fee on any client that signs up."
> 

## Referral Tracking

- Log in HubSpot: "Referral Source" field on every deal
- Monthly review of referral-sourced revenue
- Thank referrers with a personalised note when the deal closes

# 📰 Component 2: Content Flywheel {#content}

Every piece of content we produce:

1. Gets published on the blog
2. Gets repurposed into 5 LinkedIn posts
3. Gets repurposed into 3 short-form social posts
4. Gets shared in the newsletter
5. Generates SEO traffic for 12+ months

**1 article = 1 blog + 5 LinkedIn + 3 social + 1 newsletter mention = ~10 content pieces**

Target: 8 articles/month = 80 content pieces/month

# 🤝 Component 3: Partnership Network {#partnerships}

**Partner types:**

- Chartered accountants / CPA firms (referral both ways)
- Law firms / solicitors (compliance overlap)
- Business consultants (strategy + execution split)
- Staffing/recruitment agencies (VA referrals)
- Software vendors (HubSpot, Xero, n8n partners)

**Partnership model:**

- Formal agreement with referral fee terms
- Co-marketing opportunities (joint LinkedIn posts, webinars)
- White-label options for complementary services
