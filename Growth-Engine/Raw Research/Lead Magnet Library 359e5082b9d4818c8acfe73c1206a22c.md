# 💡 What is a Lead Magnet? {#what}

A lead magnet is a free, high-value resource offered in exchange for an email address or a discovery call booking. It solves one specific problem for the ICP and positions Nivy Digital as the expert.

**Formula:** Specific Problem + Quick Win + Nivy Digital as the solution

---

# 🎯 Lead Magnet Strategy {#strategy}

- Create **1 hero lead magnet** per ICP (4 ICPs = 4 magnets)
- Distribute via: Website opt-in forms, LinkedIn posts, cold email footer, email signature
- Gate with email only (no long forms — reduce friction)
- Follow up with 3-email nurture sequence after download
- Review performance quarterly — retire magnets with <2% conversion

# ⚙️ Production SOP {#sop}

1. Pick lead magnet from priority list above
2. Write content (VA can draft, founder reviews)
3. Design in Canva (use brand colours, clean layout)
4. Save as PDF and upload to website
5. Create opt-in landing page (or embed form on blog)
6. Connect form to email platform (Mailchimp/Brevo)
7. Write 3-email nurture sequence (welcome → value → CTA)
8. Test full flow end-to-end before launching
9. Promote via LinkedIn post, cold email footer, website pop-up
