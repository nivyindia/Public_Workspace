# 1.0 LinkedIn Profile Photo Setup (For Agency & BDE Profiles)

To make a **strong LinkedIn profile photo** for your **digital marketing / lead generation agency profile**, the goal is to look **professional, trustworthy, and approachable** so that founders and international clients feel comfortable connecting with you.

Here is the **exact specification you should follow** 👇

---

# 

| Element | How to Set It Up | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Face Visibility | Your face should take **60–70% of the frame** | Makes the profile recognizable |
| Background | Use **plain white, light grey, or office background** | Looks professional |
| Lighting | Natural light from front or soft light | Avoid shadows and dark photos |
| Dress | **Business casual or formal shirt/blazer** | Builds credibility with clients |
| Expression | Slight smile, confident look | Makes you approachable |
| Camera Angle | Camera at **eye level** | Looks natural and confident |
| Image Quality | High resolution (minimum **400×400 px**) | Prevents blurry profile |

---

# Ideal Look for Your Agency Profile

Since you are targeting **international clients for digital marketing, CPA, automation, and development**, the photo should look like:

- Founder / consultant style
- Professional but friendly
- Clean background
- Clear face visibility

Think: **consultant or marketing strategist look**.

---

# Good Example Style

Appearance:

- Light background
- Collared shirt or blazer
- Clean haircut
- Slight smile
- Shoulders visible
- Neutral professional pose

This type of photo works best for:

✔ B2B sales

✔ agency founders

✔ consultants

✔ business development executives

---

# Things to Avoid 🚫

Avoid these common mistakes:

❌ Selfie photos

❌ Sunglasses

❌ Party photos

❌ Group photos

❌ Low quality images

❌ Dark lighting

These reduce **trust and response rate**.

---

# Best Photo Structure

The frame should look like this:

```
Head and shoulders visible
Face centered
Neutral background
Good lighting
Professional attire
```

---

# Extra Trick (Used by Top LinkedIn Profiles)

Use a **very slight blur background (portrait mode)**.

This makes the face stand out and looks more professional.

---

✅ **Simple formula**

```
Clear face + professional dress + simple background + friendly expression
```

That alone increases **profile trust dramatically**.
