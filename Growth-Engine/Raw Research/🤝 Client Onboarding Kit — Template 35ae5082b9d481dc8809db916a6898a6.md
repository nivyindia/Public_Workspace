## ❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: How do I request changes to the work?**

A: Send a WhatsApp message to your account manager. Changes within scope are handled within 48 hours.

**Q: What if I’m not happy with something?**

A: Tell us immediately. We operate a 30-day satisfaction guarantee — we’ll fix it or refund.

**Q: Can I upgrade my package?**

A: Yes, anytime. We’ll adjust your invoice from the next billing cycle.

**Q: How are results reported?**

A: Via a detailed monthly report sent by the 5th of each month, plus our monthly call.

**Q: Who owns the work created?**

A: You do, once payment is made. Full IP transfer upon payment.
