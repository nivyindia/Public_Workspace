# ⚙️ Supervisor Onboarding Checklist — Before Team Starts Day 1

## Purpose

This checklist is for supervisors and team leaders. Complete everything on this page **before your VA team's first day of work**. A team that starts without this preparation loses its first 2–3 days to confusion, access problems, and missing information.

---

## 1. Team Setup

- [ ]  Team size confirmed with VA Manager (aim for 8–10 VAs per supervisor)
- [ ]  All VA names, WhatsApp numbers, and email addresses collected
- [ ]  VA Roster spreadsheet created and shared with VA Manager:
    - Columns: VA Name | Phone | Email | Start Date | Niche Assigned | CRM Access Status
- [ ]  Each VA added to the correct reporting WhatsApp group
- [ ]  Each VA added to the team announcements group (view only)

---

## 2. Tool Access — Confirm Before Day 1

| Tool | Action Required |
| --- | --- |
| Google Sheets CRM | Share with each VA (Editor access) — confirm they can open and edit |
| Notion workspace | Share the VA Training Program (Start Here) page — confirm they can view all sub-pages |
| Calendly link | Send the team booking link to all VAs |
| Reporting sheet | Share with all VAs — confirm submission channel |
| Email account | Issue company email credentials to each VA (or confirm personal professional email) |
- [ ]  All tool access confirmed for every VA (test by asking each to screenshot the open CRM on Day 0)

---

## 3. Niche & Target Assignment

- [ ]  Each VA has been assigned exactly ONE niche before Day 1
- [ ]  Niche is written in the VA Roster with target country included
- [ ]  ICP page shared with all VAs and confirmed read
- [ ]  Each VA knows their niche and target country before their first message

**Niche assignment options (pick one per VA):**

- Roofing companies (USA)
- HVAC businesses (USA / UK)
- Digital marketing agencies
- Business coaches and consultants
- Recruitment firms
- Accounting firms (if assigned to Nivy Advisory outreach)

---

## 4. Scripts & Training Pages Confirmed Open

- [ ]  VA Training Program (Start Here) confirmed accessible for all VAs
- [ ]  Before You Start — Setup Checklist sent to all VAs with instruction to complete before Day 1
- [ ]  Outreach Script Library confirmed accessible
- [ ]  CRM Usage Guide confirmed accessible
- [ ]  Daily Reporting Master Page confirmed accessible

---

## 5. Day 1 Briefing Prepared

Prepare a WhatsApp briefing message to send to the team group at 10:00 AM on Day 1. Include:

```
Good morning team!

Welcome to Day 1. Here is your plan for today:

1. Open the Setup Checklist and confirm everything is ready
2. Read Stage 1 — Day 1 Starter Kit fully
3. Your assigned niche: [FILL IN]
4. Your daily targets today:
   - Add 10+ leads to CRM
   - Send 5+ connection requests or messages
   - Update CRM after every action
5. Submit your daily report by 7:00 PM

Any problems → message me directly. Let's go!
```

- [ ]  Day 1 briefing message drafted and ready to send

---

## 6. Reporting Structure Confirmed

- [ ]  VAs know which WhatsApp group to submit daily reports in
- [ ]  Report submission time communicated: 7:00 PM daily
- [ ]  Supervisor confirms they will review reports within 2 hours of submission
- [ ]  Escalation path communicated: VA → Supervisor → VA Manager → Operations Head

---

## 7. KPI Targets Set and Communicated

Before Day 1, each VA must know their daily targets for Stage 1:

| Task | Day 1 Target |
| --- | --- |
| Leads added to CRM | 20 |
| Messages sent (connection requests + DMs) | 10–20 |
| CRM updates | After every action |
| Daily report | By 7:00 PM |
- [ ]  Stage 1 targets communicated to all VAs
- [ ]  VAs know targets increase in Stage 2 and beyond

---

## 8. Final Supervisor Readiness Check

- [ ]  I can access and view all VAs' CRM entries
- [ ]  I know each VA's assigned niche
- [ ]  I have the escalation contact for my VA Manager
- [ ]  I know what to do if a VA misses their first day report
- [ ]  I know the offboarding process if a VA needs to be removed
- [ ]  I am ready to send the Day 1 briefing at 10:00 AM

> Once all boxes are ticked, the team is ready to start. Signal readiness to the VA Manager.
