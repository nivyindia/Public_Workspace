## 📝 REAL SCRIPTS — OBJECTION HANDLING (Stage 4)

> Use these exact replies when a lead pushes back.
> 

### ❌ "Not interested"

```
No problem at all, [NAME].

Just to understand — is it because you're already getting enough leads, or is the timing just not right?
```

### ❌ "Too expensive" / "What's the cost?"

```
Totally fair question. Pricing depends on your setup and goals.

Honestly, it makes more sense to figure out if this is even a fit first — then we can go through numbers on a quick call.

Are you free for 15 minutes this week?
```

### ❌ "I'll think about it"

```
Of course, no rush.

Can I ask — what part are you unsure about? Maybe I can answer it right now.
```

### ❌ "Send me more info first"

```
Sure, I can send something over.

To make sure I send the right info — what's your main challenge right now with getting new clients?
```

### ❌ "We already have someone doing this"

```
That’s great! Most of our best clients said the same thing before we started.

We often work alongside existing systems — happy to show you what’s different on a quick call if it’s useful.
```

---

## ✅ REAL SCRIPTS — YES FLOW (When They Show Interest)

> These are the exact messages to send when a lead says YES or shows buying signals.
> 

### When they say “Yes, I’m interested”

```
Fantastic! Let’s get a quick 15-minute call scheduled.

What day works better for you — [Day Option 1] or [Day Option 2]?
```

### When they say “Tell me more”

```
Happy to!

The quickest way is a short call — I can walk you through exactly what we’d do for [their business type].

Are you free [specific day] at [time]?
```

### When they say “How soon can we start?”

```
That’s great to hear! We can move fast once we know your setup.

Let’s do a quick call first to make sure it’s the right fit — does [Day] at [Time] work?
```

### Confirming the booked call

```
Perfect — I’ve noted you down for [Day] at [Time].

I’ll send a calendar invite shortly. Let me know if anything changes!
```

> ⚠️ Rule: Never negotiate on price over chat. Always move objections toward a call. Once they say YES — book immediately, don’t keep talking.
