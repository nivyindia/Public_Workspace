## 20. Critical Efficiency KPI (Used by Global Consulting Firms)

**Revenue per Employee**

Formula:

Total Revenue ÷ Total Team Members

Example:

₹10,00,000 revenue / 10 employees = ₹1,00,000 revenue per employee

This metric indicates whether the company is efficient or overstaffed.
