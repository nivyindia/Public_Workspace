# 🗺️ CJE Funnel Architecture — Complete Overview

> This page documents the complete funnel structure of the Nivy Customer Journey Engineering OS — how many funnels exist, how they combine, and how they map across TOFU, MOFU, BOFU, and POST-SALE stages.
> 

---

## Core Insight

The entry point is what matters, not the method. The **method** (Reels, Cold Email, Ads, SEO) is how people find you. The **entry point** (Form, WhatsApp link, Calendly, Landing page) is where automation begins. No matter which method brings someone in, once they hit an entry point — n8n takes over and the same funnel runs for everyone.

## Stage-by-Stage Breakdown

### 🔵 TOFU — Top of Funnel (2 Engines → 2 Combined)

**Engine 1: Attention Engine**

Methods: Educational Reels, Viral content, Cold email sequences, LinkedIn outreach, Meta/Google Ads, SEO, Founder branding, Influencer collabs, Contest campaigns

**Engine 2: Interest & Trust Engine**

Methods: Free audits, Ebooks, Case studies, Webinars, Testimonials, Retargeting ads, ROI examples, Community invites

**Combined into:**

- **C-T1** (Paid + Outbound): Meta Ads + Google Ads + Cold Email + LinkedIn Outreach → single landing page / lead capture
- **C-T2** (Organic + Interest): Reels + SEO + YouTube + Lead magnets + Webinars → organic inbound interest layer

---

### 🟡 MOFU — Middle of Funnel (3 Engines → 2 Combined)

**Engine 3: Lead Capture Engine**

Methods: Google/Tally forms, WhatsApp opt-in, Calendly booking, Landing page forms, Chatbots, Webinar registrations, Free audit requests

**Engine 4: Lead Management Engine**

Methods: Lead scoring, CRM segmentation, Lead routing, AI enrichment, Pipeline management, Auto-tagging

**Engine 5: Nurturing Engine**

Methods: Email drip sequences, WhatsApp follow-ups, Educational content, Retargeting ads, Community engagement, Webinar nurturing

**Combined into:**

- **C-M1** (Capture + Management): All capture points feed directly into CRM scoring, tagging, segmentation — one unified pipeline
- **C-M2** (Nurture + Retargeting): Email sequences, WhatsApp drips, retargeting ads merged into one behaviour-triggered automation

---

### 🟢 BOFU — Bottom of Funnel (3 Engines → 2 Combined)

**Engine 6: Conversion Engine**

Methods: Discovery calls, Business audits, ROI forecasting, Proposal presentation, Objection handling, Custom pricing, Scarcity & urgency

**Engine 7: Client Onboarding Engine**

Methods: Welcome systems, Kickoff calls, SOP explanation, Timeline sharing, Team introduction, Requirement collection, Shared dashboards

**Engine 8: Delivery Experience Engine**

Methods: KPI reporting, Weekly updates, Strategy reviews, Campaign optimization, Performance tracking, Client portals

**Combined into:**

- **C-B1** (Conversion standalone): Sales/discovery kept separate — high-pressure, dedicated pipeline
- **C-B2** (Onboarding + Delivery): Welcome → Kickoff → SOP → KPI Tracking → Reports. Same team, same tools, one connected flow

---

### 🩷 POST-SALE — Expansion Layer (5 Engines → 3 Combined)

**Engine 9: Retention Engine** — Community, VIP support, renewal systems

**Engine 10: Expansion Engine** — Upsells, cross-sells, consulting upgrades

**Engine 11: Referral & Viral Engine** — Referral programs, affiliate systems, ambassador programs

**Engine 12: Reactivation Engine** — Win-back campaigns, inactive lead tracking, seasonal offers

**Engine 12B: Ecosystem Expansion Engine** — Partner networks, freelancer ecosystem, business alliances

**Combined into:**

- **C-P1** (Retention + Expansion): Same clients, same relationship — one LTV maximization system
- **C-P2** (Referral + Reactivation): Shared WhatsApp/email channels and community re-entry points
- **C-P3** (Ecosystem standalone): Entirely separate pipeline targeting businesses and collaborators

---

## Key Principles

1. **TOFU compresses the most** (many channels → 2 funnels) because awareness channels overlap heavily
2. **MOFU and BOFU** don't compress as much because each engine serves a distinct intent
3. **POST-SALE has the most engines** (5) — this is what separates a standard funnel from a full Business Acquisition OS
4. **Your mental model is correct** — many funnels converge from top to bottom, narrowing as intent increases
