# 💼 LinkedIn Playbook

**Goal:** Generate 5+ qualified leads per week

**Posting frequency:** 5 posts/week (Mon–Fri)

**Best posting times:** 8–9am or 12–1pm IST

**Content mix:**

- 40% — Educational posts (tips, how-tos, industry insights)
- 30% — Authority posts (our process, our results, opinions)
- 20% — Social proof (client wins, case studies)
- 10% — Personal/culture (behind the scenes, team)

**Format rotation:** Text post → Carousel → Poll → Video → Text

**Hashtags:** 3–5 per post, niche-specific (e.g., #SMEAccounting #UKBookkeeping)

**CTA on every post:** Comment, DM, or book a call

---

# 📸 Instagram Playbook

**Goal:** Brand awareness + inbound DMs

**Posting frequency:** 4 posts/week + 5 Stories/week

**Best posting times:** 9–11am or 6–8pm IST

**Content mix:**

- 30% — Educational carousels ("5 signs your books are a mess")
- 25% — Quote graphics (finance, business growth, success)
- 25% — Behind the scenes (team, process, workspace)
- 20% — Promotional (services, offers, booking link)

**Reels strategy:** 1 Reel/week — 30–60 seconds, hook in first 3 seconds

**Stories:** Daily poll, tip of the day, or question box to drive engagement

**Bio link:** Linktree or direct to booking page

---

# 👥 Facebook Playbook

**Goal:** Reach Indian SME owners + generate local leads

**Posting frequency:** 3 posts/week + active in 5 groups

**Content mix:**

- Educational posts (GST tips, compliance news)
- Engagement posts (polls: "Do you outsource your accounting?")
- Group activity: answer questions, share resources, no hard selling

**Group strategy:**

- Join 10 relevant Indian SME/business groups
- Post value 3x/week per group
- DM interested commenters within 24 hours

---

# 🐦 X (Twitter) Playbook

**Goal:** Build thought leadership for US/UK audience

**Posting frequency:** 3–5 posts/day (tweets are short)

**Content mix:**

- Business tips and insights (accounting, tech, marketing)
- Engagement with founders and business influencers
- Threads (1/week) — in-depth takes on industry topics
- Reply to trending conversations in our niche

---

# 📺 YouTube Playbook

**Goal:** Long-form authority + SEO-ranked video content

**Posting frequency:** 1 video/week (minimum)

**Video types:**

- How-to tutorials ("How to register for GST in India")
- Explainers ("What is UAE Corporate Tax?")
- Interviews / case studies
- Screen recordings (tool walkthroughs)

**SEO:** Keyword-optimised title, description, 10 tags, custom thumbnail

---

# 📲 WhatsApp Channel Playbook

**Goal:** Nurture warm audience, drive repeat enquiries

**Posting frequency:** 3x/week

**Content:** Tips, service updates, new blog posts, limited offers

**Rule:** Never hard-sell on the channel — value first, always
