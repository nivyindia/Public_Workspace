# PART 1 — ONLINE PATIENT ACQUISITION SYSTEM

Goal:

Create **local partnerships + patient database** before doing offline demos.

Target **2–3 km radius around clinic**.

# 1. Target Local Partners Online

Reach out to:

| **Category** | **Why** |
| --- | --- |
| Small companies | employees have posture pain |
| Apartments / societies | elderly parents |
| Medical stores | pain patients visit daily |
| Gyms | injury cases |
| Yoga studios | joint pain |
| Coaching institutes | neck/back pain |
| Salons / spas | posture problems |
| Retail shops | standing jobs |
| Schools | teacher back pain |

# 3. WhatsApp Outreach Message

Send a short partnership message.

Hello,

We run a physiotherapy clinic in [Area Name].

Many employees and shop owners suffer from back pain, neck pain, knee pain and sciatica due to long sitting or standing.

We are offering a **FREE 10-minute pain relief session** for employees of nearby businesses.

If your team finds it helpful, we can also:

- conduct a short pain-relief demo session at your officeprovide exercise guides for staffoffer discounted treatment for employees and their parents

Would you be open to this for your team?

# 4. Apartment Society Outreach

Apartments are **very powerful patient sources**.

Hello,

We run a physiotherapy clinic near [Area].

Many senior citizens suffer from:

- knee painback painfrozen shoulderwalking difficulty

We are offering a **FREE 10-minute physiotherapy consultation** for residents of your society.

If residents are interested, we can also organize a **pain relief camp in the society clubhouse**.

Would it be possible to share this in your society WhatsApp group?

# 5. Offer Referral Partnerships

Partners are more interested if there is **benefit**.

Example model:

| **Partner** | **Referral Benefit** |
| --- | --- |
| Gym | ₹200 per patient |
| Medical store | ₹200 |
| Offices | employee discounts |
| Apartments | free health camp |

Referral message:

If any of your employees or customers visit us through your reference, we also provide a **referral benefit for partners.**

Many local businesses collaborate with us this way.
