# 🇮🇳 Market Overview {#overview}

- India has **63M+ MSMEs** — one of the world's largest SME ecosystems
- Growing digital adoption: 820M+ internet users, rapid move to online business
- Government push for formalization (GST, digital invoicing, e-filing) creates compliance demand
- Rising demand for outsourced services — businesses want to focus on core, delegate operations
- Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities increasingly adopting digital tools and services

# 🎯 Target Segments {#segments}

## Primary Segments

- **Startups and D2C brands** — need tech + marketing + compliance in one
- **Manufacturing SMEs** — complex GST (ITC), payroll, e-invoicing
- **Professional services** (CA firms, law, consulting) — need websites, digital presence
- **E-commerce sellers** (Amazon, Flipkart) — GST, TCS, returns filing complexity
- **Real estate developers** — GST on construction, digital marketing for projects

## Secondary Segments

- Educational institutions (EdTech, coaching)
- Healthcare clinics and diagnostic centers
- Retail chains expanding online

# ⚔️ Competitive Landscape {#competition}

| Competitor Type | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local CAs/Firms | Trust, local presence | Expensive, limited digital/IT |
| Freelancers | Cheap | Unreliable, no accountability |
| Large IT agencies | Full service | Expensive, slow, impersonal |
| Digital marketing agencies | Specialised | No accounting/compliance integration |

**Nivy's Edge in India:**

- Full-suite offering (accounting + IT + marketing) under one roof
- Competitive pricing with professional quality
- Supervised, managed delivery — not a freelancer marketplace
- International exposure adds credibility and learning

# 🚀 Go-to-Market Approach {#gtm}

## Channels

- **LinkedIn** — target founders, directors, CFOs of SMEs
- **WhatsApp** — highly effective in India for B2B outreach and follow-up
- **Referral** — CA networks, startup communities, coworking spaces
- **Content marketing** — GST tips, compliance checklists, digital growth guides
- **Google Ads + SEO** — local search for accountant, website developer, digital marketing

## Positioning in India

> "One team. Accounting, IT, and marketing — all professionally managed, at SME-friendly pricing."
> 

## Pricing Signals

- India pricing should be 30–50% below international rates
- Monthly retainer model preferred by clients
- GST-registered invoicing builds trust
