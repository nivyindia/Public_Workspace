# Phase 12 — Follow-Up System

## 

This is the phase where **most deals are actually won**. Many prospects don’t decide on the first call, so structured follow-up keeps the opportunity moving. 📈

---

# Goal

Turn **discovery calls into signed clients** through consistent follow-up and nurturing.

---

# Step 1 — Follow Up After the Meeting

Send a follow-up message within **24 hours** after the call.

Example:

> Hi {{First Name}},
> 
> 
> Great speaking with you today. I enjoyed learning about {{Company}} and your goals for improving lead generation.
> 
> I’ll send over the proposal shortly.
> 

This keeps the conversation **professional and organized**.

---

# Step 2 — Send the Proposal

Prepare and send your offer.

Common tools used:

- Google Docs
- PandaDoc
- DocuSign
- Notion

Your proposal should include:

- Problem summary
- Proposed solution
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Pricing

Keep proposals **clear and simple**.

---

# Step 3 — Send Reminders

If the prospect hasn’t responded, follow up politely.

Suggested schedule:

**Day 2**

> Just checking if you had a chance to review the proposal.
> 

**Day 5**

> Happy to answer any questions if anything needs clarification.
> 

**Day 10**

> Let me know if you'd like to move forward or discuss further.
> 

This prevents the deal from **going cold**.

---

# Step 4 — Continue Nurturing

Not every prospect is ready immediately.

Ways to nurture leads:

- Share relevant insights
- Send useful articles
- Comment on their posts on LinkedIn
- Check in periodically

This keeps you **top of mind**.
