# 🎨 CONTENT STRATEGY OVERVIEW {#strategy}

## The Content Pyramid

| Level | Format | Frequency | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pillar** | Long-form guide (2,000+ words) | 1x/month | "Complete Guide to Hiring a VA in 2026" |

# 🤖 AI BLOG WRITING WORKFLOW {#workflow}

## Step-by-Step (Every Blog Post)

**Step 1 — Keyword + Title (5 min)**

1. Check Ubersuggest or Google Search Console for keyword
2. Write title including keyword: "How to [Action] + [Target]"
3. Example: "How to Hire a Virtual Assistant for Your Real Estate Business"

**Step 2 — Claude Draft (10 min)**

Prompt template:

```
Write a 1,500-word blog post titled "[TITLE]" for Nivy Digital,
a VA & digital marketing agency based in India serving US/UK/AU/UAE clients.
Tone: professional but approachable, clear, action-oriented.
Include: intro, 5–6 main sections with H2 headings, real examples, 
conclusion with CTA to book a free strategy call.
Target keyword: [KEYWORD] — include naturally 4–6 times.
End with: "Ready to delegate? Book your free 30-min call at [Cal.com link]"
```

**Step 3 — Human Edit (15 min)**

- Add 1–2 real client examples or specific numbers
- Replace any generic AI phrases with specific Nivy language
- Check that keyword appears in: title, H1, first paragraph, 2–3 H2s
- Add internal links to relevant service pages

**Step 4 — Format + Publish (10 min)**

- Add featured image (Unsplash/Pexels, keyword in alt text)
- Fill in Rank Math SEO meta title + description
- Set category + tags
- Publish (or schedule for Mon/Fri)

**Step 5 — Repurpose (10 min using Claude)**

```
Take this blog post and create:
1. A 300-word LinkedIn post with a hook opening line
2. 5 short LinkedIn post ideas (1–3 sentences each) from key insights
3. 1 Instagram carousel with 7 slides (slide titles + 1-sentence bullets)
4. 1 newsletter section (200 words)
5. 3 tweet-length posts
```

**Total time per blog post: ~50 minutes (with AI assistance)**
