# ⚖️ Dispute & Grievance Process

Process for handling disputes and grievances fairly and consistently.

---

## Purpose

Ensure every dispute — whether between VAs, between VA and Supervisor, or about a process — is handled fairly, promptly, and with documentation.

---

## Types of Disputes This Covers

1. VA disputes Supervisor's QC flag or scoring
2. VA reports unfair treatment
3. Supervisor disputes Manager's decision
4. Performance rating dispute
5. Incentive or salary calculation dispute

---

## The Process

### Step 1 — Raise It (within 48 hours of the issue)

- Person raises the dispute in writing (message or email to Manager)
- Clearly describe: what happened, when, who was involved, what resolution they want

### Step 2 — Acknowledge (within 24 hours)

- Manager acknowledges receipt
- Manager logs it in the Dispute Log below
- Manager confirms timeline for review (standard: 5 working days)

### Step 3 — Review

- Manager reviews all relevant documentation (QC logs, messages, SOPs)
- Manager speaks to all parties separately
- Manager applies the relevant SOP or policy as the benchmark — not personal judgment

### Step 4 — Decision

- Manager communicates decision in writing to all parties
- Decision includes: what was found, what decision was made, why
- If the SOP was wrong: update the SOP and log in System Feedback Log
- If the person was wrong: coaching note added to their record

### Step 5 — Close

- All parties confirm they have received the decision
- Case marked resolved in Dispute Log
