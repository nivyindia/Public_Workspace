# 🚪 Offboarding & Exit Process

## Overview

This page covers what happens when a VA exits the program — whether by choice, performance, or completion. The exit process protects both the VA and the company. It must be followed in every case.

---

## Exit Types

| Type | Description | Notice Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voluntary resignation | VA chooses to leave | 3 days notice via WhatsApp to supervisor |
| Performance removal | Below 50% KPI for 2+ consecutive weeks, or after a formal warning | Immediate, notified by supervisor |
| Program completion | VA completes the full 90-day program and exits or converts to hired staff | Planned, with supervisor sign-off |
| Disciplinary removal | Data violation, fake leads, severe misconduct | Immediate, no notice period |
| Training failure | Failed Day 7 test, failed re-test after coaching | Immediate, with feedback provided |

---

## Step-by-Step Exit Checklist (Supervisor Completes This)

### Step 1 — Notify the VA (same day decision is made)

- [ ]  Send a written message to the VA via WhatsApp confirming exit date and reason
- [ ]  If voluntary: confirm last working day
- [ ]  If performance/disciplinary: confirm immediate removal

### Step 2 — CRM Handover (within 24 hours of exit)

- [ ]  Export or screenshot all leads the VA was working on
- [ ]  Update CRM status for all their active leads (reassign to another VA or supervisor)
- [ ]  Confirm no leads are left in "Messaged" or "Interested" status without a follow-up owner
- [ ]  Note: Any leads in "Booked" status must be immediately reassigned and the closer informed

### Step 3 — Account Access Revocation (same day)

- [ ]  Remove VA from all team WhatsApp groups
- [ ]  Remove VA access to the Google Sheets CRM (revoke sharing)
- [ ]  Remove VA access to the Notion training workspace (revoke sharing)
- [ ]  Remove VA from any reporting sheets or dashboards
- [ ]  Change any shared passwords or access codes that the VA had

### Step 4 — Data Confirmation (within 48 hours)

- [ ]  Confirm VA has not downloaded or exported any CRM data
- [ ]  If data breach is suspected, escalate to management immediately
- [ ]  Log the exit in the team roster: name, exit date, exit type, leads reassigned

### Step 5 — Final Settlement (if applicable)

- [ ]  Calculate final salary and incentives owed up to last working day
- [ ]  Confirm payment timeline with management
- [ ]  Issue completion certificate if VA completed Stage 1–5 in good standing (voluntary exit or completion only)
- [ ]  Disciplinary removals do not receive certificates

---

## What the Exiting VA Receives

| Exit Type | Certificate | Final Pay | CRM Access After Exit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Voluntary resignation | If Stage 3+ completed | Paid for days worked | None |
| Program completion | Full VA Completion Certificate | Full month paid | None |
| Performance removal | None | Paid for days worked | None |
| Disciplinary removal | None | Withheld pending investigation | None |
| Training failure | None | None (intern/unpaid period) | None |

---

## Data Handover Rules

- All leads generated by the exiting VA remain the property of Nivy
- The VA has no claim to any lead, contact, or client they worked on
- If the VA was a Team Leader, all sub-VA reports and performance data are transferred to the replacement supervisor immediately
- If the VA had access to client-specific data (onboarding VA, delivery VA), a full data inventory must be submitted to the supervisor before final exit

---

## Escalation

If the exiting VA refuses to cooperate with the exit process, or if data theft is suspected:

1. Escalate to the VA Manager immediately
2. Suspend their access to all tools within the hour
3. Document all evidence of the suspected breach
4. Management will determine next steps, which may include legal action if client data was compromised
