# Phase 11 — Meeting Handover

## 

This phase is used when **Virtual Assistants (VAs) act as appointment setters** and the **sales closer handles the discovery/sales call**. The goal is to ensure a **smooth transfer of the lead** so the closer has all the context needed to convert the prospect. 🤝

---

# Goal

Make sure the **sales team receives a fully prepared lead** with all relevant information.

Typical structure:

**Prospecting → VA Setter → Sales Closer**

---

# Step 1 — Book the Meeting

The VA schedules the discovery call using a scheduling tool such as:

- Calendly
- Google Calendar

Meeting platform examples:

- Zoom
- Google Meet

The appointment should include:

- Date and time
- Meeting link
- Client name and company

---

# Step 2 — Add Meeting Notes

The VA should add **important lead details** to the CRM before handing the lead over.

Example information:

- Client name
- Company name
- Industry
- Current marketing strategy
- Problem they mentioned
- Budget indication (if known)

Example CRM note:

> Prospect runs a roofing company with 20 employees.
> 
> 
> Currently relies on referrals for leads and wants more consistent lead generation.
> 

You can track this in tools like:

- HubSpot CRM
- Airtable
- Pipedrive

---

# Step 3 — Hand the Lead to the Sales Closer

After the meeting is booked and notes are added, the VA transfers the lead to the **sales team or closer**.

The closer should receive:

✔ Meeting link

✔ Client details

✔ Notes from previous conversations

✔ Lead qualification score

Example internal message:

> New discovery call booked.
> 
> 
> Client: John Smith – Peak Roofing
> 
> Interested in improving lead generation through paid ads.
> 

---

# Example Lead Handover Workflow

Lead generation → Conversation → Qualification → Meeting booked → Notes added → Sales closer takes the call

---

# Simple Handover Checklist

Before transferring the lead:

✔ Meeting scheduled

✔ CRM updated

✔ Client notes recorded

✔ Meeting link confirmed

✔ Closer notified

---

# Why This Step Matters

Without a proper handover:

❌ Sales team lacks context

❌ Calls feel unprepared

❌ Lower closing rate

With a structured handover:

✔ Better conversations

✔ More personalized pitches

✔ Higher client conversion
