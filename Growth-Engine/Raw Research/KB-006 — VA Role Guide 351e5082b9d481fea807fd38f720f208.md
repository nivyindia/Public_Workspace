## The 3 VA Levels

Nivy's VA team operates on a 3-level structure. Every VA enters at Level 1 and progresses based on proven performance — not time spent or effort perceived.

---

## Level 1 — VA Intern

**Who this is:** A new VA who has completed the 5-day onboarding gate and is cleared for live work.

**Owns:**

- Lead scraping from assigned sources (LinkedIn, Google Maps, Apollo)
- Basic data entry into CRM
- Sending first outreach messages on assigned leads (once approved by Manager)
- Daily report submission

**Decides independently:**

- Which leads to skip when the hard disqualifier is obvious (see KB-001)
- Message timing within the defined follow-up cadence

**Escalates to:** VA Manager

**Cannot do:**

- Modify outreach scripts without Manager approval
- Mark leads as Closed Won or Closed Lost
- Assign leads to other VAs
- Engage in pricing conversations with leads
- Skip the QC checklist before submitting work

**Progression to VA Executive:** 30 consecutive days at 🟢 80%+ on the weekly scorecard + minimum 10 calls booked

---

## Level 2 — VA Executive

**Who this is:** A VA who has earned Level 2 status through consistent performance at Level 1.

**Owns:**

- Full outreach cycle: lead scraping → outreach → follow-up → CRM management → booking
- CRM accuracy and completeness for their assigned leads
- Managing their own follow-up pipeline independently
- Mentoring Interns when assigned by Manager

**Decides independently:**

- Reply handling within approved response frameworks
- Follow-up timing adjustments based on lead behaviour (within SOP)
- Which leads to move to Dead status (after full sequence completed)

**Escalates to:** VA Manager

**Cannot do:**

- Change KPI targets
- Assign tasks to other VAs
- Approve another VA's onboarding
- Make changes to SOPs or scripts without Manager approval

**Progression to VA Manager:** 60 consecutive days at ⭐ 90%+ on the weekly scorecard + demonstrated leadership in at least 1 documented situation

---

## Level 3 — VA Manager

**Who this is:** A VA who has demonstrated sustained excellence and leadership capability.

**Owns:**

- Daily team review: QC of all VA reports before they reach Ops Head
- Lead assignment across the team
- Onboarding gate review and sign-off for all new VAs
- Identifying patterns in performance problems and leading retraining
- Weekly scorecard review every Monday
- Escalation management within the team

**Decides independently:**

- Retraining decisions for underperforming VAs
- Daily task priorities and lead assignments
- Whether a VA is ready to progress to live work (onboarding gate sign-off)
- Whether a lead should be moved to Dead status at team level

**Escalates to:** Ops Head / Founder

**Cannot do:**

- Change strategy, pricing, or package structure
- Hire or dismiss VAs
- Make decisions about client delivery
- Bypass the Ops Head on any escalation that involves a client complaint

---

## Progression Summary

| Level | Progression Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Intern → Executive | 30 days at 80%+ scorecard + 10 calls booked |
| Executive → Manager | 60 days at 90%+ scorecard + 1 documented leadership moment |
| Manager → Senior/Lead | Ops Head nomination only — based on team results |

> **Promotions are earned, not given.** Every promotion is based on documented, tracked performance — not on who has been here longest.
> 

---

## The Golden Rule of Role Boundaries

If you are unsure whether something is your decision to make — **it is not**. Escalate it. Being right 90% of the time and escalating the 10% is the correct approach. Making a decision that is outside your authority and getting it wrong costs far more.
