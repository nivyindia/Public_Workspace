# 🖊️ CONTENT ENGINE {#content}

## AI-Assisted Content Workflow

1. **Keyword Research** → Google Search Console + Ubersuggest → identify target keyword
2. **Draft** → Claude/ChatGPT generates 1,500-word draft in 5 minutes
3. **Edit** → Human adds real examples, client stories, local context
4. **Publish** → WordPress/Webflow with proper SEO meta
5. **Repurpose** → Blog → LinkedIn post → Newsletter section → 3 short social posts
6. **Automate** → n8n auto-posts summary to all social channels on publish
