# 🧠 SUPPLY ↔ DEMAND MATCHING SOP

*(Client → Right Partner → Deal → Revenue Share)*

---

## 1️⃣ PURPOSE OF THIS SOP

To **systematically match**:

- A **qualified client demand**
with
- The **right partner company / freelancer**

Without:

- Confusion
- Bias
- Manual decision chaos
- Founder bottleneck

---

## 2️⃣ WHO USES THIS SOP

| Role | Uses SOP |
| --- | --- |
| Outreach Team | To handover correctly |
| Matching Manager / Ops | To assign partners |
| Founder | To audit decisions |
| Partner Companies | To know expectations |

---

## 3️⃣ INPUTS REQUIRED (STRICT)

### From Outreach Team (MANDATORY)

- Client name
- Industry
- Problem statement (1–2 lines)
- Urgency (Immediate / 30–60 days / Open)
- Budget signal (Low / Medium / High / Unknown)
- Geography
- Contact readiness (Yes/Maybe)

❌ Missing info = NOT PROCESSED

---

### From Partner Database (PRE-BUILT)

Each partner must already have:

Field

---

Services offered

---

Ideal client size

---

Geography served

---

Price range

---

Delivery capacity

---

Past proof (optional)

---

Revenue share agreed

---

---

## 4️⃣ MATCHING LOGIC (NO THINKING)

### 🎯 Matching Priority Order (DO NOT CHANGE)

1. **Service Fit** (Exact > Partial > No)
2. **Geography Match**
3. **Client Size Compatibility**
4. **Availability**
5. **Past Performance (if exists)**

If **Service Fit ≠ Exact → Reject**

---

## 5️⃣ MATCHING DECISION TABLE

| Client Need | Partner Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 clear service | Assign 1 partner |
| 2 related services | Assign lead partner |
| Exploratory | Assign discovery partner |
| High ticket | Assign senior / proven partner |
| Low ticket | Assign volume partner |

---

## 6️⃣ ASSIGNMENT PROCESS (STEP-BY-STEP)

### 🔹 STEP 1: Log Client in “Qualified Demand Sheet”

Fields:

- Demand ID (Auto)
- Outreach owner
- Status = “Awaiting Match”

---

### 🔹 STEP 2: Shortlist Partners (Max 3)

Rules:

- Never more than **3**
- Prefer **1 primary**
- 2 backups only if needed

---

### 🔹 STEP 3: Partner Availability Check (Template)

```
Hi {{Partner}},

We have a qualified lead:
• Industry: {{industry}}
• Problem: {{problem}}
• Timeline: {{urgency}}

Can you take a discovery call in next 48 hrs?
Reply YES / NO.

```

⏱ Wait max **12 hours**

---

### 🔹 STEP 4: Final Assignment

| Scenario | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 YES | Assign immediately |
| Multiple YES | Choose highest fit |
| All NO | Recycle to backup pool |

Update status → **Partner Assigned**

---

## 7️⃣ CLIENT ↔ PARTNER INTRO TEMPLATE

```
Hi {{Client}},

```

After this → **You step back**

---

## 8️⃣ REVENUE SHARE RULES (CLEAR & SAFE)

### 🔐 Non-Negotiable

- Share applies **only if deal closes**
- Share applies on **collected amount**
- No involvement in delivery disputes

### 📊 Typical Models

| Deal Type | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Low ticket | 10–15% |
| Mid ticket | 15–25% |
| High ticket | 20–30% |
| Retainer | Monthly % |

---

## 9️⃣ STATUS FLOW (SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH)

```
Qualified → Matched → Partner Assigned → Call Done → Proposal Sent → Won / Lost

```

Only **Ops / Founder** can change post-assignment status.

---

## 🔍 QUALITY CONTROL (FOUNDER VIEW)

Weekly check:

- Avg time to match (<24 hrs)
- Partner acceptance rate
- Win % per partner
- Revenue per demand source

Bad partner = removed

Bad demand source = fixed at outreach
