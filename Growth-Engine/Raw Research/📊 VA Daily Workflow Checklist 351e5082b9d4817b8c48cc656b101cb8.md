# 📊 VA Daily Workflow Checklist

Open this alongside the VA Dashboard every morning. Complete every item every day. This is not optional.

---

## Morning (Start of Day)

- [ ]  Open VA Dashboard — Start Here
- [ ]  Check messages / leads received overnight
- [ ]  Log any new leads
- [ ]  Review today's follow-up list
- [ ]  Prioritize: who needs a follow-up today?

---

## Mid-Day (Active Outreach Block)

- [ ]  Send new outreach messages (per daily target)
- [ ]  Respond to all replies within 2 hours
- [ ]  Move qualified leads into booking flow
- [ ]  Update your lead log

---

## Afternoon (Follow-Up Block)

- [ ]  Follow up with all leads pending 3+ days
- [ ]  Archive leads with no reply after 7 days
- [ ]  Confirm any booked calls are in the calendar
- [ ]  Complete handoffs for today's bookings

---

## End of Day

- [ ]  Update daily tracker (leads contacted, replies, bookings)
- [ ]  Note any blockers or unusual situations
- [ ]  Submit EOD report to Supervisor
- [ ]  Close out cleanly. Tomorrow starts fresh.

---

## Your Daily Targets (Set by Supervisor)

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| New outreach sent | To be set |
| Follow-ups done | To be set |
| Replies responded to | All, within 2 hours |
| Calls booked | Weekly target |

Ask your Supervisor if you don't have targets yet.
