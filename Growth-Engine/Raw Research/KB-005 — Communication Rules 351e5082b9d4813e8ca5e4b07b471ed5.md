# KB-005 — Communication Rules

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

---

## Platform Rules

| Platform | Used For | NOT Used For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp** | Daily check-ins, quick updates, urgent flags, team coordination | Performance disputes, strategy, client details, long explanations |
| **Notion** | All documentation, reports, SOPs, QC checklists, handover forms | Casual conversation |
| **Email** | External client/lead communication only | Internal team communication |
| **LinkedIn/Instagram/etc.** | Lead outreach only | Internal team updates |

> **Rule:** If it needs to be documented or referenced later — it goes in Notion, not WhatsApp.
> 

---

## Response Time Expectations

| Situation | Expected Response |
| --- | --- |
| Manager message during working hours | Within 2 hours |
| Team WhatsApp group message | Acknowledge within 2 hours |
| Lead replies to outreach (interested) | Escalate to CRM within 30 minutes |
| Urgent flag from Manager | Within 30 minutes |
| Non-urgent question or update | Same working day |

> **Working hours are your contracted hours.** Outside those hours, you are not expected to respond unless pre-agreed.
> 

---

## Tone Standards

**Internal (team) communication:**

- Direct and professional — don't be rude, but don't add unnecessary filler
- Use specifics: "I sent 22 messages today" not "I did a lot of outreach"
- Flag blockers early — don't wait until end of day to say you couldn't complete a task

**External (lead) communication:**

- Friendly, confident, professional
- No slang, no typos, no overly casual language
- Match the tone of the market (see KB-004 for market-specific guidance)
- Always re-read before sending — one typo in outreach undermines credibility

---

## Escalation Rules

Escalate to your Manager immediately (within 30 minutes) if:

- A lead responds aggressively or makes a complaint
- A lead asks about pricing, contracts, or delivery details
- A system or tool is broken (CRM access, LinkedIn limits, email issues)
- You cannot complete more than 50% of your daily target due to a genuine blocker
- Anything happens that you are not sure how to handle

**Do NOT sit on a problem for hours.** Escalate early — every problem gets smaller when flagged early and larger when hidden.

---

## What to Handle vs What to Escalate

| Handle Yourself | Escalate to Manager |
| --- | --- |
| Deciding which leads to skip (clear hard disqualifier) | Any lead reply asking about pricing |
| Choosing follow-up timing within the defined cadence | Complaint or negative response from a lead |
| Formatting your own daily report | System or tool failure |
| Basic CRM data entry and updates | Any situation you've never been trained on |

---

## Daily Report is the Communication Channel

Do NOT send informal updates like:

> "Hey, I did around 20 messages today and got a couple of replies."
> 

Instead, fill in your daily report via SOP-VA-015 by 6:00 PM. The report IS your update. WhatsApp is for genuine flags and coordination only — not performance updates.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Messaging the Manager on WhatsApp for things that should be in the daily report
- Waiting until end of day to flag a blocker that happened at 10am
- Not responding to a Manager message within the 2-hour window
- Using informal language in lead outreach
- Discussing client details or performance issues over WhatsApp
