# SOP-VA-016 — Weekly Review Report

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who Uses This:** VA Executive, VA Manager
> 

---

## Purpose

To submit a weekly performance summary every Friday — giving the Manager a complete picture of the week’s results, patterns, and the VA’s own assessment of what to improve.

---

## Submission Deadline

> **Every Friday by 6:00 PM (your local time).**
> 

---

---

## How to Use the Weekly Review

This is not a formality. It is a **self-audit**. The VA who writes an honest weekly review every week improves faster than one who submits the minimum.

- Pull numbers from daily reports — do not recalculate from memory
- Be specific in "What Worked" and "What Didn't Work" — not "outreach was good"
- The goals for next week must be specific and measurable

---

## Manager Use of Weekly Reviews

Every Monday morning, the Manager reads all VA weekly reviews and:

- Checks metrics against scorecard targets
- Identifies any VA below 60% performance — schedules 1:1
- Shares team aggregate to Ops Head (one summary message)
- Acknowledges top performer in team channel

---

## Output Standard

- All fields completed
- Numbers match daily report totals for the week
- "What Worked" and "What Didn't Work" have specific, actionable content
- Next week goals are measurable, not vague
- Submitted by 6:00 PM Friday

---

## Common Mistakes

- Writing vague answers ("it was a normal week") — this defeats the purpose
- Not matching numbers to daily reports — inconsistency flags data quality issues
- Skipping the blockers section — blockers exist every week in some form
- Leaving next week goals blank or generic ("keep improving") — must be specific

---

## QC Checklist

- [ ]  All fields completed
