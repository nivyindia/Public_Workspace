# 🎯 PRE-CALL PREPARATION {#pre-call}

**Do this 15 minutes before every discovery call:**

- [ ]  Research the prospect: LinkedIn profile, company website, recent posts
- [ ]  Check HubSpot: review all notes, emails, lead source, lead score
- [ ]  Identify their likely pain point based on industry + company size
- [ ]  Prepare 3 discovery questions tailored to their situation
- [ ]  Open [Cal.com](http://Cal.com) confirmation — confirm they've received the Zoom/Meet link
- [ ]  Have objection handling library open (SD-07 Reference)
- [ ]  Have pricing page open for quick reference
- [ ]  Set timer: 30 minutes strict

# 📞 DISCOVERY CALL SOP {#discovery}

**Duration:** 30 minutes | **Format:** Google Meet / Zoom

## Minute-by-Minute Structure

| Time | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 min | Welcome + quick rapport ("How's your week going?") |
| 2–5 min | Set the agenda: "I'll ask you some questions, then share how we might help — and we'll see if it's a fit." |
| 5–15 min | SPIN Discovery Questions (see below) |
| 15–22 min | Present the relevant solution (1–2 service options max) |
| 22–27 min | Handle objections |
| 27–29 min | Agree on next step (send proposal / book follow-up / close on call) |
| 29–30 min | Thank them + confirm email for proposal |

## SPIN Discovery Questions

**Situation (understand their setup):**

- "Tell me a bit about your current team — is it just you, or do you have support?"
- "What tools are you using to manage clients/projects right now?"

**Problem (surface the pain):**

- "What's eating the most of your time right now that you wish you could hand off?"
- "What's not working in your current setup?"

**Implication (deepen the cost):**

- "If this problem continues another 6 months, what does that mean for your growth?"
- "How much is this costing you in time or missed opportunities each week?"

**Need-Payoff (paint the picture):**

- "If you had someone handling [X task] completely, what would you do with that time?"
- "What would it mean for your business if you could solve this in the next 30 days?"

## Key Rules During the Call

- **Listen 70%, talk 30%**
- Take notes in HubSpot (deal notes field) during the call
- Never give pricing before you understand the full picture
- Never offer more than 2 service options — it creates confusion
- End with a clear next step — never let a call end with "I'll think about it" without a follow-up date locked

# 📄 PROPOSAL CREATION SOP {#proposal}

**Timeline:** Send within 24 hours of discovery call

**Tool:** Google Docs (branded) OR Canva presentation (more visual)

## Step-by-Step

1. **Open the proposal template** (below)
2. **Fill in client details:** name, company, problem statement (from call notes)
3. **Select the right package:** based on their budget and need
4. **Customize the solution section:** reference their specific pain points
5. **Add relevant case study** (same industry if possible)
6. **Calculate ROI** for their specific situation
7. **Set proposal expiry:** "This proposal is valid for 7 days"
8. **Send via email** with personal video message (Loom — 60 sec) summarizing the proposal
9. **Log in HubSpot:** move deal to "Proposal Sent" stage
10. **Create follow-up task:** if no reply in 48 hours, follow up

# 🤝 FOLLOW-UP & CLOSING SOP {#closing}

## After Proposal Sent

| Day | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Day 0 | Send proposal + Loom video |
| Day 2 | If no reply: "Just checking you received this — any questions?" |
| Day 5 | If no reply: "Happy to jump on a 10-min call to walk through it" |
| Day 7 | If no reply: "This proposal expires in 48 hours — just flagging!" |
| Day 10 | If no reply: Move to "Closed Lost" → tag for 90-day re-engagement |

## Closing Techniques

**Assumptive close:**

> "So based on what you've told me, the Growth package makes the most sense. Shall I send the contract over?"
> 

**Alternative close:**

> "Would you prefer to start with the Starter package to test the fit, or go straight to Growth?"
> 

**Urgency close:**

> "We have one VA slot opening up next Monday — would you like to reserve it?"
> 

**Summary close:**

> "So just to recap: you're spending 20+ hours on admin, it's costing you growth opportunities, and our VA can handle all of that for $600/month. That's less than $30 per hour saved. Does this make sense to move forward?"
>

# ✍️ CONTRACT & ONBOARDING HANDOFF {#contract}

## Contract Flow

1. **Verbal yes received** → immediately send contract via Docuseal
2. Contract includes: scope, pricing, payment terms, NDA, 30-day replacement guarantee
3. **Contract signed** → move deal to "Closed Won" in HubSpot
4. **n8n trigger fires:** Closed Won → welcome email + Slack alert + onboarding sequence
5. **First invoice sent** via Razorpay (India) or PayPal/Wise (international)
6. **Onboarding call scheduled** within 48 hours of payment

## Onboarding Handoff Checklist

- [ ]  Welcome email sent (automated via n8n)
- [ ]  Onboarding questionnaire sent ([Tally.so](http://Tally.so) form)
- [ ]  VA assigned and introduced via email
- [ ]  Shared workspace created (Notion or Google Drive)
- [ ]  Communication channel set up (Slack or WhatsApp)
- [ ]  First 3 tasks briefed to VA
- [ ]  30-day milestone check-in scheduled

```
• Short bio, team size, countries served, years operating
• 2-3 client testimonial quotes

• Step 1: Sign the contract (Docuseal link)
• Step 2: Make first payment (Razorpay/PayPal link)
• Step 3: We schedule your onboarding call
• Step 4: Meet your VA on Day 1
• "Questions? Book a 10-min call: [Cal.com link]"
```
