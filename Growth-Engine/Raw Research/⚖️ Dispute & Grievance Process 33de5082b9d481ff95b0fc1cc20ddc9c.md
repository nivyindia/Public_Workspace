# ⚖️ Dispute & Grievance Process

## Purpose

This page explains what a VA should do if they disagree with a decision made about their performance, score, salary, or treatment. Every VA has the right to raise a concern through the proper channel. This process exists to make sure disputes are handled fairly, quickly, and documented.

---

## What Can Be Raised as a Dispute

| Issue Type | Example |
| --- | --- |
| QC score dispute | "My leads were marked as bad quality but they met the ICP criteria" |
| Salary / incentive dispute | "My commission wasn't paid for 3 deals that I booked and the closer confirmed" |
| KPI calculation dispute | "My reply rate was flagged as low but I believe the count was incorrect" |
| Disciplinary action dispute | "I received a warning but the reason was not accurate" |
| Supervisor conduct | "My supervisor is applying rules inconsistently or behaving unprofessionally" |
| Unfair removal | "I was removed from the program but I met all performance criteria" |

---

## The 3-Step Process

### Step 1 — Raise with Your Supervisor (within 24 hours)

Most disputes can be resolved at this level. Send a WhatsApp message to your supervisor:

```
Hi [Supervisor name], I'd like to raise a concern about [brief description].
The specific issue is: [what happened / what you believe is incorrect]
I have the following supporting evidence: [CRM screenshots / message history / report]
Can we review this together?
```

- Your supervisor must acknowledge the message within 4 hours
- Your supervisor must provide a resolution or explanation within 24 hours
- If resolved: document the outcome in writing in the team chat

---

### Step 2 — Escalate to VA Manager (if unresolved after 48 hours)

If your supervisor has not resolved the issue within 48 hours, or if the dispute involves your supervisor directly:

- Message the VA Manager directly (contact provided by your supervisor on Day 1)
- Include: your name, the issue, what you raised with the supervisor, and why it was not resolved
- The VA Manager will review within 48 hours and provide a written resolution

---

### Step 3 — Escalate to Operations Head (for serious or unresolved issues)

If the VA Manager's resolution is still unsatisfactory, or if the dispute involves the VA Manager:

- Request escalation to the Operations Head through the VA Manager
- The Operations Head's decision is final
- All salary, incentive, and removal disputes at this level are reviewed with full data (CRM records, reports, QC logs)

---

## Evidence You Should Keep

For any dispute, keep the following as evidence before raising it:

- Screenshots of your CRM entries (date-stamped)
- Screenshots of your daily reports
- Screenshots of any messages with your supervisor
- Your KPI calculations from the reporting page
- Call booking confirmations (for commission disputes)

> The more evidence you have, the faster the dispute is resolved. Disputes without evidence may take longer and may default to the supervisor's records.
> 

---

## What Will NOT Be Accepted as a Dispute

- Disagreements with the KPI targets themselves (targets are set by management and are not negotiable)
- Performance removal after consistent KPI failure over 2+ weeks
- Disciplinary removal for data violations or fake leads (zero tolerance — no dispute process)
- Requests to change or override a supervisor's quality assessment without evidence

## Timeline Summary

| Step | Who | Timeframe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raise with supervisor | VA → Supervisor | Within 24 hours of incident |
| Supervisor resolution | Supervisor | Within 24 hours of raise |
| Escalate to VA Manager | VA → VA Manager | If unresolved after 48 hours |
| VA Manager resolution | VA Manager | Within 48 hours |
| Final escalation | VA Manager → Ops Head | For serious or unresolved issues |

---

> Raising a genuine dispute will never be held against you. Every VA has the right to question a decision through this process. What is NOT acceptable: going public, messaging external parties, or threatening the company. Any of those actions result in immediate removal.
