## Purpose

This page defines the exact criteria for VA progression between levels, the incentive structure, and the recognition system. Every VA knows exactly what they need to do to get promoted.

---

## Progression Criteria

### VA Intern → VA Executive

**Both conditions must be met:**

| Requirement | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Consecutive scorecard performance | 30 days at 🟢 80%+ |
| Calls booked | Minimum 10 confirmed calls booked |
| Onboarding gates | All 4 gates passed and on record |
| QC pass rate | ≥90% across the qualifying period |
| No active retraining plan | Must not be in a retraining period at time of nomination |

**Process:**

1. VA Manager identifies the VA has hit all criteria
2. Manager documents evidence in the VA’s scorecard
3. Manager nominates to Ops Head: “[VA Name] eligible for Executive promotion — criteria met [dates].”
4. Ops Head reviews and confirms
5. Promotion effective from next Monday. VA notified in team channel.

---

### VA Executive → VA Manager

**All conditions must be met:**

| Requirement | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Consecutive scorecard performance | 60 days at ⭐ 90%+ |
| Calls booked | Minimum 30 confirmed calls booked (cumulative) |
| Leadership demonstration | Documented evidence of 1+ leadership moment (e.g., trained a new VA, identified a system issue and proposed a fix, handled a team problem independently) |
| Reliability record | No missed reports, no SLA breaches in final 30 days |
| No active retraining plan | Must not be in retraining at time of nomination |

**Process:**

1. Manager identifies criteria are met. Documents in scorecard.
2. Manager nominates to Ops Head with evidence summary.
3. Ops Head conducts 1 conversation with the VA (informal assessment).
4. If approved: promotion effective from next Monday.
5. New Manager receives a 3-day handover briefing on Manager SOPs before taking the role.

---

### VA Manager → Senior / Lead Manager

**Nomination only.** Ops Head assesses:

- Team results under this Manager’s leadership (team average score ≥80% for 60+ days)
- Number of VAs this Manager has onboarded and progressed
- System contributions (SOPs updated, issues identified, team culture improved)
- Founder’s personal assessment of reliability and strategic thinking

No fixed timeline. This role is created when the team grows to a size that requires a lead layer.

---

## Fast-Track Promotion

If a VA hits VA Executive criteria in under 21 days (exceptionally fast), the Manager can nominate for early promotion without waiting for Day 30.

Conditions:

- All scorecard metrics must be consistently above 90% (not just 80%)
- Minimum 10 calls booked
- Manager documents justification: specific examples of exceptional performance

Fast-track promotions are rare and meaningful. They should be genuinely earned, not used as motivation tools.

## Incentive Structure

| Incentive | Trigger | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Performance Bonus** | ⭐ 90%+ for 4 consecutive weeks | Bonus amount set by Ops Head. Communicated at the start of each quarter. |
| **Fast-Track Promotion** | Exceptional performance — see above | Promotion to next level ahead of standard timeline |
| **Weekly Recognition** | Top performer each week | Name announced in team WhatsApp group every Monday |
| **Progression Milestone Gift** | Promotion to Executive or Manager | Small recognition gift or bonus (Ops Head discretion) |

> **Important:** Bonus amounts are set and communicated by the Ops Head at the start of each quarter, not in the moment. Ambiguity about pay creates distrust. Set it clearly, in writing, before the quarter starts.
>

## Demotion & Performance Protocol

This is not punitive — it is protective. A VA in the wrong role fails faster than one given a chance to stabilise.

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 2 consecutive weeks below 60% | Mandatory 1:1 + written retraining plan |
| 4 consecutive weeks below 60% | Role review — step back to previous level or exit conversation |
| VA Manager whose team average drops below 60% for 4+ weeks | Ops Head review — assess management decisions and support needed |
| Serious SLA breach (e.g., multiple missed reports, no-show) | Written formal warning. Second instance = exit process. |
