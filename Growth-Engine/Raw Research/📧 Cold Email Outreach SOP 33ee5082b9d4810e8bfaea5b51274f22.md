# 📧 Cold Email Outreach SOP

<aside>
📌

This SOP covers cold email outreach from setup to execution. Email has a lower response rate than LinkedIn or WhatsApp but scales the furthest and reaches decision makers who aren’t active on social media. Follow this SOP exactly — unstructured email outreach gets sent to spam and can permanently damage the domain.

</aside>

---

## Why Cold Email Still Works

Email is the only channel where you can reach a prospect at scale without platform restrictions. LinkedIn has connection limits. WhatsApp has daily message caps. Email, when done correctly, can reach thousands of qualified prospects per week.

The challenge is deliverability. Emails sent from a bad setup go directly to spam and are never seen. This SOP ensures your emails get delivered, opened, and read.

---

## Before You Start — Email Account Setup

Do not use your personal Gmail or your main Nivy email address for cold outreach. Cold outreach needs a dedicated sending address. Ask your supervisor to set this up for you before you start.

**What your supervisor will provide:**

- A dedicated outreach email address (e.g. [name@outreach.thenivy.com](mailto:name@outreach.thenivy.com) or similar)
- Access to the email sending tool (currently: [ask supervisor — likely Apollo, Instantly, or Lemlist])
- The email signature template to use
- Confirmation that SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are configured (technical setup that prevents spam filtering)

**Do not start cold email outreach until these are confirmed.**

## How to Find Verified Email Addresses

Never guess email addresses. Only send to addresses that have been verified.

**Tool 1 — [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io):** Search by job title, industry, company size, and location. Apollo provides verified email addresses for decision makers. Export to your CRM sheet.

**Tool 2 — [Hunter.io](http://Hunter.io):** Enter a company domain and Hunter finds all publicly associated email addresses. Good for finding the right person at a specific company.

**Tool 3 — LinkedIn + Apollo combined:** Find the prospect on LinkedIn, copy their name and company, search in Apollo to get the verified email.

**Tool 4 — Google:** Search *"[first name] [last name] [company name] email"* or *"[company name] CEO email"*. Many business owners list their email publicly.

**Tool 5 — Company website:** Check the About, Team, or Contact pages. Many SMEs list direct emails.

**Verification step:** Before adding to your sending list, verify the email using [Hunter.io](http://Hunter.io)’s free verifier or NeverBounce. Sending to unverified emails increases bounce rate and damages deliverability.

## Daily Limits — Follow Exactly

| Account Age | Max Emails/Day |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1–2 (warming up) | 10–20 |
| Week 3–4 | 20–40 |
| Month 2+ (warmed) | 40–70 |

> A new email address that sends 100 cold emails on Day 1 will be flagged as spam by Google/Outlook and potentially blacklisted. Always warm up slowly.

## Subject Line Rules

- Keep subject lines under 50 characters
- Never use ALL CAPS in subject lines
- Avoid spam trigger words: *Free, Guarantee, Limited time, Act now, Click here, Best price, 100%*
- Personalise where possible — their name or company name increases open rate significantly
- Question-based subject lines outperform statement-based ones

---

## Email Writing Rules

- Maximum 5–8 lines total per email — shorter is better
- One clear ask per email — usually a question or a call booking
- No attachments in Email 1 (spam trigger)
- No images or HTML formatting — plain text emails have higher deliverability
- Always personalise the first line — never start with "I wanted to reach out..."
- Always end with a question or a clear next step

---

## CRM Tracking for Email

Every email contact must be in your CRM with:

- Name, business, email address
- Which email in the sequence was last sent
- Date sent
- Status (Sent / Opened / Replied / Interested / Booked / Not Interested)
- Next action date

If using a tool like Apollo or Instantly, check the open/reply tracking dashboard daily and update CRM accordingly.

---

## If They Reply

**Interested reply:** Move immediately to booking a call.

> *"Great to hear from you — the best next step is a quick 15-minute discovery call with our team. Would [day] or [day] work?"*
> 

**Unsubscribe / remove me request:** Remove immediately from your list and mark as **Do Not Contact** in CRM. This is legally required in US/UK/AU markets.

**Rude reply:** Do not respond. Mark as **Do Not Contact**. Flag to supervisor if needed.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Sending from your main personal email (ruins your personal domain reputation)
- Not warming up the account before sending at volume
- Sending attachments in the first email
- Using HTML templates that trigger spam filters
- Sending to unverified emails (increases bounce rate above 3% = deliverability problems)
- Copying the same email word-for-word to 50 people (spam filters detect this)
- Not tracking replies and letting interested leads go cold
