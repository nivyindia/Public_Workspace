# ✅ Can You Send Bulk Email Campaigns Using Free Gmail / Outlook?

### Short Answer:

**Yes, but only for small-scale outreach**, not real marketing campaigns.

Free email accounts like:

- Gmail (@gmail.com)
- Outlook/Hotmail (@outlook.com)

are designed for **personal emailing**, not mass marketing.

If you send too many emails at once:

- Your account can get restricted
- Emails go to spam
- Delivery drops badly

# ⚠ Gmail & Outlook Sending Limits (Important)

## Gmail Free Account Limits:

- 500 emails/day max
- Safe outreach: **100–200/day**

## Outlook Free Account Limits:

- ~300 emails/day
- Safe outreach: **50–150/day**

If exceeded → temporary ban.

# ✅ Recommended Setup for Bulk Email Campaigns (Free + Safe)

## Step-by-Step System:

### Step 1: Collect Emails Properly

Use:

- Google Forms
- Website opt-in
- Lead magnets

Never scrape random emails.

---

### Step 2: Use Brevo or MailerLite Free Plan

Example:

- Upload contacts CSV
- Create email template
- Schedule campaign

---

### Step 3: Connect Gmail/Outlook Sender

In Brevo/MailerLite:

- Settings → Sender → Add Email
- Verify OTP
- Start sending

---

### Step 4: Warm Up Your Email First (Very Important)

Before sending 300/day:

Week 1: 20–30/day

Week 2: 50–100/day

Week 3: 150+/day

This avoids spam flagging.

---

### Step 5: Use Proper Email Structure

Good campaign emails must include:

✅ Subject line

✅ Personal name

✅ Unsubscribe option

✅ Real business info

✅ Not spammy words ("FREE!!!", "Buy now")
