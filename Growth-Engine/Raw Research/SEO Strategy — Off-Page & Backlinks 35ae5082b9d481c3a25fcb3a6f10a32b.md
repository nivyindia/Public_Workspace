# 🌐 Why Off-Page SEO Matters {#why}

Off-page SEO = signals from other websites that tell Google your site is trustworthy and authoritative. The more high-quality backlinks you have, the higher you rank.

**Key metric: Domain Authority (DA)** — scores 0–100. New site starts at ~10. Target: DA 30+ in 12 months.

---

# 🔗 Backlink Building Tactics {#tactics}

## Tier 1 — High Impact

| Tactic | Effort | Quality | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Guest posting on industry blogs | High | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Best long-term investment |
| Digital PR (journalist mentions) | High | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Highest authority links |
| Podcast appearances (with website mention) | Medium | ⭐⭐⭐ | Build brand + backlinks |
| Resource page link building | Medium | ⭐⭐⭐ | "Useful links" pages on other sites |

## Tier 2 — Medium Impact

| Tactic | Effort | Quality | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Business directory listings | Low | ⭐⭐ | Google My Business, Clutch, Bark, etc. |
| Partner website mentions | Low | ⭐⭐⭐ | Referral partner link exchanges |
| Forum / community profiles | Low | ⭐ | Reddit, Quora, niche forums |
| Social profile links | Low | ⭐ | LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram |

## Tier 3 — Do Not Do

- ❌ Buying backlinks from link farms
- ❌ PBN (private blog network) links
- ❌ Automated link building tools
- ❌ Comment spam on blogs

# ✏️ Guest Posting System {#guest-post}

## Step 1: Find Opportunities

- Google: `write for us + accounting` / `write for us + digital marketing` / `write for us + small business`
- Ahrefs: find where competitors have guest posts (Site Explorer → Backlinks)
- Target: DA 30+ sites in our niche

## Step 2: Pitch

Email the editor:

> Subject: Guest Post Pitch — [Topic Idea]
> 

> "Hi [Name], I’m [Your name], founder of Nivy Digital. We help SMEs across the UK, US, and UAE with accounting, IT, and marketing. I’d love to contribute a guest post to [Site Name] on [Topic]. Here’s a quick outline: [3 bullet points]. Would this be a fit?"
> 

## Step 3: Write & Submit

- Write 1,000–1,500 word post
- Include 1 natural backlink to Nivy Digital website (relevant anchor text)
- Follow their editorial guidelines

## Monthly Target: 2 guest posts per month

# 📰 Digital PR {#pr}

## Tools

- **HARO (Help a Reporter Out):** Journalists seeking expert sources — respond to relevant queries
- **Qwoted / SourceBottle:** Similar to HARO
- **ResponseSource (UK):** UK journalist queries

## Process

1. Sign up to HARO (free) — receive 3 emails/day with journalist queries
2. Scan for accounting, business, SME, fintech, or tech queries
3. Respond within 1 hour of receiving (journalists move fast)
4. Provide a sharp, quotable expert response
5. If featured: major DA boost + brand mention in national/industry press

**Target: 2–3 PR mentions per month by Month 6**

# 📊 Tracking Backlinks {#tracking}

| Tool | What to Track | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ahrefs / Semrush | New backlinks, referring domains, DA | Monthly |
| Google Search Console | External links section | Monthly |
| Ahrefs Alerts | New + lost backlinks email alert | Ongoing |

**Targets:**

- Month 3: 20+ referring domains
- Month 6: 50+ referring domains, DA 20+
- Month 12: 100+ referring domains, DA 30+
