# ⚙️ SENDING SETUP ([Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io))

1. Import lead list from Apollo or HubSpot
2. Create Sequence in Apollo
3. Set timing: Email 1 (Day 0), Email 2 (Day 3 or 4), Email 3 (Day 7–9), Email 4 (Day 14)
4. Set send time: 9:00 AM–11:00 AM in recipient's timezone
5. Enable tracking: opens, clicks, replies
6. Connect to HubSpot: all replies auto-sync
7. Review replies daily — respond within 2 hours

**Daily Volume Limits:**

- New domain (under 3 months): max 30 emails/day
- Warmed domain (3–6 months): max 100 emails/day
- Established domain: max 200 emails/day
