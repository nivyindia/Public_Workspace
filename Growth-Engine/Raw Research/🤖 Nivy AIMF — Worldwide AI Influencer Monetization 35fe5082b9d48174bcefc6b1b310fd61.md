# 📌 BUSINESS MODEL OVERVIEW

**What is Nivy AIMF?**

Nivy AIMF creates, manages, and monetizes AI-generated influencer identities (avatars, voices, personas) across global platforms. Revenue streams include brand deals, affiliate marketing, digital products, subscriptions, ad revenue, shoutouts, licensing, and platform monetization funds.

**Core Value Proposition:**

- Zero human burnout — AI creates content 24/7
- Scalable across unlimited niches and languages
- Localised content for each country/market
- Compounding revenue as audience grows

**Three AI Persona Types:**

1. **Creator Persona** — Lifestyle, finance, tech, travel content
2. **Expert Persona** — Coaching, consulting, course delivery
3. **Character Persona** — Entertainment, gaming, storytelling

```
     ↓

     ↓

     ↓

```

```
[1] Viewer sees content on any platform
     ↓

     ↓

     ↓

     ↓
[5] Email sequence → Digital product upsell
     ↓
[6] Stripe/Paddle processes payment
     ↓
[7] Automatic digital delivery via Teachable/Gumroad
     → Upsell to membership/course
     ↓
[8] Retargeting ads via Meta/Google for non-buyers
```
