## 🎯 What Changes at Tier 3

At Tier 1 and 2, your job was to qualify leads and book calls.

At Tier 3, you learn how to:

- Position the right package for the right client
- Turn a Level 1 client into a Level 2 retainer
- Recognise upsell and cross-sell moments in conversation
- Handle the most common objections before escalating
- Adapt your pitch for USA vs International markets

This is what separates a VA Intern from a VA Executive.

## 📚 Tier 3 Documents — Read in Order

### 1. Package Differentiator Guide

What makes Level 1 different from Level 2? Why would a client pay more for Level 3?

This guide answers those questions so you can explain differences confidently.

→ 🔍 **Package Differentiator Guide — What's Different Between Levels**

### 2. Upsell & Cross-Sell Playbook

The most valuable skill a VA can have. This playbook shows you:

- When to suggest an upsell (timing in the conversation)
- Exactly what to say (scripts included)
- How to cross-sell a second service without being pushy

→ 🔁 **Sell → Upsell → Cross-Sell Playbook**

### 3. USA Market Playbook

High-ticket clients in the USA have different expectations, vocabulary, and decision timelines.

This playbook tells you how to position Nivy for the USA market.

→ 🌎 **USA Market Playbook — High-Ticket Positioning**

### 4. International Market Playbook

Clients from UK, Australia, Canada, Middle East, and South Asia respond to different framing.

This playbook tells you how to adapt your pitch by market.

→ 🌏 **International Market Playbook — Value Framing**

### 5. Common Client Questions & Answers

40+ questions clients actually ask — with answers you can use directly.

Read this once fully. Refer back when a client asks something you haven't handled before.

→ ❓ **Common Client Questions & Package Answers**
