# ✅ Lead Qualification Guide

##

This phase ensures you **only spend time on prospects who can actually become clients**. The goal is to filter out **unqualified leads early** so your outreach focuses on people **likely to buy services**. 🎯

---

# Main Qualification Criteria

When evaluating a lead found through LinkedIn, Apollo.io, or ZoomInfo, check these factors:

### 1. Do They Run a Business?

Look for titles like:

- Founder
- CEO
- Owner
- Managing Director
- Marketing Director

These roles **usually control budget decisions**.

Avoid:

❌ Interns

❌ Junior employees

❌ Students

---

### 2. Do They Outsource Work?

Signs they outsource:

- They mention **marketing agencies**
- They hire **contractors**
- Their company website lists **external partners**

Example indicators:

- “Working with external consultants”
- “Partnering with marketing agencies”

These companies are **more comfortable paying for services**.

---

### 3. Do They Have a Team?

Companies with **multiple employees** are better prospects.

Look for:

- **10–50 employees** → ideal for agencies
- **50–200 employees** → higher budgets

You can check this on:

- LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Company LinkedIn page
- Company website

Avoid:

❌ Solo freelancers with no revenue

---

### 4. Do They Hire Freelancers?

If a company hires freelancers on platforms like:

- Upwork
- Fiverr

It means:

✔ They already spend money on services

✔ They understand outsourcing

These leads convert **much easier**.

---

# Quick Qualification Checklist

Before contacting a prospect, confirm:

✔ They run or manage the business

✔ They have a team

✔ They invest in external help

✔ They operate in your niche

If **3–4 criteria match**, they are **high-quality leads** worth messaging.

You can score leads in your CRM:

| Score | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Low-quality lead |
| 2 | Some potential |
| 3 | Good lead |
| 4 | Very strong lead |
| 5 | Ideal client |

Example:

| Name | Company | Team Size | Score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| John Smith | Peak Roofing | 25 employees | 5 |
| Maria Lee | FitLab Gym | 8 employees | 3 |

---

# Ideal Lead Profile Example

A strong prospect might look like this:

- Founder of a **20–50 employee company**
- Active on LinkedIn
- Already outsourcing some work
- Hiring freelancers or agencies

This type of lead is **much more likely to convert**.
