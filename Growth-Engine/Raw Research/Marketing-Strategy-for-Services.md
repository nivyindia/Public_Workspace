## What makes something go viral?

Most viral offers share several characteristics:

- **Simple:** Easy to understand in one sentence.
- **High perceived value:** Feels like a great deal.
- **Low friction:** Easy to join.
- **Shareable:** People want to tell others.
- **Time-limited or scarce:** Encourages action.
- **Aligned with your business:** The offer attracts people who may become customers.
