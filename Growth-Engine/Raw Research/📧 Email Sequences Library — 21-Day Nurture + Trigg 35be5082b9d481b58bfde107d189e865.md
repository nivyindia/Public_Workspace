## 📊 Sequence Overview

| Day | Channel | Type | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day 0 | Email | Welcome + value | Open + first impression |
| Day 1 | WhatsApp | Personal check-in | Establish direct line |
| Day 3 | Email | Pain-point education | Build relevance |
| Day 5 | Email | Case study | Build credibility |
| Day 7 | WhatsApp | Soft CTA | Invite to call |
| Day 10 | Email | Objection pre-empt | Remove hesitation |
| Day 12 | Email | Lead magnet / resource | Deepen trust score |
| Day 14 | Email | Direct ask | Book the call |
| Day 16 | LinkedIn | Connection + message | Multi-channel touch |
| Day 18 | WhatsApp | Final personal message | Last chance CTA |
| Day 21 | Email | Breakup email | Force a decision |

**After Day 21 with no response:** Move to COLD list (monthly newsletter only).

## 📧 THE 8 EMAILS

---

### EMAIL 1 — Day 0 — Welcome + Value

**Subject:** One thing most [industry] businesses get wrong

**Body:**

```
Hi {{first_name}},

Thanks for connecting — I wanted to reach out personally.

We work with [industry] businesses on [service area], 
and the single most common issue we see is [pain point].

Most owners are so focused on [surface problem] that they 
miss the real issue underneath, which is [root cause].

The good news: it’s fixable, usually within 60 days.

I’ll share a bit more about how we approach this over 
the next few days — but if you’d rather jump straight 
to a conversation, here’s my calendar: [Cal.com link]

[Your name]
Nivy Digital
```

**Trust score impact:** +5 (open), +10 (click)

### EMAIL 8 — Day 21 — Breakup Email

**Subject:** Closing your file, {{first_name}}

**Body:**

```
Hi {{first_name}},

I’ve reached out a few times over the past few weeks, 
and I haven’t heard back — which I completely understand.

I’m going to take you off my follow-up list. 
I don’t want to keep sending emails you’re not finding useful.

If the timing changes — or if something I mentioned 
stays with you — you’re always welcome to reach out: 
[your email] or [Cal.com link]

I genuinely wish {{company}} all the best.

[Your name]
```

**Note:** This email often gets the highest reply rate of the entire sequence. The feeling of loss triggers responses.

## 📧 THE 1 LINKEDIN TOUCH

### LINKEDIN — Day 16

1. Connect with the lead on LinkedIn (if not already connected)
2. Send connection note: *"Hi [Name], been following [Company] — impressed by [specific thing on their profile]. Thought it was worth connecting directly."*
3. After they accept, send message: *"Thanks for connecting! I’ve actually sent you a couple of emails over the past few weeks — just wanted to reach out here too in case that's a better channel. Happy to chat whenever the timing's right."*

## ⚡ BEHAVIORAL TRIGGER EMAILS

These run **in parallel** to the sequence and fire based on lead behaviour. They are **NOT** part of the 21-day cadence — they interrupt it and override it.

---

### TRIGGER 1 — Pricing Page Visit (fires within 1 hour)

**Condition:** Lead visits the pricing/services page on your website

**Subject:** Noticed you checked out our services — quick question

```
Hi {{first_name}},

I noticed you had a look at our services page — 
wanted to reach out while it’s fresh.

Is there a specific service you were looking at, 
or were you getting a general sense of what we offer?

Happy to walk you through anything in a 10-minute call 
if that’s useful.

[Cal.com link]

[Your name]
```

**Also:** Send internal HOT alert to founder if trust score hits ≥80 after this visit.

---

### TRIGGER 2 — Email Re-Open After 7+ Days Silence (fires within 24 hours)

**Condition:** Lead re-opens an email after 7+ days of zero email activity

**Subject:** Good to hear from you — still thinking about it?

```
Hi {{first_name}},

I saw you revisited one of my earlier emails — 
wanted to check in.

If something has changed in your situation, or 
if you had a question you didn’t get to ask, 
I’m here.

Even a quick reply to let me know where things 
stand would be helpful.

[Your name]
```

---

### TRIGGER 3 — Audit Requested (fires immediately)

**Condition:** Lead submits the Free Business Audit form (Tally)

**Subject:** Got your audit request — here’s what happens next

```
Hi {{first_name}},

Thank you for requesting a free audit — I’ve seen 
your details and will personally review your setup.

You can expect to hear from me within 24 hours with 
either initial findings or a request for any additional 
information I might need.

In the meantime, if there’s anything specific you’d 
like me to focus on, just reply and let me know.

[Your name]
```

**Trust score impact:** +40. Move to HOT queue immediately.
