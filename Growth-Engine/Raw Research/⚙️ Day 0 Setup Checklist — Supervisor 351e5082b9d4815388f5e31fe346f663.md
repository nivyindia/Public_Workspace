# ⚙️ Day 0 Setup Checklist — Supervisor

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** Ops Head | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who uses this:** New Supervisors and QC Officers — complete before your first day managing a team
> 

---

> ⚠️ **Complete every item before managing your first team session.** A supervisor who starts without these in place loses the first day to setup, not to managing.
> 

---

## 📊 1. Access Confirmation

- [ ]  CRM access confirmed — you can VIEW all lead rows (not just your VA's rows)
- [ ]  CRM access confirmed — you can add notes and flag entries
- [ ]  Notion access confirmed — you can open [👔 Supervisor & Management Tools](%F0%9F%91%94%20Supervisor%20&%20Management%20Tools%2033ee5082b9d481818219e86da0da14fc.md)
- [ ]  Notion access confirmed — you can open [📊 Supervisor Tracking & QC System](%F0%9F%93%8A%20Supervisor%20Tracking%20&%20QC%20System%2033ee5082b9d4812ebaf5d656d4a56326.md)
- [ ]  Management WhatsApp group — you have been added and have confirmed receipt

---

## 👥 2. Team Setup

- [ ]  You know the names and WhatsApp numbers of all VAs you are managing
- [ ]  You have created or been added to a team WhatsApp group with your VAs
- [ ]  You have confirmed each VA's working hours and timezone
- [ ]  You have the list of CRM lead rows assigned to each of your VAs
- [ ]  You have confirmed each VA has completed their Day 0 setup

---

## 📚 3. Read Before Day 1

- [ ]  Read [👔 Supervisor SOP](%F0%9F%91%94%20Supervisor%20SOP%2033ee5082b9d481d3843de5fc6be8c416.md) — your full daily operating protocol
- [ ]  Read [🃏 Supervisor Quick Reference Card](https://app.notion.com/p/351e5082b9d4816ea8d0ffa2ecde2b03?pvs=21) — your daily cheat sheet
- [ ]  Read [📋 Document Control Standards & Naming Guide](%F0%9F%93%8B%20Document%20Control%20Standards%20&%20Naming%20Guide%20351e5082b9d4814ea2e7dbc6ab9155d4.md) — how the system is structured
- [ ]  Read [⚠️ VA Error Handling System](%E2%9A%A0%EF%B8%8F%20VA%20Error%20Handling%20System%20351e5082b9d48110875cf4d20131bdf9.md) — how errors are handled
- [ ]  Skim [🧭 VA Trainee Path](%F0%9F%A7%AD%20VA%20Trainee%20Path%2033ee5082b9d481a2ba46df5a8f3482c1.md) — understand what your VAs are experiencing

---

## 📝 4. Daily Report Prep

- [ ]  You have the Supervisor Daily Report format saved (in [👔 Supervisor & Management Tools](%F0%9F%91%94%20Supervisor%20&%20Management%20Tools%2033ee5082b9d481818219e86da0da14fc.md))
- [ ]  You know the submission channel and deadline (7:00 PM daily, management WhatsApp)
- [ ]  You understand which metrics to track: leads, messages, follow-ups, QC violations, calls booked

---

## 📅 5. First Day Plan

- [ ]  Morning brief prepared: daily targets for each VA ready to send at start of shift
- [ ]  You know which 5 messages per VA you will spot-check (QC review)
- [ ]  1:00 PM midday checkpoint reminder set
- [ ]  End-of-day report reminder set for 6:30 PM

---

## ✔️ 6. Final Confirmation

- [ ]  All items above are checked
- [ ]  Message sent to your VA Manager: *"Supervisor Day 0 complete — [Your Name]. Team of [X] VAs. Ready to start."*
