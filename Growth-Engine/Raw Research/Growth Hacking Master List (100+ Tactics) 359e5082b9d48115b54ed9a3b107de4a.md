# 🔧 How to Use This List {#how}

1. Browse by category
2. Score each tactic: **Impact (1–5) × Effort (1–5) = Priority Score** (high impact, low effort = highest priority)
3. Pick 3–5 tactics per month to test
4. Log results in Growth Experiments Tracker
5. Scale what works; kill what doesn’t after 30 days

# 💼 LinkedIn Growth Tactics {#linkedin}

1. Post a controversial opinion in your niche
2. Share a “I wish someone told me this” list
3. Write a carousel on a common mistake your ICP makes
4. Post a before/after result (client outcome)
5. Comment on 10 posts by ideal clients every day
6. Send 20 personalised connection requests daily
7. Publish a LinkedIn article (SEO value)
8. Run a LinkedIn poll on a hot topic
9. Tag clients/partners in milestone posts
10. Post a behind-the-scenes look at your process
11. Share a stat with your hot take
12. Create a "Day in the life of a VA" Reel
13. Do a LinkedIn Live Q&A (monthly)
14. Feature a client spotlight post
15. Repost a client's win with commentary
16. Create a "10 things I learned" post
17. Ask a question your ICP is Googling
18. Post your system / framework as a visual
19. Write about a failure and what you learned
20. Create a niche resource list (tools, templates)

# 📧 Email & Outbound Tactics {#email}

1. A/B test 2 subject lines on every campaign
2. Add a P.S. with lead magnet link in every cold email
3. Send a "break-up" email to cold leads at day 14
4. Re-engage cold leads with a new angle every 90 days
5. Send a "value bomb" email with no CTA (just goodwill)
6. Use video loom in outreach to top 10 prospects
7. Run a 5-day email challenge for your list
8. Segment list by ICP and send tailored sequences
9. Send a case study email to warm leads monthly
10. Use voice notes on WhatsApp for warm prospects
11. Follow up every proposal within 48 hours
12. Send a "one year from now" visioning email to cold leads
13. Create a "results roundup" email (monthly client wins)
14. Personalise email openers using AI (recent post, news)
15. Test plain text emails vs. formatted ones

# 👥 Community & Network Tactics {#community}

1. Join 5 Facebook Groups where your ICP hangs out
2. Engage authentically in groups for 30 days before pitching
3. Host a free monthly webinar for your ICP
4. Create a WhatsApp community for founders/coaches
5. Attend 1 virtual networking event per month
6. Partner with complementary service providers
7. Create a referral army from past clients
8. Ask for LinkedIn recommendations from every client
9. Feature partners in your content (they’ll share it)
10. Sponsor or speak at niche events (local or virtual)
11. Start a Slack or Discord community for your niche
12. Participate in Twitter/X Spaces in your niche
13. Build relationships with podcast hosts before pitching
14. Offer free audits to community members (top of funnel)
15. Create a free accountability group for your ICP

# 🤖 Automation & Leverage Tactics {#automation}

1. Auto-share new blog posts to LinkedIn via n8n
2. Set up automated welcome email for every new subscriber
3. Auto-tag CRM leads by source using form UTMs
4. Build a chatbot on website to capture leads 24/7
5. Auto-send review request after 30 days of client onboarding
6. Set up a re-engagement automation for 90-day cold leads
7. Auto-notify VA when a new form lead comes in
8. Use Zapier/n8n to push WhatsApp leads into CRM
9. Build a LinkedIn auto-post scheduler (Buffer/Taplio)
10. Create automated monthly reporting dashboards
11. Set up deal stage notifications in HubSpot
12. Auto-assign leads to outreach sequences by ICP type
13. Set up abandoned booking recovery (email if Calendly not completed)
14. Build a client check-in automation (day 7, 30, 60)
15. Auto-create Notion task when new client is won

# 🤝 Partnership & Collaboration Tactics {#partnerships}

1. Partner with web design agencies (they have VA-needing clients)
2. Partner with accountants/CFOs (SME clients need VA support)
3. Partner with business coaches (their clients are our ICP)
4. Offer co-branded lead magnets with partners
5. Run a joint webinar with a complementary provider
6. Create a white-label offering for agencies
7. Approach HR/recruitment firms for VA referrals
8. Build relationships with coworking spaces in India
9. Partner with SaaS tools that serve your ICP (integrations, co-marketing)
10. Create a partner directory on your website (mutual linking)

# 💰 Paid & Distribution Tactics {#paid}

1. Run LinkedIn Lead Gen ads to warm audiences (retargeting)
2. Boost top-performing LinkedIn posts ($10–30/post)
3. Run Google Ads on high-intent keywords (VA agency + location)
4. Create a retargeting pixel campaign for website visitors
5. Sponsor a relevant newsletter in your niche
6. List on Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush (free + paid)
7. Run a limited-time offer campaign (first 3 months 20% off)
8. Test Facebook/Instagram ads to founder audiences
9. Pay for a feature in a founder-focused newsletter
10. Run a referral contest (first 3 referrers win a prize)
