# KB-003 — What is a Booked Call

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

---

## What is a Booked Call?

A Booked Call is a **confirmed discovery call between a qualified lead and a Nivy closer** — where the lead has agreed to a specific date and time and the calendar invite has been sent and accepted (or confirmed via message).

---

## What Counts as a Booked Call

✅ Lead replied positively AND gave availability

✅ Calendar invite sent via Calendly AND lead confirmed receipt

✅ OR lead confirmed the time directly in the chat (WhatsApp/LinkedIn/email)

✅ Call details passed to closer via SOP-VA-014 format

✅ Call appears in the manager's CRM with status: **Booked**

---

## What Does NOT Count as a Booked Call

❌ Lead said "maybe" or "let me think about it"

❌ Lead asked for more information (this is "Interested", not "Booked")

❌ You sent a Calendly link but they haven't clicked or responded

❌ Lead cancelled and no reschedule confirmed

❌ You logged it as booked before receiving confirmation

> **Rule:** A call is only Booked when the lead has confirmed the time. Until then, status is "Interested".
> 

---

## The Booking Flow

1. Lead replies positively ("Tell me more", "That sounds interesting", "How does it work?")
2. VA escalates to **Interested** status in CRM immediately (within 30 minutes)
3. VA sends a soft confirmation message with Calendly link OR 2–3 time options
4. Lead selects a time or confirms availability
5. Calendar invite sent via Calendly
6. VA updates CRM to **Booked**
7. VA completes Call Handover Form (SOP-VA-014) and submits to closer minimum 2 hours before the call
8. VA notifies Manager in the daily report

---

## Exactly What to Send the Closer

When a call is booked, submit this to the closer via SOP-VA-014:

```
Lead Name:
Company:
Role/Title:
Market (US/UK/Canada/AU/UAE):
Platform where booked:
Call Date & Time (with timezone):
Calendly link / invite confirmed: Yes/No
Conversation summary (2–3 lines — what they said, what problem they mentioned):
Suggested Nivy service area:
Any special notes:
```

> **Submit this minimum 2 hours before the scheduled call.** A closer who goes into a call without context is at a disadvantage.
> 

---

## CRM Status Flow

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| New | Just added, no contact yet |
| Contacted | First outreach sent |
| Following Up | In follow-up sequence (Day 3, 7, 14, 30) |
| Interested | Replied positively, not yet confirmed time |
| Booked | Call confirmed with date and time |
| Called | Discovery call completed |
| Closed Won | Became a client |
| Closed Lost | Did not convert |
| Dead | Unresponsive after full follow-up sequence |

---

## Common Mistakes

- Logging as "Booked" when lead only said "sounds interesting" — this inflates numbers and misleads the closer
- Not sending the handover form — the closer has no context for the call
- Sending the Calendly link and not following up when they don't click — one Calendly link is not a booking
- Forgetting to update CRM status after the call happens
