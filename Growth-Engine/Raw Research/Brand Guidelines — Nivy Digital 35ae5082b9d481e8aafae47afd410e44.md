# 💡 Brand Values & Identity {#voice}

**Brand personality:** Professional, trustworthy, modern, approachable, globally minded

**What Nivy Digital stands for:**

- Quality without the premium price tag
- Accountability and transparency
- Global expertise with a personal touch
- Empowering businesses to grow without operational overwhelm

**Brand tagline ideas:**

- *"Your Business. Our Expertise."*
- *"Accounting. IT. Marketing. All in One."*
- *"Built for business. Designed to grow."*

# 🖼️ Logo Usage {#logo}

**Primary logo:** Full horizontal lockup (icon + wordmark)

**Secondary logo:** Icon only (for small formats, favicons, profile pictures)

**Dark background version:** White/light version of logo

**Light background version:** Dark/colour version of logo

**Rules:**

- Never stretch or distort the logo
- Never place logo on a busy or clashing background
- Maintain clear space (minimum padding = height of the logo icon on all sides)
- Never change logo colours outside approved palette
- Minimum digital size: 120px wide

**File formats to use:**

- SVG — for website and digital use (scalable)
- PNG (transparent background) — for documents and presentations
- PDF — for print

# 🎨 Colour Palette {#colours}

| Colour Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary Blue | #1A3C6E | Headlines, CTAs, primary buttons |
| Accent Gold | #F5A623 | Highlights, icons, accents |
| Light Grey | #F5F7FA | Backgrounds, section fills |
| Dark Charcoal | #1C1C2E | Body text, dark sections |
| White | #FFFFFF | Clean backgrounds, text on dark |
| Success Green | #27AE60 | Confirmation, positive signals |

**Colour usage rules:**

- Primary Blue is the dominant brand colour — use on headers and main CTAs
- Accent Gold is a highlight colour only — use sparingly
- Never use more than 3 colours in a single design element
- Text on coloured backgrounds must meet WCAG AA contrast ratio

# ✏️ Typography {#typography}

| Use | Font | Weight | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Headlines (H1) | Inter or Poppins | Bold (700) | 36–56px web / 28–40pt print |
| Sub-headlines (H2) | Inter or Poppins | Semi-Bold (600) | 24–32px |
| Body text | Inter | Regular (400) | 16–18px |
| Captions / Labels | Inter | Medium (500) | 12–14px |
| CTA Buttons | Inter | Bold (700) | 16px, all caps or title case |

**Rules:**

- Never use more than 2 font families in one design
- Minimum body text size: 14px (web), 10pt (print)
- Line height: 1.5–1.7x font size for body text

# 🖼️ Visual Style {#style}

**Photography style:** Real people, bright and natural lighting, professional settings

**Illustration style:** Minimal, geometric, clean line icons

**Icons:** Use a consistent icon set (e.g., Phosphor Icons, Lucide Icons — free and consistent)

**Imagery:** No stock photos of fake-smiling people — use real team photos where possible

**Social graphics:** Always include logo in bottom corner, consistent background colour, branded fonts

---

# 🗣️ Tone of Voice {#tone}

**Be:** Clear, confident, helpful, professional, internationally aware

**Never be:** Salesy, jargon-heavy, condescending, overly formal, vague

**Examples:**

| Instead of... | Say... |
| --- | --- |
| "We leverage cutting-edge solutions" | "We use the right tools to solve your problem" |
| "Our bespoke offerings" | "Our services, tailored to you" |
| "Synergising your financial ecosystem" | "Getting your finances in order" |

---

# 🚫 Brand Don'ts {#donts}

- ❌ Don't use the logo on a gradient background
- ❌ Don't use Comic Sans, Papyrus, or decorative fonts
- ❌ Don't use more than 3 colours in one piece of content
- ❌ Don't publish content with spelling or grammar errors
- ❌ Don't mix formal and casual tone in the same piece
- ❌ Don't use competitor brand names or logos in our content
