# Phase 15 — Client Onboarding

## Phase 15 — Client Onboarding

**Goal:** Collect all information and access required to **start the project efficiently** and align expectations with the client. Proper onboarding prevents confusion and delays later in the project. 🚀

---

# Step 1 — Send Onboarding Questionnaire

After the contract is signed and payment is received, send an onboarding form.

Tools commonly used:

- Google Forms
- Typeform
- Notion

The questionnaire gathers key details about the client's business and objectives.

Example message:

> To get started, please complete our onboarding form so we can better understand your business and goals.
> 

---

# Step 2 — Collect Business Information

Gather important details about the company.

Information to collect:

- Company overview
- Products or services offered
- Target customers
- Current marketing strategies
- Main competitors

This helps the team **understand the business context**.

---

# Step 3 — Collect Account Access

Request access to necessary tools and accounts needed for the project.

Examples:

- Website or CMS access
- Advertising accounts
- CRM systems
- Analytics tools
- Email marketing platforms

Access is usually shared through secure password managers or invitations.

---

# Step 4 — Define Success Metrics

Agree on **how success will be measured**.

Examples of metrics:

- Number of leads generated
- Meetings booked
- Conversion rate
- Website traffic
- Campaign ROI

Defining metrics early ensures **everyone is aligned on expectations**.

---

# Step 5 — Confirm Communication Channels

Establish how the team and client will communicate.

Common communication tools:

- Slack
- Microsoft Teams
- Gmail
- Zoom

Typical communication structure:

- Slack or email for daily communication
- Monthly calls for performance reviews

---

# Typical Onboarding Information Collected

| Item | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Target audience | Understand ideal customers |
| Business goals | Define success metrics |
| Brand assets | Maintain brand consistency |
| Past campaigns | Learn what worked previously |
| Account logins | Access required tools |
| Communication channel | Establish collaboration |

---

# Example Onboarding Flow

Contract Signed

→ Payment Received

→ Onboarding Form Sent

→ Business Information Collected

→ Account Access Granted

→ Communication Channels Established

---

# Best Practices

✔ Keep onboarding **structured and simple**

✔ Store client information in a centralized system

✔ Confirm all access before project start

✔ Schedule a kickoff meeting after onboarding
