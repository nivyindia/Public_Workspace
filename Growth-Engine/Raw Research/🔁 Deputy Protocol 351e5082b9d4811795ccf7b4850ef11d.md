# 🔁 Deputy Protocol

What happens when the Supervisor is absent. Zero gap in coverage.

---

## Purpose

Ensure the VA team is never unsupervised. This protocol activates whenever the Supervisor is absent for any reason — planned or unplanned.

---

## Planned Absence Protocol

1. Supervisor notifies Manager at least 24 hours in advance
2. Supervisor briefs Deputy on:
    - Current team status (who is on track, who is flagged)
    - Any open flags or escalations in progress
    - Any VA in onboarding who needs extra attention
3. Supervisor hands over Supervisor Tracking & QC System access
4. Deputy runs morning QC check and EOD report as normal
5. Supervisor reviews Deputy's log on return

---

## Unplanned Absence Protocol

1. Supervisor notifies Manager as soon as possible
2. Manager activates Deputy immediately
3. Deputy reviews current Supervisor Tracking & QC System for open flags
4. Deputy continues daily protocol without interruption
5. No QC day is skipped — Deputy runs the check regardless

---

## Deputy Responsibilities During Absence

- Run morning QC on 3 VA conversations
- Respond to VA escalations within 2 hours
- Submit EOD report to Manager
- Log any new flags in Supervisor Tracking & QC System

---

## What Deputy Does NOT Do

- Does not make progression decisions (Manager handles those)
- Does not change SLA standards
- Does not approve onboarding gate passes without Manager sign-off

---

## Return Protocol

1. Supervisor reviews Deputy's log for the absence period
2. Supervisor picks up any open flags
3. Supervisor thanks Deputy and confirms handback to Manager
