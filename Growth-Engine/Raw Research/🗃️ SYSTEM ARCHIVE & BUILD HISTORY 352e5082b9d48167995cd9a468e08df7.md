# What Is This Folder?

As the NIVY VA system was built across multiple phases, several plan documents, master plans, audit commands, and execution trackers were created. These are no longer active — the current system supersedes them all — but they are preserved here as a full build history.

They are kept for:

- Understanding the evolution of the system
- Reverting decisions if needed
- Onboarding future Ops Heads who need the full backstory
