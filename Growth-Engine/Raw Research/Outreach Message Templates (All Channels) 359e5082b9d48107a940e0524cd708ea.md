# 📱 WhatsApp Outreach Templates {#whatsapp}

> WhatsApp is for warm leads only — people who have engaged on LinkedIn, responded to email, or opted in via website.
> 

**Initial Message (Warm lead who engaged elsewhere):**

> Hi [Name] 👋, this is [Your Name] from Nivy Digital. We connected on LinkedIn — just wanted to say hi here too. Do you use WhatsApp for business conversations?
> 

**Follow-Up (After confirmation):**

> Great! Quick context: we help [ICP] with [outcome]. Would you be open to a 15-min call this week? Here's a link to book: [Calendly] 🙏
> 

**Value Message (nurture):**

> Hey [Name], sharing something your [niche] peers are doing right now: [insight]. Let me know if you'd like to chat about it!
> 

**Rules for WhatsApp outreach:**

- Only contact warm leads (prior engagement required)
- Max 1 unsolicited message — wait for reply before continuing
- Never send bulk/broadcast to cold contacts
- Always introduce yourself fully in first message
- Respect timezone — send between 9am–6pm recipient local time

# 📚 Template Usage Rules {#rules}

1. **Always personalise** — replace every `[bracket]` with real, researched info
2. **One template per contact per channel** — don't double up on LinkedIn + email with same message
3. **Track which template was used** in HubSpot contact notes
4. **Flag top-performing templates** weekly — update this library monthly
5. **Never copy-paste without reading** — review for tone fit before sending
6. **A/B test** — try 2 variants on 50 contacts each before scaling
