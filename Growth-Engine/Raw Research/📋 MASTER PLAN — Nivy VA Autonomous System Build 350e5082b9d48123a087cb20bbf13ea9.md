## 🔵 PHASE 1 — Foundation: Knowledge Base & Role Clarity

**Status:** 🔲 Not started | **Timeline:** Week 1

**Goal:** Every VA knows exactly what they are doing, why, and where to find answers — without asking anyone.

### 1A — VA Knowledge Base (6 pages to create)

Create a Knowledge Base section with the following pages, each named with a code:

- **KB-001 — What is a Lead** — definition, qualified vs unqualified, ICP criteria, real examples with screenshots
- **KB-002 — What is Outreach** — purpose, platforms (LinkedIn / WhatsApp / Email / Instagram), good vs bad outreach examples
- **KB-003 — What is a Booked Call** — what counts, what doesn't, how to confirm, exactly what to send the closer
- **KB-004 — Nivy Products Quick Reference (VA Version)** — what to mention at outreach stage (not full package details)
- **KB-005 — Communication Rules** — platform rules, tone, response time expectations, what to escalate vs handle yourself
- **KB-006 — VA Role Guide** — the 3 levels (Intern / Executive / Manager), what each owns, what decisions each can make independently

**Standards for every KB page:**

- Named with code: KB-001, KB-002 etc.
- Max 2 minutes to read — no long theory
- Version number + Last Updated + Owner at the top
- Ends with: "Next → [link to next SOP or task]"

### 1B — VA Role & Responsibility Matrix (1 page to create)

| Role | Owns | Decides | Escalates To | Cannot Do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VA Intern | Lead scraping, data entry, basic outreach | Which leads to skip (clear disqualifier) | VA Manager | Modify scripts, mark leads as closed |
| VA Executive | Full outreach, follow-ups, CRM management | Reply handling, follow-up timing | VA Manager | Change KPIs, assign other VAs |
| VA Manager | Review, assign, team QC, escalation | Retraining decisions, daily priorities | Ops Head | Strategy, hiring, pricing |
| Ops Head / Founder | Strategy, hiring, system design | Everything | — | Should not be in daily ops |

**International add-on:** The "Cannot Do" column removes 80% of daily escalations. When people know their boundary, they stop asking.

**Phase 1 completion test:** A new VA reads KB-001 through KB-006 in sequence and understands their role, tasks, and product — without asking a single question.

## 🟡 PHASE 3 — Control: QC, SLA & Communication System

**Status:** 🔲 Not started | **Timeline:** Week 2

**Goal:** Quality is caught at VA level before it reaches the manager. Reporting is standardised. Communication has defined rules that remove ambiguity.

### 3A — QC Checklist Library (5 checklists)

VAs complete these before submitting any work. Manager does not see unreviewed work.

- **QC-001 — Lead Quality Checklist** — valid contact, correct ICP, no duplicate, source filled, all fields complete
- **QC-002 — Outreach Quality Checklist** — personalised opener, correct template, no typos, CRM updated same day
- **QC-003 — Follow-Up Checklist** — correct follow-up day, correct template, CRM status updated, not over-messaged
- **QC-004 — Report Quality Checklist** — all fields filled, calculations correct, submitted by deadline, no blanks
- **QC-005 — Call Handover Checklist** — all handover fields complete, conversation context included, submitted before call

**Rule:** No work reaches Manager review without the VA completing the relevant QC checklist first. Self-check is non-negotiable.

### 3B — VA SLA Document (1 page)

Time-bound rules for every output type:

| Output | SLA |
| --- | --- |
| Lead CRM entry after scraping | Same day |
| First outreach after lead assignment | Within 2 hours |
| CRM update after any reply or status change | Within 1 hour |
| Follow-up messages | Within defined cadence windows — not early, not late |
| Daily report submission | By 6:00 PM (VA local time) every working day |
| Manager review turnaround | Within 12 hours of report submission |
| Interested lead escalation | Within 30 minutes of reply received |
| Call handover to closer | Minimum 2 hours before scheduled call |

**Why this matters:** SLA removes the "I was going to do it" problem. It makes time a system rule, not a personal habit.

### 3C — VA Communication System (1 page)

- **Primary platform:** WhatsApp (operational updates), Notion (reference + documentation), Email (external only)
- **Update format:** Use the daily report — not informal WhatsApp messages like "I did X today"
- **Response time:** Within 2 hours during working hours
- **Escalation rule:** If issue cannot be resolved in 15 minutes, escalate immediately — do not sit on it
- **What NOT to discuss over WhatsApp:** Performance disputes, client details, strategy — these go through Notion or formal 1:1

**Phase 3 completion test:** Manager spends less than 30 minutes per day on quality review. Report submissions arrive on time without being chased. QC failures are caught before reaching manager 90% of the time.

## 🟠 PHASE 4 — Structure: Onboarding Gate System

**Status:** 🔲 Not started | **Timeline:** Week 2–3

**Goal:** New VAs go through a tested, gated onboarding flow. They earn access to live work by proving readiness — never by default.

### 5-Day Gated Onboarding Flow

**Day 0 — Before Day 1:** Setup Checklist — LinkedIn profile, WhatsApp, email, tool access, CRM access, profile optimisation complete

**Day 1 — Understand the System**

- Read KB-001 through KB-006
- Read VA Role & Responsibility Matrix
- Complete: Role Understanding Quiz (5 questions — must score 4/5 or above)
- Gate: Quiz reviewed and passed → Manager signs off → proceed

**Day 2 — Learn the Tasks**

- Read SOP-VA-001, SOP-VA-005, SOP-VA-015
- Practice task: Scrape 20 leads from LinkedIn, format in CRM sheet, submit for review
- Gate: Manager reviews quality — Pass (proceed) or Fail (redo with written feedback)

**Day 3 — Practise Outreach**

- Write 5 personalised LinkedIn connection requests for provided leads
- Submit with QC-002 checklist completed
- Gate: Manager scores pass / fail with written feedback

**Day 4 — Supervised Live Work**

- Assigned 20 live leads by Manager
- Executes full lead → outreach flow
- Submits daily report using SOP-VA-015
- Gate: Manager reviews report — if quality meets standard → cleared for independent work

**Day 5+ — Independent Execution**

- Full daily targets apply from Day 5
- KPI tracking begins
- Scorecard started

### Onboarding Completion Record (template page)

Create for every new VA: **✅ [VA Name] — Onboarding Completion Record**

- Lists every gate with date completed and Manager sign-off
- Never skipped or fast-tracked
- This is proof of readiness before live work

**International add-on:** The gate system is the single rule that eliminates most early-stage quality problems. A VA who has not passed all gates is not on live leads — period.

**Phase 4 completion test:** Every new VA goes through exactly this 5-day process. No VA touches live leads without Day 4 Manager clearance.

> ✅ **Phase 4 COMPLETE — April 29, 2026.** VA Onboarding Gate System + Onboarding Completion Record (template) created.

## 🔴 PHASE 5 — Scale: Performance Management System

**Status:** 🔲 Not started | **Timeline:** Week 3–4

**Goal:** Every VA knows how they are performing. Performance is visible, reviewed weekly, and tied to clear progression criteria. The team is self-correcting.

### 5A — VA Weekly Scorecard (template)

Track weekly per VA:

| Metric | Target | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leads generated | Role target | 20% |
| Messages sent | Role target | 15% |
| Reply rate % | ≥8% | 20% |
| Calls booked | Role target | 25% |
| Report submission rate | 100% on time | 10% |
| QC pass rate | ≥90% | 10% |

**Score tiers:** 🔴 Below 60% | 🟡 60–80% | 🟢 80%+ | ⭐ 90%+

### 5B — Weekly Performance Review System

Every Monday morning, Manager:

- Reviews all VA scorecards from previous week (20 minutes maximum)
- Any VA below 60% gets a 1:1 coaching call (not a firing — a fix)
- Top VA of the week recognised in team channel
- Team aggregate metrics shared with Ops Head in one summary message

### 5C — Performance-Linked Progression Criteria

- **VA Intern → VA Executive:** 30 consecutive days at 🟢 80%+, minimum 10 calls booked
- **VA Executive → VA Manager:** 60 consecutive days at ⭐ 90%+, demonstrated leadership in 1+ situation
- **VA Manager → Senior/Lead:** Ops Head nomination only — based on team performance under their management

### 5D — Incentive Structure

- **Performance bonus:** VAs scoring ⭐ 90%+ for 4 consecutive weeks receive a defined bonus (set by Ops Head)
- **Fast-track promotion:** VAs hitting Manager criteria in under 45 days get early promotion
- **Weekly recognition:** Top performer announced in team group

**International add-on:** When people can see their own score in real time, they self-correct before the manager intervenes. This is the shift from a managed team to a self-managed team.

**Phase 5 completion test:** Every VA has a live scorecard. Every Monday there is a 20-minute performance review. No VA is surprised by their performance status.

> ✅ **Phase 5 COMPLETE — April 29, 2026.** VA Weekly Scorecard, Team Performance Dashboard, and Progression Criteria all created under 📊 Phase 5 — Performance Management.

## ⚫ PHASE 6 — Systemise: Handoff, Error Handling & Document Standards

**Status:** 🔲 Not started | **Timeline:** Week 3–4

**Goal:** The system is professional, documented to international standard, and survives when any single person leaves.

### 6A — VA Handoff System

Every transition point defined:

| Handoff | What is Passed | Format | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VA → Manager | Daily report + QC checklist + CRM updated | Notion / Sheet | By 6:00 PM daily |
| Manager → Closer | Call Handover Form (SOP-VA-014) | Notion form | Min 2 hours before call |
| Sales → Delivery | Client details + package + payment confirmed | Notion page | On payment received |
| Delivery → QC | Deliverable + QC checklist | Notion / Drive | Before client send |

### 6B — VA Error Handling System

| Error Type | Who Owns Fix | Response Action | Timeline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wrong / duplicate CRM data | VA | Rework immediately, resubmit | Within 2 hours |
| Missed daily target (<50%) | VA + Manager | Manager 1:1 call, identify blocker | Same day |
| QC checklist not completed | VA | Complete checklist, resubmit | Immediate |
| Report not submitted by deadline | VA | Submit immediately + written explanation | Same day |
| Outreach quality rejected by Manager | VA | Redo with feedback, resubmit | Within 4 hours |
| Lead mistreated / prospect complaint | Manager + Ops Head | Escalate within 1 hour, formal review | Immediate |
| VA no-show / no contact | Manager | Contact attempt x2, escalate to Ops Head | Within 2 hours |

### 6C — Document Control Standards

Applied to every page created across all phases:

- **Naming format:** KB-001, SOP-VA-001, QC-001 (no exceptions)
- **Header block on every page:** Version | Last Updated | Owner | Status
- **Version numbering:** 1.0 = first live | 1.1 = minor update | 2.0 = major rebuild
- **Review cycle:** All SOPs reviewed every 30 days or after any process change
- **Monthly audit:** Check for broken links, outdated content, missing pages

**Phase 6 completion test:** Any page in the VA system can be found in under 10 seconds. Every document has a named owner. The system functions if any one person leaves tomorrow.

## ⚪ PHASE 7 — Automate: Self-Running System Layer

**Status:** 🔲 Not started | **Timeline:** Month 2 (only after Phases 1–6 are stable with real people for 3+ weeks)

**Goal:** Remove manual coordination from all repeatable processes. The system assigns, follows up, tracks, and reports without human trigger.

> ⚠️ **Non-negotiable rule:** Do NOT start Phase 7 until Phases 1–6 have been tested with real VAs for a minimum of 3 weeks. Automating a broken system makes it break faster.
> 

### Automation Priority Order

**Priority 1 — CRM status triggers**

- Lead marked "Booked" → auto-generate Call Handover Form template
- Lead marked "Closed" → move to client onboarding pipeline

**Priority 2 — Follow-up reminder system**

- Day 3, Day 7, Day 14, Day 30 reminders triggered automatically per lead from first contact date
- Reminder appears in VA task view — not a manual memory task

**Priority 3 — Reporting aggregation**

- Daily VA reports auto-aggregated into Manager dashboard
- Manager sees team-wide numbers without compiling manually
- Weekly summary auto-generated from daily data

**Priority 4 — Lead assignment automation**

- Leads entered → auto-assigned to VA by current capacity
- Manager removed from daily lead distribution

**Priority 5 — Live performance dashboard**

- Scorecard data pulled from CRM / Sheets in real time
- Manager sees team performance without building reports

**Tools:** Google Sheets + Apps Script (free, immediate start), Zapier or Make (after initial budget), Notion automations for task triggers, existing CRM

**Phase 7 completion test (Autonomy Test):** Founder is absent for 4 weeks. Tasks flow. Reports generate. Quality is maintained. Manager makes 80%+ of decisions independently. This is the end goal.
