# 👤 Profile Optimisation {#profile}

**Founder Profile Checklist:**

- [ ]  Professional headshot (light background, smile, eye contact)
- [ ]  Banner image: branded, shows service value proposition
- [ ]  Headline: role + who you help + outcome (not just job title)
    - ❌ "Founder at Nivy Digital"
    - ✅ "Helping SMEs across UK, US & UAE with Accounting, IT & Marketing | Founder @ Nivy Digital"
- [ ]  About section: 3 paragraphs — who you help, what you do, how to work with you
- [ ]  Featured section: 3 items — lead magnet, case study, booking link
- [ ]  Experience: Nivy Digital with full description
- [ ]  Skills: Accounting, Digital Marketing, Business Consulting, IT Services
- [ ]  Recommendations: minimum 3 (ask past clients/colleagues)
- [ ]  Custom URL: [linkedin.com/in/yourname](http://linkedin.com/in/yourname)

# 📝 Content Strategy {#content}

**Posting frequency:** 5x per week (Mon–Fri)

**Best times:** 7–9am or 12–1pm IST

**Weekly Content Mix:**

| Day | Content Type | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Educational tip | Text post (1,300 chars) |
| Tuesday | Carousel | 8–10 slide PDF |
| Wednesday | Opinion / hot take | Text post with bold hook |
| Thursday | Case study snippet | Text + results |
| Friday | Behind the scenes / culture | Photo or short video |

**Post Hook Formula (first line is everything on LinkedIn):**

- Problem-led: "Most UK businesses are overpaying their accountant by 40%."
- Question-led: "What’s the biggest mistake SMEs make when expanding to the UAE?"
- Number-led: "5 signs your bookkeeping is costing you more than it should."

**Always end with a CTA:** Comment, DM me, book a call, click the link.

# 💬 Engagement Tactics {#engagement}

**Daily engagement routine (15–20 mins):**

1. Comment meaningfully on 10 posts by ICP accounts (add insight, not just "great post!")
2. Reply to all comments on your own posts within 2 hours
3. Like 20–30 posts from ICP accounts (stay visible in feed)
4. Check connection requests — accept and send welcome message
5. Respond to DMs within 24 hours

**Why this works:** LinkedIn's algorithm rewards engagement. Your comments appear in commenters' followers' feeds. You become visible to thousands of ICP accounts without posting.

# 📨 Inbound DM Handling {#dms}

When someone reaches out after seeing your content:

**Response within 2 hours:**

> "Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out! Really glad the post resonated. Happy to share more about what we do at Nivy Digital. Would a quick 15-minute call work to explore if there’s a fit? Here’s my booking link: [[Cal.com](http://Cal.com) link]"
> 

**If they want more info first:**

> "Of course — happy to send over a quick overview. We work with [their type of business] on [service]. Here’s a short overview: [1 paragraph]. If that sounds relevant, I’d love to connect on a call!"
> 

**Never:** Ghost an inbound lead. Never take longer than 24 hours to reply.
