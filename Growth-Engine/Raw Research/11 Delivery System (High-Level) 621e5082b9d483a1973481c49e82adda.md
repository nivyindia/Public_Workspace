# 11. Delivery System (High-Level)

## 

### Purpose of Delivery System

- Execute the **6D Growth Engine™** in a structured, phased manner
- Deliver visible progress and measurable results
- Maintain clarity for the client while protecting advanced methods

---

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–4)

**Goal:** Build strong base for visibility and trust

**Key Activities:**

- Google Business Profile optimization (categories, services, posts)
- NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone)
- Initial local SEO setup (on-page basics, keywords)
- Review acquisition system setup (ask flows, templates)
- Basic tracking setup (calls, forms, UTMs if applicable)

**Client Outcome:**

- Improved local presence
- Initial increase in visibility and inquiries

---

### Phase 2: Growth (Weeks 5–8)

**Goal:** Generate consistent inbound leads

**Key Activities:**

- Referral system rollout (past customers, partners)
- Content distribution (before/after, project highlights)
- Local listing expansion and citations
- Offer positioning (seasonal offers, service bundles)
- Conversion improvements (call scripts, response time, FAQs)

**Client Outcome:**

- Noticeable increase in leads
- Better trust and engagement

---

### Phase 3: Scaling (Weeks 9–12)

**Goal:** Maximize lead flow and conversions

**Key Activities:**

- Lead capture optimization (forms, CTAs, landing alternatives)
- Follow-up systems (reminders, simple automations)
- Review velocity & reputation management scaling
- Area/keyword expansion (nearby cities, high-intent terms)
- Performance optimization based on data

**Client Outcome:**

- Stable and scalable lead pipeline
- Higher conversion rates

---

### What Client Sees vs What Is Hidden

**Client Sees:**

- Clear phases and milestones
- Visible improvements (rankings, reviews, leads)
- Simple explanations of actions

**Hidden (Internal Systems):**

- Advanced local domination tactics
- Competitor capture methods
- Automation workflows and optimization logic
- Detailed keyword & ranking strategies

**Strategy:**

- Show progress and outcomes
- Keep advanced methods proprietary

---

### Reporting Basics

**Frequency:** Weekly summary + Monthly report

**Weekly (Simple Update):**

- Tasks completed
- Quick wins (reviews, rankings, inquiries)

**Monthly Report (Client-Facing):**

- Leads generated (calls/forms)
- Google performance (views, actions)
- Reviews gained & rating change
- Key improvements made

**Format:**

- Keep it simple, visual, and outcome-focused

---

### Key Rules (CRITICAL)

- Do not overwhelm client with technical details
- Always tie work to business outcomes
- Maintain consistent communication
- Focus on momentum (small wins → big results)

---

### Output of This System

- Structured execution without confusion
- Strong client confidence
- Measurable growth over 90 days
- High retention and upsell potential
