# 👔 Supervisor SOP

---

# 🎯 Role Objective

> Ensure all VAs complete daily targets, submit proof, and maintain discipline in execution.
> 

---

# 📅 DAILY SOP (Step-by-Step)

## 🌅 STEP 1 — Morning Setup (9:00–10:00 AM)

### ✅ What to do:

1. Check yesterday’s report
2. Identify:
    - Low performers
    - Missed targets
3. Assign today’s targets to each VA

Assign targets clearly every morning — numeric and specific. Example: Rahul → 40 LinkedIn connects + 50 DMs | Aman → 30 connects + 30 follow-ups | Priya → 50 Instagram DMs.

### 🔥 Rule:

- Targets must be **clear & numeric**
- No vague instructions

---

## 🕐 STEP 2 — Mid-Day Check (1:00–2:00 PM)

### ✅ What to do:

- Check tracker sheet (partial progress)
- Ensure:
    - Work has started
    - Entries are being filled

### 🚨 If someone has:

- 0 work done
- No updates

If someone has 0 work done or no updates, immediately message them: "Update your tracker and start work now. Will review again in evening."

---

## 🌆 STEP 3 — Evening Final Check (6:00–8:00 PM)

### ✅ What to verify:

For EACH VA:

- Target vs Done
- Proof link attached
- Work looks genuine (basic check)

---

### 📊 Mark Status:

| Condition | Status |
| --- | --- |
| 100% done + proof | ✅ Completed |
| Partial done | ⚠️ Incomplete |
| No proof | ❌ Invalid |
| No work | 🚫 Not Done |

---

## 🧠 STEP 4 — Coordinate with QC  VA

### ✅ Do this daily:

- Share VA list + their work
- Ask for:
    - Quality score
    - Issues found

---

## 📊 STEP 5 — Create Daily Report (MOST IMPORTANT)

Send to YOU every night.

Date: 19 March

Team Size: 10 VAs

Work Summary:

- Total Messages Sent: 2,300
- Targets Completed: 8/10 VAs

Top Performers:

- Rahul (100% + good quality)
- Priya (95% + consistent)

Low Performers:

- Aman (50% work only)
- Neha (No proper proof)

Quality Issues (from Exp VA):

- 3 VAs using copy-paste messages
- 2 VAs poor follow-up replies

Actions Taken:

- Warning given to 2 VAs
- Targets increased for top performers

---

# ⚠️ STRICT RULES (Supervisor Must Enforce)

## ❌ Rule 1: No Proof = No Work

- Even if they say “done”
- Without proof → mark INVALID

---

## ❌ Rule 2: Late Submission = Count as Incomplete

---

## ❌ Rule 3: Fake Work = Immediate Warning

- Repeated → remove from team

---

## ❌ Rule 4: Daily Reporting is Mandatory

- No report = Supervisor failure

- Low performers → warning / replace

# 🚨 ESCALATION SOP

Supervisor must inform YOU immediately if:

- 3+ VAs underperforming
- Fake work detected
- Targets consistently not met
- System breakdown
