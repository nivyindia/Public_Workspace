## 📅 Weekly Review Cadence

**Every Monday, 9:00am — 30 minutes maximum.**

1. Open this page
2. Check the automated KPI report (sent by n8n at 8am — Phase 3)
3. Mark each KPI: ✅ On Track | ⚠️ Watch | 🔴 Action Needed
4. For every 🔴: assign a fix task with a deadline before next Monday
5. Note any patterns forming over 2+ weeks
