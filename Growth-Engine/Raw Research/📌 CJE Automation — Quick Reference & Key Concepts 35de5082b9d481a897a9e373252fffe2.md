# 📌 CJE Automation — Quick Reference & Key Concepts

> A fast-reference summary of all key concepts discussed in the CJE automation sessions. Use this as a cheat sheet.
> 

---

## The Single Most Important Concept

> **It doesn’t depend on the method but the entry point.**
> 
- The **method** (Reels, Cold Email, Ads, SEO, Freelance bidding) = how they find you
- The **entry point** (Form, WhatsApp link, Calendly, Landing page) = where automation begins

No matter which method brings them in, once they hit an entry point — **n8n takes over and the same funnel runs for everyone.**

---

## Two Automations Running at All Times

**1. Publishing Automation** — Attracts people

- Scheduling reels, sending cold emails, bidding on freelance sites, posting on social
- Tools: Buffer, Instantly, PhantomBuster, GPT-4o, Apify

**2. Pipeline Automation** — Converts people

- Capturing, scoring, nurturing, selling, onboarding, retaining
- Tools: n8n, HubSpot, WATI, Brevo, Calendly

These are **separate workflows** but work together. Publishing brings someone in. Pipeline takes over the moment they respond.

## All Publishing Automation Methods

1. Daily AI content generation + scheduling (all platforms)
2. Trend monitoring + viral content hijacking
3. AI-personalized cold email sequences (Apollo → Clay → Instantly)
4. LinkedIn connect + DM sequence (PhantomBuster)
5. Freelance platform bidding (Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer, PeoplePerHour)
6. Social listening — finds buyer intent posts on LinkedIn, Facebook, Twitter, Reddit
7. Comment keyword detection → auto-DM (Instagram/Facebook)
8. WhatsApp broadcast outreach (segmented by industry)
9. Google Maps local business scraping → personalized outreach
10. Referral loop (Day 60 of client relationship → auto referral request)

---

## All Pipeline Automation Methods

1. Universal lead capture gateway (any channel → CRM in 60 seconds)
2. Lead scoring + routing (hot/warm/cold/unqualified)
3. 7-day email + WhatsApp nurture sequence
4. Sales call booking + pre-call briefing + follow-up
5. Proposal auto-generation (PandaDoc)
6. Client onboarding (payment → everything created in 2 minutes)
7. Monthly auto-performance report (GA4 + Meta + Google Ads → GPT summary)
8. Renewal reminder sequence (30/7/1 day before contract end)
9. AI upsell signal detection
10. Cold lead & lost client reactivation

## The Lead Journey in One Line Per Stage

```
STRANGER → sees content/ad/outreach
LEAD     → fills form / replies / books call
WARM     → receives nurture sequence
HOT      → books sales call
CLIENT   → pays → onboarded in 2 minutes
SUCCESS  → gets monthly reports automatically
LOYAL    → renewal reminder sent before contract ends
ADVOCATE → refers others → new strangers enter the top
```
