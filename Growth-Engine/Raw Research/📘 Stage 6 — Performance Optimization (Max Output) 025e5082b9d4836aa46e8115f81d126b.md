# 📊 STEP 6 — PERFORMANCE PUSH TARGETS + SCORECARD

## 🎯 Weekly Targets

**Leads:** 200–300 | **Messages:** 150–250 | **Follow-ups:** 150+ | **Calls Booked:** 5–10

## 📊 Performance Scorecard

> Track these 5 metrics every day. Bring your scorecard to every supervisor check-in.
> 

| Metric | What to Count | Target (Weekly) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Leads** | New leads added to CRM | 200–300 |
| **Messages** | All outreach messages sent | 150–250 |
| **Replies** | All responses received | Track all |
| **Calls** | Confirmed booked calls | 5–10 |
| **Conversion %** | Calls Booked ÷ Replies × 100 | 10%+ target |

## 📌 How to Use:

- Record your numbers **every day** in your daily report
- At end of each week, calculate your **Conversion %**
- Conversion % below 10% → review scripts and ICP targeting
- Conversion % above 20% → you are a top performer
- Bring your scorecard to every supervisor check-in

## 💡 Rule

> What you don't measure, you can't improve. These 5 numbers tell your entire performance story.
>
