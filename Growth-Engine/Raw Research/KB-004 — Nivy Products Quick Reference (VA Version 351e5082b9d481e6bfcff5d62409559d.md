## Nivy Digital — Two Divisions

### 🏦 Nivy Advisory

Accounting, bookkeeping, tax, compliance, and financial services for international businesses.

**Who needs this:**

- Business owners handling their own books
- Companies that have grown past spreadsheets
- International businesses needing local compliance (US, UK, Canada, AU, UAE)
- Startups preparing for investment or audit

**Pain language to listen for:**

- "I don't understand my numbers"
- "Tax season is a nightmare"
- "We're not sure if we're compliant"
- "My accountant is too slow / too expensive"
- "We just hired staff and things are getting complex"

**What to say at outreach stage:**

> "We help [type of business] get their books clean and stay compliant across [market] — without the overhead of a full in-house finance team."
> 

---

### 💻 Nivy Next

IT services, digital marketing, web/app development, and automation for growing businesses.

**Who needs this:**

- Businesses with outdated websites or no digital presence
- Companies spending too much time on manual tasks
- Founders who want more leads but don't have a marketing system
- Businesses looking to automate repetitive workflows

**Pain language to listen for:**

- "We need a new website"
- "Our marketing isn't working"
- "We spend hours on manual tasks"
- "We're not getting enough leads online"
- "We need an app / system built"

**What to say at outreach stage:**

> "We help [type of business] build out their digital presence and automate the back-end — so the team spends time on clients, not on admin."
>

## What a VA Should and Should NOT Say

| ✅ Say This | ❌ Never Say This |
| --- | --- |
| "We help businesses like yours with [problem area]" | Exact package prices |
| "Many of our clients in [market] come to us when [pain]" | Guaranteed results or timelines |
| "We'd love to show you what that looks like on a call" | "We are cheap" or "We are the best" |
| "Would a quick 15-minute call make sense?" | Anything about contracts or commitments |

## Script Starters by Market

**US/Canada:** Slightly more direct. Leads respond to ROI and time-saving language.

> "Most founders I speak to in [industry] are losing 5–10 hours a week to [pain]. Is that something you're running into?"
> 

**UK/Australia:** More relationship-first. Don't rush the pitch.

> "I came across your business and thought there might be a natural fit with what we do for [industry] companies here. Happy to share a bit more if useful?"
> 

**UAE:** Formal opener, then conversational. Respect is important.

> "Hello [Name], I noticed your work in [industry] and wanted to reach out personally. We work with businesses here to [solve pain]. Would it be worth a brief conversation?"
> 

---

## Common Mistakes

- Quoting prices — this is the closer's job, not yours
- Overselling in the first message — your only goal is to book the call
- Sending brochures or long service lists — too much, too soon
- Describing both divisions at once — pick the most relevant one based on the lead's profile
