## How the Score is Calculated

Each metric is scored as % of target achieved, then weighted.

| Metric | Target | Weight | How to Score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Leads generated | Role target (set by Manager) | 20% | Leads added ÷ Target × 100 × 0.20 |
| Messages sent | Role target | 15% | Messages sent ÷ Target × 100 × 0.15 |
| Reply rate % | ≥88% | 20% | If reply rate ≥8%: full 20 pts. Below 8%: (actual rate ÷ 8%) × 20 |
| Calls booked | Role target | 25% | Calls booked ÷ Target × 100 × 0.25 |
| Report submission rate | 100% on time | 10% | Days submitted on time ÷ Working days × 100 × 0.10 |
| QC pass rate | ≥90% | 10% | QC passes ÷ Submissions × 100 × 0.10 |

**Total score = sum of all weighted scores (max 100)**

## Score Tiers

| Score | Tier | What It Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 90%+ | ⭐ Elite | Eligible for promotion fast-track + bonus |
| 80–89% | 🟢 On Track | Meeting standard. Eligible for progression timer |
| 60–79% | 🟡 Needs Improvement | Manager monitors. 1:1 if below 70% for 2 consecutive weeks |
| Below 60% | 🔴 At Risk | Mandatory 1:1 this week. Retraining plan initiated |
