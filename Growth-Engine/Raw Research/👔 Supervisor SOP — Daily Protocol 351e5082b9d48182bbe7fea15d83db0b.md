# 👔 Supervisor SOP — Daily Protocol

SOP-SUP-001 | Daily Operating Protocol

---

## Purpose

Ensure every VA is performing to standard, every day, without waiting to be asked.

---

## Morning Block (15–20 min)

1. Open Supervisor Dashboard
2. Check VA activity logs — confirm all VAs logged in and have started outreach
3. Select 3 VAs for QC rotation (rotate so every VA gets checked every 2-3 days)
4. Review 3 recent conversations per selected VA against QC checklist:
    - Message tone: professional, warm, SOP-compliant?
    - Timing: sent within SLA window?
    - Logging: lead and message logged in tracker?
    - Follow-up: scheduled if no reply?
5. Flag any issues in Supervisor Tracking & QC System
6. Respond to any VA escalations from overnight

---

## Midday Check (5–10 min)

1. Check for VA messages or escalations
2. Respond to all within 2 hours
3. If escalation is above your authority → escalate to Manager with context

---

## End of Day Block (15 min)

1. Confirm all VAs have submitted EOD reports
2. Chase any missing reports (message VA directly)
3. Update team activity log
4. Flag any unresolved issues for tomorrow
5. Submit your own Supervisor EOD report to Manager

---

## Non-Negotiables

- QC check happens every day — no exceptions
- Every flag is logged — no verbal-only feedback
- Every escalation gets a response within 2 hours
- Every EOD report confirmed before you sign off
