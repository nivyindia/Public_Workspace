# ⏱️ VA SLA — Service Level Agreements

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** Ops Head | **Status:** Live
> 

---

## Purpose

This document defines the time-bound rules for every output the VA team produces. SLA (Service Level Agreement) removes ambiguity about when things should be done — turning time into a system rule, not a personal habit.

---

## VA Output SLAs

| Output | SLA | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New lead CRM entry after scraping | Same day | VA |
| Qualification score entered | Same day as lead added | VA |
| First outreach after lead assignment | Within 2 hours of assignment | VA |
| CRM status update after any message sent | Within 1 hour | VA |
| CRM status update after any reply received | Within 30 minutes | VA |
| Interested lead escalation to Manager | Within 30 minutes of reply | VA |
| Follow-up messages | Within the defined cadence window (Day 3/7/14/30 ±1 day) | VA |
| Daily report submission | By 6:00 PM (VA local time) every working day | VA |
| Call Handover Form submission | Minimum 2 hours before scheduled call | VA |
| 24-hour call reminder to lead | 24 hours before call | VA |
| Weekly review report | By 6:00 PM every Friday | VA |
| Data cleaning | Every Friday (end of day) | VA Executive |

---

## Manager SLAs

| Output | SLA | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily report review | Within 12 hours of submission | Manager |
| Handover form acknowledgement | Within 1 hour of submission | Manager |
| Onboarding gate review (Day 2, 3, 4) | Same day as submission | Manager |
| QC failure feedback to VA | Within 4 hours of identifying failure | Manager |
| Scorecard update (weekly) | Every Monday morning before 10:00 AM | Manager |
| Team performance summary to Ops Head | Every Monday before 12:00 PM | Manager |
| Retraining plan for underperforming VA | Within 48 hours of identifying consistent failure | Manager |

---

## SLA Breach Consequences

| Breach | First Instance | Repeated Breach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily report late | Written note in daily report + flagged in scorecard | Formal 1:1 with Manager |
| CRM not updated within SLA | Manager reminder + same-day correction required | Scorecard impact |
| Handover form late | Immediate submission required + written explanation | Performance review |
| Follow-up outside cadence | Correction + notes in CRM | Retraining required |

---

## International Note

SLAs are defined in each VA’s **local time** unless otherwise stated. A VA in India submits their report by 6:00 PM IST. A VA in the UAE submits by 6:00 PM GST. Timezone must be confirmed with Manager at the start of each engagement.

---

**The SLA is not a suggestion. It is the operating standard of a professional team.**
