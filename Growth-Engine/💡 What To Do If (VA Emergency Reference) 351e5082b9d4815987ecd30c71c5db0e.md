## 💬 Lead / Prospect Situations

**"A lead responded very aggressively or rudely"**

Do NOT reply emotionally. Send: *"I apologise for the interruption — I’ll remove you from our list immediately."* Mark CRM as Dead. Notify Supervisor via WhatsApp with a screenshot. Do not engage further.

**"A lead asked for pricing / cost"**

Do NOT quote any prices. Reply: *"Great question — that depends on your specific situation. The easiest way to give you accurate numbers is a quick 15-minute call. Here’s a link to book: [Calendly link]"*. Update CRM to Interested. Notify Supervisor.

**"A lead asked 'Who are you?' or 'How did you get my details?'"**

Reply honestly and professionally: *"Hi [Name] — I’m [Your Name] from Nivy Digital. I came across your business through [platform/search] and reached out because [1-line relevant reason]. Happy to remove you from our list if this isn’t relevant."* If they ask to be removed, do so immediately. Mark CRM as Dead.

**"A lead said they’re already working with a competitor"**

Do NOT disparage the competitor. Reply: *"Totally understand — sounds like you’re in good hands. If circumstances ever change, feel free to reach out."* Mark CRM as Dead.

**"A lead seems very interested but wants everything by email first"**

Send a brief 3-line summary of what Nivy does. Then follow up once more for the call. If still no call → add to follow-up sequence. Do not send full proposals without Supervisor approval.

**"A lead booked a call and then cancelled"**

Reply: *"No problem at all — happy to reschedule. Here’s the link to pick a new time: [Calendly link]"*. Update CRM notes. If they don’t rebook within 7 days → send one final follow-up. No response after that → Dead.

**"A lead did not show up for the call"**

Wait 5 minutes. Send: *"Hi [Name] — we’re ready for our call whenever you are. Let me know if something came up and we can reschedule."* If no response in 1 hour → update CRM to Called (No Show). Attempt reschedule once. No response → Closed Lost. Notify Supervisor.

**"A lead is asking about services we don’t offer"**

Do not confirm or deny services you’re unsure about. Say: *"Let me connect you with the right person — they’ll be able to answer that properly on a quick call."* Escalate to Supervisor.

## 🛠️ Tool & Platform Situations

**"My LinkedIn account is restricted / asked for verification"**

Stop all activity immediately. Do not send any more messages. Complete the verification LinkedIn requests. Notify Supervisor. Log in daily report: *"LinkedIn restricted — verification in progress. Zero outreach today."*

**"LinkedIn says I’ve hit my weekly connection limit"**

Stop sending connection requests for the rest of the week. Switch to follow-up messages for existing connections and in-mail if available. Notify Supervisor. Resume next Monday.

**"My email account is blocked / bouncing messages"**

Stop sending. Notify Supervisor immediately. Use WhatsApp or LinkedIn for outreach that day. Do not create a new email without Supervisor approval.

**"The CRM is not loading / I can’t access the sheet"**

Check your internet connection first. Try a different browser. If still broken, notify Supervisor and continue outreach manually — log all activity in a temporary note and enter it into CRM once access is restored. Do not skip CRM entries.

**"I accidentally deleted a row or edited the wrong CRM entry"**

Do NOT try to undo or fix it secretly. Notify Supervisor immediately with: which row, what was changed, what the correct information was. The Supervisor will handle recovery.

**"Notion is not loading"**

Use your Quick Reference Card (which you should have saved or bookmarked). Check WhatsApp for team updates. Notify Supervisor if critical reference is needed urgently.

## 📊 Performance & Target Situations

**"I can only reach 30–40% of my daily target today due to a genuine reason"**

Complete as much as you can. Document the genuine reason. Flag in daily report’s Blockers section. Do NOT submit a falsified report. Supervisor will review.

**"I sent messages to the wrong leads by mistake"**

Do not send a retraction or apology to the leads — it draws more attention. Mark those leads correctly in CRM. Notify Supervisor with the specifics. Learn from the process: review QC-002 checklist before next batch.

**"I forgot to submit my daily report before 7:00 PM"**

Submit immediately when you realise, no matter how late. Add a note at the top: *"Submitted late — [reason]. Apologies."* Message Supervisor. This is logged on your scorecard. It happens once — it should not happen twice.

**"I have a very low reply rate this week (below 5%)"**

Do not panic and change everything. Flag in weekly review report. Check if your openers are personalised. Check if you are in the right market. Check volume limits haven’t been exceeded. Supervisor will review with you on Monday.

## 👥 Team & Interpersonal Situations

**"I disagree with my Supervisor’s feedback on my messages"**

Accept the feedback, implement it for now. If you genuinely believe the feedback is wrong, raise it calmly with your Supervisor in a 1:1 — not in the group. Do not ignore feedback you disagree with.

**"Another VA is clearly not doing their work"**

This is not your responsibility to manage. If it is affecting your targets, flag it privately to your Supervisor. Do not raise it in the group chat.

**"I have a concern about my pay, score, or how I was treated"**

Do not raise it informally. Use the [⚖️ Dispute & Grievance Process](https://app.notion.com/p/351e5082b9d48152a6fad9a0c0b10bd6?pvs=21). This is the formal channel and it exists to protect you.

**"I need to take a day off unexpectedly"**

Message your Supervisor as early as possible — ideally before your shift starts. One message is enough: *"[Name] — I can’t work today due to [brief reason]. I will catch up tomorrow on [specific task]."* No daily report needed for that day but flag it.

---

**If your situation is not covered here → escalate to your Supervisor. Include: (1) what happened, (2) what you did so far, (3) what you need.**
