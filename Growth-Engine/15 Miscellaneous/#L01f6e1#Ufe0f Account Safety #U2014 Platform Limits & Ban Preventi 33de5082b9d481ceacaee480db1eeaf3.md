# 🛡️ Account Safety — Platform Limits & Ban Prevention

> Read this before you send your first message on any platform. Account bans are permanent and cannot be reversed. One banned account = all your leads and conversation history gone.
> 

---

## Why Accounts Get Banned

Platforms detect unusual activity using automated systems. The most common triggers are:

- Sending too many messages in too short a time
- Sending identical messages to many people (copy-paste spam)
- Getting reported by multiple users
- Using automation tools not approved by the platform
- Creating new accounts immediately after a ban
- Logging in from too many different IP addresses or devices

## LinkedIn Account Safety

### Daily Hard Limits

| Action | Safe Daily Limit | Absolute Maximum | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Connection requests | 15–20 | 25 | New accounts: start at 10/day for first 2 weeks |
| DMs to existing connections | 20–30 | 40 | Never send identical messages |
| Profile visits | Unlimited | — | No limit but don't visit 500+ in one day |
| Post comments | 10–15 | 20 | Natural engagement, not spam |
| InMails (if Sales Navigator) | 5–10 | 15 | Highly personalised only |

### Weekly Warm-Up Schedule (New Accounts Only)

| Week | Connection Requests/Day | Messages/Day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | 5–10 | 5–10 |
| Week 2 | 10–15 | 10–20 |
| Week 3+ | 15–20 | 20–30 |

> Never skip the warm-up period. An account that jumps to 30 requests/day from Day 1 will be flagged within 5–7 days.
> 

### Warning Signs to Watch For

- "You’ve reached your weekly invitation limit" — Stop immediately. Wait 5–7 days.
- "Your account is restricted" message — Report to supervisor immediately.
- Connection acceptance rate drops to below 5% — Your account may be shadowbanned. Stop for 48 hours.
- DMs stop delivering — Platform may be rate-limiting you. Stop and report.

### What to Do If Restricted

1. Stop all outreach on that account immediately
2. Message your supervisor with a screenshot of the restriction message
3. Do NOT create a new account without supervisor approval
4. Wait 5–7 days before resuming at lower volume
5. If permanently banned: escalate to supervisor — new account setup requires management approval

### Message Quality Rules (Prevents Spam Flags)

- Never send the same message to more than 3 people in a row without changing at least the opening line
- Always personalise the {Name} and {niche} fields — generic messages = spam reports
- Never include external links in your first message to a new connection
- Avoid trigger words: "free", "100% guaranteed", "limited time offer", "click here"
- Keep messages under 150 words

## WhatsApp Account Safety

| Action | Safe Daily Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New cold contacts messaged | 20–30 | New numbers only — not re-messaging |
| Follow-up messages | 30–40 | To existing contacts only |
| Group messages | Avoid unless approved | Bulk group messages trigger bans |

### WhatsApp Ban Triggers

- Multiple people reporting your messages as spam
- Messaging numbers that don't have you saved and who block you
- Using WhatsApp Web on too many devices simultaneously
- Sending the identical message to 20+ people in under an hour

### If WhatsApp Bans Your Number

- Your number is banned, not your phone — a new SIM is needed
- Report to supervisor immediately
- Do not attempt to use a virtual number without management approval

## Email Account Safety

| Action | Safe Daily Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold outreach emails | 20–30 | From a new account; 50 from a warmed account |
| Follow-up emails | 30–40 | Space 3+ days apart |
| Emails per hour | 10 max | Never burst-send |

### Email Deliverability Rules

- Never use Gmail (@[gmail.com](http://gmail.com)) for bulk cold email — use the company email
- Use plain text format, not HTML templates, for cold outreach
- Include an unsubscribe line: "If you'd prefer not to hear from me, just reply 'remove' and I'll take you off my list."
- Verify email addresses before sending (use [Hunter.io](http://Hunter.io) or [Snov.io](http://Snov.io)) — bounce rate above 5% damages your domain
- Warm up a new email account over 2 weeks before sending more than 30 emails/day

## Universal Rules Across All Platforms

- **One lead, one platform per day** — Do not message the same lead on LinkedIn and WhatsApp on the same day
- **No automation tools** without supervisor approval — These violate platform terms and result in permanent bans
- **Space out your messages** — Send in batches with 10–20 minute gaps, not all at once
- **Stop immediately if warned** — Never push through a platform warning
- **Report all restrictions to supervisor same day** — Do not try to self-fix
