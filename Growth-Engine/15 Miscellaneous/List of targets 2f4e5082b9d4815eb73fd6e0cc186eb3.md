## 3. What customers experience (simple flow)

**Offline → Online → Delivery**

1. Customer walks in
2. Executive explains services (using printed rate card)
3. Documents collected (physical + scanned)
4. Payment taken (UPI / cash)
5. Backend team processes
6. Delivery after X days via:
    - WhatsApp
    - Email
    - Printed copy pickup

This builds **huge trust**.
