## 1. What kind of offline stores can you offer?

You don’t need full offices everywhere. You can create **3 outlet models** 👇

### A. Micro Enquiry Kiosk (50–100 sq ft)

**Best for:** Tier-2, Tier-3 cities, near markets

**Purpose**

- Enquiry + document collection
- Payment assistance
- No technical work done here

**Staff**

- 1 trained executive (non-CA)

**Monthly cost**

- ₹8k–₹15k rent
- ₹10k–₹12k staff

---

### B. Service Facilitation Center (100–200 sq ft)

**Best for:** Tier-1 localities, business areas

**Purpose**

- Enquiry + document verification
- Form filling support
- Video call with backend experts

**Staff**

- 1–2 executives

---

### C. Partner Franchise Desk (inside other shops)

**Best for:** Fast scaling

Examples:

- Computer centres
- Cyber cafés
- GST/IT return filing shops
- Mobile shops / printing shops

You only give:

- Branding
- Training
- Commission

⚠️ **No technical decision-making at outlet**

All processing = **central backend team**

## 3. What customers experience (simple flow)

**Offline → Online → Delivery**

1. Customer walks in
2. Executive explains services (using printed rate card)
3. Documents collected (physical + scanned)
4. Payment taken (UPI / cash)
5. Backend team processes
6. Delivery after X days via:
    - WhatsApp
    - Email
    - Printed copy pickup

This builds **huge trust**.

## 5. Training required (MOST IMPORTANT)

You **do NOT train them as accountants**.

You train them as **Service Coordinators**.

### Training Modules (5–7 days total)

### Day 1 – Company & Trust

- Who we are
- Why customers should trust us
- What we do / don’t do

### Day 2 – Services Understanding

- What is GST, ITR, Company registration
- Common customer questions
- Approx timelines (no guarantees!)

### Day 3 – Enquiry Handling Script

- How to talk politely
- How to identify service needed
- How to say “backend expert will confirm”

### Day 4 – Document Checklist

- GST docs
- ITR docs
- Business registration docs

(They only **tick checklist**, not verify legality)

### Day 5 – Software Training

- Google Form filling
- File naming system
- WhatsApp template usage

### Day 6 – Payment & Compliance

- Payment receipt rules
- No cash misuse
- Daily closing report

### Day 7 – Live Practice

- Mock customers
- Role play
- Error handling

## 6. When an enquiry comes – EXACT SOP

### Step 1: Enquiry Capture

- Name
- Phone
- Service needed
- City
- Urgency level

### Step 2: Document List

Give **printed checklist** + explain calmly

### Step 3: Upload & Tag

- Scan documents
- Upload to Drive
- Name folder:
    
    ```
    Service_CustomerName_Mobile
    
    ```
    

### Step 4: Payment

- Collect fee / advance
- Update CRM
- Send receipt

### Step 5: Backend Handover

- Auto notification to central team
- Backend assigns case owner
