> **Who this is for:** VA Intern — Day 1 to Day 7 | Training Stage 1–2
> 

> **Rule:** You are NOT selling packages yet. You are learning what exists so you can qualify leads correctly.
> 

> **Time to complete:** Read everything here in under 60 minutes on Day 1.

---

## 🎯 Your Only Goal at This Stage

When a lead asks *"What do you do?"* — you can answer clearly in 2 sentences.

When a lead asks *"What does it cost?"* — you know the right level (not the exact price).

That is all that is required at Stage 1–2. Nothing more.

---

## 📚 What to Read — In This Order

### Step 1 — The Package Ladder (Read First — 5 minutes)

Nivy has 4 levels of packages. Think of it as a ladder:

| Level | Name | Who It's For | Price Range | VA Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟢 Level 1 | Entry Packages | Businesses starting out | Fixed one-time fee | VA explains and qualifies |
| 🔵 Level 2 | Growth Packages | Businesses wanting consistent results | Monthly retainer | VA explains and books |
| 🟣 Level 3 | System Packages | Businesses wanting full systems | High-ticket, custom | VA qualifies only — Closer sells |
| 🔴 Level 4 | Enterprise & AI | Large businesses or scaling ops | Enterprise pricing | VA identifies signal only |
| 🧩 Add-Ons | Add-On Packages | Any level client needing extras | Variable | VA flags to Supervisor |

**Memory rule:** The higher the level, the bigger the client, the more the Closer handles it. Your job is to find the right level — not to close.
