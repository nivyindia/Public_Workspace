# Checklists — 03 Buyer Persona

> Part of Stage 03 (Buyer Persona). See [README.md](README.md) for the full stage overview.

---

## Pre-Persona Checklist

- [ ] Linked ICP is `active` in Stage 02 (not still draft)
- [ ] Any available buyer interview notes pulled and reviewed

## Persona Card Completion Checklist

- [ ] Persona name/label assigned (first name + ICP descriptor)
- [ ] Age range defined
- [ ] Tech/tool stack listed
- [ ] Media & community habits listed
- [ ] Tone preference documented
- [ ] Day-in-the-life narrative written, including at least one pain-point moment
- [ ] Persona-specific objections listed (distinct from generic ICP objections)

## Quality Control Gates

- [ ] Persona validated against at least 2-3 real client interactions before being marked `active`
- [ ] Any specific named publication, podcast, or community fact-checked as real and current
- [ ] Tone guide specific enough for two copywriters to produce consistently-toned output
