# FAQ — 03 Buyer Persona

> Part of Stage 03 (Buyer Persona). See [README.md](README.md) for the full stage overview.

---

**Q: Do we need a persona for every ICP, or can one persona cover multiple ICPs?**
A: One persona per active ICP. Collapsing personas across ICPs re-introduces the generic-messaging problem this stage exists to solve.

**Q: What if AI suggests a specific podcast/community/publication we can't verify exists?**
A: Mark the persona `unverified-draft` and either fact-check it manually or remove the specific claim and replace it with a general category (e.g. "business-growth podcasts" instead of a specific named show) until verified.

**Q: How is this different from just writing better ICP descriptions?**
A: ICP data (Stage 02) is structured and criteria-based — useful for scoring and qualification. Persona is narrative and human — useful for writing copy that doesn't read like a spec sheet. Both are needed; they serve different downstream consumers.

**Q: Should personas be shared outside the marketing/copy team?**
A: Yes — sales (for call tone), content (for topic selection), and customer success (for onboarding communication style) all benefit from the same shared persona reference.

**Q: How often should personas be updated?**
A: Alongside the ICP refresh cycle (every 2 quarters), or sooner if buyer interviews surface a meaningfully different picture than what's currently documented.
