# FAQ — 54 Advocacy

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: How is this different from Stage 52 Testimonials or Stage 53 Referrals?**
Stage 52 and 53 capture specific, largely one-off actions (a testimonial, a referral). This stage is about the ongoing relationship with a small group of clients who are willing to keep vouching for the company over time — reference calls, content participation, co-marketing — not a single transaction.

**Q: How many advocates should we have?**
This is deliberately a small, curated list — quality over quantity. A handful of genuine advocates outperforms a large list of loosely-engaged names.

**Q: Does becoming an "advocate" mean they've pre-approved every future ask?**
No — every specific ask (a particular reference call, a particular piece of content) needs its own current, explicit consent. Standing advocate status is a relationship, not a blanket permission slip.

**Q: What does "closing the loop" back to Stage 01-03 mean?**
Advocate feedback and market insight — gathered through the ongoing relationship — feeds back into refining the ICP and persona definitions for the next cohort of prospects, making this a genuinely cyclical funnel rather than a one-directional pipeline.

**Q: Why does this stage emphasize recognition/thanks so much?**
Because the entire value of an advocate relationship depends on it feeling reciprocal rather than extractive — an advocate who only ever hears from the company when there's an ask will disengage.
