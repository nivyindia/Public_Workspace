## Presentation Structure (5-8 slides)

1. **Problem** — restate the prospect's top ranked problems, in their own words (from Stage 30)
2. **Solution** — the recommended package/deliverables, mapped 1:1 to those problems (from Stage 31)
3. **Process** — what onboarding and ongoing engagement actually looks like (steps, cadence, communication)
4. **Proof** — 1-2 relevant case studies/results (from Stage 31's matched case studies)
5. **Next Steps** — proposal timing, what happens after they say yes

## Fold-In Script (if presented at the tail of the discovery call)
```
"Based on everything you've shared, here's what I think would work best
for you..." [walk through Problem → Solution → Process → Proof briefly,
using the solution map] "Does this feel like the right fit for where
you are right now?"
```

## Dedicated-Session Opening (if run as its own call)
```
"Thanks for making time for this — I want to walk you through exactly
how we'd approach [their top problem], based on everything you shared
last time. Feel free to jump in with questions as we go."
```
