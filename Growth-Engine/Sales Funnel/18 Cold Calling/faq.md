# FAQ — 18 Cold Calling

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: Do we need to check a Do-Not-Call registry before every call?**
Yes, where the target market has one (e.g., the US National DNC Registry). Requirements vary by country — verify the correct compliance mechanism for each market before calling into it, rather than assuming one market's rules apply everywhere.

**Q: What if the same contact was already emailed or DM'd on LinkedIn?**
That's expected — see Stage 21 Multi-Channel Sequencing for how calling fits into a coordinated cross-channel cadence rather than running in isolation.

**Q: How many attempts before I stop calling a no-answer contact?**
Follow the follow-up cadence defined by your team (typically 3-4 attempts across different times/days) before moving the contact to a lower-touch channel or marking dormant — exact cadence should be set and reviewed by the team rather than treated as fixed here.

**Q: Can I record calls for training/QA purposes?**
Only where legally permitted — consent requirements (one-party vs. two-party) vary by US state and by country. Confirm the legal basis for recording in that specific market before doing so.

**Q: What's the difference between this stage and Stage 29 Discovery Call Execution?**
This stage is the cold outbound call trying to get a meeting booked. Stage 29 is the discovery call itself, once booked.
