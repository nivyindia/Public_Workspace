# Templates — 29 Discovery Call Execution

## Warm Opening (Min 0-2)
```
"Hi [Name], great to connect! Thanks for taking the time. Before I dive
into anything, I want to make sure this call is genuinely useful for you,
so I'll ask a few questions first. Sound good?"

"Perfect. So tell me — what made you reach out today? What's the main
thing on your mind?"
```

## Situation Questions (Min 2-10)
```
"To make sure I understand your situation properly — can you tell me a
bit about your business? What do you do and who do you serve?"
```
Follow-ups: team size, weekly operations, current VAs/freelancers/agencies, tools used.

## Problem Questions (Min 10-18)
```
"What's taking up the most of your time right now that you wish you
could hand off?"
```
Follow-ups: "If you could eliminate one frustration tomorrow, what would it be?" · "Have you tried solving this before?" · "Bandwidth, skills, or systems problem — or all three?"

## Implication Questions (Min 18-22)
```
"If this doesn't change over the next 6 months, what does that look
like for you and the business?"
```

## Need-Payoff Questions (Min 22-26)
```
"If we could solve [their core problem] within the next 30 days, what
would that mean for you?"
```

## Present & Close (Min 26-30)
```
"Based on everything you've shared, here's what I think would work
best for you..."
[Name the package] → [Connect to their stated pain] → [State expected outcome] → [Mention guarantee]

"Does this feel like the right fit for where you are right now?"
```

**If YES:** "Great! I'll send the proposal to [email] within 24 hours."
**If MAYBE:** "Totally understand. What's the main question you need answered before you can move forward?" → rebook with specific date/time.
**If NO:** "I appreciate your honesty. Is it the fit, the timing, or the budget?" → route to Stage 26 objection library.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Pitching before asking | Sounds like every other salesperson | Ask 3+ situation questions first |
| Giving 5 pricing options | Creates confusion and paralysis | Recommend ONE package clearly |
| Not confirming next step | Lead goes cold | Always end with a clear next action |
| Not sending proposal same day | Momentum dies | Send within 24 hours maximum |

## Key Phrases That Close
- "Based on what you've told me, I'd recommend..."
- "Most clients in your situation start with..."
- "We offer a satisfaction guarantee — so there's no risk on your end."
- "What would need to be true for you to say yes today?"
