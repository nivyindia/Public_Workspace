# Methods — 34 Pricing and Packaging

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

## Traditional
Hourly billing negotiated deal-by-deal — abandoned in favor of value-based, tier-based retainer pricing (see source doc's Pricing Philosophy).

## Modern / Tool-Assisted
A fixed tier catalog (Starter / Growth / Pro / Enterprise) with published hours, deliverables, and price, maintained in a shared doc/CRM product catalog so every rep quotes the same numbers.

## AI-Assisted
LLM-assisted drafting of ROI-anchoring language for a specific prospect's numbers (e.g., translating "5 extra discovery calls, 1 closes, $2,000 deal size" into the investment-framing paragraph used in Section 3 of the proposal).

## Manual
Founder-level review of any custom Enterprise-tier scope, since Enterprise pricing is explicitly "custom" rather than catalog-fixed.

## Automated
CRM product/price-book sync so quote/proposal tools (Stage 33) always pull current tier pricing rather than a rep re-typing figures.

## API / Integration
Pricing catalog exposed to the proposal tool (HubSpot Quotes / PandaDoc) via product library so Section 3 of every proposal is generated from the single source of truth, not copy-pasted.

## Browser Automation
Not applicable to this stage.

## Scraping
Not applicable to this stage directly, though competitor pricing benchmarking (Stage 04 Competitor Research) is an upstream input worth periodically refreshing.

## Public Database / Government
Not applicable — India vs. international pricing split reflects internal purchasing-power segmentation, not a public benchmark source.

## Community / Referral
Referral discount (1 month free per referred client who signs) is itself a packaged pricing lever — see [templates.md](templates.md).

## Method Selection Guidance
Use the published tier table as the default for every quote; only escalate to founder-level custom pricing for Enterprise-tier or any discount request above the standard discretionary threshold (see [checklists.md](checklists.md)).
