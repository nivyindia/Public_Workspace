## Pricing Philosophy
1. **Value-based, not hourly** — price on outcomes and scope, not hours worked
2. **Retainer-first** — monthly retainers are the core model; one-off projects are secondary
3. **Market-segmented** — India pricing vs. international pricing reflects real purchasing-power differences
4. **Transparent** — client knows exactly what's included; no hidden fees
5. **Results-anchored** — every proposal frames ROI, not just cost

## Discount & Negotiation Policy

**Discounts offered:**
- 3-month prepay: 10% discount
- 6-month prepay: 15% discount
- Referral: 1 month free per referred client who signs
- Early adopter (first 10 clients): 20% off first 3 months

**Never discounted:**
- Hourly rate — never negotiate below the stated effective-rate floor (verify current floor)
- Scope creep — extra work beyond agreed scope is billed as an add-on
- Rush delivery — premium pricing applies for same-day requests

**If prospect pushes back on price (escalation ladder):**
1. Reframe on ROI and outcomes
2. Offer a smaller starting scope (Starter tier) with an upgrade path
3. Offer a prepay discount if cash flow is the stated concern
4. If still not viable — politely decline and add to 90-day nurture (see Stage 24 Follow Up Systems)

## Pricing Communication Guide

**Never lead with price.** Sequence: understand the problem (discovery call) → propose a solution (scope of work) → present price in context of value/ROI.

**Proposal structure (Section 3 of Stage 33):** summary of what was heard → proposed solution → outcomes/what success looks like → investment (price) → next steps.

**ROI anchor example:**
> "Our Growth package is $1,000/month. If we book you 5 extra discovery calls per month and you close 1 — at an average deal size of $2,000 — you've already 2x'd your investment in month one."
