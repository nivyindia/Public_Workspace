# 15 Outreach Channel Strategy — FAQ

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

---

**Q: Isn't channel choice just up to whoever is doing the outreach?**
It can start that way, but without a documented decision the same VA defaults to their comfort channel regardless of fit, and there's no way to learn which channel actually works best for a given segment/market over time.

**Q: How is this different from Stage 21 (Multi Channel Sequencing)?**
Stage 15 decides *which channels* and *roughly how they combine* (parallel or sequential) at the planning level. Stage 21 defines the *detailed timing and message sequence* once the channel mix has already been chosen here.

**Q: What if we don't have performance data yet for a new segment?**
Use the persona-driven manual selection method with the starting fit table in [templates.md](templates.md), treat the first campaign as a test, and log results to build the performance data for future decisions.

**Q: Is cold calling always compliant?**
No — it varies significantly by country (Do-Not-Call registries, permitted calling hours, business-to-business exemptions in some jurisdictions). Never assume cold calling is permitted in a new market without checking — see the New-Market Entry Checklist in [checklists.md](checklists.md).

**Q: Can AI make the final channel decision?**
No — AI can draft a recommendation and a compliance-summary starting point, but a human owner approves the final channel plan, especially for compliance-sensitive markets.
