## Standard Closing CTA (from the proposal, Stage 33)
> "Sign below / reply to confirm / book a call if you have questions. This proposal is valid for 14 days."

## Buying-Signal-Triggered Nudge Template
```
Hi [Name],

Saw you had a chance to look through the proposal again — happy to
answer anything that would help you decide, or if you're ready, I can
get the agreement over to you today.

[Your name]
```

## Signal → Action Guide

| Signal Observed | Suggested Action |
|---|---|
| Proposal re-opened after a period of silence | Send the buying-signal-triggered nudge above |
| Direct question about start date | Treat as a close signal — move straight to contract (Stage 36) |
| Proposal forwarded internally (visible via tracking, if available) | Offer a short call to answer any internal-stakeholder questions |
| No engagement at all after Day 12 follow-up | Move to 90-day nurture (Stage 24), do not force a close |

## What to Avoid
- Fabricated urgency ("this price expires tomorrow" when it doesn't)
- Multiple competing CTAs in one message
- Pressuring a prospect who has clearly said "not now" — route to nurture instead
