# Methods — 24 Follow Up Systems

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

## Traditional
A rep manually tracking who needs a follow-up and when, in a spreadsheet or memory.

## Modern / Tool-Assisted
CRM/sequencer-native follow-up scheduling (HubSpot workflows, sequencer step timing) that auto-queues the next touch.

## AI-Assisted
LLM-drafted value-add follow-up copy referencing the case-study library; LLM-flagged contacts approaching a re-engagement date.

## Manual
Reviewing and approving new value-add content before it enters the follow-up library.

## Automated
HubSpot/n8n workflows advancing a contact through the follow-up cadence automatically, halting on reply.

## API / Integration
CRM ⇄ channel tools (Stage 16-20) integration so follow-up counts and dates sync centrally rather than being tracked per-tool.

## Browser Automation
Not applicable directly; inherited from the underlying channel stages (Stage 17's PhantomBuster, etc.).

## Scraping
Not applicable to this stage.

## Public Database / Government
Not applicable to this stage.

## Community / Referral
Not a typical trigger for this stage; follow-up cadences apply after any first touch, regardless of source.

## Method Selection Guidance
Automate the scheduling/reminder layer fully; keep new value-add content creation and library curation as a periodic manual/AI-assisted task rather than fully automated.
