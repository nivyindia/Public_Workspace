## Follow-Up Philosophy (applies to every channel)
> "The money is in the follow-up." Most deals close on the 3rd-5th touchpoint, not the first.

**Rules:**
- Maximum 3 follow-ups per channel before marking cold
- Always add value in each follow-up — never a bare "just checking in"
- Standard spacing: Day 1 → Day 4 → Day 10 → Day 21 (final, channel-dependent — see Stage 16-20 for exact per-channel timing)
- After marking cold on a channel: enter the 90-day re-engagement sequence
- Never follow up more than 4 times on one channel without a response

## Follow-Up 2 Template (value-add, not "checking in")
```
Hi [Name], just wanted to bump this to the top of your inbox in case it got
buried. [One-sentence value reminder / stat / case study reference]. Worth
a quick 15 minutes?
```

## Follow-Up 3 Template (final on this channel)
```
Hi [Name], I'll keep this brief — I know your schedule is busy. We've
recently helped [similar company type] with [specific result]. If you ever
want to explore something similar, I'm one message away. Either way, I wish
you well!
```

## 90-Day Re-Engagement Template
```
Hi [Name], it's been a little while since we last connected about [pain
area]. Wanted to check back in — has that become more of a priority since
we last spoke, or still not the right time?
```
