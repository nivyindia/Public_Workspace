## Formatting Standards Quick-Reference

```markdown
- Date format: yyyy-mm-dd
- Market values: US / UK / Canada / AU / UAE (exact)
- Status values: fixed enum only — no free text
- LinkedIn URLs: full https:// format
- Phone numbers: country code included (+1, +44, +971, etc.)
```
