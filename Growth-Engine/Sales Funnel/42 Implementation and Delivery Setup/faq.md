# FAQ — 42 Implementation and Delivery Setup

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: How is this different from Stage 40 (Client Onboarding) and Stage 41 (Kickoff and Expectation Setting)?**
Stages 40 and 41 are about the client relationship and information/access gathering. This stage is the internal operational handoff — turning the agreed project plan into an actual task board, assigning the delivery team, configuring tracking, and shipping the first real deliverable.

**Q: Should every client get a custom-built delivery plan?**
No — start from the standard, proven delivery SOP for that service type (e.g., DELIVERY-SOP-001 for Social Media Management, a different SOP for SEO or paid media) and layer in only the client-specific customizations surfaced during kickoff. A fully custom plan for every client is slower and less consistent than a standard template with targeted adjustments.

**Q: What's the target time to first deliverable?**
Within 7 days of payment/kickoff, per the Stage 7 Onboarding Engine benchmark — this validates the client's decision to hire quickly rather than leaving them waiting weeks for visible progress.

**Q: Why does tracking setup get its own QC checkpoint?**
Because broken tracking silently undermines every later stage — Stage 43/44 account management and customer success planning depend on accurate data, and a client who later discovers their reports were built on broken tracking loses trust fast.

**Q: Who reviews the first deliverable before it goes live?**
A team lead or supervisor, never the delivery specialist alone — this is the client's first tangible proof point and deserves an extra quality gate beyond standard work.
