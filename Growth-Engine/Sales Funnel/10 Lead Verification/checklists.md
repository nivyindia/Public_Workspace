# Checklists — 10 Lead Verification

> Part of Stage 10 (Lead Verification). See [README.md](README.md) for the full stage overview.

---

## Pre-Verification Checklist

- [ ] Lead has passed Stage 09 Data Cleaning (no duplicates, formatting standardized)
- [ ] Basic email syntax/format check passed before spending verification credits

## Verification Checklist (per batch)

- [ ] Bulk email verification run (Reoon/NeverBounce/equivalent)
- [ ] Valid leads tagged and passed through
- [ ] Invalid/Disposable leads discarded, domain blacklisted where relevant
- [ ] Risky leads routed to manual review, not auto-included or auto-excluded
- [ ] Phone numbers verified where cold-calling is part of the campaign
- [ ] Role/company currency spot-checked for high-value or older-batch leads

## Quality Control Gates

- [ ] No `invalid` or `disposable` status lead proceeds to outreach
- [ ] Risky-lead review decisions logged with reasoning
- [ ] Verification date recorded for every lead
- [ ] Lists older than 90 days re-verified before reuse
