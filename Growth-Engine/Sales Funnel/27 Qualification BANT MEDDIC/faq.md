# FAQ — 27 Qualification BANT MEDDIC

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: How is this different from Stage 11 Lead Scoring and Prioritization?**
Stage 11 scores fit *before* any conversation, using firmographic/enrichment data. This stage qualifies the *opportunity* through an actual conversation, since a contact can look like a great fit on paper and still not be a real near-term opportunity.

**Q: Should we use BANT or MEDDIC?**
BANT for most segments — it's faster and sufficient for simpler sales cycles. MEDDIC for complex/enterprise deals with multiple stakeholders, where identifying the Economic Buyer and a Champion matters more.

**Q: What happens to a Not-Yet-Qualified opportunity?**
It's routed to an appropriate nurture cadence (see Stage 24's follow-up/re-engagement approach) rather than dropped — missing one or two elements today doesn't mean it never becomes a real opportunity.

**Q: Can AI make the qualification decision?**
No — AI can draft a summary and flag gaps from a call transcript, but the Qualified/Not-Yet-Qualified/Disqualified decision is a human sales judgment call.

**Q: Where does this happen relative to the discovery call?**
Qualification questions are typically asked during the discovery call itself (Stage 29); this stage documents the framework and question bank used there and the assessment logged afterward.
