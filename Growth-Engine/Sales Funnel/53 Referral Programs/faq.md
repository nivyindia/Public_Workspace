# FAQ — 53 Referral Programs

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: Isn't this the same as the "Affiliate & Referral Program Setup" package Nivy sells?**
No — that internal doc describes a service Nivy sells *to its clients* to help them build their own referral programs. This stage adapts the same underlying structure for Nivy's own client-referral program. See the README source note.

**Q: Who should be asked for a referral?**
Clients flagged as promoters by Stage 51 (NPS 9-10) — asking clients who haven't expressed strong satisfaction risks a weak or reluctant referral.

**Q: What incentive should be offered?**
This should be a deliberate business decision (commission, discount, or other reward) documented consistently — not negotiated ad hoc per referrer. See the program structure reference in [templates.md](templates.md) for a starting framework.

**Q: How does a referred lead enter the funnel?**
Often warmer than a cold lead — depending on how warm the introduction is, they may enter at Stage 07 (Contact Discovery) or skip directly toward Stage 25/28 if the referrer has already made a warm introduction conversation happen.

**Q: How is this different from Stage 54 Advocacy?**
This stage is specifically the structured, incentivized referral program with tracking and payouts. Stage 54 covers the broader advocacy relationship — clients acting as references, speaking on the company's behalf, beyond just sending referrals.
