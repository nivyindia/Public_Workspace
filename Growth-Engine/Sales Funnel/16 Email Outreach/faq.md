# FAQ — 16 Email Outreach

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: Can I send cold email from my personal or main company inbox?**
No — use a dedicated sending domain/mailbox. Cold outreach carries deliverability risk that should never touch the primary company domain's sender reputation.

**Q: What if bounce rate goes above 3%?**
Pause sending on that mailbox immediately, re-verify the remaining list against Stage 10 status, and investigate the source of the bad addresses before resuming.

**Q: How many emails can a new mailbox send per day?**
Follow the warm-up schedule in [methods.md](methods.md) — roughly 10-20/day in week 1-2, scaling to 40-70/day by month 2+. Never send at full volume from Day 1.

**Q: Someone replied asking to unsubscribe — what do I do?**
Suppress them immediately and permanently across all sequences; mark **Do Not Contact** in CRM. This is a compliance requirement in most markets, not optional courtesy.

**Q: Can AI write the entire email for me?**
AI can draft a first pass, but every template should be human-reviewed before it's used at volume, and personalized fields should be spot-checked per batch.
