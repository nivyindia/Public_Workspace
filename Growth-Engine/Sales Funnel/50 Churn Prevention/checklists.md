# Checklists — 50 Churn Prevention

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

## Detection QC
- [ ] Monthly CSAT/NPS survey dispatched to all active clients — guards against silent churn
- [ ] CRM activity monitored for warning signs (no reply 7+ days, reduced task volume) without waiting for the client to say something

## Response-Matching QC
- [ ] 🟡 Medium signal (e.g., no reply 7+ days, reduced task volume) → VA/Account Manager proactive check-in
- [ ] 🔴 High signal (e.g., negative report feedback, invoice dispute) → founder call within 24 hours
- [ ] 🔴 Critical signal (cancellation inquiry, NPS <6) → founder call same day

## Save-a-Client Conversation QC
- [ ] Founder asks directly whether the issue is results, process, or budget — doesn't assume
- [ ] A specific, concrete offer is made (reassign VA / adjust scope / pause instead of cancel / bonus hours), not a vague "we'll do better"
- [ ] Client is genuinely listened to before any offer is made, not talked over

## Outcome QC
- [ ] Every churn-risk flag has a logged outcome (retained/churned/pending), not left open indefinitely
- [ ] Churned-client feedback captured for pattern analysis, even when the client can't be saved

## Duplicate / Accuracy / Completeness Checks
- [ ] No client has an unresolved 🔴 Critical flag older than 24 hours without a founder response logged
