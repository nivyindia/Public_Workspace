# Checklists — 01 Market Research

> Part of Stage 01 (Market Research). See [README.md](README.md) for the full stage overview.

---

## Pre-Research Checklist

- [ ] Target geography confirmed and added to the active market list
- [ ] Existing service catalog reviewed so research scope matches what's actually sellable
- [ ] Prior brief for this market (if any) pulled and reviewed before starting fresh research

## Brief Completion Checklist (per market)

- [ ] Market Overview section written with at least one sourced statistic
- [ ] Market Size (TAM/SAM/SOM) section filled — estimates clearly marked if unsourced
- [ ] Service Demand table covers every current service line
- [ ] Primary and Secondary Target Segments listed
- [ ] Competitive Landscape section names at least 3 competitor types/companies
- [ ] Regulatory Environment section present (or explicitly "none identified")
- [ ] Buying Behavior / decision process documented
- [ ] Go-to-Market channel notes included
- [ ] Cross-Market Score fields filled (size, price sensitivity, compliance complexity, trust threshold, best channel)

## Quality Control Gates

- [ ] Every quantitative claim tagged with source type or marked "estimated — unverified"
- [ ] No fabricated statistics — if a number can't be found, the brief says so rather than inventing one
- [ ] Brief dated and `Last Updated` field set
- [ ] Brief cross-linked to the next stage it feeds ([02 ICP Definition](../02 ICP Definition/README.md))

## Refresh Checklist (recurring, every 2 quarters)

- [ ] Re-check regulatory environment for changes (new tax regimes, compliance mandates)
- [ ] Re-check competitor landscape for new entrants or repositioning
- [ ] Re-score cross-market priority if conditions have shifted
