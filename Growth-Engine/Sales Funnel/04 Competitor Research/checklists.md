# Checklists — 04 Competitor Research

> Part of Stage 04 (Competitor Research). See [README.md](README.md) for the full stage overview.

---

## Shortlisting Checklist

- [ ] At least 3 local competitors identified
- [ ] At least 3 national-level competitors identified
- [ ] At least 2 niche-specialist competitors identified
- [ ] At least 2 high-end/premium competitors identified
- [ ] Every tracked competitor maps to at least one active ICP (Stage 02)

## Audit Completion Checklist (per competitor)

- [ ] Company Overview
- [ ] Services & Pricing (marked "approximate, verify")
- [ ] Target Industries
- [ ] Website Quality
- [ ] SEO Performance
- [ ] Blog Content Strategy
- [ ] Social Media Performance
- [ ] Branding Quality
- [ ] Paid Ads Strategy
- [ ] Portfolio Quality
- [ ] Client Testimonials (only what's publicly disclosed — nothing invented)
- [ ] Sales Funnel & CTAs
- [ ] At least one weakness converted into a positioning response

## Quality Control Gates

- [ ] No fabricated pricing, client counts, or testimonials anywhere in the audit
- [ ] Every pricing figure marked "approximate, verify"
- [ ] Positioning response uses the acknowledge-strength-then-pivot pattern, not an attack
- [ ] Audit `Last Reviewed` date current within the monitoring cadence window
