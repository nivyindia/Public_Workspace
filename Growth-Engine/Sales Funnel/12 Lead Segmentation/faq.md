# 12 Lead Segmentation — FAQ

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

---

**Q: How is segmentation different from scoring (Stage 11)?**
Scoring ranks a lead by fit + intent (a number). Segmentation groups leads by *who they are and what campaign they belong in* (a category). A lead can be Hot-tier and belong to any segment — the two are independent dimensions used together when planning outreach.

**Q: What if a lead matches two personas equally?**
Assign one Primary Segment based on the strongest signal (usually job title over secondary signals), and add the second as a Secondary Segment. Avoid leaving Primary Segment blank or double-primary — it breaks list-building logic downstream.

**Q: Do segments change over time for the same lead?**
Yes — if a lead's company size, title, or geography changes materially (e.g., promoted, company grew), re-run the tagging rule and update. Segment tags are not permanent.

**Q: What happens to leads tagged "Needs Manual Review"?**
They stay out of automated campaign lists until a human assigns a segment. This is intentional — a wrong auto-assigned segment is worse than a short delay.

**Q: Should every stage of the funnel use the same segment definitions?**
Yes — segment names should be identical from Stage 12 through Stage 22 (Personalization) and Stage 52 (Case Studies). Divergent naming between stages breaks the ability to trace a lead's journey end-to-end.
