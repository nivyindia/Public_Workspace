# M17 — Templates

## 1. Welcome Sequence Outline (3–5 emails)

- Email 1 (immediate): Confirm signup, deliver lead magnet if promised, set expectations for what's next
- Email 2 (+2–3 days): Core value proposition / brand story, from M01/M03
- Email 3 (+4–5 days): Best/most representative content piece (from M09/M08)
- Email 4 (+7 days): Soft engagement invitation (reply, book a call — matched to actual sales motion)
- Email 5 (optional, +10 days): Transition into the regular newsletter cadence

## 2. Nurture Segment Definitions

| Segment | Trigger | Sequence focus |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Signed up via top-of-funnel content/lead magnet, no further engagement yet | Educational content, brand trust-building |
| Consideration | Engaged with comparison/how-to content, or explicitly self-identified interest | Case studies, deeper product/service education |
| Decision | Clicked pricing/demo link, or high engagement with decision-stage content | Direct, low-friction path to a sales conversation — flag for M17.6 |
