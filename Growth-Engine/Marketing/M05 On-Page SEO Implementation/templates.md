## 3. Image Alt-Text Convention

- Filename: `{topic}-{descriptor}.{ext}` (e.g., `n8n-workflow-automation-diagram.png`)
- Alt text: describes what the image actually shows, in plain language, including the keyword only if it's a natural fit — never alt-text stuffed with unrelated keywords
