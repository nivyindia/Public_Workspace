# M19 — Methods

## Method 1 — Founder-Led Seeding (Phase 1)

- Founder personally invites the first 10–30 members, one at a time, from existing warm contacts (past clients, engaged LinkedIn commenters, newsletter replies).
- Founder is heavily involved day-to-day: starting conversations, replying to every message, setting the tone.
- Best for: the first 14–30 days of any new community — per the phased approach in the existing internal "Community of Growth Engine" doc, this is non-negotiable groundwork, not something to skip by going straight to broad promotion.

## Method 2 — Content-Driven Growth (Phase 2, After Signal)

- Once the seeded group shows signal (people replying to each other, not just to the founder), community membership becomes a stated CTA in M09–M17 content ("join the [name] community" link in bio, newsletter footer, post CTAs).
- Best for: scaling membership past the first 30 once the space has proven it's worth joining — promoting an empty room is worse than not promoting it at all.

## Method 3 — Member-Invites-Member (Growth Loop)

- Existing members are given a simple, frictionless way to invite peers (shareable invite link, "bring a founder" prompts).
- A light leaderboard or recognition mechanic (top inviter shoutout monthly) — mined from the ambassador/leaderboard concept in the existing internal material — can be layered in once the group is established, without adopting a full paid-commission structure.
- Best for: sustainable growth once Method 1/2 have built enough critical mass that member-to-member trust exists.

## Method 4 — Event-Anchored Engagement

- Recurring live touchpoints (monthly AMA, office hours, or themed discussion thread) give members a reason to show up on a schedule rather than the space going quiet between founder posts.
- Best for: keeping an established community active long-term — this is the retention lever, not the acquisition lever (Methods 1–3 cover acquisition).

## Choosing a Method

| Community stage | Recommended method |
|---|---|
| Day 1–30, no members yet | Method 1 |
| 30+ engaged members, ready to open up | Method 2 |
| Established base, want compounding growth | Method 3 |
| Any stage, for retention | Method 4 (layered on top of whichever acquisition method is active) |

Every method still runs through the moderation and culture-setting checklist in `checklists.md` — an active community without ground rules degrades into noise, per the standing caution in the existing internal material this stage draws from.
