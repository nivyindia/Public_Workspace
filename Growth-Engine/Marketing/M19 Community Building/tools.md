# M19 — Tools

Pulls from the shared free/OSS stack in `IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` §2, plus stage-specific additions below. All pricing/limits marked "verify current" — free-tier terms change without notice.

## From the Shared Stack

| Function | Tool | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Community hosting | WhatsApp Business app (free), Discord (free) | Named directly in the shared stack as fitting the ICP's likely channels |
| Workflow automation | n8n (self-hosted) | Powers welcome-message and engagement-prompt automation in `automation.md` |
| Experiment tracking | Airtable free tier or Notion free tier | For the member/growth-loop tracker in `templates.md` |

## Stage-Specific Additions

| Function | Free / OSS tool | Notes | Paid upgrade path |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community platform (alternative to WhatsApp/Discord) | Slack free tier | Free tier caps message history — verify current limit — fine for a small, active community | Slack paid tiers, or Circle/Mighty Networks once a branded, owned platform is justified |
| Invite link tracking | Native Discord/Slack invite-link analytics (built in, free) | Enough to see which invite source brought which members without a dedicated tool | — |
| Event scheduling (Method 4 — AMAs, office hours) | Google Calendar (free) + Google Meet or Zoom free tier | Zoom free tier time-caps group calls — verify current limit | Zoom paid tier for longer group sessions |
| Member recognition graphics | Canva free tier | Simple "top inviter this month" shoutout graphics | Canva Pro |

## Notes

- No tool in this list requires a paid tier to run a community end-to-end through Method 1–3 in `methods.md`.
- Verify current message-history and group-call-length caps on the free tiers of Slack and Zoom before relying on them for an active, ongoing community — these change without notice.
