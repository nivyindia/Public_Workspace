## Template 4 — Human Review Sign-Off Block

Appended to the bottom of every draft doc before it can move to "Approved":

```
Reviewed by:
Date:
Fact-check pass complete: Y/N
No fabricated stats/tools/case studies confirmed: Y/N
On-page SEO checked (handoff to M05): Y/N
Approved for publish: Y/N
```
