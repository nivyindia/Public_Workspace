# M02 — Methods

Like M01, this stage is mostly a judgment call, not a production task with a manual-vs-automated spectrum. What varies is **how rigorously the channel decision is justified**, not how fast it's executed.

## Method 1 — ICP-Backward Channel Selection

- Start from the Sales Funnel Stage 02 ICP profile (roles, industries, where each ICP can be found — sub-stage 2G "Channel Mapping" already captures this) and pick channels the ICP is actually known to use, rather than picking channels because they're trendy or easy to start.
- This is the primary method used to build this stage's current shortlist — the SD-05 Inbound Channels table already reflects this (LinkedIn and email skew toward founders/consultants/SMEs, matching the ICP defined in Stage 02).

## Method 2 — Capacity-Constrained Shortlisting

- List every channel that scores well on Method 1, then cut the list down to only what current team capacity (VAs, freelancers, founder time) can actually run to a real posting/response cadence.
- A channel run inconsistently (e.g., 1 LinkedIn post every 3 weeks) is worse for brand credibility than a channel not attempted at all — this method exists specifically to prevent over-committing.
- Best for: any point where the "ideal" channel list from Method 1 is larger than the team can realistically execute.

## Method 3 — Full Channel-Universe Reference Scan

- Periodically re-scan the full channel universe (organic, paid, partnership, referral, community, marketplace, PR, influencer, product-led — the full category breakdown in `resources.md`) for channels not yet considered, in case the ICP's behavior or the market has shifted.
- This is a review method, not a monthly task — it's meant to catch blind spots (e.g., a professional-service marketplace like Clutch or GoodFirms that fits an agency's ICP but was never evaluated) rather than to re-litigate the whole shortlist every cycle.

## Method 4 — Explicit Non-Selection Documentation

- For every channel considered and rejected, write down *why* (not enough ICP fit, not enough capacity, wrong stage of company maturity) rather than just silently omitting it.
- Mirrors the "Negative ICP" pattern already established in Sales Funnel Stage 02 — makes the decision auditable later instead of leaving future team members to wonder "did we ever consider X?"

## Choosing a Method

| Situation | Recommended method |
|---|---|
| First time picking channels for a new positioning/ICP | Method 1 |
| Channel list from Method 1 is bigger than the team can run | Method 2 |
| Suspect there's a channel opportunity nobody's considered | Method 3 |
| Documenting a decision for future team members / AI agents | Method 4 |

No method here produces a workflow to automate — see `automation.md` for the one place automation genuinely helps (tracking and notifying, not deciding).
