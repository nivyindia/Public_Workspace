# M20 — Methods

## Method 1 — Referral Partnership (Lightest Commitment)

- A partner sends leads in exchange for a commission — informal, often just a shared link or named introduction.
- Structure: mined directly from the existing internal Partnership & JV Strategy doc's tiering system — Bronze tier (10% MRR commission for the first 3 months), just a link, no formal agreement needed to start.
- Best for: the first move with any new partner candidate — low commitment on both sides, easy to say yes to.

## Method 2 — Co-Marketing Partnership

- Joint content, joint webinars, or a co-branded lead magnet with a complementary (non-competing) service provider serving the same ICP.
- Structure: Silver/Gold tier territory — 15–20% MRR commission plus co-marketing access, with a monthly or quarterly check-in cadence.
- Best for: partners with an existing audience of 50+ relevant contacts (per the Ideal Partner Profile below) who are willing to actively promote jointly, not just passively refer.

## Method 3 — White-Label / Platinum Partnership

- Partner resells Nivy's services under their own brand, or vice versa.
- Structure: Platinum tier — custom margin structure, formal agreement and SLA required.
- Best for: agencies or firms with a client base that consistently needs the exact service Nivy provides, where a one-off referral relationship undersells the volume potential.

## Method 4 — PR & Media Outreach

- Distinct from partnership-building: this is outreach to journalists, bloggers, and podcast hosts to secure press coverage and guest appearances, not a revenue-share relationship.
- Process (mined from the existing internal PR & Media Outreach Package doc): strategy/angle session → media list build (20–50 relevant journalists, blogs, podcasts) → press release writing → outreach and follow-up → coverage tracking and reporting.
- Best for: building third-party credibility that content marketing alone can't buy — a press mention or podcast appearance carries trust a paid ad doesn't.

## Method 5 — Formal Affiliate/Referral Program (Structured, Scaled)

- Where Method 1's informal referral relationship is proven and needs to scale past a handful of hand-tracked partners, this method formalizes it: a dedicated platform, tracking links, an affiliate onboarding page, and a launch sequence to existing clients/partners.
- Mined from the existing internal Affiliate & Referral Program Setup doc's process: program structure design → platform setup → materials/copy → launch campaign → performance review.
- Best for: once informal referrals (Method 1) are generating enough volume that manual tracking becomes the bottleneck.

## Ideal Partner Profile (Applies to Methods 1–3)

A good partner, per the existing internal Partnership & JV Strategy doc:

- Serves the same ICP (founders, coaches, SMEs, agencies) without competing directly
- Has an existing audience or client base of 50+ relevant contacts
- Values quality and reliability, not just the cheapest option
- Is active and engaged, not a dormant business

Best partner categories: business coaches/consultants, web design/development agencies, accountants/financial advisors serving SMEs, HR/recruitment firms, complementary marketing agencies needing capacity, and SaaS tools used by the same ICP.

## Choosing a Method

| Situation | Recommended method |
|---|---|
| First contact with a new partner candidate | Method 1 |
| Partner has an engaged audience and wants to actively promote | Method 2 |
| Partner's client base consistently needs the service at volume | Method 3 |
| Want third-party press credibility, not a revenue-share relationship | Method 4 |
| Informal referrals have outgrown manual tracking | Method 5 |
