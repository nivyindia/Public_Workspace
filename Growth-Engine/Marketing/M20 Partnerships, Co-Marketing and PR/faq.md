# M20 — FAQ

**Q: How is this different from M19 Community Building?**
M19 is about an owned community of your ICP (prospects/clients gathering in a space you run). M20 is about relationships with *other businesses and media* — partners, agencies, journalists, podcast hosts — who have their own separate audience you're trying to reach through the relationship, not a space you host yourself. They overlap only where a partner wants access to your community, or their audience becomes a source of new community members — each stage owns its own side of that exchange.

**Q: The source PR and affiliate-program docs have client-facing pricing ($2,000–$5,000/month, etc.) — does that pricing apply to running M20 internally?**
No — flagged explicitly in `resources.md`. Those figures describe packages Nivy sells *to clients* as a service. M20 is about running partnerships and PR *for Nivy's own brands*, which is a different budget question entirely (mostly time, not a client-billable rate). Don't confuse the two when planning this stage's actual cost.

**Q: Which tier should a new partner start at?**
Bronze, almost always — see Method 1 in `methods.md`. It's the lowest-commitment ask and the natural first step in the 5-step outreach process, regardless of how promising the partner looks on paper. Tier upgrades happen after the relationship proves out, not before.

**Q: Do we need a paid affiliate platform?**
Not at first. Method 1 (informal referral) and early Method 5 volume can run on a spreadsheet/Airtable base. A dedicated platform (Tapfiliate, ReferralHero, etc.) is flagged as the realistic upgrade path once manual tracking becomes the bottleneck — see `tools.md`.

**Q: How does a PR mention or partner-driven guest post feed back into content (M09/M10)?**
Every secured coverage or guest post gets logged with the repurposing handoff template in `templates.md` (Template 7), so M09/M10 knows it exists and can turn it into a LinkedIn post, newsletter mention, or "As Seen In" website update.

**Q: What if a partner referral turns into a lead — where does it go?**
Same place every Track M lead goes: tagged with its source and handed to M22 (Inbound-to-CRM Bridge) once that stage is built, entering Sales Funnel Stage 06 as an inbound record.
