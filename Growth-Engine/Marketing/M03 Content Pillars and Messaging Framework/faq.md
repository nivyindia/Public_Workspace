# M03 — FAQ

**Q: There were two different "content pillars" tables in the existing docs (SD-05 and the Email Newsletter System) — which one is correct?**
Neither is wrong, but they weren't reconciled with each other before this stage. SD-05's table (VA Services, Digital Marketing, Automation, Client Success) is treated here as the primary, cross-channel pillar set. The Email Newsletter System's pillars are reconciled as a newsletter-specific *expression* of that same set (see `templates.md` Template 2), not a 5th independent pillar list — running two inconsistent pillar languages across channels was the actual problem worth fixing here.

**Q: Why isn't there a bigger automation workflow for this stage?**
Same reason as M01/M02 — defining pillars and messaging is a judgment call, not a production pipeline. The one real automation opportunity (tracking pillar balance once real content volume exists) is covered in `automation.md` Tier 3, and it's explicitly marked as premature until M08/M09 have real publishing history.

**Q: Does every piece of content have to map to exactly one pillar?**
In most cases yes, per the Content Brief template's single "pillar" field — this keeps tagging simple and pillar-balance tracking meaningful. A piece that genuinely spans two pillars can still be tagged to its primary one; forcing a second field isn't worth the added complexity at current content volume.

**Q: What if a new service line gets added later — does that mean a new pillar?**
Not automatically. Per Method 1 in `methods.md`, check whether the new service line is distinct enough to warrant its own pillar or whether it fits inside an existing one first — pillar sprawl (too many pillars, each covered too rarely) is a real risk the balance checklist in `checklists.md` is meant to catch.

**Q: How does this stage relate to M04 (Keyword Research & Mapping)?**
M03 does a light, pillar-level keyword check (just enough to confirm real search demand plausibly exists). M04 takes each pillar and does the full keyword research and mapping work — specific target keywords, search volume, difficulty, and content-cluster planning. M03 is the starting point, not a substitute for M04.
