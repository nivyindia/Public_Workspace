## Template 4 — Tone-of-Voice Quick Reference

| Don't write | Write instead |
|---|---|
| Vague corporate jargon ("leverage synergistic solutions") | Plain, concrete language ("build systems that grow your business") |
| Generic credential claims ("highly skilled professionals") | Specific proof ("handled 200+ projects across 3 continents") |
| Passive/soft closers ("please don't hesitate to reach out") | Direct, time-bound offers ("drop us a message — we reply within 4 hours") |
