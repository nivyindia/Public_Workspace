# M01 — FAQ

**Q: Why doesn't this stage have a big n8n automation workflow like M09 does?**
Because there's genuinely nothing here to automate at the decision level — positioning, voice, and identity are human judgment calls, not a production pipeline. `automation.md` is honest about this rather than inventing a workflow diagram for the sake of matching M09's format. The one real automation (notifying the team when the brand doc updates) is covered there.

**Q: The color palette and fonts in `templates.md` — are those final?**
No. The source "Brand Guidelines" doc explicitly marks these as "action needed: confirm" as of its last update. This stage carries them over as the recommended candidates that doc proposed, not as a locked-in final brand kit. Treat them as a strong starting point pending an actual design sign-off.

**Q: How does this stage relate to Sales Funnel Stages 02 and 03?**
Those stages define *who* the buyer is (ICP) and *what the individual human is like* (persona). M01 takes that as a given input and answers a different question: *how do we present ourselves* to that buyer — voice, visuals, positioning. M01 does not redefine the ICP or persona; it should be checked against them (see the alignment step in `methods.md` Method 2).

**Q: Who should actually make M01's decisions — a VA, a freelancer, an AI drafting tool?**
Per the source doc's own ownership field, positioning and brand decisions are owned by the founder. VAs, freelancers, and AI tools should execute against the finalized guide (writing on-brand copy, building Canva templates) — they shouldn't be the ones deciding what the positioning or voice *is*.

**Q: What if the positioning statement changes later — does every downstream stage need to be rebuilt?**
No. M02–M03 and the content stages (M08/M09, M11–M17) reference this stage's output rather than duplicating it. If positioning changes, update this stage's files and the Tier 2 automation notifies the team — downstream stages don't need a rewrite, just a re-check against the new version.

**Q: Do we need a paid designer to finish the visual identity?**
Not necessarily — Canva's free tier, Google Fonts, and the approved free imagery sources can produce a workable brand kit end-to-end. A paid designer is only needed if free/DIY output doesn't hit the quality bar the founder wants, per the note in `tools.md`.
