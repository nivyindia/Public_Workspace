# ⚙️ Day 0 Setup Checklist — VA

> ✅ **THIS IS THE OFFICIAL DAY 0 SETUP CHECKLIST — MASTER VERSION**
> 

> All links across the system point here. If someone sends you a different setup checklist, ignore it and use this one.
> 

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** Ops Head | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who uses this:** VA Trainees — complete before Day 1 starts
> 

---

> ⚠️ **Do not start your first working day without completing every item on this checklist.** Missing setup causes access failures, missed groups, and delays on Day 1. This takes 30–45 minutes. Do it the day before.
> 

---

## 💻 1. Device & Internet Check

- [ ]  You have a working laptop or desktop (not just a phone)
- [ ]  Your internet connection is stable enough for a video call
- [ ]  Your browser is up to date (Chrome or Firefox recommended)

---

## 👤 2. LinkedIn Profile Setup

- [ ]  LinkedIn account created (or existing account confirmed active)
- [ ]  Profile photo added (professional, clear face, light background)
- [ ]  Headline written: e.g. *"Business Development | Client Acquisition | Nivy Digital"*
- [ ]  About section written (minimum 3 sentences about helping businesses grow)
- [ ]  Current position added: *Virtual Assistant | Nivy Digital*
- [ ]  Profile URL customised (e.g. [linkedin.com/in/yourname](http://linkedin.com/in/yourname))
- [ ]  50+ connections (if new account, connect with colleagues and professional contacts immediately)
- [ ]  Account NOT set to private or limited visibility
- [ ]  Confirm you can send connection requests without immediately hitting limits

> ✅ Send your LinkedIn profile URL to your Supervisor via WhatsApp for confirmation before Day 1.
> 

---

## 📱 3. WhatsApp Setup

- [ ]  WhatsApp installed on your phone
- [ ]  Your Supervisor has your number and has added you to the team WhatsApp group
- [ ]  You have sent a confirmation message in the group: *"[Your Name] — setup complete, ready for Day 1"*
- [ ]  You have saved your Supervisor's number

---

## 📧 4. Email Setup

- [ ]  Professional email address confirmed (provided by Supervisor or created as instructed)
- [ ]  You can send and receive email from this address
- [ ]  Email is NOT your personal Gmail/Yahoo — must be business-appropriate

---

## 📊 5. CRM Access

- [ ]  CRM link received from Supervisor
- [ ]  You can open the CRM sheet
- [ ]  You can add a test row (then delete it)
- [ ]  You understand the column headers (read the CRM Entry SOP if unsure: [SOP-VA-011](SOP-VA-011%20%E2%80%94%20CRM%20Entry%20&%20Status%20Update%20351e5082b9d481a6bd67cc7c4787a52f.md))
- [ ]  You have NOT made any changes to existing rows

---

## 🔖 6. Notion Access

- [ ]  You can open and read this page (confirms Notion access)
- [ ]  You can navigate to [🧭 VA Trainee Path](%F0%9F%A7%AD%20VA%20Trainee%20Path%2033ee5082b9d481a2ba46df5a8f3482c1.md)
- [ ]  You can navigate to [📚 VA Training Program](%F0%9F%93%9A%20VA%20Training%20Program%20(Start%20Here)%20b23e5082b9d48210b8e301d063930fb0.md)
- [ ]  Notion search works: press Cmd/Ctrl + K and search "KB-001"

---

## 📅 7. Calendly Setup

- [ ]  Calendly account created using your business email
- [ ]  Discovery call event type created: *"15-Minute Discovery Call — Nivy Digital"*
- [ ]  Duration set to 15–20 minutes
- [ ]  Your availability set correctly (your working hours, your timezone)
- [ ]  Calendly link copied and saved somewhere accessible
- [ ]  You have sent your Calendly link to your Supervisor for confirmation
