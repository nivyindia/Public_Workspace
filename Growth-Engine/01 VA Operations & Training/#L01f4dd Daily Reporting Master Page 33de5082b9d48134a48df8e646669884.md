## 🧠 How to Calculate Your KPIs

**Reply Rate**

> Replies Received ÷ Messages Sent × 100
> 

> Example: 8 replies ÷ 50 messages × 100 = **16% reply rate**
> 

> Target: 10% minimum. 15–20%+ is strong.
> 

**Booking Rate**

> Calls Booked ÷ Replies Received × 100
> 

> Example: 2 calls ÷ 8 replies × 100 = **25% booking rate**
> 

> Target: 20% minimum. 30%+ is strong.
> 

**Call Show-Up Rate**

> Calls Attended ÷ Calls Booked × 100
> 

> Example: 3 attended ÷ 4 booked × 100 = **75% show-up rate**
> 

> Target: 70% minimum. Below 60% means your confirmation process needs work.
>

## 🚨 Report Rules

- Submit by **7:00 PM every working day** — no exceptions
- If you cannot work that day, still send a message to your supervisor before 10:00 AM
- Do not round up numbers — report real figures only
- Do not leave any field blank — write 0 if the number is zero
- Do not send screenshots of sheets as your report — copy the text format above
- Three missed reports in a row = removed from the program
