# SOP-VA-002 — Google Maps Lead Scraping

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who Uses This:** VA Intern, VA Executive
> 

---

## Purpose

To find qualified local business leads using Google Maps search, extract key contact details, and enter them into the CRM in correct format.

---

## Pre-requisites

- CRM access confirmed
- KB-001 (What is a Lead) read and understood
- Daily lead target assigned by Manager
- Industry and location target assigned by Manager for the day

---

## Step-by-Step

**Step 1 — Open Google Maps**

1. Go to [maps.google.com](http://maps.google.com)
2. In the search bar, enter your assigned search query

**Step 2 — Use Correct Search Query Format**

Format: `[business type] in [city], [country]`

Examples:

- `accounting firm in Manchester, UK`
- `digital marketing agency in Dubai, UAE`
- `IT services company in Toronto, Canada`
- `law firm in Sydney, Australia`

> Use specific city names. Broad searches like "accounting USA" will return poor results.
> 

**Step 3 — Review Each Result (30 seconds max)**

For each result, check:

- [ ]  Is this a real business (not a residential address)?
- [ ]  Does it match your assigned industry?
- [ ]  Does it appear to be an SME (not a mega-corporation)?
- [ ]  Does it have a listed phone number or website?

Click into listings that pass the check.

**Step 4 — Capture Data**

For each qualified lead, record:

- Business Name
- Phone Number (if visible)
- Website URL (if listed)
- Address / Location
- Google Maps URL of the listing
- Industry / Category
- Source: "Google Maps"
- Date Added
- Assigned VA
- Status: "New"
- Notes: Any relevant detail (rating, years in business, category tag)

**Step 5 — Cross-Reference for Contact Person**

For higher-quality leads:

1. Copy the business name
2. Search it on LinkedIn
3. Find the Owner / Founder / Director
4. Add their LinkedIn URL to the CRM entry
5. This makes the lead usable for LinkedIn or email outreach

**Step 6 — Enter into CRM**

1. Open CRM sheet
2. Enter each qualified lead into a new row
3. Search for duplicates before adding
4. Report count to Manager at end of day

---

## Output Standard

- Minimum 20 qualified leads per working day
- All required fields complete
- Zero duplicates
- Website OR phone number captured for every lead
- Same-day CRM entry

---

## Common Mistakes

- Entering businesses with no contact information — these are unusable
- Not cross-referencing on LinkedIn to find the decision maker
- Using broad geographic searches ("marketing agencies USA") instead of city-specific
- Entering franchise locations instead of independent businesses
- Adding chains or multinationals (Starbucks, KPMG — not our ICP)

---

## QC Checklist

- [ ]  All required fields complete for every lead
- [ ]  No duplicate entries
- [ ]  Every lead has at least a phone number OR website URL
- [ ]  All leads are in the assigned industry and market
- [ ]  Daily count reported to Manager
