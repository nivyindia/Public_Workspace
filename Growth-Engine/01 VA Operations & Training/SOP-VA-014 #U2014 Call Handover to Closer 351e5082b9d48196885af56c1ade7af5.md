# SOP-VA-014 — Call Handover to Closer

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who Uses This:** VA Executive
> 

---

## Purpose

To pass a complete, accurate context package to the Nivy closer before every booked discovery call — so the closer walks in prepared and the call has the best chance of converting.

---

## Pre-requisites

- Call is confirmed as Booked in CRM
- Lead has confirmed the time (via Calendly or direct message)
- CRM entry is up to date

---

## Submission Deadline

> **Submit minimum 2 hours before the scheduled call.** Late submissions reduce the closer’s preparation time and lower conversion rates.
> 

---

## Handover Form — Complete This in Full

Copy and fill this format exactly. Submit via Notion page (to the shared handover section) or the assigned channel confirmed by Manager.

```
————————————————————
📞 CALL HANDOVER — [DATE]
————————————————————

Lead Name:
Job Title:
Company Name:
Company Website:
Market (US/UK/Canada/AU/UAE):
Platform where booked (LinkedIn/WhatsApp/Email/Instagram):

Call Date:
Call Time (with Timezone):
Calendar Invite Sent: Yes / No

Conversation Summary:
(2–3 lines — what did they say? What problem did they mention? What triggered them to respond?)

Suggested Nivy Service Area:
(Nivy Advisory / Nivy Next / Both / Unsure)

Pain Keywords from Conversation:
(e.g. "too busy for bookkeeping", "website needs a redesign", "trying to scale in the UK")

Lead Warmth: Cold / Warm / Hot
(Cold = never heard of Nivy. Warm = knows about us or asked smart questions. Hot = already wants to buy, just exploring.)

Any Special Notes:
(Sensitive context, competitor mentioned, specific ask, urgent deadline, etc.)

Submitted by:
Submission Time:
————————————————————
```

---

## After Submitting

1. Notify Manager: "Handover submitted for [Name], [Company] — call on [date/time]."
2. Update CRM Notes with "Handover submitted [date/time]"

---

## After the Call

After the closer completes the call, update CRM:

- Status → **Called**
- Notes → Add outcome as communicated by closer ("Closed Won", "Needs follow-up", "Closed Lost", etc.)

---

## Output Standard

- Form submitted minimum 2 hours before every call
- All fields complete — no blanks
- Conversation summary is in plain language (not bullet fragments)
- CRM updated after call outcome

---

## Common Mistakes

- Submitting the form 15 minutes before the call — this is not sufficient
- Leaving the Conversation Summary as 1 vague word ("interested") — it must be 2–3 real lines
- Not including Pain Keywords — these are the closer’s entry point into the conversation
- Forgetting to update CRM after the call outcome is known

---

## QC Checklist

- [ ]  Submitted minimum 2 hours before call
- [ ]  All fields filled — no blanks
- [ ]  Conversation summary is 2–3 real lines
- [ ]  Pain keywords included
- [ ]  Manager notified of submission
- [ ]  CRM updated after call outcome
