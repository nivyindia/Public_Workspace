# SOP-VA-006 — WhatsApp Outreach

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who Uses This:** VA Executive
> 

---

## Purpose

To execute structured WhatsApp outreach on qualified leads — using short, professional messages with a clear follow-up cadence.

---

## Pre-requisites

- Lead has a phone number in CRM
- Lead is from an assigned target market
- KB-002 read and understood
- Manager has confirmed WhatsApp is an approved channel for your assigned leads

---

## Daily Volume Limits

| Action | Daily Max |
| --- | --- |
| New WhatsApp messages | 15–20 |
| Follow-up messages | 10 |
| Total | ~25–30 |

> Sending too many WhatsApp messages from a single number risks the number being flagged or banned. Never exceed the daily limit.
> 

---

## Message Format Rules

- Max 4 lines for the first message
- No links, brochures, or attachments in message 1
- Greet by name
- Reference their business or role
- End with a soft question, not a pitch
- Use clear, conversational language — WhatsApp is less formal than email but still professional

---

## Message Templates

**Message 1 — First Contact**

> "Hi [Name] — my name is [Your Name] from Nivy Digital. I came across [Company Name] and wanted to reach out. We work with [business type] in [market] to help with [1-line pain]. Would it be worth a brief conversation?"
> 

**Message 2 — Follow-Up Day 3 (if no reply)**

> "Hi [Name] — just following up on my earlier message. Completely understand if the timing isn't right. We've helped businesses like [Company Name] with [pain] — happy to share a quick example if useful?"
> 

**Message 3 — Follow-Up Day 7 (if no reply)**

> "[Name] — one last follow-up. If there's ever a point where [pain area] becomes a priority, feel free to reach back out. Happy to help."
> 

After 3 messages with no reply → Mark as **Dead** in CRM.

---

## Reply Handling

| Reply | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Positive | Update CRM to Interested, escalate within 30 mins, go to SOP-VA-010 |
| "Who is this?" / "How did you get my number?" | Respond professionally: "Sorry to bother you — [Name from Nivy Digital]. Found your business through [source]. I'll leave you to it." Mark Dead. |
| Not interested | "No problem at all — thanks for your time." Mark Dead. |
| No reply after 3 messages | Mark Dead. |

---

## Output Standard

- 15–20 first messages sent daily
- All within daily limit
- CRM updated same day after every message
- Positive replies escalated within 30 minutes

---

## Common Mistakes

- Sending to leads without a confirmed phone number in CRM
- Not acknowledging negative replies — always close professionally
- Sending more than 3 messages on WhatsApp (unlike LinkedIn, WhatsApp has stricter cultural norms)
- Sending at unsociable hours (after 8pm or before 8am lead's local time)

---

## QC Checklist

- [ ]  All recipients have a verified phone number
- [ ]  Daily volume within limits
- [ ]  CRM updated after every message
- [ ]  Positive replies escalated same day
- [ ]  No message sent outside 8am–8pm lead’s local time
