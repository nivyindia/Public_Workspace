## 🚪 Section 1 — Entry Point Packages (What to Lead With)

The entry package is the first thing you offer a new prospect. Your job is to match it to what they actually need — not just pitch whatever you remember.

> **Rule:** The right entry package is the one that solves the most visible, urgent problem in the prospect's business right now.
> 

### 🇺🇸 USA Prospects — Entry Package Guide

| Prospect Signal | Best Entry Package | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "We need more leads / clients" | Basic Ads Setup or SEO Starter | Paid & organic traffic are the fastest credibility builders in USA market |
| "Our website looks outdated" | Basic Website Starter | Visual credibility is essential before any other marketing |
| "We're not on social media" | Social Media Starter | USA brands expect active social presence |
| "We sell products online" | Shopify Setup Starter | E-commerce entry; natural upsell path to Growth System |
| "We want to run ads" | Basic Ads Setup | High intent — move fast, pitch a call quickly |
| "We need content / videos" | Video Editing or Graphic Design Starter | Creative-first pitch for content businesses |

> **USA framing tip:** Lead with outcomes, not services. Don't say "we do SEO." Say "we help businesses like yours show up when people search for what you offer."
> 

### 🌍 International Prospects (UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, India) — Entry Package Guide

| Prospect Signal | Best Entry Package | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "We need more enquiries" | SEO Starter or Social Media Starter | ROI-first framing works — "more visibility = more enquiries" |
| "We need a website" | Basic Website Starter | High demand in developing markets; strong ROI story |
| "We use WhatsApp for business" | Business Email Setup | Professionalise their communication — easy win |
| "We want to grow on Instagram" | Social Media Starter | Instagram/WhatsApp-heavy markets respond well |
| "We sell products" | Shopify Setup Starter | E-commerce growing rapidly across UAE, India, Australia |
| "We run ads but they don't work" | Basic Ads Setup | Reframe as "fixing a broken system" — reduces resistance |

> **International framing tip:** Lead with value-for-money and ROI. These markets are price-conscious. Show them what they get, not just what it costs.

## 📈 Section 2 — The Upsell Ladder (Level 1 → Level 2 → Level 3 → Level 4)

Every client starts somewhere. Your job is to move them up the ladder as they see results and trust grows. This is how Nivy generates recurring and high-ticket revenue.

```
🟢 Level 1 — Entry (One-time / small monthly)
        ↓  Client sees results → introduce Level 2
🔵 Level 2 — Growth (Monthly retainer)
        ↓  Business scaling → introduce Level 3
🟣 Level 3 — Systems (High-ticket retainer)
        ↓  Enterprise / AI needs → introduce Level 4
🔴 Level 4 — AI & Automation (Premium / Enterprise)
```

### Upsell Timing — When to Introduce the Next Level

| From | To | Trigger Signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 → Level 2 | Any starter → matching growth package | Client mentions wanting "consistent results" / "ongoing support" / "monthly" / "we like what we're seeing" |
| Level 2 → Level 3 | Growth package → matching system | Client mentions scaling, team growth, multiple channels, "we need a full system" |
| Level 3 → Level 4 | System → AI/Enterprise | Client is an enterprise, mentions AI tools, automation, large team, complex operations |

### Upsell Scripts (Exact Language)

**From Level 1 → Level 2**

> *"Great that you're happy with the setup. Most of our clients who want to keep building on this move to our [SEO/Social/Ads] Growth package — it's a monthly retainer that handles everything ongoing so you don't have to restart every few months. Want me to walk you through what that looks like?"*
> 

**From Level 2 → Level 3**

> *"Based on where you're heading, it sounds like you might benefit from a full system rather than individual services. We have a package that integrates everything — growth, automation, lead gen — into one system. It's what businesses use when they're scaling fast. Worth a conversation?"*
> 

**Introducing Level 4**

> *"If you're thinking about AI or automation at a business-wide level, we have an enterprise tier for that. It's not for everyone — but based on what you've described, it might be worth a senior-level call to explore. Can I arrange that?"*

## 🔀 Section 3 — Cross-Sell Combos (What Goes Together)

Cross-selling means pitching a second package that naturally complements what the client already has or wants. Done well, it feels like advice, not sales.

> **Rule:** Only cross-sell when there's a genuine logical connection. Never bundle for the sake of it.
> 

### High-Performance Cross-Sell Combinations

| Anchor Package | Cross-Sell With | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Website Starter | SEO Starter | *"A website no one finds is a missed opportunity — SEO makes sure people actually land on it."* |
| Basic Website Starter | Social Media Starter | *"We build the website and then drive traffic to it through social."* |
| SEO Starter | Social Media Starter | *"Search and social together dominate local and national visibility."* |
| Basic Ads Setup | SEO Starter | *"Ads work fast, SEO builds long-term. Together they cover both."* |
| Social Media Starter | Graphic Design Starter | *"Great content needs great visuals — they work together."* |
| Social Media Starter | Video Editing Starter | *"Reels and short video are the highest-performing social content right now."* |
| Shopify Setup Starter | Basic Ads Setup | *"Once the store is live, ads drive the traffic that converts."* |
| Shopify Setup Starter | SEO Starter | *"Product pages that rank on Google = free traffic forever."* |
| Any starter × 2+ | NIVY NEXT Final Package Tables | *"If you're looking at multiple services, our bundled package pricing often works out better — want me to share that?"* |

### Cross-Sell Script

> *"One thing I'd mention — [Anchor package] works really well alongside [Cross-sell]. A lot of our clients do both because [specific reason]. It's not required, but it tends to get results faster. Worth including on the call?"*

## 🌐 Section 4 — Market-Specific Guidance

### 🇺🇸 USA Market — High-Ticket Framing

- **Lead with outcomes, not deliverables.** USA prospects buy results. Say *"more qualified leads"* not *"10 LinkedIn messages per day."*
- **Price anchoring works.** Reference what US agencies charge vs what Nivy offers. *"A US agency charges $3,000–$5,000/month for this. We deliver the same outcome at a fraction of that cost."*
- **Move fast.** USA prospects have short attention spans in outreach. Get to the point in 2–3 sentences. Ask for the call by message 2.
- **Use the USA Package Blueprint** for exact language, framing, and positioning → [📦 USA Package Blueprint](https://www.notion.so/34fe5082b9d480c5b54bd7a0e40127c9)

### 🌍 International Markets — Value & ROI Framing

- **Lead with ROI and affordability.** International prospects (especially India, UAE, Southeast Asia) compare price heavily. Show the value math.
- **Tangible outcomes beat jargon.** Avoid agency buzzwords. Say *"your business shows up when people search for [their category]"* not *"organic SERP optimisation."*
- **WhatsApp is primary.** Most international outreach happens on WhatsApp. Keep messages shorter, warmer, and conversational.
- **Trust builds slower.** Expect 3–5 follow-ups before serious interest. Don't push too hard too fast.
- **UAE & Australia** respond to professional, structured pitches similar to UK/US but with a relationship-first tone.

## 💬 Section 5 — Conversation Triggers (Signals That Tell You What to Pitch)

These are things a prospect says (or implies) in a conversation that tell you exactly which package to pitch next.

| What They Say / Imply | What It Means | What to Pitch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *"No one finds us online"* | Low visibility / no SEO | SEO Starter → SEO Growth (Level 2 upsell) |
| *"Our website is old / we need a new one"* | Website credibility gap | Basic Website Starter → Outcome-Based positioning |
| *"We're not active on social"* | Social absence | Social Media Starter → Social Growth System |
| *"We tried ads but they didn't work"* | Poor ad setup / targeting | Basic Ads Setup → reframe as "fixing the foundation" |
| *"We need more clients consistently"* | Wants ongoing results | Level 2 Growth package (retainer) |
| *"We sell products / we have a store"* | E-commerce | Shopify Setup → Basic Ads → Social Media |
| *"We want to scale fast"* | Growth-stage business | Level 3 System package |
| *"We use a lot of AI / we're a tech company"* | Enterprise / automation need | Level 4 AI & Automation — escalate to closer |
| *"What do other businesses like ours use?"* | Wants social proof + guidance | Reference case studies → pitch most relevant starter |
| *"We already have [X service] with someone else"* | Competitive objection | Cross-sell what they don't have → show Nivy's integrated value |

### Package-Specific Objections

**"SEO takes too long to show results"**

> *"That's actually true for most agencies. The difference is we start with technical fixes and quick wins — local listings, on-page — that often show movement in 4–6 weeks. Long-term ranking is built over months, but you'll see early signals quickly."*
> 

**"We already have a website"**

> *"Great — is it generating enquiries for you consistently? A lot of businesses have a website that's live but not working. We can look at whether it's set up to convert, or whether an upgrade would make a meaningful difference."*
> 

**"Social media doesn't work for B2B"**

> *"It depends on the platform and the approach. LinkedIn works very well for B2B — we use a content + outreach combination that builds authority and generates leads directly. Happy to show examples on a call."*
> 

**"Ads are expensive"**

> *"The setup package is a one-time cost that gets the foundation right — targeting, creatives, pixel, tracking. Without that, ad spend is wasted. Once the setup is done, the ongoing costs are entirely in your control."*
> 

**"We don't have a budget right now"**

> *"Totally understand. Can I ask — is budget tight across the board, or is it specifically about committing to this kind of spend without seeing results first? There are starter options that are low commitment — worth a 15-minute conversation to see if anything fits."*

## 🔁 Quick Reference — The Selling Framework in 5 Steps

1. **Identify the problem** — listen for pain points, frustrations, or goals in the conversation
2. **Match the entry package** — use Section 1 to find the right starting point
3. **Pitch the outcome, not the service** — "more clients from Google" not "we do SEO"
4. **Move toward a call** — every conversation should end with a call ask, not a price discussion
5. **Plant the upsell seed** — mention that there are growth options once they're happy with results

> **The golden rule of selling Nivy packages:** The goal of outreach is not to close a deal. It is to book a call. Closing happens on the call. Your job is to get them curious, interested, and on a calendar.
