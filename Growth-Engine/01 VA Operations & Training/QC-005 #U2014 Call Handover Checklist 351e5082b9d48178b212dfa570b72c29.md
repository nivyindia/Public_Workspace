## QC-005 — Call Handover Checklist

### Completeness Check:

- [ ]  Lead Name filled in (full name, correctly spelled)
- [ ]  Job Title filled in
- [ ]  Company Name filled in
- [ ]  Company Website filled in (or noted as "not found")
- [ ]  Market confirmed (US / UK / Canada / AU / UAE)
- [ ]  Platform where booked confirmed
- [ ]  Call Date filled in (dd/mm/yyyy)
- [ ]  Call Time filled in WITH timezone (e.g. 3:00 PM EST)
- [ ]  Calendar Invite status confirmed (Yes / No)
- [ ]  Conversation Summary is 2–3 full sentences (not 1 word like "interested")
- [ ]  Suggested Nivy Service Area filled in
- [ ]  Pain Keywords from conversation filled in (minimum 2 pain phrases)
- [ ]  Lead Warmth level filled in (Cold / Warm / Hot)
- [ ]  Special Notes completed (or "None")
- [ ]  Submitter name and submission time filled in

### Timing Check:

- [ ]  Submission time is minimum 2 hours before the scheduled call

### Post-Submission:

- [ ]  Manager notified: "Handover submitted for [Name], [Company] — [date/time]"
- [ ]  CRM updated: "Handover submitted [date/time]"
- [ ]  24-hour reminder to lead scheduled

---

**The closer is your partner. A complete handover form is the best thing you can do to maximise the chance of closing this call. Never rush it.**
