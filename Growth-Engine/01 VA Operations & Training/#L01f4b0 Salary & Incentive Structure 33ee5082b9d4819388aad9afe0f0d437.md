# 💰 Salary & Incentive Structure

<aside>
💰

This page covers exactly what you earn as a VA at Nivy — base pay, per-deal commission, weekly rewards, and promotion levels. Read this before Stage 5.

</aside>

## Base salary

**VA Base Salary: $300 USD/month**

Requirements to receive full base salary:

- Minimum 22 daily reports submitted in the 30-day period
- KPI completion score of 65%+ for the month

| Performance score | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| 65%+ | Full base salary ($300) |
| 40–64% | Reduced payout (proportional) |
| Below 40% | Per-deal commission only — no base |

---

## Per-deal commission

Earned when a call you booked converts to a paying client (confirmed by the closer).

| Deals closed in the month | Your commission per deal |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 deals | $15 per deal |
| 3–5 deals | $20 per deal |
| 6–10 deals | $25 per deal |
| 11+ deals | $30 per deal |

> Commission is calculated on deals closed in that calendar month. Deals that close in the next month count toward the next month's total.
> 

---

## Weekly performance rewards

| Award | Criteria | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🥇 Top Performer | Highest combined calls booked + reply rate for the week | Food voucher ~$8–10 |
| 🥈 Most Improved | Biggest percentage improvement vs your own previous week | Entertainment voucher ~$5–8 |
| 🟢 Perfect Attendance | Daily report submitted every Mon–Sat | +$10 added to monthly salary |

---

## Team leader salary

**Team Leader Base: $450 USD/month**

Plus:

- Same per-deal commission as above (for your own booked calls)
- $5 per deal closed by VAs under your supervision

---

## KPI scoring system (how your monthly % is calculated)

| Component | Weight | How it is calculated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outreach completion | 30% | Total actions done ÷ total targets × 30 |
| Engagement + follow-ups | 25% | Follow-ups sent ÷ target × 25 |
| Leads generated | 20% | Leads added ÷ target × 20 |
| Calls booked | 15% | Calls booked ÷ target × 15 |
| Revenue / pipeline contribution | 10% | Based on supervisor assessment |

**Final score = sum of all above out of 100**

---

## Career levels and pay progression

| Level | Role | Requirements | Pay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Starter VA (training) | Joined program | Unpaid / stipend |
| Level 2 | Performing VA | 70%+ score consistently | $300/month base + commission |
| Level 3 | Senior VA / Top Performer | 80%+ for 3+ weeks | $300 base + higher commission tier + weekly awards |
| Level 4 | Team Leader | Top 10%, nominated by supervisor | $450/month + team commission |
| Level 5 | Supervisor | Proven leadership, manages 10+ VAs | Negotiated salary |

---

## Quarterly bonuses (paid every 3 months)

| Achievement | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| 100+ calls booked in a quarter | $50 bonus |
| 0 fake leads reported across the quarter | $25 quality bonus |
| 100% report submission for 60+ days | $25 attendance bonus |
| Promoted to Team Leader in the quarter | $50 promotion bonus |

---

## Payment timing

- Base salary: paid on the 5th of the following month
- Commission: paid on the 10th of the following month (after deal confirmation)
- Weekly rewards: paid same week (Friday)
- Quarterly bonuses: paid on the 5th after the quarter ends

> All payments subject to report submission confirmation and CRM audit. If reports are missing, payment may be delayed until the audit is cleared.
>
