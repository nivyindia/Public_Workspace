# 👥 VA Role & Responsibility Matrix

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** Ops Head | **Status:** Live
> 

---

## Purpose

This matrix defines exactly what each VA role **owns**, **decides independently**, **escalates**, and **cannot do**. It removes 80% of daily escalations by giving every team member a clear boundary.

When in doubt, refer here first. If your situation is not listed, escalate to your Manager.

---

## Role & Responsibility Matrix

| Role | Owns | Decides Independently | Escalates To | Cannot Do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **VA Intern** | Lead scraping, data entry, basic outreach | Which leads to skip (clear hard disqualifier from KB-001) | VA Manager | Modify scripts, mark leads Closed, assign other VAs, engage in pricing conversations |
| **VA Executive** | Full outreach, follow-ups, CRM management, booking flow | Reply handling, follow-up timing within SOP, moving leads to Dead after full sequence | VA Manager | Change KPI targets, assign tasks to other VAs, approve onboarding |
| **VA Manager** | Team QC review, lead assignment, onboarding gate sign-off, retraining, weekly scorecard review | Retraining decisions, daily priorities, lead assignment, onboarding gate pass/fail | Ops Head | Change strategy, hire or fire, alter pricing, make decisions on client delivery |
| **Ops Head / Founder** | Strategy, system design, hiring, pricing, client relationships | Everything at the system level | — | Should not be pulled into daily ops decisions |

---

## Escalation Flow

```
VA Intern
    ↓
 VA Executive
    ↓
 VA Manager
    ↓
 Ops Head / Founder
```

> Skip a level only if the Manager is unreachable and the situation is urgent (e.g., client complaint, system failure). Always notify the skipped level after.
> 

---

## Decision Authority Quick Reference

| Decision | Who Makes It |
| --- | --- |
| Skip a clearly disqualified lead | VA Intern / Executive |
| Adjust follow-up timing (within SOP) | VA Executive |
| Move lead to Dead status | VA Executive (after full sequence) |
| Approve a VA for live work after onboarding | VA Manager |
| Reassign leads between VAs | VA Manager |
| Retrain a VA showing consistent errors | VA Manager |
| Escalate a client complaint | VA Manager + Ops Head |
| Change the outreach script | Ops Head (with Manager input) |
| Promote a VA to next level | Ops Head (based on scorecard) |
| Set or change KPI targets | Ops Head |

---

## The Boundary Rule

> **If you are unsure whether the decision is yours to make — it is not. Escalate it.**
> 

Role boundaries are not about distrust. They are about clarity. A team where everyone knows their boundary runs faster, makes fewer errors, and requires less founder involvement.
