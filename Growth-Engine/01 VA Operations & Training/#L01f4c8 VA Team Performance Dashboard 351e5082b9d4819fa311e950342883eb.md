# 📈 VA Team Performance Dashboard

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

---

## Purpose

This page is the Manager’s weekly performance review hub. Every Monday by 10:00 AM, the Manager reviews all VA scorecards from the previous week, identifies patterns, takes action where needed, and reports to the Ops Head by 12:00 PM.

**Time budget: 20 minutes total.**

---

## Monday Morning Review — Step-by-Step

**Step 1 (5 min) — Pull all VA scores**

Open each VA’s scorecard. Record last week’s total score in the Team Summary table below.

**Step 2 (5 min) — Identify flags**

- Any VA below 60%? → Schedule 1:1 this week (not next week)
- Any VA at ⭐ 90%+? → Flag for recognition
- Any metric that’s consistently low across 2+ VAs? → Flag as a system issue, not a VA issue

**Step 3 (5 min) — Write team aggregate**

Complete the Team Weekly Summary (below).

**Step 4 (5 min) — Send summary to Ops Head**

Forward the Team Weekly Summary via WhatsApp by 12:00 PM Monday.
