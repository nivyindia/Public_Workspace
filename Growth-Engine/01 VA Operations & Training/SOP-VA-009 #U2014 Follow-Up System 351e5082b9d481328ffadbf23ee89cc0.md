# SOP-VA-009 — Follow-Up System

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who Uses This:** VA Intern, VA Executive
> 

---

## Purpose

To execute a time-based follow-up sequence for every lead that has been contacted but has not yet replied — ensuring no lead falls through the cracks.

---

## The Follow-Up Schedule

| Day | Action | Message Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 0 | First outreach sent | Introduction + soft question |
| Day 3 | Follow-Up 1 | Different angle or observation |
| Day 7 | Follow-Up 2 | Add value / insight |
| Day 14 | Follow-Up 3 | Short check-in |
| Day 30 | Follow-Up 4 (Final) | Polite close |
| Day 31+ | No reply = Dead | Mark CRM as Dead |

> **Rule:** Follow-up messages are sent on the exact day in the schedule — not earlier, not later. CRM must have the First Contact Date filled in for this to work.
> 

---

## Follow-Up Message Templates (Platform-Agnostic)

**Follow-Up 1 (Day 3)**

> "Hey [Name] — just following up on my last message. I know timing isn't always right. Wanted to share that we've recently helped a [similar business type] in [their market] with [specific pain]. Would it make sense to have a quick call?"
> 

**Follow-Up 2 (Day 7)**

> "[Name] — checking in again. One thing we've noticed for [their industry] businesses is [1-line insight]. Happy to walk you through how that applies to [Company Name]."
> 

**Follow-Up 3 (Day 14)**

> "[Name] — didn't want to lose touch. Still happy to have a quick 15-minute call if [pain area] is ever on your radar."
> 

**Follow-Up 4 (Day 30 — Final)**

> "[Name] — I'll leave it here. If circumstances change and [pain area] becomes a priority, feel free to reach out anytime. All the best."
> 

After Day 30 with no reply → Mark CRM status as **Dead**.

---

## Step-by-Step

**Step 1 — Check CRM daily for leads due a follow-up**

- Filter by "First Contact Date" column
- Identify any lead where today is Day 3, 7, 14, or 30 from their first contact

**Step 2 — Send the correct follow-up message**

- Use the template for the correct follow-up day
- Personalise the opener where possible (add a reference or context)

**Step 3 — Update CRM immediately**

- Change "Last Contact Date" to today
- Change "Follow-Up Stage" to the number just sent (FU1, FU2, FU3, FU4)

**Step 4 — Mark Dead after Day 30**

- If Day 30 follow-up has been sent and no reply received after 1 day → Status = Dead

---

## When to Break the Sequence

- Lead replies at any point → Stop the sequence. Move to SOP-VA-010 (Interested Lead Handling)
- Lead says "not interested" → Stop the sequence. Mark Dead. Always close professionally.
- Lead unsubscribes (email) or asks to stop being messaged → Mark Dead immediately. No more contact.

---

## Output Standard

- Zero leads missed in follow-up schedule
- All follow-ups sent on the correct day (±1 day tolerance)
- CRM updated same day as every follow-up
- Positive replies escalated within 30 minutes of reply received

---

## Common Mistakes

- Not updating CRM with follow-up stage after sending — this breaks the sequence tracking
- Sending all follow-ups in 1 week because they "forgot" earlier — not acceptable
- Sending 6 or 7 messages to a lead beyond the sequence — maximum is 5 total (including first contact)
- Not personalising — using the exact same text for every follow-up

---

## QC Checklist

- [ ]  CRM checked daily for leads due follow-up
- [ ]  Correct follow-up number sent for the right day
- [ ]  CRM updated after every follow-up
- [ ]  Dead leads marked same day as Day 30 follow-up
- [ ]  No lead received more than 5 messages total across the sequence
