# 🌍 INTERNATIONAL MARKET PLAYBOOK — VA Sales Guide

> **Who this is for:** All VAs selling to International prospects (India, Middle East, Southeast Asia, Africa, UK, Europe, Australia). Read before your first international conversation.
> 

---

## 🌍 Why International Markets Are Different

International prospects are highly value-conscious and ROI-focused. They want to understand exactly what they're getting and why it's worth it. **Trust is built through clarity, not credentials.** Unlike the USA, price IS often discussed early — be prepared with clear value justification.

- Real estate businesses needing SEO + Google Ads

- Luxury retail and hospitality businesses

### 🇬🇧 UK / Europe / Australia

- SMBs wanting consistent monthly marketing management
- Coaches, consultants, and service businesses
- Local businesses needing SEO + social media

### 🇿🇦 Africa / Southeast Asia

- Growing businesses wanting first-time professional digital marketing
- E-commerce startups
- Businesses moving from offline to online

## 💬 International Language & Framing Guide

### ✅ DO:

- Lead with ROI and business outcomes ("more leads", "more revenue", "lower cost per acquisition")
- Use WhatsApp as primary communication channel where appropriate
- Be warm, clear, and patient — relationship-first in Middle East and South Asian markets
- Offer clear package breakdowns and what's included
- Reference local market examples where possible

### ❌ AVOID:

- Overly formal or corporate language (except UAE/UK)
- Assuming all international clients have small budgets
- Rushing to close — international markets often need 2–3 more touchpoints than USA

## 🚀 Top 5 Entry Packages for International Prospects

1. **SEO Starter Package** — High demand across all markets. Local businesses everywhere want Google rankings.
2. **Social Media Starter Package** — B2C businesses in every market need social media. Easy conversation starter.
3. **Basic Website Starter Package** — Massive market. Millions of businesses still have no website or an outdated one.
4. **Google Ads PPC Growth System (Level 2)** — Strong in Middle East and India where Google Ads ROI is well understood.
5. **Business Email Setup** — Extremely easy sell in India, SEA, and Africa. Professional email = credibility.

We work with [similar businesses] in [their region] and have helped them [specific result]. Would you be open to a quick 10-minute call to see how it could work for you?

## ⚠️ International Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "It's too expensive" | "I understand. Let me show you exactly what's included and what ROI you can realistically expect. Most clients recover their investment within 60–90 days." |
| "We tried digital marketing and it didn't work" | "That's really common. The issue is usually strategy, not the channel. What specifically did you try, and what results did you get?" |
| "We do it in-house" | "That's great — many of our clients have internal teams. We often work alongside them to handle the specialist work they don't have time for." |
| "Can you share some results?" | "Absolutely — what industry are you in? I'll share the most relevant case study." |

## 📌 Key Rules for International Sales

1. Match your communication channel to the market (WhatsApp for India/ME, LinkedIn for UK/AU)
2. Always have a local pricing reference ready — "compared to local agencies, we offer..."
3. Follow up more frequently — international markets often need 5–7 touchpoints
4. Never quote prices via WhatsApp without qualifying the lead first
5. Use the VA Upsell & Cross-Sell guide to identify the right next package at every stage
