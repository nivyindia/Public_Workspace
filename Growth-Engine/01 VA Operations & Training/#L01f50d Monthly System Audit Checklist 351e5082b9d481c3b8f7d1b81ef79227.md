# 🔍 Monthly System Audit Checklist

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> ⚠️ Run this audit every calendar month. Target time: 60–90 minutes. Log the outcome in the Monthly Audit Log at the bottom of this page.
> 

---

## Purpose

The Monthly System Audit ensures the VA system stays accurate, complete, and functional over time. Systems decay without maintenance. This checklist is the maintenance schedule.

---

## Section 1 — Knowledge Base Audit (15 min)

- [ ]  Open each KB page (KB-001 through KB-006) and read through
- [ ]  Are the ICP criteria, platforms, and role descriptions still accurate?
- [ ]  Are any product details in KB-004 outdated (pricing tier references, division names)?
- [ ]  Does anything contradict a process change that happened this month?
- [ ]  Is the Last Updated date correct on each page?

**Action:** Update any page that is out of date. Increment version number. Log changes.

---

## Section 2 — SOP Audit (20 min)

- [ ]  Open each SOP (SOP-VA-001 through SOP-VA-016)
- [ ]  Are all steps still accurate for how VAs actually work today?
- [ ]  Have any platform limits changed (LinkedIn connection limits, Instagram DM caps)?
- [ ]  Have any CRM fields changed since the SOP was written?
- [ ]  Are the volume limits still correct and safe?
- [ ]  Have any tools been added or removed from the tech stack?
- [ ]  Does each SOP still end with a "Next →" link pointing to the correct page?

**Action:** Update any SOP that has drifted from actual practice. Note all changes.

---

## Section 3 — QC Checklist Audit (10 min)

- [ ]  Open QC-001 through QC-005
- [ ]  Have there been recurring errors this month that suggest a check is missing from a checklist?
- [ ]  Have any new common mistakes emerged that should be added?
- [ ]  Are the SLA deadlines in QC-004 and QC-005 still correct?

**Action:** Add or update checklist items as needed.

---

## Section 4 — Scorecard & Performance Audit (10 min)

- [ ]  Are all active VA scorecards up to date with last week’s scores?
- [ ]  Is any VA’s scorecard missing weeks of data?
- [ ]  Does the Team Performance Dashboard have entries for the last 4 weeks?
- [ ]  Are all 1:1 coaching sessions from this month logged in the coaching log?
- [ ]  Are promotion-eligible VAs flagged and actioned?

**Action:** Fill in any gaps in scorecard data. Ensure no VA has been overlooked.

---

## Section 5 — Onboarding Records Audit (5 min)

- [ ]  Does every VA who joined this month have an Onboarding Completion Record?
- [ ]  Are all gate sign-offs completed and dated?
- [ ]  Are there any VA onboarding records that are incomplete or missing gates?

**Action:** Complete or flag any outstanding onboarding records.

---

## Section 6 — Document Standards Audit (10 min)

- [ ]  Scan all pages in the VA system. Does every page have the required header block (Version / Last Updated / Owner / Status)?
- [ ]  Is there any page with a vague or non-coded name?
- [ ]  Are there any blank or placeholder pages that have never been completed?
- [ ]  Are there duplicate pages for the same topic?
- [ ]  Are any pages still in “Draft” status that should be Live by now?

**Action:** Fix naming, complete drafts, delete genuine duplicates (confirm with Ops Head before deletion).

---

## Section 7 — Broken Links & Navigation Check (5 min)

- [ ]  Click the "Next →" link at the bottom of each KB page. Does it load correctly?
- [ ]  Does the Phase 1–6 folder structure still make sense? Is everything easy to find?
- [ ]  Are there any pages that are orphaned (not linked from anywhere)?

**Action:** Repair broken links. Add orphaned pages to the relevant folder.

---

## Section 8 — CRM Health Check (10 min)

- [ ]  Are there any leads still at "New" status that are more than 5 days old (not yet contacted)?
- [ ]  Are there any leads stuck at "Interested" for more than 7 days without a follow-up?
- [ ]  Are there any leads with no Last Contact Date filled in?
- [ ]  Run duplicate check (SOP-VA-012): any duplicates found this month?
- [ ]  Are all leads that completed the full follow-up sequence marked Dead?

**Action:** Fix CRM issues. Flag any patterns to Manager for team briefing.
