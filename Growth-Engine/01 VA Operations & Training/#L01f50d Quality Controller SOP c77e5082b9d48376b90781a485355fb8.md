# 🔍 Quality Controller SOP

---

# 

## 🎯 Role Objective

> Ensure all outreach is **high-quality, personalized, and conversion-focused** — not spam.
> 

👉 You are responsible for:

- Message quality
- Reply handling
- Conversion improvement

---

# 📅 DAILY SOP (Step-by-Step)

## 🌅 STEP 1 — Review Assigned Work (Morning)

### ✅ Get from Supervisor:

- List of all VAs
- Their work (tracker + proof links)

---

## 🔍 STEP 2 — Quality Check (Core Work)

### You DO NOT check everything

👉 Check **random samples (20–30%) per VA**

---

### ✅ What to Check:

## 1. Outreach Message Quality

Check:

- Is it personalized? (Name, business, context)
- Is it relevant to prospect?
- Is it human or copy-paste spam?

---

### ❌ Bad Example:

```
Hi sir, we provide digital marketing services, are you interested?
```

### ✅ Good Example:

```
Hi Rahul, saw your clinic in Delhi — noticed you're not running ads currently. We recently helped a similar clinic get 30+ monthly leads. Would you like a quick idea?
```

---

## 2. Targeting Quality

Check:

- Are they messaging the **right audience**?
- Or random people?

👉 Example:

- Tax service → business owners ✔
- Tax service → students ❌

---

## 3. Conversation Flow (VERY IMPORTANT)

Check chats:

- Are they replying properly?
- Or just sending 1 message and stopping?

---

### Ideal Flow:

1. Hook message
2. Prospect replies
3. VA asks qualifying question
4. Moves to appointment

---

## 4. Follow-Up Quality

Check:

- Are they sending follow-ups?
- Or abandoning leads?

---

### Example Follow-Up:

```
Hey Rahul, just checking if you saw this — can share a quick idea for your clinic growth.
```

---

## 📊 STEP 3 — Give Quality Score (Daily)

Each VA gets score:

| Parameter | Marks |
| --- | --- |
| Personalization | /10 |
| Relevance | /10 |
| Conversation handling | /10 |

👉 Total out of 30 → convert to %

---

### Example:

| VA | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Rahul | 24/30 (80%) |
| Aman | 15/30 (50%) |

---

## ⚠️ STEP 4 — Identify Issues

### Common Issues to Flag:

- Copy-paste messaging
- No personalization
- Poor replies
- No follow-ups
- Wrong audience targeting

---

## 📢 STEP 5 — Give Feedback (MANDATORY)

### Daily feedback in simple format:

Rahul:

Good personalization, improve follow-ups.

Aman:

Messages are copy-paste. Add personalization.

Neha:

Targeting wrong audience. Focus on business owners only.

---

## 🤝 STEP 6 — Coordinate with Supervisor

- Share:
    - Quality scores
    - Problem VAs
- Align on:
    - Who needs warning
    - Who needs training
