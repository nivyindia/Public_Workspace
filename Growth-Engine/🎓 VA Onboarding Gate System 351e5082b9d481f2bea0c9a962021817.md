# 🎓 VA Onboarding Gate System

Every new VA must pass through this gate before going live on outreach.

---

## Purpose

Ensure no VA sends outreach messages until they have demonstrated minimum readiness. This protects lead quality and brand reputation.

---

## The Gate Criteria

A VA passes the gate when ALL of the following are confirmed:

### Gate 1 — System Access ✅

- [ ]  Notion workspace accessed
- [ ]  VA Dashboard bookmarked
- [ ]  VA Daily Workflow Checklist bookmarked
- [ ]  VA Quick Reference Card bookmarked
- [ ]  Supervisor contact saved

### Gate 2 — Knowledge Baseline ✅

- [ ]  Can explain the VA workflow in their own words (Lead → Outreach → Follow-up → Book → Handoff)
- [ ]  Can name at least 2 Level 1 packages and describe them in one sentence each
- [ ]  Knows where to find SOPs and what each major SOP covers

### Gate 3 — SOP Comprehension ✅

- [ ]  Has read SOP-VA-001 (Outreach Protocol)
- [ ]  Has read SOP-VA-002 (Message Templates)
- [ ]  Can demonstrate one outreach message written to SOP standard (practice message, not live)

### Gate 4 — Supervisor Sign-Off ✅

- [ ]  Supervisor has reviewed practice outreach message
- [ ]  Supervisor confirms VA is ready to go live
- [ ]  VA has been assigned their first lead list

## Gate Failure Protocol

- If a VA fails Gate 2: Assign specific reading. Re-test in 24 hours.
- If a VA fails Gate 3: Practice more messages. Supervisor reviews. Re-test in 24 hours.
- If a VA fails Gate 3 twice: Escalate to Manager with notes.

---

## Non-Negotiable

No VA goes live on outreach without Gate 4 sign-off from Supervisor.

No exceptions.
