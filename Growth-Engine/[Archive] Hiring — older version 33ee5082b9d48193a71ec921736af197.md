## Quick hiring decision guide

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Need more outreach volume | Hire Lead Research VA or Outreach VA |
| Too many unqualified calls | Promote an Appointment Setter with QC oversight |
| Team growing beyond 10 VAs | Promote top VA to Supervisor (Stage 8 criteria) |
| Closing rate dropping | Review Closer pipeline — not a VA hiring issue |
