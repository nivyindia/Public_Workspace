## How to Handle Pricing Questions During Outreach

When a prospect asks "how much does it cost?" during initial outreach, do NOT quote exact prices. Use this response:

> *"That depends on the scope of what you need — our team does a quick discovery call to understand your situation and then puts together a tailored proposal. It usually takes 15–20 minutes. Would [day] or [day] work for you?"*
> 

This keeps control of the conversation and routes them to the closer. Only share rough ranges if they push hard and you need to qualify their budget.

## When a Prospect Asks About Price — What to Say

**"How much does it cost?"**

> *"It depends on what you need — our packages start from $300/month for smaller businesses and scale from there. The best way to give you an accurate number is a quick 15-minute call where we understand your situation. Would that work?"*
> 

**"That's too expensive."**

> *"I understand — what's your current setup for [accounting/marketing/etc.]? Most of our clients find that what they're currently spending on [in-house staff / tools / time] actually costs more than our service once they add it up. Happy to share how that usually looks if it's helpful."*
> 

**"Can you send me a proposal first?"**

> *"Absolutely — our team prepares custom proposals after a quick discovery call so we can tailor it to your exact needs. It takes 15–20 minutes. Would [day] or [day] suit you?"*
> 

**"We already have someone for this."**

> *"That's great — a lot of our clients came to us when their existing setup wasn't scaling with them or when costs were rising. Not suggesting there's an issue, but if you ever want a second opinion or a cost comparison, happy to connect you with our team."*
>
