## 📊 Section 1 — Package Levels Overview

Nivy packages are organised into 4 levels. Each level represents a higher investment, longer commitment, and deeper integration with the client's business. VAs start selling Level 1. Upsells move clients up the ladder.

| Level | Name | Who It's For | Price Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟢 Level 1 | Entry Service Packages | Businesses starting out, testing digital marketing | One-time / small monthly |
| 🔵 Level 2 | Growth Management Packages | Businesses wanting consistent monthly results | Monthly retainer |
| 🟣 Level 3 | System Packages | Scaling businesses needing full-stack systems | High-ticket retainer |
| 🔴 Level 4 | AI & Automation Systems | Enterprise / high-growth businesses | Premium / Enterprise |
