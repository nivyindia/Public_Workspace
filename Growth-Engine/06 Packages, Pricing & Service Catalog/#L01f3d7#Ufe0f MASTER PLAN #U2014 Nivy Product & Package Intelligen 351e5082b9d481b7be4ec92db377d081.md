**Format every CPA package page must follow (based on SOP/VA Version Format):**

```
——————————————————
[PACKAGE NAME] — Full Detail Page
——————————————————
1. What is this package? (1-paragraph plain English explanation)
2. Who is it for? (ICP — business type, size, situation)
3. What’s included? (Full deliverables list)
4. What is NOT included? (Clear scope boundary)
5. Pricing (Starter / Growth / Scale tiers where applicable)
6. Markets (US / UK / Canada / AU / UAE — which apply)
7. VA Sales Script (how to introduce this in outreach)
8. Pain signals to listen for (words/phrases that indicate this package fits)
9. Objection handling (top 5 objections + responses)
10. Upsell / Cross-sell path (what to pitch next)
11. Escalation rule (when to hand over to closer)
12. Compliance notes (anything market-specific the VA must know)
——————————————————
```
