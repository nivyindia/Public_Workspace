## **1. Purpose of Verification**

Verification ensures that your ISO certificate is:

- **Authentic** and issued by an IAF-accredited body
- **Recognized globally** for tenders, exports, and vendor approvals
- **Linked to your company** for transparency and trust

## **2. How to Verify Your Certificate Online**

### **Step 1: Locate Your Certificate**

- Open your ISO certificate PDF
- Note the **Certificate Number / Verification Code**

### **Step 2: Access the Verification Portal**

- Visit our portal: [Verification Portal Link]

### **Step 3: Enter Certificate Details**

- Enter the **Certificate Number / Verification Code**
- Enter **Company Name** exactly as on certificate

### **Step 4: View Verification Result**

- Portal will display:
    - Certificate status (Valid / Expired / Revoked)
    - Issue date & validity
    - Scope of ISO standard
- You can **download verification proof** for tenders or clients

## **3. Tips for Smooth Verification**

- Ensure your **internet connection** is stable
- Use the **latest version of your browser**
- Enter the certificate details **exactly as provided**
- Contact your **compliance manager** if verification fails

## **4. Why Verification Matters**

- Builds **global trust with clients & partners**
- Prevents **rejection of tenders or contracts** due to invalid certification
- Provides **proof of compliance** for audits
