# ⏰ When to Send a Proposal {#when}

**Only send a proposal after:**

- [ ]  Discovery call completed
- [ ]  Client needs clearly understood (pain, timeline, budget)
- [ ]  Verbal interest confirmed (“yes, send me something”)
- [ ]  Correct service package identified

**Never send a proposal cold** — it becomes a price comparison document with no context.

---

# 📋 Proposal Structure {#structure}

**Section 1: Executive Summary**

- Who we are (2 sentences)
- What we understood their challenge to be
- What we’re proposing and why

**Section 2: Recommended Solution**

- Service package selected
- Specific deliverables (bullet list)
- What’s included / what’s not included

**Section 3: Pricing**

- Monthly retainer or one-time fee (clearly stated)
- Payment terms (e.g., invoiced monthly, due within 14 days)
- Optional add-ons with pricing

**Section 4: Timeline & Onboarding**

- Start date
- Onboarding steps (what happens after they sign)
- Expected time to see results

**Section 5: Why Nivy Digital**

- 2–3 relevant case studies or client results
- Credentials/certifications
- 1–2 testimonials

**Section 6: Next Steps**

- Clear CTA: "Sign below / reply to confirm / book a call if you have questions"
- Proposal validity: "This proposal is valid for 14 days"

| Tool | Use | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |

| HubSpot Quotes | Basic proposal + tracking | Free with HubSpot |
| Google Docs | Simple proposal (editable) | Free, less professional |
| Canva | Designed PDF proposal | Free, high-impact visual |

**Recommendation:** Use **Canva-designed PDF** for high-value deals (£500+/mo). Use **HubSpot Quotes** for fast/simple proposals.

# 📤 Sending & Follow-Up {#send}

**Sending:**

- Send via email with a personal note (not just attached without context)
- Subject: "Your Nivy Digital Proposal — [Company Name]"
- Email body: 3 sentences summarising what’s inside, enthusiasm, CTA

**Follow-Up:**

- Day 3: "Just checking you received the proposal — any questions?"
- Day 7: "Happy to jump on a 10-minute call to walk through any questions before you decide."
- Day 12: Final follow-up (see Sales Automation System)

# 🤝 Handling Negotiation {#negotiation}

**Common asks and how to handle them:**

| Ask | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "Can you do it cheaper?" | "Our pricing reflects the quality and expertise we bring. If budget is a concern, we could start with a smaller scope and expand from there." |
| "Can we start with just one service?" | "Absolutely — let’s start with [service], and we can add more as you see results." |
| "We need to think about it" | "Of course! What information would help you decide? Happy to answer any specific questions." |
| "We’re comparing with others" | "That makes sense. When you’re comparing, look beyond price — consider quality assurance, qualifications, and how responsive the team is." |

**Never discount more than 10% without founder approval.**

# ✒️ Contract & Sign-Off {#contract}

1. Use a simple service agreement (1–2 pages)
2. Include: scope, pricing, payment terms, notice period, confidentiality
3. Sign via PandaDoc, DocuSign, or Signable
4. Send signed copy to client + file in company records (Google Drive / Notion)
5. Log in HubSpot: deal stage → Closed Won
6. Trigger onboarding workflow

**Notice period:** 30 days written notice to terminate (monthly contracts)

**Payment terms:** Invoice monthly, payment due within 14 days
