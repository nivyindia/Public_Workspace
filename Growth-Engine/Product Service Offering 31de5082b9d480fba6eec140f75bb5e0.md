# Product / Service Offering

# *(Nivy Empires – Sales & Marketing / Business Plan Section)*

## 1. Overview of Product and Service Lines

Nivy Empires provides a comprehensive portfolio of professional services designed to support businesses across multiple operational and growth functions. The company delivers integrated solutions that combine financial management, digital marketing, technology development, and strategic consulting.

Rather than offering isolated services, the company operates as a multi-service ecosystem that allows clients to access a coordinated set of business support capabilities within a single framework.

The primary service lines include:

- Taxation and compliance services
- Accounting and financial management
- Digital marketing and growth services
- Website design and development
- Business consulting and strategy
- Lead generation and marketing automation

These services are delivered through a hybrid model combining internal experts, freelancers, strategic partners, and technology platforms to ensure scalability and cost efficiency.

### Growth Package

Designed for growing small and medium businesses.

### Scale Package
