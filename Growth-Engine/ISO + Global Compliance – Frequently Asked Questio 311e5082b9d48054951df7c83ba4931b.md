## **1. What is ISO Certification and why do I need it?**

ISO (International Organization for Standardization) certification ensures that your company meets **globally recognized quality, safety, or management standards**.

- Improves credibility with international clients
- Required for tenders, exports, and vendor approvals
- Streamlines internal processes and quality controls

---

## **2. How long does the certification process take?**

- Small companies (1–10 employees): 7–10 days
- Medium companies (11–50 employees): 10–15 days
- Large companies (50+ employees): 15–25 days
    
    *Timeline may vary depending on documentation completeness and audit scheduling.*

Yes, all certificates are **IAF-accredited**, verifiable online, and globally recognized.
