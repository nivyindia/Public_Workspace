# 🤝 Partnership Types {#types}

| Type | Structure | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Referral Partner | Bidirectional lead referrals + commission | CPA firm refers IT clients to us, we refer tax clients to them |
| White-Label Partner | We deliver services under their brand | Accounting firm white-labels our digital marketing service |
| Co-Marketing Partner | Joint content, webinars, campaigns | Joint LinkedIn series with a UAE business consultant |
| Strategic JV | Formal revenue-share partnership | Shared client acquisition cost + split margin on new clients |
| Reseller Partner | They sell our services to their client base | Business consultant resells our VA program to their clients |

# 📤 Partnership Outreach Process {#outreach}

**Step 1 — Identify:**

- Search LinkedIn for ideal partner profiles in target markets
- Find through referral networks and existing client relationships
- Look for complementary (not competitive) businesses

**Step 2 — Connect:**

- LinkedIn connection + personalised note
- Reference their work specifically
- Initial message: explore if there’s a fit before pitching anything

**Step 3 — Introductory Call:**

> "We work with [their type of clients] and often find they also need [service you offer]. I’d love to explore if there’s a way we can refer clients to each other. Are you open to a 20-minute call?"
> 

**Step 4 — Formalise:**

- Send partner agreement (1-page)
- Share partner intro pack
- Set up referral tracking in CRM
- Schedule monthly check-in

# 📊 Joint Venture Structures {#jv}

## JV Model 1: Revenue Share on New Clients

- Partner promotes Nivy Digital to their network
- For every client that signs, partner receives 15% of monthly revenue
- No time limit — partner earns as long as client stays
- Best for: strategic partners with large, warm audiences

## JV Model 2: Co-Created Service Package

- Partner + Nivy Digital create a bundled offer
- E.g., Business consultant’s strategy + Nivy Digital’s execution
- Split pricing, split delivery, split revenue
- Best for: consultants who want to add execution to their advisory

## JV Model 3: White-Label

- Nivy Digital delivers services under partner’s brand
- Partner adds margin on top of our pricing
- Client only knows the partner’s brand
- Best for: agencies that want to expand offerings without hiring
