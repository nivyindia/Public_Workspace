# 🔍 Demand Generation vs Lead Generation {#demand-vs-lead}

| Demand Generation | Lead Generation |
| --- | --- |
| Creates awareness and intent | Captures existing intent |
| Educates the market about us | Converts people who are already aware |
| Longer time horizon (3–6 months) | Immediate results |
| Feeds inbound pipeline | Feeds outbound pipeline |
| Examples: blog, LinkedIn, YouTube | Examples: cold email, forms, ads |

**We need both.** Outbound captures now; demand generation builds the engine that feeds us 12 months from now.

# 📡 Demand Generation Channels {#channels}

## 1. Content Marketing (Owned Media)

- Weekly blog posts targeting ICP search terms
- LinkedIn daily posts from the founder account
- YouTube tutorials and explainers
- Email newsletter (weekly)
- **Timeline to results:** 3–6 months
- **Effort:** High | **Cost:** Low | **Compounding:** ✅ Yes

## 2. LinkedIn Organic (Thought Leadership)

- Founder posts daily on LinkedIn (tax tips, business growth, international compliance)
- Company page posts 3x/week
- Comment on ICP posts to appear in their feed
- **Timeline to results:** 4–8 weeks for DMs, 3–6 months for brand recognition

## 3. SEO (Search-Led Demand)

- Blog posts rank for high-intent search terms
- Service pages rank for "accountant for SMEs UK" type searches
- Google Business Profile drives local/regional trust
- **Timeline to results:** 4–6 months
- **Compounding:** ✅ Yes — grows permanently

## 4. Referral & Word-of-Mouth

- 90-day referral ask to all active clients
- Partner network generating warm introductions
- **Timeline to results:** 3–4 months (after client base established)

## 5. Paid (Optional from Month 6)

- Google Ads: target high-intent keywords ("UK bookkeeping services")
- LinkedIn Ads: target job titles and company sizes
- Facebook Ads: India/UAE SME segment
- **Only activate after organic channels are proven**

# 📚 Content-Led Demand Generation {#content}

## The Content Engine

1. **Research:** Use Ahrefs/Google to find ICP search queries
2. **Create:** Write 1,500–2,500 word blog post targeting that query
3. **Publish:** Post on website + optimise for SEO
4. **Repurpose:** Turn into 5 LinkedIn posts + 1 newsletter section + 3 social snippets
5. **Distribute:** Share across all channels
6. **Repeat:** 8+ times per month

**One blog post = 10+ pieces of content = permanent SEO asset**

# 📈 Pipeline Velocity {#pipeline}

Pipeline velocity = (Number of deals × Average deal value × Win rate) ÷ Sales cycle length

**Nivy Digital targets:**

- Deals in pipeline: 30+
- Average deal value: £500/month retainer
- Win rate: 30%
- Sales cycle: 14 days average
- **Monthly revenue from pipeline:** 30 × £500 × 30% = £4,500/month from active pipeline

**To hit £50k MRR:** Need 100 deals in pipeline simultaneously at month 12 with scale.
