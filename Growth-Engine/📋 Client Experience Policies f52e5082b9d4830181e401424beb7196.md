# 📋 Client Experience Policies

# 1. CLIENT ONBOARDING PROCESS

### Phase 1: Pre-Onboarding (1-3 Days)

**Kickoff Package Delivery:**

- Welcome email with timeline and next steps
- Access to client portal/login credentials
- Onboarding checklist and requirements list
- Introductory video call scheduling

**Document Collection:**

- Brand assets (logos, style guides, fonts)
- Existing marketing materials
- Access to analytics accounts (Google Analytics, social media)
- Competitor information
- Business goals and KPIs

### Phase 2: Strategy Development (3-7 Days)

**Discovery Session:**

- 90-minute strategy meeting with key stakeholders
- SWOT analysis discussion
- Target audience profiling
- Competitive landscape review
- Goal setting and KPI establishment

**Strategy Presentation:**

- Customized marketing strategy document
- 30-day action plan
- Resource allocation and team introduction
- Tool setup and integration

### Phase 3: Implementation (7-10 Days)

**Account Setup:**

- Platform configurations (Google Ads, Facebook Business Manager, etc.)
- Tracking implementation (pixels, conversion tracking)
- Reporting dashboard setup
- Communication channels establishment

**Final Kickoff:**

- Team introduction meeting
- Campaign launch timeline review
- First month objectives confirmation
- Success metrics alignment

## 2. FEEDBACK & COMPLAINT RESOLUTION PROCESS

### 2.1 Feedback Collection System

**Regular Feedback Channels:**

- Monthly satisfaction surveys (1-5 scale)
- Quarterly strategic review meetings
- Post-campaign debrief sessions
- Annual relationship health check

**Informal Feedback:**

- End-of-call quick ratings
- Slack/email check-ins
- Project completion feedback forms

### 2.2 Complaint Resolution Framework

### Tier 1: Immediate Resolution (24-Hour Response)

**For minor issues:**

- Service delays under 48 hours
- Communication response time issues
- Reporting discrepancies under 10%

**Process:**

- Acknowledge receipt within 4 business hours
- Assign dedicated contact person
- Resolution within 24 hours
- Follow-up confirmation

### Tier 2: Strategic Issues (3-5 Day Resolution)

**For performance concerns:**

- Campaign underperformance vs. benchmarks
- Strategy alignment issues
- Team member performance concerns

**Process:**

- Senior account director involvement within 24 hours
- Root cause analysis meeting
- Corrective action plan within 3 business days
- Implementation and monitoring

### Tier 3: Escalation Process (7-10 Day Resolution)

**For serious concerns:**

- Contractual disputes
- Significant service failures
- Relationship-threatening issues

**Process:**

- Executive leadership involvement
- Formal review and documentation
- Mediation session if required
- Contract amendment or termination discussion

### 2.3 Escalation Matrix

```
Level 1: Account Manager → 4-hour response
Level 2: Account Director → 2-hour response
Level 3: Client Services Director → 1-hour response
Level 4: CEO/Founder → Immediate response

```

## 4. REVIEW & TESTIMONIAL REQUEST POLICY

### 4.1 Testimonial Collection Process

**Timing of Requests:**

- After 3 months of successful partnership
- Following major campaign successes
- At project completion milestones
- During annual renewal discussions

**Request Methodology:**

- Personalized email from account director
- Multiple format options (written, video, audio)
- Guided questions to make it easy for clients
- Clear explanation of how testimonial will be used

### 4.2 Review Platform Guidelines

**Where We Request Reviews:**

- Google Business Profile
- [Clutch.co](http://clutch.co/)
- UpCity
- Featured customers
- Industry-specific directories

**Best Practices:**

- Never offer incentives for positive reviews
- Make the process simple and time-efficient
- Respect client preferences for anonymity
- Provide multiple submission options

### 4.3 Testimonial Usage Policy

**Permissions Required:**

- Written consent for all testimonial usage
- Clear agreement on usage duration
- Specific platforms where testimonial will appear
- Right to withdraw consent at any time

**Ethical Guidelines:**

- Never edit testimonials to change meaning
- Always attribute accurately with name/company
- Maintain context when using excerpts
- Regular updates to keep testimonials current

### 4.4 Client Success Story Development

**For Case Study Creation:**

- Minimum 6-month partnership required
- Significant results demonstrated
- Client participation agreement
- Mutual approval process for final content

**Case Study Components:**

- Challenge, solution, results framework
- Quantitative data validation
- Client quotes and team insights
- Professional design and formatting
