# 📝 IP & Content Ownership Policy

**Policy Name:** Intellectual Property & Content Ownership Policy

**Effective Date:** 26 August 2025

**Version:** 1.0

**Applicable To:** All employees, contractors, clients, and third parties engaged with Nivy Tech.

---

### **1. Purpose**

This policy defines the ownership, usage, and protection of intellectual property (IP) created or used by Nivy Tech during the course of its business operations. It ensures clarity for clients, employees, and contractors regarding IP rights and prevents disputes.

---

### **2. Definitions**

- **Pre-Existing IP:** Intellectual property created by Nivy Tech or the client before the start of a project.
- **Project IP:** Intellectual property created specifically for a client during a project.
- **Background IP:** Pre-Existing IP incorporated into a client project.
- **Foreground IP:** New IP created during a client project.

---

### **3. Ownership of Pre-Existing IP**

3.1. Nivy Tech retains full ownership of all Pre-Existing IP, including but not limited to:

- Proprietary strategies, methodologies, and frameworks.
- Software tools, algorithms, and internal systems.
- Templates, designs, and content not created for a specific client.

3.2. Clients retain ownership of their Pre-Existing IP provided to Nivy Tech for project execution.

---

### **4. Ownership of Project IP**

4.1. **Full Delivery Model:** Upon full payment by the client, Nivy Tech assigns all ownership rights to the Foreground IP created for the client. This includes:

- Custom designs, logos, and branding materials.
- Website code, copy, and content developed exclusively for the client.
- Campaign strategies, reports, and analytics specific to the client.

4.2. **Limited License Model:** For fixed-fee or subscription-based projects, Nivy Tech retains ownership of the Foreground IP and grants the client a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive license to use the IP for their business. The license terminates if the client terminates the contract without fulfilling payment obligations.

---

### **5. Background IP Usage**

5.1. Nivy Tech may use its Background IP (e.g., analytics frameworks, campaign templates) across multiple client projects. Clients receive a license to use Background IP incorporated into their projects for as long as they work with Nivy Tech.

5.2. Clients must not reuse, resell, or modify Nivy Tech’s Background IP for purposes outside the agreed project scope.

---

### **6. Third-Party IP**

6.1. Nivy Tech licenses third-party assets (e.g., stock images, fonts, software) for client projects. These assets are governed by their respective license terms.

6.2. Clients are responsible for complying with third-party license restrictions. Nivy Tech will inform clients of any applicable limitations.

---

### **7. Moral Rights**

Nivy Tech waives moral rights to Project IP where legally permissible upon transfer of ownership to the client. Employees and contractors working for Nivy Tech are required to waive their moral rights to IP created during their engagement.

---

### **8. IP Infringement Claims**

8.1. Nivy Tech warrants that all Project IP delivered to clients does not infringe on third-party rights.

8.2. If a third party claims IP infringement against a client based on Project IP provided by Nivy Tech, Nivy Tech will indemnify the client for legal costs and damages, provided the client notifies Nivy Tech promptly and cooperates in the defense.

---

### **9. Employee and Contractor IP**

9.1. All IP created by employees or contractors within the scope of their engagement with Nivy Tech is the property of Nivy Tech.

9.2. Employment and contractor agreements include clauses affirming Nivy Tech’s ownership of IP created during their engagement.

---

### **10. Policy Enforcement**

Violations of this policy by employees or contractors may result in disciplinary action, including termination. Client violations may lead to contract termination and legal action.

---

### **11. Policy Review**

This policy will be reviewed annually by the Director to ensure compliance with applicable laws and business needs.
