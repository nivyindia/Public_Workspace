## Week 3, Day 1-2 — How to Run a Discovery Call

**Before the call:**

1. Look up the client's business on Instagram, Google Maps, their website if they have one
2. Write down 3 things you noticed that could be improved
3. Open a Google Doc and note their name, business, and your observations
4. Have your Calendly confirmation email ready to reference
5. Be ready 5 minutes early

**The 30-minute call structure (follow this exactly):**

Minutes 0-5 — Warm up:

"Great to meet you! Tell me quickly — what does [business name] do and who are your best customers?"

Just listen. Let them talk. Take notes.

Minutes 5-15 — Diagnose their problem:

- "What is your biggest challenge with getting clients right now?"
- "What have you tried before for marketing? What worked, what didn't?"
- "How are you currently getting most of your leads or customers?"
- "If I could solve one marketing problem for you, what would it be?"

Listen more than you talk. Take notes.

Minutes 15-22 — Present your thinking:

"Based on what you've told me, I can see 2-3 specific things we could do to help you get more [leads/customers/revenue]. Here's what I'd recommend..."

Tell them specifically what you'd do for them. Be concrete.

Minutes 22-27 — Handle the obvious questions:

They will ask: "How much does it cost?" — Say: "It depends on the exact scope, but our packages start from 15,000/month. I'll send you a proposal today with 2-3 options tailored to your situation."

They will ask: "How long before I see results?" — Say: "Honestly, most clients see early signs in 30-45 days, with meaningful results by Month 2-3. I'll be transparent with you throughout."

Minutes 27-30 — Close the call:

"I'll send your proposal today. It will have 3 package options — you pick what makes sense for your budget. Is there anything specific you want me to include or address in the proposal?"

**Immediately after the call:**

1. Update their HubSpot contact to "Proposal Sent" stage
2. Open your proposal template
3. Fill in their specific details (personalize pages 2-3)
4. Send it via email within 2 hours
5. WhatsApp them: "Great speaking with you [Name]! I've sent your proposal to [email]. Take a look when you get a chance and let me know if you have any questions."

## Week 3, Day 3-4 — Set Up Free Brevo Email Account

**Why now:** As you start getting leads, you need a way to send follow-up emails professionally. Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) is free for up to 300 emails/day.

**What to do:**

1. Go to [brevo.com](http://brevo.com)
2. Click "Sign up free"
3. Sign up with your business email
4. Complete the setup (select "Marketing Agency" as your business type)
5. Verify your sending email address
6. Create your first email template — the follow-up email:
    - Click "Email" > "Templates" > "Create a template"
    - Name it: "Post-Call Follow Up"
    - Subject line: "Your Nivy Digital proposal + next steps"
    - Body: "Hi {{FirstName}}, It was great speaking with you today. As promised, please find your proposal attached. I've recommended [specific service] based on our conversation about [specific problem they mentioned]. I'm confident we can [specific result you discussed]. The proposal includes 3 options at different investment levels — please choose what makes sense for where you are right now. Happy to jump on a quick 10-minute call to answer any questions before you decide. Just reply to this email or WhatsApp me. Looking forward to working together. [Your name], Nivy Digital [your WhatsApp number]"
7. Save the template

## Week 3, Day 5-7 — Follow-Up System (Manual for Now)

**The most important sales skill is following up. Most agencies lose clients because they follow up once and stop.**

**Your follow-up sequence for every proposal sent:**

Day 0 (proposal sent): Email + WhatsApp

Day 2: WhatsApp message: "Hi [Name], just checking you received the proposal okay? Happy to answer any questions!"

Day 5: Email: "Hi [Name], wanted to follow up on the proposal I sent. Have you had a chance to look at it? If the timing isn't right or you have concerns, happy to discuss."

Day 8: WhatsApp: "Hi [Name], I know you're busy — just wanted to check in one last time. Even if you're not ready to move forward now, I'd love to know if there's anything in the proposal I can clarify."

Day 14: Final email: "Hi [Name], I'll leave the proposal open. Whenever you're ready to grow your marketing, we're here. Feel free to reach out anytime."

**Log every follow-up in HubSpot.** Add a note on each contact with the date and what you sent.

## Week 4 — Closing Your First Client

**When someone says yes:**

1. Reply immediately: "That's great news! Let's get you started. I'll send the agreement and invoice today."
2. Create a simple agreement in Google Docs:
    - Scope of work (exactly what you will deliver each month)
    - Timeline (start date, deliverable dates)
    - Payment terms (monthly in advance, due on 1st of each month)
    - Notice period (30 days written notice to cancel)
    - Confidentiality (you won't share their data)
    - Keep it to 1-2 pages. Simple and clear.
3. Send the invoice:
    - Use Razorpay (free to set up) for Indian clients: [razorpay.com](http://razorpay.com) > sign up > create a payment link
    - Or use Google Pay / NEFT for now if Razorpay setup takes time
    - Invoice should show: your business name, client name, service description, amount, due date
4. Once payment clears, send an onboarding WhatsApp:
    
    "Hi [Name]! Payment received - welcome to Nivy Digital! We're excited to start working on your growth. I'll send you a quick questionnaire today to get everything we need to begin. Your kickoff call will be [date/time]. We're going to do great things together!"
    
5. Send them the onboarding questionnaire via Tally (create a new form with these questions):
    - Login details needed (Facebook Business Manager, Google Analytics, Instagram login)
    - Your target customer (describe them in detail)
    - Your 3 biggest competitors
    - Your best-selling product or service
    - What has worked for you before in marketing?
    - What is your monthly revenue target?
    - Who is our main point of contact?
6. Add them to HubSpot as a Client (create a new pipeline called "Active Clients" and add them)
