## 📅 MONTHLY DELIVERY CYCLE (ALL TIERS)

### WEEK 1 — DOCUMENT COLLECTION

**Day 1–5: Collect from client:**

- Bank statements (all accounts) for the previous month
- Sales invoices issued
- Purchase bills / vendor invoices received
- Salary register (if applicable)
- Any notices received from GST/IT department
- Loan statements (if any)

**Collection Protocol:**

- Send collection request via WhatsApp + email on Day 1 of the month
- Follow up on Day 3 if no response
- Escalate to manager if documents not received by Day 7
- Log document receipt status in delivery tracker: Received / Pending / Partial

---

### WEEK 2 — BOOKKEEPING & RECONCILIATION (Growth+)

**Day 6–10:**

- Enter all transactions into accounting software (Tally / Zoho Books / QuickBooks)
- Reconcile bank statements against ledger entries
- Categorise all expenses by head (Salary / Rent / Marketing / Vendor / Misc)
- Flag any unusual transactions for client clarification
- Update accounts receivable and payable ledgers
- Reconcile GST input credit (ITC) against GSTR-2B

**QC before proceeding:**

- [ ]  Bank balance in books = bank statement closing balance
- [ ]  All invoices entered (sales + purchase)
- [ ]  ITC reconciliation complete
- [ ]  No unreconciled items > ₹500 left unresolved

---

### WEEK 3 — FILING PREPARATION

**Day 11–15 (Starter+):**

- Prepare GSTR-1 data: all outward supplies for the month
- Verify HSN codes and tax rates for all items
- Cross-check GSTR-1 with sales invoices (zero discrepancy)
- Prepare GSTR-3B: net GST payable after ITC
- Get client approval before filing (send summary via WhatsApp)
- File GSTR-1 before 11th, GSTR-3B before 20th
- Download and save acknowledgement in client folder

**Day 11–15 (Scale+):**

- Prepare P&L statement for the month
- Prepare Balance Sheet (updated)
- Prepare cash flow summary
- Flag any item that needs director / owner decision

---

### WEEK 4 — REPORTING & ADVISORY

**Day 20–28:**

**MIS Report (Growth+):** Monthly Management Information System report:

- Revenue this month vs last month vs same month last year
- Top 5 expense heads and trend
- GST paid vs collected (net position)
- Outstanding receivables and payables
- Cash balance
- 1–2 advisory notes: "Based on this month's numbers, we recommend..."

**CFO Advisory (CFO tier only):**

- Tax planning review: estimated advance tax for next quarter
- Identify tax-saving opportunities (Section 80C, 80D, depreciation, etc.)
- Review any notices or assessments in progress
- Strategic recommendation: structure, investment, dividend timing

**Report Delivery:**

- Send MIS report to client by Day 28
- Format: PDF + WhatsApp summary (3 bullet points)
- Log delivery in tracker

## 🚨 ESCALATION RULES

| Situation | Action | Escalate To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GST or IT notice received | Acknowledge to client within 24 hours. Do NOT respond to notice without senior CA review. | CPA Team Lead immediately |
| Client documents missing and Day 7 passed | Stop preparation. Formal written notice to client. Flag to manager. | Manager |
| Bank reconciliation cannot close (difference > ₹10,000) | Do not file. Investigate. If unresolved in 24 hours, escalate. | CPA Team Lead |
| Client requests something outside package scope (e.g. legal opinion, court representation) | Do NOT promise. Flag to manager. Quote separately. | Manager |
| Late filing penalty incurred due to team error | Notify manager immediately. Client must be informed within same day. | Manager immediately |
| Client receives assessment order or scrutiny notice | Escalate to CA immediately. Do not respond without CA sign-off. | CA / Team Lead |
| Tax demand raised by department | Notify client within 24 hours. Do not pay without client approval. | Client + Manager |
