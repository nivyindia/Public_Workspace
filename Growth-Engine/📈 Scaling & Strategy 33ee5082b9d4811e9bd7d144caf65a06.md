## 🎯 Purpose of This Section

This section answers one question: **how do we build a self-running VA machine that generates consistent, scalable revenue without founder involvement in day-to-day operations?**

It contains the strategic architecture behind the VA program — the business model, the growth flywheel, and the roadmap to scaling from 10 to 100+ VAs.

## 📈 Scaling Phases at a Glance

| Phase | VA Count | Key Infrastructure | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 — Launch | 10 to 20 VAs | 1 to 2 supervisors, Google Sheets CRM, WhatsApp reporting, manual leaderboard | Prove the model. Get first clients. |
| Phase 2 — Growth | 20 to 50 VAs | Dedicated QC team, partial automation (n8n/Make), dedicated recruiter, automated leaderboard | Systemise. Remove founder from daily ops. |
| Phase 3 — Scale | 50 to 100+ VAs | Full CRM system, automated lead scraping, engagement tracking, VA self-service reporting portal | Scale revenue. Franchise the model. |
