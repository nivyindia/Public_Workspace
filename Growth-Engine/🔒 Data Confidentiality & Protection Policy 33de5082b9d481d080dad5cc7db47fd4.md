# 🔒 Data Confidentiality & Protection Policy

## Why This Exists

As a VA at Nivy you will handle real client leads, company CRM data, business contacts, and internal SOPs. This data is confidential business property. Misuse, sharing, or leaking any of this data — whether intentional or accidental — has legal and professional consequences.

This policy applies from your first day and remains in effect permanently, even after you leave the program.

---

## What Is Confidential

The following is considered confidential company information:

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Lead data | Names, phone numbers, emails, LinkedIn profiles in the CRM |
| Client data | Names, business details, deal information, conversations |
| Company SOPs | Scripts, outreach templates, training materials, process documents |
| CRM content | Sheet data, status updates, conversation notes |
| Internal communications | Team WhatsApp messages, supervisor instructions, performance reports |
| Incentive/salary details | Pay structures, commission rates, reward amounts |

---

## What You Must NOT Do

- Share lead or client data with any person outside the Nivy team — including family, friends, or other companies
- Download, copy, or export CRM data to personal devices or personal Google accounts
- Screenshot or photograph internal documents, scripts, or reports and share them externally
- Contact any lead or client directly for your own personal business purposes
- Use Nivy's leads, scripts, or brand to run a competing service — during or after the program
- Discuss salary, incentive, or commission details with other VAs or external parties
- Share login credentials for any Nivy tool with anyone — including other VAs

---

## What Happens If You Violate This Policy

| Violation | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Accidental sharing (first instance) | Formal warning + immediate investigation |
| Intentional sharing of client/lead data | Immediate removal from program |
| Using company data for personal business | Immediate removal + potential legal action |
| Exporting or downloading CRM data | Immediate removal |
| Repeat violations of any kind | Removal + reporting to relevant authorities |

> There are no warnings for data violations involving client data or competitor use. These result in immediate removal on the first instance.
> 

---

## Data Handling Rules (Daily Practice)

- Only access CRM data assigned to you by your supervisor
- Log out of all Nivy tools at the end of every shift
- Do not save any lead data in personal spreadsheets, notes, or phone contacts
- If you accidentally see data that was not meant for you, inform your supervisor immediately
- If you suspect a data breach by another VA, report it to your supervisor immediately

---

## Digital Security Rules

- Use a strong password for all Nivy-related accounts (minimum 10 characters, mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
- Never share your passwords with anyone — including your supervisor
- Enable two-factor authentication on LinkedIn and email accounts used for Nivy work
- Do not use public WiFi for accessing the CRM or sending outreach messages
- If your device is lost or stolen, report it to your supervisor immediately

---

## Acknowledgement

By starting work as a VA at Nivy, you confirm that:

1. You have read and understood this policy
2. You agree not to share, export, or misuse any confidential data
3. You understand the consequences of violations
4. You will report any suspected data breach immediately

> This policy is part of your onboarding and is binding from Day 1.
