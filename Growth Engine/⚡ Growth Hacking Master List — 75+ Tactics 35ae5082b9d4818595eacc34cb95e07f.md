# 📖 HOW TO USE THIS LIST {#how-to-use}

1. Review the list at the start of each month
2. Pick **5 tactics** that match your current stage and resources
3. Assign each to a VA or do it yourself
4. Track results in HubSpot (source tagging)
5. Double down on what works; drop what doesn't after 30 days

**Effort key:** 🟢 Low | 🟡 Medium | 🔴 High

**Impact key:** ⭐ Small | ⭐⭐ Medium | ⭐⭐⭐ Large
