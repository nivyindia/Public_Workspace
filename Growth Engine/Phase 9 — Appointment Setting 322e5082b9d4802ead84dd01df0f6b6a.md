# Phase 9 — Appointment Setting

## 

**Goal:** Book a **discovery call** with qualified prospects so you can understand their needs and present your solution. 📅

This step turns **interested leads into actual meetings**.

---

# Step 1 — Ask if They Need Support

After a conversation on LinkedIn or email, confirm whether they need help.

Example message:

> Out of curiosity, are you currently looking for help with **[your service]**, or are things running smoothly right now?
> 

Examples:

- lead generation
- paid ads
- content marketing
- SEO

This question identifies **buying intent**.

---

# Step 2 — Offer a Quick Call

If they show interest, suggest a short meeting.

Example:

> Got it. If you're open to it, we could jump on a quick **15-minute call** and see if there’s an opportunity to improve your current results.
> 

Keep the call:

- Short
- Casual
- Low pressure

---

# Step 3 — Send Scheduling Link

Instead of going back and forth on times, send a booking link using:

- Calendly
- Google Calendar

Example:

> Feel free to grab any time that works for you here:
> 
> 
> (Calendly link)
> 

This lets the prospect **pick a convenient time**.

---

# Step 4 — Confirm the Meeting Time

Once they book the call, send a confirmation message.

Example:

> Perfect — looking forward to speaking on **Thursday at 2 PM**.
> 

This reassures them and reduces **no-shows**.

---

# Step 5 — Send the Meeting Link

Use a video call tool such as:

- Zoom
- Google Meet

Your calendar invite should include:

- Date
- Time
- Meeting link
- Agenda (optional)

Example agenda:

1. Understand your business
2. Review current lead generation
3. Explore possible improvements

---

# Example Appointment Flow

Conversation → Qualification → Meeting

Example message sequence:

**Message 1**

> Are you currently looking to generate more leads for your business?
> 

**Message 2**

> Got it — happy to show a couple strategies that have worked for similar companies.
> 

**Message 3**

> Want to jump on a quick 15-minute call?
> 

**Message 4**

> Here’s my calendar if you want to pick a time: (Calendly link)
> 

---

# Best Practices

✔ Offer **15-minute calls** (lower commitment)

✔ Be flexible with time zones

✔ Send reminders before the call

---

# Reminder System (Reduces No-Shows)

Send reminders:

1 day before

1 hour before

Example:

> Looking forward to our call tomorrow!
> 

---

# Simple Appointment Funnel

Lead → Conversation → Qualification → Call Booked → Discovery Call

Typical results:

- 20 conversations
- 5–8 booked calls
- 1–3 new clients
