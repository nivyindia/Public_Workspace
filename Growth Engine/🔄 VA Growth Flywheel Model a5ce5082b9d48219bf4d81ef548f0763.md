# Virtual Assistant Training + Lead Generation Ecosystem

*(Lean Startup Plan)*

## 1. Core Business Idea

You are building a multi-layer business model that combines:

1. Virtual Assistant Training Program / Institute
2. Lead Generation Workforce
3. Service Agency

Services your team will sell:

- Digital marketing services
- Chartered accountant / accounting services
- AI automation / IT services
- Virtual assistant services
- Online courses
- Other professional services in your ecosystem

The idea is that trainees become your workforce.

---

# 2. The Business Flywheel

The system works in a self-reinforcing loop.

```
Ads / Recruitment
      ↓
VA Students Join Training
      ↓
Students Do Outreach
      ↓
Leads Generated
      ↓
Clients Acquired
      ↓
Projects Executed by Experienced VAs
      ↓
More Experience + Case Studies
      ↓
More Students Join
```

- Real client work experience
- Opportunity to get hired
