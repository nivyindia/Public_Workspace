# SOP-VA-010 — Interested Lead Handling

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who Uses This:** VA Executive
> 

---

## Purpose

To handle a positive lead reply correctly and efficiently — escalating to the booking flow without losing momentum or letting the lead go cold.

---

## What is an "Interested" Lead?

An interested lead is one who has replied with any positive signal:

- "Tell me more"
- "Sounds interesting"
- "How does that work?"
- "What do you do exactly?"
- "This could be relevant to us"
- Any reply that is NOT "not interested" or no reply

> **Speed matters here.** The window between a positive reply and a booked call is short. If you wait 12 hours to respond, the lead goes cold.
> 

---

## Step-by-Step

**Step 1 — Update CRM Immediately (within 30 minutes of seeing the reply)**

- Status → **Interested**
- Last Contact Date → Today
- Notes → Copy the lead's exact reply text

**Step 2 — Notify Manager**

- Send a quick WhatsApp message to Manager: "Interested lead: [Name] from [Company], [Market]. Replied: [1-line summary]."
- This is the only time WhatsApp updates are appropriate.

**Step 3 — Reply to the Lead**

Reply within 2 hours of the original reply. Match the tone they used.

*Template:*

> "Great to hear, [Name]! Happy to walk you through what we do. The easiest way is a quick 15-minute call — no commitment at all. Here’s a link to pick a time that works: [Calendly link]. Does that work for you?"
> 

*Alternative if they seem hesitant:*

> "Of course — happy to share more first. We work with [business type] in [market] to [solve specific pain]. The typical result we see is [outcome]. Want me to send over a quick overview, or would a 15-min call be easier?"
> 

**Step 4 — Follow Up if They Don't Book**

If the lead hasn't booked after 2 days:

> "Hey [Name] — just checking you got my last message. Happy to work around your schedule — even a 10-minute call works!"
> 

If still no booking after 5 days:

> "[Name] — I'll pass this to the team and we can circle back when the timing works better. Feel free to reach out anytime."
> 

Update CRM to **Following Up** and add a follow-up reminder for 14 days.

**Step 5 — Once a Call is Confirmed**

- Update CRM status to **Booked**
- Complete the Call Handover Form (SOP-VA-014)
- Submit handover to closer minimum 2 hours before the call

---

## Output Standard

- CRM updated to Interested within 30 minutes of reply
- Manager notified same day
- Lead replied to within 2 hours
- Calendly link sent in first or second reply
- Call Handover Form submitted minimum 2 hours before call

---

## Common Mistakes

- Not updating CRM status immediately — Interested leads are invisible to Manager until CRM is updated
- Waiting hours before replying — leads go cold fast
- Over-explaining Nivy's services in the reply instead of just booking the call
- Forgetting to submit the Call Handover Form before the call

---

## QC Checklist

- [ ]  CRM updated to Interested within 30 minutes
- [ ]  Manager notified via WhatsApp
- [ ]  Lead replied to within 2 hours
- [ ]  Calendly link included in reply
- [ ]  If call booked — Handover Form submitted min 2 hours before call
