# SOP-VA-013 — Appointment Setting

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who Uses This:** VA Executive
> 

---

## Purpose

To convert an Interested lead into a Booked call — by proposing a time, sending the calendar invite, and confirming attendance.

---

## Pre-requisites

- Lead is marked as **Interested** in CRM
- Calendly account set up with the correct Nivy discovery call link
- Manager notified of the interested lead (per SOP-VA-010)

---

## Step-by-Step

**Step 1 — Send the Booking Invitation**

Reply to the interested lead with one of the following:

*Option A — Calendly Link:*

> "Great to hear, [Name]! The easiest way is a quick 15–20 minute call to understand your situation and see if there’s a fit. Here’s a link to pick a time that works for you: [Calendly Link]"
> 

*Option B — Offer Time Slots (if link feels too automated for the platform):*

> "Happy to jump on a call. A few options that work this week: [Time 1 + timezone], [Time 2 + timezone], or [Time 3 + timezone]. What works for you?"
> 

> **Always include the timezone** (EST, GMT, AEST, GST, etc.) — we operate across 5 time zones.
> 

**Step 2 — Send the Calendar Invite (If Using Calendly)**

1. Calendly sends automatically when the lead books via the link
2. Confirm the booking appears in your assigned Calendly account
3. Verify the date and time match what was agreed

**Step 3 — Send a Confirmation Message**

Once the lead books (or confirms the time):

> "Perfect, [Name]! Confirmed for [Day, Date] at [Time + Timezone]. You'll receive a calendar invite shortly. Looking forward to it."
> 

**Step 4 — Update CRM**

- Status → **Booked**
- Add the call date and time in the Notes field
- Notify Manager in daily report

**Step 5 — Send a Reminder (24 hours before the call)**

> "Hi [Name] — just a quick reminder about our call tomorrow at [Time + Timezone]. Looking forward to it! If anything comes up, feel free to let me know."
> 

**Step 6 — If Lead Does Not Show Up**

- Wait 5 minutes past the scheduled start time
- Send: "Hi [Name] — we’re on for our call now. Let me know if something came up and we can reschedule."
- If no reply within 1 hour: Update CRM status to **Called** (No Show). Flag in daily report.
- Attempt reschedule once. If no reply → Mark as **Closed Lost**.

---

## Output Standard

- Calendly link or time options sent within 2 hours of Interested status
- Confirmation message sent after every booking
- Reminder sent 24 hours before call
- CRM updated to Booked within 30 minutes of confirmation
- No-show handled per protocol above

---

## Common Mistakes

- Not including the timezone in proposed times — this causes missed calls
- Not sending the 24-hour reminder — no-show rates double without reminders
- Not following up on no-shows — some are reschedulable
- Marking as Booked before the lead has confirmed — a Calendly link sent is not a booking

---

## QC Checklist

- [ ]  Calendly link or time options sent within 2 hours of Interested status
- [ ]  Booking confirmed (not just Calendly link sent)
- [ ]  CRM updated to Booked
- [ ]  Confirmation message sent
- [ ]  Reminder sent 24 hours before call
- [ ]  Call Handover Form submitted to closer (SOP-VA-014)
