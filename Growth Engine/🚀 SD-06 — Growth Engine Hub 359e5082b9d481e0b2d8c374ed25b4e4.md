# 🔎 OVERVIEW {#overview}

Most agencies grow linearly — 1 client at a time. The Growth Engine is designed for non-linear, compounding growth. Every client should refer 1 more. Every piece of content should attract 10 more leads. Every partner should open a new market.

## The Three Growth Pillars

| Pillar | Mechanism | Revenue Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Referral Engine | Client refers → earns reward | 30-40% of new revenue (target) |
| Partnership Network | Referral partners, JVs, agencies | 20-30% of new revenue (target) |
| Viral Content | Content that spreads organically | Compounding brand awareness |

---

# ⚙️ GROWTH LEVERS {#levers}

## 1. Referral Program

- **Offer:** 10% of first month's contract value for successful referral
- **Trigger:** n8n automation — deal closed → auto-email referral invitation to client
- **Track:** HubSpot custom property "Referred By"

## 2. Viral Loop Engine

- **Case study sharing:** Every client result → mini case study → client shares on their LinkedIn
- **Testimonial widget:** "Powered by Nivy" (optional) on client deliverables
- **Review request:** Every completed project → auto-request for Google + Clutch review

## 3. Partnership & JV Strategy

- **Target partners:** Web dev agencies, accountants, HR consultants (who serve same clients but don't compete)
- **Structure:** Mutual referral arrangement — they refer clients needing VA/marketing; Nivy refers clients needing their services
- **Target:** 5 active referral partners within 6 months

## 4. Distribution System

- All content syndicated to: Blog → LinkedIn → Twitter → Facebook → Newsletter → YouTube description
- One piece of content = 6+ distribution touchpoints
- n8n automates all syndication

# ⚡ GROWTH HACKING QUICK WINS {#quickwins}

These can be executed immediately with zero budget:

1. Add "Schedule a free call" link to every email signature
2. Comment meaningfully on 5 LinkedIn posts/day in target niche
3. Post client results (anonymized) as LinkedIn carousels weekly
4. Create a free lead magnet (checklist/template) to collect emails
5. Ask every past client for a Google review this week
6. Join 5 Facebook Groups where ideal clients gather — provide value daily
7. Create a Canva infographic from this plan and post to Pinterest
8. Set up a WhatsApp broadcast to all past clients with a monthly value update
