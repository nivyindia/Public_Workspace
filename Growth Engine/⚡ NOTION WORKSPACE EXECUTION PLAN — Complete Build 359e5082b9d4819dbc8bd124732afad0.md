# 📐 RULES & STANDARDS FOR THIS WORKSPACE {#standards}

## Every Page Must Have

1. **Icon** — Emoji that matches the section
2. **Header line** — Owner | Status | Last Updated | Version
3. **Tags line** — backtick-wrapped tags for search
4. **Description callout** — 1-2 sentence purpose statement
5. **Quick Links / Index** — anchor links to all major sections
6. **Content** — actual operational content (not just headers)
7. **Metadata footer** — standardized block at bottom of every page
8. **No orphan pages** — every page is reachable from its parent hub

## Naming Convention

- Department hubs: `🔣 SD-XX — [Name] Hub`
- Sub-pages: `[Topic] — [SOP/Guide/Tracker/Playbook]`
- Databases: `📊 [Name] Database`

## Link Discipline

- Every sub-page links back to its parent hub
- Every hub page lists all its children
- Cross-links between related sections are explicit (e.g., SD-04 Outbound → SD-03 Lead Gen)
