# 📐 RULES & STANDARDS {#standards}

## Naming Convention

- Hub pages: `🔣 SD-XX — [Name] Hub`
- VA Hub: `👥 SD-VA — VA Sales Team Hub`
- Sub-pages: `[Descriptive Title] — [SOP/Guide/Tracker/Playbook]`
- Legacy versions: suffix with `— Legacy` or `— Earlier Draft`
- Old empty containers: prefix with `📦` to signal "emptied out"
- Databases: `📊/📋/📅 [Name] [type]`

## Every Page Must Have

1. **Icon** — Emoji matching the section
2. **One-line header** — Company | Owner | Status | Last Updated
3. **Purpose callout** — 1-2 sentence "what this page does"
4. **Quick Nav** — clickable anchors to major sections
5. **Actual content** — not just headers and placeholders
6. **Back link** — `← Back: [Parent Hub]` at the top
7. **Metadata footer** — Section | Parent | Tags | Related Pages

## Zero Orphan Policy

Every page must be reachable from its hub index. If it's not in an index, it doesn't exist for anyone new joining the team.

## VA Sales Team Hub — What It Must Contain

The SD-VA hub is not an HR page — it is the **command center for the VA outreach team.** It must include:

- Daily/weekly/monthly outreach targets per VA
- 3-month probation milestones and KPIs
- Full QC scoring system (50-point)
- Error log and micro-training framework
- Leaderboard and incentive structure
- Complete operating system SOP
