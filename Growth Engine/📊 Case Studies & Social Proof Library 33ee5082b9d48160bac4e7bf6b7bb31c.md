## Why Case Studies Matter in Outreach

Anybody can say "we’re great at what we do." A case study says it for you without you having to say it. When you drop a specific result into a conversation — a number, a timeline, a before/after — it does two things: it builds instant credibility and it makes the prospect picture themselves getting the same result.

Use case studies:

- In follow-up message #2 or #3 (after the intro message)
- When a prospect asks "what kind of results do you get?"
- In LinkedIn comments to demonstrate authority
- When handling the "I’ve never heard of you" objection

## How to Use These in Messages

Do not paste the full case study. Pick the most relevant one and use the one-line version:

**In a follow-up message (Day 2–4 of sequence):**

> *"One thing I thought might be relevant — [one-line case study]. Happy to share more detail if useful."*
> 

**When asked "what results do you get?":**

> *"Depends on the service and the situation. One example: [one-line case study]. Are you facing something similar?"*
> 

**In a LinkedIn comment:**

> *"This is exactly what we’ve seen — [one-line insight from case study]. Consistent execution always wins."*
> 

> If a prospect asks for a specific case study document, pass to your supervisor. Do not share any client names or detailed documents without approval.
