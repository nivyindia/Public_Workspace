# 🆕 New Joiner Start Here

Welcome to Nivy VA Operations. This page tells you exactly what to do next based on your role.

---

## Who Are You?

Choose your role below to go directly to your starting point.

---

### VA Intern / VA Executive

You are here to do outreach, follow up with leads, and book calls.

Your first step: Go to the VA Portal and open the VA Dashboard.

Day 1 goal: Complete the Day 0 Setup Checklist.

Time to first task: Under 30 minutes.

---

### Supervisor

You are here to oversee VA performance, run QC checks, and manage daily operations.

Your first step: Go to the Supervisor Portal and open the Supervisor Dashboard.

Day 1 goal: Complete Day 0 Setup for Supervisors.

---

### Manager

You are here to run the system, manage performance, and scale the team.

Your first step: Go to the Manager Portal and open the Manager Dashboard.
