# 📊 10. CRM SYSTEM (VERY IMPORTANT)

Update the CRM after **EVERY single action**. A lead with no CRM update is a lost lead.

### Status Flow:

| Status | Meaning | What To Do Next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New | Lead added, not yet contacted | Send connection request or Message 1 |
| Messaged | First message sent | Wait for reply. Follow up on Day 2–3 if no reply |
| Replied | They responded (any response) | Engage, qualify, move toward call |
| Interested | They’ve shown genuine interest | Send calendar link immediately, book call |
| Booked | Call scheduled | Fill handover form, confirm time with lead |
| No Response | 5+ follow-ups, still no reply | Move to re-activation list after 3 weeks |

### 📌 Also track:

- Last message date
- Next follow-up date
- Notes from conversation

> If a lead becomes **Interested**, do not wait. Send the calendar link in the same reply and update CRM immediately.

# 🔐 16. ACCOUNT SAFETY — PLATFORM LIMITS (READ BEFORE SENDING ANYTHING)

Ignoring these limits = account banned on Day 1. Follow them strictly.

| Platform | Connection Requests / Day | Direct Messages / Day | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | 10–15 (new account, Week 1) → 15–20 (from Week 2) | 20–30 | New accounts: warm up slowly. Never send same message twice. |
| WhatsApp | Max 20–30 new contacts | Max 30 | Do not blast same message to multiple people at once |
| Instagram | Max 20–30 DMs | Max 20–30 | New accounts: start with 10/day for first week |
| Facebook | Max 10–15 DMs | Max 15–20 | Do not DM in groups — connect first, then DM |
| Email | Max 30–50 cold emails | — | Always use professional email. Never use Gmail for bulk send. |

### 🚨 Warning Signs Your Account Is Being Restricted:

- "You’ve reached your weekly invitation limit" (LinkedIn)
- Messages not delivering (WhatsApp)
- DM option disappearing (Instagram)

### 🛡️ What To Do If Restricted:

1. Stop sending immediately for 24–48 hours
2. Report to your supervisor
3. Do NOT create a new account without supervisor approval

> **Day 1 Rule:** Stay under 50% of each limit. You are warming up a new account.
