# 📊 SALES DECK / PITCH DECK FORMAT (USA & INTERNATIONAL – TOPICS ONLY)

# 

---

## 1. Cover Slide

- Company name
- Package / Service name
- Tagline (core result)

---

## 2. Attention Hook

- Bold statement / key problem
- Market insight or statistic

---

## 3. About Us (Credibility)

- Who you are
- Experience / expertise
- Industries served

---

## 4. Problem Statement

- Key pain points clients face
- Current gaps in their system

---

## 5. Opportunity / Impact

- What happens if problem is solved
- Business upside (growth, revenue, efficiency)

---

## 6. Our Solution

- High-level explanation of your service
- How it solves the problem

---

## 7. Core Outcome / Transformation

- Clear result client gets
- Before vs After positioning

---

## 8. Service Breakdown

- Key deliverables (simple bullets)
- What is included

---

## 9. How It Works (Process)

- Step-by-step flow (3–5 steps)
- Onboarding → Execution → Results

---

## 10. Proof / Case Studies

- Client results
- Testimonials
- Metrics (if available)

---

## 11. Why Choose Us

- Unique approach
- Differentiation from freelancers/agencies

---

## 12. KPI / Success Metrics

- What will be measured
- How success is tracked

---

## 13. Pricing Overview

- Package pricing (range or tier)
- Value justification (optional)

---

## 14. Risk Reversal / Guarantee

- Confidence-building statement
- Process guarantee

---

## 15. Timeline & Expectations

- Setup timeline
- When results start

---

## 16. Client Responsibilities

- What client needs to provide
- Collaboration expectations

---

## 17. Next Steps

- What happens after agreement
- Onboarding steps

---

## 18. Call to Action (CTA)

- Book call / start now / sign

---

## 19. Q&A Slide

- Open for questions

---

# RULES

- Visual, clean, minimal text
- Focus on story + flow
- 10–15 slides ideal
- Use results, not jargon

---

# PURPOSE

- Support sales calls
- Build trust quickly
- Close high-value clients

---

# END
