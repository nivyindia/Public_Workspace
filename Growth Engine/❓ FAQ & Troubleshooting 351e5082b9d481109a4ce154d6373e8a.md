# ❓ FAQ & Troubleshooting

Answers to the most common questions VAs ask.

---

## General

**Q: Where do I start every day?**

A: VA Dashboard — Start Here. Always.

**Q: What if I don't have any leads?**

A: Tell your Supervisor immediately. Leads are assigned by the team, not generated by you unless told otherwise.

**Q: How do I know if I'm doing well?**

A: Check your Weekly Scorecard. Green = good. If you don't have a scorecard set up yet, ask your Supervisor.

---

## Messages & Outreach

**Q: Which template should I use?**

A: Follow SOP-VA-002 (Message Templates). It tells you which template for which situation.

**Q: How many messages should I send per day?**

A: Check your daily targets, set by your Supervisor.

**Q: What if a lead doesn't reply?**

A: Follow SOP-VA-003 (Follow-Up Protocol). Follow up at day 3 and day 7. Archive if no reply after 7 days.

---

## Packages

**Q: I don't know the packages well. What should I do?**

A: Start with Tier 1 in Package Intelligence Hub. Read the Package Cheat Sheet. That gives you enough for outreach.

**Q: A lead asked about a specific package I haven't heard of.**

A: Look it up in Tier 2. If you can't find it, ask Supervisor.

**Q: Should I give pricing when asked?**

A: No. Always say "I'll connect you with our specialist" and book or hand off.

---

## Escalation

**Q: When do I escalate to my Supervisor?**

A: After you've checked What To Do If... Emergency and still don't have an answer.

**Q: What information should I give when escalating?**

A: Lead name, screenshot of conversation, what you tried, what you need.

---

## System

**Q: A page I need is missing.**

A: Check System Navigation Map. If it's listed but not working, tell Supervisor.

**Q: I think the SOP is wrong or outdated.**

A: Tell Supervisor. Do not edit it yourself.
