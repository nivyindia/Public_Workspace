# Phase 5 — LinkedIn Connection Strategy

Here is a **clear, simple, and practical LinkedIn Connection Strategy** you can use for **lead generation for your agency**.

---

# 

## Daily Target

Send **20–30 connection requests per day per account** on LinkedIn.

Why?

- Safe limit (avoids account restriction)
- Builds **600–900 new connections per month**
- Creates a large audience for outreach

If you use **3 LinkedIn accounts**, you can reach **60–90 prospects per day**.

---

# Step-by-Step Process

## 1. Find the Prospect

Use:

- LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Basic LinkedIn search
- Leads from Apollo.io

Search filters example:

- Industry
- Company size (10–200 employees)
- Location
- Job title (Founder, CEO, Owner)

Example search:

```
Founder
Owner
CEO
Marketing Director
```

---

# 2. Visit the Prospect Profile

Before sending a request:

Check quickly:

- Company
- Role
- Industry
- Recent posts

This helps you personalize your request.

---

# 3. Send a Personalized Connection Request

Avoid generic messages like:

❌ “I'd like to connect with you.”

Use **simple personalized messages**.

Example:

**Connection Message**

> Hi John,
> 
> 
> Saw you're running marketing at Peak Roofing.
> 
> I work with roofing companies to generate more local leads — thought it would be great to connect.
> 

Another simple one:

> Hi Sarah,
> 
> 
> I’ve been connecting with SaaS founders in the space.
> 
> Would love to add you to my network.
> 

Keep it **short (under 200 characters)**.

---

# 4. Track the Request in CRM

Record it in your **Google Sheet CRM**.

Example entry:

| Name | Company | LinkedIn | Outreach Stage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| John Smith | Peak Roofing | linkedin.com/john | Connection Sent |

Status:

- Connection Sent
- Accepted
- Message Sent
- Follow-up

---

# 5. Follow Up After Connection Accepted

When the prospect accepts:

Send a **soft conversation starter**, not a sales pitch.

Example:

> Thanks for connecting John.
> 
> 
> Curious — how are you currently generating leads for Peak Roofing?
> 

Another example:

> Appreciate the connection!
> 
> 
> Are you currently running any paid ads or mostly relying on referrals?
> 

Goal:

Start **conversation**, not selling.

---

# LinkedIn Safety Rules (Important)

To avoid limits on LinkedIn:

- Max **20–30 requests/day**
- Personalize messages
- Do not spam links
- Warm up accounts slowly

---

# Weekly Result Expectation

From **150 connection requests per week**:

- ~30–50 acceptances
- ~10–20 conversations
- ~3–7 discovery calls
