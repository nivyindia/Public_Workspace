# KB-002 — What is Outreach

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

---

## What is Outreach?

Outreach is the process of **initiating contact with a qualified lead** to start a conversation that could lead to a booked discovery call.

Outreach is NOT cold spamming. It is a deliberate, personalised, respectful message to a business owner who matches our ICP — delivered on the right platform, at the right time, in the right tone.

---

## Purpose of Outreach

The only goal of outreach is **to start a conversation and eventually book a call**.

You are not trying to:

- Sell a package in the first message
- Explain everything Nivy does
- Send a wall of text
- Sound like a robot or a template

You are trying to: **Make them curious enough to reply.**

---

## Outreach Platforms

| Platform | Best For | Daily Volume (per VA) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LinkedIn DM** | B2B decision makers, founders, directors | 20–30 connection requests + 10–15 follow-ups |
| **WhatsApp** | Warm leads, referrals, short outreach | 15–20 messages |
| **Email** | Cold B2B, formal markets (US/UK/Canada) | 20–30 emails |
| **Instagram DM** | Creative businesses, personal brands, agencies | 10–15 DMs |

> **Rule:** Always follow the volume limits in the relevant SOP. Over-messaging risks account bans.

## Good Outreach vs Bad Outreach

**✅ Good LinkedIn DM:**

> "Hi Sarah — noticed your agency is expanding into paid ads. Curious how you're handling the back-office side as you scale? We work with agencies like yours on exactly that."
> 

**❌ Bad LinkedIn DM:**

> "Hi, I am from Nivy Digital and we offer accounting, bookkeeping, IT services, digital marketing, web development and automation services. Please let me know if you are interested. We have great packages."
> 

**Why the bad one fails:**

- No personalisation
- Leads with a services list, not a problem
- Sounds like mass spam
- No clear reason for them to reply

**✅ Good Email:**

> Subject: Quick question about your books
> 

> 
> 

> Hi James — I work with consultants in the UAE who've grown past the point where spreadsheets make sense. Is that something you're running into? Happy to share what we've done for similar businesses.
> 

**❌ Bad Email:**

> Subject: We offer the best accounting services
> 

> 
> 

> Dear Sir/Madam, Our company Nivy Digital is a leading provider of accounting and bookkeeping services. We would like to offer you our services. Please find attached our brochure...
>

## Core Outreach Principles

1. **One clear problem, one clear question** — don't list features
2. **Personalise every message** — reference their business, industry, or a recent post
3. **Short messages convert better** — under 5 lines for the first message
4. **Never attach anything in the first message** — it gets ignored or marked as spam
5. **Follow up professionally** — most bookings happen on follow-up 2 or 3, not message 1

---

## Common Mistakes

- Sending the same template to 50 people with no changes — always personalise the opener
- Writing a full pitch in message 1 — less is more
- Not updating CRM after sending — every message must be logged same day
- Messaging over the daily volume limit — this is an account risk
- Giving up after 1 message — the follow-up sequence is mandatory

---

## QC Before Sending Any Outreach

1. Is the opener personalised to this specific person?
2. Is the message under 5 lines?
3. Does it end with a soft question (not a pitch)?
4. Have I checked their platform is correct for this lead?
5. Have I updated the CRM with today's outreach date and status?
