# 📋 LEADERSHIP SOP

> 👉 Needed for scaling team structure
> 

## 👥 Team Structure

- Each Team Leader manages **5–10 VAs**
- You are the first point of contact for your team
- Escalate only what you cannot solve yourself

## ☀️ Daily Check-In Protocol

Run a daily check-in with your team every morning. Cover:

| Item | What to Confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Targets for the day | Each VA knows their lead, message, and follow-up targets |
| Blockers | Anyone stuck on tools, access, or scripts? Solve it now |
| Yesterday's report review | Did everyone submit? Any low numbers to address? |
| Platform focus today | Which platform is the team prioritising? |

**Check-in message template (WhatsApp/Slack):**

> Good morning team 👋 Today's focus: [Platform]. Targets: [Leads] leads / [Messages] messages / [Follow-ups] follow-ups. Reports due by 7:00 PM. Any blockers? Reply here 👇
> 

## 🎯 Coaching Framework

Use this when a VA is underperforming:

1. **Identify** — Which number is low? (Leads, messages, replies, or calls?)
2. **Diagnose** — Skill issue (don't know how) or effort issue (not doing the work)?
3. **Skill issue** → Review scripts together, give feedback on 5 messages
4. **Effort issue** → Direct 1-on-1 conversation about expectations
5. **Follow up** — Check their numbers again the next day

> Rule: Never coach in public. Always address performance issues 1-on-1.
> 

## ⚠️ Escalation Protocol

Only escalate to your supervisor when:

- A VA is unresponsive for 2+ days with no explanation
- Reports appear to have fake or inflated numbers
- A technical or access issue you cannot resolve within 24 hours
- A conduct issue (inappropriate messaging, complaints from leads)

**Escalation message format:**

> VA Name: / Issue: / What you already tried: / What you need:
>

# 📊 STEP 2 — PERFORMANCE MONITORING

## How to Aggregate Your Team's Numbers

Your team submits daily reports to the WhatsApp reporting group. Your job is to consolidate them into one Team Leader report each evening.

**Where to track:** Use the same Google Sheets CRM. Create a separate tab labelled "Team Summary" with columns: VA Name | Leads | Messages | Replies | Calls Booked | Report Submitted (Y/N). Add each VA's daily numbers from their report.

**Alternatively:** Ask your supervisor for the Team Dashboard sheet — some supervisors maintain a shared aggregation sheet for all TLs.

## Track daily:

- Leads
- Messages
- Replies
- Calls booked
