# SOP-VA-001 — LinkedIn Lead Scraping

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who Uses This:** VA Intern, VA Executive
> 

---

## Purpose

To find and extract qualified leads from LinkedIn using Boolean search and advanced filters, then enter them into the CRM with correct formatting.

---

## Pre-requisites

- LinkedIn account set up and profile optimised (per Day 0 onboarding checklist)
- CRM access confirmed
- KB-001 (What is a Lead) read and understood
- Daily lead target assigned by Manager

---

## Step-by-Step

**Step 1 — Set Up Your Search**

1. Go to LinkedIn and click the search bar
2. Select "People" from the filter tabs
3. Click "All Filters"
4. Set the following filters:
    - **Connections:** 2nd (warm connections — better response rate)
    - **Location:** Set to your assigned target market (US / UK / Canada / AU / UAE)
    - **Industry:** Set to your assigned industry target for the day
    - **Current Company:** Leave blank unless targeting a specific company type
    - **Title keywords:** Use one of these patterns: `Founder OR CEO OR Director OR Owner OR "Head of"`

**Step 2 — Run Boolean Search**

Example Boolean strings to use in the search bar:

- `(Founder OR CEO OR Owner) AND (marketing agency OR digital agency)`
- `(Director OR Head) AND (accounting OR finance OR bookkeeping)`
- `(Founder OR CEO) AND (SaaS OR software OR tech startup)`

Do NOT use more than 2–3 keyword groups per search. Overly complex searches return fewer results.

**Step 3 — Review Each Profile (30 seconds max per profile)**

Check:

- [ ]  Is this person a decision maker? (Founder / CEO / Director / Owner)
- [ ]  Is their company a real business (not 1 person, not 1000+ employees)?
- [ ]  Are they in the right market?
- [ ]  Do they pass the hard disqualifiers from KB-001?
- [ ]  Are they already in the CRM? (Search name + company before adding)

**Step 4 — Capture Data**

For each qualified lead, record:

- Full Name
- Job Title
- Company Name
- LinkedIn Profile URL
- Location (City + Country)
- Industry
- Source: "LinkedIn"
- Date Added
- Assigned VA
- Status: "New"
- Notes: Any pain signal from their profile or recent posts (1 line max)

**Step 5 — Enter into CRM**

1. Open CRM sheet
2. Enter each qualified lead into a new row using the exact field format
3. Do NOT enter duplicate rows — search name before adding
4. Submit the batch to Manager at end of day with count: "Added X leads from LinkedIn today"

---

## Output Standard

- Minimum 20 qualified leads per working day (Intern) / 30 (Executive)
- All fields complete — no blank cells in required columns
- Zero duplicates
- All leads pass the 5-point qualification check
- Submitted to CRM same day as scraped

---

## Common Mistakes

- Adding leads just to hit the number without checking qualification
- Not searching CRM for duplicates before adding
- Leaving the Notes column blank — always add a 1-line context note
- Using overly complex Boolean strings that return 0 results
- Adding employees instead of founders / decision makers

---

## QC Checklist (Complete Before Submitting)

- [ ]  All required CRM fields filled for every lead
- [ ]  No duplicate entries
- [ ]  Every lead passes the hard disqualifier check (KB-001)
- [ ]  Notes column has at least 1 line of context per lead
- [ ]  Daily count reported to Manager
