# 🔒 Data Protection & Privacy Policy

**Policy Name:** Nivy Tech Data Protection & Privacy Framework

**Effective Date:** Aug 26, 2025

**Version:** 3.4

**1.1 Purpose**

Nivy Tech is committed to protecting the privacy and security of all personal data we process. This framework formalizes our procedures for complying with global data protection laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). It applies to all personal data processed by Nivy Tech.

**1.2 Scope**

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and sub-processors acting on behalf of Nivy Tech.

---

### **Policy 1: Data Classification & Inventory**

Nivy Tech will maintain a Record of Processing Activities (ROPA). This register documents all personal data processing activities including the data subject categories, data categories, processing purposes, lawful bases, data recipients, international transfers, and retention periods. The Data Protection Officer (DPO) is responsible for maintaining the ROPA. The ROPA is reviewed and updated quarterly.

---

### **Policy 2: Lawful Basis for Processing**

All processing of personal data must have a identified lawful basis prior to processing. The lawful bases used by Nivy Tech are Performance of a Contract, Legitimate Interests, Consent, and Legal Obligation. The specific basis for each processing activity is documented in the ROPA. Where Legitimate Interests is the basis, a Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA) must be completed and filed. Where Consent is required, it must be explicit, unambiguous, and recorded.

---

### **Policy 3: Data Subject Rights Procedure**

Nivy Tech will facilitate data subject requests within one month of receipt. The procedure is as follows:

1. **Receipt:** Requests received via [privacy@nivytech.com](https://mailto:privacy@nivytech.com/) are logged in the Data Subject Request Tracker.
2. **Verification:** The identity of the data subject must be verified before proceeding.
3. **Fulfillment:** The DPO coordinates with the relevant team to action the request (e.g., access, erasure, portability).
4. **Documentation:** All actions taken are documented in the Data Subject Request Tracker.

---

### **Policy 4: Data Protection by Design and by Default**

All new projects, campaigns, and systems must integrate data protection principles from the initial design stage. This includes minimizing data collection, pseudonymizing data where possible, and ensuring transparency. A Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) is mandatory for any project involving high-risk processing, such as systematic profiling or large-scale use of sensitive data. The DPIA must be approved by the DPO before processing begins.

---

### **Policy 5: Data Breach Response Protocol**

1. **Identification & Reporting:** Any employee who identifies a potential breach must immediately notify the DPO via the dedicated internal channel.
2. **Containment:** The DPO and IT team will immediately work to contain the breach.
3. **Assessment:** The DPO will assess the risk to individuals and determine if the breach is notifiable to a supervisory authority or the data subjects.
4. **Notification:** Where required, the supervisory authority will be notified within 72 hours. Affected data subjects will be notified without undue delay if the breach is high-risk.
5. **Documentation & Review:** All breaches, regardless of severity, are documented. A post-incident review is conducted to implement preventive measures.

---

### **Policy 6: Third-Party Vendor & Sub-Processor Management**

Nivy Tech will only engage sub-processors (e.g., cloud providers, analytics platforms) that provide sufficient contractual guarantees to implement appropriate technical and organizational measures. A due diligence questionnaire must be completed for all new vendors. A written Data Processing Addendum (DPA) that meets GDPR Article 28 requirements is mandatory before any data sharing commences. A list of approved sub-processors is maintained by the DPO and provided to clients upon request.

---

### **Policy 7: Data Retention and Erasure Policy**

Personal data will not be kept for longer than necessary for the purposes for which it was collected. Nivy Tech has defined maximum retention periods for all data categories based on the processing purpose. These periods are documented in the ROPA. Upon expiry of the retention period, data is securely and permanently deleted from all systems, including backups, according to the Secure Deletion Procedure.

---

### **Policy 8: Employee Training and Confidentiality**

All employees must complete mandatory data protection training upon hiring and annually thereafter. Training covers the principles of this framework, incident reporting, and individual responsibilities. Employees are bound by confidentiality obligations through their employment contracts. Access to personal data is granted on a strict need-to-know basis and is reviewed regularly.
