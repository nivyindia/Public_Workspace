# 🚪 Offboarding & Exit Process

<aside>
⚠️

This page is for Supervisors and VA Managers only. Follow this process every time a VA leaves the program — whether they resign, are removed for performance, complete their internship, or reach a contract end. No step should be skipped.

</aside>

> **Back to hub:** [👥 People & VA Operations](https://www.notion.so/33ee5082b9d48178b344e6f0afed7cab) → [👔 Supervisor & Management Tools](https://www.notion.so/33ee5082b9d481818219e86da0da14fc)
> 

## Step 1 — Confirm exit type

| Exit type | Definition | Notice required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voluntary resignation | VA chooses to leave | 3 working days notice |
| Performance removal | Below threshold for 5+ days or formal warning threshold reached | Immediate |
| Internship completion | Training period ends, VA not selected | Communicated on final test day |
| Contract end | Fixed-term ends | Per contract terms |

---

## Step 2 — Final report and CRM handover

Before the VA's last day:

- [ ]  VA submits a final daily report covering their last day
- [ ]  Supervisor exports or screenshots the VA's CRM entries for the last 30 days
- [ ]  All leads currently in "Interested" or "Booked" status are reassigned to another VA
- [ ]  CRM filter applied to remove VA's name from active tracking
- [ ]  Any hot leads (status = Interested) personally handed over verbally by the VA

---

## Step 3 — Access revocation

Revoke within 24 hours of exit confirmation:

- [ ]  Remove from Notion workspace (People & VA Operations → Members)
- [ ]  Remove from team WhatsApp group(s)
- [ ]  Remove from Google Sheets / CRM (revoke edit access)
- [ ]  Remove from any shared email aliases or inboxes
- [ ]  Remove from LinkedIn team account (if applicable)
- [ ]  Remove from Apollo or any lead tool accounts
- [ ]  Remove from reporting WhatsApp group

---

## Step 4 — Data security confirmation

- [ ]  Confirm VA has not downloaded CRM data (check Google Sheets version history)
- [ ]  Confirm VA has not exported lead lists from Apollo or similar tools
- [ ]  If any breach is suspected — escalate to management immediately, do not inform the VA

---

## Step 5 — Exit note to team

Send a brief message to the team group after exit is complete:

```
Team update: [VA Name] has left the program as of [Date].
Their leads have been reassigned. If you have any open conversations with leads they were handling, check the CRM and continue the follow-up.
Any questions — message me directly.
```

Do not share the reason for exit publicly.

---

## Step 6 — Performance record

Log the following in the internal VA record (supervisor's copy):

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| VA name | Full name |
| Dates active | Start and end date |
| Final KPI score | Average monthly score across tenure |
| Total calls booked | Cumulative |
| Exit type | Resignation / removal / completion / contract end |
| Re-hire eligible? | Yes / No — with brief reason |

---

## For internship completions — feedback message

Send individually, not in the group:

```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for completing the Nivy VA internship program.

[If selected:] Based on your performance, we'd like to offer you a position as a performing VA. Your supervisor will be in touch with next steps.

[If not selected:] Based on this batch's results, we are not moving forward with an offer at this time. Your overall score was [X]%. The main areas to improve for future opportunities are [1–2 specific things]. We will keep your profile on file and may reach out for future batches.

Wishing you the best.
[Your name]
```
