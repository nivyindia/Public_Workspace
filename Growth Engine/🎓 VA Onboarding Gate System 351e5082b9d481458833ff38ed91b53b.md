# 🎓 VA Onboarding Gate System

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

---

## Purpose

Every new VA goes through this gated 5-day onboarding before touching live leads. Gates are not optional or skippable. A VA who has not passed all gates is not cleared for independent work.

---

## The Golden Rule

> **No VA touches live leads without Day 4 Manager clearance. No exceptions.**
> 

---

## Day 0 — Before Day 1 Starts

**Responsible:** VA + Manager

The Manager sends the Day 0 Setup Checklist. The new VA completes it before their first working day.

- [ ]  LinkedIn profile set up and optimised (professional photo, headline, 500+ character summary)
- [ ]  WhatsApp number confirmed and shared with Manager
- [ ]  Professional email address set up (assigned by Manager)
- [ ]  CRM access confirmed (can open and edit the sheet)
- [ ]  Notion workspace access confirmed (can view all Phase 1 and Phase 2 pages)
- [ ]  Calendly account set up with correct discovery call link
- [ ]  Tool access tested: all platforms load and are functional

Manager sign-off: Day 0 complete → VA cleared to start Day 1.

---

## Day 1 — Understand the System

**Goal:** VA reads and understands all Knowledge Base pages and their role.

**Tasks:**

1. Read KB-001 through KB-006 in order
2. Read the VA Role & Responsibility Matrix
3. Complete the Role Understanding Quiz (5 questions — must score 4/5 or above)

**Role Understanding Quiz — 5 Questions:**

1. What is the difference between a qualified and an unqualified lead?
2. What is the only goal of outreach (first message)?
3. What does a VA do when a lead replies positively?
4. What is the deadline for submitting the daily report?
5. What are the 3 things a VA Intern cannot do (from the Role Matrix)?

VA submits answers in writing to Manager by end of Day 1.

**Gate:** Manager scores the quiz. 4/5 or above = proceed to Day 2. Below 4/5 = redo KB reading and resubmit tomorrow.

---

## Day 2 — Learn the Tasks

**Goal:** VA demonstrates they can find and enter leads correctly.

**Tasks:**

1. Read SOP-VA-001 (LinkedIn Lead Scraping), SOP-VA-004 (Qualification Scoring), SOP-VA-015 (Daily Report)
2. Practice task: Scrape 20 leads from LinkedIn using SOP-VA-001, qualify them using SOP-VA-004, enter them into CRM, and submit the batch
3. Complete QC-001 (Lead Quality Checklist) before submitting

VA submits: CRM batch + completed QC-001 + a brief note on any challenges.

**Gate:** Manager reviews lead quality —

- Pass: All 20 leads are qualified, fields complete, no duplicates → Day 3
- Fail: Feedback given in writing. VA redoes the task with 15 new leads. Resubmit tomorrow.

---

## Day 3 — Practise Outreach

**Goal:** VA writes personalised outreach messages that meet quality standards.

**Tasks:**

1. Read SOP-VA-005 (LinkedIn DM Outreach), KB-002 (What is Outreach)
2. Write 5 personalised LinkedIn connection request notes for 5 provided leads (Manager assigns the leads)
3. Complete QC-002 (Outreach Quality Checklist) for all 5 messages
4. Submit all 5 messages (written, not sent) + completed QC-002

**Gate:** Manager scores each message —

- Pass: At least 4/5 messages meet quality standard (personalised opener, correct length, soft question) → Day 4
- Fail: Written feedback on what was wrong. Rewrite 3 messages and resubmit tomorrow.

---

## Day 4 — Supervised Live Work

**Goal:** VA executes the full day's workflow on live leads under Manager supervision.

**Tasks:**

1. Manager assigns 20 live leads
2. VA executes: qualify → outreach → CRM update → daily report
3. VA completes QC-002 before sending any message
4. VA submits daily report (SOP-VA-015) by 6:00 PM

**Gate:** Manager reviews daily report and CRM quality —

- Pass: Report submitted on time, CRM complete, outreach quality meets standard → Cleared for independent work (Day 5+)
- Fail: Written feedback. One more supervised day before re-assessment.

---

## Day 5+ — Independent Execution

- Full daily targets apply from Day 5
- KPI tracking begins
- Scorecard started
- VA operates independently, escalating to Manager as per KB-005 rules

---

## Onboarding Completion Record

A separate **✅ [VA Name] — Onboarding Completion Record** page must be created for every new VA. It records every gate with date and Manager sign-off. This record is never skipped or backdated.

---

**International add-on:** The gate system eliminates early-stage quality problems. The 4-day investment prevents months of bad output and retraining.
