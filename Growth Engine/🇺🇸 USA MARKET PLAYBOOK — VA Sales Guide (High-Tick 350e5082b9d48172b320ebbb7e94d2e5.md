## 💬 USA Language & Framing Guide

### ✅ DO use:

- "ROI-driven"
- "Guaranteed delivery dates"
- "Dedicated account manager"
- "No lock-in contracts" (where applicable)
- "Results-focused"
- "We report on what matters — revenue and leads, not vanity metrics"

### ❌ AVOID:

- Overpromising specific results ("We will get you to page 1")
- Informal tone in first contact
- Spelling errors (use American English: -ize not -ise, optimization not optimisation)
- Mentioning you're based outside the USA unless asked

## ⚠️ USA Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "We already have an agency" | "That's great — a lot of our best clients came to us while still working with another agency. What results are you seeing currently?" |
| "Your team is offshore" | "We work with clients across the USA full-time. Our team communicates during your business hours and delivers agency-standard work at a fraction of US agency pricing." |
| "Can you guarantee results?" | "We don't guarantee specific rankings or revenue — no ethical agency does. What we do guarantee is delivery on schedule, transparent reporting, and a dedicated team that optimises every month." |
| "Your price seems too low" | "Our pricing reflects our team structure. We're able to offer premium quality at a better rate because of how we've built our delivery model. Our results speak for themselves — want to see a case study?" |

## 📌 Key Rules for USA Sales

1. Always use professional, outcome-focused language
2. Always follow up at least 5 times before marking a lead as dead
3. Never discuss pricing in your first message — qualify first, present on a call
4. Always have a case study or proof point ready
5. Book all USA leads for a closer call — do not attempt to close high-ticket yourself
