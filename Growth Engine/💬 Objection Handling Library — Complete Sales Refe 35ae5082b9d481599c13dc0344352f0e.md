## 💰 PRICE OBJECTIONS

| Objection | Your Response |
| --- | --- |
| "It’s too expensive" | "I understand. Let me show you the ROI. If we save you 20 hours/week at ₹500/hour, that’s ₹40,000/month in time value alone — for a ₹20,000 service. You’re doubling your money, not spending it." |
| "Can you give me a discount?" | "Our pricing reflects the quality of work and the team behind it. What I can do is start you on our Starter package — it’s the most cost-effective entry point, and you can scale up once you see results." |
| "I found someone cheaper on Fiverr" | "Fiverr is a marketplace of individuals. We’re a managed agency — you get a team, quality control, account management, and a guarantee. The risk of a bad Fiverr hire costs more than our service." |
| "No budget right now" | "Completely understand. When does your next budget cycle open? Most clients start small and within 60 days the service pays for itself. Can we schedule a call for [next month]?" |
| "That’s above my budget" | "Let’s find something that works for you. What’s the maximum you can invest monthly? I’ll see if there’s a package that fits." |

## ⌛ TIMING OBJECTIONS

| Objection | Your Response |
| --- | --- |
| "Not the right time" | "I hear that a lot. The businesses that grow fastest are the ones that invest before they’re ready. What would make this the right time?" |
| "Let me think about it" | "Of course. What specifically do you need to think through? I’m happy to answer any questions now so you have everything you need." |
| "I’ll come back to you next quarter" | "Absolutely. I’ll send you our case studies in the meantime. Just know our client spots fill up quickly — shall I note your name for priority when you’re ready?" |
| "We’re too busy right now" | "That’s exactly why our service exists — to take work off your plate so you’re less busy. If anything, this is the best time to start." |

## 🔄 COMPETITION OBJECTIONS

| Objection | Your Response |
| --- | --- |
| "I’m already working with someone" | "Great! What results are you seeing? [Listen]. If you’re happy, I wouldn’t change it. But if there’s a gap, we could fill it or you could compare us side by side." |
| "I’m going with [competitor]" | "Smart to explore options. What’s making you lean their way? [Listen]. Can I show you one thing we do differently before you decide?" |
| "Your competitor is cheaper" | "You’re right, some are cheaper. The question is: what does ‘cheaper’ cost you in results? Our clients consistently outperform those who went with lower-cost alternatives. Let me show you a comparison." |
