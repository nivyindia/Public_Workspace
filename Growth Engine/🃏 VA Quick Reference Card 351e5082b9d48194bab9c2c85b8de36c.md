# 🃏 VA Quick Reference Card

Bookmark this. Refer to it when you forget something. It is one page.

---

## The VA Workflow

1. Lead in → Log it
2. Outreach sent → Log it
3. Reply received → Respond within 2 hours
4. Interest shown → Move to booking flow
5. Call booked → Handoff to closer
6. No reply after 3 days → Follow-up message
7. No reply after 7 days → Archive lead

## Your Daily Non-Negotiables

- Open VA Dashboard every morning
- Complete VA Daily Workflow Checklist every day
- Log every lead, every message, every update
- Submit EOD report before signing off

## The 3 Rules

1. If you don't know — check the SOP before asking
2. If the SOP doesn't cover it — check What To Do If... Emergency
3. If still stuck — message your Supervisor with context
