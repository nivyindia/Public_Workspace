# 📞 THE 30-MINUTE DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT

## ⏱️ Minute 0–2 — Warm Opening

**You say:**

> "Hi [Name], great to connect! Thanks for taking the time. I'm Abhishek from Billion Dreams United — we work as The Nivy. Before I dive into anything, I want to make sure this call is genuinely useful for you, so I'll ask a few questions first. Sound good?"
> 

*Wait for confirmation. Then:*

> "Perfect. So tell me — what made you reach out today? What's the main thing on your mind?"
> 

🔑 **Let them talk. Don't interrupt. Take notes.**

---

## ⏱️ Minute 2–10 — SITUATION Questions

*Goal: Understand their current setup.*

**Script:**

> "To make sure I understand your situation properly — can you tell me a bit about your business? What do you do and who do you serve?"
> 

**Follow-up probes:**

- "How big is your team right now?"
- "What does a typical week look like for you operationally?"
- "Are you currently using any VAs, freelancers, or agencies?"
- "What tools do you use to manage your business? (CRM, project management, etc.)"

📝 **Note their answers in HubSpot after the call.**

---

## ⏱️ Minute 10–18 — PROBLEM Questions

*Goal: Surface their pain. Let them feel heard.*

**Script:**

> "Thank you — that's really helpful context. Now I want to understand what's not working. What's taking up the most of your time right now that you wish you could hand off?"
> 

**Follow-up probes:**

- "If you could eliminate one frustration from your day tomorrow, what would it be?"
- "Have you tried solving this before? What happened?"
- "Is it a bandwidth problem, a skills problem, or a systems problem — or all three?"
- "How long has this been a challenge for you?"

🔑 **Dig until you find the REAL problem. The first answer is rarely the core pain.**

---

## ⏱️ Minute 18–22 — IMPLICATION Questions

*Goal: Help them feel the cost of NOT solving this.*

**Script:**

> "I appreciate you sharing that. Let me ask — if this doesn't change over the next 6 months, what does that look like for you and the business?"
> 

**Follow-up probes:**

- "What opportunities are you missing because of this bottleneck?"
- "What's this costing you in terms of time, revenue, or stress?"
- "Is this affecting your ability to take on more clients or grow?"

🔑 **Don't sell yet. Just let them articulate the cost themselves.**

---

## ⏱️ Minute 22–26 — NEED-PAYOFF Questions

*Goal: Get them to describe the solution in their own words.*

**Script:**

> "Okay — so if we could solve [repeat their core problem] within the next 30 days, what would that mean for you?"
> 

**Follow-up probes:**

- "How would your week look different if this was taken care of?"
- "What would you be able to focus on instead?"
- "If you could quantify it — how much more revenue or time would that unlock?"

🔑 **When they describe the ideal outcome, you have their buying criteria.**

---

## ⏱️ Minute 26–30 — PRESENT & CLOSE

*Goal: Recommend ONE clear solution. Don't overwhelm.*

**Script:**

> "Based on everything you've shared, here's what I think would work best for you..."
> 

**Then:**

1. Name the specific package (e.g., "Our Growth VA package at ₹35,000/month includes...")
2. Connect it directly to their stated problem: "This means [their pain point] is handled completely"
3. State the outcome: "Most clients see [result] within the first 30 days"
4. Mention the guarantee: "We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee — if you're not happy, we make it right"

**Closing question:**

> "Does this feel like the right fit for where you are right now?"
> 

**If YES:**

> "Great! I'll send the proposal to [email] within 24 hours. It'll include all the details, pricing, and a simple agreement. You can review it at your own pace and sign digitally."
> 

**If MAYBE / NEEDS TIME:**

> "Totally understand. What's the main question you need answered before you can move forward?"
> 

→ Answer it → rebook follow-up call with specific date/time

**If NO:**

> "I appreciate your honesty. Can I ask — is it the fit, the timing, or the budget that's the sticking point?"
>

# 🚫 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

| Mistake | Why It Kills the Sale | What To Do Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pitching before asking | Sounds like every other salesperson | Ask 3+ SITUATION questions first |
| Giving 5 pricing options | Creates confusion and paralysis | Recommend ONE package clearly |
| Underselling to close faster | Attracts wrong clients, high churn | Be confident in your pricing |
| Not confirming next step | Lead goes cold | Always end with a clear next action |
| Rushing through the call | Misses the real pain | Pause. Let silence work. |
| Not sending proposal same day | Momentum dies | Send within 24 hours maximum |

# 💬 KEY PHRASES THAT CLOSE

- *"Based on what you've told me, I'd recommend..."* — positions you as advisor, not seller
- *"Most of our clients in your situation start with..."* — social proof + guidance
- *"We have a 30-day satisfaction guarantee — so there's no risk on your end"* — removes fear
- *"The proposal will be in your inbox by tomorrow morning"* — creates momentum
- *"What would need to be true for you to say yes today?"* — surfaces the real objection
