# 6. CRM & Tracking System

## 

### Purpose of CRM & Tracking System

- Organize all leads in one place
- Track every interaction (outreach, replies, follow-ups)
- Ensure no lead is missed or forgotten
- Measure performance and improve results

This system acts as the **control center of your entire operation**.

---

### Google Sheet Structure

Create a sheet named: **Lead Tracker**

**Columns:**

- Company Name
- Contact Name
- Platform (Google, LinkedIn, FB, etc.)
- City
- Phone / Email
- Website
- Date Contacted
- Status
- Follow-up Stage
- Next Follow-up Date
- Call Booked (Yes/No)
- Closed (Yes/No)
- Notes

Optional (Advanced):

- Google Rating
- Number of Reviews

---

### Lead Status Stages

Standard Status Flow:

- Not Contacted
- Contacted
- Replied
- Interested
- Call Booked
- Closed
- Not Interested

Rule:

- Every lead must always have a status
- Status must be updated immediately after any action

---

### Follow-Up Tracking

Each lead must include:

- Follow-up Stage (0, 1, 2, 3)
- Last Contacted Date
- Next Follow-up Date

Example Flow:

- Day 0 → Stage 0 (initial message)
- Day 2 → Stage 1
- Day 4 → Stage 2
- Day 7 → Stage 3

Rule:

- CRM should clearly show **who needs follow-up today**

---

### Daily Reporting Format (For VAs)

Each VA submits at end of day:

- Leads added
- Messages sent
- Follow-ups sent
- Replies received
- Calls booked

Example:

- Leads: 220
- Messages: 70
- Follow-ups: 60
- Replies: 8
- Calls Booked: 2

---

### Performance Tracking Metrics

Track these daily & weekly:

**Activity Metrics:**

- Leads added per day
- Messages sent per day
- Follow-ups sent

**Conversion Metrics:**

- Reply rate (%)
- Interested rate (%)
- Call booking rate (%)
- Close rate (%)

**Business Metrics:**

- Clients closed per month
- Revenue generated

---

### Key Rules (CRITICAL)

- If it’s not in CRM → it doesn’t exist
- Update CRM in real-time (no backlog)
- Keep data clean and consistent
- Review metrics weekly to improve system

---

### Output of This System

- Clear visibility of pipeline
- Better decision-making
- Higher conversion rates through tracking
- Scalable team operations
