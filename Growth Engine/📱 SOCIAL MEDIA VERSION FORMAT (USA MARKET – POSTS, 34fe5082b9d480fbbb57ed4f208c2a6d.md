# 📱 SOCIAL MEDIA VERSION FORMAT (USA MARKET – POSTS, REELS, HOOKS – TOPICS ONLY)

# 

---

## 1. Content Pillars (Core Themes)

- Problem Awareness (pain points)
- Solution / Education (how you solve it)
- Proof / Case Studies
- Authority (expert insights)
- Offer / CTA (conversion posts)

---

## 2. Content Angle Library (CRITICAL)

- Educational (teach something valuable)
- Story-based (client story / founder story)
- Authority / Opinion (strong viewpoints)
- Behind-the-scenes (process, workflow)
- Trend-based (viral/trending formats)

---

## 3. Hook Frameworks (First 3 Seconds)

- Pain-based hook ("Struggling with…?")
- Result-based hook ("Get X result in Y time")
- Curiosity hook ("No one tells you this about…")
- Contrarian hook ("Stop doing this if…")
- Data-driven hook ("90% of businesses fail because…")

---

## 4. Virality Elements (HIGH IMPACT)

- Pattern interrupts (visual/text changes)
- Fast cuts & transitions
- On-screen captions
- Trending sounds/music
- Scroll-stopping visuals

---

## 5. Content Formats

- Short-form videos (Reels / Shorts)
- Carousel posts (educational slides)
- Static posts (quick tips)
- Story content (behind-the-scenes, updates)
- Live sessions / Q&A (optional)

---

## 6. Funnel Mapping (CONTENT → CONVERSION)

- Awareness → Problem & value posts
- Consideration → Proof & authority posts
- Conversion → Offer & CTA posts

---

## 7. Post Structure (Standard Template)

- Hook (attention)
- Context (problem)
- Value (solution/tips)
- Mini CTA (engagement)
- Final CTA (DM / Book call / Link)

---

## 8. Reel / Video Structure

- Hook (0–3 sec)
- Problem statement
- Solution / insight
- Quick example or proof
- CTA (follow / DM / link)

---

## 9. CTA Types (Conversion Layer)

- DM keyword ("DM ‘SEO’ to start")
- Book a call link
- Free audit / lead magnet
- Follow for more

---

## 10. Offer Integration

- Soft sell (value-first posts)
- Hard sell (offer-focused posts)
- Weekly offer push content

---

## 11. Content Calendar Structure

- Weekly themes
- Posting frequency (3–7 posts/week)
- Mix of value + proof + offer

---

## 12. Content Production Workflow (SOP)

- Idea → Script → Design/Edit → Post → Analyze

---

## 13. Branding & Tone (USA Market)

- Clear, direct communication
- Result-focused messaging
- Professional but simple language
- Avoid jargon overload

---

## 14. KPI Tracking (Social Performance)

- Views / Reach
- Engagement rate
- Saves / Shares
- Leads generated (DMs, clicks)

---

## 15. Hashtag & Distribution Strategy

- Niche hashtags
- Broad reach hashtags
- Platform-specific optimization

---

## 16. Repurposing Strategy

- One content → multiple formats
- Video → carousel → post → email

---

## 17. Comments & DM Handling

- Lead qualification replies
- CTA reinforcement
- Conversion scripts (basic)

## 18. Proof Content System

- Before/After results
- Client wins
- Testimonials

## 19. Compliance & Platform Rules

- Avoid misleading claims
- Follow ad/content policies
