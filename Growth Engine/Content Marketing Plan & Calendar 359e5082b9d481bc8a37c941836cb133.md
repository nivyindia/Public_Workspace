# 📊 Content Strategy Overview {#strategy}

Nivy Digital's content exists to do three things:

1. **Build awareness** — Get discovered by founders, SMEs, and coaches who need VA support
2. **Build trust** — Position Nivy as the expert in AI-powered VA services
3. **Generate inbound leads** — Turn content readers into discovery call bookings

**Primary content channels:** LinkedIn (priority), Blog/SEO, Email newsletter

**Secondary channels:** Instagram (visual), YouTube Shorts (repurposed)

# ⚙️ Content Production SOP {#sop}

## LinkedIn Post Workflow

1. Pull topic from content calendar
2. Write draft (150–300 words, hook + value + CTA)
3. Review for tone (conversational, not corporate)
4. Add relevant hashtags (3–5 max)
5. Schedule via Buffer or LinkedIn native scheduler
6. Engage with comments within 1 hour of posting

## Blog Article Workflow

1. Choose SEO keyword (from keyword master list — SD-05)
2. Write outline (H1, H2s, intro, sections, CTA)
3. Write full draft (800–1500 words)
4. Add internal links to relevant Notion/website pages
5. Publish on website CMS
6. Repurpose key points into LinkedIn post same week

## Email Newsletter Workflow

1. Compile 3–5 curated insights or tips
2. Write intro (2–3 sentences, personal tone)
3. Add 1 featured blog article
4. Add 1 soft CTA (discovery call or resource)
5. Send via email platform (Mailchimp / Brevo)
6. Track: open rate, click rate, unsubscribes
