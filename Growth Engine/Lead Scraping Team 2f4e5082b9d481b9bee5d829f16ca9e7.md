## 2️⃣ WHAT THIS TEAM DOES / DOES NOT DO

### ✅ THEY DO

- Find companies

### ❌ THEY DO NOT

## 4️⃣ STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION (MANDATORY)

### 🔹 TASK 1: Identify Target Companies

**Filters (DO NOT CHANGE)**

- Geography: USA / Europe
- Company size: 5–500 employees
- Status: Active business
- Industry: As shared by founder

**Sources**

- LinkedIn
- Google
- Business directories
- Apollo / Similar tools

---

### 🔹 TASK 2: Identify Decision Makers

**Accepted Titles ONLY**

- Founder
- Co-Founder
- CEO
- Managing Director
- Operations Head
- Finance Head (if service related)

❌ Reject:

- Interns
- Assistants
- Generic “info@” emails

---

### 🔹 TASK 3: Collect Contact Details

**Required Fields**

- Full Name
- Job Title
- Company Name
- Company Website
- Country
- Business Email OR LinkedIn URL

Optional (bonus):

- Phone number
- Company LinkedIn page

---

### 🔹 TASK 4: Verify Data (CRITICAL)

Every contact MUST be:

- Valid email OR active LinkedIn
- Correct job title
- Currently employed

❌ No guessed emails

❌ No outdated profiles

❌ No bulk junk

---

### 🔹 TASK 5: Format & Upload

**Google Sheet Columns (EXACT ORDER)**

1. First Name
2. Last Name
3. Job Title
4. Company Name
5. Company Website
6. Country
7. Business Email
8. LinkedIn Profile URL
9. Source (LinkedIn / Website / Tool)

---

### 🔹 TASK 6: Self Quality Check (MANDATORY)

Before submission:

- Randomly check 10 leads
- Ensure no duplicates
- Ensure all required fields filled
