# 🖼️ Visual Identity — Photo, Banner & Headline

This page covers everything that makes a prospect stop and look at your profile. Your photo, banner, and headline work together as one visual unit — they appear before anyone reads a single word of your About section.

---

## Why this matters

On LinkedIn, a prospect sees your photo and headline before they open your profile. If these don't communicate credibility and relevance within 2 seconds, they won't read further. Think of your photo as your handshake, your banner as your business card, and your headline as your elevator pitch.

---

## 1. Profile Photo

Your photo is the first trust signal. Profiles with a professional headshot receive significantly higher connection acceptance rates than those without.

**Requirements:**

- Clean, professional headshot — head and shoulders visible, face centred
- Expression: friendly, approachable, confident — a slight smile works best
- Attire: business casual minimum (collared shirt, blazer) — no casual t-shirts or hoodies
- Background: plain white, light grey, or subtle office setting — no clutter, no busy patterns
- Lighting: face the light source so your face is well-lit and clear — avoid backlit or shadowy photos
- Resolution: minimum 400×400px, no pixelation
- Format: square crop — LinkedIn displays photos as circles

**Avoid:**

- Selfies or casual phone photos
- Group photos or cropped group photos
- Sunglasses, hats, or heavy filters
- Photos from events or parties
- Low-quality or blurry images

**Pro tip:** Use Canva or [Remove.bg](http://Remove.bg) to clean or replace the background. A consistent style across all BDE profiles strengthens Nivy's brand identity.

## 2. Banner / Cover Image

Your banner should answer three questions at a glance: who you help, what you do, and what brand you represent. It sits at the top of your profile and is visible to every visitor.

**Banner dimensions:** 1584 × 396px

**What to include:**

- Nivy logo or brand name
- Core service categories (accounting, automation, lead generation, etc.)
- Target markets (US, UK, CA, AU, UAE)
- A short tagline or value statement

**Approved banner options — choose one:**

**Option A — Core Services Focus** *(recommended for most BDEs)*

Nivy | Helping Service Businesses Scale Globally

Accounting • CPA • Automation • Lead Generation

US | UK | CA | AU

**Option B — Industry Focused** *(use if your outreach targets roofing/HVAC/home services)*

Roofing • HVAC • Agencies • Consultants

Accounting, Bookkeeping & CPA Services | Automation & Lead Generation

Nivy Ecosystem

**Option C — Outcome-Oriented** *(use if your outreach is to founders and SMEs)*

Grow Clients, Streamline Operations, Automate Business

Accounting & CPA | Tech & Marketing

Nivy Ecosystem

**Design rules:**

- Use Nivy brand colours
- Keep text minimal and readable — no more than 3 lines of text
- Add relevant icons (finance, automation, digital growth) to communicate at a glance
- Create using Canva — ask your supervisor for the Nivy brand kit template

## 3. Headline

Your headline is the single most important SEO element on your profile. It determines whether you appear in LinkedIn search results and whether prospects click to view your profile.

**Rules:**

- Maximum 220 characters
- Include: your role, core services, target industries, and Nivy brand name
- Use pipe symbols (|) or bullet points (•) to separate elements for readability
- Place the highest-value keywords first

**Approved headline templates — choose one:**

**Option A — Services + Industries** *(broadest reach)*

Business Development Executive | Accounting Outsourcing | Bookkeeping | CPA Services | Automation | Roofing & HVAC | Agencies | Nivy Ecosystem

**Option B — Outcomes Focused** *(higher conversion for warm outreach)*

Helping Service Businesses Scale | Accounting, CPA & Automation | Lead Generation | Nivy Ecosystem

**Option C — Authority + Services** *(good for senior profiles)*

BDE at Nivy | CPA Services, Bookkeeping, Business Automation | Agencies, Coaches, Home Services

**Option D — Keyword Heavy for SEO** *(best for inbound discovery)*

Accounting Outsourcing • CPA Services • Bookkeeping • Automation • Lead Generation • Roofing • HVAC • Agencies | Nivy Ecosystem

> Do not write a generic headline like "Student" or "Looking for opportunities" — this immediately disqualifies you in the eyes of a prospect.

## 4. Custom URL

A custom URL looks cleaner when you share your profile in outreach messages and makes you easier to find.

**How to set it:**

1. Go to your LinkedIn profile
2. Click "Edit public profile & URL" (top right)
3. Under "Edit your custom URL", click the pencil icon
4. Set it to: `linkedin.com/in/yourfirstname-nivy`

**Example:** `linkedin.com/in/rahul-nivy` or `linkedin.com/in/priya-nivy`

Keep it simple, professional, and include the Nivy brand name.

## 5. Location Setting

Setting your location to your primary outreach market significantly improves connection acceptance rates. Prospects are more likely to accept from someone who appears to be in their region.

**How to set it:**

- Go to your profile → Edit intro → Location
- Type the city or region manually

**Location targets by market:**

| Target Market | Set Location To |
| --- | --- |
| United States | Austin, Texas, United States |
| United Kingdom | London, England, United Kingdom |
| Canada | Toronto, Ontario, Canada |
| Australia | Sydney, New South Wales, Australia |
| UAE | Dubai, United Arab Emirates |

> If targeting multiple markets, use your highest-priority market and mention others in your About section. Ask your supervisor which market you are assigned to before setting your location.
