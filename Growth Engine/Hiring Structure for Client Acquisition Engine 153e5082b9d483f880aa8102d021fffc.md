# Hiring Structure for Client Acquisition Engine

![image.png](image%207.png)

This structure is designed for a **freelancer + student + expert mentor model** so the company can scale quickly with low fixed costs.

Levels:

- **L1 – Entry Level:** Students, freshers, housewives (basic execution work)
- **L2 – Execution Level:** Skilled freelancers doing the work
- **L3 – Specialist / Trainer:** Experienced professionals guiding juniors
- **L4 – Manager / Head:** Department leaders

# Hiring Strategy for the Model

**Level 1 – Students / Freshers**

- Incentives
- Commission
- Training

**Level 2 – Freelancers**

- Project based pay
- Revenue share

**Level 3 – Experts**

- Higher revenue share
- Leadership roles

**Level 4 – Managers**

- Salary + revenue bonus

---

# Recommended Starting Teams (Lean Setup)

To reach the **first 100 clients**, start with these core teams:

1. Lead Generation
2. Outbound Sales
3. Freelance Platform Acquisition
4. Community Growth
5. Client Success

Expand the remaining departments as revenue grows.
