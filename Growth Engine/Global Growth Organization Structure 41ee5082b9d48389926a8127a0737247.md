This document outlines the full organizational structure required to operate a global client acquisition, marketing, sales, and growth ecosystem. It provides a clear hierarchy of departments, sub‑departments, roles, and responsibilities so leadership can scale operations smoothly.

---

# 1. EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP

Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

- Overall company vision
- Strategic growth direction
- Partnerships and ecosystem building

Chief Operating Officer (COO)

- Daily operations
- Process optimization
- Team coordination

Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)

- Marketing strategy
- Demand generation
- Brand growth

Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)

- Sales systems
- Revenue strategy
- Sales team leadership

Chief Community Officer (CCO)

- Community ecosystems
- Youth networks
- Ambassador programs
