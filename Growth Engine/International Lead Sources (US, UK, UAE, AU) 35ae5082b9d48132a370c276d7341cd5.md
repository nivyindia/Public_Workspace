# 🇺🇸 United States

## Best Lead Sources

| Source | What You Find | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) | Email + phone of SME decision-makers | $49–$99/mo |
| Crunchbase | Funded startups, tech companies | Free + paid |
| LinkedIn Sales Navigator | US founders and directors | $80/mo |
| Product Hunt | SaaS/tech companies recently launched | Free |
| G2 / Capterra | Software companies (review sites = company lists) | Free |
| IRS EIN database | Registered US businesses | Free (public) |

## Target Job Titles (US)

CEO, Founder, COO, VP Finance, Controller, Director of Marketing

## Best Outreach Channel

Cold email (Apollo sequences) + LinkedIn DM

## Hook That Works

> "We handle bookkeeping and tax for US-based SMEs — remotely, at 50% of what a local CPA charges. Open to a quick call?"
>

# 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

## Best Lead Sources

| Source | What You Find | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Companies House | Every registered UK company | Free |
| LinkedIn | UK founders, directors, FDs | Free + Sales Nav |
| [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) | UK SME email lists | $49/mo |
| [Clutch.co](http://Clutch.co) | UK agencies and service businesses | Free |
| Federation of Small Businesses (FSB) | UK SME community | Free to search |
| AccountingWeb | UK accountants and firms | Free |

## Target Job Titles (UK)

Managing Director, Director, Finance Director, Founder, Owner

## Best Outreach Channel

LinkedIn (primary) + cold email (secondary)

## Hook That Works

> "We support UK SMEs with bookkeeping, VAT returns, and Making Tax Digital — fully remotely. Happy to share what we do?"

# 🇦🇺 Australia

## Best Lead Sources

| Source | What You Find | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ABR (Australian Business Register) | All registered AU businesses | Free |
| LinkedIn Australia | Founders, directors, FDs | Free + Sales Nav |
| [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) (AU filter) | AU SME email lists | $49/mo |
| TrueLocal / Yellow Pages AU | Local business directory | Free |
| ASIC Connect | AU company register | Free |

## Target Job Titles (AU)

Director, Sole Trader/Owner, CFO, Operations Manager, Founder

## Best Outreach Channel

Cold email (Australians are email-responsive) + LinkedIn

## Hook That Works

> "We handle BAS, GST, and payroll compliance for Australian SMEs — all remote, Xero-ready. Open to a 15-min intro call?"
