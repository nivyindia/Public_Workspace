# 👤 ICP #1 — The Overwhelmed Founder {#icp1}

## Profile

- **Role:** Founder, Co-founder, Solopreneur
- **Company Size:** 1–5 employees
- **Revenue:** $5K–$50K/month
- **Location:** US, UK, Australia, India (metros)
- **Industry:** SaaS, consulting, e-commerce, info products, services

## Demographics

- Age: 28–45
- Tech-savvy, uses Notion, Slack, HubSpot, Calendly
- Active on LinkedIn, sometimes Twitter/X
- Listens to: My First Million, Diary of a CEO, How I Built This

## Psychographics

- Driven, ambitious, growth-obsessed
- Frustrated that admin is eating their creative/CEO time
- Has tried hiring freelancers — inconsistent, unreliable
- Wants systems, not just task-doers
- Values speed, reliability, and communication above all

## Top Pain Points

1. Spending 4+ hours/day on email, scheduling, admin
2. No consistent outreach or lead generation system
3. Social media and content always deprioritised
4. Missed follow-ups losing them deals
5. Can't afford a full-time hire yet

## Goals

- Free up 20+ hours per week
- Have a consistent lead pipeline running in the background
- Look professional and credible online
- Close more deals with less personal effort

## Buying Triggers

- Hit a growth ceiling and realised they need support
- Just raised funding / got a big client
- Burned out from doing everything themselves
- Referred by a peer who uses a VA service

## Where to Find Them

- LinkedIn (search: Founder, CEO, solopreneur + industry)
- Twitter/X communities
- Facebook Groups (founders, entrepreneurs)
- Product Hunt, Indie Hackers
- Podcasts / newsletters they follow

## Messaging That Works

> *"You started this business to do the work you love — not to spend your day on emails and admin. We handle the operations. You handle the vision."*

# 🎓 ICP #2 — The Growth-Stage Coach / Consultant {#icp2}

## Profile

- **Role:** Business coach, life coach, marketing consultant, fractional CMO/CFO
- **Company Size:** 1–3 (often solo with 1 assistant)
- **Revenue:** $5K–$30K/month
- **Location:** US, UK, Australia primarily

## Top Pain Points

1. No time to post content consistently
2. Outreach to new prospects is sporadic
3. CRM is disorganised — leads falling through cracks
4. Proposal and follow-up process is manual

## Goals

- Grow from 5 to 20 clients
- Have a content engine running on autopilot
- Build a credible LinkedIn presence
- Fill their calendar without doing the prospecting themselves

## Messaging That Works

> *"We handle your LinkedIn outreach, content calendar, and CRM — so you can focus on coaching, not chasing."*
>

# 🏢 ICP #3 — The SME Owner (5–50 staff) {#icp3}

## Profile

- **Role:** Managing Director, Business Owner, Operations Head
- **Company Size:** 10–50 staff
- **Revenue:** $100K–$2M/year
- **Location:** India (metros), UAE, UK
- **Industry:** Real estate, recruitment, education, professional services, retail

## Top Pain Points

1. Marketing team is thin or non-existent
2. No structured outreach or lead generation process
3. Website and social media not converting
4. No CRM — contacts managed in spreadsheets

## Goals

- Build a professional marketing operation without hiring a full team
- Consistent lead flow from multiple channels
- CRM set up and maintained
- Monthly reporting on marketing performance

## Messaging That Works

> *"We give growing businesses a full marketing operations team — at a fraction of the cost of hiring in-house."*
>

# 🏭 ICP #4 — The Digital Agency (White-Label) {#icp4}

## Profile

- **Role:** Agency Founder, Operations Director
- **Company Size:** 5–30
- **Revenue:** $500K–$5M/year
- **Location:** US, UK, Australia
- **Services They Offer:** SEO, PPC, web design, social media, PR

## Top Pain Points

1. Capacity constrained — can't hire fast enough
2. Delivery quality inconsistent across freelancers
3. Need white-label VA support they can trust
4. Margins being squeezed — need cost-efficient delivery

## Goals

- Add VA capacity without headcount risk
- Reliable, trained team they can white-label
- Structured SLA and reporting

## Messaging That Works

> *"Need white-label VA capacity your clients will never know isn't in-house? We're your delivery team."*
>

# ❌ Negative ICP — Who We Do NOT Serve {#negative}

| Type | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Pure price shoppers (want VA for $3/hr) | Not our market — wrong value alignment |
| One-time task only (no retainer intent) | Not scalable for us |
| Highly regulated industries (legal, medical records) | Compliance risk without specialist setup |
| Clients who won't onboard properly | Systems-first model requires their participation |
| Companies with no digital presence at all | Needs too much foundational work before VA is useful |
