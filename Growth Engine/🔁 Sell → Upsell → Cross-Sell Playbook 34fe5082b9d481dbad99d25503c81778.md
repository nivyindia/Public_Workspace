# 🔁 Sell → Upsell → Cross-Sell Playbook

> **This page teaches you the money-making logic.** Getting a client to say yes once is good. Getting them to stay and buy more is how Nivy scales. Every VA must understand this flow.
> 

---

## 🧠 THE CORE PRINCIPLE

> Start them small. Deliver results. Expand the relationship. Every client you bring in at $300/month has the potential to become a $3,000/month account.
> 

**The flow is always:**

`ENTRY (Level 1)` → `RETAINER (Level 2)` → `SYSTEM (Level 3)` → `ENTERPRISE (Level 4)`

Your job as a VA: **get them to Level 1 or Level 2.** The closer and account manager handle the upgrade from there. But you need to know the path so you can set it up correctly.

---

## 🟢 STEP 1 — THE ENTRY SELL (Level 1)

### What you're doing:

Selling one low-risk, fixed-price service to get the client's first experience with Nivy.

### Rules for the entry sell:

- Always lead with the simplest, clearest offer
- Never pitch more than 2 options at once
- Always connect the service to a pain they mentioned
- Price objection? Offer the lowest-tier Level 1 option

### Best entry packages by client type:

| Client Type | Best Entry Package | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business with no website | Basic Website Starter | Immediate need, obvious value |
| Business with no social presence | Social Media Starter | Easy to visualise |
| Business wanting leads fast | Basic Ads Setup | Fast results |
| Business wanting to sell online | Shopify / WooCommerce Setup | Clear goal |
| Business with no professional email | Business Email Setup | Quick win, cheap |
| Business with a website but no leads | SEO Starter | Easy to explain ROI |
| Business needing design work | Graphic Design Starter | Low commitment |

### Entry sell script:

> *"Based on what you've described, the best place to start would be [PACKAGE]. It's [PRICE], takes [TIMELINE], and solves [PROBLEM]. Most of our clients start here before moving to our monthly growth services. Want me to get you the details?"*
>

## 🔵 STEP 2 — THE UPSELL (Level 1 → Level 2)

### When to upsell:

- After the Level 1 delivery is done (or nearly done)
- When client expresses satisfaction or asks "what's next?"
- When you see an obvious gap (they have a website but no SEO, for example)

### The upsell is always: one-time → monthly retainer

### Level 1 → Level 2 Natural Upsell Map:

| Level 1 (What They Have) | Natural Level 2 Upsell | Script Bridge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Website | SEO Growth Starter | *"Now that your site is live, let's get it ranking on Google"* |
| Business Website | SEO Growth + Social Media Growth | *"Let's drive traffic to the website we just built"* |
| Shopify Setup | E-commerce Growth | *"Now let's run ads and drive sales to your new store"* |
| Basic Ads Setup | Ads Management Growth | *"Let's have our team manage and optimise those ads monthly"* |
| Social Media Starter | Social Media Growth | *"Let's level up to a full content strategy with better results"* |
| SEO Starter | SEO Growth | *"Let's scale the keywords and add content marketing"* |
| Business Email Setup | Email Marketing Growth | *"Now let's use that email to run campaigns and automations"* |
| Landing Page Setup | Funnel & Conversion Growth | *"Let's run traffic to that landing page and optimise the funnel"* |
| Video Editing Starter | Video Growth | *"Let's scale to 20–40 videos/month and build a real content engine"* |

### Upsell script:

> *"Now that [LEVEL 1 RESULT] is done, the logical next step is [LEVEL 2 SERVICE]. This is what our clients use to keep results growing every month. The Starter tier is [PRICE]/month. Want me to book a quick call so the team can walk you through what that looks like?"*
>

## 📦 STEP 3 — THE CROSS-SELL (Multiple Level 2 Services)

### What is a cross-sell?

Adding a second or third service to a client who's already on one Level 2 retainer.

### The golden cross-sell combinations:

| Primary Service (What They Have) | Cross-Sell 1 | Cross-Sell 2 | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SEO Growth | Social Media Growth | Copywriting Growth | SEO needs content. Social amplifies content. Copy makes both convert. |
| Social Media Growth | Video Growth | Graphic Design | Video + graphics = complete content system |
| Ads Management | Funnel & Conversion | Copywriting | Ads bring traffic. Funnel captures it. Copy converts it. |
| Email Marketing | Copywriting | Funnel Growth | Email needs copy. Funnels need email. |
| E-commerce Growth | Google Ads | Email Marketing | Full ecom growth stack |
| Local SEO | Google Ads | Reputation Management | Local businesses need reviews + rankings + paid |
| Outreach System | Copywriting | CRM / Sales Enablement | Outreach needs copy + tracking |

### Cross-sell script:

> *"Since you're already doing [SERVICE A] with us, a lot of clients at your stage also add [SERVICE B] because [REASON]. It's [PRICE]/month and it directly supports what we're already doing for you. Want me to show you what that would look like combined?"*
>

## 🟣 STEP 4 — THE SYSTEM UPGRADE (Level 2 → Level 3)

### When to recommend a Level 3 system:

- Client is already on 2+ Level 2 services
- Client wants faster or more integrated results
- Client's budget has grown
- Client says: "I want everything working together"

### Level 2 combinations that signal Level 3 readiness:

| What They Have | Level 3 Upgrade | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEO + Social + Ads | Growth Engine System | It's the same services, integrated with strategy |
| Ads + Funnel + Email | Lead Generation System | Full lead gen engine |
| E-com Growth + Ads | E-commerce System | Complete ecom scaling |
| Outreach + CRM | Outreach Engine System | Full outbound machine |
| Social + Video + Copy | Authority System | Content-based authority building |

### System upgrade script:

> *"You're currently doing [SERVICE A] and [SERVICE B] with us. At this point, it actually makes more sense to move to our [LEVEL 3 SYSTEM], which combines everything into one integrated strategy with a dedicated team. The results are faster because everything is connected. Let me book you a call with our strategy team to walk through what that looks like."*
>

## 🇺🇸 USA vs INTERNATIONAL — Sell Differently

### USA Clients:

- Lead with **ROI and results**, not features
- Use **dollar amounts and percentages**: *"Most clients save $2,000/month"*, *"We generate 3–8 qualified leads per week"*
- They expect **professional process** — mention SLAs, account managers, reporting
- **High-ticket is normal** — don't undersell. USA businesses pay $2,000–$5,000/month for marketing
- Level 3 and Level 4 sell well here
- Best entry: Business Website, Ads Setup, Outreach System

### International Clients (UK, AU, CA, India, Middle East):

- Lead with **value for money** and **what competitors charge locally**
- Show **INR or local pricing comparison** when relevant
- They are more price-conscious at first — start with Level 1
- Build trust before pitching Level 3
- Best entry: Basic Website, Social Media Starter, SEO Starter
