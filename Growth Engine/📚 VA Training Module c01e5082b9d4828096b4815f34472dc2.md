# 🧪 TESTING & SELECTION SYSTEM

| Stage | Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| Mid-Test | 50 leads, 20 requests, 10 emails |
| Final Test | 100 leads, 30–40 requests, 20–30 messages |
| Selection | Top 20–30% |

## Selection Rubric — How "Top" Is Defined

Selection is not subjective. VAs are scored on 5 measurable criteria:

| Criteria | Weight | What Is Measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead Accuracy | 30% | % of leads that pass the ICP check (correct industry, role, country) |
| Message Quality | 25% | Script compliance + personalization (no copy-paste violations) |
| CRM Accuracy | 20% | % of leads correctly entered with all fields filled and status updated |
| KPI Completion | 15% | % of daily targets hit across the test period |
| Attendance & Reporting | 10% | % of daily reports submitted on time during the test period |

**Scoring:**

| Total Score | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| 85–100% | Fast-tracked to live deployment + eligible for incentives |
| 70–84% | Deployed with 2-day coaching on weakest areas |
| 55–69% | 3-day re-training on specific modules, then re-tested |
| Below 55% | Exited from program with feedback |

> "Top 20–30%" means VAs with overall scores of 85%+, prioritised by Lead Accuracy and Message Quality — since those two criteria directly affect revenue generation.
