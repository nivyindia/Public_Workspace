## 1. Profile Photo – How to Make It Professional

A strong LinkedIn profile photo builds trust and increases connection acceptance. Guidelines:

- **Expression:** Friendly, approachable, confident. Smile slightly.
- **Attire:** Business casual (blazer or formal shirt) for professional appeal. Avoid casual t-shirts.
- **Background:** Plain or subtle office setting. Avoid clutter or busy backgrounds.
- **Lighting:** Natural light is best. Face the light source for clear, well-lit face.
- **Framing:** Head and shoulders visible, face centered. Avoid extreme zoom or too far away.
- **Resolution:** High-quality (no pixelation), minimum 400x400px.
- **Consistency:** If multiple BDEs, maintain similar style for brand identity-.
- 

**Pro Tip:** Use tools like **Canva** or **Remove.bg** to clean or blur the background if needed.
