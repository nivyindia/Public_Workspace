# 📊 Phase 5 — Performance Management

This folder contains the full Performance Management System for the Nivy VA team.

- **📊 VA Weekly Scorecard (Template)** — per-VA weekly performance tracking
- **📈 VA Team Performance Dashboard** — Manager’s weekly review system
- **🏆 VA Progression Criteria** — promotion standards, incentive structure, recognition system

> **Rule:** Every VA has a live scorecard from Day 5 of onboarding. Every Monday by 10:00 AM the Manager reviews all scorecards. No VA is surprised by their performance status.
