## 🎯 ROLE DEFINITION

### What a Closer Does

- Receives warm leads from VAs (post-booking)
- Runs structured discovery + pitch calls
- Converts booked calls into signed clients
- Logs every call outcome in the tracker
- Hands off signed clients to the delivery team

### What a Closer Does NOT Do

- Cold outreach (that's the VA's job)
- Delivery or account management
- Negotiate pricing outside the approved range
- Promise features not in the package

## 🔁 THE CLOSER'S DAILY FLOW

1. **Morning** — Check call schedule for the day. Review each lead's handover notes from VA.
2. **Pre-call (15 min before)** — Open Script Library + Package Quick-Reference. Review lead details.
3. **During call** — Follow Call Protocol SOP. Use scripts as decision trees.
4. **Post-call (within 1 hour)** — Log outcome in Performance Tracker. Send follow-up if not closed.
5. **End of day** — Review close rate. Flag any lead needing manager attention.

---

## 🚨 ESCALATION — WHEN TO CALL THE MANAGER

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Client wants a custom package or pricing | Do NOT quote. Tell client you'll confirm in 24 hours. Escalate to manager. |
| Client is aggressive or abusive on call | End the call politely. Log the incident. Notify manager same day. |
| Client requests a refund or threatens chargeback | Do not engage on the topic. Transfer to manager immediately. |
| Lead quality is consistently poor (< 10% show rate) | Flag to manager after 3 days of pattern. |
| Call recording reveals compliance concern | Flag to manager with timestamp. |

---

> ⚠️ **Rule:** Every call gets logged. No exceptions. An unlogged call didn't happen.
