## ⚡ Non-Negotiable Daily Operating Habits

Every single day, no exceptions:

1. Send **10 outreach messages** (WhatsApp + Instagram/LinkedIn DM)
2. Post **1 content piece** (Instagram, LinkedIn, or WhatsApp Status)
3. Run **1 enquiry method post** (LinkedIn post, Facebook Group, or WA group)
4. Open **HubSpot** — update every active deal and conversation
5. Follow up on **every open lead** per the follow-up sequence
6. Log all activity — every person you contacted, every reply received

> 💡 **Revenue first. Systems second. Perfect last.**
