> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** Ops Head | **Status:** Live

---

## Purpose

Every transition point in the VA operation is defined here — who passes what, in what format, and by when. A handoff failure is always traceable to a missing entry in this system.

---

## Handoff Map

| Handoff | Who Passes | To Whom | What is Passed | Format | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lead scraping → Outreach** | VA (Intern/Executive) | Themselves | CRM batch, QC-001 completed | CRM sheet | Same day as scraping |
| **Outreach sent → CRM log** | VA | CRM | Updated status, last contact date, follow-up stage | CRM sheet | Within 1 hour of each send |
| **Positive reply → Manager alert** | VA | Manager | Lead name, company, reply summary (1 line) | WhatsApp | Within 30 minutes of reply |
| **Interested lead → Booking flow** | VA | Themselves + Manager | CRM status updated to Interested, Calendly link sent to lead | CRM + direct message | Within 2 hours of reply |
| **Booked call → Closer** | VA | Closer | Call Handover Form (SOP-VA-014) | Notion / assigned channel | Min 2 hours before call |
| **Daily work → Manager review** | VA | Manager | Daily report (SOP-VA-015) + CRM updated | Notion / assigned channel | By 6:00 PM daily |
| **Weekly work → Manager review** | VA | Manager | Weekly review report (SOP-VA-016) | Notion / assigned channel | By 6:00 PM Friday |
| **Call completed → CRM update** | VA (post-call feedback from closer) | CRM | Status updated to Called or Closed | CRM sheet | Same day as call |
| **Sales closed → Delivery** | Closer / Manager | Delivery team | Client name, package, payment status, service start date | Notion page (new client brief) | On payment confirmed |
| **Deliverable ready → QC** | Delivery team | QC reviewer | Deliverable + relevant QC checklist completed | Notion / Drive | Before sending to client |
| **VA onboarding gate → Next day** | VA | Manager | Gate submission (quiz / practice task / messages) | Notion / as instructed | End of each gate day |
| **VA promotion → Ops Head** | Manager | Ops Head | Promotion nomination with evidence (scorecard link, dates) | WhatsApp + written record | At point of eligibility |

## Handoff Failure Protocol

If a handoff is missed:

1. **Identify** which handoff point failed (refer to the table above)
2. **The owner of that handoff** is responsible for immediate correction
3. **Correct within the same working day** wherever possible
4. **Document in the Error Handling System** (see ⚠️ VA Error Handling System) if the failure caused downstream impact
5. **If the same handoff is missed twice in one week** — the Manager reviews the SOP for that step and determines if retraining is needed

---

## Handoff Quality Standard

A handoff is only complete when the **receiving party has everything they need to continue without asking a question.**

- A Call Handover Form with a blank Conversation Summary is not a complete handoff
- A daily report with blank fields is not a complete handoff
- A CRM entry with no Notes is not a complete handoff

**Incomplete handoffs are returned to the sender for completion.** They do not move forward.
