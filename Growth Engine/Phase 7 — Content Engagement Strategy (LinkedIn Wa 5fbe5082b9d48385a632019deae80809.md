# Phase 7 — Content Engagement Strategy (LinkedIn Warm-Up)

## 

This strategy **warms up prospects before you DM them**, making them **more likely to reply** on LinkedIn. When someone sees your name multiple times on their posts, your message feels familiar instead of cold. 🔥

---

# Daily Target

Engage with **10–20 prospects per day**.

Types of engagement:

👍 Like their posts

💬 Comment on posts

🔁 Share insights

👀 View their profile

Total time needed: **15–20 minutes per day**

---

# Step 1 — Like Prospect Posts

After sending a connection request:

- Visit their profile
- Like **1–2 recent posts**

Why?

This creates **recognition**.

When you message them later, they remember your name.

---

# Step 2 — Comment on Their Posts

Comments are **more powerful than likes**.

Example comments:

If they post about business growth:

> Great point. Many companies underestimate how important consistent lead generation is.
> 

If they post about hiring:

> Congrats on the growth — exciting to see companies expanding right now.
> 

Rules:

✔ Be **supportive**

✔ Be **short**

✔ Be **relevant**

Avoid spam comments like:

❌ “Great post!!!”

---

# Step 3 — Share Valuable Insights

Sometimes add **short insights** that show expertise.

Example:

> We've been seeing more companies in the SaaS space shifting toward performance marketing lately.
> 

This positions you as **knowledgeable**.

---

# Step 4 — Build Familiarity

Your goal is to appear **3–5 times before messaging**.

Example engagement timeline:

Day 1

👍 Like their post

Day 2

💬 Comment on their post

Day 3

👀 View profile

Day 4

💬 Send DM

When they see your message, they think:

> "I've seen this person before."
> 

This increases reply rates.

---

# Step 5 — Track Engagement in CRM

Add a column in your CRM:

**Engagement Status**

Example:

| Name | Company | Engagement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| John Smith | Peak Roofing | Liked post |
| Maria Lee | FitLab Gym | Commented |

This keeps outreach **organized**.

---

# Example Weekly Workflow

Monday

Find 30 prospects

Tuesday

Like and comment on posts

Wednesday

Send connection requests

Thursday

Engage again

Friday

Send DM

---

# Tools That Help With Engagement

Useful tools agencies use:

- LinkedIn
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Taplio
- Shield Analytics

These help monitor engagement and prospects.

---

# Why This Strategy Works

Cold outreach alone:

📉 ~5–10% reply rate

Warm outreach (engagement first):

📈 ~20–40% reply rate
