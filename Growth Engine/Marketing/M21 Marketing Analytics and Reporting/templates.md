# M21 — Marketing Analytics and Reporting: Templates

## UTM Tagging Template

```
?utm_source=[platform: linkedin|instagram|youtube|twitter|facebook|newsletter|whatsapp]
&utm_medium=[organic|paid|referral|email]
&utm_campaign=[stage-content-date, e.g. m09-pillar-post-jul26]
```

## Weekly Channel Snapshot

```
Week of: [date]

Sessions (total): [n]  |  vs last week: [+/-%]
Top channel by sessions: [channel] — [n sessions]
Top content page: [page/title] — [n sessions]
Leads generated (all sources): [n]
Notable spike/drop: [channel/page] — [brief note, no speculation beyond the data]
```

## Monthly Business Rollup
