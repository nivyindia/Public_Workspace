# M07 — Off-Page SEO & Authority Building

**Phase:** B — SEO Engine
**Feeds:** M20 (Partnerships, Co-Marketing & PR — shares link-earning tactics), M21 (Marketing Analytics & Reporting)
**Fed by:** M04 (Keyword Research & Mapping — informs which pages need authority most), M09 (Long-Form Content Production — link-worthy content is what earns links)
**Build status:** 🟡 Built to pilot depth this session (Batch 2). See `references.md` for a data-gap note.

## Purpose

M07 builds the site's authority signals outside the site itself — primarily backlinks, but also brand mentions and citations. On-page and technical SEO (M05, M06) make a page *eligible* to rank; off-page authority is what convinces Google the domain and page are trustworthy enough to actually rank well for competitive terms. This is the slowest-moving stage in the SEO Engine phase — authority compounds over months, not days, and there is no legitimate shortcut to it.

## Inputs

- Published, link-worthy content (from M09) — off-page work has nothing to promote without this
- Target keyword/page priorities (from M04) — informs which pages need authority-building focus first
- Competitor backlink profiles (for gap analysis, where visible via free tools)

## Outputs

- Growing backlink profile (tracked list of earned links, source, and target page)
- Directory/citation listings (for local/regional relevance where applicable)
- Digital PR / guest content placements

## Sub-Stages

| # | Sub-stage | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| M07.1 | Link-worthy asset identification | Identifies which existing/planned content is genuinely worth other sites linking to |
| M07.2 | Outreach & link building | Manual and semi-automated outreach to earn backlinks from relevant sites |
| M07.3 | Digital PR & guest content | Pitches guest posts, expert-source opportunities (e.g., HARO-style), and press mentions |
| M07.4 | Directory & citation building | Lists the business in relevant, reputable directories (especially valuable for local/regional relevance) |
| M07.5 | Backlink monitoring & disavow hygiene | Tracks the growing backlink profile and flags spammy/toxic links for review |

## How This Stage Is Used

M07.1 runs whenever new cornerstone content ships from M09. M07.2–M07.4 are ongoing outreach work, tracked in a running pipeline (see `templates.md`). M07.5 runs monthly as a hygiene check, not because toxic links are expected often, but because catching one early is cheap and catching it late isn't.
