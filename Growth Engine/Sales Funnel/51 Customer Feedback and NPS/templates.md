## Standard NPS Survey
```
On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend [Company] to a
colleague or friend?

[0 — Not at all likely] ... [10 — Extremely likely]

What's the main reason for your score?
[open text]
```

## Score Classification
| Score | Classification |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Promoter |
| 7-8 | Passive |
| 0-6 | Detractor |

**NPS = % Promoters − % Detractors**

## Detractor Follow-Up Message
```
Hi [Name], thanks for your honest feedback. I want to understand more —
do you have 10 minutes this week for a quick call? I'd like to make
sure we address [specific issue mentioned] properly.
```

## Promoter Follow-Up (routes to Stage 52/53)
```
Hi [Name], really glad to hear things are going well! Would you be open
to a quick case study or testimonial about your experience? [Stage 52]

Also — if you know anyone else who might benefit from what we do, we'd
love an introduction. [Stage 53]
```
