# FAQ — 24 Follow Up Systems

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: How is this different from each channel stage's own follow-up steps?**
Stages 16-20 each implement a channel-specific version of the follow-up cadence in their templates.md. This stage documents the shared philosophy and rules those channel-level sequences are built on, so the discipline is explicit and consistent across channels rather than reinvented per channel.

**Q: Why max 3 follow-ups per channel?**
Beyond that, response rates drop sharply and continued contact risks reading as spam/harassment rather than persistence. Three follow-ups (plus the opener) matches the "3rd-5th touchpoint" pattern where most replies happen.

**Q: What happens to a contact after they're marked cold?**
They enter a 90-day re-engagement sequence automatically — they aren't deleted or forgotten, just given a cooling-off period before a lighter-touch re-approach.

**Q: Can the same contact be "cold" on email but still active on LinkedIn?**
Yes — cold status is tracked per channel. See Stage 21 Multi-Channel Sequencing for how this interacts with a coordinated cross-channel cadence.

**Q: What counts as a "value-add" follow-up vs. a bare bump?**
A value-add follow-up references something specific — a stat, case study, or relevant example — rather than only saying "just following up" or "checking in." See [templates.md](templates.md) for examples.
