# Checklists — 48 Cross Sell Strategy

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

## Combination-Validity QC
- [ ] Cross-sell offered matches an actual row in the golden combination table for the client's current primary service
- [ ] Rationale communicated (why the two services compound), not just offered as a bolt-on

## Timing QC
- [ ] Client is already seeing results on their current service before a cross-sell is pitched
- [ ] Cross-sell delivered during a natural touchpoint (quarterly review, delivery milestone), not as a cold interruption

## Level 3 Upgrade QC
- [ ] Level 3 system-upgrade only recommended once a client holds 2+ Level 2 services or has explicitly signaled wanting integration ("can you do everything?")
- [ ] Level 3 conversation booked as a call with the strategy team, not closed informally by the person who spotted the signal

## Regional QC
- [ ] USA-market conversations lead with ROI/dollar-amount framing; international-market conversations lead with value-for-money and local price comparison, per the source playbook's regional guidance
- [ ] Regional framing reflects current company guidance — verify before using deal-size figures as literal targets

## Duplicate / Accuracy / Completeness Checks
- [ ] Cross-sell attempts logged in the same tracker as upsell attempts (Stage 47), no parallel untracked log
