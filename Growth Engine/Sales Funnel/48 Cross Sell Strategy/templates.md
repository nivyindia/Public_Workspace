## The Sell → Upsell → Cross-Sell Flow
```
ENTRY (Level 1) → RETAINER (Level 2) → SYSTEM (Level 3) → ENTERPRISE (Level 4)
```
A VA's job is getting a client to Level 1 or Level 2; the closer/account manager handles the upgrade path from there — but everyone needs to know the map to set it up correctly.

## Golden Cross-Sell Combinations

| Primary Service (What They Have) | Cross-Sell 1 | Cross-Sell 2 | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEO Growth | Social Media Growth | Copywriting Growth | SEO needs content. Social amplifies content. Copy makes both convert. |
| Social Media Growth | Video Growth | Graphic Design | Video + graphics = complete content system |
| Ads Management | Funnel & Conversion | Copywriting | Ads bring traffic. Funnel captures it. Copy converts it. |
| Email Marketing | Copywriting | Funnel Growth | Email needs copy. Funnels need email. |
| E-commerce Growth | Google Ads | Email Marketing | Full ecom growth stack |
| Local SEO | Google Ads | Reputation Management | Local businesses need reviews + rankings + paid |
| Outreach System | Copywriting | CRM / Sales Enablement | Outreach needs copy + tracking |

## Cross-Sell Script
> "Since you're already doing [SERVICE A] with us, a lot of clients at your stage also add [SERVICE B] because [REASON]. It's [PRICE]/month and it directly supports what we're already doing for you. Want me to show you what that would look like combined?"

## Level 3 System-Upgrade Signals

| What They Have | Level 3 Upgrade | Why |
|---|---|---|
| SEO + Social + Ads | Growth Engine System | Same services, integrated with strategy |
| Ads + Funnel + Email | Lead Generation System | Full lead gen engine |
| E-com Growth + Ads | E-commerce System | Complete ecom scaling |
| Outreach + CRM | Outreach Engine System | Full outbound machine |
| Social + Video + Copy | Authority System | Content-based authority building |

## Level 3 System-Upgrade Script
> "You're currently doing [SERVICE A] and [SERVICE B] with us. At this point, it actually makes more sense to move to our [LEVEL 3 SYSTEM], which combines everything into one integrated strategy with a dedicated team. The results are faster because everything is connected. Let me book you a call with our strategy team to walk through what that looks like."

## Quick Cross-Sell Triggers — What to Listen For

| Client Says | Your Move |
|---|---|
| "We just launched our website" | Mention SEO Growth and Social Media Growth |
| "We're spending on ads but not getting results" | Funnel & Conversion Growth + Copywriting |
| "We want to grow our social media" | Upgrade from Starter to Growth tier |
| "We need more leads" | Outreach System or Lead Gen System (Level 3) |
| "We're spending a lot on marketing but it's not connected" | Level 3 Growth Engine System |
| "We want to automate" | Level 4 AI Systems — book call immediately |
| "Can you do everything?" | Level 3 System Package — book call immediately |

## Regional Framing (verify current guidance before use)

**USA clients:** lead with ROI and dollar amounts/percentages; expect professional process (SLAs, account managers, reporting); high-ticket ($2,000–$5,000/mo) is normal.

**International clients (UK/AU/CA/India/Middle East):** lead with value-for-money and local price comparison; more price-conscious initially — build trust before pitching Level 3.
