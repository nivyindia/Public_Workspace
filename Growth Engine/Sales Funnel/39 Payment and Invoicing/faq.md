# FAQ — 39 Payment and Invoicing

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: What's the standard payment structure for a new client?**
It depends on the deal type: project-based work is 50% advance / 50% on completion, monthly retainers are paid in full in advance each month, quarterly packages are 100% advance with a 5% discount, annual contracts can be paid quarterly (25% every 3 months) or in full for a 10% discount, and media-buying/ad-spend work requires 100% advance before campaign launch — per the internal Payment Terms Policy.

**Q: How long does a client have to pay an invoice?**
7 calendar days from the invoice issue date, per standard policy. A late fee (1.5-2% per week/month depending on the specific policy version — verify current figure) applies after that.

**Q: What happens if a client is significantly late on payment?**
Services may be temporarily suspended if payment is delayed more than 14 days. Recurring late payments can result in service termination or a requirement of 100% advance payment for all future work with that client.

**Q: How are international clients billed differently from domestic clients?**
Domestic (India) invoices are in INR with 18% GST applied. International clients may be billed in USD, GBP, or EUR at prevailing exchange rates, with tax applied per relevant treaties rather than Indian GST.

**Q: Who bears payment gateway or bank transfer fees?**
The client — this is stated explicitly in the standard Payment Terms Policy, not absorbed by the company.

**Q: Can pricing or payment terms change mid-contract?**
Pricing may be revised with 30 days' notice for long-term contracts, per policy. Any other mid-contract deviation should go through Stage 38 (Deal Desk and Approval Workflows) rather than being agreed informally.
