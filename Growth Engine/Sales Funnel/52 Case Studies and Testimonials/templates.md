## Case Study Template
```
=== CASE STUDY: [Client Type] in [Industry/Country] ===

CLIENT SNAPSHOT:
• Industry: [e.g., Real Estate / SaaS / E-commerce]
• Location: [e.g., United States / UK / India]
• Company size: [e.g., Solo founder / 5-person team]
• Service received: [e.g., VA (20 hrs/week) + Social Media Management]
• Duration: [e.g., 3 months]

THE CHALLENGE:
[2-3 sentences describing their situation BEFORE working with us.
Be specific: time lost, tasks piling up, growth stalling.]

THE SOLUTION:
[What exactly did we do? Be specific about tasks, tools, process.]

THE RESULTS:
• Time saved: [X hrs/week freed up]
• Growth impact: [e.g., "Closed 2 extra deals in Month 1"]
• Cost saving: [e.g., specific $/month figure]
• Satisfaction: [NPS score or direct quote]

CLIENT QUOTE:
"[Direct testimonial in their words — 1-3 sentences.]"
— [First name only or initials], [Title], [Industry]

LESSON LEARNED:
[1 sentence insight for prospects reading this.]
```

## When to Ask for a Testimonial
- After Month 1 if NPS ≥ 8
- After a project milestone is completed
- When a client says something spontaneously positive — ask immediately, don't wait

## Testimonial Ask — WhatsApp/Informal
```
Hi [Name]! So glad the [result] worked out 🎉

Would you be open to writing a quick 2-3 sentence review for us? It
helps other businesses find us and means a lot to our team.

You can post it here: [Google link] [Clutch link]

Or if it's easier, just reply here with a few sentences and I'll format
it for our website. 😊
```

## Testimonial Ask — Email (Formal)
```
Subject: Quick favor — would you leave us a review?

Hi [Name],

Working with you on [project] has been fantastic, and I'm really proud
of the results we delivered together.

Would you spare 2 minutes to share your experience?
⭐ Google: [link]
⭐ Clutch: [link]

Or if you prefer, reply to this email and I'll format something for our
website.

Thank you so much!
[Your name]
```
