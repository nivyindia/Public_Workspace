# FAQ — 02 ICP Definition

> Part of Stage 02 (ICP Definition). See [README.md](README.md) for the full stage overview.

---

**Q: How many ICPs should we define?**
A: 3-5. Fewer than 3 usually means the segments are too broad to drive differentiated messaging; more than 5-6 fragments outreach volume and copywriting effort across too many angles to execute well.

**Q: What if we don't have enough existing client data to ground an ICP?**
A: Lean on Stage 01 market research and competitor client-pattern inference (who competitors showcase/price for), and mark the ICP as a hypothesis to validate rather than a confirmed profile — revisit after the first 10-20 closed deals.

**Q: How is ICP different from Buyer Persona (Stage 03)?**
A: ICP is firmographic and behavioral — company size, role, budget, pain points, buying triggers. Persona adds the individual-human layer — demographics, media habits, tone of voice, day-in-the-life detail — that makes messaging feel personal rather than generic. ICP answers "which companies/roles do we target"; Persona answers "how do we talk to the human in that role."

**Q: Should Negative ICP be shared with the outreach/sales team the same way as positive ICP?**
A: Yes — it prevents wasted time on leads that will never close well, and protects service quality by keeping out clients the delivery model isn't built to serve (e.g. highly regulated industries without specialist compliance setup).

**Q: How often should ICPs be revisited?**
A: At minimum quarterly, and immediately if closed-won deal patterns start diverging meaningfully from the current ICP definitions.
