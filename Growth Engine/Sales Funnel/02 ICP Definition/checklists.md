# Checklists — 02 ICP Definition

> Part of Stage 02 (ICP Definition). See [README.md](README.md) for the full stage overview.

---

## Pre-Definition Checklist

- [ ] Stage 01 Market Research brief reviewed for the relevant geography/geographies
- [ ] Existing client/CRM data pulled and reviewed for evidence
- [ ] Sales team debrief conducted (informal pattern-recognition captured)

## ICP Card Completion Checklist (per ICP)

- [ ] Role/title(s) defined
- [ ] Company size range defined
- [ ] Revenue band defined
- [ ] Geography defined
- [ ] Industry/verticals defined
- [ ] At least 3 top pain points listed
- [ ] At least 2 goals listed
- [ ] At least 1 buying trigger identified
- [ ] Budget range grounded in evidence (client data or market research), not guessed
- [ ] "Where to find them" channels listed
- [ ] One-sentence messaging angle drafted

## Negative ICP Checklist

- [ ] At least 3 explicit exclusions listed
- [ ] Each exclusion has a stated reason grounded in positive ICP criteria (not just "we don't like them")

## Quality Control Gates

- [ ] No two active ICPs are indistinguishable from each other on the scoring criteria that will feed Stage 11
- [ ] Every ICP reviewed by whoever owns sales/strategy before being marked `active`
- [ ] ICP document dated with `Last Updated`
