# FAQ — 17 LinkedIn Outreach

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: How many connection requests can I send per day safely?**
Follow the daily limit in [methods.md](methods.md)/source SOP (roughly 25-30/day) — this figure should be verified against current LinkedIn behavior-detection norms before relying on it, as thresholds change without public notice.

**Q: Can I DM someone before they accept my connection request?**
No — the DM sequence only starts after acceptance. Messaging non-connections requires InMail (a different, credit-limited LinkedIn feature) and is out of scope for this stage's standard flow.

**Q: What if I get a LinkedIn warning about automated behavior?**
Pause all automated sending immediately and flag to a supervisor before resuming. Do not attempt to "push through" a warning by continuing at the same volume.

**Q: Should every connection get the same DM sequence?**
No — sequences should be segmented by persona/industry per Stage 15's channel plan; a single generic sequence sent to everyone converts worse and reads as obviously templated.

**Q: How is this different from Stage 21 Multi-Channel Sequencing?**
This stage covers how to execute LinkedIn outreach well on its own. Stage 21 covers combining LinkedIn with other channels (e.g., email + LinkedIn + call) in a coordinated sequence.
