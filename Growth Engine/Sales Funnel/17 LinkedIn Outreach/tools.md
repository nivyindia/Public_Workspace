## Selection Notes
- Sales Navigator materially improves targeting precision over free LinkedIn search and is close to mandatory for volume outreach at this stage.
- Any browser-automation tool carries account-restriction risk if daily limits are exceeded — always err conservative over the published limit, not up to it.
