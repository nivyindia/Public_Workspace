# FAQ — 50 Churn Prevention

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: How is this different from Stage 49 Renewal Management?**
Stage 49 is the scheduled, calendar-driven process run in the 60 days before a contract's natural expiry. Stage 50 is ongoing, signal-driven monitoring that can trigger intervention at any point in the client lifecycle — a client can raise a churn-risk signal in month 2 of a 12-month contract, well outside any renewal window.

**Q: What counts as "silent churn" and how is it guarded against?**
A client who never explicitly complains but is quietly disengaging — the monthly CSAT/NPS survey exists specifically to catch this, since it doesn't rely on the client proactively raising an issue.

**Q: What's the very first thing to say when a client raises cancellation?**
Per the save-a-client script: acknowledge their directness, then ask specifically whether the issue is results, process, or budget — don't assume which one it is.

**Q: Is a discount or refund always offered to a dissatisfied client?**
No — a concrete improvement plan comes first. Goodwill offers (like a free month or partial refund) are reserved for genuine dissatisfaction cases and should follow current authorization policy, not be offered automatically.

**Q: What happens if a client decides to leave anyway, even after the save-a-client conversation?**
Exit gracefully and ask for honest feedback — the source material is explicit that pushing further at that point isn't the right move, and the feedback itself has value for pattern analysis (informing Stage 51 Customer Feedback and NPS).
