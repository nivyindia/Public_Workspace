# FAQ — 44 Customer Success Planning

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: How is this different from Stage 43 (Account Management)?**
Stage 43 is the baseline operating rhythm every client gets (weekly update, regular reporting, monthly call). This stage is the structured success framework layered on top of it — lifecycle touchpoint mapping, churn-risk scoring, NPS-driven action, and a defined save-a-client process for at-risk accounts.

**Q: What are realistic churn-rate targets over a client's lifetime?**
Per the source Customer Retention System, targets typically tighten over time: under 20% in months 1-3, under 15% in months 4-6, under 10% in months 7-12, and under 5% after month 12 — reflecting that early-tenure churn is the highest-risk period.

**Q: What's the "3 retention pillars" framework?**
Results (deliver measurable outcomes every month), Relationship (be proactive, communicative, human), and Value expansion (always show what else is possible) — per the source doc's stated retention philosophy.

**Q: What should happen the moment a client asks about the cancellation process?**
This is flagged as a critical warning sign requiring a same-day founder call, not a routine account-manager follow-up — see the save-a-client script in [templates.md](templates.md).

**Q: Should every client be asked for a public review?**
No — only promoters (NPS 9-10). Passives should be asked what would improve their experience, and detractors should get a direct founder call to resolve their concern, not a review request.
