# FAQ — 36 Contract and Legal

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: Can work begin before the contract is signed or payment received?**
No — per the source material, work should not begin until payment is received, and the contract should be signed before the invoice/payment link is sent.

**Q: What are the three payment structures typically offered?**
Monthly retainer (client pays every month), upfront deposit (e.g., 50% upfront / 50% at milestone), and first-month-in-advance (common for marketing/VA services).

**Q: Who reviews non-standard contract clauses?**
Non-standard clauses, client redlines, or custom Enterprise-tier terms should get legal review before signature — this stage's standard template covers routine deals only.

**Q: What happens automatically once the contract is signed?**
CRM deal stage updates to Closed Won, an invoice/payment link is sent (Stage 39), and the onboarding workflow triggers (Stage 40).

**Q: Is the 30-day notice period and 14-day payment term fixed?**
These figures are carried over from internal reference material — verify they're still current company policy before including them in a live contract.
