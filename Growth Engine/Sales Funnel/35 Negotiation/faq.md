# FAQ — 35 Negotiation

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: How is this stage different from Stage 26 Objection Handling?**
Stage 26 covers objections raised earlier in the funnel (before/during the discovery and demo process). Stage 35 covers negotiation specifically around a sent proposal's price and terms — narrower in scope and directly tied to the pricing catalog (Stage 34) and the eventual contract (Stage 36).

**Q: What's the maximum discount a rep can offer without approval?**
10% per the source SOP as a starting figure — verify this is still current company policy before relying on it. Anything above it requires a named approver on record.

**Q: What should happen if a prospect won't move off a price the business can't sustainably accept, even after the full escalation ladder?**
Politely decline and route to 90-day nurture (Stage 24) rather than under-pricing the engagement — this is explicit policy, not just a fallback.

**Q: Can the hourly-rate floor ever be discounted below the stated minimum?**
No — per the source doc, the effective hourly rate is one of the items explicitly never discounted, regardless of negotiation pressure.

**Q: Does the referral discount need separate approval each time?**
No — it's pre-approved standing policy (Stage 34), unlike case-by-case discount requests which do need sign-off.
