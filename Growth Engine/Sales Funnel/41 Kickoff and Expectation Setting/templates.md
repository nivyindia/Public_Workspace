## Kickoff Call Agenda (45 minutes)
```
1. Welcome + introductions (5 min)
2. Understanding their business deeper — 3 smart discovery questions
   personalized to their industry and stated goals (10 min)
3. Confirming scope and deliverables (10 min)
4. Setting KPIs and success metrics together (10 min)
5. Communication cadence agreement (5 min)
6. Access and assets needed (5 min)
7. Next steps and timeline confirmation (5 min)
```

## Expectation-Setting Script
```
Timeline:
"Initial setup takes 2-4 weeks; results build over time, not instantly."

Role clarity:
"We handle system setup and optimization. You provide content, approvals,
and timely communication when we need something from you."

Results expectation:
"Leads/results increase gradually as the system builds momentum — we'll
show you exactly what's happening at each stage, not just ask you to trust
the process."
```

## Success Metrics Agreement Template
```
Primary KPI: [e.g., leads generated / meetings booked / conversion rate]
Secondary KPIs: [e.g., website traffic, engagement rate]
Baseline (current state): [value, if known]
Target / expected trajectory: [what "good" looks like by when]
Reporting cadence: [weekly / bi-weekly / monthly]
```

## Communication Cadence Agreement

| Method | Used For | Response Time |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp / Slack | Quick updates, time-sensitive questions | Within 2-4 business hours |
| Email | Formal requests, reports, documentation | Within 24 hours |
| Monthly call | Strategy review, goals, feedback | Scheduled monthly |
| Client portal | Ongoing progress visibility | Always accessible |
