# FAQ — 07 Contact Discovery

> Part of Stage 07 (Contact Discovery). See [README.md](README.md) for the full stage overview.

---

**Q: Is it okay to send outreach to a pattern-inferred email without validating it first?**
A: No. An inferred email is a hypothesis, not a confirmed contact — always run it through validation (Stage 10 or an inline check) before it's used for outreach. Sending to unvalidated inferred addresses drives up bounce rate and damages sender reputation.

**Q: How much manual effort should go into resolving a single stubborn lead?**
A: Scale effort to ICP-fit. A high-scoring ICP match is worth 2-3 discovery method attempts and a manual fallback check; a marginal-fit lead may not be worth more than the automated tool pass before being flagged unresolved.

**Q: What if the contact-page fallback surfaces a general info@ email instead of a named contact?**
A: Use it only if no named-individual channel exists, and flag it as lower-confidence — general inboxes convert worse than direct contacts and should be deprioritized where a real alternative exists.

**Q: How does this stage differ from Stage 10 (Lead Verification)?**
A: This stage finds a plausible contact channel. Stage 10 confirms that channel is actually deliverable/valid (email doesn't bounce, phone is in service). A lead can be "resolved" here and still fail verification later.

**Q: What if resolution rate is consistently below the 80% benchmark?**
A: Flag it to Stage 05 (Lead Source Selection) — a persistently low resolution rate often signals the source itself has weak contact-data coverage for that ICP/market, not a discovery-process problem.
