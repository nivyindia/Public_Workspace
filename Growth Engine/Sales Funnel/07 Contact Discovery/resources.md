This stage relies primarily on tools (see [tools.md](tools.md)) rather than a website library. Supplementary sources:

| Source | Category | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company "Contact Us" / Team pages | Fallback contact source | Free | Manual or browser-automation pull when tools fail |
| WHOIS / domain registration lookup | Confirm domain ownership before pattern-guessing | Free (basic lookup) | Useful sanity check on obscure/small-company domains |
| LinkedIn company pages | Cross-check role/title before committing discovery effort | Free | Confirms the target still holds the role before spending discovery effort |
