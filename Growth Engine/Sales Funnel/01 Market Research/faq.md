# FAQ — 01 Market Research

> Part of Stage 01 (Market Research). See [README.md](README.md) for the full stage overview.

---

**Q: How deep should market research go before moving to ICP Definition (Stage 02)?**
A: Deep enough that Service Demand, Target Segments, and Competitive Landscape are filled with real (even if rough) data — not exhaustive. A brief with sourced directional data beats a perfect brief that takes a month, since ICP work will surface follow-up questions research alone can't answer.

**Q: What if we can't find a sourced figure for TAM/SAM/SOM?**
A: Mark it "estimated — unverified" rather than inventing a number or omitting the section. A labeled estimate is usable; a fabricated precise-looking number is actively misleading downstream.

**Q: How often should a market brief be refreshed?**
A: Every 2 quarters minimum, or immediately after a known regulatory change (e.g. a new tax regime) that could shift demand or buyer urgency.

**Q: Do we need primary research (interviews/surveys) for every market, or is secondary research enough to start?**
A: Secondary research (public stats, review mining, competitor scans) is enough to produce a first-pass brief and start Stage 02-05. Primary research (buyer interviews) is worth adding once a market is confirmed as a priority, to validate willingness-to-pay assumptions before scaling outreach spend.

**Q: Who owns keeping the market brief current?**
A: Whoever owns the Strategy & Intelligence function for that market — the brief should have a named owner, not be treated as a one-time artifact.
