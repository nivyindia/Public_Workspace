# FAQ — 04 Competitor Research

> Part of Stage 04 (Competitor Research). See [README.md](README.md) for the full stage overview.

---

**Q: How many competitors do we really need to track?**
A: At least 10, split 3 local / 3 national / 2 niche-specialist / 2 high-end. Fewer than that leaves gaps in the objection-response coverage sales actually needs; many more becomes unmaintainable at a monthly cadence.

**Q: Is it okay to scrape competitor review sites for bulk sentiment analysis?**
A: Default to manual reading or official export/API access. Most review platforms' terms of service prohibit automated scraping — respect that rather than treating public visibility as permission.

**Q: What do we do if we can't find a competitor's pricing anywhere public?**
A: Mark it "not publicly disclosed" rather than guessing or inventing a number. If pricing intelligence is critical, a mystery-shopper style inquiry (a real request for a quote) is a legitimate manual method, distinct from scraping.

**Q: How should positioning responses be used — as scripts to recite verbatim?**
A: As a reasoning pattern (acknowledge strength → pivot to USP → tie to client situation), not a verbatim script. Sales should adapt the specific wording to the actual conversation.

**Q: What if a prospect mentions a competitor we haven't tracked yet?**
A: Log it in CRM notes immediately — this is one of the highest-signal ways new competitors surface, often faster than desk research would find them.
