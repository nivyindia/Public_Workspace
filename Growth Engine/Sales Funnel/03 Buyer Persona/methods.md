# Methods — 03 Buyer Persona

> Part of Stage 03 (Buyer Persona). See [README.md](README.md) for the full stage overview.

---

## ICP-Expansion Methods

- **Direct expansion** — take the demographic/psychographic fields already present in a well-built ICP card (age, tools, media habits) and expand them into a narrative persona rather than starting from zero
- **Client interview mining** — pull direct quotes and context (paraphrased, not verbatim beyond short attributed fragments) from buyer interview notes to ground the day-in-the-life narrative in real behavior

## Behavioral & Digital-Footprint Methods

- **Social media habit observation** — where does this type of buyer actually spend time (LinkedIn vs. Twitter/X vs. niche forums) — cross-check against Stage 01/02 channel notes
- **Community/forum lurking** — reading (not scraping) the actual language buyers use in relevant communities to calibrate tone, without treating any one thread as universally representative

## AI-Assisted Methods

- LLM expansion of ICP card into full persona narrative (see [README.md §7](README.md#7-ai-section))
- AI-drafted persona-specific objections distinct from generic ICP-level objections

## Manual vs. Automated

| Method | Manual | Semi-Automated | Fully Automated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Persona narrative drafting | Analyst writes from ICP + interviews | LLM drafts from pasted ICP, human edits | Agent pulls finalized ICP and drafts automatically on ICP update |
| Tone guide creation | Analyst writes style notes | LLM extracts tone patterns from real client emails/calls (with permission) | N/A — tone calibration benefits from human judgment |

## Validation Method

- Every draft persona should be read against 2-3 real client interactions (calls, emails, interview notes) by someone who has actually spoken with buyers in that ICP, before being marked `active` — this is the single highest-value QC step and should not be skipped even under time pressure
