# Resources — 03 Buyer Persona

> Part of Stage 03 (Buyer Persona). See [README.md](README.md) for the full stage overview.

---

This stage relies primarily on internal data (ICP cards, buyer interviews) rather than external sources. Where external validation is useful:

| Source | Category | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn (persona's likely profile type) | Validate real-world presence of the persona type | Free | Cross-check that the persona's described role/behavior matches real profiles you can find |
| Podcast directories (Apple Podcasts, Spotify) | Verify a named podcast actually exists and is active before citing it in a persona | Free | Use to fact-check any AI-suggested media habit before finalizing |
| Reddit / niche forums matching the persona's world | Tone and language calibration | Free | Read-only — do not scrape or misrepresent participation |
