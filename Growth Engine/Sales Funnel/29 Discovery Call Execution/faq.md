# FAQ — 29 Discovery Call Execution

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: What is SPIN and why use it?**
Situation → Problem → Implication → Need-Payoff. It's a widely-used B2B sales methodology built on the discipline of asking before pitching, so the prospect articulates their own pain and desired outcome rather than being told what they need.

**Q: How many pricing options should I present?**
One. Recommending a single clear package outperforms presenting multiple options, which tends to create confusion and stalls decisions.

**Q: What if the prospect says "let me think about it"?**
Don't accept it as a final answer — ask what specific question they need answered before moving forward, then either answer it live or rebook a follow-up with a specific date/time. See [templates.md](templates.md).

**Q: How fast does the proposal need to go out after a committed call?**
Within 24 hours — momentum drops off quickly after a good call.

**Q: What's the difference between this stage and Stage 30 Needs Analysis?**
This stage covers running the full discovery call, including a lightweight needs discussion (Problem/Implication/Need-Payoff). Stage 30 goes deeper on structuring and documenting the needs analysis itself, particularly for more complex opportunities.
