# Methods — 32 Demo and Presentation

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

## Traditional
A rep verbally walks through the solution live on the discovery call or a follow-up call, without a formal deck.

## Modern / Tool-Assisted
A short slide deck (Google Slides/PowerPoint/Canva) structured as Problem → Solution → Process → Proof, screen-shared live.

## AI-Assisted
LLM-drafted deck outline from the approved solution map; LLM-summarized session notes into proposal adjustments.

## Manual
Live delivery and handling of questions/objections during the session — always a human skill.

## Automated
Not much to automate directly in this stage beyond the outline-drafting step; the presentation itself is a live conversation.

## API / Integration
CRM opportunity record capturing session notes/questions, feeding directly into Stage 33's proposal drafting.

## Browser Automation
Not applicable to this stage.

## Scraping
Not applicable to this stage.

## Public Database / Government
Not applicable to this stage.

## Community / Referral
Not applicable to this stage.

## Method Selection Guidance
For straightforward standard-package opportunities, fold this into the discovery call's closing minutes (Stage 29's Present & Close) rather than adding a separate session. For larger or customized opportunities, run a dedicated session with a short deck so the prospect can review the material at their own pace afterward.
