# Checklists — 33 Proposal Creation

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

## Pre-Send QC
- [ ] Proposal built from an approved solution map (Stage 31), not generic boilerplate
- [ ] All 6 sections present: Executive Summary, Recommended Solution, Pricing, Timeline & Onboarding, Why Us, Next Steps
- [ ] Pricing matches the current catalog (Stage 34) exactly
- [ ] Proposal validity window stated (e.g., "valid for 14 days")

## Send QC
- [ ] Sent within 24 hours of the qualifying call/presentation
- [ ] Sent with a personal email note, not attached without context
- [ ] Subject line follows the standard format

## Follow-Up QC
- [ ] Day 3, Day 7, Day 12 follow-ups sent if no response
- [ ] Follow-up tone stays helpful, not pushy (see [templates.md](templates.md))

## Negotiation & Discount QC
- [ ] Any discount above the discretionary threshold has a named approver on record before the revised proposal goes out
- [ ] Negotiation responses use the standard scripts, not ad hoc concessions

## Contract & Sign-Off QC
- [ ] Signed contract filed and CRM deal stage updated to Closed Won
- [ ] Onboarding workflow triggered immediately on signature

## Duplicate / Accuracy / Completeness Checks
- [ ] No opportunity with two active, conflicting proposal versions
- [ ] Discount approvals logged with the approver's name, not left blank
