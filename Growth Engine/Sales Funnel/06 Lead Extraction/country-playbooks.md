## Applying a Vertical Filter On Top of a Country Playbook

Example — sourcing companies likely to need ISO certification in the US (from an internal Apollo filter reference):

- **Location:** United States (optionally narrow to industrial-hub states — Michigan, Texas, California)
- **Industry:** Manufacturing (Automotive, Aerospace, Electronics) · Construction & Engineering · Healthcare (Pharma, Medical Devices) · Food & Beverage · IT/Software · Energy (Oil & Gas, Renewables)
- **Company size:** Revenue $5M–$500M, 50–5,000+ employees (mid-to-large companies are more likely to invest in certification)
- **Compliance keyword tags (where available):** "ISO certified," "quality management," "environmental management," "safety compliance," "ISO 27001" (for IT/InfoSec)
- **Target titles:** CEO, COO, Quality Manager, Compliance Officer, Operations Manager, Safety Manager

This pattern — country playbook + vertical filter stack — generalizes to any niche offer and should be replicated per campaign rather than treated as ISO-specific.
