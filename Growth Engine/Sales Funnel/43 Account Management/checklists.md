# Checklists — 43 Account Management

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

## Weekly QC
- [ ] Every active client receives a WhatsApp/personal update (key win from the week, not just "all good")
- [ ] Internal KPI check completed — any metric flagged below threshold reviewed before the client notices
- [ ] Any client message from the week responded to within the defined response-time SLA

## Bi-Weekly / Monthly Reporting QC
- [ ] Report generated and reviewed by the AM before sending — never sent as an unreviewed AI draft
- [ ] Report reflects actual performance data, not a templated placeholder
- [ ] Report delivered on the agreed schedule (e.g., by the 5th of each month)

## Monthly Strategy Call QC
- [ ] Call scheduled and confirmed ahead of month-end
- [ ] Deep KPI review and at least one concrete optimization recommendation prepared beforehand
- [ ] Call notes logged and any follow-up actions tracked to completion

## Portfolio Health QC
- [ ] No account manager holds more active accounts than the defined capacity threshold (~8, verify current policy)
- [ ] CSAT monitored per client; any score below the defined threshold triggers immediate AM follow-up, not passive tracking
- [ ] Renewal radar checked weekly — no contract expiry surprises

## Escalation QC
- [ ] Any client issue beyond the AM's authority routed to the correct escalation tier (see Stage 46) rather than sat on
