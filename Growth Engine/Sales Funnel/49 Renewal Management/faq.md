# FAQ — 49 Renewal Management

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: When does the renewal process start relative to the contract's actual expiry?**
60 days before expiry, with the Account Manager booking a retention call — not at 30 days or later, which the source system treats as already a later-stage step in the sequence.

**Q: What's the primary way to handle renewal price resistance?**
Show ROI clearly before the price conversation — the 45-day ROI-summary email exists specifically to front-load this before the 30-day incentive offer and price discussion.

**Q: How is a renewal incentive chosen?**
Based on the client's LTV, package, and history — not a flat, uniform discount. The source system frames this as choosing between a discount, a bonus service, or an upgrade depending on what best fits that specific client.

**Q: What happens if a client still hasn't renewed by day 14?**
The Account Manager calls directly. If it's a high-value account and still unresolved by day 7, it escalates to the founder for a final retention offer.

**Q: How does this stage relate to Stage 50 Churn Prevention?**
Renewal Management is the structured, scheduled process for contracts approaching their natural expiry date. Stage 50 covers the broader, ongoing churn-risk monitoring (CSAT trends, engagement signals) that can trigger intervention at any point in the client lifecycle, not just near a renewal date.
