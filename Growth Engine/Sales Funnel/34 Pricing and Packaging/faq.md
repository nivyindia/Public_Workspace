# FAQ — 34 Pricing and Packaging

[⬅ Back to README](README.md)

**Q: Why does India pricing differ from International pricing for the same tier?**
The source doc's Pricing Philosophy explicitly segments by market to reflect real purchasing-power differences, not a discount — same scope, different local price point.

**Q: Can a rep quote a price they negotiate on the spot?**
No — reps quote from the published tier catalog. Any deviation (custom Enterprise scope, or a discount beyond the standard threshold) requires founder/named-approver sign-off, per the Discount & Negotiation Policy.

**Q: What's the single biggest pricing-communication mistake to avoid?**
Leading with price before discovery and solution framing — the source doc is explicit that price should always come after the prospect's problem and the proposed solution are established, never before.

**Q: Where does the "never discount below X/hr" floor come from and can it move?**
It's carried over from internal reference material as a starting policy — verify the current floor with leadership before quoting, since it may have been revised since this doc was last updated.

**Q: What happens if a prospect still can't afford any tier after the full escalation ladder?**
Per the source doc: politely decline and add them to 90-day nurture (Stage 24 Follow Up Systems) rather than under-pricing the engagement to force a close.
