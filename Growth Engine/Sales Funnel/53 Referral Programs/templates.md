## Referral Program Qualification Script (internal team framing)
```
Do your existing clients ever refer other businesses to you? And do you
have a formal system to track and reward those referrals?

Referrals are the highest-converting leads any business gets. But
without a formal program, most referrals happen by accident. A
structured program turns word-of-mouth into a predictable channel.
```
