# Checklists — 08 Lead Enrichment

> Part of Stage 08 (Lead Enrichment). See [README.md](README.md) for the full stage overview.

---

## Per-Lead Enrichment Checklist

- [ ] Company size resolved (or marked missing)
- [ ] Industry + sub-industry resolved
- [ ] Tech stack checked where relevant to messaging
- [ ] Revenue estimate resolved where available
- [ ] LinkedIn company URL captured
- [ ] Segmentation tags applied using the fixed taxonomy
- [ ] Enrichment confidence marked per field (tool-confirmed / AI-inferred / missing)

## Conflict Handling Checklist

- [ ] Multi-source conflicts flagged, not silently resolved
- [ ] Implausible values (e.g. revenue estimate wildly out of line with company size) flagged for human review

## Batch-Level Checklist

- [ ] Enrichment completion rate checked against the >80% benchmark
- [ ] Country tag applied to 100% of contacts
- [ ] Service-interest tag applied to >90% of contacts
