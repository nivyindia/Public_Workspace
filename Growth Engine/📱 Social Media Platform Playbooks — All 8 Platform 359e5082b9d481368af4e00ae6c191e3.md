# 💼 1. LINKEDIN {#linkedin}

**Goal:** B2B lead generation + authority building

**Post Frequency:** 5x/week (Company page: 3x | Founder: 3x — some overlap)

## Profile Optimization Checklist

- [ ]  Company page: logo (400x400px), banner (1128x191px), tagline, about section, website URL
- [ ]  Founder profile: professional photo, headline includes keywords ("VA Agency | Digital Growth | Lucknow")
- [ ]  Featured section: link to website, lead magnet, or case study
- [ ]  500+ connections (priority outreach to decision makers)
- [ ]  Weekly Creator Mode posts enabled

## Content Mix (Weekly)

| Day | Content Type | Topic Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Founder insight | "3 things I learned building a VA agency" |
| Tue | Case study snippet | "How we saved a US client 20 hrs/week" |
| Wed | Company update | New service, milestone, team highlight |
| Thu | Industry news take | "Why Indian VAs are dominating in 2026" |
| Fri | Weekly win | This week's results, lessons learned |

## Post Formats That Work

- **Text-only posts** (short, punchy) — highest reach on LinkedIn
- **Carousel PDFs** (5–10 slides) — highest saves
- **Video clips** (60–90 sec) — highest comments
- **Poll posts** — high engagement, algorithm boost
- **Article posts** (1x/week) — SEO + authority

## Automation

- Buffer: schedule posts 1 week in advance
- PhantomBuster: 30 auto-connection requests/day (with personalized note)
- n8n: New connection accepted → auto DM sequence (3 messages over 7 days)

# 📸 2. INSTAGRAM {#instagram}

**Goal:** Brand visibility + client testimonials + trust building

**Post Frequency:** 5x/week (3 feed + 2 Stories)

## Account Setup

- [ ]  Switch to Professional Account → Business
- [ ]  Bio: "Nivy Digital | VA + Marketing Agency 🇮🇳 | Helping businesses grow globally | 👇 Book free strategy call"
- [ ]  Link in bio: Linktree or direct [Cal.com](http://Cal.com) booking link
- [ ]  Profile picture: logo (clean, centered)
- [ ]  Instagram highlights: Services | Results | About Us | FAQ | Testimonials

## Content Mix

| Format | Frequency | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reels (60–90 sec) | 3x/week | "How to hire a VA in 5 steps", behind-the-scenes |
| Carousels (5–10 slides) | 2x/week | Tips, case studies, comparisons |
| Stories | Daily | Polls, Q&A, quick tips, repurposed content |
| Static posts | 1x/week | Quote cards, service spotlights |

## Hashtag Strategy

- Niche: #virtualassistant #VAservices #outsourcing
- Location: #lucknow #indiabusiness #indianentrepreneur
- Audience: #smallbusiness #founders #entrepreneur
- Mix: 5 large (1M+) + 5 medium (100k–1M) + 5 small (<100k)

# 👥 3. FACEBOOK {#facebook}

**Goal:** SEO presence + community + future ads

**Post Frequency:** 3x/week

## Setup Checklist

- [ ]  Facebook Business Page created (fully verified)
- [ ]  Page category: "Marketing Agency" or "Business Service"
- [ ]  About section: full description, website, phone, hours
- [ ]  Facebook Group created: "VA Hiring & Business Automation — Nivy Community"
- [ ]  Messenger auto-reply configured: greeting + [Cal.com](http://Cal.com) link
- [ ]  Facebook Pixel installed on website (for future ads)
- [ ]  ManyChat connected to Messenger

## ManyChat Automation Flows

1. **New page follower** → Auto DM: "Thanks for following! Here's a free resource: [lead magnet link]"
2. **Comment on post** → Auto DM to commenter with relevant resource
3. **Keyword in Messenger** → Auto-response ("price" → pricing info + CTA)

# 🐦 4. TWITTER/X {#twitter}

**Goal:** Brand awareness + SEO backlinks + thought leadership

**Post Frequency:** 5 tweets/week

## Content Types

- Industry stats with commentary
- "Hot takes" on business/VA/outsourcing
- Repurposed LinkedIn text posts (shorten to 280 chars)
- Retweets + comments on influencer posts (gets you visibility)
- Thread posts (1x/week — detailed how-to, performs well)

## Growth Tactics

- Follow 10 ideal clients/day (some follow back)
- Reply meaningfully to top VA/business accounts
- Use trending hashtags in niche: #VirtualAssistant #Outsourcing #Entrepreneur

# 📺 5. YOUTUBE {#youtube}

**Goal:** Long-term SEO engine + trust building

**Post Frequency:** 2 videos/month minimum

## Channel Setup

- [ ]  Channel name: "Nivy Digital" with logo + channel art
- [ ]  Channel description: keywords-rich ("VA agency India, hire virtual assistant, digital marketing")
- [ ]  Playlists: VA Tips | Client Results | Automation Guides | Marketing Tips

## Video Content Plan

| Title | Type | Target Keyword |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "How to Hire a VA in 2026 (Without Getting Burned)" | Education | hire virtual assistant |
| "How We Generated 150 Leads in 30 Days" | Case Study | lead generation agency |
| "Top 5 Tasks to Outsource to a VA" | List | outsource tasks VA |
| "n8n Tutorial: Automate Your Lead Follow-Up" | Tutorial | n8n automation |
| "Why Indian VAs Are the Best Value in 2026" | Opinion | Indian virtual assistant |

## Video Production Workflow

1. Claude drafts script (15 min)
2. Record screen share or talking head (Loom or OBS free)
3. Edit with CapCut (free, mobile) or DaVinci Resolve (desktop)
4. Upload → write description with keywords + links back to website
5. Share clip as Instagram Reel + LinkedIn video same week

# 🔄 CONTENT REPURPOSING SYSTEM {#repurposing}

**One piece of content = 6+ distribution touchpoints:**

```
1 Blog Post (1,500 words)
    ↓
→ LinkedIn long-form post (condensed to 500 words)
    ↓
→ 10 LinkedIn short posts (one insight each)
    ↓
→ 5 Instagram carousel slides
    ↓
→ 1 YouTube video script (talking head or screen share)
    ↓
→ 1 Newsletter section
    ↓
→ 3 Tweets/Threads posts
    ↓
→ 1 Pinterest infographic
```

**AI Workflow:** Claude drafts all repurposed versions from original blog post in one prompt. VA reviews and schedules via Buffer.
