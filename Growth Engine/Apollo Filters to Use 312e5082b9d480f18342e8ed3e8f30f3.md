# Apollo Filters to Use

## 

Here’s how you can set up Apollo filters to find companies in the USA likely to buy ISO certifications:

1. **Location**
    - Country: United States
    - Optional: Narrow by state if targeting industrial hubs (e.g., Michigan, Texas, California)
2. **Industry / Sub-Industry**
    - Manufacturing → Automotive, Aerospace, Electronics
    - Construction & Engineering → Civil Engineering, Infrastructure
    - Healthcare → Pharmaceuticals, Medical Devices, Biotechnology
    - Food & Beverage → Food Production, Packaging
    - IT → Software Development, IT Services, Data Centers
    - Energy → Oil & Gas, Renewable Energy, Utilities
3. **Company Size**
    - Revenue: $5M–$500M (mid to large companies are more likely to invest in certifications)
    - Employees: 50–5000+
4. **Technology & Compliance Tags (if available)**
    - Keywords like: “ISO certified”, “quality management”, “environmental management”, “safety compliance”
    - For IT: “Information Security”, “Data Protection”, “ISO 27001”
5. **Job Titles / Roles** (optional, for decision makers)
    - CEO, COO, Quality Manager, Compliance Officer, Operations Manager, Safety Manager
