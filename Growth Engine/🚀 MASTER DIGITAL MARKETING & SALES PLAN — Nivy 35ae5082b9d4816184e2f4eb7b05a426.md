# 📦 PART 2 — PACKAGE-LINKED MARKETING STRATEGY {#package-strategy}

## How We Market Each Package Level

### 🟢 LEVEL 1 — ENTRY PACKAGES (Website, E-Commerce, Ads Setup, Email Setup)

**Target Client:** Indian SMBs, startups, new businesses, local shops going online

**Price Range:** ₹20,000 – ₹1,20,000 (one-time)

**Marketing Channels:**

- **IndiaMART & JustDial** — Primary inbound for Indian market
- **WhatsApp outreach** — Direct to local businesses
- **Google Local Ads** — "website design Lucknow", "digital marketing agency Lucknow"
- **Instagram Reels** — Before/after website transformations, client wins
- **Facebook Ads** — Target Indian business owners, 28-55, ₹5,000 budget/month

**Sales Approach:**

- Quick pitch (5 min): Show portfolio + price immediately
- Offer a free audit or free consultation
- Close in 1 call — short decision cycle
- Use client testimonials and case studies as primary proof

**Goal:** 4-6 Level 1 projects/month by Month 4

---

### 🔵 LEVEL 2 — GROWTH RETAINERS (SEO, Social Media, Email Marketing, Video, Ads Management)

**Target Client:** Established businesses wanting consistent marketing, international clients needing managed services

**Price Range:** $300 – $2,500/month (International) | ₹15,000 – ₹60,000/month (India)

**Marketing Channels:**

- **LinkedIn outreach** — Target founders, marketing managers, CMOs (international)
- [**Clutch.co](http://Clutch.co) + GoodFirms** — International B2B discovery
- [**Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) cold email** — Target US/UK SMBs and e-commerce brands
- **Content marketing** — Blog posts ranking for "digital marketing agency India"
- **YouTube case studies** — "How we grew [client] from X to Y"
- **Referral program** — Existing clients refer, get 10% off next month

**Sales Approach:**

- 30-min discovery call (use SPIN framework)
- Custom proposal within 24 hours
- 3-tier presentation (Starter/Growth/Scale) — anchor to middle
- 30-day guarantee to reduce risk
- Close cycle: 3-7 days

**Goal:** 5 Level 2 retainer clients by Month 4, 12 by Month 8

---

### 🟣 LEVEL 3 — SYSTEM PACKAGES (Growth Engine, Lead Gen System, Ecom System)

**Target Client:** Fast-growing businesses needing complete systems, e-commerce brands scaling

**Price Range:** $2,000 – $10,000 (range, scoped per client)

**Marketing Channels:**

- **LinkedIn (Founder personal brand)** — Case study posts, industry commentary
- **Webinars/workshops** — "How to build a lead gen machine" (capture high-intent leads)
- **Partnerships** — Business coaches, accountants, law firms referring clients
- **Clutch/Agency Spotter** — International discovery
- **Case study videos** — YouTube + LinkedIn long-form

**Sales Approach:**

- 2-call close: Discovery (problems) → Proposal presentation
- ROI-first proposal with 3-month projection
- Scoped proposal (not fixed price) — range based on audit
- Close cycle: 7-14 days

**Goal:** 2-3 Level 3 projects/quarter by Month 6

---

### 🔴 LEVEL 4 — ENTERPRISE & AI (AI Marketing, AI Sales, Command Center)

**Target Client:** Medium-large businesses, tech companies, businesses with complex automation needs

**Price Range:** $3,000 – $50,000+ (custom)

**Marketing Channels:**

- **Founder LinkedIn + speaking** — Thought leadership on AI in business
- **NASSCOM events & networking** — India enterprise sector
- **Cold outreach — highly targeted** (max 10 prospects/month, fully personalised)
- **Referrals from Level 3 clients**
- **Industry conferences + podcasts**

**Sales Approach:**

- Multi-touch, 3-4 week close cycle
- Executive presentation deck
- Detailed ROI model with CFO-level numbers
- Phased implementation plan to de-risk
- May require NDA before proposal

**Goal:** 1 Level 4 client by Month 6, 3 by Month 12

## HubSpot CRM Setup for The Nivy

**Contact Properties:**

- Name, Email, Phone, Company, Country, City
- Lead Source (website/LinkedIn/WhatsApp/directory/referral/ads)
- Service Interest (VA / Digital Marketing / AI & Automation / Website / E-Commerce)
- Market (India / US / UK / UAE / AU / Other)
- Lead Score (0-100, auto-calculated)
- Package Level (L1/L2/L3/L4)
- Assigned VA / Account Manager
- Last Touch Date

**Lead Scoring Rules:**

- Opened email: +5 points
- Clicked pricing page: +15 points
- Booked a call: +30 points
- Replied to outreach: +10 points
- International client: +20 bonus points
- Watched YouTube video: +8 points

**Automation Rules:**

- Score ≥60: Slack alert to founder immediately
- Source = IndiaMART: Assign to India VA team
- Country = US/UK/AU: Flag as international priority
- No activity 14 days: Trigger re-engagement sequence
