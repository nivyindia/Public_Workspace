# 💡 What To Do If... Emergency

Check this page before escalating to your Supervisor. Most situations are covered here.

---

## 1. I don't know what to do today

Open VA Dashboard — Start Here. Follow the 5 things listed. That is your job.

## 2. A lead is being rude or aggressive

Do not engage further. Copy the conversation. Message your Supervisor immediately.

## 3. I made a mistake in a message I already sent

Do not send a correction right away. Screenshot the mistake. Message your Supervisor with the screenshot. Wait for guidance.

## 4. I can't find an SOP for what I need to do

Check the SOP Master Index in System Engine. If it's not there, flag to Supervisor — it may need to be created.

## 5. My Supervisor is not responding

Check the Deputy Protocol page in Supervisor Portal. That page names who covers when Supervisor is absent.

## 6. I accidentally sent a message to the wrong lead

Do not send another message. Screenshot both. Message Supervisor immediately.

## 7. A lead wants a quote or pricing

Do not give pricing. Say: "Let me connect you with our specialist for that." Then book the call or hand off.

## 8. I don't understand a package

Open Package Intelligence Hub — Tier 2. Find the package. If still unclear, ask Supervisor.

## 9. My daily target seems wrong

Check with Supervisor. Do not assume. Targets are set weekly.

## 10. I need to be absent today

Message your Supervisor before your shift starts. Do not just disappear.

## 11. I found a lead that looks very big (enterprise)

Do not pitch. Flag to Supervisor immediately with the lead details. This may need a Manager or Closer.

## 12. A lead asked something I don't know how to answer

Say: "Great question, let me check on that and get back to you." Then find the answer in the KB or SOP, or ask Supervisor.

## 13. My Notion access broke

Message Supervisor via WhatsApp or alternate channel. Do not wait.

## 14. I think a lead is ready to buy but won't book a call

Use the objection-handling SOP (SOP-VA-007). If still stuck, escalate to Supervisor.

## 15. I finished all my tasks and have time left

Go back to training. Open your current stage in VA Trainee Path and study the next module.
