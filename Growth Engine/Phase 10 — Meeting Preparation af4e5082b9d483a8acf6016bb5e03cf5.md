# Phase 10 — Meeting Preparation

## 

This phase ensures you **enter the discovery call fully prepared** so you can understand the prospect’s business and present your service confidently. Good preparation increases your **closing chances significantly**. 🎯

---

# Step 1 — Research the Client Company

Before the meeting, review the prospect’s business.

Places to check:

- Their **company website**
- Their profile on LinkedIn
- Company page on LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Business listings on Google

Look for:

- What products/services they offer
- Target market
- Current marketing strategy
- Company size

This helps you **speak specifically about their business**.

---

# Step 2 — Identify Their Problems

Try to spot potential problems before the call.

Examples:

Marketing issues:

- Low lead generation
- Weak online presence
- Poor social media engagement
- No advertising strategy

Business issues:

- Limited brand awareness
- Inconsistent sales pipeline
- Low website conversion

Write **2–3 possible problems** you want to discuss.

---

# Step 3 — Prepare Your Service Pitch

Prepare a **short explanation of your service**.

Structure:

Problem → Solution → Result

Example:

> Many companies struggle with consistent lead generation.
> 
> 
> We help businesses generate qualified leads using targeted advertising campaigns.
> 

Keep it **simple and clear**.

---

# Step 4 — Prepare Your Portfolio

Have proof ready.

Examples of proof:

- Case studies
- Client results
- Before-and-after metrics
- Testimonials

You can show these during the meeting using:

- Zoom screen share
- Google Meet presentation

---

# Example Portfolio Items

Include things like:

- Campaign results
- Client revenue growth
- Lead generation numbers
- Marketing performance improvements

Example:

> Helped a SaaS company increase leads by 40% in 3 months.
> 

---

# Simple Pre-Meeting Checklist

Before the call:

✔ Research their business

✔ Identify possible problems

✔ Prepare service explanation

✔ Prepare portfolio examples

✔ Test your meeting software

Meeting platforms commonly used:

- Zoom
- Google Meet
- Microsoft Teams

---

# Why Preparation Matters

Without preparation:

📉 Generic conversation

📉 Low trust

📉 Lower conversion

With preparation:

📈 Better understanding of the client

📈 More relevant solutions

📈 Higher closing rate
