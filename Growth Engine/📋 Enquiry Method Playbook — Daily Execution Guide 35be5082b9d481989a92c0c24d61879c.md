## 🌍 What Is the Enquiry Method?

The Enquiry Method is a proactive outbound approach where you **position yourself as a buyer looking for the right supplier** — rather than a seller pitching your services. You post "looking for" style messages across 6 platforms. People who fit what you're describing respond, DM you, or self-identify. You then qualify them and route them into your pipeline.

**Why it works:** It flips the social dynamic. Instead of "we offer X", you say "looking for X" — this triggers self-selection. Only people who match respond.

### 📧 EMAIL OUTREACH — Enquiry-Style Subject Lines

These go with your cold email sequence in Instantly. The enquiry-style approach uses subject lines that feel like a request rather than a pitch.

**Subject line formulas:**

- "Looking for [service type] in [industry/location]"
- "Quick question about [their industry]"
- "Referral needed — [service]"
- "Is [COMPANY] taking on new clients?"
- "Who handles [X] at [COMPANY]?"

**Subject line examples:**

- `Quick question about your bookkeeping setup`
- `Looking for a marketing agency in [City] — recommendation?`
- `Who handles digital marketing at [Company]?`
- `Is [Company] open to a free audit?`
- `Referred to you by [mutual connection or source]` (if applicable)

**Email body template (enquiry style):**

```
Hi [First Name],

I came across [Company] while looking for [type of business]
in [location/industry].

We’ve been helping [industry] businesses [specific result] —
wondering if this is something that’s on your radar.

Not a sales pitch — just want to understand if there’s a fit.
Would a 15-minute call this week work?

[Your name]
```

## 💬 DM Qualification Script

When someone responds to an enquiry method post, use this sequence:

**Message 1 (within 5 mins of response):**

*"Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out! Just to make sure I can point you in the right direction — what type of business do you run and what's the main challenge you're trying to solve right now?"*

**Message 2 (after they answer):**

*"That makes a lot of sense. We’ve actually helped [similar type of business] with exactly this. Would it be worth a quick 15-minute call to see if we can do the same for you? I have slots [Day] and [Day] this week."*

**Message 3 (if they say yes):**

*"Great! Here’s my booking link — pick whatever time works: [[Cal.com](http://Cal.com) link]. See you then!"*

**If they go cold after Message 1:** Follow up after 24 hours: *"Just checking back in — did you get a chance to think about it?"*
