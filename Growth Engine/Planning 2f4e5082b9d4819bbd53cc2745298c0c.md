# Planning

This is the **exact fork where most founders fail**, so I’ll be very clear and honest.

### Short answer

👉 **STOP planning. START working — but in a controlled way.**

Yes, **you can make money**, but **only if you switch from planning → execution now**, with a **small, test-first approach**.

Planning more will **not** increase your chances meaningfully anymore. Execution will.

---

## 1️⃣ SHOULD YOU STOP PLANNING?

### Yes — for **strategy-level planning**

You have already:

- Defined the model
- Defined roles
- Defined incentives
- Defined scaling logic
- Identified risks

Doing more planning now gives **diminishing returns**.

### No — for **execution-level assets**

You still need to build **a few execution tools**, not new ideas.

---

## 2️⃣ THE CORRECT MOVE NOW (THIS IS CRITICAL)

### You do **NOT** “launch the business”

You run a **controlled pilot**.

Think like this:

> “I am testing whether money flows, not building an empire yet.”
> 

---

## 3️⃣ THE 14–30 DAY MONEY TEST (REALISTIC & SAFE)

### 🎯 Goal

- Close **1–2 real deals**
- Prove success-fee model
- Validate micro-agency + deal facilitation works

If you do this → money will come.

If not → you pivot early.

---

### WEEK 1: LOCK & PREP (NO MORE THINKING)

**Decisions (1 day max):**

- Choose **ONE industry**
- Choose **ONE layer**
- Choose **ONE geography**

**Build (3–4 days):**

- Simple website / deck (credibility only)
- Success-fee trigger rule
- Deal ID sheet
- Outreach rules (1 page)

No perfection. Just “clear”.

---

### WEEK 2: MARKET CONTACT (THIS IS REAL WORK)

- Onboard **ONE micro-agency**
- Give them:
    - Script
    - ICP
    - Outreach rules
- Start outreach immediately
- You personally validate first responses

No scaling. Just signal.

---

### WEEK 3–4: DEAL FLOW & CLOSURE

- Qualify responses
- Match **real demand + supply**
- Facilitate discussions
- Push toward a clear trigger (PO / contract / invoice)

Even **one paid deal** proves everything.

---

## 4️⃣ WILL YOU MAKE MONEY “PROPERLY”?

### Honest answer:

- **Not big money in first 30 days**
- But **real money is possible** (and likely)

If you:

- Pick the right industry
- Avoid being broad
- Stay disciplined

You’re not gambling — you’re testing.

---

## 5️⃣ WHEN YOU SHOULD STOP IF IT FAILS

You are NOT allowed to drag this forever.

### Kill rules:

- 30 days → zero serious conversations = pivot industry
- 60 days → no closed deal = change approach
- 90 days → no revenue = pause and reassess

This protects your time and energy.

❌ Not lack of planning

❌ Not lack of money

### ⚠️ The real risk:

**Staying in thinking mode because it feels productive and safe.**

Execution feels uncomfortable — that’s normal.
