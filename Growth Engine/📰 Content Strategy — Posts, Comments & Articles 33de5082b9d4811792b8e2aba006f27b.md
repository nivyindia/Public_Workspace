# 📰 Content Strategy — Posts, Comments & Articles

> Use this system to post consistently and build LinkedIn authority. Consistent posting makes prospects more likely to accept your connection request and reply to messages.
> 

---

## Posting Rules

- Post **3–5 times per week** — not every day, consistency over volume
- Comment on **5–10 posts per day** (founders, CEOs, agencies, roofing/HVAC companies, consultants)
- Publish **2 articles per month** — long-form builds search ranking
- Share **2 lead magnet posts per month**

**Content ratio to avoid over-promotion:**

- 40% Education
- 30% Insights / Trends
- 20% Case studies
- 10% Direct service promotion

## Comment Strategy

Comment daily on posts from your target audience. LinkedIn rewards active commenters with increased profile visibility.

**Comment types that work best:**

**Insight comment** — add a relevant data point or observation

> *"Great insight. Many businesses underestimate how automation can streamline operations. We've seen companies save 10–20 hours weekly by implementing workflow automation and CRM systems."*
> 

**Value comment** — add something the original post missed

> *"This is very true. One challenge we often see is businesses focusing only on lead generation without optimizing internal systems like CRM and financial reporting."*
> 

**Conversation comment** — ask a genuine question

> *"Interesting perspective. Do you think AI will replace traditional bookkeeping or simply enhance accounting processes?"*
> 

**Authority comment** — share a relevant experience

> *"Many growing companies eventually reach the stage where outsourcing accounting and automation becomes necessary to scale efficiently."*
>

## Article Writing Guide

Publish 2 articles per month. Articles rank in LinkedIn search and on Google, positioning you as an expert.

**Article topic ideas:**

| Article Title | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| The Future of Accounting: How AI & Automation Are Changing Bookkeeping | Nivy Advisory |
| Why Outsourced Accounting Is the Smartest Move for Growing Businesses | Advisory |
| How Service Businesses Can Build Predictable Lead Generation Systems | Nivy Next |
| The Complete Guide to Business Automation for Service Companies | Next |
| Cloud Accounting vs Traditional Accounting: What Businesses Should Know | Advisory |
| How Agencies Can Scale Operations with Outsourcing & Automation | Both |
| AI + Human Strategy: The Future of B2B Lead Generation | Next |
| 10 Financial Metrics Every Founder Should Track | Advisory |

**Article structure:**

1. Headline — SEO + curiosity (e.g. *"How AI Is Transforming Accounting for Modern Businesses"*)
2. Introduction — describe the problem founders face
3. Main sections — 3–5 insights with subheadings
4. Conclusion — summarize key takeaways
5. CTA — *"At Nivy, we help businesses streamline accounting, automation, and digital growth. Feel free to connect."*
