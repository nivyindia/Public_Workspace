# 📦 Service Delivery & Scope

---

### **Nivy Tech Service Delivery & Scope Management Policy**

**Policy Name:** Service Delivery & Scope Management Policy
**Effective Date:** 26 August 2025
**Version:** 1.0

**1.1 Purpose**
This policy establishes clear guidelines for project scope, deliverables, revisions, and change management to ensure transparent, efficient, and high-quality service delivery for our clients. It defines the responsibilities of both Nivy Tech and the Client to prevent misunderstandings and ensure project success.

**1.2 Scope**
This policy applies to all client projects and ongoing retainer agreements undertaken by Nivy Tech.

---

### **Policy 1: Clear Scope of Work (SOW)**

**1.1 Definition of SOW**
Every project or retainer engagement must be governed by a mutually signed Scope of Work (SOW) document. The SOW is the single source of truth and will explicitly detail:

- **Project Objectives:** The specific, measurable goals of the engagement.
- **Deliverables:** A precise list of all tangible outputs (e.g., 5 blog posts, 1 website landing page, 1 monthly performance report).
- **Included Services:** A detailed description of the tasks Nivy Tech will perform to create the deliverables (e.g., keyword research, copywriting, design, implementation, distribution).
- **Excluded Services:** A clear list of services, tasks, or deliverables that are not included in the agreement. Common exclusions include:
    - Website hosting fees and domain purchases.
    - Premium stock photography, font, or software licenses.
    - Third-party advertising spend (e.g., Google Ads budget).
    - Content creation or modifications outside the agreed number of revisions.
    - Work on platforms or assets not explicitly named in the SOW.

**1.2 SOW Approval**
No work will commence until the Client has reviewed and digitally signed the SOW. The signed SOW constitutes agreement to the defined scope, inclusions, and exclusions.

---

### **Policy 2: Revision Policy**

**2.1 Included Revisions**
To ensure client satisfaction, each deliverable includes a set number of revision rounds. A "revision round" is defined as the Client providing consolidated feedback on a delivered asset, which Nivy Tech then implements.

- **Standard Deliverables (e.g., ad copy, social media graphics):** Include **two (2) revision rounds**.
- **Major Deliverables (e.g., website wireframes, brand style guides, video scripts):** Include **three (3) revision rounds**.
- The specific number of revisions for each deliverable will be stated in the SOW.

**2.2 Process**

1. Client receives the first draft of the deliverable.
2. Client provides clear, consolidated feedback within five (5) business days.
3. Nivy Tech implements the feedback and returns the revised deliverable.
4. This cycle repeats until the included revision rounds are exhausted.

**2.3 Additional Revisions**
Requests for revisions beyond the included number will be treated as a Change Request (see Policy 4) and may incur additional fees based on the time required.

---

### **Policy 3: Turnaround Time for Deliverables**

**3.1 Standard Timelines**
Nivy Tech is committed to delivering high-quality work within reasonable timeframes. Standard turnaround times commence after all necessary assets, information, and approvals have been received from the Client.

- **Initial Project Kick-off:** Scheduled within 3-5 business days of SOW signing.
- **Website Landing Page (from approved wireframe):** 10-15 business days.
- **Blog Post (800-1000 words):** 5-7 business days.
- **Social Media Content Calendar (Monthly):** Delivered by the 25th of the preceding month.
- **Monthly Performance Reports:** Delivered by the 5th business day of the following month.

*Note: Specific turnaround times for all deliverables will be clearly outlined in the project timeline within the SOW.*

**3.2 Rush Requests**
Requests for deliverables that require a turnaround time shorter than the standard period will be considered "Rush Requests." Nivy Tech will accommodate these requests subject to team availability and a **15-25% rush fee** on the cost of the deliverable, which must be pre-approved by the Client.

---

### **Policy 4: Change Request Policy**

**4.1 Definition of a Change Request**
A Change Request is any modification to the agreed SOW that introduces new deliverables, tasks, or significant alterations to existing deliverables after the SOW has been signed. This includes:

- Requests for additional revisions beyond the included number.
- Adding new pages to a website project.
- Requesting a new service not included in the original SOW (e.g., adding email marketing to a social media management retainer).

**4.2 Change Request Process**

1. **Request:** The Client submits a formal Change Request via email to their Account Manager.
2. **Assessment:** Nivy Tech assesses the impact of the request on the project timeline, resources, and budget.
3. **Quotation:** Nivy Tech provides the Client with a written Change Order detailing the additional cost (if any) and the revised timeline.
4. **Approval:** Work on the change will only commence after the Client has provided written approval (email suffices) of the Change Order.
5. **Implementation:** Upon approval, the Change Order is integrated into the project plan, and work proceeds.

**4.3 Scope Creep**
Nivy Tech defines "scope creep" as the accumulation of small, unapproved changes that expand the project scope without a formal Change Request. Our team is trained to identify and communicate potential scope creep. Any work falling outside the signed SOW that is performed without an approved Change Order is not billable.
