## Month 3, Week 1 — Run Paid Ads for Nivy

**You've been doing organic outreach. Now it's time to add paid traffic. Start with Meta ads (Facebook + Instagram).**

**Budget to start: 5,000-10,000/month for Nivy's own ads. This should generate 10-20 leads/month if done right.**

**What to do:**

1. Go to [business.facebook.com](http://business.facebook.com)
2. Create a Meta Business account if you don't have one
3. Create an Ad Account under your Business account
4. Install the Meta Pixel on your website (if you have one) or skip for now
5. Your first ad campaign:
    - Campaign objective: Lead Generation
    - Audience: Business owners in India, age 25-45, interests: entrepreneurship, small business, marketing
    - Ad format: Single image or short video (15-30 seconds)
    - Ad copy: "Are you a business owner spending money on marketing but not seeing results? We audit your entire marketing for FREE and show you exactly what's missing. 100+ businesses helped. Book your free audit: [Calendly link]"
    - Budget: 300-500/day to start
    - Destination: Your Tally form OR Calendly link directly
6. Run for 7 days without changing anything
7. After 7 days, check which ad is performing best and pause the others

## Month 3, Week 2 — Set Up [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io) for Outbound Prospecting

**What is [Apollo.io](http://Apollo.io)?** It is a database of business contacts. You can search for specific types of businesses, get their email addresses, and send them automated cold email sequences. Free plan gives you 50 email credits/month.

**What to do:**

1. Go to [apollo.io](http://apollo.io)
2. Click "Sign up free"
3. Sign up with your business email
4. Once inside, click "Search" > "People"
5. Use these filters to find your ideal clients:
    - Location: India (or specific cities)
    - Job title: Founder, CEO, Owner, Director
    - Industry: Choose your target niches (restaurants, coaching, e-commerce, etc.)
    - Company size: 1-50 employees (small businesses)
6. Click "Search" — you'll see hundreds of potential clients
7. Select 50 contacts per week (free plan limit)
8. Click "Save to sequence"
9. Create a cold email sequence:
    - Email 1 (Day 1): Short intro + one specific observation about their business + ask if they'd want a free audit
    - Email 2 (Day 3): Follow up with a case study or specific result
    - Email 3 (Day 7): Final follow-up, short and direct
10. Activate the sequence

**You now have automated outbound running every week finding new leads.**

## Month 3, Week 3 — Launch the Referral Program

**Your existing clients are your best salespeople. Make it easy and rewarding for them to refer.**

**What to do:**

1. Create a Tally referral form with these fields:
    - Your name
    - Your WhatsApp number
    - Who you are referring (name + WhatsApp + business type)
    - Why you think they need Nivy
2. Set the form redirect to a thank-you page: "Thank you! Once your referral becomes a client, you'll receive 3,000 cash or one month's service credit (your choice). We'll contact you within 24 hours of them signing."
3. Write a WhatsApp message to send to each existing client:

"Hi [Name]! We love working with you and wanted to share something exciting. We've just launched Nivy's referral program. If you know any business owner who needs better marketing results, refer them to us and earn either 3,000 cash or one free month of services when they sign up. Here's your referral link: [Tally form link]. If you have any questions, just ask! And thank you for being such a great client."

1. Send this to every active client
2. Add a referral program reminder to the footer of every weekly report email

## Month 3, Week 4 — Your First Hire

**By now you should have 3-5 clients. That is too much for one person to deliver perfectly. Time to hire your first team member.**

**Who to hire first:** A Virtual Assistant (VA) or Delivery Specialist who can handle content creation, scheduling, or basic campaign management.

**Where to find them:**

- Internshala ([internshala.com](http://internshala.com)) — great for affordable freshers
- LinkedIn Jobs — post a free job listing
- [Naukri.com](http://Naukri.com) — free listings
- Your own network — ask if anyone knows a good social media person

**What to pay:** 8,000-15,000/month for a fresher VA. 15,000-25,000 for someone with 1-2 years experience.

**What they handle:**

- Creating and scheduling client content posts
- Basic reporting (pulling numbers from Meta and GA4)
- Client WhatsApp message drafts (you approve before sending)
- Admin tasks (invoicing, follow-up logs)

**You then focus on:** Sales calls, client relationships, strategy, and business development.

## Month 4 — Add WATI for WhatsApp Automation

**WATI is a WhatsApp Business API platform. It lets you send automated WhatsApp messages to clients — weekly updates, renewal reminders, CSAT surveys. Cost: Free tier available, paid from $40/month.**

**Only add this when you have 5+ clients. Before that, personal WhatsApp is enough.**

**What to do:**

1. Go to [wati.io](http://wati.io)
2. Click "Get started"
3. Sign up with your business email
4. Connect your WhatsApp Business number (follow their setup wizard — takes about 30 minutes)
5. Create your first broadcast template:
    - Name: Weekly Client Update
    - Message: "Hi {{1}}! Here's your Nivy update for this week. {{2}} Your next report will be sent on {{3}}. Any questions? Just reply here!"
    - Submit for WhatsApp approval (takes 24-48 hours)
6. In n8n, connect WATI and trigger this message every Friday for each active client

## Month 4-5 — Upgrade to GoHighLevel (Optional)

**GoHighLevel (GHL) is an all-in-one CRM + email + WhatsApp + funnel + booking platform built specifically for marketing agencies. Cost: $97/month.**

**Only consider this when:**

- You have 10+ clients
- You have a team member managing it
- HubSpot + Brevo + Tally feels messy and disconnected

**What GHL replaces:** HubSpot + Brevo + Tally + Calendly + some of n8n (all in one place)

**How to set it up:**

1. Go to [gohighlevel.com](http://gohighlevel.com)
2. Start the 14-day free trial
3. Watch the onboarding tutorial videos they provide (2-3 hours total)
4. Import your HubSpot contacts (export from HubSpot as CSV, import to GHL)
5. Set up your pipeline (same 7 stages as your HubSpot pipeline)
6. Move your email sequences from Brevo into GHL workflows
