# ❓ FAQ & Troubleshooting

> If you are stuck on something, check here first before messaging your supervisor. Most common problems are answered below.
> 

---

## 📘 SECTION 1 — LINKEDIN PROBLEMS

**Q: LinkedIn says I’ve reached my weekly invitation limit. What do I do?**

Stop sending connection requests immediately. This is LinkedIn’s automated protection. Wait 5–7 days before resuming. In the meantime, switch your outreach to LinkedIn DMs to existing connections, WhatsApp, or Email. Report this to your supervisor the same day it happens.

**Q: My LinkedIn account has been restricted or flagged. What do I do?**

Do not panic. Do not create a new account. Report to your supervisor immediately. Follow LinkedIn’s appeal process if prompted. Avoid all outreach from that account until the restriction is lifted. Your supervisor will redirect your targets to another platform while you wait.

**Q: My connection acceptance rate is very low. Nobody is accepting.**

Check two things: (1) Is your profile fully set up with a photo, headline, and about section? Incomplete profiles get ignored. (2) Are you personalizing your connection request notes? Generic requests get declined. Go back to Section 5 of Stage 1 and follow the profile setup guide.

**Q: I am getting connections but nobody replies to Message 1. What’s wrong?**

This usually means your Message 1 is too long, too salesy, or too generic. Go back to the Outreach Script Library and use the exact template. Make sure you are replacing {niche} with something specific to that person’s industry.

## 📱 SECTION 2 — WHATSAPP & OTHER PLATFORM PROBLEMS

**Q: WhatsApp is not delivering my messages to some contacts.**

This means either: (1) the number is not on WhatsApp, (2) the contact has blocked you, or (3) you have hit WhatsApp’s daily messaging limit. Stop for the day if you suspect it’s the limit. Mark those leads as “No Response” temporarily and try again after 48 hours.

**Q: Instagram is not letting me send DMs to new accounts.**

Instagram restricts new accounts from mass DMs. Start with 10 DMs/day maximum for your first week, then slowly increase. If your account is flagged, stop for 24–48 hours and resume at a lower volume.

**Q: A prospect on Facebook asked me not to contact them again. What do I do?**

Apologize briefly, remove them from your outreach list immediately, and update their CRM status to “No Response” with a note: “Requested no further contact.” Never message them again.

## 📊 SECTION 3 — CRM & REPORTING PROBLEMS

**Q: I forgot to update the CRM for 2 days. What do I do now?**

Go back and update it now. Reconstruct from your message history on each platform. It will take some time but it must be done. Going forward, update after every action — not at the end of the day.

**Q: I don’t know what to put in the Notes column for a lead.**

Write a one-line summary of the most recent exchange. Examples: “Said they handle leads via referrals, open to ideas.” or “Agreed to receive free plan, waiting for response.” or “Not interested, asked not to follow up.”

**Q: I submitted my report late. Will I be marked absent?**

Reports submitted after 7:00 PM but before midnight are counted as late — not absent. Reports not submitted at all are marked absent. Three absences in a row triggers a review. If you know you will be late or miss a day, message your supervisor before 10:00 AM that day.

**Q: My daily message count seems low compared to my targets. How do I improve?**

First check: Are you spending too long on lead research before messaging? Try batching — spend 30 minutes finding leads, then 60 minutes messaging, then 30 minutes on follow-ups. Do not research and message at the same time.

## 💬 SECTION 4 — LEAD & OUTREACH PROBLEMS

**Q: Nobody is replying. What do I do?**

This is normal in Week 1. The reply rate across all platforms averages 10–20%, meaning 80–90% of people will not reply to your first message. Keep going. Check: (1) Are your messages personalized? (2) Are you targeting the right niche? (3) Are you following up? Most replies come from follow-ups 2–5, not Message 1.

**Q: A lead replied but I don’t know what to say next.**

Open the Outreach Script Library and find the matching situation (positive reply, objection, DIY response, etc.). If their reply is unusual and not covered, describe it to your supervisor and ask for guidance before responding.

**Q: A lead said yes to a call but I don’t have a calendar link. What do I do?**

Message your supervisor immediately to get the Calendly or booking link. Do not ask the lead to “wait” for more than a few hours — warm leads go cold fast.

**Q: Can I message the same lead on multiple platforms?**

Yes — but space it out. If you messaged on LinkedIn on Day 1, you can follow up on Email on Day 3–4. Do not message on LinkedIn and WhatsApp on the same day to the same person — it feels like spam.

**Q: Can I message a lead a second time after they said “not interested”?**

Not for at least 4–6 weeks. After that, you can send one very gentle re-engagement message with a completely different angle. Never argue with or push a lead who has declined.

**Q: A lead asked me to call them directly. What do I do?**

Do not call without supervisor approval. Respond with: “I’d love to connect you with our team directly — let me send you a quick booking link so we can get the right person on the call.” Then send the Calendly link.

## 💰 SECTION 5 — CALLS & HANDOVER PROBLEMS

**Q: I booked a call but the lead didn’t show up. What do I do?**

Wait 5–10 minutes. Then send a WhatsApp or LinkedIn message: “Hey {Name} — just checking if we’re still on for our call? Happy to reschedule if now isn’t a good time.” Update CRM status to “No Show” and set a follow-up for 2 days later. Report it in your daily report.

**Q: A lead on the call started asking detailed questions about pricing. What should I say?**

You are the appointment setter, not the closer. Say: “Great question — our specialist will walk you through the exact pricing based on your specific situation on this call.” Then pass to the closer immediately.

**Q: I forgot to fill the handover form before the call happened. What do I do?**

Fill it immediately after the call from memory and conversation notes. Send it to your supervisor. Going forward: fill the handover form within 30 minutes of booking a call, not right before it.

## 🛡️ SECTION 6 — ACCOUNT & SECURITY PROBLEMS

**Q: I think my account was hacked or accessed by someone else. What do I do?**

Change your password immediately. Enable two-factor authentication. Report to your supervisor right away. Do not continue outreach from that account until you confirm it is secure.

**Q: Someone is threatening me or being aggressive in messages. What do I do?**

Do not engage or respond. Screenshot the conversation. Block the contact. Update CRM status to “No Response”. Report to your supervisor with the screenshot.

## 👥 SECTION 7 — TEAM & ESCALATION

**Q: Who do I contact if I have a problem?**

| Issue | Who to Contact | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CRM access / broken links | Your supervisor | WhatsApp message |
| Account restriction | Your supervisor | WhatsApp message, same day |
| Aggressive or threatening lead | Your supervisor | WhatsApp message + screenshot |
| Technical issue (Notion, sheet) | Your supervisor | WhatsApp message |
| Not sure how to reply to a lead | Your supervisor | WhatsApp message with screenshot of conversation |
| Health / absence | Your supervisor | WhatsApp message before 10:00 AM |

**Q: How do I escalate something to senior management?**

All escalations go through your supervisor first. If your supervisor does not respond within 24 hours, you may message the next level up. Do not skip levels.

**Q: What happens if I consistently miss targets?**

Below 50% of KPI targets for one week = formal warning and coaching session. Below 50% for two weeks = performance review. Targets are not a punishment — they are the baseline that makes the system work.
