## Why This Matters

The most common mistake new VAs make is sending outreach about services they don't fully understand. When a prospect replies and asks a simple question — "what exactly does your accounting service include?" or "how is your automation different from Zapier?" — a vague or confused answer kills the conversation instantly.

You don't need to be an expert. You need to understand enough to have a natural conversation, identify if someone needs the service, and move them toward the call. The closer handles everything after that.

## Service 1 — Accounting & Bookkeeping (Nivy Advisory)

### What is it?

Most businesses — especially small and medium ones — generate transactions every day: sales, expenses, payroll, invoices, bank transfers. Someone needs to record all of this accurately, organise it by category, reconcile it with bank statements, and turn it into reports. This is bookkeeping.

Accounting goes further: tax filing, financial forecasting, CPA review, and strategic financial advice.

Nivy Advisory does both — as an outsourced service. The client doesn’t hire an accountant. They pay Nivy a monthly fee and get a dedicated accounting team.

### Why do businesses buy it?

- Hiring a full-time in-house accountant costs $40,000–$80,000/year in the US. Nivy costs $300–$3,000/month.
- Many small business owners are doing their own books badly or not at all — creating tax problems they don’t see coming.
- Tax season becomes extremely stressful without clean books maintained throughout the year.
- Investors and banks require accurate financial statements.

### Pain signals to listen for in outreach:

- "We’re behind on our books"
- "Tax season is a nightmare for us"
- "Our accountant quit / retired"
- "We’re growing and our current setup isn’t keeping up"
- "We use QuickBooks but nobody really manages it properly"

### One-sentence pitch:

> *"We help businesses maintain clean, compliant financial records without the cost of hiring a full-time accountant — starting from $300/month."*

## Service 2 — Digital Marketing (Nivy Next)

### What is it?

Digital marketing is how businesses get found online and generate inbound customers. It covers:

- **SEO** — making a business’s website appear in Google search results
- **Paid Ads** — running targeted ads on Google and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) to get immediate traffic
- **Social Media Management** — creating and posting content on LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook
- **Lead Funnels** — building a system that captures website visitors’ contact information and follows up automatically
- **Content Marketing** — blogs, videos, and posts that position the business as an expert

Nivy Next manages all of this for clients who don’t have the skills or time to do it themselves.

### Why do businesses buy it?

- Most businesses know they need a better online presence but don’t know how to build one.
- Paid ads can generate fast results but require expertise to run profitably — most businesses waste money on ads without a specialist.
- SEO takes time but generates free traffic long-term — valuable for any business that wants to reduce dependence on referrals.

### Pain signals to listen for:

- "We rely mostly on referrals"
- "We tried Google ads but lost money"
- "We don’t have time to post on social media"
- "Our competitors keep showing up above us on Google"
- "We need more leads but don’t know where to start"

### One-sentence pitch:

> *"We manage the full digital marketing operation for service businesses — from ads and SEO to social media — so they generate consistent inbound leads without doing it themselves."*

## Service 4 — AI & Business Automation (Nivy Next)

### What is it?

Automation is removing manual, repetitive work by connecting business tools and creating workflows that run themselves.

Examples of what automation can do:

- When a new lead fills in a contact form → automatically add to CRM, send welcome email, notify the sales team, and create a follow-up task — all without anyone touching it
- Generate weekly sales reports automatically from CRM data
- Send invoice reminders automatically when payment is overdue
- Route customer service tickets to the right team member based on keywords
- Sync data between accounting software and project management tools

Nivy builds these systems using tools like n8n, Zapier, Make, and custom integrations.

### Why do businesses buy it?

- Growing businesses get bogged down in repetitive admin that takes hours every day.
- Manual processes create errors — things get missed, data gets duplicated.
- Staff spend time on work that a tool could do in seconds.

### Pain signals to listen for:

- "We do a lot of manual data entry"
- "Things fall through the cracks in our follow-up process"
- "We use [X tool] and [Y tool] but they don’t talk to each other"
- "Our team spends hours every week on reporting"
- "We want to scale but can’t keep hiring for every task"

### One-sentence pitch:

> *"We automate the repetitive manual processes that are eating your team’s time — most clients save 10–20 hours a week within 30 days."*

Trained, supervised VA teams that handle specific business tasks for clients: lead generation, outreach, data research, appointment setting, admin support.

This is essentially what you are doing right now — being sold as a service to businesses that need the same kind of output but don’t want to recruit and train their own VA team from scratch.

### Why do businesses buy it?

- Training and managing VAs takes time that most business owners don’t have.
- Nivy provides trained VAs with a supervisor and quality control already in place.

### Pain signals to listen for:

- "We need help with outreach but don’t have time to train someone"
- "We need someone to do lead research / data entry / appointment setting"
- "We’re growing fast and need more hands"
- "Hiring locally is too expensive for this kind of work"

### One-sentence pitch:

> *"We provide trained VA teams with built-in supervision and quality control — businesses get the output of a full outreach or admin team at a fraction of the cost of hiring locally."*
