# 🎧 Support & Communication Guide

### Channels of Communication

- **Primary Communication:** Email ([support@offdigital.in](mailto:support@offdigital.in)) for all formal requests, approvals, and documentation
- **Quick Updates:** WhatsApp Business account (+91 9876543210) for time-sensitive matters and quick clarifications
- **Project Management:** Dedicated client portal with messaging system for project-specific communication and file sharing
- **Campaign Reporting:** Monthly video conference calls via Zoom or Google Meet for performance reviews
- **Emergency Contact:** Direct phone line for urgent matters requiring immediate attention

### Working Hours & Response Time Policy

- **Standard Business Hours:** Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST
- **Response Time Commitments:**
    - Emails: Within 4 business hours during working hours
    - WhatsApp messages: Within 2 business hours during working hours
    - Client portal messages: Within 4 business hours during working hours
    - Phone calls: Immediate if available; callback within 1 hour if missed
    - After-hours emergency contact: Response within 2 hours for critical issues
- **Out-of-Office Protocol:** Automatic notifications with alternative contact information during holidays, team retreats, or planned absences
- **Weekend Support:** Available for Premium tier clients and active campaigns requiring weekend monitoring
