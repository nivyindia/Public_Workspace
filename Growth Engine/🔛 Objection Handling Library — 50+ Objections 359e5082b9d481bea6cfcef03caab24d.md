# 📖 HOW TO USE THIS LIBRARY {#how-to-use}

**The Golden Rule:** Never argue. Always acknowledge → reframe → question back.

**Framework:** **ARP**

- **Acknowledge:** "That's completely fair / I understand that concern"
- **Reframe:** "Here's a different way to look at it..."
- **Probe:** "What would need to be true for you to move forward?"

---

# 💰 PRICE OBJECTIONS {#price}

**"It's too expensive"**

> "That's fair — let me ask: compared to what? If a VA saves you 20 hours a week, and your time is worth even $50/hour, that's $4,000/month in value for $600. Does that math work for you?"
> 

**"I can hire a local VA for less"**

> "Absolutely you could. The difference is our VAs come trained, managed, and with SOPs on day one. Most clients who hired cheaper options elsewhere came to us after 2–3 rounds of re-hiring and training. What's your time worth per re-hire?"
> 

**"Can you lower the price?"**

> "I want to make this work for you. Let me ask — is it the total investment that's the concern, or the monthly cash flow? We have a Starter package at $[X] that covers the core tasks. Would that be a better starting point?"
> 

**"I don't have budget right now"**

> "Totally understand. When would budget open up? Many clients start on our minimum package to see ROI first, then upgrade. Would $[starter price] be more workable to start?"
> 

**"I'll wait until business picks up"**

> "Here's the thing — most clients tell us business picks up *because* they hired a VA, not the other way around. The VA handles tasks that were blocking growth. What's one thing you'd do differently if you had 15 more hours this month?"
>

# 🤝 TRUST & CREDIBILITY OBJECTIONS {#trust}

**"I've never heard of Nivy Digital"**

> "Fair — we're a growing agency. Here's what I can do: I'll share 2–3 case studies from clients in your industry, and you can speak with one of them directly. Would that help?"
> 

**"How do I know your VAs are qualified?"**

> "Great question. Every VA goes through a [X]-step vetting: skills test, English assessment, tool certification, and trial period. And we assign a dedicated account manager — so you're never working with an unmanaged freelancer. Want to see the hiring rubric?"
> 

**"I had a bad experience with a VA before"**

> "I hear that a lot, and I'm sorry that happened. The most common issues are: unclear brief, no accountability, and no replacement guarantee. We solve all three: we write the brief with you, your VA reports daily, and we replace for free if it's not working. Does that address what went wrong last time?"
> 

**"You're based in India — is the quality good?"**

> "Our VAs work with US, UK, and Australian clients every day, in English, using the same tools you use. Our [industry] clients see response times under 2 hours and quality on par with US-based VAs at 60% of the cost. Would it help to do a 1-week paid trial before committing?"
>

# ⏰ TIMING OBJECTIONS {#timing}

**"Now's not a good time"**

> "Completely understand — when would be better? And what would make the timing right? I want to make sure we pick this back up when it makes sense for you."
> 

**"I need to think about it"**

> "Of course! What specifically would be helpful to think through? I'd rather answer any remaining questions now than have you sitting with uncertainty. Is it the price, the process, or something else?"
> 

**"Let me talk to my partner/team first"**

> "Absolutely. Would it help if I joined that conversation — even for 10 minutes — so I can answer their questions directly? Or I can send a one-pager you can share with them."
> 

**"I'm too busy to onboard right now"**

> "That's actually exactly when our clients say they needed us most! Our onboarding takes 2 hours of your time total. The VA handles the rest. Would a 2-hour investment to get back 20 hours/week make sense?"
>

# 🎯 COMPETITION OBJECTIONS {#competition}

**"We're already using [competitor]"**

> "That's great — what do you love about them? [listen] And is there anything you wish was different? [listen] Based on what you just described, we might actually be a good fit for [the gap they mentioned]."
> 

**"I found someone cheaper on Upwork"**

> "Upwork is great for one-off projects. Where clients find the difference is in consistent daily work — managed, accountable, and with SOPs. Freelancers disappear; our VAs are your team. Would it help to compare total cost of ownership over 3 months?"
> 

**"We do this in-house"**

> "That's smart. What tasks are still falling through the cracks despite having in-house support? Often our clients use us for overflow or for specialized tasks their in-house team isn't best suited for."
>

# ⚠️ QUALITY & RISK OBJECTIONS {#quality}

**"What if the VA doesn't work out?"**

> "We replace at no charge within 30 days if it's not a fit. We also provide a 7-day trial period before you commit. You have very little to lose and a lot of time to gain."
> 

**"How do I manage someone remotely?"**

> "Most clients spend 30–45 minutes/week managing their VA — via a daily update Slack message and a weekly 15-min check-in. We set up the system for you. You don't need to be a manager to benefit."
> 

**"What about confidentiality / data security?"**

> "All VAs sign NDAs before starting. We use secure shared tools (Google Workspace, LastPass for passwords, no direct client data access unless needed). Want me to share our security protocol document?"
>

# 📜 COMMITMENT OBJECTIONS {#commitment}

**"I don't want a long-term contract"**

> "We operate on month-to-month after the first month. No lock-in, no penalty for leaving. We earn your continued business every month."
> 

**"What's the minimum commitment?"**

> "Our minimum is 1 month, with a 2-week notice to cancel after that. You can start with as little as [X] hours/week to test the fit."
>

**"Your prices are high for India"**
