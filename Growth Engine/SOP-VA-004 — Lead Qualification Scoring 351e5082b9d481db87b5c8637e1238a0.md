# SOP-VA-004 — Lead Qualification Scoring

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who Uses This:** VA Intern, VA Executive
> 

---

## Purpose

To apply a consistent 5-point scoring system to every lead before outreach — ensuring VAs only send messages to leads with a genuine chance of converting.

---

## Pre-requisites

- KB-001 (What is a Lead) read and understood
- Lead has been added to CRM with basic info filled in

---

## The 5-Point Qualification Score

Score each lead out of 5. Give 1 point for each criterion met:

| # | Criterion | 1 Point If... |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Decision maker** | Contact is Founder / CEO / Owner / Director |
| 2 | **Right company size** | Company has 1–50 employees |
| 3 | **Right market** | Based in US, UK, Canada, AU, or UAE |
| 4 | **Pain signal visible** | Their profile, website, or post shows a pain Nivy solves |
| 5 | **Reachable** | Has a LinkedIn URL, email, phone, or Instagram DM access |

---

## Scoring Interpretation

| Score | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 5/5 | Priority lead — outreach today |
| 4/5 | Good lead — outreach this week |
| 3/5 | Borderline — add to CRM, flag for Manager review |
| 1–2/5 | Disqualified — do not add to CRM |

---

## Hard Disqualifiers (Auto-Fail — Score = 0, Do Not Add)

If any of these apply, do not score — skip immediately:

- They are a student, intern, or job seeker
- They are outside our 5 markets
- They are a large enterprise (500+ employees)
- They are already in the CRM
- They are clearly a competitor

---

## Step-by-Step

**Step 1:** Open the lead’s profile (LinkedIn, website, or Google Maps listing)

**Step 2:** Score against all 5 criteria. Fill the "Qualification Score" column in CRM.

**Step 3:** Add notes: 1 line explaining the pain signal or why they scored low.

**Step 4:** Set priority:

- Score 5 → Tag as "Priority"
- Score 4 → Standard outreach queue
- Score 3 → Flag for Manager
- Score ≤2 → Do not enter CRM

---

## Output Standard

- Every lead in CRM has a qualification score filled in
- Every 3/5 lead is flagged for Manager review before outreach
- Zero 1/5 or 2/5 leads in the active outreach queue

---

## Common Mistakes

- Skipping the scoring and doing outreach anyway — this wastes time and message limits
- Scoring 3/5 leads as 4/5 to hit targets — inflated scores lead to bad outreach
- Not flagging 3/5 leads for Manager review — the Manager decides, not the VA

---

## QC Checklist

- [ ]  Every CRM entry has a qualification score
- [ ]  Hard disqualifiers checked before scoring
- [ ]  All 3/5 leads flagged for Manager
- [ ]  No 1–2/5 leads in outreach queue
