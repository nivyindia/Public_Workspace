Since your goal is **global client acquisition for digital marketing + accounting services**, the decision is mainly about **speed vs control**.

You basically have **3 execution models**:

| Model | How It Works | Pros | Cons | When To Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Country-by-Country Expansion** | Pick 1 country → build outreach → get clients → move to next | High focus, easier learning | Slow scaling | Small teams |
| **Multi-Country Parallel Outreach** | Target 8–15 countries simultaneously with same outreach system | Fast growth, more chances of deals | Needs system + team | Best for scalable agencies |
| **Test & Scale Model (Recommended)** | Test many countries quickly → double down on top performing ones | Balanced risk + speed | Requires tracking system | Best for your case |

For your case (**Nivy Empires global growth system**), the **Test & Scale model is the best**.
