## 🗓️ MONTHLY DELIVERY CYCLE

### WEEK 1 — ONBOARDING & PLANNING (Day 1–5)

**Day 1 — Client Onboarding (New Clients Only)**

- [ ]  Send Client Onboarding Form (collect: brand tone, target audience, content pillars, colours, do-not-post list, login credentials)
- [ ]  Confirm platform access: Instagram, Facebook Business Manager, Meta Business Suite
- [ ]  Add client to internal tracking sheet
- [ ]  Confirm content approval process with client (WhatsApp/email, turnaround time)

**Day 2–3 — Content Planning**

- [ ]  Create monthly content calendar (use template: 4 content pillars, rotate weekly)
- [ ]  Content pillars (default unless client specifies): Educational, Promotional, Behind-the-Scenes, Engagement/CTA
- [ ]  Plan post types: static graphic, carousel, reel, story poll
- [ ]  Submit calendar to Supervisor for review before sending to client

**Day 4–5 — Client Calendar Approval**

- [ ]  Send content calendar to client for approval
- [ ]  Chase once at 24 hrs if no reply
- [ ]  If no reply at 48 hrs → escalate to Supervisor
- [ ]  Log approval status in tracking sheet

---

### WEEK 2–3 — CONTENT PRODUCTION (Day 6–21)

**Graphic Post Creation**

- [ ]  Use approved brand kit (colours, fonts, logo — collected in onboarding)
- [ ]  Tool: Canva (use client's brand template)
- [ ]  Each post: graphic + caption + 3–5 hashtags
- [ ]  Caption structure: Hook → Value/Story → CTA
- [ ]  Batch-create all posts for the week in one sitting (do not create day-by-day)

**Reel Creation**

- [ ]  Script reel (15–30 sec) using Hook → Value → CTA format
- [ ]  Edit reel: captions ON, trending audio (check Reels audio trends weekly), transitions
- [ ]  Export at 1080×1920 (9:16 ratio)
- [ ]  Add reel to approval batch

**Content Approval**

- [ ]  Bundle all posts for the week into one approval folder (Google Drive or WhatsApp)
- [ ]  Send for client approval with clear labels: Post 1 (Mon), Post 2 (Wed) etc.
- [ ]  Revisions: max 1 round of revisions per post. Second revision = flag to Supervisor.

---

### WEEK 2–4 — SCHEDULING & PUBLISHING (Ongoing)

**Scheduling**

- [ ]  Use Meta Business Suite to schedule all approved posts
- [ ]  Best posting times (defaults): Instagram: 7–9am, 12–2pm, 7–9pm IST / Facebook: 9–11am, 1–3pm IST
- [ ]  Adjust based on client's audience insights after Month 1
- [ ]  Never publish without client approval

**Daily Engagement (30–60 min per client)**

- [ ]  Reply to all comments within 4 hours
- [ ]  Reply to DMs within 2 hours (business hours)
- [ ]  Like + comment on 5–10 posts from ICP accounts (build visibility)
- [ ]  Log engagement actions in daily tracker

---

### WEEK 4 — REPORTING & RENEWAL (Day 25–30)

**Analytics Compilation (L2 Growth only)**

- [ ]  Pull monthly data from Meta Business Suite: reach, impressions, engagement rate, follower growth, best-performing post
- [ ]  Fill Monthly Report Template (attach below)
- [ ]  Write 2–3 line insight summary: what worked, what to change next month

**End-of-Month Review**

- [ ]  Submit completed monthly report to Supervisor for QC
- [ ]  Supervisor sends to client (VA does not send directly unless authorised)
- [ ]  Flag any client issues, requests, or feedback to Supervisor
- [ ]  Prepare next month's content calendar draft (start Day 28)

---

## ✅ QUALITY CONTROL CHECKLIST (Supervisor Use)

Before approving any content batch for client delivery:

- [ ]  Brand colours and fonts match client brand kit
- [ ]  No spelling or grammar errors in any caption
- [ ]  CTA present in every post (follow, comment, book, click, DM)
- [ ]  Hashtags relevant (no banned hashtags, no irrelevant tags)
- [ ]  Reels: captions visible, audio trending, ratio correct (9:16)
- [ ]  Calendar submitted to client on Day 4 (not later)
- [ ]  Approval confirmed before any post is scheduled
- [ ]  Report submitted by Day 28 (L2 only)

## ⚠️ ESCALATION RULES

| Situation | Action | Who Handles | Client not approving calendar after 48 hrs | Escalate to Supervisor immediately | Supervisor contacts client |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client requesting work outside package scope | Do NOT deliver. Flag to Supervisor. | Supervisor upsells or declines | Negative comment / PR issue on client page | Do NOT respond. Screenshot + escalate within 1 hr. | Supervisor handles response |
| Platform access lost or restricted | Stop all work. Notify Supervisor within 30 min. | Supervisor coordinates with client | VA unable to complete deadline | Notify Supervisor 48 hrs before deadline, not after. | Supervisor reassigns or extends |
