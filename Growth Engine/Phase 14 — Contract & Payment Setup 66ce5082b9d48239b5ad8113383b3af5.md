# Phase 14 — Contract & Payment Setup

## 

**Goal:** Secure the agreement legally and collect the **first payment** so the project can officially begin. This step converts a **verbal yes into a formal client**. 💼

---

# Step 1 — Send Service Agreement / Contract

After the client agrees, send a formal contract for signature.

Common tools used:

- DocuSign
- Dropbox Sign

The contract protects both sides and defines the **working relationship**.

Key contract sections usually include:

- Scope of services
- Payment terms
- Project duration
- Termination clause
- Confidentiality agreement

---

# Step 2 — Send Invoice or Payment Link

Once the contract is signed, send the **payment request**.

Common payment tools:

- Stripe
- PayPal
- Wise

Typical payment structures:

**Option 1 — Monthly Retainer**

Client pays every month.

**Option 2 — Upfront Deposit**

Example:

- 50% upfront
- 50% after milestone

**Option 3 — First Month in Advance**

Common for marketing or VA services.

---

# Step 3 — Receive Initial Payment

Work **should not begin until payment is received**.

Typical payments include:

- First month retainer
- Setup fee
- Project deposit

Once payment is received, mark the deal as **Closed Won** in your CRM.

---

# Step 4 — Confirm Onboarding Start Date

After payment, confirm when the project will start.

Example message:

> Thanks for completing the agreement and payment.
> 
> 
> We’ll begin onboarding on **Monday** and send you the onboarding questionnaire shortly.
> 

This creates **clear expectations**.

---

# Key Documents Required

Every client project should have these documents.

### 1. Service Agreement

Defines legal relationship and responsibilities.

### 2. Statement of Work (SOW)

Specifies:

- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Project scope

### 3. Invoice

Shows payment details including:

- Amount due
- Payment method
- Due date

---

# Example Contract → Payment Flow

Discovery Call

→ Client agrees

→ Contract sent

→ Contract signed

→ Invoice sent

→ Payment received

→ Onboarding begins

---

# Best Practices

✔ Always use a **written contract**

✔ Collect **payment before starting work**

✔ Store documents in a shared folder (Google Drive or CRM)

✔ Confirm the onboarding schedule immediately
