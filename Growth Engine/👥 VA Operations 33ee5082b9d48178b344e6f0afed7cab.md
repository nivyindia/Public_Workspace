## 🔄 The VA System — End-to-End Flow

Every person in this system is part of one revenue engine. Understanding the full flow helps everyone play their part correctly.

```
RECRUIT → ONBOARD → TRAIN (8 Stages / 3 Months) → DEPLOY → SUPERVISE → PROMOTE or EXIT
```

---

## 📊 VA Lifecycle — Phase Breakdown

| Phase | Timeline | Owned By | Key Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🎯 Recruitment | Ongoing | Hiring Manager | Candidates screened and placed into training |
| 📋 Onboarding | Before Day 1 | Supervisor | Tools set up, WhatsApp group joined, CRM access granted |
| 📚 Training — Stages 1 to 4 | Days 1 to 28 | Supervisor + VA (self-learning) | VA running outreach independently |
| 🔗 Integration — Stage 5 | Days 29 to 60 | Supervisor | VA fully integrated into team workflow |
| 📊 Performance — Stage 6 | Days 45 to 90 | Supervisor + QC Officer | Consistent KPIs, message quality assured |
| 🌟 Specialisation — Stage 7 | Month 3+ (approval required) | Manager Approval | VA focused on highest-output activity |
| 🏆 Leadership — Stage 8 | Merit-based only | Founder / Ops Head | VA managing their own team of VAs |

## 📐 Team Structure & Reporting Lines

| Role | Reports To | Manages | Max Span |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🧑‍💻 VA Trainee | Supervisor | Nothing — focus on output | N/A |
| 👔 Supervisor / QC Officer | VA Manager | VA Trainees | Maximum 10 VAs per supervisor |
| 🗂️ VA Manager | Founder / Ops Head | Supervisors | 3 to 5 supervisors |
| 📈 Founder / Ops Head | N/A | All teams | Strategic oversight only |
