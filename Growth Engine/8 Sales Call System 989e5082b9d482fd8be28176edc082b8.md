# 8. Sales Call System

## 

### Purpose of Sales Call System

- Convert booked calls into paying clients
- Diagnose the client’s current situation
- Present a clear growth path
- Close without aggressive selling

This is where **revenue is generated**.

---

### 15-Minute Call Structure

**Total Duration: 10–15 minutes**

**Step 1 — Warm Start (2 min)**

- Build rapport
- Understand business basics

> “Tell me a bit about your roofing business.”
> 

---

**Step 2 — Discovery (4–5 min)**

- Understand current lead flow
- Identify current marketing efforts

---

**Step 3 — Gap Identification (4–5 min)**

- Show what they are missing
- Compare current vs potential

---

**Step 4 — System Pitch (2–3 min)**

- Introduce 6D Growth Engine
- Keep it high-level

---

**Step 5 — Close (1–2 min)**

- Ask for decision

---

### Discovery Questions

Ask simple, direct questions:

- How many leads do you get per month?
- Where do most of your leads come from?
- Are you getting leads from Google consistently?
- What’s your average job value?

Goal:

- Understand baseline
- Identify weaknesses

---

### Gap Identification

Structure:

1. Current Situation

> “You’re getting around 10 leads/month”
> 
1. Market Opportunity

> “In your area, there’s demand for 30–50 leads/month”
> 
1. Gap

> “So there’s a clear gap of missed opportunities”
> 

Psychology:

- Make problem visible
- Create urgency without pressure

---

### Pitch Using 6D Framework

Keep it simple:

> “We use a system called the **6D Roofing Growth Engine™** that helps roofing companies generate consistent leads.”
> 

Briefly mention:

- Local visibility (Google)
- Trust (reviews & proof)
- Referrals
- Content
- Conversion system

Rule:

- Do NOT explain deeply
- Keep it high-level and structured

---

### Closing Techniques

Use soft closes:

- “Would you like help implementing this?”
- “Should we set this up for your business?”
- “Want us to handle this for you?”

If interested:

- Move directly to onboarding/payment

---

### Objection Handling Basics

**Objection: Price**

> “Totally fair — most clients see ROI within a few jobs, so it usually pays for itself quickly.”
> 

---

**Objection: Need to think**

> “Of course — what part are you unsure about?”
> 

---

**Objection: Not interested**

> “No problem — if things change, happy to help in the future.”
> 

---

Rule:

- Stay calm
- Do not push aggressively
- Guide conversation logically

---

### Key Rules (CRITICAL)

- Keep call short and focused
- Let client talk more
- Do not overload with information
- Always guide toward decision

---

### Output of This System

- 20–40 calls/month
- 15–30% close rate
- 3–10 clients/month
