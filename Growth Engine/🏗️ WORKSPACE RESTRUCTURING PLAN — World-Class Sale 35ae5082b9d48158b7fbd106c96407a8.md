- Month 3: ₹1,50,000/month + $1,000 international
- Month 6: ₹3,00,000/month + $3,000 international
- Month 12: ₹10,00,000/month + $20,000 international

- Level 1 (₹20-120K one-time) → IndiaMART, JustDial, Google Ads, Instagram Reels
- Level 2 (₹15-60K/month) → LinkedIn outreach, Clutch, cold email, content marketing
- Level 3 ($2-10K systems) → LinkedIn founder brand, webinars, partnerships
- Level 4 ($3-50K AI/enterprise) → NASSCOM, thought leadership, referrals from L3

**Online Channels (World-Class System — 5 Layers):**

1. Foundation: Website, Google Business, Email, WhatsApp Business, [Cal.com](http://Cal.com)
2. Social: LinkedIn (5x/week), Instagram (4x/week), Facebook, YouTube, Twitter, Pinterest, Threads, WhatsApp
3. Directories: 35+ platforms (Clutch, GoodFirms, IndiaMART, JustDial, Upwork, Trustpilot...)
4. Content Machine: 2 blogs/month, 1 YouTube/week, daily social, weekly newsletter
5. Automation Layer: n8n connecting all sources → HubSpot CRM → nurture sequences

**Sales System:**

- 7-stage HubSpot pipeline
- 100 outreach touchpoints/day per VA
- 7-touch follow-up sequence (14 days)
- 30-min discovery call framework (SPIN)
- 30-day satisfaction guarantee as trust builder
