# 🧭 VA Trainee Path

Your 8-stage journey from new joiner to autonomous VA. Each stage has a clear milestone. You move to the next stage when you hit the milestone — not based on time.

---

## Stage 1 — System Orientation

Goal: Know where everything is.

Milestone: Day 0 Setup Checklist completed. VA Dashboard bookmarked.

Focus: VA Dashboard, VA Quick Reference Card, VA Daily Workflow Checklist.

---

## Stage 2 — Product Basics

Goal: Know what you're selling at a surface level.

Milestone: Can explain Level 1 and Level 2 packages in one sentence each.

Focus: Tier 1 — Know What You're Selling (Package Cheat Sheet + Package Ladder).

---

## Stage 3 — Outreach Begins

Goal: Send your first 10 outreach messages using the SOP.

Milestone: 10 outreach messages sent and logged. Zero errors on SOP compliance.

Focus: SOP-VA-005 (LinkedIn DM Outreach), SOP-VA-002 (WhatsApp Outreach), SOP-VA-003 (Cold Email Outreach), KB-004 (Products Quick Reference).

> ⚠️ Note: SOP-VA-001 is LinkedIn Profile Setup — complete that in Stage 1/Day 0, not here.
> 

---

## Stage 4 — Follow-Up Mastery

Goal: Manage follow-ups without being reminded.

Milestone: 0 missed follow-ups in a 5-day window. All tracked in daily log.

Focus: SOP-VA-009 (Follow-Up Sequence), SOP-VA-008 (Positive Reply Flow), VA Daily Workflow Checklist.

---

## Stage 5 — First Booking

Goal: Book your first call.

Milestone: 1 call booked and handed off cleanly to closer.

Focus: SOP-VA-010 (Appointment Setting), SOP-VA-014 (Call Handover), SOP-VA-007 (Objection Handling).

---

## Stage 6 — Consistent Output

Goal: Maintain consistent outreach + bookings for 2 weeks.

Milestone: 2-week average meets team target (set by Supervisor). Weekly scorecard green.

Focus: My Scorecard, Tier 2 packages (look up any package when needed).

---

## Stage 7 — Sales Intelligence

Goal: Begin upselling and cross-selling.

Milestone: 1 upsell or cross-sell identified and handed to closer.

Focus: Tier 3 — How to Sell & Upsell. Package Differentiator Guide. Upsell Playbook.

---

## Stage 8 — Autonomous VA

Goal: Run your workflow without supervision.

Milestone: 30 consecutive days with green scorecard and zero escalations.

Reward: Progression review with Manager. Eligible for VA Executive title and/or incentive upgrade.

---

## Memory Aid

Orient → Know → Outreach → Follow-Up → Book → Consistent → Sell → Autonomous
