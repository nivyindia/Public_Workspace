## 🎯 PURPOSE

This SOP governs the **build and management** of lead generation systems for clients. A lead generation package is NOT just a landing page — it is the complete system from traffic → capture → nurture → booking. Every VA delivering this package must build the full system, not just one component.

> ⚠️ **Hard rule:** Do not mark a lead gen project as complete until the WhatsApp/email automation is live and tested with a real lead submission. A landing page without automation is an incomplete delivery.

## 🗓️ STARTER L1 — FUNNEL BUILD DELIVERY (One-Time, 7–10 Days)

### Day 1–2 — Client Brief & Setup

- [ ]  Send Lead Gen Brief Form: collect offer (what are we promoting?), target audience, desired CTA (book a call / download / sign up / get quote), WhatsApp number for automation, email for automation, any existing brand assets (logo, colours, photos)
- [ ]  Confirm platform: use [**Systeme.io**](http://Systeme.io) (free tier) as default. Only use alternatives if client has existing tool.
- [ ]  Confirm domain: use client’s existing domain, or set up free subdomain on [Systeme.io](http://Systeme.io)
- [ ]  Create project folder: `/Client Name/Lead Gen/`

### Day 3–4 — Landing Page Build

- [ ]  Build landing page on [Systeme.io](http://Systeme.io) using this structure:
    - **Section 1 — Hero:** Headline (outcome-focused, max 10 words) + sub-headline + CTA button
    - **Section 2 — Problem:** 3 bullet points of what the client’s audience struggles with
    - **Section 3 — Solution:** What you get (3–5 benefit bullets)
    - **Section 4 — Proof:** Testimonial or result (if client provides) or credibility statement
    - **Section 5 — Form:** Name + Phone/WhatsApp + Email. Max 3 fields. Do NOT add more.
    - **Section 6 — CTA repeat:** Button at bottom
- [ ]  Thank-you page: “Thanks! We’ll be in touch within 24 hours.” + WhatsApp button
- [ ]  Mobile responsiveness check: view on mobile before submitting
- [ ]  Page load speed check: must load in under 3 seconds (test on GTmetrix or PageSpeed Insights)
- [ ]  Submit page preview to Supervisor for QC before going live

### Day 5–6 — WhatsApp Automation Setup

- [ ]  Tool: **WhatsApp Business** auto-reply OR **ManyChat** (if client has account)
- [ ]  Immediate welcome message (fires within 60 seconds of form submit):
    
    ```
    Hi [Name]! 👋 Thanks for your interest in [Offer].
    We’ve received your details and will reach out within 24 hours.
    In the meantime, here’s what to expect: [1-line next step].
    — [Client Name] Team
    ```
    
- [ ]  Test: submit a test lead → confirm WhatsApp message fires within 60 seconds
- [ ]  Log test confirmation in tracker

### Day 6–7 — Email Automation Setup

- [ ]  Tool: [**Systeme.io**](http://Systeme.io) email (built-in) or **Mailchimp** free tier
- [ ]  Welcome email (fires immediately on form submit):
    - Subject: “We got your request, [Name]!”
    - Body: Thank you + what happens next + contact details
- [ ]  Confirm email is landing in inbox (not spam) — test with 2 different email addresses

### Day 8–9 — Full System Test & Launch

- [ ]  End-to-end test: submit a real test lead through the live form
    - [ ]  Landing page loads correctly
    - [ ]  Form submission works
    - [ ]  Thank-you page appears
    - [ ]  WhatsApp welcome message fires within 60 seconds
    - [ ]  Welcome email arrives in inbox (not spam)
    - [ ]  Lead appears in tracking sheet or CRM
- [ ]  Supervisor QC sign-off before sharing with client
- [ ]  Send client the live URL + WhatsApp/email confirmation
- [ ]  Send client a 1-page “How Your Funnel Works” summary

## 🔄 GROWTH L2 — MONTHLY FUNNEL MANAGEMENT CYCLE

### WEEK 1 — PERFORMANCE REVIEW (Day 1–5)

- [ ]  Pull last month’s funnel data: visitors, form submissions, conversion rate (submissions ÷ visitors), lead quality (how many booked a call)
- [ ]  Benchmark: conversion rate should be 15–30% for a warm audience funnel. Under 10% = problem.
- [ ]  Identify drop-off: are visitors not converting? Headline problem. Are leads not booking? Nurture problem.
- [ ]  Write Month N+1 optimisation plan: submit to Supervisor

### WEEK 2 — FUNNEL OPTIMISATION (Day 6–12)

- [ ]  A/B test: change ONE element (headline, CTA button text, hero image, form field count)
- [ ]  Update WhatsApp sequence (add follow-up message Day 2 if no reply)
- [ ]  Update email sequence (Growth): review open rates, improve subject lines if under 25% open rate
- [ ]  Check all automation still firing correctly (test monthly)

### WEEK 3–4 — LEAD NURTURE & REPORTING (Day 13–28)

**5-Message WhatsApp Nurture Sequence (Growth L2)**

- Message 1 (Day 0): Welcome (immediate)
- Message 2 (Day 1): Value message — tip or insight related to their problem
- Message 3 (Day 3): Social proof — result or testimonial
- Message 4 (Day 5): Soft pitch — “Ready to get started? Book a call here: [link]”
- Message 5 (Day 7): Last follow-up — “Just checking in — any questions?”

**5-Email Nurture Sequence (Growth L2)**

- Email 1 (Day 0): Welcome + what to expect
- Email 2 (Day 2): Teach something valuable (1 tip)
- Email 3 (Day 4): Case study or result
- Email 4 (Day 6): Offer + CTA (book a call)
- Email 5 (Day 9): Final follow-up

**Monthly Lead Report (Day 28)**

- [ ]  Fill Monthly Lead Report Template: total leads, conversion rate, best traffic source, nurture sequence open/response rates, leads converted to calls/sales
- [ ]  Recommendations for next month
- [ ]  Submit to Supervisor by Day 28

## ⚠️ ESCALATION RULES

| Situation | Action | Who Handles | WhatsApp automation not firing after testing | Flag to Supervisor immediately. Do not launch. | Supervisor + VA troubleshoot together |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client wants custom domain or complex integrations | Flag to Supervisor. May require scope change. | Supervisor quotes separately | Conversion rate under 5% after 2 weeks | Flag to Supervisor. Propose headline/CTA test. | Supervisor + VA optimise together |
| Client sharing funnel URL before VA has completed setup | Flag immediately. Pause sharing until tested. | Supervisor contacts client | Lead data privacy concern (client storing leads incorrectly) | Flag to Supervisor immediately. | Supervisor advises client on data handling |
