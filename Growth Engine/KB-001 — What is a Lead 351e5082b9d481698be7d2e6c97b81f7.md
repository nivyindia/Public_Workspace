# KB-001 — What is a Lead

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

---

## What is a Lead?

A lead is a **person or business that could realistically become a Nivy client** — based on what they do, how they operate, and whether they have a problem Nivy solves.

A lead is NOT just anyone with a LinkedIn profile or a phone number. A lead must pass a basic qualification check before entering the CRM.

---

## Qualified vs Unqualified Lead

|  | Qualified Lead ✅ | Unqualified Lead ❌ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Business type** | Runs a real business (SME, agency, consultant, service provider) | Freelancer with no team, student, job seeker |
| **Revenue signal** | Has clients, active social presence, or runs ads | No visible business activity |
| **Decision maker** | Owner, founder, director, or C-suite | Employee with no buying authority |
| **Market** | US, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE | Outside Nivy's target markets |
| **Problem fit** | Likely needs accounting, IT, marketing, or automation | Already has a full in-house team for all services |

---

## ICP — Ideal Customer Profile

Nivy's ICP (the type of lead we want most):

- **Business size:** 1–50 employees (SME, growing startup, boutique agency)
- **Role of contact:** Founder / CEO / Director / Head of Operations
- **Industry:** Professional services, e-commerce, real estate, tech, consulting, healthcare admin
- **Pain signals:** Talking about growth, hiring, compliance, scaling, saving time, or being overwhelmed
- **Geography:** US, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE

---

## Hard Disqualifiers — Skip Immediately

Do NOT add to CRM if any of these apply:

- [ ]  They are a job seeker, student, or intern
- [ ]  They are based in a country outside our 5 markets
- [ ]  They have no visible business (no website, no LinkedIn company page, no posts about their business)
- [ ]  They are a large enterprise (500+ employees) — not our target
- [ ]  Duplicate — already in CRM
- [ ]  Clearly a competitor

## Real Examples

**✅ Qualified Lead:**

> Sarah Thompson — Founder, Bloom Digital Marketing, Sydney. 8-person agency. Posts about client results on LinkedIn. No visible accountant or finance team mentioned.
> 

**❌ Unqualified Lead:**

> James Chen — Looking for remote work opportunities. Open to new roles. Recently graduated.
> 

**✅ Qualified Lead:**

> Ravi Patel — CEO, TechSpark Solutions, Dubai. Runs a 15-person IT company. Recent post: "Scaling is breaking our back-office processes."
> 

**❌ Unqualified Lead:**

> Global Corp Inc — Fortune 500 company. 10,000 employees. Has a full finance division.
> 

---

## Common Mistakes

- Adding leads just to hit the number — quantity without qualification wastes outreach budget
- Adding someone because they have a large following — followers ≠ business owner
- Adding leads outside our markets — time zone and legal compliance issues
- Not checking for duplicates — VAs must search CRM before adding any lead

---

## QC Before Adding to CRM

Before entering any lead, ask:

1. Is this person a decision maker at a real business?
2. Are they in one of our 5 target markets?
3. Are they in the right company size range?
4. Are they already in the CRM?
5. Do they have at least one pain signal I can reference in outreach?

If the answer to any question 1–4 is No → **Do not add. Skip the lead.**
