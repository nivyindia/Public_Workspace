# 🎯 Lead Magnets — Templates & Post Examples

> Lead magnets convert profile visitors and cold leads into warm conversations. Use them in your Featured section, in posts, and as part of your outreach scripts.
> 

---

## How to Use Lead Magnets

- **Featured section** — add the guide as a PDF or link so profile visitors can download it
- **Posts** — create a post offering the lead magnet (see post example below)
- **DM outreach** — mention the free resource in your follow-up messages to warm up cold leads

---
