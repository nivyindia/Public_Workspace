# 🧬 What is a Lead Magnet? {#what}

A lead magnet is a **free, high-value resource** offered in exchange for an email address or contact detail. It:

- Solves a specific problem your ICP has
- Demonstrates your expertise immediately
- Starts the relationship before any sales conversation
- Feeds your email nurture sequence

## 📋 Lead Magnet Creation Template

For each lead magnet, define:

- **Title:** Specific, benefit-driven, numbered if possible
- **Format:** PDF, Notion doc, video, checklist, template
- **ICP:** Who is it for? What's their pain?
- **Page count/length:** Keep it actionable, not long (5–12 pages max for PDF)
- **Call to action:** What do we want them to do after?
- **Delivery:** Via email autoresponder (HubSpot/Mailchimp)
