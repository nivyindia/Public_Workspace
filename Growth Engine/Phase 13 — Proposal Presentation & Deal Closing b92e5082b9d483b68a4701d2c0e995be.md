# Phase 13 — Proposal Presentation & Deal Closing

Your **Phase 13 — Proposal Presentation & Deal Closing** is already good. Below is a **clean, operational version** you can add to your SOP so a **closer or sales rep can actually execute it during the call**.

---

## 

**Goal:** Convert a qualified prospect into a **paying client** by presenting a clear solution and securing commitment.

---

# Step 1 — Review Client Goals

Start by confirming what you learned during the discovery call.

Example:

> “Just to recap, your main goal is to generate more qualified leads for your business while reducing reliance on referrals. Is that correct?”
> 

Purpose:

- Shows you listened
- Confirms understanding
- Builds trust

---

# Step 2 — Present the Solution Clearly

Explain **how your service solves their problem**.

Structure:

**Problem → Solution → Result**

Example:

> Based on what you shared, the biggest issue is inconsistent lead flow.
> 
> 
> Our approach would be to implement a structured outreach system that generates qualified prospects consistently.
> 

Focus on **outcomes**, not just tasks.

---

# Step 3 — Explain Deliverables & Timeline

Tell the client **exactly what they will receive**.

Example:

Deliverables:

- Lead research & prospecting
- LinkedIn outreach management
- CRM tracking
- Appointment setting

Timeline example:

Week 1: Setup and prospect list building

Week 2: Outreach campaign launch

Week 3–4: Lead conversations and meeting bookings

Clarity **reduces hesitation**.

---

# Step 4 — Present Pricing Packages

Offer **1–3 options** to make decision easier.

Example:

**Starter Package**

- Outreach management
- 20 connections/day
- Basic reporting

**Growth Package**

- Outreach + CRM management
- 30 connections/day
- Lead qualification
- Weekly reporting

**Scale Package**

- Full outreach team
- Lead research + outreach + appointment setting
- Advanced reporting

Giving options increases **close rate**.

---

# Step 5 — Handle Objections

Common objections:

**Price**

> “We’re not sure about the budget.”
> 

Response:

> I understand. Many clients initially felt the same way until they saw the cost of missed opportunities from inconsistent lead flow.
> 

---

**Timing**

> “Now may not be the right time.”
> 

Response:

> Totally fair. Out of curiosity, when would be a better time to start improving your lead generation?
> 

---

# Step 6 — Ask for Commitment

Always **ask directly**.

Example closing question:

> Based on what we discussed, would you like us to move forward and start implementing this for you?
> 

Alternative:

> Would you prefer starting with the Growth package or the Starter package?
> 

---

# Key Elements Every Proposal Must Include

Your proposal should clearly show:

**Problem Summary**

What challenge the client is facing.

**Proposed Solution**

How your service solves it.

**Scope of Work**

Specific tasks included.

**Timeline**

Expected implementation stages.

**Pricing**

Package options and cost.

**Expected Outcomes**

What results they should expect.
