# 📤 Outreach System

## Purpose of Outreach System

- Convert raw leads into **conversations**
- Create curiosity and get permission to send value
- Move prospects from cold → interested → call

---

### Platform-wise Outreach Strategy

**1) LinkedIn (High Intent, B2B)**

- Connect → short message after acceptance
- Focus on owners/decision makers
- Personalize using company/activity

**2) Email (Scalable, Direct)**

- Use short, clean emails
- 1 idea per email
- Avoid attachments in first touch

**3) Facebook (Local Businesses)**

- Message business pages or owners
- Slightly casual tone works better

**4) Instagram (Quick Attention)**

- Short, curiosity-first DMs
- Ask a question to trigger reply

**Channel Priority:** LinkedIn + Email → Facebook → Instagram

---

### Cold Message Scripts

**LinkedIn / Facebook**

> Hi, I created a simple breakdown of our **6D Roofing Growth Engine™** that helps roofing companies generate **10–15 jobs/month consistently.**
> 
> 
> Happy to share it if you'd like.
> 

**Email**
Subject: Quick idea for your roofing business

> Hi,
> 
> 
> I came across your roofing business and noticed a few opportunities to increase your local leads. 
> 
> I’ve put together a short **Roofing Growth Blueprint** showing how contractors generate **10–15 jobs/month using a simple system.**
> 
> Happy to send it if you're interested.
> 

**Instagram DM**

> Quick question — would you be interested in a simple plan showing how roofing companies get consistent jobs every month?
> 

---

### Personalization Method

Goal: Make the message feel **relevant, not generic**.

Use 1-line personalization:

- Mention city: “in [City]”
- Mention observation: “noticed you’re not ranking in top results”
- Mention asset: “saw your recent projects”

**Formula:**
Observation + Value + Permission

Example:

> Noticed your roofing company in Dallas isn’t ranking in top results — I created a simple system that could help generate consistent jobs. Want me to share it?
> 

---

### Outreach Limits per Platform (Safety)

**LinkedIn**

- 20–40 connection requests/day (new accounts)
- 40–80/day (warmed accounts)

**Email**

- 50–150 emails/day per account
- Use multiple accounts for scaling

**Facebook**

- 30–50 messages/day

**Instagram**

- 20–40 DMs/day

**Rule:** Scale gradually to avoid bans

---

### VA Outreach SOP (Daily)

**Step 1 — Load Leads (30 min)**

- Pick 50–80 fresh leads from CRM

**Step 2 — Send Messages (2–3 hours)**

- Use platform scripts
- Add light personalization

**Step 3 — Handle Replies (1–2 hours)**

- If YES → send blueprint + invite audit
- If questions → answer briefly and redirect to audit

**Step 4 — Update CRM (30 min)**

- Update status (Contacted / Replied / Interested)

**Step 5 — Schedule Follow-ups (15 min)**

- Mark leads for Day 2 / 4 / 7 follow-ups

---

### Outreach Rules (CRITICAL)

- Keep messages **short and clear**
- Do NOT sell in first message
- Focus on **curiosity + value**
- Always ask for **permission to send**
- Consistency > perfection

---

### Output of This System

Per VA (daily):

- 50–80 messages sent
- 5–10 replies expected
- 1–2 audit opportunities created
