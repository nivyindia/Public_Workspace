# 💬 Outreach Script Library

> This is your master script library. Every message template the team uses lives here. Use these exactly as written for your first 2 weeks — do not modify until your supervisor approves changes.

## 🔵 SECTION 1 — LINKEDIN SCRIPTS

### 1A. Connection Request Note

*Sent when you click “Connect” — must be short and curiosity-based, never a pitch.*

**Template (adapt niche):**

> Hi {Name},
> 

> I’ve been looking into what’s working for {niche} companies to get more clients lately.
> 

> Thought it’d be great to connect 👍
> 

**Roofing example:**

> Hi James, I’ve been looking into what’s working for roofing companies to get more local leads lately. Thought it’d be great to connect 👍
> 

**Agency example:**

> Hi Sarah, I’ve been looking into what’s working for marketing agencies to grow their client base right now. Thought it’d be great to connect 👍
> 

**Consulting example:**

> Hi David, I’ve been looking into how consulting firms are scaling their client pipeline this year. Thought it’d be great to connect 👍
> 

---

### 1B. Message 1 — After Connection Accepts (Send Same Day)

*Goal: start a conversation, never pitch*

> Thanks for connecting {Name}!
> 

> Quick one — how are you currently getting most of your {niche} clients?
> 

> Referrals, online leads, or something else?
> 

---

### 1C. Message 2 — After They Reply

*Goal: transition to value, introduce the free plan*

> Got it 👍 that’s helpful.
> 

> I’ve been working with {niche} businesses and noticed most are missing a few simple things that can significantly increase inbound leads.
> 

> I actually put together a **free DIY sales & marketing plan** for {niche} businesses.
> 

> Want me to send it over?
> 

---

### 1D. Message 3 — If They Say YES to the Plan

*Goal: deliver value and position authority*

> Awesome — here it is 👇
> 

> This covers:
> 

> ✔ How to get consistent local leads
> 

> ✔ A simple outreach strategy most {niche} businesses miss
> 

> ✔ A follow-up system to close more clients
> 

> Take a look — and if you want, I can suggest improvements based on your specific situation.
> 

---

### 1E. Message 4 — Qualification (1–2 Days After Sending Plan)

*Goal: find out if they want help or will DIY — most important step*

> Quick question —
> 

> are you planning to implement this yourself, or would you want help setting it up properly?
> 

---

### 1F. Positive Reply — They Want Help (Book the Call)

*Goal: move to call immediately*

> Makes sense 👍
> 

> I can map out a quick game plan for your situation and show you exactly how we’d implement it.
> 

> Want to jump on a quick 15-min call this week?
> 

> Here’s my calendar: {Calendly link}
> 

---

### 1G. DIY Reply — They Will Do It Themselves (Nurture)

*Goal: stay warm, don’t push*

> That’s great 👍
> 

> If you get stuck anywhere or want feedback on how it’s going, just message me — happy to guide you.
> 

---

### 1H. Not Interested Reply — Soft Exit

*Goal: leave door open, never argue*

> No worries at all 👍
> 

> If you ever want to revisit this in the future, just let me know.
> 

> Wishing you great business ahead!
> 

---

### 1I. Pricing Question — What to Say

*Goal: move to call, never give numbers over DM*

> It really depends on what you need — we have different options.
> 

> The easiest way is a quick 15-min call where I can show you what fits your situation.
> 

> Want to book one? {Calendly link}
>

## ❗ OBJECTION HANDLING SCRIPTS

| Objection | Your Response |
| --- | --- |
| “Not interested” | “No problem at all 👍 Just wanted to check — are you currently handling this internally?” |
| “Too expensive” | “Got it — let’s first see if this can actually help your business, then you can decide if it makes sense.” |
| “I’ll think about it” | “Sure — what part are you unsure about? Happy to clear anything up.” |
| “I already have someone for this” | “That’s great — are you happy with the results you’re getting from them?” |
| “Send me more info” | “Happy to — what’s the one thing you’d most want to improve right now? I’ll send something specific.” |
| “Call me next month” | “Absolutely — should I send a calendar invite for early next month so we don’t lose track?” |
| “We’re not ready” | “Completely understand — what would need to change for it to make sense?” |
| “How did you get my number?” | “I found your business through {platform} — I apologize if the message felt unexpected. I can remove you from my list if you prefer.” |

---

## ⚠️ Script Rules

- Do NOT modify these scripts in your first 2 weeks
- Always replace {Name}, {niche}, {business} with real details before sending
- Never send the same message twice to the same person
- Never pitch in a connection request — only in Message 2 onwards
- Short messages get more replies than long ones
- Always end with a question to keep the conversation going

## 🔴 SECTION 7 — OBJECTION + REFERRAL + RE-ENGAGEMENT SCRIPTS

### 7A. Objection — "Already Working With Someone"

> That’s great to hear — are you happy with the results you’re getting from them?
> 

*If they say yes:*

> Completely makes sense. I’ll leave you to it — if anything changes or you want a second opinion down the line, feel free to reach out.
> 

*If they say it’s not going well:*

> Got it — happy to show you what we’re doing differently for similar businesses. Worth a quick 15-min call? {Calendly link}
> 

---

### 7B. Objection — "Call Me Later" / "Not the Right Time"

> Absolutely — should I send a calendar invite for early next month so we don’t lose track? That way it’s already in the diary and you don’t have to remember.
> 

*If they don’t want an invite:*

> No problem — I’ll check back in with you then. Wishing you great business in the meantime 👍
> 

---

### 7C. Referral Handling — When a Lead Refers You to Someone Else

*When they say: "You should speak to [Name] instead" or "My colleague handles this"*

> Thanks so much for pointing me in the right direction! Would you be comfortable making a quick intro, or should I reach out to them directly?
> 

*If they make an intro — your opening message to the referred person:*

> Hi {Name}, {Referrer} suggested I reach out — apparently you’re the right person to speak with about {relevant topic}.
> 

> Quick question: is growing your client base a current priority for you?
> 

---

### 7D. Dead Lead Re-Engagement — Reactivating Cold Leads (30+ Days Inactive)

*Use when a lead went cold after showing interest or replying:*

> Hey {Name}, hope things have been going well since we last spoke.
> 

> I wanted to follow up — we recently helped a {niche} business [get X result / solve X problem].
> 

> Thought it might be relevant to you. Is this still something you’re working on?
> 

*Variation — softer re-entry:*

> Hi {Name}, it’s been a while! Just checking in — has anything changed on your end regarding {their challenge}? Happy to share what’s been working if useful.
> 

*Re-engagement rule: Only use once per dead lead. If no reply — mark as "Closed - No Response" in CRM.*
