## ⚡ QUICK DECISION GUIDE — "What Do I Recommend?"

**Client has NO website** → Basic Website Starter

**Client has a website but no leads** → SEO Starter or Funnel Setup

**Client wants more social media** → Social Media Starter

**Client wants to run ads** → Basic Ads Setup → then Ads Management Growth

**Client wants to sell products online** → Shopify or WooCommerce Setup

**Client has budget $500+/month** → Jump straight to Level 2 Growth

**Client is an agency or growing fast** → Level 3 System Packages

**Client mentions AI or automation** → Level 4, book the call immediately

**Client has no professional email** → Business Email Setup (quick win)

**Client wants leads fast** → Outreach System Growth or Lead Gen System

---

> 💡 Rule: Always start with Level 1 if they're new. Always mention the Level 2 retainer after any Level 1 conversation. That's how we grow revenue per client.
