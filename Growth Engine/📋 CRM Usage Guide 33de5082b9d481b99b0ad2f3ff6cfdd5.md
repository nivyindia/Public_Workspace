# 📋 CRM Usage Guide

## What Is the CRM?

The CRM (Customer Relationship Management) sheet is the single most important tool in your daily work. Every lead you find, every message you send, every follow-up you do — it all lives here. If it is not in the CRM, it did not happen.

> **Rule:** Update the CRM after every single action. Not at the end of the day — after every action.
> 

---

## How to Access It

Your supervisor will share the Google Sheet link before Day 1. If you do not have it, message your supervisor immediately — do not start outreach without CRM access.

---

## The 7 Columns You Must Fill

| Column | What to Enter | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lead Name** | Full name of the person | James Carter |
| **Business Name** | Company or business name | Carter Roofing LLC |
| **Industry / Niche** | Your assigned niche | Roofing |
| **Platform** | Where you found / messaged them | LinkedIn |
| **Status** | Current stage in the pipeline | Messaged |
| **Last Contact Date** | Date you last sent a message | 09 Apr 2026 |
| **Next Follow-up Date** | When to follow up next | 12 Apr 2026 |
| **Notes** | Any important detail from the conversation | Said they handle leads via referrals only |

---

## The 6 Status Values — What Each Means and What To Do

| Status | Meaning | Your Next Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New** | Lead added, not yet contacted | Send connection request or Message 1 today |
| **Messaged** | You sent the first message | Wait 2–3 days, then send Follow-up 1 if no reply |
| **Replied** | They responded (any response, even negative) | Engage with the reply, qualify, move toward call |
| **Interested** | They showed genuine interest or asked questions | Send calendar link immediately — do not delay |
| **Booked** | Call has been scheduled | Fill in the handover form, confirm the time with the lead |
| **No Response** | You have sent 5+ follow-ups with zero reply | Mark as No Response, add to re-activation list after 3 weeks |

> **Critical:** When a lead moves to **Interested**, send the calendar link in your very next message. Do not wait. Every hour of delay reduces the chance they show up.
>

## How to Find Today's Follow-Ups

1. Open the CRM sheet
2. Sort or filter the **Next Follow-up Date** column by today's date
3. Every lead with today's date needs a follow-up message right now
4. After sending, update the **Last Contact Date** and set a new **Next Follow-up Date**

> Follow-up timing guide:
> 

> - After Message 1 → follow up on Day 2–3
> 

> - After Follow-up 1 → follow up on Day 5–6
> 

> - After Follow-up 2 → follow up on Day 8–9
> 

> - After Follow-up 3 → follow up on Day 12–14
> 

> - After Follow-up 4 → final follow-up on Day 16–18, then mark No Response
>

## How to Filter by Status

1. Click the dropdown arrow on the **Status** column header
2. Select the status you want to view (e.g. "Replied" to see all leads who responded)
3. Work through those leads before moving to new outreach

**Priority order each day:**

1. First — work all **Interested** leads (send calendar link)
2. Second — work all **Replied** leads (continue conversation)
3. Third — do today's scheduled **follow-ups** (Messaged leads past their follow-up date)
4. Fourth — send new **outreach** to fresh leads

---

## Common CRM Mistakes (Avoid These)

- Adding a lead but never updating the status after messaging → you will forget to follow up
- Not setting a Next Follow-up Date → leads die in the sheet
- Marking a lead as No Response after just 1–2 messages → too early, always do minimum 4–5 follow-ups
- Writing vague notes like "replied" → write what they actually said so you remember context next time
- Duplicate entries → always search the sheet before adding a new lead
