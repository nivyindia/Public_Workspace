# ✅ On-Page SEO Checklist {#onpage}

For every blog article and web page:

- [ ]  Target keyword in H1 title
- [ ]  Target keyword in first 100 words of body
- [ ]  Target keyword in at least 1 H2 subheading
- [ ]  Keyword density: 1–2% (natural, not stuffed)
- [ ]  Meta title: 50–60 characters, includes keyword
- [ ]  Meta description: 150–160 characters, includes keyword + CTA
- [ ]  URL slug: short, keyword-rich (e.g. `/virtual-assistant-agency-india`)
- [ ]  At least 1 internal link to another page
- [ ]  At least 1 external link to authoritative source
- [ ]  Images have alt text with keyword
- [ ]  Article is 800+ words
- [ ]  CTA at end of article (book a call / download resource)
- [ ]  Schema markup added (Article, FAQ, or Service schema)

# 🔗 Off-Page / Backlink Strategy {#offpage}

## Backlink Targets (Priority)

| Source Type | Examples | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business directories | Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush | Submit profile |
| Guest posts | VA/productivity blogs, startup blogs | Pitch articles |
| LinkedIn articles | Publish long-form, link to blog | Ongoing |
| Podcast appearances | Founder / entrepreneur podcasts | Pitch as guest |
| PR / Media | Business Standard, YourStory (India) | Press release |
| Forum participation | Reddit (r/entrepreneur), Quora | Answer + link |

## Monthly Backlink Target

- 5–10 quality backlinks/month in months 1–3
- 10–20 backlinks/month from month 4 onwards
- Domain Authority target: DA 20+ by month 6, DA 35+ by month 12

## Guest Post Outreach Process

1. Identify blogs accepting guest posts (search: `write for us + virtual assistant`)
2. Check their DA (min DA 25 for worthwhile link)
3. Pitch 3 topic ideas via email
4. Write article (800–1200 words) with 1 link back to Nivy site
5. Track in Guest Post Log spreadsheet
