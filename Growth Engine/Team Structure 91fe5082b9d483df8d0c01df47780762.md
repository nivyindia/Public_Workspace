# Leadership Hierarchy

| Level | Positions |
| --- | --- |
| Executive | CEO |
| Department Heads | Head of Strategy, Head of Brand, Head of Acquisition, Head of Growth/Product, Head of Ecosystem/Partnerships, Head of Revenue |
| Team Leaders | SEO Manager, Content Manager, Community Lead, SDR Manager, etc |
| Specialists | Analysts, marketers, designers, sales reps |
| Support / Freelancers | Writers, creators, community moderators |

# Ecosystem Growth Layer (Important)

These programs sit mainly inside **Department 5 (Community & Ecosystem)**.

| Program | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Ambassador Program | Students and creators promote the brand |
| Creator Network | Influencers and freelancers create content |
| Referral Programs | Customers bring new users |
| Freelancer Ecosystem | External contributors work on projects |
| Partner Network | Agencies and businesses sell or promote product |
| Gamification System | Rewards for engagement and promotion |

These **turn your audience into a growth engine**.
