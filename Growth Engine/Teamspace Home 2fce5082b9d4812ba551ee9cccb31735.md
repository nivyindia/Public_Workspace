- Don’t ask professionals for raw client lists

- You have **10,000+ contacts**

## 5️⃣ DATA ORGANISATION SYSTEM (STEP-BY-STEP)

We designed a **simple but scalable system**:

### A. One core rule

> **One MASTER database, not many files**
> 

---

### B. Folder structure (clear separation)

- RAW data (never touched)
- CLEANED data
- MASTER contacts
- CAMPAIGN usage
- ARCHIVE

---

### C. MASTER_CONTACTS database

A single sheet with:

- Identity (name, phone, email)
- Source & sub-source
- Data category (organic / bought / scraped / partner)
- Consent level
- Risk level
- Usage channel
- Status & notes

This becomes your **single source of truth**.

---

## 6️⃣ HANDLING BOUGHT DATA (SPECIAL DISCUSSION)

Because you already bought data, we defined strict rules:

- Keep it **clearly tagged**
- Use **email first**, small batches
- Never WhatsApp or bulk SMS
- Mark as **High Risk / Unknown Consent**

This protects:

- Your WhatsApp number
- Your email domain
- Your brand reputation

---

## 7️⃣ DEDUPLICATION & CLEANUP LOGIC

We discussed:

- One person = one row
- Phone = primary key
- Email = secondary key
- Update records, don’t duplicate

Cleanup plan:

- Merge first
- Deduplicate second
- Tag third
- Campaign later

---

## 8️⃣ CHANNEL-WISE USAGE RULES

We created discipline for:

- Email
- WhatsApp
- Calls

So you never mix:

- Bought data + WhatsApp
- Scraped data + SMS blast

This is how businesses **survive long-term**.
