# 🗓️ WEEKLY PLAN — MON TO SAT (MANDATORY STRUCTURE)

> This is your non-negotiable weekly operating rhythm. Follow it every week of Stage 3. No random effort — every day has a clear focus.
> 

---

## 📅 MONDAY — Lead Generation Day

**Focus:** Build your pipeline for the whole week

- 30 min → Review CRM — clean up statuses from last week
- 2.5 hrs → Lead Gen: Find 40–50 new qualified leads (LinkedIn / Apollo / Manual)
- 1.5 hrs → Add all new leads to CRM with status = New
- 1 hr → Send first outreach messages to 20–25 new leads
- 30 min → Submit daily report

> 🎯 Monday target: 40–50 new leads added, 20–25 messages sent
> 

---

## 📅 TUESDAY — Outreach Expansion Day

**Focus:** Reach more people across multiple platforms

- 30 min → Follow up on Monday's messages (no reply yet)
- 2 hrs → Lead Gen: Add 25–30 more leads
- 2.5 hrs → Outreach: Send 25–30 new messages across LinkedIn + WhatsApp + Email
- 30 min → Handle any replies from Monday
- 30 min → Update CRM + submit daily report

> 🎯 Tuesday target: 25–30 messages sent, all Monday replies handled
> 

---

## 📅 WEDNESDAY — Follow-Up Heavy Day

**Focus:** Revive cold leads and push warm leads forward

- 30 min → Lead Gen: Add 15–20 leads to keep pipeline topped up
- 3 hrs → Follow-ups: Send Follow-Up 2 to Mon–Tue leads with no reply; Follow-Up 3 to any leads from last week still cold
- 1 hr → Reply handling: Convert interested leads toward booking
- 30 min → Update CRM + submit daily report

> 🎯 Wednesday target: 20+ follow-ups sent, 0 interested leads left waiting
> 

---

## 📅 THURSDAY — Conversation + Booking Day

**Focus:** Turn replies into booked calls

- 30 min → Lead Gen: Add 15–20 leads
- 1.5 hrs → Follow-ups: Final follow-up (FU3) on all non-responders from Mon–Tue
- 2 hrs → Conversations: Focus entirely on leads that replied — move each toward booking
- 1 hr → Call confirmations: Confirm any booked calls, prepare handover notes
- 30 min → Update CRM + submit daily report

> 🎯 Thursday target: At least 1 call booked this week by end of day
> 

---

## 📅 FRIDAY — Review + Catch-Up Day

**Focus:** Close the week strong and fix gaps

- 1 hr → Lead Gen: Fill any gap in weekly lead target
- 1.5 hrs → Follow-ups: Final attempts on all this week's non-responders
- 1 hr → Reply handling: Clear all open conversations
- 1 hr → Weekly KPI review: What was my Reply Rate? Booking Rate? How many leads moved Messaged → Replied → Booked?
- 30 min → Plan Monday — identify who to follow up with next week
- 30 min → Update CRM fully + submit daily report

> 🎯 Friday target: CRM 100% up to date, weekly KPIs calculated and noted
> 

---

## 📅 SATURDAY — Light Work Day (Optional Boost)

**Focus:** Get ahead for next week

- 1–2 hrs → Lead Gen: Bank 20–30 leads ready for Monday
- 1 hr → Send outreach to any warm leads that haven't been messaged yet
- 30 min → Review any replies received Friday evening
- 30 min → Submit report (short format is fine)

> 🎯 Saturday is optional but highly recommended. VAs who work Saturday consistently outperform those who don't within 30 days.

# 💬 STEP 3 — BETTER CONVERSATIONS

---

## 📝 REAL SCRIPTS — CONVERSATION FLOW (Stage 3)

> These are word-for-word replies to use when someone responds.
> 

### When they say “Yes, what do you do?”

```
Great to hear!

We help [NICHE] businesses get more qualified leads and booked sales calls through targeted outreach.

Can I ask — are you currently getting consistent leads every week?
```

### When they say “How does it work?”

```
Essentially we build a targeted outreach system for your business — reaching out to your ideal clients on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and email.

We qualify them, book the calls, and hand them to your sales team.

It’s easier to explain on a quick call — are you free this week for 15 minutes?
```

### When they ask “Who is this for?”

```
We work mainly with [NICHE] businesses doing $5k+ per month that want to scale their client base without relying on ads.

Does that sound like your situation?
```

### When they ask “Can you send more info?”

```
Of course — I can send details, but honestly most of it depends on your setup.

Would a 10-minute call work? I can walk you through exactly what we'd do for your business.
```

> ⚠️ Rule: Never send a wall of text. One question per message. Always move toward a call.
>
