## Your job at Nivy — understand this before Stage 1

You are a **Virtual Assistant (VA)** at Nivy. Your job is to find potential clients, start conversations, and book calls for our sales team to close.

**You do not sell. You do this:**

```
Find leads → Add to CRM → Send message → Follow up → Book call → Update CRM → Submit report
```

That cycle, repeated every day, is your entire job. The training teaches you how to do it better and faster over 3 months.

## Why VAs fail — read this now

- Skipping CRM updates — leads get lost, follow-ups never happen
- Reading training without executing — training is worthless without same-day action
- Giving up after no replies in Week 1 — rejection is normal, consistency wins
- Sending generic copy-paste messages — personalisation doubles reply rate
- Not submitting daily reports — no report = no record of work = no pay

## The lead lifecycle — understand this before Day 1

```
New → Messaged → Replied → Interested → Booked → [Closer takes over]
```

| Status | Meaning | Your next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New | Lead added, not contacted | Send message today |
| Messaged | First message sent | Follow up in 2–3 days if no reply |
| Replied | They responded | Engage, qualify, move toward call |
| Interested | Showing genuine interest | Send calendar link immediately |
| Booked | Call confirmed | Fill handover form, send confirmation |
| No Response | 5+ follow-ups, no reply | Mark and re-approach in 3 weeks |

> Update the CRM after every single action. If it is not in the CRM, it did not happen.
