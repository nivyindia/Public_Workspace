# 💳 Payment Terms Policy

- **Project-Based Services:** 50% advance payment required before project initiation, with the remaining 50% due upon completion.
- **Monthly Retainer Services:** Full payment due in advance at the beginning of each month.
- **Quarterly Packages:** 100% payment in advance with a 5% discount on total package value.
- **Annual Contracts:** Option to pay quarterly (25% every 3 months) or receive 10% discount for full advance payment.
- **Media Buying/Ad Spend:** 100% advance payment required before campaign launch.

### Accepted Payment Methods

- Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS)
- Credit/Debit Cards
- PayPal
- UPI Payments
- Net Banking
- International Wire Transfer (for overseas clients)

### Late Payment Policy

- Invoices must be paid within 7 calendar days of issuance.
- A late payment fee of 1.5% will be charged per week on outstanding amounts.
- Services may be temporarily suspended if payment is delayed by more than 14 days.
- Recurring late payments may result in termination of services or requirement of 100% advance payment for future work.

### Currency & Taxation

- All invoices are issued in Indian Rupees (INR) for domestic clients.
- International clients may be billed in USD, GBP, or EUR, subject to prevailing exchange rates.
- All prices are exclusive of applicable taxes.
- 18% GST will be applied to all invoices for Indian clients as per government regulations.
- TDS certificates must be provided within 30 days if tax is deducted at source by the client.
- For international clients, taxes will be applied as per relevant tax treaties and regulations.

### Additional Terms

- Setup fees, onboarding costs, and third-party tool subscriptions are non-refundable.
- Any bank charges or payment gateway fees are to be borne by the client.
- Pricing may be revised with 30 days' notice for long-term contracts.
