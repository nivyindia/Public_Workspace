## QC-003 — Follow-Up Checklist

### Before Sending Each Follow-Up:

- [ ]  Correct follow-up day confirmed (Day 3 / 7 / 14 / 30) — check First Contact Date in CRM
- [ ]  Lead has not already replied (check LinkedIn / WhatsApp / email for any missed reply)
- [ ]  Lead has not asked to be removed or said they’re not interested (if yes — mark Dead, stop sequence)
- [ ]  Correct follow-up template used for this day number
- [ ]  Message opener personalised (not identical to previous message)
- [ ]  Message is under 6 lines
- [ ]  No typos or errors
- [ ]  Lead has not received more than 5 messages total (including first contact)

### After Sending:

- [ ]  CRM Last Contact Date updated to today
- [ ]  CRM Follow-Up Stage updated (FU1, FU2, FU3, or FU4)
- [ ]  Status remains "Following Up" (do not change unless lead replied)

### After Day 30 Follow-Up:

- [ ]  If no reply — Status updated to "Dead" same day
- [ ]  Notes column updated: "Full sequence complete. No reply. Marked Dead [date]."

---

**Do not send a follow-up early to "get it done." The timing of follow-ups is part of the strategy. Day 3 means Day 3.**
