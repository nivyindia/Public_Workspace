# 💬 WhatsApp Outreach SOP

<aside>
📌

This SOP covers WhatsApp outreach from start to finish — how to find numbers, how to message, daily limits, what to say, and what to avoid. WhatsApp is one of the highest-response platforms for Indian and Middle Eastern markets. Follow this SOP exactly to avoid account bans.

</aside>

---

## Why WhatsApp Works for B2B Outreach

WhatsApp has a 90%+ open rate vs 20–25% for email. In markets like India, UAE, and Southeast Asia, most business owners check WhatsApp before email. A well-crafted WhatsApp message from a real profile gets read almost every time.

The challenge is that WhatsApp is also very easy to abuse — mass messaging and spam will get your number banned fast. This SOP tells you exactly how to use it properly.

---

## Before You Start — Account Setup

**Use a dedicated WhatsApp number for outreach.** Do not use your personal WhatsApp. Ask your supervisor to provide or assign an outreach number.

**Profile setup requirements:**

- Profile photo: professional headshot (same as LinkedIn)
- Name: Your real first name + "| Nivy" (e.g. "Priya | Nivy")
- About/Bio: *"Business Development | Nivy Global Services | [www.thenivy.com](http://www.thenivy.com)"*
- Status: Keep it professional or blank — no personal statuses

**WhatsApp Business App:** Use WhatsApp Business (not regular WhatsApp). It allows you to set a business profile, quick replies, and labels for lead tracking.

## How to Find WhatsApp Numbers Legally

Never buy or scrape phone number lists. Use only these approved sources:

**Source 1 — LinkedIn:** When a prospect accepts your LinkedIn connection, some profiles show a phone number. If they do, it may be their business WhatsApp. Only use numbers they have made public.

**Source 2 — Google Business Profiles:** Search for the business type in a target city. Google Business listings often show WhatsApp numbers or general contact numbers.

**Source 3 — Website contact pages:** Many small business websites list a WhatsApp number directly, sometimes with a click-to-chat link.

**Source 4 — Facebook Business Pages:** Many Indian and UAE businesses list their WhatsApp number in their Facebook page’s About section or in posts.

**Source 5 — Instagram Bios:** Service businesses in India and UAE frequently list WhatsApp numbers in their Instagram bio.

**Source 6 — JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMART:** Indian business directories that list mobile numbers for business owners.

> Before messaging any number: verify the business is real, confirm the person is a decision maker, and check they fit the ICP.

## Daily Limits — Follow These Exactly

| Account Age | Max New Contacts/Day | Max Total Messages/Day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 (new number) | 10–15 | 20–30 |
| Week 2–4 | 20–30 | 40–60 |
| Month 2+ (warmed) | 30–40 | 60–80 |

> Exceeding these limits on a new number will trigger a ban. Start slow. Increase only after the number is warmed.
> 

**Signs your account is at risk:**

- Messages showing only one tick (not delivered) to multiple contacts
- Contacts reporting you (shown by warning message in WhatsApp)
- Sudden drop in delivery rate

If any of these happen: stop outreach immediately and report to supervisor.

## The WhatsApp Outreach Sequence

### Message 1 — Introduction (Day 1)

Keep it short. No pitch. Start a conversation.

**Template A — Accounting/Finance:**

> *"Hi [Name], I came across [Business Name] and wanted to reach out. I work with Nivy — we help businesses like yours with accounting and bookkeeping. Quick question — are you currently managing your books in-house or through an external firm?"*
> 

**Template B — Digital Marketing:**

> *"Hi [Name], I found [Business Name] while researching [industry] businesses in [city/area]. I work with Nivy — we help service businesses with lead generation and digital marketing. Are you currently doing anything online to bring in new clients?"*
> 

**Template C — General (when unsure which service fits):**

> *"Hi [Name], I’m reaching out from Nivy — we help businesses with accounting, marketing, and automation. I noticed [something specific about their business]. Quick question — what’s your biggest operational challenge right now?"*
> 

**Rules for Message 1:**

- Always use their first name
- Always mention something specific about their business (shows it’s not bulk spam)
- End with a question — not a pitch
- Maximum 3 lines

---

### Message 2 — Value Follow-Up (Day 3, if no reply)

> *"Hi [Name], following up on my message — I wanted to share something that might be relevant: [one-line case study]. If that’s something you’d like to explore, happy to set up a quick 15-minute call. If not, no problem at all."*
> 

---

### Message 3 — Final Follow-Up (Day 7, if no reply)

> *"Hi [Name], last message from my side — if you’re ever looking for support with [accounting / marketing / automation], feel free to reach out. Happy to help."*
> 

After this: do not message again unless they reply. Mark as **Follow-up in 30 days** in CRM.

---

### When They Reply — Conversation Handling

**If they’re interested:**

Move immediately toward booking a call. Do not keep chatting.

> *"Great — the best next step would be a quick 15-minute call with our team to understand your situation properly and see if there’s a fit. Would [day] or [day] work for you?"*
> 

**If they ask about price:**

Do not quote prices. Redirect to call.

> *"It depends on what you need — can vary quite a bit. The best way is a quick call where we can give you an accurate number based on your situation. Shouldn’t take more than 15 minutes."*
> 

**If they say not interested:**

> *"No problem — appreciate you letting me know. If anything changes, feel free to reach out."*
>

## CRM Tracking for WhatsApp

Every WhatsApp contact must be logged in your CRM sheet with:

- Name and business
- WhatsApp number
- Date of first message
- Current status (New / Messaged / Replied / Interested / Booked / Not Interested)
- Next follow-up date
- Notes on last conversation

Do not rely on WhatsApp to remember conversations. If you lose your phone or the account gets banned, your CRM is the only record of your work.

---

## What NOT to Do on WhatsApp

- Never send bulk messages using any third-party tool (instant ban)
- Never send voice notes unless the prospect initiated one first
- Never add contacts to WhatsApp groups without their permission
- Never copy-paste the same message to 20 contacts in a row without changing it
- Never send images, PDFs, or links in the first message (spam trigger)
- Never chase aggressively — maximum 3 messages if no reply, then stop
- Never continue messaging after a prospect says not interested or stop
