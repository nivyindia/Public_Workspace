# SOP-VA-007 — Email Outreach

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who Uses This:** VA Executive
> 

---

## Purpose

To send structured cold email outreach to qualified leads using proven subject line formulas, short body copy, and a defined follow-up cadence.

---

## Pre-requisites

- Lead has a verified email address in CRM
- Email outreach tool set up (assigned by Manager)
- KB-002 read and understood
- Manager has confirmed email is the assigned channel for this lead batch

---

## Daily Volume Limits

| Action | Daily Max |
| --- | --- |
| New cold emails | 20–30 |
| Follow-up emails | 10–15 |

> Do not use personal Gmail accounts for cold email. Use the assigned Nivy outreach email address.
> 

---

## Subject Line Formulas

Choose one per email. Do not reuse the same subject line more than 3x per day.

- `Quick question about [Company Name]`
- `[Industry] businesses in [City] — quick thought`
- `[Pain-related question]` (e.g., "Still doing bookkeeping manually?")
- `Intro from Nivy — [relevant context]`
- `[First name] — worth 5 minutes?`

---

## Email Body Structure

**Line 1:** Personal opener (reference their business, role, or a post)

**Line 2:** What you help with (1 sentence, problem-first, not feature-first)

**Line 3:** Social proof or outcome ("We've helped similar businesses in [market] with [result]")

**Line 4:** CTA (one soft question — not a pitch, not a brochure link)

Total: 4–5 lines max.

## Follow-Up Cadence

| Follow-Up | Timing | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Follow-Up 1 | Day 4 after no reply | Add a different angle or social proof |
| Follow-Up 2 | Day 9 | Short — just checking in |
| Follow-Up 3 | Day 16 | Final — polite close |

After Follow-Up 3 with no reply → Mark as **Dead** in CRM.

---

## Reply Handling

| Reply | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Positive | CRM → Interested. Escalate within 30 mins. Move to SOP-VA-010. |
| Unsubscribe / Remove me | Immediately mark Dead. Do NOT email again. |
| Out of office | Note return date in CRM. Follow up after return. |
| Not interested | Professional close. Mark Dead. |

---

## Output Standard

- 20–30 new emails sent daily within limits
- All follow-ups sent on the correct day
- CRM updated same day
- Positive replies escalated within 30 minutes
- Zero emails to unsubscribed contacts

---

## Common Mistakes

- Sending from a personal Gmail — always use the assigned outreach address
- Attaching a brochure to email 1 — it gets filtered as spam
- Writing more than 5 lines in the email body — longer emails get ignored
- Not marking unsubscribes immediately — re-emailing an opt-out is a compliance issue

---

## QC Checklist

- [ ]  Correct outreach email address used
- [ ]  Subject line is personalised
- [ ]  Body is under 5 lines
- [ ]  No attachments in first email
- [ ]  CRM updated after every send
- [ ]  Unsubscribes marked immediately
