# 1. Most Common Problems in Indian Homes (High Demand for Physiotherapy)

These are **very common in Indian families**, especially in urban areas.

| **Rank** | **Problem** | **Who Usually Has It** | **Reason** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Lower Back Pain | Office workers, shopkeepers | Sitting long hours |
| 2 | Knee Pain | Parents 45+ | Arthritis, weight, stairs |
| 3 | Neck Pain / Cervical Pain | Laptop & mobile users | Poor posture |
| 4 | Frozen Shoulder | Adults 40+ | Diabetes, inactivity |
| 5 | Sciatica Pain | Working professionals | Nerve compression |
| 6 | Heel Pain / Plantar Fasciitis | Shopkeepers, teachers | Standing long hours |
| 7 | Post-pregnancy Back Pain | Women | Weak core muscles |
| 8 | Sports injuries | Students | Playing cricket/football |
| 9 | Slip Disc / Spine Pain | IT professionals | Sitting + weak muscles |
| 10 | Old age stiffness | Parents 60+ | Low mobility |

These problems are **perfect for physiotherapy demos** because patients often feel **quick relief**, which builds trust.
