# 📝 CASE STUDY TEMPLATE {#template}

**Every case study follows this structure:**

```
=== CASE STUDY: [Client Type] in [Industry/Country] ===

CLIENT SNAPSHOT:
• Industry: [e.g., Real Estate / SaaS / E-commerce]
• Location: [e.g., United States / UK / India]
• Company size: [e.g., Solo founder / 5-person team]
• Service received: [e.g., VA (20 hrs/week) + Social Media Management]
• Duration: [e.g., 3 months]

THE CHALLENGE:
[2-3 sentences describing their situation BEFORE Nivy.
Be specific: time lost, tasks piling up, growth stalling.]
Example: "[Client] was spending 25+ hours per week on admin tasks — 
CRM updates, email follow-ups, invoice tracking — leaving no time 
for client acquisition or business development."

THE SOLUTION:
[What exactly did Nivy do? Be specific about tasks, tools, process.]
Example: "We placed a dedicated VA who took over: HubSpot CRM 
updates, daily lead follow-up emails, calendar management, and 
weekly reporting. The VA was fully onboarded in 4 days."

THE RESULTS:
[The most important section. Always include numbers.]
• ⏱️ Time saved: [X hrs/week freed up]
• 📈 Growth impact: [e.g., "Closed 2 extra deals in Month 1"]
• 💰 Cost saving: [e.g., "Saved $2,400/month vs hiring locally"]
• 😊 Satisfaction: [NPS score or direct quote]

CLIENT QUOTE:
"[Direct testimonial in their words — 1-3 sentences.]"
— [First name only or initials], [Title], [Industry]

LESSON LEARNED:
[1 sentence insight for prospects reading this.]
```

# 🎙️ TESTIMONIAL COLLECTION SOP {#collection}

## When to Ask

- After Month 1 if NPS ≥ 8
- After a project milestone is completed
- When client says something positive ("This is amazing!") — ask immediately

## How to Ask (WhatsApp/Slack)

```
Hi [Name]! So glad the [result] worked out 🎉

Would you be open to writing a quick 2-3 sentence review 
for us? It helps other businesses find Nivy and means 
a lot to us.

You can post it here: [Google link] [Clutch link]

Or if it's easier, just reply here with a few sentences 
and I'll format it for our website. 😊
```

## Testimonial Request Email (more formal)

```
Subject: Quick favor — would you leave us a review?

Hi [Name],

Working with you on [project] has been fantastic, and 
I'm really proud of the results we delivered together.

Would you spare 2 minutes to share your experience?
A short review on Google or Clutch would mean the world:

⭐ Google: [link]
⭐ Clutch: [link]

Or if you prefer, reply to this email and I'll 
format something for our website.

Thank you so much!
— Abhi, Nivy Digital
```
