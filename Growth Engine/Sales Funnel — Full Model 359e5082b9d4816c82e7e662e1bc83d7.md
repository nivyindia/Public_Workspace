# 📊 Funnel Overview {#overview}

```
AWARENESS
   ↓  (LinkedIn content, cold email, referrals, SEO)
FIRST TOUCH
   ↓  (Connection request, email reply, inbound inquiry)
DISCOVERY CALL BOOKED
   ↓  (Calendly link sent, confirmed)
DISCOVERY CALL HELD
   ↓  (Needs identified, fit assessed)
PROPOSAL SENT
   ↓  (Custom proposal delivered within 24 hrs)
FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE
   ↓  (2–3 follow-ups over 7–14 days)
NEGOTIATION / OBJECTIONS
   ↓  (Handle objections, adjust if needed)
CLOSED WON
   ↓  (Contract signed, invoice sent)
ONBOARDING
   ↓  (48-hour onboarding — SD-08 Automation)
```

# 📍 Stage-by-Stage Breakdown {#stages}

## Stage 1: Lead (New Contact)

- **What it is:** A qualified lead has entered the CRM
- **How they get here:** Apollo export, LinkedIn connect, inbound form, referral
- **Owner:** Lead Gen VA
- **Action:** Assign to outreach sequence
- **Exit criteria:** They reply positively OR book a call

## Stage 2: Engaged (Replied / Interested)

- **What it is:** Lead has responded positively to outreach
- **How they get here:** Email reply, LinkedIn DM reply, inbound inquiry
- **Owner:** Outreach VA
- **Action:** Send Calendly link, qualify via 2–3 pre-call questions
- **Exit criteria:** Discovery call booked

## Stage 3: Discovery Scheduled

- **What it is:** Discovery call is confirmed in calendar
- **How they get here:** Calendly booking received
- **Owner:** Outreach VA + Founder
- **Action:** Send confirmation email + context form. Prepare call notes.
- **Exit criteria:** Call happens

## Stage 4: Discovery Held

- **What it is:** Call completed, needs understood
- **How they get here:** Call logged
- **Owner:** Founder / Senior VA
- **Action:** Score the lead. Decide: Proposal, Nurture, or Disqualify.
- **Exit criteria:** Proposal sent OR decision made

## Stage 5: Proposal Sent

- **What it is:** Custom proposal delivered to prospect
- **How they get here:** Qualified call held, prospect wants to proceed
- **Owner:** Founder
- **Action:** Send proposal within 24 hours. Follow up in 2 days.
- **Exit criteria:** Prospect responds (positive, negative, or question)

## Stage 6: Negotiation

- **What it is:** Prospect has questions, objections, or wants to negotiate
- **How they get here:** Proposal response with pushback
- **Owner:** Founder
- **Action:** Use Objection Handling Library. Adjust if needed. Hold firm on core value.
- **Exit criteria:** Closed Won or Closed Lost

## Stage 7: Closed Won ✅

- **What it is:** Deal signed, invoice sent
- **Owner:** Founder
- **Action:** Send contract (DocuSign), send invoice, trigger onboarding sequence
- **Exit criteria:** Payment received, onboarding started

## Stage 8: Closed Lost ❌

- **What it is:** Prospect said no or went cold
- **Owner:** Founder / VA
- **Action:** Tag reason in HubSpot. Add to 90-day nurture sequence.
- **Exit criteria:** N/A — stays in nurture

# 🎤 Sales Conversation Framework {#framework}

## The SPIN Structure for Discovery Calls

**S — Situation** (Understand their current state)

> "Tell me about how you're currently handling [area of pain]."
> 

> "How many leads are you generating per week right now?"
> 

**P — Problem** (Surface the pain)

> "What's the biggest challenge with your current approach?"
> 

> "How much time per week are you spending on [task]?"
> 

**I — Implication** (Make pain real)

> "What does that cost you in terms of missed deals / lost time?"
> 

> "If this stays the same for another 6 months — where does that leave you?"
> 

**N — Need-Payoff** (Show the future)

> "If we could take [pain area] completely off your plate — what would that be worth to you?"
> 

> "What would change in your business if [desired outcome] was already happening?"
>

# ✅ Closing Checklist {#closing}

Before sending a contract:

- [ ]  ICP confirmed — client is a good fit
- [ ]  Scope of work agreed verbally on call
- [ ]  Pricing confirmed (no surprises in proposal)
- [ ]  Payment terms agreed (monthly retainer, upfront, etc.)
- [ ]  Start date confirmed
- [ ]  Contract generated and reviewed
- [ ]  DocuSign / e-signature sent
- [ ]  Invoice sent simultaneously with contract
- [ ]  Onboarding email triggered
- [ ]  HubSpot updated to Closed Won
