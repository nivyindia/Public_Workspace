# SOP-VA-005 — LinkedIn DM Outreach

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who Uses This:** VA Intern (after Manager approval), VA Executive
> 

---

## Purpose

To execute a structured 5-message LinkedIn outreach sequence on qualified leads — from connection request to booked call.

---

## Pre-requisites

- Lead has a qualification score of 4 or 5
- LinkedIn account is warmed (min 50+ connections, complete profile)
- KB-002 (What is Outreach) read and understood
- Daily volume limits understood

---

## Daily Volume Limits

| Action | Daily Max |
| --- | --- |
| Connection requests sent | 20–25 |
| Follow-up DMs sent | 10–15 |
| Total outreach actions | ~35–40 |

> **Do not exceed these limits.** LinkedIn restricts accounts that send too many requests. Account bans cannot be undone.
> 

---

## The 5-Message Sequence

**Message 1 — Connection Request (Note)**

> Short. Personal. No pitch.
> 

*Template:*

> "Hi [Name] — came across your work in [industry] and thought it was worth connecting. [Optional: 1 personal observation about their content or business]. Looking forward to being in your network."
> 

*Rule:* Max 300 characters. Do NOT mention Nivy or your service in Message 1.

---

**Message 2 — After Connection Accepted (Day 1–2)**

> Open the door. Still no pitch.
> 

*Template:*

> "Thanks for connecting, [Name]. I work with [business type] in [market] helping them with [1-line pain area]. Noticed your [specific observation]. Would love to hear more about what you're working on."
> 

---

**Message 3 — Follow-Up (Day 4–5 if no reply)**

> Add a hook or observation.
> 

*Template:*

> "Hey [Name] — just following up. Saw your recent post about [topic] — interesting perspective. We've been helping similar [business type] with [brief pain area]. Is that something on your radar at all?"
> 

---

**Message 4 — Value Follow-Up (Day 8–9 if no reply)**

> Offer something useful.
> 

*Template:*

> "[Name] — one thing we’ve seen work really well for [their industry] is [1-line insight or result]. Happy to share a quick breakdown if you're curious."
> 

---

**Message 5 — Final Attempt (Day 14 if no reply)**

> Polite close.
> 

*Template:*

> "[Name] — I'll leave it here. If timing ever makes sense to explore what we do for [industry] businesses, feel free to reach out. Happy to help."
> 

After Message 5 with no reply → Mark as **Dead** in CRM.

---

## Personalisation Rules

- Always use their first name (not "Hi there" or "Dear [Name]")
- Reference something specific: their industry, a post, their company name, or their role
- Never copy-paste the same opener to 20 people — vary at minimum the first line

---

## Reply Handling

| Reply Type | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Positive ("Tell me more", "Sounds interesting") | Update CRM to **Interested**, escalate to Manager within 30 minutes, move to SOP-VA-010 |
| Question ("What does Nivy do?") | Give 2-line answer, end with soft CTA to book a call |
| Not interested | Reply: "No worries at all — feel free to reach out anytime." Update CRM to **Dead** |
| No reply after Message 5 | Update CRM to **Dead** |

---

## Output Standard

- 20–25 connection requests sent daily
- 10–15 follow-up messages sent daily
- Every action logged in CRM same day
- Positive replies escalated within 30 minutes

---

## Common Mistakes

- Sending all 5 messages too quickly — follow the day spacing exactly
- Not personalising message 1 — generic requests get ignored
- Not updating CRM after each message sent
- Pitching Nivy's full service list in message 1
- Missing a positive reply because CRM wasn't checked

---

## QC Checklist

- [ ]  Daily volume within limits
- [ ]  Every message personalised (at minimum the opener)
- [ ]  CRM updated after every send
- [ ]  Positive replies escalated same day
- [ ]  No lead received more than 5 messages total
