## ❓ 16. FAQ SECTION

**Q: Which platform should I use?**

Depends on your audience. Skool and Circle for professional communities, Discord for younger or tech audiences, Facebook for broad B2C.

**Q: How big does my existing audience need to be?**

Ideal with 1,000+ email subscribers or a social following. Smaller is fine but growth takes longer.

**Q: How do you monetise the community?**

Free community drives trust → paid membership tier → high-ticket offer conversion. We design the ladder.

**Q: Can you manage an existing community?**

Yes — we audit what’s there and take over management.
