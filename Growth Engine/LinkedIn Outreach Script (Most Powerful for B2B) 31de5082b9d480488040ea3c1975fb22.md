# LinkedIn Outreach Script (Most Powerful for B2B)

Below are **proven outreach scripts used by global agencies** for **digital marketing, web, and outsourcing services**.

They work because they are **short, personalized, and value-focused**, not salesy.

I will organize them by **channel**, because each platform needs a different style.

---

# 1. LinkedIn Outreach Script (Most Powerful for B2B)

### Step 1 — Connection Request (No Pitch)

Keep it **very short**.

```
Hi [Name],
I came across your profile while researching companies in [industry].
Would love to connect and learn more about what you're building.
```

Alternative:

```
Hi [Name],
I work with companies in [industry] helping them with digital growth and backend support.
Thought it would be great to connect.
```

Purpose: **just get the connection accepted.**

---

### Step 2 — After Connection (Start Conversation)

Send after **1 day**.

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for connecting.

Quick question — are you currently handling your [marketing/accounting/website] work in-house or through an agency?
```

This works because it **starts a conversation instead of pitching.**

---

### Step 3 — Value Message

If they reply.

```
Interesting.

We’ve been helping companies in [industry] reduce their marketing/accounting workload by handling things like:

• social media management
• paid ads optimization
• bookkeeping & financial reports
• website improvements

Would you be open to a quick 10-minute call to see if it could help your team as well?
```

# 2. Cold Email Script (Global Agency Style)

Subject lines are **very important**.

### Subject Line Examples

- Quick question about [Company Name]
- Idea for improving [their marketing/website]
- Helping [industry] companies grow online
- Can I share a quick suggestion?

---

### Email Script

```
Hi [Name],

I was checking out [Company Name] and noticed that you’re doing some interesting work in the [industry] space.

We’ve been helping companies like yours improve their digital presence and handle backend operations such as:

• social media management
• ad campaign optimization
• bookkeeping & financial reporting
• website improvements

Many teams prefer outsourcing these tasks so they can focus on growth.

If you're open to it, I’d be happy to share a few quick ideas that could help [Company Name].

Would a short call sometime this week work?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

# Outreach Follow-Up System (Most Important)

Most agencies **fail because they don’t follow up.**

Use this schedule.

| Day | Message |
| --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Initial outreach |
| Day 3 | Follow up |
| Day 7 | Share value |
| Day 14 | Final follow up |

Example follow-up:

```
Hi [Name],

Just wanted to follow up in case my last message got buried.

Happy to share a few ideas that could help [Company Name].
```

# Golden Rules Used by Global Agencies

1️⃣ **Never pitch in the first message**

2️⃣ **Ask questions first**

3️⃣ **Keep messages under 60 words**

4️⃣ **Personalize the first line**

5️⃣ **Follow up at least 3 times**
