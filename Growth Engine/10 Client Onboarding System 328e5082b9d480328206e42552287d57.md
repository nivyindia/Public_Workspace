# 10. Client Onboarding System

## 

### Purpose of Client Onboarding System

- Ensure smooth transition from sale → delivery
- Collect all required data and access quickly
- Set clear expectations from day 1
- Build trust and professionalism

This stage determines **client experience and retention**.

---

### Onboarding Form Structure

Create a simple Google Form with:

**Basic Details:**

- Business Name
- Owner Name
- Phone Number
- Email

**Business Info:**

- Services offered
- Service areas (cities/locations)
- Average job value

**Online Presence:**

- Website link
- Google Business Profile link
- Social media links

---

### Data Collection (Access, Assets, Info)

Collect access to:

- Google Business Profile (manager access)
- Website / hosting / CMS
- Social media accounts (if applicable)

Collect assets:

- Logo (high quality)
- Past project photos (before/after)
- Testimonials / reviews

Collect info:

- Target locations
- Key services (repair, replacement, etc.)
- Unique selling points

---

### Client Expectation Setting

Communicate clearly:

- Timeline:
    
    > “Initial setup takes 2–4 weeks, results build over time.”
    > 
- Role clarity:
    - We handle system setup & optimization
    - Client provides content, approvals, and communication
- Results expectation:
    - Leads increase gradually, not instantly

---

### Kickoff Process

**Step 1 — Welcome Message**

> Welcome onboard — excited to help grow your roofing business.
> 

**Step 2 — Confirm Access & Data**

- Ensure all required details are received

**Step 3 — Internal Setup**

- Assign team (VA / manager)
- Create project tracker

**Step 4 — Kickoff Call (Optional but Recommended)**

- Align on goals
- Clarify next steps

---

### Key Rules (CRITICAL)

- Do onboarding within 24–48 hours of payment
- Do not start work without required access
- Keep communication clear and simple
- Set realistic expectations early

---

### Output of This System

- Smooth project start
- Better client trust
- Fewer misunderstandings
- Higher retention rate
