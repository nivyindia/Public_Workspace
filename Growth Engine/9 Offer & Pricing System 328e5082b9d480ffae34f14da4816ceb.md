# 9. Offer & Pricing System

## 

### Purpose of Offer & Pricing System

- Convert interest into revenue with clear packages
- Position services as a **system implementation**, not tasks
- Maximize client value and retention

---

### Offer Structure (3 Tiers)

### 🔹 Starter (Foundation)

- Google Business optimization
- Basic local SEO setup
- Review system setup
- Basic lead tracking

**Best For:** Small roofing businesses starting growth

---

### 🔹 Growth (Core System)

- Everything in Starter
- Referral system setup
- Content strategy (before/after, projects)
- Local visibility expansion

**Best For:** Businesses wanting consistent leads

---

### 🔹 Authority (Full System)

- Everything in Growth
- Full 6D Growth Engine implementation
- Automation (follow-ups, lead capture)
- Advanced local domination strategy

**Best For:** Companies aiming to dominate their area

---

### Pricing Strategy

Instead of pricing as services:

❌ Monthly marketing fee

Use:

👉 “Growth Engine Implementation”

Suggested Pricing Range:

- Starter: ₹50,000 – ₹80,000/month
- Growth: ₹80,000 – ₹1,50,000/month
- Authority: ₹1,50,000 – ₹3,00,000+/month

---

### Value Framing

Always connect price to ROI:

Example:

> If your average job is ₹1,00,000, even 2–3 extra jobs/month covers the cost.
> 

Position as:

- Investment, not expense
- System that generates revenue

---

### Packaging as “Growth Engine Implementation”

Instead of saying:
❌ SEO + Ads + Social Media

Say:
👉 “We implement your complete Roofing Growth Engine over 90 days.”

Phases:

- Phase 1: Foundation
- Phase 2: Growth
- Phase 3: Scaling

This increases perceived value and clarity.

---

### Payment & Contract Flow

**Step 1 — Agreement**

- Confirm interest on call

**Step 2 — Send Payment Link**

- Razorpay / Stripe / Bank transfer

**Step 3 — Send Contract / Agreement**

- Basic service agreement (scope, duration, payment terms)

**Step 4 — Onboarding Form**

- Collect client data and access

---

### Key Rules (CRITICAL)

- Never sell small services individually
- Always sell as a system
- Keep pricing confident (don’t justify too much)
- Focus on outcomes, not tasks

---

### Output of This System

- Higher ticket clients
- Better retention
- Clear expectations
- Scalable revenue model
