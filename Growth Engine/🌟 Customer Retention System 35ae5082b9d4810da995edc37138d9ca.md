# 💡 RETENTION PHILOSOPHY {#philosophy}

> **"Happy clients don't leave. They refer. They upgrade. They become case studies."**
> 

## The 3 Retention Pillars

1. **Results** — deliver measurable outcomes every month
2. **Relationship** — be proactive, communicative, and human
3. **Value expansion** — always show what else is possible

## Retention Targets

| Month | Churn Rate Target |
| --- | --- |
| Month 1–3 | <20% |
| Month 4–6 | <15% |
| Month 7–12 | <10% |
| Month 12+ | <5% |

# 📋 MONTHLY CLIENT SUCCESS SOP {#monthly-sop}

**Every month, for every active client:**

## VA's Weekly Duties

- [ ]  Send Monday update: "Here's what I'm working on this week: [list]"
- [ ]  Log all completed tasks in shared Notion or Google Doc
- [ ]  Flag any blockers immediately (don't wait until monthly review)
- [ ]  Track hours if on hourly plan (Clockify)

## Monthly Report Template (sent by 3rd of each month)

```
📊 MONTHLY REPORT — [Month] [Year]
Client: [Company Name] | VA: [VA Name] | Plan: [Package]

✅ COMPLETED THIS MONTH
• [Task 1 — include result/output]
• [Task 2]
• [Task 3]
[List all significant tasks done]

📈 RESULTS & IMPACT
• [Quantifiable result if possible — e.g., "Responded to 47 customer emails"]
• [Time saved estimate]

🔜 PLANNED FOR NEXT MONTH
• [Priority 1]
• [Priority 2]
• [Priority 3]

💬 YOUR FEEDBACK
We'd love to hear how we're doing:
[NPS survey link] (takes 30 seconds)

Thank you for being an amazing client! 🙏
— [VA Name] + Abhi, Nivy Digital
```

# 🚨 CHURN WARNING SIGNS & RESPONSE {#churn}

## Warning Signs to Watch

| Warning Sign | Severity | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client hasn't replied in 7+ days | 🟡 Medium | VA checks in proactively |
| Negative feedback on report | 🔴 High | Founder calls within 24 hrs |
| Client reduces task volume | 🟡 Medium | Founder checks on satisfaction |
| Client asks about cancellation process | 🔴 Critical | Founder calls same day |
| NPS score < 6 | 🔴 Critical | Founder personal email + call |
| Client disputes invoice | 🔴 High | Founder handles immediately |

## Save-a-Client Script (when cancellation is raised)

```
"[Name], I really appreciate you telling me directly.
Can I ask — is this about the results, the process, 
or the budget? I want to understand completely.

[Listen carefully]

"What would need to change for this to work for you?"

[Offer: reassign VA / adjust scope / pause instead of cancel / add bonus hours]

"I don't want to lose you as a client. Here's what I can do:
[specific offer]. Would that work?"
```

# 📈 UPSELL & EXPANSION PLAYBOOK {#upsell}

## When to Upsell

- After Month 1 if NPS ≥ 8
- When client comments that VA is "keeping up easily"
- When client mentions a new project or challenge
- During Quarterly Strategy Review

## Upsell Paths

| Current Service | Natural Upsell |
| --- | --- |
| VA Starter (40 hrs) | → VA Growth (80 hrs) |
| VA Growth (80 hrs) | → VA Full-Time (160 hrs) |
| VA Only | → VA + Digital Marketing Bundle |
| Digital Marketing | → Add SEO / Paid Ads / YouTube |
| Any plan | → Automation Package (n8n setup) |

## Upsell Email Template

```
Subject: An idea for [Company] next month

Hi [Name],

[VA Name] mentioned you've been crushing it lately — 
[specific result they've achieved].

I wanted to share a quick idea: given your momentum,
we could [specific upsell proposal — e.g., "expand to 20 hrs/week
+ add social media management"] for [price].

Would it make sense to explore this on a quick call?
[Cal.com link]

Either way, keep up the great work!
— Abhi
```

# 🌟 NPS & FEEDBACK SYSTEM {#nps}

## NPS Survey (sent monthly via Brevo)

```
Question 1: On a scale of 0–10, how likely are you to recommend 
Nivy Digital to a friend or colleague?
[0–10 slider]

Question 2: What's the main reason for your score?
[Open text]

Question 3: What could we do better?
[Open text]
```

## NPS Score Actions

| Score | Label | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9–10 | Promoter | Ask for public review (Google/Clutch) + referral invitation |
| 7–8 | Passive | Ask: "What would make this a 10?" + address gap |
| 0–6 | Detractor | Founder calls within 24 hrs — understand and resolve |

## Review Platforms to Target

- Google Business Profile (highest SEO value)
- [Clutch.co](http://Clutch.co) (B2B credibility)
- GoodFirms (directory trust)
- LinkedIn (public recommendation)
- Trustpilot (international credibility)
