# 📋 CLOSER SOP — FULL CALL PROTOCOL

## STAGE 1 — PRE-CALL PREP (15 minutes before)

**What to do before you pick up:**

1. Open the lead's handover notes from the VA. Find:
    - Lead's name, business type, pain point mentioned
    - Which package the VA pitched or discussed
    - Any objections already raised
    - Timezone and language preference
2. Open the Package Quick-Reference page. Identify which 1–2 packages are most relevant to this lead.
3. Set your mental frame:
    
    > *"I am not selling. I am diagnosing a problem and showing them the solution."*
    > 
4. Have the Script Library open. Know which scenario you're likely walking into.

---

## STAGE 2 — CALL OPENING (First 2 minutes)

**Goal:** Build trust + confirm context. Do NOT pitch yet.

**Standard Opening:**

> *"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from Nivy. How are you doing today?"*
> 

> *(Wait for response)*
> 

> *"Great. So [VA Name] mentioned you'd been looking into [brief context from notes]. I just wanted to jump on a quick call to understand where you're at and see if we can actually help. Does that work?"*
> 

**If they seem rushed:**

> *"I know your time is valuable — this will be 20 minutes max. Is now still okay or should we reschedule?"*
> 

---

## STAGE 3 — DISCOVERY (5–8 minutes)

**Goal:** Understand their situation before you say anything about packages. Whoever talks most in discovery, loses least in negotiation.

**The 5 Core Discovery Questions:**

| # | Question | Why You Ask It |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "What does your business do and who are your ideal clients?" | Understand their market and ICP |
| 2 | "What's the biggest growth challenge you're facing right now?" | Identify primary pain point |
| 3 | "What have you tried before? What happened?" | Understand history, avoid repeating their failures |
| 4 | "If we solved [pain point] in the next 90 days, what would that mean for your business?" | Get them to articulate the value of solving it |
| 5 | "What's your timeline and budget ballpark for this?" | Qualify before pitching |

**Rules during discovery:**

- Listen. Take notes.
- Do NOT pitch until you have answers to at least Q1, Q2, Q4.
- Reflect back: *"So what I'm hearing is... [restate their problem in their words]"*

---

## STAGE 4 — PACKAGE POSITIONING (5–7 minutes)

**Goal:** Present 1–2 packages as the specific solution to their specific problem. Never present a menu of options.

**Positioning Framework:**

> *"Based on what you've told me, here's what I think makes sense for you..."*
> 

| Their Situation | Package to Lead With | Framing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early stage, limited budget, wants visibility | SMM L1 or Lead Gen L1 | "Start with consistent presence and incoming leads — build from there" |
| Already running ads, needs better results | Performance Marketing L2/L3 | "You're spending money — let's make that money work harder" |
| Needs leads but no ad budget | Lead Gen L1 or L2 | "Organic outreach to decision-makers — no ad spend required" |
| Has team, needs content volume | Content Creation L2 | "Batch production — 30 days of content built in one sitting" |
| Website exists, wants Google traffic | SEO L1 or L2 | "You have the asset — let's make Google send people to it" |
| Business is growing, needs financial clarity | CPA Starter or Growth | "As you scale, compliance and clean books protect you — and reduce your tax" |

**Pitch Format (per package):**

1. State the problem they described (in their words)
2. State what the package does (one sentence)
3. State the outcome they can expect (concrete, time-bound)
4. State the investment
5. Ask: *"Does that feel like the right direction?"*

---

## STAGE 5 — OBJECTION HANDLING

> See **Script Library — Objection Handling Section** for full decision trees.
> 

**Most common objections and the principle behind the response:**

| Objection | Principle | Never Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "It's too expensive" | Price is never the real objection. Find the real one. | "We can reduce the price" |
| "I need to think about it" | They need more information, not more time. | "Sure, take your time" |
| "I'll start next month" | Urgency without pressure. What are they losing right now? | "Okay, I'll follow up" |
| "I tried this before and it didn't work" | Their experience was with someone else. This is a systems conversation. | Defend the industry |
| "Can you send me more information?" | Information doesn't close deals. Clarity does. Clarify on the call. | "Sure, I'll email you a deck" |

## STAGE 6 — CLOSE SEQUENCE

**Goal:** Get a clear yes or no. Not a maybe.

**Step 1 — Trial Close:**

> *"Based on everything we've discussed, does this feel like the right fit for where you want to take [business name]?"*
> 

**Step 2 — Commitment Close (if yes to step 1):**

> *"Great. The way we work is simple — once you confirm, we send an agreement and get started within [X days]. Does that work for your timeline?"*
> 

**Step 3 — Handling a soft no:**

> *"I appreciate you being straight with me. Can I ask — what would need to be different for this to be a yes?"*
> 

> *(Listen. Address the one remaining concern. Do NOT negotiate price without manager approval.)*
> 

**Step 4 — Hard no:**

> *"That's completely fine. I'd rather you make the right decision for your business. If anything changes or you want to revisit this in the future, I'll be here."*
> 

> *(Log as 'Not Now', set follow-up reminder for 30 days.)*
> 

---

## STAGE 7 — POST-CALL PROTOCOL (within 1 hour)

**If CLOSED:**

- [ ]  Log in Performance Tracker: Date, Lead Name, Package, Value, Close status = Won
- [ ]  Send agreement/invoice link to client (or notify manager to send)
- [ ]  Notify delivery team lead: "[Client name] signed for [Package]. Expected start: [Date]."
- [ ]  Send WhatsApp confirmation to client: *"Great speaking with you today, [Name]. You'll receive the agreement shortly. Looking forward to working together."*

**If NOT closed (interested, needs time):**

- [ ]  Log in Performance Tracker: Date, Lead Name, Objection, Status = Follow-Up
- [ ]  Send follow-up message within 24 hours (use Script Library — Follow-Up section)
- [ ]  Set reminder for 3-day and 7-day follow-up

**If NO (not interested):**

- [ ]  Log in Performance Tracker: Date, Lead Name, Reason, Status = Lost
- [ ]  No further follow-up unless lead re-initiates

---

> ⚠️ **Hard Rule:** Do NOT quote custom pricing, offer discounts, or promise timelines that are not in the package. If a client asks for something outside the standard, say: *"Let me confirm that with my team and get back to you within 24 hours."*
