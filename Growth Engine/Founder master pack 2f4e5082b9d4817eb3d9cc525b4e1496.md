- Monthly revenue
- Gross margin %
- Cash in bank

### 2️⃣ OUTREACH PARTNER (Company or Small Team 2–5)

**Role**

- Cold email
- LinkedIn outreach
- Data scraping
- Lead qualification

**KPIs**

- Leads/day
- Reply rate
- Qualified leads/week

**Payment Model**

- ❌ No fixed pay
- ✅ % per qualified lead OR % per closed deal

### 3️⃣ SALES / DEAL CLOSER (YOU initially → later partner)

**Role**

- Proposal
- Closing
- Upsell

**KPIs**

- Lead → deal %
- Avg deal value
- Time to close

**Payment Model**

- 10–20% per closed deal

### 4️⃣ DELIVERY PARTNERS (Core Supply Chain)

**Role**

- Actual service delivery
- Timelines
- Quality control

**KPIs**

- Delivery TAT
- Error rate
- Client satisfaction
