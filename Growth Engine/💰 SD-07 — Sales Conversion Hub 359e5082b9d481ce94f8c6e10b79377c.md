# 📞 DISCOVERY CALL FRAMEWORK {#discovery}

**Duration:** 30 minutes | **Format:** Video (Google Meet / Zoom)

## SPIN Selling Structure

1. **Situation (5 min)** — "Tell me about your current setup. What does your team look like?"
2. **Problem (8 min)** — "What's taking up the most of your time right now? What's not working?"
3. **Implication (7 min)** — "If this problem continues for 6 more months, what does that cost you?"
4. **Need-Payoff (5 min)** — "If we could solve X in 30 days, what would that mean for your business?"
5. **Proposal (5 min)** — Present the right package. Recommend clearly. Don't give 5 options.

## Proposal Template Structure

- Cover page (branded)
- Executive Summary (1 page — the transformation they get)
- Solution Overview (what we do, how it works)
- Deliverables & Timeline
- Pricing (3 tiers: Starter / Growth / Enterprise)
- ROI Calculator (show the value, not just the cost)
- Case Studies (1-2 relevant ones)
- Next Steps (clear CTA + contract link via Docuseal)
