# 💡 PRICING PHILOSOPHY {#philosophy}

## Core Principle: Value-Based Pricing

- Never compete on price alone — always anchor to the value delivered
- Show ROI: "This VA saves you 20 hrs/week at $50/hr = $4,000 value for $600/month"
- 3-tier structure (Starter / Growth / Scale) gives options without complexity
- Month-to-month after Month 1 = reduces risk objection
