## 🧭 Your Role in Plain English

As a VA at Nivy, your entire job is this sequence:

**Find leads → Message them → Follow up → Get a reply → Book a call → Update CRM → Submit daily report**

Every stage of your training teaches you how to do one part of that sequence better. Nothing else matters until you can do this consistently.

## 📋 Your Daily Non-Negotiables

These 5 things must happen every single working day, no exceptions:

| Non-Negotiable | Deadline | Consequence of Missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Submit daily report | 7:00 PM | Marked absent. No pay for that day. |
| Update CRM after every action | Same day | Leads fall through cracks. You lose credit for your work. |
| Send outreach messages | During work hours | Zero pipeline. No calls. No progress. |
| Follow up on cold leads | Per schedule (Day 3, 7, 14) | 80% of bookings come from follow-up. Skipping = lost revenue. |
| Read your current stage page | Morning of each stage | You will not know what to do or how to improve. |

---

## 📥 Daily Report Format — Submit Every Day by 7:00 PM

Copy this format and fill in every field. Send to your team WhatsApp group. Never combine two days into one report. Never leave a field blank — write 0 if the number is zero.

```
DAILY REPORT — [Your Name] — [Date] — Stage [X]
Leads Added Today: __
Messages Sent: __
Follow-ups Sent: __
Replies Received: __
Calls Booked: __
Platforms Used: __
Issues / Blockers: __
```

> **No report = marked absent. No exceptions. No pay for that day.**

## 📅 Your 3-Month Journey — What to Expect

| Period | Stage | Focus | Key Milestone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Stage 1 | Learn the system, send first messages | First message sent, first CRM entry logged |
| Days 2 to 7 | Stage 2 | Expand to multiple platforms | 1 to 2 calls booked |
| Week 2 (Days 8 to 14) | Stage 3 | Build consistency and daily structure | 3 to 5 calls booked |
| Weeks 3 to 4 (Days 15 to 28) | Stage 4 | Improve quality and handle objections | First qualified handover to a closer |
| Month 2 (Days 29 to 60) | Stage 5 | Full system integration, team rules | Stable daily performance, 3 to 7 calls/week |
| Month 3 (Days 45 to 90) | Stage 6 | Peak performance, maximum output | 5 to 10 calls/week, revenue visible |
| Month 3+ (merit only) | Stage 7 | Specialisation in highest-output channel | Top performer track unlocked |
| By merit only (top 20%) | Stage 8 | Team leadership | Managing your own VA team |

## 📊 How Your Performance Is Measured

You are scored on 5 metrics every month. Falling below 65% on KPI completion puts your base salary at risk.

| Metric | What It Means | Minimum Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead Accuracy | Are your leads the right people in the right industry? | 90%+ ICP match |
| Message Quality | Are you following approved scripts without deviation? | No deviations in first 2 weeks |
| CRM Accuracy | Is every lead updated after every action, same day? | 100% same-day updates |
| KPI Completion | Are you hitting your daily targets? | 65%+ to keep base salary |
| Report Submission | Did you submit your report every working day? | 22+ reports in 30 days |
