# 🔍 Package Differentiator Guide — What’s Different Between Levels

> **The #1 VA confusion:** "How do I explain the difference between Level 1 and Level 2? Why would someone buy Level 3? What makes SEO Starter different from SEO Growth?" This page answers all of it — in plain language you can use during a conversation.
> 

---

## 🧠 THE BIG PICTURE FIRST — One Analogy

Think of it like a gym:

- **Level 1** = Joining the gym. You pay once, you get access, you're set up. *(One-time)*
- **Level 2** = Hiring a personal trainer every month. Ongoing work, ongoing results. *(Monthly retainer)*
- **Level 3** = A full transformation program — trainer, nutritionist, and plan together. *(High-ticket system)*
- **Level 4** = The gym builds a custom facility just for you with AI tracking everything. *(Enterprise/custom)*

---

## 🟢 LEVEL 1 vs LEVEL 2 — The Most Common Confusion

### What's the difference?

|  | Level 1 — Entry | Level 2 — Growth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Type** | One-time setup | Monthly retainer |
| **Goal** | Get you started | Keep growing you |
| **Duration** | 5–14 days delivery | Ongoing every month |
| **Who runs it after?** | The client | Nivy continues running it |
| **Results** | Foundation built | Compound results over time |
| **Price model** | Fixed, pay once | Monthly, predictable |

### How to explain it to a client:

> *"Level 1 is like getting your website built — we build it, hand it over, done. Level 2 is like hiring a team to run your marketing every month — we keep working on it, keep improving it, keep sending you results."*
> 

---

## 🔵 LEVEL 2 TIERS — Starter vs Growth vs Scale

Every Level 2 service has 3 tiers. Here's how to explain the difference:

| Tier | What It Means | Who It's For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | We do the basic work. Set up systems, execute fundamentals. | Businesses just starting with this service |
| **Growth** | We go deeper. More volume, more strategy, more optimization. | Businesses already doing basics, want more |
| **Scale** | Full aggressive mode. Multiple channels, advanced targeting, max output. | Businesses ready to scale fast and invest seriously |

### Script for moving tiers:

> *"We always recommend starting at Starter to get the systems right, then moving to Growth once we see what's working. Most clients upgrade within 60–90 days."*
> 

---

## 🟣 LEVEL 2 vs LEVEL 3 — Service vs System

### What's the difference?

|  | Level 2 — Growth | Level 3 — System |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scope** | One service (e.g. just SEO) | Multiple services working together |
| **Strategy** | Execution-focused | Strategy + execution + integration |
| **Team size** | 1–2 specialists | Full cross-functional team |
| **Reporting** | Monthly report per service | Full business growth dashboard |
| **Price** | $300–$4,000/month | $1,000–$10,000 (range) |
| **Who closes it** | VA can qualify, closer confirms | Closer always handles this |

### How to explain it to a client:

> *"Level 2 is one service being managed — like just your social media. Level 3 is a complete growth system — social, ads, SEO, and funnels all connected and working together toward one goal."*
> 

### When to mention Level 3:

- Client says: *"I want everything done"*
- Client says: *"We've tried individual services and they didn't work"*
- Client has $2,000+/month budget
- Client is scaling fast and needs integrated growth

---

## 🔴 LEVEL 3 vs LEVEL 4 — System vs Enterprise

|  | Level 3 — System | Level 4 — Enterprise & AI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Complexity** | Complex but defined scope | Fully custom, varies widely |
| **AI involvement** | Minimal | Core to delivery |
| **Company size** | SMB to mid-market | Mid-market to enterprise |
| **Who decides** | Marketing/Ops head | CEO / CTO level |
| **Timeline** | Weeks to set up | Months to implement |
| **Price** | $1,000–$10,000 range | $3,000–$50,000+ custom |

### When to flag Level 4:

- Client mentions *AI*, *automation*, *enterprise*, *multiple locations*, *data systems*
- Client has 50+ employees
- Client has existing marketing team but wants to supercharge it
- Budget is clearly high — no hesitation on pricing

## 🔄 WITHIN SAME LEVEL — How to Differentiate Similar Services

### SEO Starter vs Social Media Starter vs Ads Setup

|  | SEO Starter | Social Media Starter | Ads Setup |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **How it gets results** | Google rankings over time | Brand presence + engagement | Paid traffic immediately |
| **Speed of results** | 3–6 months | Slow build | Immediate (when live) |
| **Cost to client after setup** | Just our fee | Just our fee | Our fee + ad spend |
| **Best for** | Long-term inbound | Brand awareness | Fast leads |

> Tell a client: *"If you want results in the next 30 days, ads are your fastest option. If you want to build something sustainable that brings leads for years without paying per click, SEO is the play. Social media sits in between — it builds trust and warms people up."*
>

### Basic Website vs Business Website

|  | Basic Website ($300–$1,500) | Business Website ($700–$1,500) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pages** | 3–5 pages | 6–10 pages |
| **Design** | Template-based | Semi-custom |
| **Strategy** | None — just presence | Designed to convert visitors |
| **SEO** | Basic meta setup | Full on-page SEO |
| **Best for** | Just getting online | Businesses serious about growth |

> *"If you just need a professional online presence, Basic works. If you want a website that actively generates enquiries for you, Business Website is what you need."*
>

## ⚡ THE 3 QUESTIONS TO QUICKLY FIND THE RIGHT PACKAGE

1. **"What's the main problem you're trying to solve right now?"** → Maps to service type
2. **"Are you looking for something one-time or ongoing support?"** → Maps to Level 1 vs 2
3. **"What's your monthly budget for this?"** → Maps to tier (Starter/Growth/Scale)
