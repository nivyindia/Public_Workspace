# Hiring Requirements Matrix

# Complete

This document defines **skills, tools, responsibilities, experience level, and evaluation criteria for every role across departments and sub‑departments**. It is designed for a **freelancer + trainee + senior mentor structure**.

Structure philosophy:

- Entry Level: Students, interns, fresh freelancers
- Mid Level: Skilled freelancers / specialists
- Senior Level: Team leaders / strategists

---

# 1. SALES & CLIENT ACQUISITION DEPARTMENT

| Role | Experience | Skills Required | Tools | Responsibilities | Evaluation Criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales Intern / Lead Generator | 0–1 years | Communication, basic business understanding, persistence | Google Sheets, WhatsApp, LinkedIn | Find leads, send outreach messages, collect contact details | Leads generated per day, response rate |

| Client Acquisition Specialist | 2–5 years | Negotiation, proposal creation | CRM, Zoom, Proposal tools | Close deals, send proposals, negotiate pricing | Deals closed, revenue generated |
| Sales Team Leader | 5+ years | Leadership, pipeline management | CRM dashboards | Train team, monitor pipeline, improve conversion | Team revenue performance |
| Head of Sales / Client Acquisition | 7+ years | Sales strategy, forecasting | CRM analytics | Build sales system, partnerships, expansion | Total revenue growth |

# Hiring Evaluation System

Every candidate must pass:

1. **Skill Test**
2. **Trial Task (Real Work)**
3. **Interview**
4. **1–4 Week Probation Project**
