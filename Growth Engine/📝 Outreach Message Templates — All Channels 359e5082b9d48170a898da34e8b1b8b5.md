# 💼 LINKEDIN CONNECTION NOTES {#linkedin-connect}

**Rule:** Max 300 characters, personalized, no hard pitch

| Template | Best For |
| --- | --- |
| "Hi [Name], saw your work in [industry] — impressive. I work with founders on scaling ops. Love to connect." | Cold outreach |
| "Hi [Name], fellow [India/entrepreneur/startup] community member. Thought it'd be great to connect and share notes!" | Warm community |
| "Hi [Name], we just helped a [industry] founder free up 20 hrs/week. Thought you might find our work interesting. Happy to connect!" | Result-led |
| "Hi [Name], I noticed you [specific observation from profile]. I work with similar founders on [solution]. Happy to connect!" | Hyper-personalized |

# 💬 LINKEDIN DM SEQUENCES {#linkedin-dm}

## Sequence A — General (VA Services)

**DM 1 (after connection):**

```
Hi [Name]! Thanks for connecting.

I run Nivy Digital — we help founders like you 
reclaim 15–25 hours/week by placing dedicated 
VAs for admin, ops, and marketing.

Curious — what's the one task you wish you 
could just hand off right now?
```

**DM 2 (Day 3, no reply):**

```
Hey [Name] — following up in case this got buried.

We work with [industry] founders who are stretched 
thin across ops and marketing. Recently helped 
one save 22 hrs/week.

20 mins to see if we could do the same? 
[Cal.com link]
```

**DM 3 (Day 7, break-up):**

```
[Name] — last message, I promise! 😄

If VA support for your business ever makes 
sense, here's how to book a free call:
[Cal.com link]

Good luck with everything!
```

## Sequence B — Digital Marketing Services

**DM 1:**

```
Hi [Name]! Thanks for connecting.

I noticed [Company] is building a strong presence in [industry].

We help brands like yours with social media, SEO, and 
content — without the full-time hire cost.

Are you handling marketing in-house or looking for support?
```

# 📧 COLD EMAIL SUBJECT LINES {#subject-lines}

## High-Performing Subject Lines (A/B test these)

**Pain-focused:**

- "Quick question, [First Name]"
- "Drowning in admin tasks, [First Name]?"
- "The 20 hours/week you're probably losing"
- "[Company] + virtual assistant"

**Result-focused:**

- "How [similar company] saved 22 hrs/week"
- "3x more leads, same team size — here's how"
- "[First Name], free 30-min growth audit"

**Curiosity:**

- "Weird question, [First Name]"
- "Noticed something about [Company]"
- "Should I send this, [First Name]?"

**Numbers:**

- "$600/month to get your time back"
- "5 tasks you could stop doing this week"

# 💬 WHATSAPP OUTREACH TEMPLATES {#whatsapp}

**Initial Message (India market):**

```
Hi [Name],

I'm Abhi from Nivy Digital, Lucknow. We help businesses 
like yours with virtual assistants and digital marketing.

We've helped [X] businesses reduce operations costs 
by 40–60% with our VA services.

Could I share how we can help [Company] specifically? 
Takes just 10 minutes.
```

**WhatsApp Follow-Up (Day 2):**

```
Hi [Name], just following up on my last message.

I'd love to share a quick case study relevant to 
your business. Shall I send it over?
```

**WhatsApp Broadcast (to existing contacts):**

```
Hi [Name] 👋

Monthly update from Nivy Digital:

✅ We now offer [new service or highlight]
💡 This month's free resource: [lead magnet]
📅 Book a free 30-min call: [Cal.com link]

Reply if I can help with anything!
— Abhi, Nivy Digital
```

# 🔄 FOLLOW-UP TEMPLATES (ALL CHANNELS) {#followups}

**Universal Follow-Up #1 (2 days):**

```
Hey [Name] — just bumping this up in case it got buried. 
Would a quick call make sense? [Cal.com link]
```

**Universal Follow-Up #2 (5 days):**

```
[Name] — sharing one thing before I stop following up:
[Relevant stat or case study — 1 sentence]

Still worth a chat? [Cal.com link]
```

**Break-Up Message (Universal):**

```
Hi [Name], this is my last follow-up — promise! 😄

If working with a VA or growing your business 
digitally ever becomes a priority, here's where 
to find us: [website] or book a call: [Cal.com link]

Best of luck with [Company]!
```

# 🎯 NICHE-SPECIFIC VARIATIONS {#niche}
