# 📅 Weekly Self-Review Template

<aside>
📌

Complete this review every Friday before submitting your end-of-week report. It takes 15–20 minutes. This is the single most important habit that separates improving VAs from VAs who plateau. Do not skip it.

</aside>

---

## Why Weekly Review Matters

Most VAs who underperform do so for the same reason: they repeat the same actions every day without analysing what’s working and what isn’t. A weekly review forces you to look at your own numbers honestly, identify exactly where leads are dropping off, and make one specific improvement before the next week starts.

The review should take 15–20 minutes. Do it every Friday.

## Part 2 — Drop-off Analysis

For each stage of the lead funnel, ask: where did I lose the most leads this week?

**Stage 1 — Message → Reply**

If reply rate was below 10%:

- Were the leads properly qualified (right ICP)?
- Was the opening message personalised or generic?
- Was the message too long?
- Did I change platforms enough (LinkedIn vs WhatsApp vs Email)?

**Stage 2 — Reply → Interested**

If I got replies but few interested:

- Did I respond fast enough (within 2 hours)?
- Was my reply too salesy too quickly?
- Did I ask a question to keep the conversation going?
- Did I handle any objections that came up?

**Stage 3 — Interested → Booked**

If people were interested but didn’t book:

- Did I ask for the call clearly?
- Did I offer specific days/times?
- Did I follow up after they said "maybe" or "later"?

**Stage 4 — Booked → Showed Up**

If booked calls had no-shows:

- Did I send a confirmation message after booking?
- Did I send a reminder 24 hours before?
- Was the handover form submitted to the closer on time?

## Part 7 — Blockers & Support Needed

Is there anything stopping you from hitting your targets that is outside your control?

- Tool or system issue?
- Script that isn’t working but don’t know why?
- A type of objection you don’t know how to handle?
- Something your supervisor needs to know?

Write it here and flag to supervisor in your Friday report.

## Friday Report Format

Your end-of-week report includes your daily numbers AND a summary of this review. Use this format:

```
WEEKLY REVIEW SUMMARY | [Your Name] | Week of [Date]

Total Leads: __
Total Messages: __
Total Replies: __
Reply Rate: __%
Calls Booked: __
Booking Rate: __%

Best result this week: [one sentence]
Biggest weakness: [one sentence]
What I’m changing next week: [one specific action]
Blockers / support needed: [or "None"]
```
