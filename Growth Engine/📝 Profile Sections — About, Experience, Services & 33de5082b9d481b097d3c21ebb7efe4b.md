# 📝 Profile Sections — About, Experience, Services & Skills

These are the sections that turn a profile visitor into a lead. Each section below has a purpose, a rule, and a ready-to-use template. Copy, personalise where indicated, and paste directly into LinkedIn.

---

## 1. About Section

The About section is your pitch. It should tell a prospect exactly what Nivy does, who it helps, and what to do next. Keep it professional, outcome-focused, and easy to skim.

**Rules:**

- Start with a strong opening line — no generic introductions
- Use short paragraphs and line breaks — LinkedIn compresses long blocks
- End with a clear call to action
- Include keywords naturally (accounting, CPA, automation, lead generation)
- Maximum 2,000 characters

**Copy-paste template:**

---

I work with Nivy — a global professional services ecosystem that helps businesses simplify operations, finance, compliance, technology, and growth.

Working with the Nivy ecosystem to connect businesses with professional, technical, and growth services across international markets.

## 5. Featured Section

The Featured section is premium real estate — it appears directly under your About section and is the first thing a prospect clicks after reading your bio. Use it to build instant credibility.

**How to add:**

- Go to your profile → Add section → Featured
- Add links, posts, or media

**What to add (in order of priority):**

1. Nivy website link — [www.thenivy.com](http://www.thenivy.com) *(title: "Nivy — Global Business Services")*
2. Calendly booking link — *(ask your supervisor for the team booking link)*
3. A lead magnet — the Accounting Checklist or B2B Lead Generation Playbook *(see Content & Lead Magnets sub-page)*
4. A company introduction post you have published *(pin your best-performing post)*

> Never leave the Featured section empty. An empty profile looks incomplete and reduces credibility.

## 7. Recommendations

Recommendations are the strongest social proof on LinkedIn. Even 2–3 recommendations significantly increases connection acceptance and response rates from prospects.

**How to request one:**

- Go to your profile → Recommendations → Ask for a recommendation
- Send a personalised request (don't use the default LinkedIn message)

**Who to ask:**

- Your supervisor or team manager
- A colleague you have worked closely with
- Any client or partner contact if available

**What to suggest they mention:**

- Your professionalism and communication
- Your ability to understand client requirements
- Your work ethic and reliability

**Sample recommendation request message:**

Hi [Name], I hope you're doing well. I'm building out my LinkedIn profile as part of my role at Nivy and I'd really appreciate a short recommendation from you. Even 2–3 sentences about [working together / my communication / the work I've done] would be very helpful. Happy to write one for you as well. Thank you!

> Aim for a minimum of 3 recommendations before starting heavy outreach. This significantly increases your acceptance rate.
