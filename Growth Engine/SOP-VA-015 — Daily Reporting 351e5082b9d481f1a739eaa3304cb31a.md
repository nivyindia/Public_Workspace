# SOP-VA-015 — Daily Reporting

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** VA Manager | **Status:** Live
> 

> **Who Uses This:** VA Intern, VA Executive
> 

---

## Purpose

To submit an accurate, complete daily performance report every working day — giving the Manager full visibility of VA activity and results without needing to ask.

---

## Submission Deadline

> **Every working day by 6:00 PM (your local time).** Late reports are a performance issue and logged on the scorecard.
> 

---

## Daily Report Format

Copy and complete this format every day. Submit via Notion or the assigned channel.

```
————————————————————
📊 DAILY REPORT — [YOUR NAME] — [DATE]
————————————————————

LEADS
New leads added to CRM today: ___
Total active leads in pipeline: ___

OUTREACH
New outreach messages sent: ___
Platform(s) used: ___
Follow-up messages sent: ___

RESPONSES
Total replies received today: ___
Positive replies (Interested): ___
Not interested / Dead: ___
Other (questions, OOO, etc.): ___

BOOKINGS
Calls booked today: ___
Call Handover Forms submitted today: ___

KEY METRICS
Reply rate today: ___% (Replies / Messages Sent × 100)
Booking rate today: ___% (Booked / Positive Replies × 100)

BLOCKERS
Any issues, tool problems, or delays today? (write "None" if none)

TOMORROW'S PLAN
Target leads: ___ | Target messages: ___ | Priority task: ___
————————————————————
```

---

## How to Calculate Key Metrics

**Reply Rate:**

> Total replies received ÷ Total messages sent × 100
> 

> Example: 4 replies ÷ 22 messages = 18.2% reply rate
> 

**Booking Rate:**

> Calls booked ÷ Positive replies × 100
> 

> Example: 1 booked ÷ 3 positive replies = 33.3% booking rate
> 

> **Important:** Reply rate below 8% for 3 consecutive days = flag to Manager.
> 

---

## Step-by-Step

1. Open your daily report template at 5:30 PM
2. Pull figures from CRM — do not estimate from memory
3. Calculate reply rate and booking rate
4. Write a 1-line blocker note (write "None" if nothing)
5. Write tomorrow’s plan (3 fields)
6. Submit via assigned channel by 6:00 PM

---

## Output Standard

- All fields filled — no blanks
- Metrics calculated correctly (not guessed)
- Submitted by 6:00 PM every working day
- Data matches CRM — no rounding or inflating

---

## Common Mistakes

- Estimating numbers from memory instead of pulling from CRM
- Leaving the "Blockers" field blank when there was a genuine issue
- Submitting late without flagging it to Manager first
- Calculating reply rate wrong (dividing by total leads instead of messages sent)
- Not filling in Tomorrow's Plan — this is a planning commitment, not optional

---

## QC Checklist

- [ ]  All fields filled
- [ ]  Numbers pulled from CRM (not estimated)
- [ ]  Reply rate and booking rate correctly calculated
- [ ]  Submitted by 6:00 PM
- [ ]  Blockers field completed (or "None")
- [ ]  Tomorrow's plan included
