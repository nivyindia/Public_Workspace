# 📞 VA Communication System

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 29, 2026 | **Owner:** Ops Head | **Status:** Live
> 

---

## Purpose

This document defines the communication rules for the Nivy VA team — which platforms to use, when, for what, and how to escalate. Clear communication rules remove ambiguity and reduce the number of messages that should have been a Notion entry.

---

## Platform Rules

| Platform | Used For | NOT Used For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp** | Urgent flags, interested lead alerts, quick team coordination, time-sensitive blockers | Performance disputes, client details, strategy discussions, status updates that belong in a report |
| **Notion** | All documentation, reports, SOPs, QC checklists, handover forms, plans | Casual conversation |
| **Email** | External communication with leads/clients only | Internal team messages |
| **LinkedIn / Instagram / WhatsApp outreach** | Lead outreach only | Internal team communication |

> **The test:** If it needs to be referenced later or reviewed by someone — it goes in Notion.
> 

---

## Response Time Standards

| Situation | Expected Response |
| --- | --- |
| Manager message during working hours | Within 2 hours |
| Team group message | Acknowledge within 2 hours |
| Urgent flag from Manager (marked urgent) | Within 30 minutes |
| Interested lead reply (from outreach) | Escalate to CRM within 30 minutes |
| Non-urgent question or update | Same working day |
| Outside working hours | Not required unless pre-agreed |

---

## Escalation Matrix

| Situation | Who to Contact | How | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead replies positively (Interested) | VA Manager | WhatsApp | Within 30 minutes |
| Lead asks about pricing or contracts | VA Manager | WhatsApp | Within 30 minutes |
| Lead makes a complaint | VA Manager | WhatsApp + Notion note | Immediately |
| System / tool failure | VA Manager | WhatsApp | Within 1 hour |
| Cannot complete >50% of daily target due to genuine blocker | VA Manager | WhatsApp | Same day |
| VA Manager needs Ops Head input | Ops Head | WhatsApp | Within 2 hours |
| Client complaint or serious escalation | Ops Head | WhatsApp + call | Immediately |
| VA no-show or unreachable | Manager → Ops Head | WhatsApp x2, then escalate | Within 2 hours |

---

## What to Handle vs What to Escalate

| Handle Yourself | Escalate to Manager |
| --- | --- |
| Deciding to skip a clearly disqualified lead | Any reply asking about pricing or delivery |
| Choosing follow-up timing within cadence | Any complaint or negative response from a lead |
| Formatting and submitting your daily report | System failure or tool access issue |
| Basic CRM data entry and status updates | Any situation not covered in your SOPs |
| Moving a lead to Dead after full sequence | Any quality failure flagged by a lead or client |

---

## Communication Don'ts

- Do NOT send your performance update as an informal WhatsApp message. That’s what the daily report is for.
- Do NOT discuss a lead’s personal details over WhatsApp.
- Do NOT send Nivy pricing, proposals, or contracts to any lead without Manager approval.
- Do NOT have a performance or HR-related conversation over WhatsApp. Those go through a formal 1:1.
- Do NOT sit on a blocker for more than 1 hour without escalating.

**Phase 3 completion test:** Manager spends less than 30 minutes per day on quality review. Reports arrive on time without being chased. QC failures are caught before reaching Manager 90% of the time.
