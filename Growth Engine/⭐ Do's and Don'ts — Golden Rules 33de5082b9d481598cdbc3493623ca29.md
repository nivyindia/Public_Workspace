# ⭐ Do's and Don'ts — Golden Rules

> Save this page. Read it on Day 1. Re-read it every time something feels unclear.
> 

---

## ✅ TOP 10 MUST-DOs

**1. Update the CRM after every single action**

Every message sent, every reply received, every call booked — update the CRM immediately. Your report numbers come from the CRM. If the CRM is wrong, everything is wrong.

**2. Submit your daily report by 7:00 PM every day**

No exceptions. Even if you had a bad day with zero calls booked, submit the report. What you report is used to improve your training and support. No report = absent.

**3. Use the approved scripts for your first 2 weeks**

Do not rewrite the scripts. They have been tested. Personalize only the {Name} and {niche} fields. Once you are consistently getting replies, your supervisor will help you improve them.

**4. Send at least 3 follow-ups before giving up on a lead**

Most conversations start on follow-up 3, 4, or 5. The VA who follows up wins. Do not mark a lead as No Response after 1–2 messages.

**5. Focus only on your assigned niche**

Do not target random industries. Your scripts, your ICP, and your research are all built around one niche. Spreading across niches on Day 1 will get you zero results.

**6. Reply to leads within 60 minutes**

A fast reply keeps momentum alive. A 24-hour delay often kills the conversation. Keep your platforms open during work hours.

**7. Book only qualified calls**

Before sending a calendar link, confirm: Do they have a real business? Do they actually need help? Are they genuinely interested? A bad call wastes everyone’s time.

**8. Fill out the handover form before every call is passed to the closer**

Client Name, Business Type, Requirement, Conversation Summary, Call Date & Time — all 5 fields. Incomplete handovers lose deals.

**9. Ask your supervisor before changing anything**

Niche, scripts, CRM structure, platform, reporting format — do not change any of these without approval. The system works when everyone follows the same process.

**10. Do the work before reading more training**

This training is useful only after you have tried executing. Do Stage 1, submit your report, then read Stage 2. Do not read 3 stages before doing any work.

---

## ❌ TOP 10 MUST-NEVER-DOs

**1. Never add fake leads or invent activity numbers**

This is immediate removal from the program. Your reports are checked against the CRM. If numbers do not match, you will be asked to explain.

**2. Never skip CRM updates**

A lead without a CRM entry does not exist. If it is not in the sheet, you cannot follow up, you cannot report it, and you cannot be paid for it.

**3. Never pitch in your connection request**

Asking for a meeting or selling in the first message gets you ignored and possibly flagged as spam. Build curiosity first. Sell later.

**4. Never exceed daily platform limits**

LinkedIn: 15–20 connection requests/day max. WhatsApp: 20–30 new contacts/day max. Instagram: 20–30 DMs/day max. Exceeding these gets your account restricted or banned.

**5. Never send pricing without moving to a call first**

If a lead asks about pricing, say: “It depends on your situation — easiest to go through it on a quick call.” Never quote numbers over DM.

**6. Never share company data or client information outside the team**

Lead data, CRM content, client names, call recordings — these are confidential. Sharing them externally is grounds for immediate removal and potential legal consequences.

**7. Never create a new social account after a restriction without approval**

If LinkedIn or Instagram restricts you, report it to your supervisor immediately. Do not create a backup account without permission — this can get the original account permanently banned.

**8. Never contact a client directly for personal business**

All leads and clients belong to the company. Using company data to run a side deal is a serious violation.

**9. Never go silent on your supervisor**

If you are sick, have a problem, or are stuck — communicate. Disappearing without notice is treated as abandonment.

**10. Never skip follow-ups because you are embarrassed to send another message**

Leads expect follow-ups. They are busy. A polite follow-up is professional, not annoying. The VA who follows up gets the call.

> 

> Results follow consistency — never the other way around.
