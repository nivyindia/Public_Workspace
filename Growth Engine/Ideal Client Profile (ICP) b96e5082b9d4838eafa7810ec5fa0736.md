# 5. Buying Signals (Very Important)

These companies are **most likely to buy**.

Look for profiles or companies that:

- Have **active website**
- Run **ads or marketing**
- Are **hiring staff**
- Mention **growth or expansion**
- Already work with **agencies or freelancers**
